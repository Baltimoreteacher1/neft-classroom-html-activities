Answer Key — Unit 3 Practice Test, Form B

Ratios & Rates · Teacher copy: answers, explanations, and scoring rubrics.

These are MSTAR-style questions written for this curriculum to rehearse the format. They are not official Maryland assessment items. MSTAR — Maryland's new state test, first given in spring 2027 — uses the same kinds of questions you see here: selected response, select-all, two-part evidence questions, and written responses.
16.AT.13-1 · Understand Ratios

Part A

A class has 12 sixth graders and 18 seventh graders. What is the ratio of sixth graders to the TOTAL number of students, written in simplest form?

A. 2 : 5

The total is 12 + 18 = 30. The ratio 12 : 30 divides by 6 to give 2 : 5.

Part B

Which statement explains why 2 : 3 is NOT the answer to Part A?

A. 2 : 3 is the part-to-part ratio of sixth graders to seventh graders, not part-to-whole.

12 : 18 simplifies to 2 : 3, which compares the two subgroups to each other. Part A asked for a part compared to the whole group of 30.

26.AT.23-2 · Understand Rates and Unit Rates

Part A

Booth A charges $3 for 5 games. What is the unit rate in dollars per game?

B. $0.60 per game

Unit rate = total cost ÷ number of games = 3 ÷ 5 = $0.60 per game.

Part B

Booth B charges $5 for 8 games. Which booth is the better deal, and why?

D. Booth A, because $0.60 per game is less than $0.625 per game.

Booth B's unit rate is 5 ÷ 8 = $0.625 per game. Comparing per-game costs, $0.60 < $0.625, so Booth A costs less per game.

36.AT.3a3-3 · Determine Equivalent Ratios Using Tables

Select all that apply

Select ALL statements that are true about ratio tables and equivalent ratios:

A, B, D, E. Multiplying both terms of a ratio by 3 produces an equivalent ratio. · Dividing both terms by a common factor produces an equivalent ratio. · Every row of a correct ratio table simplifies to the same ratio. · A ratio table can be extended in either direction, up or down.

A, B, D, and E are true. C is false — equivalence comes from multiplying or dividing both terms, never from adding.

46.AT.3a3-4 · Determine Equivalent Ratios Using Graphs

Part A

A ratio table pairs 1 hour with 45 miles, 2 hours with 90 miles, and 3 hours with 135 miles. Which ordered pair belongs on the graph of this relationship?

A. (4, 180)

The relationship is 45 miles per hour, so 4 hours pairs with 4 × 45 = 180 miles, giving the point (4, 180).

Part B

What does it mean that the graph of this relationship passes through the origin (0, 0)?

A. When zero hours have passed, zero miles have been traveled.

A proportional relationship passes through the origin because zero of one quantity corresponds to zero of the other — no distance is covered in no time.

56.AT.33-5 · Compare Ratio Relationships

Written response — 2 points

Jaden compared two paint sizes: a 3-gallon can for $54 and a 5-gallon can for $85. He wrote: 'The 3-gallon can is the better deal because $54 is less than $85.'

Explain Jaden's misconception and determine which can is actually the better deal.

Score 2: Student computes both unit prices ($18 per gallon and $17 per gallon), identifies the 5-gallon can as the better deal, and explains that a lower total price does not mean a lower price per gallon.

Score 1: Student identifies the 5-gallon can but does not show or explain the unit prices.

Score 0: Student agrees with Jaden or gives an unrelated response.

Model: 54 ÷ 3 = $18 per gallon; 85 ÷ 5 = $17 per gallon. The 5-gallon can is the better deal. Jaden compared totals for different amounts of paint; only the price per gallon compares them fairly.

66.AT.33-6 · Ratio Reasoning: Convert Measurements within the Same System

Part A

A rope measures 7 feet. Using 1 foot = 12 inches, how long is the rope in inches?

D. 84 inches

7 feet × 12 inches per foot = 84 inches.

Part B

How do you know the answer to Part A should be a LARGER number than 7?

C. An inch is smaller than a foot, so it takes more inches to measure the same length.

The smaller the unit, the more of them are needed to cover the same length. Since an inch is smaller than a foot, the count in inches must be larger.

76.AT.33-7 · Ratio Reasoning: Convert Measurements Between Systems

Select all that apply

Select ALL statements that are true about converting measurements between systems:

A, B, C, E. A conversion ratio connects a unit in one system to a unit in another. · Converting to a smaller unit produces a larger number. · Between-system conversions are often approximate. · A ratio table can organize a between-system conversion.

A, B, C, and E are true. D is false — converting changes the unit and the number, but the physical amount being measured stays exactly the same.

86.AT.23-8 · Solve Problems with Unit Rates

Written response — 2 points

Rosa knew 8 notebooks cost $20 and wanted the cost of 14 notebooks. She wrote: '14 is 6 more than 8, so add $6: the cost is $26.'

Explain Rosa's misconception and give the correct cost.

Score 2: Student explains that the relationship is multiplicative, not additive — 6 more notebooks cost 6 × $2.50 = $15, not $6 — and gives $35.00.

Score 1: Student gives $35.00 without explaining why adding is wrong.

Score 0: Student agrees with Rosa or gives an unrelated response.

Model: The unit price is 20 ÷ 8 = $2.50. Six more notebooks cost 6 × $2.50 = $15, so the total is $35.00. Rosa added the DIFFERENCE IN COUNT to the price, mixing notebooks and dollars.

96.AT.33-9 · Equivalent Ratios

Part A

Solve the proportion 4/10 = x/25 for x.

B. 10

The scale factor from 10 to 25 is 2.5, so x = 4 × 2.5 = 10. Cross products confirm it: 4 × 25 = 100 and 10 × 10 = 100.

Part B

How do cross products show that two ratios are equivalent?

B. If a/b = c/d, then a × d equals b × c.

Multiplying both sides of a/b = c/d by b·d gives a·d = b·c, so equal cross products mean the ratios are equivalent.

106.AT.3c3-10 · Convert Measurement Units

A, B, C, E. The given measurement is written as a fraction over 1. · Conversion ratios are arranged so unwanted units cancel. · Only the unit that remains after cancelling appears in the answer. · More than one conversion ratio can be used in a single chain.
