16.AT.44-1 · Understand Percent

Select all that apply

Select ALL statements that correctly represent a rate per 100 or compare percents to one whole:

A, B, C, E. 100% represents exactly one whole quantity. · 45 out of 100 squares shaded on a 10×10 grid represents 45%. · A percent greater than 100% represents an amount that is more than the original whole. · A percent is a ratio that compares a number to 100.

Statements A, B, C, and E are true. Statement D is false because 0.5% is 0.5 out of 100 (1 out of 200), whereas 50 out of 100 is 50%.

26.AT.44-2 · Relate Fractions, Decimals, and Percentages

Written response — 2 points

Elena wrote that 0.08 is equal to 80% because 'there is an 8 in the number.'

Explain Elena's misconception and determine the correct percent value.

Score 2: Student clearly identifies that 0.08 has 8 in the hundredths place (8/100 = 8%) and explains that 80% is 0.80 (80/100).

Score 1: Student provides the correct answer (8%) but provides an incomplete or vague explanation of place value.

Score 0: Student agrees with Elena or gives an incorrect response.

Model: 0.08 = 8%. Elena confused hundredths with tenths; 80% is 0.80 (80/100), whereas 0.08 is 8 hundredths (8/100 = 8%).

Instant scoring looks for (2 pts = value + reasoning; 1 pt = one of them):

Value: 8

Reasoning words (any 2): hundredths

Word matching cannot judge an argument. A student who reasons correctly in other words will score low here; re-score from the rubric above.

36.AT.44-3 · Estimate the Percent of a Number

Part A

A jacket originally priced at $78.99 is on sale for 25% off. Which is the best estimate of the discount amount?

C. $20

Round $78.99 to $80. 25% is 1/4 of the whole. 1/4 of $80 is $20.

Part B

Which benchmark strategy gives the most accurate estimate in Part A?

B. Round $78.99 to $80 and find 1/4 of $80 (half of $80 is $40, and half of $40 is $20).

25% equals 1/4. Finding half of half of a rounded friendly whole ($80) produces an accurate mental benchmark.

46.AT.44-4 · Find and Compare with Percentages

Part A

At West Middle School, 45% of the 360 sixth-grade students participate in after-school sports. How many sixth-grade students participate in sports?

C. 162 students

45% of 360 = 0.45 × 360 = 162 students.

Part B

Which calculation method accurately justifies this solution in Part A?

A. Multiply the decimal representation of the percent by the whole: 0.45 × 360 = 162.

Finding a percent of a number requires multiplying the rate per hundred (0.45) by the total population (360).

56.AT.44-5 · Determine the Whole Given the Part and Percent

Select all that apply

A theater is at 80% capacity with 240 seats occupied. Select ALL statements that are mathematically true regarding this situation:

A, B, C, E. The total seating capacity of the theater is 300 seats (240 ÷ 0.80 = 300). · There are 60 empty seats in the theater (300 - 240 = 60). · 10% of the theater capacity is 30 seats (300 ÷ 10 = 30). · A tape diagram with 5 equal units of 60 seats represents the 300 total seats (4 units = 240 = 80%).

Statements A, B, C, and E are true. Statement D is false because 192 represents 80% of 240 (taking a percent of the part), rather than finding the whole capacity (300).
