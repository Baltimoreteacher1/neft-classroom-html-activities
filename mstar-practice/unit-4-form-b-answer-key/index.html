16.AT.44-1 · Understand Percent

Part A

A robotics club had a fundraising goal of $500. By Friday, they collected $750. What percent of their goal did they achieve?

D. 150%

$750 is 1.5 times the whole goal of $500. 1.5 × 100 = 150%, which represents 150% of the goal.

Part B

Which explanation best supports your answer to Part A?

D. Dividing $750 by $500 gives 1.5, and multiplying 1.5 by 100 gives 150%, representing 1.5 times the original whole.

A percent compares a part to a whole of 100. 750 / 500 = 1.5 = 150/100 = 150%.

26.AT.44-2 · Relate Fractions, Decimals, and Percentages

Part A

What is the fraction 7/20 written as a decimal and as a percent?

B. 0.35 and 35%

7/20 = (7 × 5) / (20 × 5) = 35/100 = 0.35 = 35%.

Part B

Which mathematical reasoning justifies the conversion in Part A?

A. Multiplying both numerator and denominator by 5 yields the equivalent fraction 35/100, which equals 0.35 and 35%.

Scaling the denominator to 100 by multiplying by 5/5 preserves equivalence and directly reveals the rate per hundred.

A, B, D, E. To find 10% of any number, divide the number by 10 (move the decimal point one place left). · To find 5% of a number, find 10% and take half of that value. · To find 20% of a number, find 10% and double it. · To find 1% of a number, divide the number by 100 (move the decimal point two places left).

46.AT.44-4 · Find and Compare with Percentages

Written response — 2 points

Store A offers a $120 skateboard at 30% off. Store B offers the same $120 skateboard with a $40 instant manufacturer coupon.

Which store offers the lower final price? Justify your decision by showing the savings and final price at both stores.

Score 2: Student calculates Store A discount as $36 (0.30 × 120) with final price $84, calculates Store B final price as $80 ($120 - $40), and concludes Store B is cheaper by $4.

Score 1: Student correctly calculates discounts for both stores but makes an arithmetic error on final price or fails to state which store is cheaper.

Score 0: Student chooses a store without supporting mathematical calculations.

Model: Store B offers the lower final price ($80 vs $84). Store A discount is 30% of $120 = $36 (final price $84). Store B discount is $40 (final price $80). Store B saves $4 more.

56.AT.44-5 · Determine the Whole Given the Part and Percent

Part A

Marcus completed 18 math problems, which represents exactly 60% of his homework assignment. How many total problems are on the assignment?

C. 30 problems

Part = Percent × Whole. 18 = 0.60 × Whole. Whole = 18 ÷ 0.60 = 30 problems.

Part B

Which equation and reasoning justifies the solution in Part A?

C. Since 60% of the whole equals 18, divide the part by the rate: 18 ÷ 0.60 = 30.

To find the whole given the part and percent, divide the given part by the percent expressed as a decimal: 18 ÷ 0.60 = 30.
