16.AT.11Explore Relationships Between Two Variables

Part A

Devon adds a different number of scoops of plant food to each tray of seedlings. Two weeks later he counts the new leaves on every tray. Which pair correctly names the variables?

Part B

Which sentence shows why the pair chosen in Part A is correct?

26.AT.11Analyze Graphs of Relationships Between Two Variables

Part A

A stadium shuttle carries fans from the parking lot. The table pairs the number of trips with the total fans carried: 2 trips with 96 fans, 5 trips with 240 fans, and 8 trips with 384 fans. On a graph with trips on the horizontal axis and fans on the vertical axis, which ordered pair belongs to 3 trips?

Part B

Which explanation justifies the ordered pair chosen in Part A?

36.AT.11Write Equations to Represent Relationships Between Two Variables

Select all that apply

A community pool charges $9 for each hour of lane rental. Let h be the number of hours rented and let c be the total cost in dollars. Select ALL statements that correctly represent this relationship.

46.AT.11Apply Two-Variable Relationships to Solve Problems

Explain the error

The paint booth coats 6 car doors every hour, so d = 6h, where h is the hours the booth runs and d is the doors coated. Marisol needs 100 doors coated. She writes 6h = 100, divides to get 100 ÷ 6 = 16.66..., says the booth needs about 16 hours, and schedules 16 hours.

Explain Marisol's misconception and give the correct number of whole hours the booth must run.
