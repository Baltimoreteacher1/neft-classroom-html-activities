26.AT.11Analyze Graphs of Relationships Between Two Variables

Explain the error

Ramona is graphing sales at the stadium pretzel stand. Pretzels cost $6 each, and her table shows 1 pretzel with $6, 2 pretzels with $12, and 3 pretzels with $18. She labels the horizontal axis 'number of pretzels' and the vertical axis 'total cost'. For 3 pretzels she plots the point (18, 3).

Explain Ramona's misconception and give the correct ordered pair for 3 pretzels.

Part A
