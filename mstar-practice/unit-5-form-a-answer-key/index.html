16.GR.15-1 · Determine the Area of Parallelograms and Rhombuses

Select all that apply

Select ALL statements that are true about finding the area of a parallelogram:

A, B, D, E. Any side can be chosen as the base, as long as the height is perpendicular to that side. · The height is the distance straight across between the two parallel bases. · A rectangle is a parallelogram, so A = b · h works for it too. · Doubling only the base doubles the area.

A, B, D, and E are true. C is false: the slanted side is longer than the perpendicular height, so using it overstates the area.

26.GR.15-2 · Determine the Area of Triangles

Written response — 2 points

Dev found the area of a triangle with a base of 10 cm and a height of 7 cm. He wrote: 'Area = 10 × 7 = 70 square centimeters.'

Explain Dev's misconception and give the correct area.

Score 2: Student identifies that Dev used the parallelogram formula and omitted the factor of ½, and gives 35 square centimeters.

Score 1: Student gives 35 square centimeters but does not explain why the ½ is needed.

Score 0: Student agrees with Dev or gives an unrelated response.

Model: Area = ½ × 10 × 7 = 35 square centimeters. Dev used b · h, which is the area of a parallelogram; a triangle is half of that parallelogram.

36.GR.15-3 · Determine the Area of Trapezoids

Part A

A trapezoidal window has parallel edges of 4 feet and 8 feet and a height of 5 feet. What is its area?

C. 30 square feet

Area = ½ × (b₁ + b₂) × h = ½ × (4 + 8) × 5 = ½ × 12 × 5 = 30 square feet.

Part B

What does the quantity (b₁ + b₂) ÷ 2 represent in the trapezoid area formula?

A. The average length of the two parallel bases.

Averaging the two bases turns the trapezoid into a rectangle of the same area with that average width, which is then multiplied by the height.

46.GR.15-4 · Apply Area Concepts to Solve Problems

Part A

An L-shaped room splits into two rectangles: one is 12 feet by 8 feet, the other is 6 feet by 5 feet. What is the total floor area?

D. 126 square feet

12 × 8 = 96 and 6 × 5 = 30. The total is 96 + 30 = 126 square feet.

Part B

Why is decomposing a composite figure into rectangles a valid strategy?

A. The areas of the non-overlapping pieces add to the area of the whole figure.

Area is additive: if the pieces do not overlap and cover the figure completely, their areas sum to the total.

56.GR.25-5 · Determine the Volume of Rectangular Prisms

Select all that apply

Select ALL statements that correctly describe volume:

A, B, C, E. Volume is measured in cubic units. · V = l × w × h for a rectangular prism. · Volume can also be found as base area × height. · Doubling only the height of a prism doubles its volume.

A, B, C, and E are true. D is false — square units measure area; volume fills space and uses cubic units.

66.GR.45-6 · Represent Three-Dimensional Figures in Two Dimensions

Part A

A net folds into a rectangular prism. How many rectangular faces does that net have?

B. 6

A rectangular prism has 6 faces — top and bottom, front and back, and two ends — so its net shows all 6.

Part B

Which statement must be true for a flat pattern to be a valid net of a solid?

C. It folds into the solid with no faces overlapping and none missing.

A net is a flat pattern that folds into the solid with every face present exactly once and no overlap.

76.GR.45-7 · Determine Surface Area of Prisms

Written response — 2 points

Priya found the surface area of a box measuring 5 by 3 by 2 units. She wrote: '5×3 + 5×2 + 3×2 = 15 + 10 + 6 = 31 square units.'

Explain Priya's misconception and give the correct surface area.

Score 2: Student identifies that a rectangular prism has 6 faces in 3 matching pairs, so each product must be doubled, and gives 62 square units.

Score 1: Student gives 62 square units without explaining the matching pairs.

Score 0: Student agrees with Priya or gives an unrelated response.

Model: Surface area = 2(15) + 2(10) + 2(6) = 62 square units. Priya found only three faces; every face has a matching opposite face, so each product is doubled.

86.GR.45-8 · Determine Surface Area of Pyramids

Select all that apply

Select ALL statements that are true about the surface area of a pyramid:

A, B, C, E. Surface area = base area + the areas of all lateral faces. · A square pyramid has 4 triangular lateral faces. · Each lateral face area is ½ × base of the triangle × slant height. · A pyramid's net shows the base and every lateral face laid flat.

A, B, C, and E are true. D is false — the slant height, not the vertical height, is the height of each triangular face.

96.GR.15-9 · Area of Regular Polygons

Part A

A regular hexagon is decomposed from its center into 6 identical triangles. Each triangle has a base of 8 cm and a height of 7 cm. What is the area of the hexagon?

C. 168 square centimeters

Each triangle = ½ × 8 × 7 = 28 square centimeters. Six of them: 6 × 28 = 168 square centimeters.

Part B

Why can a regular polygon be decomposed into identical triangles from its center?

C. All its sides are equal and all its angles are equal, so each center-to-side triangle is congruent.

Regular means equal sides and equal angles, so the triangles formed from the center to each side are congruent and share the same height.

106.GR.25-10 · Volume of Rectangular Prisms

Part A

A container measures 1½ feet long, 2 feet wide, and 3 feet tall. What is its volume?

C. 9 cubic feet

1½ = 3/2. V = 3/2 × 2 × 3 = 3 × 3 = 9 cubic feet.

Part B

Why is a mixed number converted to an improper fraction before multiplying edge lengths?

C. So the whole-number part and the fraction part are both multiplied, not just one of them.

Multiplying only the whole-number part drops the fractional part of the edge. Writing 1½ as 3/2 keeps the entire length in the product.
