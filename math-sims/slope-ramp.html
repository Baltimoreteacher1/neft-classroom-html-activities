📐 Slope Ramp

Rise (how high)

3

Run (how far across)

4
Slope = rise / run = 3/4
For every 1 across, the ramp rises 0.75.
A gentle ramp — the ball rolls slowly.

Change the rise and run to make the ramp steeper or gentler. Steeper ramps have a bigger slope — and the ball speeds up faster. Nothing to win or lose; just explore.
