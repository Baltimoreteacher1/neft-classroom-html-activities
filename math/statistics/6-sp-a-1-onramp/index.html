What Makes a Statistical Question?

I can tell a statistical question (one that expects different answers) from a non-statistical one.

📘 Word Bank + Sentence Frames

statistical questionvariability (answers differ)dataone answer vs. many

  • A statistical question expects different answers from different people or things.
  • A non-statistical question has just one answer.
  • Frame: "This is statistical because the answers would be ____ (different / the same)."

👀 See It: A Worked Example

✅ Statistical

"How tall are the students in our class?" → many different heights.

❌ Not statistical

"How tall am I?" → only one answer.

Statistical questions expect variability — the answers are not all the same.
