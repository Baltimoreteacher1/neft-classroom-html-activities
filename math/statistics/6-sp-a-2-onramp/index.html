Three Ways to Describe Data

I can describe a data set by its center, its spread, and its shape.

📘 Word Bank + Sentence Frames

center (typical)spread (varied)shape (pattern)distribution

  • Center: a typical value. Spread: how spread out. Shape: the pattern (one peak, clusters, gaps).

👀 See It

Test scores mostly near 80 (center), ranging 60–100 (spread), with one peak (shape).
