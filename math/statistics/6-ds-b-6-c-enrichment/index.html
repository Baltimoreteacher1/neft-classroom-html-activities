Stretch: Describing How Spread Out Data Is

I can describe the spread of a data set using the range and the idea of the middle group.

🧠 The Challenge

rangespreadmiddle halfvariability

Range = highest − lowest measures total spread. The middle half of the data (between the lower and upper quarters) shows where most values sit — a more stable measure of spread.

👀 Worked Example

Data: 3, 5, 6, 7, 20. Range = 20 − 3 = 17 (large, because of the 20). Most values cluster between 5 and 7.
