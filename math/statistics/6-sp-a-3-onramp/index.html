Summarizing Data with a Single Number

I can explain that a measure of center summarizes the data with one number, and a measure of spread describes its variation.

📘 Word Bank + Sentence Frames

measure of centermeasure of spreadmean / medianrange

  • Center (mean or median) = one number that summarizes the data.
  • Spread (range) = how much the data varies.

👀 See It

Data 4, 6, 8: center (mean) = 6 summarizes it; spread (range) = 8 − 4 = 4 shows the variation.
