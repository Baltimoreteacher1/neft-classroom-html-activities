Choosing Mean or Median

I can choose the better measure of center based on the shape of the data.

📘 Word Bank + Sentence Frames

meanmedianoutlierskewed / balanced

  • Use the mean for balanced data with no extreme values.
  • Use the median when there is an outlier or the data is skewed.

👀 See It

Salaries 30, 32, 33, 35, 200 (thousand): one huge outlier pulls the mean up, so the median (33) describes "typical" better.
