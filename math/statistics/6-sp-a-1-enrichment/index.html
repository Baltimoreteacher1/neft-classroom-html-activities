Stretch: Turn a Question Statistical

I can explain why a question anticipates variability and rewrite a non-statistical question to make it statistical.

🧠 The Challenge

A statistical question anticipates variability: it is about a group, and the answers are expected to differ. You can often fix a non-statistical question by widening it to a group.

Example: "How tall am I?" (one answer) → "How tall are the students in grade 6?" (many answers — statistical).
