Key Words
Learn these 6 statistics words before you begin
❓
Statistical Question
A question that expects a variety of different answers instead of just one single value.
"How much did you sleep?"
⚖️
Mean (Average)
The balance point of a dataset. Sum all values and divide by the total count.
(2+3+3+5+8+9)/6 = 5
🎯
Median
The exact middle value in a sorted list. Splits the dataset into two equal halves.
Median of [2,3,3,5,8,9] = 4
↔️
Range
The total spread of the numbers. Calculated as Maximum minus Minimum.
Range: 9 − 2 = 7
📐
MAD (Mean Absolute Deviation)
The average distance that each data value lies from the calculated mean. Measures consistency.
MAD = 2.33 hours
📈
Dot Plot
A chart that uses stacked dots above a number line to show how many times values appear.
Shows shape & gaps

Mean: Add all data values together and divide by how many values there are.
Median: Put all values in order from least to greatest. Find the middle number. If there are 2 middle numbers, find their average.

Find the Range and Mean Absolute Deviation (MAD) step-by-step
Your job: Analyze the spread of your class sleep data. Calculate the Range (overall spread) and find the MAD (average distance from the mean) step-by-step.
LEVEL 2 OUTLIER SIMULATOR

Add an outlier sleep value below (e.g. 2 hours or 14 hours) to see how much Range and MAD are affected by extreme values.

Need a hint?

Range: Subtract the smallest value from the largest value.
MAD: Find the absolute distance of every point from the mean. Add those distances together. Divide that sum of distances by the count of points.
