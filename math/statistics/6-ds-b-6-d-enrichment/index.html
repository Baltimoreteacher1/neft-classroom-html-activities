Stretch: Center, Spread, and Outliers

I can choose a measure of center or spread that fits the shape and context of the data.

🧠 The Challenge

meanmedianoutliercenterspreadrange

Pick the measure that fits the data. With a strong outlier or skew, the median describes the center better than the mean. For symmetric data with no outliers, the mean works well. To show spread, use the range or IQR.

👀 Worked Example

Incomes {20, 22, 25, 21, 300}: the 300 is an outlier, so the median (22) describes the center better than the mean (77.6).
