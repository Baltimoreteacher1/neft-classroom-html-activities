Stretch: Describe and Compare Data Shapes

I can describe a distribution by center, spread, and shape, and compare two distributions.

🧠 The Challenge

centerspreadshape (skew / clusters)compare

To compare two data sets, look at all three features: center (which is typically higher), spread (which varies more), and shape (symmetric, skewed, clustered, or with gaps).

👀 Worked Example

Class A scores cluster near 85 with small spread; Class B centers near 70 with a wide spread and a low outlier. A is higher and more consistent; B is lower and more variable.
