Words to knowPalabras clave

  • independent variablevariable independiente

    The input quantity in a model, chosen or observed.La cantidad de entrada de un modelo, elegida u observada.

    Miguel picks the number of MONTHS — nothing else decides it for him.

  • dependent variablevariable dependiente

    The quantity that changes in response to the independent variable.La cantidad que cambia en respuesta a la variable independiente.

    Total cost = $24.95 × months — the cost RESPONDS to the months.

  • depends ondepende de

    One quantity depends on another when changing the first causes the second to change.Una cantidad depende de otra cuando cambiar la primera hace cambiar la segunda.

    “Total cost depends on the number of months.” Responder first, chooser last.

  • quantitycantidad

    Something that can be counted or measured.Algo que se puede contar o medir.

    24.95 dollars per month, 12 months — each is a number with a meaning.

Key ideaIdea clave

Independent & Dependent Variables.

  1. Independent Variable (x): The input / cause that is chosen or changes freely.
  2. Dependent Variable (y): The output / effect that depends on the independent variable.
  3. Sentence check: 'The (dependent) depends on the (independent)'

Variables independientes y dependientes. 1. Variable independiente (x): la entrada o causa que se elige o cambia libremente. 2. Variable dependiente (y): la salida o efecto que depende de la variable independiente. 3. Comprobación con una oración: «(la dependiente) depende de (la independiente)»

Watch out for a common mistakeCuidado con un error común

Naming the variables backward — calling the responding quantity 'independent' because it is the one the question asks about.

Sentence frameMarco de oración

The ___ depends on the ___, so ___ is the independent variable.El ___ depende del ___, así que ___ es la variable independiente.

Apply: Selling Pizzas

At a local pizza shop, a customer can order a large pizza with up to three different toppings for $12.49. The shop is open daily from 11 a.m. to 9 p.m. and has a total of 6 employees. The shop spends $1.15 on cheese and $1.95 on sauce for each pizza. Question: How would you describe the relationship between the number of large pizzas sold and each of the following: the hours the shop is open, the number of employees who work in the shop, the cost of cheese each week, and the cost of sauce each week?

Real-world connectionConexión con el mundo real

At a local pizza shop a customer can order up to three toppings on a large pizza. The shop has 6 employees. The shop spends $1.15 on cheese and $1.95 on sauce for each pizza it makes.En una pizzería local un cliente puede pedir hasta tres ingredientes en una pizza grande. La pizzería tiene 6 empleados. La pizzería gasta $1.15 en queso y $1.95 en salsa por cada pizza que hace.

In this scenario, the ___ is the independent variable. The ___ and the ___ both depend on it. The number of ___ stays the same no matter how many pizzas are made.En esta situación, ___ es la variable independiente. ___ y ___ dependen de ella. El número de ___ se queda igual sin importar cuántas pizzas se hagan.
