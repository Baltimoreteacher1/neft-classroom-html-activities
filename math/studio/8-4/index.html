UnderstandComprende

Read the idea, follow the worked examples, and connect the picture to the numbers.Lee la idea, sigue los ejemplos resueltos y conecta el dibujo con los números.

How do I write an inequality from words?¿Cómo escribo una desigualdad a partir de palabras?

An inequality compares two amounts using <, >, ≤, or ≥. I use one when many values are allowed, not just one. I pick a variable and match the words to the right symbol.Una desigualdad compara dos cantidades usando <, >, ≤ o ≥. Uso una cuando se permiten muchos valores, no solo uno. Elijo una variable y hago corresponder las palabras con el símbolo correcto.

Watch me

  1. Clue: 'The suspect is at least 18 years old.'Pista: «El sospechoso tiene al menos 18 años.»
  2. I let a stand for the age. 'At least 18' means 18 or more.Dejo que a represente la edad. «Al menos 18» significa 18 o más.
  3. 'At least' includes 18, so I use the symbol ≥.«Al menos» incluye el 18, así que uso el símbolo ≥.
  4. My inequality is a ≥ 18. So a could be 18, 19, 20, and up.Mi desigualdad es a ≥ 18. Entonces a podría ser 18, 19, 20 y más.

Let's try together

  1. Clue: 'A bag holds fewer than 10 items.' What does 'fewer than' mean?Pista: «Una bolsa lleva menos de 10 artículos.» ¿Qué significa «menos de»?
  2. It means less than, and it does NOT include 10, so we use <.Significa menor que, y no incluye el 10, así que usamos <.
  3. We let n be the number of items: n < 10.Dejamos que n sea el número de artículos: n < 10.

Now it's your turn

  1. Sort the phrase cards into the matching symbol: >, <, ≥, or ≤.Clasifica las tarjetas de frases con el símbolo que les corresponde: >, <, ≥ o ≤.
  2. Then write inequalities for the practice situations.Luego escribe desigualdades para las situaciones de práctica.

Key ideaIdea clave

Writing & Representing Inequalities. Formula: < (less) | > (greater) | ≤ (at most / ≤) | ≥ (at least / ≥).

  1. 'At least', 'minimum', 'no less than' translate to ≥ (greater than or equal to).
  2. 'At most', 'maximum', 'no more than' translate to ≤ (less than or equal to).
  3. 'More than' is >; 'Fewer than' / 'Less than' is <

Escribir y representar desigualdades. Fórmula: < (menor) | > (mayor) | ≤ (como máximo) | ≥ (como mínimo). 1. «Al menos», «mínimo», «no menos de» se traducen a ≥ (mayor o igual que). 2. «Como máximo», «máximo», «no más de» se traducen a ≤ (menor o igual que). 3. «Más de» es >; «menos de» es <

Watch outCuidado

A common mistake in Write Inequalities is using a strict symbol (> or <) for a phrase that actually includes the boundary value. For 'the suspect is at least 18 years old,' students often write a > 18 instead of the correct a ≥ 18 — this wrongly excludes 18 itself, even though 'at least 18' means 18 counts as a match. The same error happens in reverse with 'at most': for 'the getaway bag holds at most 12 items,' students write n < 12 instead of n ≤ 12. Always ask: does the key phrase include the number ('at least,' 'at most,' 'no more than,' 'no fewer than' → use ≥ or ≤) or exclude it ('more than,' 'fewer than' → use > or <)?

Words to usePalabras para usar
  • Write InequalitiesEscribir desigualdades

    Represent a range of possible values with <, >, ≤, or ≥.Representar un rango de valores posibles con <, >, ≤ o ≥.

    You must be at least 12 years old: a ≥ 12.

  • InequalityDesigualdad

    A math sentence that compares two sides with <, >, ≤, or ≥.Una oración matemática que compara dos lados con <, >, ≤ o ≥.

    x > 5 means x can be 6, 7, 8, ... — any number greater than 5

  • Greater thanMayor que

    The > sign, showing one number is bigger.El signo >, que muestra que un número es mayor.

    8 > 3 — eight is larger than three

  • Less thanMenor que

    The < sign, showing one number is smaller.El signo <, que muestra que un número es menor.

    2 < 7 — two is smaller than seven

  • At least / At mostAl menos / A lo más

    'At least' means ≥. 'At most' means ≤.'Al menos' significa ≥. 'Como máximo' significa ≤.

    'At least 10 players' → p ≥ 10 (could be 10, 11, 12, ...); 'At most 5 tries' → t ≤ 5 (could be 5, 4, 3, ...)

  • Variable

    A letter that stands for a number that is unknown or can change.Una letra que representa un número desconocido o que puede cambiar.

    In x > 5, the letter x stands for any number greater than 5.

  • No more thanNo más de

    A phrase meaning less than or equal to (≤).Una frase que significa menor o igual que (≤).

    “No more than 8 people” means people ≤ 8.

  • ConstraintRestricción

    A limit that a solution must satisfy.Un límite que una solución debe cumplir.

    A voter must be 18 or older.

Sentence frameMarco de oración

'At least' uses ≥ instead of > because it means the value ___ be equal to the number, while 'more than' means it must be ___.«Al menos» usa ≥ en vez de > porque significa que el valor ___ ser igual al número, mientras que «más que» significa que debe ser ___.

Apply: Festival Tickets

They charge $15 per hour for yard work and will combine their earnings. Question: How many hours do the four friends need to work to earn enough money to buy four tickets? Propose a breakdown of how many hours each friend might work.
