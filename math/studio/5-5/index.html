Key ideaIdea clave

Volume of Rectangular Prisms (Whole Numbers). Formula: V = Length × Width × Height = Base Area × Height (V = l · w · h = B · h).

  1. Identify the length, width, and height of the box.
  2. Multiply base area (l × w) by height (h).
  3. Label answer in cubic units (e.g. in³, cm³, ft³)

Volumen de prismas rectangulares (números enteros). Fórmula: V = largo × ancho × alto = área de la base × altura (V = l · a · h = B · h). 1. Identifica el largo, el ancho y el alto de la caja. 2. Multiplica el área de la base (l × a) por la altura (h). 3. Rotula la respuesta en unidades cúbicas (por ejemplo, pulg³, cm³, pies³)

Watch out for a common mistakeCuidado con un error común

A common mistake in Volume with Whole Number Edges is multiplying only two of the three edges and stopping there — for example, seeing a box that is 6 in long, 4 in wide, and 3 in tall, multiplying just 6 × 4 = 24, and calling that the volume, when 24 is only the area of the bottom face (in square inches), not the volume of the whole box. The height must also be multiplied in: V = 6 × 4 × 3 = 72 cubic inches. A second common mistake is adding the three edges instead of multiplying them (6 + 4 + 3 = 13 instead of 6 × 4 × 3 = 72 cubic inches). Volume always MULTIPLIES all three edges, and the answer is always labeled in cubic units (in³), never square units (in²) or plain units (in).

Apply: Buying Popcorn

You go to the movie theater with your family, and your family likes to buy popcorn to snack on during the movie. The theater sells three different-sized boxes of popcorn. Small: width 4 in., $4.50. Medium: width 5 in., $5.75. Large: width 6 in., $7.00. Each box is a rectangular prism, and the length and height of each box grow with the width. Question: Is this information enough to determine which box has the lowest price per cubic inch? Identify any measurements you still need, and explain how you would use volume and unit rates to compare the boxes. What else would your family need to decide how many boxes to buy?
