2. What are equivalent ratios?2. ¿Qué son las razones equivalentes?

Equivalent ratios are ratios that mean the same thing. You make one by multiplying or dividing BOTH numbers by the same amount.Las razones equivalentes son razones que significan lo mismo. Formas una multiplicando o dividiendo los dos números por la misma cantidad.

3. Follow the examples3. Sigue los ejemplos

Watch me

  1. Chef Montoya's soup serves 8 people, but she needs to serve 32.La sopa de la chef Montoya rinde para 8 personas, pero necesita servir a 32.
  2. I find the scale factor: 32 divided by 8 = 4.Hallo el factor de escala: 32 entre 8 = 4.
  3. The recipe uses 3 cups of tomatoes to 2 cups of broth, a 3:2 ratio.La receta usa 3 tazas de tomate por 2 tazas de caldo, una razón de 3:2.

Key ideaIdea clave

Proportions & Equivalent Ratios. Formula: a/b = c/d.

  1. Two ratios are equivalent if their simplified forms or cross products are equal.
  2. Find the scale factor between corresponding terms.
  3. Multiply or divide to solve for the missing term in the proportion

Proporciones y razones equivalentes. Fórmula: a/b = c/d. 1. Dos razones son equivalentes si sus formas simplificadas o sus productos cruzados son iguales. 2. Halla el factor de escala entre los términos correspondientes. 3. Multiplica o divide para hallar el término que falta en la proporción

Watch out for a common mistakeCuidado con un error común

A common mistake in Equivalent Ratios is ADDING the scale factor to one part instead of MULTIPLYING both parts by it. For example, scaling 2:5 by a factor of 4 should give 8:20 (2×4 and 5×4) — not 6:9 (2+4 and 5+4). Adding changes the taste of the recipe (and the value of the ratio); only multiplying or dividing both numbers by the same amount keeps it equivalent.

Sentence frameMarco de oración

I multiplied ___ and ___ by the same number, ___, so the ratio ___ is equivalent to 3:2.Multipliqué ___ y ___ por el mismo número, ___, así que la razón ___ es equivalente a 3:2.

Apply: The Catering Order

Chef Kowalski's pancake batter recipe uses a ratio of 2 cups flour to 3 cups milk. For a big catering order, she uses 14 cups of flour and wants to keep the same ratio. How many cups of milk does she need? Show your scale factor and explain your reasoning.

Real-world connectionConexión con el mundo real

A local smoothie shop sells a Berry Blast smoothie. The recipe calls for 2 cups of strawberries for every 3 cups of yogurt. Today they need to make 15 cups of yogurt worth of smoothies for a catering order.Una juguería local vende un licuado Explosión de Frutos. La receta pide 2 tazas de fresas por cada 3 tazas de yogur. Hoy necesitan hacer licuados equivalentes a 15 tazas de yogur para un pedido de banquete.

Find the scale factor (15 ÷ 3) and use it to figure out how many cups of strawberries the shop needs. Show your steps and explain why multiplying both ingredients by the same factor keeps the smoothie tasting the same.Halla el factor de escala (15 ÷ 3) y úsalo para averiguar cuántas tazas de fresas necesita la tienda. Muestra tus pasos y explica por qué multiplicar los dos ingredientes por el mismo factor hace que el licuado siga sabiendo igual.
