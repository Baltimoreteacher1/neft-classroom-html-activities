Key ideaIdea clave

Comparing & Ordering Rational Numbers. Formula: Left is Less (<) | Right is Greater (>).

  1. Plot or visualize numbers on a horizontal number line.
  2. Numbers further to the left are always less than numbers to the right.
  3. For negatives: A negative number with a larger absolute value is smaller (-10 < -2)

Comparar y ordenar números racionales. Fórmula: a la izquierda es menor (<) | a la derecha es mayor (>). 1. Marca o imagina los números en una recta numérica horizontal. 2. Los números que están más a la izquierda siempre son menores que los que están a la derecha. 3. Con negativos: un número negativo con mayor valor absoluto es más pequeño (-10 < -2)

Watch out for a common mistakeCuidado con un error común

A common mistake in Compare and Order Integers is thinking that a negative number with a bigger digit is the bigger value — for example, believing -15 is greater than -8 because 15 is greater than 8. It's the opposite for negatives: the number farther to the left on the number line (farther from zero) is always the LESSER value, so -15 < -8. Before you submit, check your comparison against the number line, not just the digits.

Apply: A Game of Golf

Golf scores are measured as strokes over or under par. Par is the number of golf strokes considered necessary to get the ball in the hole, so a score under par is written as a negative integer and a score over par is written as a positive integer. The scorecard shows six players' scores relative to par after seven holes of a 9-hole round (par = 35): Evelyn -2, Brielle +1, Terrance 0, Reginald -3, Sofia +4, Francisco +2. Order the six scores from least to greatest to rank the players from first place (the current leader) to last place. Who is winning after 7 holes, and who has the most strokes to make up?
