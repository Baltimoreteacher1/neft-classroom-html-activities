A unit is a standard amount used to measure, like a foot or an inch. A conversion factor is a ratio that relates two units, like 1 foot = 12 inches.Una unidad es una cantidad estándar que se usa para medir, como un pie o una pulgada. Un factor de conversión es una razón que relaciona dos unidades, como 1 pie = 12 pulgadas.

Watch out for a common mistakeCuidado con un error común

A common mistake in Convert Measurement Units is applying the conversion factor in the wrong direction — for example, converting 4 feet to inches by dividing (4 ÷ 12 = 0.33 inches) instead of multiplying (4 × 12 = 48 inches), or converting 80 ounces to pounds by multiplying (80 × 16 = 1,280 pounds) instead of dividing (80 ÷ 16 = 5 pounds). Before you answer, ask: am I changing to a SMALLER unit (there will be MORE of them, so multiply) or a BIGGER unit (there will be FEWER of them, so divide)?
