  • The number that gets multiplied by itself.El número que se multiplica por sí mismo.

2. What does an exponent mean?2. ¿Qué significa un exponente?

An exponent is a small raised number that tells you how many times to multiply a number by itself. We say 2³ as "two to the third power."Un exponente es un número pequeño elevado que dice cuántas veces multiplicar un número por sí mismo. Decimos 2³ como «dos a la tercera potencia».

3. Follow the examples3. Sigue los ejemplos

Watch me

  1. I want to find the value of 2³.Quiero hallar el valor de 2³.
  2. The small 3 tells me to multiply 2 by itself 3 times.El 3 pequeño me dice que multiplique 2 por sí mismo 3 veces.
  3. So I write 2 × 2 × 2.Así que escribo 2 × 2 × 2.
  4. First 2 × 2 = 4. Then 4 × 2 = 8.Primero 2 × 2 = 4. Luego 4 × 2 = 8.
  5. So 2³ = 8.Entonces 2³ = 8.

Let's try together

  1. Now let's find 5². How many times do we multiply 5 by itself?Ahora hallemos 5². ¿Cuántas veces multiplicamos 5 por sí mismo?
  2. The exponent is 2, so we write 5 × 5.El exponente es 2, así que escribimos 5 × 5.
  3. What is 5 × 5? Yes, 25. So 5² = 25.¿Cuánto es 5 × 5? Sí, 25. Entonces 5² = 25.

Now it's your turn

  1. In the table, write each power as repeated multiplication and find its value.En la tabla, escribe cada potencia como multiplicación repetida y halla su valor.
  2. Start with 2⁴ = 2 × 2 × 2 × 2.Empieza con 2⁴ = 2 × 2 × 2 × 2.

Key ideaIdea clave

Powers & Exponents. Formula: Base^Exponent (b^n = b · b · ... · b n times).

  1. Base is the factor being multiplied repeatedly.
  2. Exponent tells how many times to multiply the base by itself.
  3. Evaluate: 2³ = 2 × 2 × 2 = 8 (not 2 × 3 = 6)

Potencias y exponentes. Fórmula: base elevada al exponente (b^n = b · b · … · b, n veces). 1. La base es el factor que se multiplica repetidamente. 2. El exponente dice cuántas veces multiplicar la base por sí misma. 3. Evalúa: 2³ = 2 × 2 × 2 = 8 (no 2 × 3 = 6)

Watch out for a common mistakeCuidado con un error común

A common mistake in Powers and Exponents is multiplying the base by the exponent instead of using the base as a repeated factor — for example, thinking 2³ = 2 × 3 = 6 instead of 2³ = 2 × 2 × 2 = 8. Before you submit, rewrite the power as repeated multiplication first, then multiply step by step.

Sentence frameMarco de oración

When the exponent increases by 1, the value is multiplied by ___ because the base is used as a factor ___ more time.Cuando el exponente aumenta en 1, el valor se multiplica por ___ porque la base se usa como factor ___ vez más.

Apply: Biology

James is studying the growth rate of a specific type of bacteria. He places three cells in a Petri dish and records the number of bacteria every five hours. His results are: 0 hours → 3 bacteria, 5 hours → 9 bacteria, 10 hours → 27 bacteria, 15 hours → 81 bacteria. Choose a question to answer. Question 1: The counts 3, 9, 27, 81 are powers of which base, and what power represents each new 5-hour reading? Question 2: What power would represent the number of bacteria after 25 hours, and what is its value?

Cada capa de pista duplica el tamaño del archivo. Una sola pista son 4 MB. El tamaño del archivo después de n capas está dado por 4 × 2ⁿ.
