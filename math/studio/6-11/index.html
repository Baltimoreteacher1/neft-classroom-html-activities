3Step 3 of 9:Paso 3 de 9: How do I solve a fraction division word problem?¿Cómo resuelvo un problema verbal de división de fracciones?

In a word problem, first decide what the question asks: how many groups fit (total ÷ size of each group) or how much is in one group (total ÷ number of groups). The total amount is the dividend and the other number is the divisor. Write the equation, solve it with a model, common denominators, or by multiplying by the reciprocal, then check by multiplying the quotient by the divisor.En un problema verbal, primero decide qué pregunta: cuántos grupos caben (total ÷ tamaño de cada grupo) o cuánto hay en un grupo (total ÷ número de grupos). La cantidad total es el dividendo y el otro número es el divisor. Escribe la ecuación, resuélvela con un modelo, con denominadores comunes o multiplicando por el recíproco, y luego comprueba multiplicando el cociente por el divisor.

Key ideaIdea clave

Fraction Division Word Problems. Formula: Total Amount ÷ Group Size = Number of Groups.

  1. Identify the Dividend (total amount being split or shared).
  2. Identify the Divisor (size of each group or part).
  3. Divide: Total ÷ Size = Number of Groups (or Total ÷ Groups = Size per Group)

Problemas verbales de división de fracciones. Fórmula: cantidad total ÷ tamaño del grupo = número de grupos. 1. Identifica el dividendo (la cantidad total que se reparte). 2. Identifica el divisor (el tamaño de cada grupo o parte). 3. Divide: total ÷ tamaño = número de grupos (o total ÷ grupos = tamaño de cada grupo)

Watch out for a common mistakeCuidado con un error común

A common mistake in Fraction Division Problem Solving is reversing which fraction is the dividend and which is the divisor when setting up the equation from a word problem. For example: a pitcher holds 3/4 cup of punch, and each serving is 1/8 cup — how many servings? The correct setup is total ÷ group size = 3/4 ÷ 1/8 = 3/4 × 8/1 = 24/4 = 6 servings. A student who reverses the setup calculates 1/8 ÷ 3/4 = 1/8 × 4/3 = 4/24 = 1/6, an answer smaller than 1 that can't possibly be a serving count. Before you submit, check: does my answer make sense as a NUMBER OF GROUPS? If the setup gives a fraction less than 1 for a 'how many' question, you likely swapped the dividend and divisor.

Sentence frameMarco de oración

The total amount is the ___ because it is being split into groups. The size of each group is the ___ because we are dividing by that amount.La cantidad total es el ___ porque se está repartiendo en grupos. El tamaño de cada grupo es el ___ porque estamos dividiendo entre esa cantidad.
