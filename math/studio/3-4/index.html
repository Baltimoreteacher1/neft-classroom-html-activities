An ordered pair (x, y) tells where to plot a point: go right x, then up y. When you graph equal ratios, the points make a straight line through the origin (0, 0).Un par ordenado (x, y) dice dónde marcar un punto: avanza x a la derecha y luego sube y. Key ideaIdea clave

Graphing Equivalent Ratios.

  1. Form ordered pairs (x, y) from columns or rows in a ratio table.
  2. Plot the points on the coordinate plane.
  3. Proportional Relationship: Points form a straight line passing through the origin (0, 0)

Graficar razones equivalentes. 1. Forma pares ordenados (x, y) con las columnas o filas de una tabla de razones. 2. Marca los puntos en el plano de coordenadas. 3. Relación proporcional: los puntos forman una línea recta que pasa por el origen (0, 0)

Watch out for a common mistakeCuidado con un error común

A common mistake in Graph Ratio Tables is writing the ordered pair in the wrong order — putting the second table value first instead of matching (x, y) to the table's column order. For example, if a recipe table shows 2 cups of sugar for every 4 cups of flour, a student might plot (4, 2) instead of (2, 4), landing the point in a completely different spot on the grid. Before you plot, check: which quantity goes on the x-axis, and does your ordered pair start with that number?

Sentence frameMarco de oración

Every time the sundaes go up by ___, the ounces go up by ___, so the points line up in a ___ starting from ___.Cada vez que los sundaes suben ___, las onzas suben ___, así que los puntos se alinean en una ___ que empieza en ___.
