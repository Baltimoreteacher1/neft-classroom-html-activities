Key ideaIdea clave

Integers & Opposites.

  1. Integers: Positive whole numbers, negative whole numbers, and zero (..., -2, -1, 0, 1, 2, ...).
  2. Opposites: Numbers that are the same distance from 0 in opposite directions (e.g. -5 and 5).
  3. The opposite of the opposite of a number is the number itself: -(-a) = a

Enteros y opuestos. 1. Enteros: números enteros positivos, números enteros negativos y el cero (…, -2, -1, 0, 1, 2, …). 2. Opuestos: números que están a la misma distancia del 0 en direcciones opuestas (por ejemplo, -5 y 5). 3. El opuesto del opuesto de un número es el número mismo: -(-a) = a

Watch out for a common mistakeCuidado con un error común

The most common mistake in this lesson is thinking that the opposite of a negative integer is still negative — for example, saying the opposite of -7 is -7, or that the opposite of -12 is -24. Opposites are the same DISTANCE from 0 but on OPPOSITE sides of 0, so taking an opposite always changes the direction and never changes the distance: the opposite of -7 is 7 and the opposite of -12 is 12. The one exception is 0, which is 0 units from itself and is therefore its own opposite. Before you submit, count the units from 0 and check that your answer is on the other side.

Real-world connectionConexión con el mundo real

To make a first down, a football team must gain at least 10 yards from where they started, a spot called the line of scrimmage. The line of scrimmage is 0: a gain moves the team above 0 and a loss moves the team below 0. During one series, a team had a 10-yard loss, a 4-yard gain, a 3-yard loss, and a 21-yard gain.Para lograr un primer intento, un equipo de fútbol americano debe ganar al menos 10 yardas desde donde empezó, un punto llamado la línea de golpeo. La línea de golpeo es 0: una ganancia mueve al equipo arriba de 0 y una pérdida lo mueve abajo de 0. Durante una serie, un equipo tuvo una pérdida de 10 yardas, una ganancia de 4 yardas, una pérdida de 3 yardas y una ganancia de 21 yardas.
