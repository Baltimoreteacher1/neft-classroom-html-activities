Words to knowPalabras clave

  • DeviationDesviación

    How far a number is from the mean.Qué tan lejos está un número de la media.

    If mean = 20 and value = 17, deviation = 17 − 20 = −3 (3 below the mean)

  • Absolute ValueValor absoluto

    How far a number is from zero. It is never negative.Qué tan lejos está un número de cero. Nunca es negativo.

    |−3| = 3 and |3| = 3 — both are 3 units from zero

  • SpreadDispersión

    How far apart the numbers are.Qué tan separados están los números.

    Low spread: 8, 9, 10, 11 (close together). High spread: 2, 9, 10, 25 (far apart)

  • Data distributionDistribución de datos

    How the data looks: where it sits and how spread out it is.Cómo se ven los datos: dónde están y qué tan separados están.

    A set clustered tightly around the mean has low MAD; a set spread far from the mean has high MAD

3Step 3 of 9:Paso 3 de 9: What is the mean absolute deviation (MAD)?¿Qué es la desviación media absoluta?

The mean absolute deviation, or MAD, is the average distance of each value from the mean. It tells you how spread out the data is: a small MAD means the data is close together, and a large MAD means it is spread out.La desviación media absoluta es la distancia promedio de cada valor a la media. Te dice qué tan dispersos están los datos: una desviación pequeña significa que los datos están juntos, y una grande significa que están dispersos.

Watch me

  1. My data set: 6, 8, 10, 12. First I find the mean: 6 + 8 + 10 + 12 = 36, then 36 ÷ 4 = 9. The mean is 9.Mi conjunto de datos: 6, 8, 10, 12. Primero hallo la media: 6 + 8 + 10 + 12 = 36, luego 36 ÷ 4 = 9. La media es 9.
  2. Next I find each deviation (value − mean): 6−9 = −3, 8−9 = −1, 10−9 = 1, 12−9 = 3.Luego hallo cada desviación (valor − media): 6−9 = −3, 8−9 = −1, 10−9 = 1, 12−9 = 3.

Key ideaIdea clave

Mean Absolute Deviation (MAD). Formula: MAD = (Sum of distances from mean) ÷ (Total count).

  1. Calculate the mean of the data set.
  2. Find the distance (absolute difference |value − mean|) for each data point.
  3. Average the distances: Add all distances and divide by the count

Desviación media absoluta. Fórmula: desviación media absoluta = (suma de las distancias a la media) ÷ (cantidad total). 1. Calcula la media del conjunto de datos. 2. Halla la distancia (diferencia absoluta |valor − media|) de cada dato. 3. Promedia las distancias: súmalas todas y divide entre la cantidad

Watch out for a common mistakeCuidado con un error común

A common mistake in Mean Absolute Deviation is adding the signed deviations (value − mean) instead of their absolute values, so the positives and negatives cancel out to 0. For 6, 8, 10, 12 (mean = 9), the deviations are −3, −1, 1, 3 — they sum to 0, but that is NOT the MAD. Before averaging, take the absolute value of every deviation so each becomes a nonnegative distance, including zero: |−3| = 3, |−1| = 1, |1| = 1, |3| = 3. Sum = 8, so MAD = 8 ÷ 4 = 2. A MAD of exactly 0 is a warning sign — it only happens if every value in the data set equals the mean.
