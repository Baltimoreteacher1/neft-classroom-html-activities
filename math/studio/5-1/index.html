UnderstandComprende

Read the idea, follow the worked examples, and connect the picture to the numbers.Lee la idea, sigue los ejemplos resueltos y conecta el dibujo con los números.

How do we find the area of a parallelogram?¿Cómo hallamos el área de un paralelogramo?

A parallelogram is a four-sided shape with two pairs of parallel sides, like a leaning rectangle. Its area is how much flat space is inside it.Un paralelogramo es una figura de cuatro lados con dos pares de lados paralelos, como un rectángulo inclinado. Su área es cuánto espacio plano hay adentro.

Watch me

  1. I have a parallelogram with a base of 14 feet and a height of 9 feet.Tengo un paralelogramo con base de 14 pies y altura de 9 pies.
  2. I write the formula: A = base × height.Escribo la fórmula: A = base × altura.
  3. I put in the numbers: A = 14 × 9.Sustituyo los números: A = 14 × 9.
  4. I multiply: 14 × 9 = 126.Multiplico: 14 × 9 = 126.
  5. So the area is 126 square feet. I used the height of 9, not the slanted side.Entonces el área es 126 pies cuadrados. Usé la altura de 9, no el lado inclinado.

Let's try together

  1. Now a smaller parallelogram: base = 5 cm, height = 4 cm.Ahora un paralelogramo más pequeño: base = 5 cm, altura = 4 cm.
  2. What is the formula? A = base × height.¿Cuál es la fórmula? A = base × altura.
  3. What do we put in? A = 5 × 4.¿Qué sustituimos? A = 5 × 4.
  4. What is 5 × 4? Yes, 20, so the area is 20 square centimeters.¿Cuánto es 5 × 4? Sí, 20, así que el área es 20 centímetros cuadrados.

Now it's your turn

  1. Next you will plot the patio on a grid and find its area yourself.Luego vas a marcar el patio en una cuadrícula y hallar su área por tu cuenta.
  2. Remember: multiply the base by the height, and always use the perpendicular height.Recuerda: multiplica la base por la altura, y usa siempre la altura perpendicular.

Key ideaIdea clave

Area of Parallelograms & Rhombuses. Formula: Area = Base × Height (A = b · h).

  1. Identify the base (any side).
  2. Identify the perpendicular height (height must be perpendicular to base, not slanted).
  3. Multiply base times height; label with square units (e.g. cm², in²)

Área de paralelogramos y rombos. Fórmula: área = base × altura (A = b · h). 1. Identifica la base (cualquier lado). 2. Identifica la altura perpendicular (la altura debe ser perpendicular a la base, no el lado inclinado). 3. Multiplica base por altura; rotula con unidades cuadradas (por ejemplo, cm², pulg²)

Watch outCuidado

The most common mistake in Area of Parallelograms is multiplying the base by the slanted side instead of the perpendicular height. For a parallelogram with a base of 16 ft, a perpendicular height of 9 ft, and a slanted side of 11 ft, students compute 16 × 11 = 176 square feet instead of the correct 16 × 9 = 144 square feet. The height must be the straight-up (perpendicular) distance between the base and its opposite side, not the length of the slanted side, even though the slanted side is often the number that 'looks' like it belongs in the formula.

Words to usePalabras para usar
  • Area of ParallelogramsÁrea de paralelogramos

    The space inside a parallelogram. Formula: A = b × h, using the perpendicular height.El espacio dentro de un paralelogramo. Fórmula: A = b × h, usando la altura perpendicular.

    A = b × h; if b = 10 cm and h = 6 cm, A = 60 cm².

  • Slanted sideLado inclinado

    A side that leans instead of going straight up and down. It is not the perpendicular height.Un lado que está inclinado en vez de subir y bajar verticalmente. No es la altura perpendicular.

    In a leaning parallelogram, use the dashed 90° height—not the slanted side—in A = b × h.

  • ParallelogramParalelogramo

    A four-sided shape with two pairs of parallel sides.Una figura de cuatro lados con dos pares de lados paralelos.

    Think of a leaning door — the top and bottom are parallel, and the two sides are parallel, like a slanted rectangle

  • ParallelParalelas

    Two lines or sides that stay the same distance apart and never meet.Dos líneas o lados que se mantienen a la misma distancia y nunca se cruzan.

    The top and bottom of a parallelogram never get closer together — matching arrowheads (›) mark one parallel pair, double arrowheads (››) mark the other.

  • BaseBase

    Any side of a parallelogram can be the base. Once you pick a base, the height must be measured perpendicular (at a 90° angle) to it.Cualquier lado de un paralelogramo puede ser la base. Una vez que eliges la base, la altura debe medirse perpendicular (en un ángulo de 90°) a ella.

    Any side can be the base: choose the 10 cm side and b = 10 cm, then draw the height at 90° to that side in A = b × h

  • HeightAltura

    The straight-up distance from the base to the top.La distancia recta hacia arriba desde la base hasta la parte de arriba.

    A dashed vertical line from the top side straight down to the base, forming a 90° angle — NOT the slanted side

  • PerpendicularPerpendicular

    Two lines that meet to make a square corner (90 degrees).Dos líneas que se cruzan formando una esquina cuadrada (90 grados).

    The dashed height meets the base at a square corner — the small 90° box is what makes it perpendicular, not slanted.

  • AreaÁrea

    How much space is inside a flat shape.Cuánto espacio hay dentro de una figura plana.

    A 3 cm × 4 cm rectangle covers 12 square centimeters — imagine 12 tiny 1×1 squares inside it

Sentence frameMarco de oración

The area of the parallelogram equals base x height = ___ x ___ = ___ square feet, which is the same as a ___ with the same base and height.El área del paralelogramo es igual a base x altura = ___ x ___ = ___ pies cuadrados, que es lo mismo que un ___ con la misma base y altura.
