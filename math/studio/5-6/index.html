Key ideaIdea clave

Representing 3D Solids with Nets.

  1. A net is a 2D flat pattern that folds into a 3D solid without overlapping.
  2. Rectangular prism net has 6 rectangular faces in 3 congruent opposite pairs.
  3. Surface Area is the total sum of the areas of all flat faces on the net

Representar sólidos en 3D con plantillas. 1. Una plantilla es un patrón plano en 2D que se dobla para formar un sólido en 3D sin traslaparse. 2. La plantilla de un prisma rectangular tiene 6 caras rectangulares en 3 pares opuestos congruentes. 3. El área de superficie es la suma total de las áreas de todas las caras planas de la plantilla

Watch out for a common mistakeCuidado con un error común

A common mistake in Surface Area Using Nets is finding the area of only one face from each of the 3 matching pairs and forgetting to double it, instead of counting all 6 faces. For a box that is 9 in × 4 in × 5 in, a student might add just one face from each pair: (9×4) + (9×5) + (4×5) = 36 + 45 + 20 = 101 in², but the net actually has two of each face, so the correct total is 2(36) + 2(45) + 2(20) = 72 + 90 + 40 = 202 in² — exactly double. Before you submit, ask: 'Did I count both matching faces in each pair, or only one from each pair?'
