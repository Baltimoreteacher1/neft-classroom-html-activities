  1. Clue: 'A total divided equally among 4 people gave each $85.'Pista: «Un total repartido por igual entre 4 personas le dio $85 a cada una.»

  3. Divided among 4 to get 85 means t ÷ 4 = 85, so t = 85 × 4 = 340.Repartido entre 4 para obtener 85 significa t ÷ 4 = 85, así que t = 85 × 4 = 340.
  4. Check reasonableness: $340 split 4 ways is $85 each. That makes sense!Comprueba que sea razonable: $340 repartidos entre 4 son $85 para cada uno. ¡Tiene sentido!

Key ideaIdea clave

Modeling Real-World Scenarios with Equations & Inequalities.

  1. Define the variable and units in the scenario.
  2. Look for keywords: 'equals/is' (=) vs 'at least/limit' (≥, ≤, >, <).
  3. Write the algebraic model, solve with inverse operations, and check in context

Modelar situaciones reales con ecuaciones y desigualdades. 1. Define la variable y las unidades de la situación. 2. Busca palabras clave: «es igual a / es» (=) frente a «al menos / límite» (≥, ≤, >, <). 3. Escribe el modelo algebraico, resuélvelo con operaciones inversas y compruébalo en el contexto

Watch out for a common mistakeCuidado con un error común

A common mistake in Equations and Inequalities Problem Solving is matching the wrong model to a key phrase. For 'the getaway car held no more than 6 bags,' students often write the equation b = 6 instead of the inequality b ≤ 6, because they see one number and assume it must be exact. The same mistake happens in reverse: for 'the reward split equally among 5 people gave each $40,' students sometimes write the inequality 5p ≥ 40 instead of the equation 5p = 40, because 'split equally' sounds like it could mean a range. Before you submit, ask: does this phrase give one exact value (equation, =) or a limit/range (inequality, <, >, ≤, ≥)?

Sentence frameMarco de oración

I use an equation when the problem says ___, but I use an inequality when the problem says ___ because ___.Uso una ecuación cuando el problema dice ___, pero uso una desigualdad cuando el problema dice ___ porque ___.

Real-world connectionConexión con el mundo real

A detective has a $500 budget for equipment. She has already spent $320. The inequality 320 + r ≤ 500 represents the remaining amount r she can spend. She also needs exactly 4 flashlights at $15 each, represented by the equation 4f = 60.Una detective tiene un presupuesto de $500 para equipo. Ya gastó $320. La desigualdad 320 + r ≤ 500 representa la cantidad restante r que puede gastar. También necesita exactamente 4 linternas a $15 cada una, representado por la ecuación 4f = 60.
