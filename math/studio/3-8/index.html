Key ideaIdea clave

Solving Problems with Unit Rates. Formula: Unit Price = Cost ÷ Quantity | Total Cost = Unit Price × Quantity.

  1. Find the unit rate by dividing total cost by quantity.
  2. Compare unit rates to determine the best deal.
  3. Multiply unit rate by desired quantity to calculate total cost

Resolver problemas con tasas unitarias. Fórmula: precio unitario = costo ÷ cantidad | costo total = precio unitario × cantidad. 1. Halla la tasa unitaria dividiendo el costo total entre la cantidad. 2. Compara las tasas unitarias para determinar la mejor oferta. 3. Multiplica la tasa unitaria por la cantidad deseada para calcular el costo total

Watch out for a common mistakeCuidado con un error común

A common mistake in Solve Problems with Unit Rates is comparing the total prices instead of the cost per item — the deal with the smaller total can still cost more per unit. Always reduce each option to its unit rate before you decide, then pick the lower one.
