Equivalent forms name the same value in different ways. Percent means "out of 100," so a fraction is already a percent once its denominator is 100.Las formas equivalentes nombran el mismo valor de maneras distintas. Key ideaIdea clave

Fraction, Decimal & Percent Equivalence.

  1. Percent to Decimal: Divide by 100 (move decimal point 2 places left).
  2. Decimal to Percent: Multiply by 100 (move decimal point 2 places right).
  3. Fraction to Percent: Divide numerator by denominator, then convert decimal to percent

Equivalencia entre fracción, decimal y porcentaje. 1. De porcentaje a decimal: divide entre 100 (mueve el punto decimal 2 lugares a la izquierda). 2. De decimal a porcentaje: multiplica por 100 (mueve el punto decimal 2 lugares a la derecha). 3. De fracción a porcentaje: divide el numerador entre el denominador y luego convierte el decimal en porcentaje

Watch out for a common mistakeCuidado con un error común

A common mistake in Relate Fractions, Decimals, and Percents is treating the numerator and denominator as the percent's digits — turning 3/5 into "35%" by just reading the 3 and the 5. Percent means out of 100, so the fix is to build an equivalent fraction with denominator 100: 5 × 20 = 100, so multiply the top by 20 too — 3/5 = 60/100 = 60%. A second version of this mistake is scaling only ONE part of the fraction (writing 3/5 = 3/100 or 60/5). Whatever you multiply the denominator by, you must multiply the numerator by the same number. When a denominator will not reach 100 evenly (8, 3, 7), divide the numerator by the denominator instead and then multiply by 100.

Sentence frameMarco de oración

I multiplied the top and bottom of ___ by ___ to get ___/100, so the percent is ___%.Multipliqué el numerador y el denominador de ___ por ___ para obtener ___/100, así que el porcentaje es ___%.
