Stretch: What Does the Remainder Mean?

I can solve multi-step division problems and interpret the remainder in context.

🧠 The Challenge

dividenddivisorquotientremainderinterpret

The same division can need different answers: sometimes you round up (need enough buses), sometimes you drop the remainder (full boxes only).

👀 Worked Example

100 students, 8 per van. 100 ÷ 8 = 12 R4. You need 13 vans (round up — the last 4 still need a ride).
