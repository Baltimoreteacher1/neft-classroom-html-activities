Key Words
Learn these 4 math words before you begin
🎁
GCF — Greatest Common Factor
The biggest number that divides evenly into two numbers.
GCF of 12 and 18 = 6
⏱️
LCM — Least Common Multiple
The smallest number that both numbers fit into evenly.
LCM of 4 and 6 = 12
💵
Decimal Operations
Adding or subtracting money amounts — line up the decimal point first.
$12.50 + $7.25 = $19.75
➗
Division
Splitting a total into equal groups to find cost per person.
$240 ÷ 8 guests = $30 each

Step 1 — Build the Goodie Bags
Use GCF to make equal bags with no leftovers
Your job: You have stickers and granola bars to pack into equal goodie bags. Enter your numbers, then find the GCF to see how many equal bags you can make.
🟢 Level 1 tip: Try friendly numbers that share factors, like 24 stickers and 36 granola bars.
LEVEL 2 CHALLENGE
Use your own numbers — they don't have to be round or easy!
Need a hint?

List all the numbers that divide evenly into each count. The GCF is the biggest number on both lists.
Example: Factors of 12: 1,2,3,4,6,12. Factors of 18: 1,2,3,6,9,18. Biggest match = 6 bags.

LEVEL 2 EXTENSION — Distributive Property (6.NS.4)

Rewrite your two counts using the GCF. Example: 24 + 36 = 12 × (2 + 3)

⏱️
Step 2 — Sync the Entertainment
Use LCM to find when both events happen at the same time
Your job: The music plays every few minutes and the bubble machine runs every few minutes. Find the LCM to discover when both happen at the exact same moment!
🟢 Level 1 tip: Try numbers like 4 and 6 — their LCM is 12.
Need a hint?

List multiples of each number until they share one.
Music (4): 4, 8, 12… Bubbles (6): 6, 12… LCM = 12 minutes.

💵
Step 3 — Track Your Budget
Add up costs, then divide to find cost per guest
Your job: Enter what you'll spend on food, decorations, and activities. Add them up, then divide by the number of guests to find the cost per person.
🟢 Level 1 tip: Try round numbers like $80 food, $40 decorations, $30 activities, 25 guests.
