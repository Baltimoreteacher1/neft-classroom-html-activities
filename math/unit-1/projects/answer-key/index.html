Improper fractions simplified cleanly.

4 Step 5 & 6 — Error Repair & Sample Defense Pitch

Error Repair Explanation

Diagnosed Error: The volunteer multiplied by 3/4 (getting 9) instead of dividing. Dividing asks how many 3/4-meter pieces fit inside 12 meters. Because each piece is shorter than 1 meter, more than 12 pieces must fit: 12 × (4/3) = 16 banners.

Sample Student Defense Pitch

"For our neighborhood block party, I planned for 738 guests. Because each table pavilion holds 24 people, 738 ÷ 24 gives 30 full tables and 18 leftover guests. Those 18 neighbors must have seats, so I ordered 31 tables total. For refreshments, our $58.50 budget divided by $2.25 per pack yields 5850 ÷ 225 = 26 dispenser packs. Finally, dividing 12 meters of banner roll by 3/4 meter gave 16 banners by multiplying by the reciprocal 4/3, and 7 1/2 pounds of snack mix yielded exactly 6 family bowls."
