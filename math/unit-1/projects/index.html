For the Teacher

How to Use These Projects

Standards: 6.NOS.1 (divide fractions & mixed numbers), 6.NOS.2 (multi-digit division with remainders), 6.NOS.3 (decimal division algorithms).

Aligned to Lessons: 2.6 (multi-digit division algorithm), 2.7 (divide decimals algorithm), 6.1 (whole number & fraction division), 6.2 (fractions & mixed numbers division).

Versions A and B are parallel. They assess the same skills through different contexts, so you can assign Version A and Version B to different students and grade on the same rubric (built into each project). Great for choice boards, make-up work, or A/B class sections. Version C is a shorter extension expedition — same four skills under mission constraints, with its own rubric; use it for early finishers or as a second pass, not as a replacement for A or B.

Built in: a Self → Peer → World arc (own real data → partner interview → real-world comparison), Visual Math Notes vocab with an EN/ES toggle, Formula Bank and Sentence Frames, live oninput calculators with instant feedback, Level 1 (support) / Level 2 (challenge) toggle, save/load, a report with Copy and .txt export, a progress bar, hints, a written deliverable, a checklist, a rubric, and print.
