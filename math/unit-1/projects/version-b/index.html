Key Words
Learn these 4 math words before you start building
🧰
GCF — Greatest Common Factor
The biggest number that divides evenly into two numbers.
GCF of 18 and 12 = 6 equal tool kits
🔋
LCM — Least Common Multiple
The smallest number that both numbers fit into evenly.
LCM of 3 and 4 = 12 hrs until both robots sync
💰
Decimal Operations
Adding up parts costs — always line up the decimal point first.
$34.75 + $12.50 = $47.25
🤖
Division
Splitting a total budget into equal shares across robots.
$360 / 4 robots = $90 per robot

Step 1 — Build Equal Tool Kits
Use GCF to split cables and sensors into equal kits with no leftovers
Your job: You have cables and sensors to pack into equal tool kits. Enter your counts, then find the GCF to see how many equal kits you can make.
🟢 Level 1 tip: Try friendly numbers that share factors, like 18 cables and 24 sensors.
LEVEL 2 CHALLENGE
Use your own numbers — they do not have to be round or easy!
Need a hint?

List all the numbers that divide evenly into each count. The GCF is the biggest number on both lists.
Example: Factors of 18: 1,2,3,6,9,18. Factors of 24: 1,2,3,4,6,8,12,24. Biggest match = 6 kits.

LEVEL 2 EXTENSION — Distributive Property (6.NS.4)

Rewrite your two counts using the GCF. Example: 18 + 24 = 6 x (3 + 4)

🔋
Step 2 — Sync the Charging Schedule
Use LCM to find when both robots finish charging at the same time
Your job: Two robots charge on different schedules. Find the LCM to discover the first hour when both robots are fully charged at the same time!
🟢 Level 1 tip: Try numbers like 3 and 4 — their LCM is 12.
Need a hint?

List multiples of each number until they share one.
Robot A (3): 3, 6, 9, 12 ... Robot B (4): 4, 8, 12 ... LCM = 12 hours.

💰
Step 3 — Track the Parts Budget
Add up parts costs, then divide by number of robots to find cost per robot
Your job: Enter what you will spend on motors, sensors, and frames. Add them up, then divide by the number of robots to find the cost per robot.
🟢 Level 1 tip: Try round numbers like $60 motors, $35 sensors, $25 frames, 4 robots.
