Stretch: Factor Out the Greatest Common Factor

I can use the GCF and the distributive property to rewrite a sum like 36 + 24 as 12 × (3 + 2).

🧠 The Big Idea

Every sum of two whole numbers can be rewritten as GCF × (sum of what's left). This is the distributive property running backward — called factoring.

Example: 36 + 24. GCF(36, 24) = 12. 36 ÷ 12 = 3 and 24 ÷ 12 = 2, so 36 + 24 = 12 × (3 + 2). Check: 12 × 5 = 60 ✓.
