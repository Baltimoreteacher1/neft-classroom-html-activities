GCF & LCM: Biggest Shared Factor, Smallest Shared Multiple

I can find the Greatest Common Factor and Least Common Multiple of two numbers.

📘 Word Bank + Sentence Frames

factor (divides evenly)multiple (counting by)GCFLCMcommon = shared

  • GCF = the biggest factor both numbers share.
  • LCM = the smallest multiple both numbers share.
  • Frame: "The GCF of ____ and ____ is ____ because it is the biggest number in both factor lists."

👀 See It: A Worked Example

Find the GCF of 12 and 18. List the factors of each:

12 → 1, 2, 3, 6, 12
18 → 1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 18

Both lists share 1, 2, 3, and 6. The biggest shared factor is 6, so GCF(12, 18) = 6.
