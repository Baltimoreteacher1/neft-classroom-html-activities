Stretch: Decimals in Real Money Problems

I can solve multi-step problems that add, subtract, multiply, and divide decimals.

🧠 The Challenge

Real problems combine operations. Track the decimal point at every step and ask: "Does my answer make sense?"

Worked example: 3 notebooks at $2.25 each, paid with $10. Cost = 3 × 2.25 = $6.75. Change = 10.00 − 6.75 = $3.25.
