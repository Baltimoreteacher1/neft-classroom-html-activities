Stretch: Converting Measurement Units with Ratios

I can use ratio reasoning and unit rates to convert measurements (feet↔inches, hours↔minutes, cm↔m).

🧠 The Challenge

unit rateconversion factorequivalent ratioscale

A conversion factor is a ratio equal to 1, like 12 in / 1 ft. Multiply by it to change units. Bigger unit → smaller unit means you multiply; smaller → bigger means you divide.

👀 Worked Example

Convert 5 ft to inches: 1 ft = 12 in, so 5 ft × (12 in / 1 ft) = 60 in. Convert 90 min to hours: 90 ÷ 60 = 1.5 h.
