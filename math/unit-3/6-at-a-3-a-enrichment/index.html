Stretch: Missing Values and Points on a Line

I can fill missing values in a ratio table and recognize that the points lie on a straight line through the origin.

🧠 The Challenge

equivalent ratioratio tablecoordinate planeline through origin

Equivalent ratios plotted as (x, y) points always fall on a straight line through the origin. The unit rate is the steepness.

👀 Worked Example

x (cups mix)1234
y (cups water)36912

Points (1,3), (2,6), (3,9)… all lie on one line through (0,0). The rate is 3 water per 1 mix.
