Building a Table of Equivalent Ratios

I can make a table of equivalent ratios and see the pattern.

📘 Word Bank + Sentence Frames

equivalent ratioratio tablescale bypattern

  • A ratio table lists equivalent ratios. Each row multiplies both numbers by the same factor.
  • Frame: "Each cup of mix needs ____ cups of water, so ____ cups of mix need ____ cups of water."

👀 See It

Cups of mix1234
Cups of water36912

The ratio stays 1 : 3 in every column. Each "water" is 3 times the "mix".
