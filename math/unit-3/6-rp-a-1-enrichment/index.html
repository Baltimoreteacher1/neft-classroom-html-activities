Stretch: Scaling Ratios Up and Down

I can reason with equivalent ratios and use part-part-whole thinking to solve problems.

🧠 The Challenge

Equivalent ratios are made by multiplying (or dividing) both parts by the same number. With part-part-whole, the two parts add to the whole.

Example: Paint mixes red : blue = 2 : 3. To make 15 total parts, scale by 3 (since 2 + 3 = 5, and 5 × 3 = 15): that's 6 red and 9 blue.
