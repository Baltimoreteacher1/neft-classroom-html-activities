What Is a Ratio? Comparing Two Amounts

I can describe a ratio relationship between two quantities in words and in numbers.

📘 Word Bank + Sentence Frames

ratiofor everyto (a : b)comparequantity

A ratio compares two amounts.
  • We write it as a : b ("a to b").
  • Frame: "For every ____ ____, there are ____ ____."

👀 See It: A Worked Example

A fruit bowl has these pieces:

🍎🍎🍎 🍌🍌

There are 3 apples and 2 bananas. The ratio of apples to bananas is 3 : 2. In words: "For every 3 apples, there are 2 bananas."
