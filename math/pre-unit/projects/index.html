For the Teacher

How to Use This Project

Where it goes: after Lesson 6-2, the last instructional lesson in the validated Pre-Unit sequence (1-1 → 2-6 → 2-7 → 6-1 → 6-2 → this project). It uses the Pre-Unit's existing additional day; no later unit date moves.

Standards: 6.NOS.2 (divide multi-digit whole numbers and interpret the remainder), 6.NOS.3 (divide with decimals using equivalent division), 6.NOS.1 (divide with fractions and mixed numbers). The explaining and defending strand comes from Lesson 1-1.

Nothing untaught appears. There is no ratio, percent, expression or equation work anywhere in the project — the difficulty comes from integrating the five Pre-Unit lessons, not from new content.

Three levels, as in every culminating project. Level 0 supplies small numbers, a worked example and a one-step-at-a-time script; Level 1 supplies friendly numbers and a strategy tip; Level 2 empties the fields, so the student chooses numbers that satisfy the constraints and then argues for a reading of their own quotient.

Built in: a step trail with progress, a no-giveaway hint ladder that names the misconception, save and resume across days, partner comparison that stays locked until the student has earned their own answer, a 4-point rubric and a closing reflection.
