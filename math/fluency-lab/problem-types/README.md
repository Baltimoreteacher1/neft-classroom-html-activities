Cells show the levels (1-5) at which a type is generated; n/a means the type is not generated for that skill.

- **missing-number** is n/a when the skill has no relation with a term that can be hidden without making the key ambiguous (counting and recognising, classification, measurement reading, data and picture-first skills).
- **number-line** is n/a when no quantity in the skill sits naturally on a line, or the numbers are too large or too many to mark honestly (categories, shapes, statistics tables, symbolic algebra, large-number arithmetic).
- **visual-model** is n/a when no graphic kind in the registry shows the skill's idea honestly, or the picture would state the answer (symbolic manipulation, procedure-only skills, large-number arithmetic beyond one block picture).
- **real-world** is n/a when the skill is a pure fact or notation skill with no authentic context at its grade (counting words, symbol comparison, basic fact recall).
- **word-problem** is n/a when the skill has no equal-groups or join/separate story structure story-problems.js can wrap, or the numbers exceed what the stories handle.
- **multi-step** is n/a when the skill is a single mathematical idea with no natural second step at its grade, or a second step would be a different skill.
- **equation** is n/a when the skill has no situation that is modelled by choosing between equations.
