Privacy and accessibility

Lab progress and learner settings are saved on this device. You can export a backup from the Progress area. Keep the file private.

The site currently runs page-usage and Web Vitals scripts to measure aggregate page use and performance. Cloudflare may also serve its site analytics script. These scripts are under review; no learner name is required to use the Lab.

Practice has no countdown. Comfort settings offer larger text, a reading font, reduced motion, and read-aloud when the browser supports it. Keyboard access and screen-reader behavior are being reviewed.
