Mission

Fraction bar

Press the arrow keys or drag on the bar to move one piece at a time. Shift moves a whole.

Number line

Press the arrow keys or touch the line to move the marker to the nearest mark.

Decimal grid

Each square is one hundredth. Arrow keys move one hundredth; Shift moves a tenth, one whole column.

Percent bar

The dashed mark is 100%, one whole. Arrow keys move 1%; Shift moves 10%.

Equation and statements

Type in any box and press Enter. Everything else updates. You can use 3/4, 1 3/4, 0.75, 0.(3) for a repeating decimal, or 75%.
