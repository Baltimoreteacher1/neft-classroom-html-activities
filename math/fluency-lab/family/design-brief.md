# Family Math Tonight visual direction

**Surface and audience:** This is a family landing and activity launcher for a shared household device. Its first job is to help a caregiver and learner move quickly into a real saved practice flow; it should feel calm, capable, and welcoming across elementary and middle-school ages.

**Visual thesis:** Use a quiet, editorial family surface: warm paper white, navy for the starting action, and teal, amber, and coral signals to distinguish choices. Use the site's self-hosted Fraunces display face with Atkinson Hyperlegible body copy to pair warmth with readable learner-facing text. The orbit illustration carries mathematical symbols without turning the page into a grade-school cartoon.

**Content plan:** The page moves from one clear nightly action to five quick routes, then grade choice, evidence-based skill practice, a flexible weekly rotation, and a caregiver coaching prompt. Progress and privacy notes close the page without taking focus away from practice.

**Interaction thesis:** Grade and learner choices update the real skill recommendation and its launch URL; English/Spanish switching updates the whole family surface; reduced-motion settings remove decorative transitions. Practice work itself remains in the established Fluency Lab engine.

**Tokens:** Navy `#17324D`; teal `#1FA6A2`; warm off-white `#F7F4EC`; amber `#F2C15B`; body text `#24323F`; coral `#D0674F`.
