# Family Math home visual direction

**Surface and audience:** This is a family landing and activity launcher for a shared household device. Its first job is to help a caregiver and learner move quickly into a real saved practice flow; it should feel calm, capable, and welcoming across elementary and middle-school ages.

**Visual thesis:** Use a quiet, editorial family surface: warm paper white, navy for the starting action, and teal, amber, and coral signals to distinguish choices. Use the site's self-hosted Fraunces display face with Atkinson Hyperlegible body copy to pair warmth with readable learner-facing text.

**Content plan:** Header with learner and language, then any saved activity to continue, then a short welcome beside one compact setup card (grade, length, mode) with a single primary Start button. Today's mission follows: each row names the skill as a link, says in one sentence what the learner does, and opens exactly that skill with a "Practice this skill" button. "Try this together" coaches the mission's main activity. Progress is a three-tile summary. Browsing (one searchable, topic-grouped skill list, the ten games, math at home, other ways to practice) comes last and stays out of the main path.

**Interaction thesis:** One primary button at a time: Start, or Continue while an activity is saved. The guided session is a quieter secondary choice because it picks its own skill. Grade, length, mode, learner and language change the mission and every link in it together. Opening a skill directly never overwrites a saved session. Reduced-motion settings remove decorative transitions. Practice work itself remains in the established Fluency Lab engine.

**Tokens:** Navy `#17324D`; teal `#1FA6A2`; warm off-white `#F7F4EC`; amber `#F2C15B`; body text `#24323F`; coral `#D0674F`.
