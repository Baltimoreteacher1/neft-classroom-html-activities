Stage 4 of 5Etapa 4 de 5 · OptionalOpcional

Two-step expressionsExpresiones de dos pasos

Break the phrase into parts: find the first operation, then apply the second.

Divide la frase en partes: halla la primera operación y luego aplica la segunda.

  1. Write an algebraic expression for "triple a number, increased by 2".
    • 3 + n + 2
    • 3n + 2
    • 3(n + 2)
    • 3n − 2
  2. Write an algebraic expression for "7 less than double a number".
    • 7 − 2n
    • 2(n − 7)
    • 2n − 7
    • 2n + 7
  3. Write an algebraic expression for "the sum of a number and 3, times 8".
    • n + 3 × 8
    • 8n + 3
    • 8(n + 3)
    • n + 24
  4. Write an algebraic expression for "the quotient of a number and 5, plus 9".
    • n ÷ 5 + 9
    • 5 ÷ n + 9
    • 5n + 9
    • (n + 9) ÷ 5
  5. Write an algebraic expression for "10 subtracted from the product of 4 and a number".
    • 10 − 4n
    • 4(n − 10)
    • 4n + 10
    • 4n − 10
  6. Write an algebraic expression for "half of the difference between a number and 6".
    • n ÷ 2 − 6
    • (n − 6) ÷ 2
    • 2(n − 6)
    • (n + 6) ÷ 2
  7. Write an algebraic expression for "the sum of a number and 8, divided by 3".
    • n + 8 ÷ 3
    • 3 ÷ (n + 8)
    • (n + 8) ÷ 3
    • 3(n + 8)
