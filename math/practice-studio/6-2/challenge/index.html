Train like a champion.Entrena como un campeón.

It is the day of the big tournament, and the supply cart is full of odd amounts. Check each plan carefully: a wrong flip means too few cups and not enough paint.

Es el día del gran torneo y el carrito de suministros está lleno de cantidades raras. Revisa cada plan con cuidado: un volteo equivocado significa muy pocos vasos y poca pintura.

1. Climb the skill ladder

Each rung is harder than the last, and the numbers change every time.

Cada peldaño es más difícil que el anterior y los números cambian cada vez.

  1. 6 fresh problems with new numbers each time (interactive only).

2. Multi-step missions

Real situations that need more than one step: plan, solve, then check that your answer makes sense.

Situaciones reales que necesitan más de un paso: planea, resuelve y revisa que tu respuesta tenga sentido.

  1. Find the Division Error
  2. Is 3/4 ÷ 1/2 the same as 1/2 ÷ 3/4? Solve both and explain why or why not. What does this tell you about the order in division?
  3. The Home Field grounds crew has 3 1/2 gallons of white paint. Marking one practice field takes 3/4 gallon. How many fields can the crew mark completely?
    • 4 2/3 fields
    • 4 fields
    • 5 fields
    • 2 5/8 fields
  4. A relay course is 2 1/4 miles long. It is split into legs that are each 3/8 mile. How many legs are there?
  5. Home Field fills cups from three sports-drink coolers. Write each amount as an improper fraction, then find how many cups each cooler fills.
  6. A sprinter runs 2/3 of a mile in 1/6 of an hour. At that steady speed, how many miles does the sprinter run in 1 whole hour?
    • 1/9 mile
    • 1/4 mile
    • 5/6 mile
    • 4 miles

3. Build it, move it, test it

Use the tools to build the idea. Then check it.

Usa las herramientas para construir la idea. Luego compruébala.

  1. Match each division to its quotient.
  2. 2 fresh problems with new numbers each time (interactive only).

4. Detective work

Somebody made a mistake. Find the step, then decide who is right.

Alguien cometió un error. Encuentra el paso y decide quién tiene razón.

  1. Tomás divides 7/8 ÷ 3/4. Tap the step where his work first goes wrong.
  2. Aisha finds 1 1/2 ÷ 1/4. Tap the step where her work first goes wrong.
  3. Wen divides 3/5 ÷ 3/10. Tap the step where his work first goes wrong.
  4. Mia says 3/4 ÷ 1/8 is greater than 1, because 1/8 fits into 3/4 many times. Leo says it is less than 1, because dividing makes numbers smaller. Who is right?
    • Leo, because dividing always gives a smaller number
    • Both, because the answer is exactly 1
    • Mia, because 1/8 is much smaller than 3/4
    • Neither, because the divisor is the bigger fraction

5. Boss level

Tell us how sure you are, then solve. Finish with a problem of your own.

Dinos qué tan seguro estás y luego resuelve. Termina con un problema tuyo.

  1. A coach has a 3 1/2-yard rope. She cuts it into jump-rope pieces that are each 7/8 yard long. How many pieces does she get?
  2. Which division has a quotient greater than 1?
    • 3/4 ÷ 1/2
    • 2/5 ÷ 4/5
    • 1/3 ÷ 2/3
    • 1/4 ÷ 5/8
  3. A water tank on the sidelines holds 5/6 of a gallon. Each bottle takes 5/9 of a gallon. How many bottles can the tank fill? Give your answer as a fraction or mixed number.
  4. 9/10 ÷ 3/5 = 3/2. Which multiplication checks this answer?
    • 3/2 × 3/5 = 9/10
    • 9/10 × 3/5 = 27/50
    • 3/2 × 5/3 = 5/2
    • 3/2 × 9/10 = 27/20
  5. A practice session lasts 2 1/4 hours. It is split into drills that each take 1 1/2 hours. How many drills fit? Give your answer as a fraction or mixed number.
  6. Create your own Home Field problem that is solved by dividing one fraction or mixed number by another. Write the division, solve it, and explain the steps, including what you did with any mixed number.
