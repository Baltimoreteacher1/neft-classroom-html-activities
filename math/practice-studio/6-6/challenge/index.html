  4. Game designers claim that 4n and n + 6 give the same score. The table shows what each formula gives for four values of n. Which statement is correct?

4. Detective work

Somebody made a mistake. Find the step, then decide who is right.

Alguien cometió un error. Encuentra el paso y decide quién tiene razón.

  1. Zara simplified 7m + 4 − 3m − 1 and got 4m + 5. Tap the step where her work goes wrong.
  2. Ravi rewrote 5(2n + 3) as 10n + 3. Tap the step where his work goes wrong.
  3. Lena wants to know whether 7n + 4 and 3n + 4 are equivalent. She tested one easy number. Tap the step where her reasoning goes wrong.
  4. Dara says 4n + 3n = 7n². Emil says 4n + 3n = 7n. Who is right?
    • Emil is right: adding like terms adds the coefficients and keeps the variable the same.
    • Dara is right: when you combine like terms, the powers add up too.
    • Both are right: 7n and 7n² are two ways to write the same value.
    • Neither is right: 4n and 3n are not like terms, so they cannot be combined.
