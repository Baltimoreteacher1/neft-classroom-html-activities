    • How many points are scored for a free throw?

    • How tall is each player on the basketball team?
  3. Why is 'How many hours of sleep did each student get last night?' a statistical question?

4. Stretch your thinking

  1. Find the Reasoning Error
  2. Write one statistical question and one non-statistical question about your school's cafeteria. Explain why each question is or is not statistical, and describe what the data would look like for the statistical question.
  3. A survey asks 30 students how many books they read last month, collecting 30 answers. What makes this a statistical question?
    • It is about books
    • Everyone reads the same number
    • The answers vary from student to student
    • It has exactly one correct answer
  4. Which rewrite turns 'What is my resting heart rate?' into a statistical question?
    • Is my heart rate high?
    • What is a heart rate?
    • What are the resting heart rates of students in my class?
    • What is the teacher's heart rate?
