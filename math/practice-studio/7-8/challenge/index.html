Power-down drill in the Robot Lab: the lights flicker and every robot forgets its map. Rebuild each route from clues about signs, moves and distances from the axes, and send every bot back to the right quadrant.

Simulacro de apagón en el Laboratorio de Robots: las luces parpadean y cada robot olvida su mapa. Reconstruye cada ruta con pistas sobre signos, movimientos y distancias a los ejes, y manda a cada robot de regreso al cuadrante correcto.

1. Climb the skill ladder

Each rung is harder than the last, and the numbers change every time.

Cada peldaño es más difícil que el anterior y los números cambian cada vez.

  1. 6 fresh problems with new numbers each time (interactive only).

2. Multi-step missions

Real situations that need more than one step: plan, solve, then check that your answer makes sense.

Situaciones reales que necesitan más de un paso: planea, resuelve y revisa que tu respuesta tenga sentido.

  1. Find the Quadrant Error
  2. A point starts in Quadrant I at (4, 6). Describe a move that puts it in Quadrant III. What must happen to both coordinates? Can you give the exact new coordinates after your move?
  3. A point (a, b) has a < 0 and b < 0. In which quadrant does it lie?
    • Quadrant I
    • Quadrant II
    • Quadrant IV
    • Quadrant III
  4. A delivery robot starts at the dock, the origin. It rolls 7 units left, then 5 units up, then 3 units right, then 8 units down. In which quadrant does it stop?
    • Quadrant I
    • Quadrant II
    • Quadrant III
    • Quadrant IV
  5. A charging pad is a rectangle centered on the dock (the origin). It is 10 units wide and 6 units tall, and its sides are parallel to the axes. Fill in the coordinates of each corner and its quadrant (I, II, III or IV).
  6. A sensor reports eight objects: A(−6, 4), B(3, −2), C(0, −5), D(−1, −1), E(7, 0), F(2, 6), G(−4, −8) and H(5, −7). How many of them are in Quadrant III or Quadrant IV?
  7. A robot starts at (−5, 3). It moves right and then down. Which pair of moves ends in Quadrant IV?
    • 5 units right, then 3 units down
    • 4 units right, then 2 units down
    • 8 units right, then 2 units down
    • 6 units right, then 4 units down

3. Build it, move it, test it

Use the tools to build the idea. Then check it.

Usa las herramientas para construir la idea. Luego compruébala.

  1. Start at the origin each time. Match each set of moves to the ordered pair where the robot ends.
  2. 2 fresh problems with new numbers each time (interactive only).

4. Detective work

Somebody made a mistake. Find the step, then decide who is right.

Alguien cometió un error. Encuentra el paso y decide quién tiene razón.

  1. Tomás names the quadrant for the point (4, −7).
  2. Mei names the quadrant for the point (−5, 2).
  3. Ravi names the location of the sensor at (0, −6).
  4. Mei plots (−3, 5) by moving 3 units left and 5 units up from the origin. Jamal plots the same pair by moving 5 units left and 3 units up. Who plots it correctly?
    • Jamal: the bigger number is the left/right move.
    • Mei: the first number is the left/right move, so −3 means 3 units left.
    • Both: their points end in the same quadrant.
    • Neither: you must move up before you move left.

5. Boss level

Tell us how sure you are, then solve. Finish with a problem of your own.

Dinos qué tan seguro estás y luego resuelve. Termina con un problema tuyo.

  1. p is a positive number and q is a negative number. In which quadrant is the point (−p, q)?
    • Quadrant I
    • Quadrant II
    • Quadrant III
    • Quadrant IV
  2. A robot starts at (3, −9) and moves straight up 14 units. What is its new y-coordinate?
  3. A point is in Quadrant III. It is 6 units from the x-axis and 4 units from the y-axis. What are its coordinates?
    • (−6, −4)
    • (4, 6)
    • (−4, 6)
    • (−4, −6)
  4. Which set of corners makes a rectangle with one corner in each of the four quadrants?
    • (−5, 4), (5, 4), (5, −2), (−5, −2)
    • (1, 4), (6, 4), (6, 1), (1, 1)
    • (−3, 2), (4, 2), (4, 0), (−3, 0)
    • (2, 3), (6, 3), (6, −3), (2, −3)
  5. Four sensors K, L, M and N are marked on the graph. Which ordered pair names the sensor that is in Quadrant IV?
    • (−4, 5)
    • (5, −4)
    • (4, −5)
    • (5, 4)
  6. Write one ordered pair in each of the four quadrants, using whole numbers from −9 to 9. Then explain how the signs of the coordinates tell you the quadrant, and say what is different about a point on an axis.
