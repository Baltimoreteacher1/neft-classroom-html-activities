Special orders fill the workshop tonight: edges written as mixed numbers, crates to fill with bags of different sizes, and cubes smaller than the box's units. Measure carefully.

Esta noche llegan pedidos especiales al taller: aristas escritas como números mixtos, cajas que hay que llenar con bolsas de distintos tamaños y cubos más pequeños que las unidades de la caja. Mide con cuidado.

2. Multi-step missions

Real situations that need more than one step: plan, solve, then check that your answer makes sense.

Situaciones reales que necesitan más de un paso: planea, resuelve y revisa que tu respuesta tenga sentido.

  1. Find the Volume Error
  2. A toy company ships action figures in boxes that are 4 × 3 × 6 inches. A shipping crate is 24 × 12 × 18 inches. How many action figure boxes fit in the crate? Explain your reasoning step by step.
  3. Fill in the table
  4. A foam block in the Maker Workshop is 3 cm long, 1.5 cm wide and 2 cm tall. It is packed with small cubes that are each 1/2 cm on every edge. How many small cubes fill the block, and what is the block's volume?
    • 9 cubes; 9 cm³
    • 72 cubes; 72 cm³
    • 72 cubes; 9 cm³
    • 36 cubes; 4.5 cm³
  5. A workshop crate is 3 ft long, 1 1/2 ft wide and 2 1/2 ft tall. It will be filled with packing shavings sold in bags of 1/2 ft³. How many bags must the workshop buy to fill the crate completely?
  6. A clay box has a base that is 6 cm long and 2.5 cm wide. It holds exactly 45 cm³ of clay. How tall is the box?
  7. The workshop is comparing three wooden blocks. Fill in the base area and the volume of each block.

4. Detective work

Somebody made a mistake. Find the step, then decide who is right.

Alguien cometió un error. Encuentra el paso y decide quién tiene razón.

  1. Esi finds the volume of a toolbox that is 4 ft long, 3 ft wide and 1.5 ft tall. Tap the step where her work first goes wrong.
  2. Yuki finds the volume of a drawer that is 2 ft long, 1 1/2 ft wide and 3 ft tall. Tap the step where her work first goes wrong.
  3. Amara fills a box that is 2 in long, 1 in wide and 1 in tall with small cubes that are 1/2 in on every edge. She wants the volume in cubic inches. Tap the step where her work first goes wrong.
  4. A box has a base area of 24 in² and a height of 1/2 in. Zoe says its volume must be more than 24 in³, because multiplying makes numbers bigger. Max says the volume is less than 24 in³. Who is right?
    • Zoe, because multiplying always gives a greater number
    • Both, because the volume is 24 in³ either way
    • Neither, because a fractional height has no volume
    • Max, because the box is only half an inch tall, so it holds half of 24

5. Boss level

Tell us how sure you are, then solve. Finish with a problem of your own.

Dinos qué tan seguro estás y luego resuelve. Termina con un problema tuyo.

  1. A toolbox is 3 1/2 ft long, 2 ft wide and 1 1/2 ft tall. What is its volume?
  2. Which expression gives the volume of a box that is 2 1/2 in long, 3 in wide and 4 in tall?
    • 2 × 3 × 4
    • 2.5 + 3 + 4
    • 2.5 × 3 × 4
    • 2.5 × 3
  3. Box A is 5 in long, 2 in wide and 1 1/2 in tall. Box B is 3 in long, 2 1/2 in wide and 2 in tall. Which box holds more?
    • Box A, because it has the longest edge
    • Box B, because it is taller
    • Box A, by 3 in³
    • They hold the same amount, 15 in³
  4. A wooden planter has a base 2 ft long and 1 1/2 ft wide. It holds 6 ft³ of soil. How tall is the planter?
  5. A sandbox is 1 1/2 ft long, 1 1/2 ft wide and 1/2 ft deep. Sand comes in bags of 1/4 ft³. How many bags must be bought to fill the sandbox completely?
  6. Create your own Maker Workshop problem about the volume of a box with at least one fractional edge. Give all three edges, find the volume, and explain how you handled the fraction.
