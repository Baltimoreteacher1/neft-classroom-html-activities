1. Climb the skill ladder

Each rung is harder than the last, and the numbers change every time.

Cada peldaño es más difícil que el anterior y los números cambian cada vez.

  1. 6 fresh problems with new numbers each time (interactive only).

2. Multi-step missions

Real situations that need more than one step: plan, solve, then check that your answer makes sense.

Situaciones reales que necesitan más de un paso: planea, resuelve y revisa que tu respuesta tenga sentido.

  1. Miguel wants the cost of a full year (12 months) at Recreation World. Using c = 24.95m, what is the total?
    • $36.95
    • $249.50
    • $299.40
    • $300.00
  2. A different gym charges $19.95 per month. How does Miguel's equation change for this gym?
    • The variable changes: c = 24.95x instead of c = 24.95m
    • The rate changes: c = 19.95m instead of c = 24.95m
    • The operation changes: c = 19.95 + m
    • Nothing changes — all gyms have the same equation
  3. What is the benefit of an equation over a table for the membership costs?
    • The equation lists more rows than the table
    • The equation is the only representation that shows the cost increasing
    • The table is always wrong and the equation is always right
    • The equation gives the total cost for ANY number of months, not just the rows listed
  4. A supply drone leaves Orbit Station and flies at a steady speed. The table shows how far it has flown. Which equation matches, with m = minutes and d = kilometers flown?
    • d = m + 8
    • d = 9m
    • m = 9d
    • d = 36m
  5. The station's water recycler makes 2.5 liters of clean water each day, so w = 2.5d, where d is the number of days and w is the liters made. A supply pod needs 40 liters before it can launch. How many days must the recycler run?
  6. A solar panel makes energy by the equation e = 18h, where h is the hours of sunlight and e is the energy units made. Fill in each missing number.
  7. An empty cargo pod always adds the same extra mass to whatever it carries. The table shows the cargo and the total pod mass. Which equation matches?
    • m = 4c
    • m = 5c
    • c = m + 4
    • m = c + 4

Use the tools to build the idea. Then check it.

Usa las herramientas para construir la idea. Luego compruébala.

  1. Match each situation to the equation that models it.
  2. 2 fresh problems with new numbers each time (interactive only).

4. Detective work

Somebody made a mistake. Find the step, then decide who is right.

Alguien cometió un error. Encuentra el paso y decide quién tiene razón.

  1. A fuel pump on the station uses 6 liters every minute. Zainab writes an equation, with m = minutes and f = liters of fuel used.
  2. The station uses 3 oxygen canisters every day. Luca writes an equation, with d = days and c = canisters used.
  3. A table shows a solar panel: 2 hours, 30 energy units; 4 hours, 60 units. Imani finds the rate, with h = hours and e = energy units.
  4. A table pairs x and y like this: (1, 3), (2, 6), (3, 9), (4, 12). Lena says y = x + 2, because 1 + 2 = 3. Omar says y = 3x. Who is right?
    • Omar: y = 3x works in every row.
    • Lena: y = x + 2 matches the first row.
    • Both: both equations give 3 when x = 1.
    • Neither: the equation should be y = 12x.

5. Boss level

Tell us how sure you are, then solve. Finish with a problem of your own.

Dinos qué tan seguro estás y luego resuelve. Termina con un problema tuyo.

  1. A rover drives 15 meters each minute. Which choice defines the variables correctly?
    • Let m = minutes and d = 15 meters.
    • Let m = a number and d = another number.
    • Let m = minutes and d = total meters driven.
    • Let r = the rover and m = minutes.
  2. A crew meal costs $3.25 per person, so c = 3.25n, where n is the number of people and c is the cost in dollars. What is c when n is 12?
  3. The table shows how y depends on x. Which equation matches?
    • y = x + 5
    • y = 3.5x
    • y = x + 7
    • y = 5x
  4. A drone's battery charges by p = 12h, where h is the hours charging and p is the percent of charge. For how many hours must it charge to reach 96 percent?
  5. Which situation matches the equation y = x + 6?
    • A pod's mass is 6 times its cargo.
    • A pod's mass is always 6 tonnes more than its cargo.
    • A pod's cargo is 6 tonnes more than its mass.
    • A pod costs $6 for each tonne of cargo.
  6. Write a situation about life on a space station that has a steady rate. Define the variables, write an equation, and use it to find one value. Explain what each part of your equation means using the words variable and rate.
