Turno de noche en el puerto: llega la niebla y el equipo del faro necesita nuevas velas de señales triangulares al amanecer. Halla cada área con cuidado, porque un corte equivocado desperdicia tela que no se puede reponer.

4. Detective work

Somebody made a mistake. Find the step, then decide who is right.

Alguien cometió un error. Encuentra el paso y decide quién tiene razón.

  1. A harbor sail has a base of 16 ft and a height of 9 ft. Yara finds its area. Tap the first step where her work goes wrong.
  2. A sail is a right triangle. Its base is 8 m, its height is 6 m, and its sloping edge is 10 m. Teo finds its area. Tap the first step where his work goes wrong.
  3. A triangular tarp has an area of 30 ft² and a base of 10 ft. Mina finds its height. Tap the first step where her work goes wrong.
  4. A right triangle has legs of 6 m and 8 m. Sana says its area changes depending on which side you pick as the base. Joon says the area stays the same if you use the matching height. Who is right?
    • Joon. With base 8 and height 6, or base 6 and height 8, the area is 24 m².
    • Sana. A different base gives a different area.
    • Neither. Only the longest side can be the base.
    • Both. The area is 24 m² with one base and 48 m² with the other.
