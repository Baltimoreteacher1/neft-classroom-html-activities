Night shift at the port: the tide table, the crane log and the sonar readings have all been mixed together. Sort out which side of 0 each reading belongs to before the morning ships arrive.

Turno de noche en el puerto: la tabla de mareas, el registro de la grúa y las lecturas del sónar se han mezclado. Averigua de qué lado del 0 va cada lectura antes de que lleguen los barcos de la mañana.

1. Climb the skill ladder

Each rung is harder than the last, and the numbers change every time.

Cada peldaño es más difícil que el anterior y los números cambian cada vez.

  1. 6 fresh problems with new numbers each time (interactive only).

2. Multi-step missions

Real situations that need more than one step: plan, solve, then check that your answer makes sense.

Situaciones reales que necesitan más de un paso: planea, resuelve y revisa que tu respuesta tenga sentido.

  1. At 6 a.m. in Reykjavik the temperature was −12°C. At 2 p.m. it was the opposite of the 6 a.m. temperature, and at 11 p.m. it was the opposite of the 2 p.m. temperature. What were the temperatures at 2 p.m. and 11 p.m.?
    • −12°C and 12°C
    • 0°C and 12°C
    • 12°C and −12°C
    • 12°C and 24°C
  2. A football team starts at the line of scrimmage, which is 0. During one series they had a 10-yard loss, a 4-yard gain, a 3-yard loss, and a 21-yard gain. Where does the series leave them?
    • 8 yards past the line of scrimmage
    • 12 yards past the line of scrimmage
    • 18 yards past the line of scrimmage
    • 38 yards past the line of scrimmage
  3. Describe two different real-world situations that use negative integers. For each one, tell exactly what 0 means in that situation, write one negative integer it could produce, and explain what the opposite of that integer would mean in the same situation.
  4. A tide gauge reads −8 at low tide. At high tide the reading is the opposite of the low-tide reading. Six hours later it is the opposite of the high-tide reading, and then it flips once more to the opposite of that. What is the final reading?
  5. The dock is level 0. Write an integer for each report, then write its opposite. Fill in every empty cell.
  6. The harbor chart sets 0 at the water surface. The number line shows a diver at −12 meters and a crane hook at 12 meters. Which statement is true?
    • They are equal, because both are 12 meters from the surface.
    • They are opposites: the same distance from the surface, on different sides.
    • Both are above the surface, because 12 is in both numbers.
    • The diver is higher, because 12 is greater than −12 in distance.
  7. A crane lifts a container to 8 meters above the dock, shown on the number line. Then it lowers the container to the opposite position. How many meters does the container travel in the second move?

3. Build it, move it, test it

Use the tools to build the idea. Then check it.

Usa las herramientas para construir la idea. Luego compruébala.

  1. Harbor logs use 0 for the starting level. Sort each report by the kind of integer it needs.
  2. 2 fresh problems with new numbers each time (interactive only).

4. Detective work

Somebody made a mistake. Find the step, then decide who is right.

Alguien cometió un error. Encuentra el paso y decide quién tiene razón.

  1. A diver is 9 meters below the water surface, which is 0. Rosa wrote the integer for the diver. Find the step where her work goes wrong.
  2. Hugo wants the opposite of the opposite of −4. Find the step where his reasoning goes wrong.
  3. Mara places the opposite of −3 on a number line. Find the step where her thinking goes wrong.
  4. Rin says, "The opposite of any number is a positive number." Leo says, "A number and its opposite are the same distance from 0." Sol says, "The opposite of 0 is 0." Who is right?
    • Rin and Leo
    • Rin only
    • Leo and Sol
    • All three

5. Boss level

Tell us how sure you are, then solve. Finish with a problem of your own.

Dinos qué tan seguro estás y luego resuelve. Termina con un problema tuyo.

  1. A freighter's anchor rests 23 meters below the water surface, which is 0. Which integer shows the anchor's position?
    • −23
    • 23
    • −32
    • 0
  2. What is the opposite of −48?
    • −48
    • 48
    • −84
    • 0
  3. How many units apart are 15 and its opposite on a number line?
  4. Which point on the number line is the opposite of point B?
    • Point A
    • Point B
    • Point C
    • Point D
  5. Dock level is 0. A tide gauge reads −4 at low tide, and the high-tide reading is its opposite. What does the high-tide reading show?
    • The water is 4 meters below dock level
    • The water is exactly at dock level
    • The water is 8 meters above dock level
    • The water is 4 meters above dock level
  6. Create your own harbor problem. Choose a situation with a starting level of 0. Write one negative integer and one positive integer from it, say what each one means, and give the opposite of each. Explain what 0 means in your situation.
