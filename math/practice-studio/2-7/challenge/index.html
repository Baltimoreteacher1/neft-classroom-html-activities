Each rung is harder than the last, and the numbers change every time.

  1. 6 fresh problems with new numbers each time (interactive only).

2. Multi-step missions

Real situations that need more than one step: plan, solve, then check that your answer makes sense.

Situaciones reales que necesitan más de un paso: planea, resuelve y revisa que tu respuesta tenga sentido.

  1. Find Derek's Mistake
  2. A gas station sells fuel at $3.45 per gallon. A customer pays $27.60 total. How many gallons did the customer buy? Set up the decimal division, show how to convert it to whole-number division, and solve.
  3. A robot welder has 9.6 meters of wire. Each weld uses 0.12 meter. How many welds can it make?
  4. A robot’s battery holds 14.7 kilowatt-hours. The robot uses 0.6 kilowatt-hour each hour. For how many hours can it run?
  5. Which division has the same quotient as 4.5 ÷ 0.15?
    • 45 ÷ 15
    • 450 ÷ 15
    • 4.5 ÷ 15
    • 450 ÷ 1.5
  6. A lab pours 2.4 liters of cleaning fluid into bottles that hold 0.08 liter each. About how many bottles can it fill?
    • 30 bottles
    • 3 bottles
    • 300 bottles
    • 0.3 bottles

3. Build it, move it, test it

Use the tools to build the idea. Then check it.

Usa las herramientas para construir la idea. Luego compruébala.

  1. Sort each division by how many places you slide both decimal points to make the divisor a whole number.
  2. Write a shipment plan that uses a decimal total and a decimal amount in each shipment. Show the decimal division algorithm, explain how the decimal point moves, and check your answer by multiplying.
  3. 4 fresh problems with new numbers each time (interactive only).

4. Detective work

Somebody made a mistake. Find the step, then decide who is right.

Alguien cometió un error. Encuentra el paso y decide quién tiene razón.

  1. Hassan solves 7.2 ÷ 0.9.
  2. Wanjiru solves 4.2 ÷ 0.07.
  3. Tomás solves 3.64 ÷ 1.4.
  4. Kiran says 0.5 ÷ 0.25 = 2. Elena says it is 0.2 because dividing makes a number smaller. Who is right?
    • Kiran, because 50 ÷ 25 = 2 and 0.25 × 2 = 0.5
    • Elena, because the quotient is always less than the dividend
    • Neither, because the answer is 20
    • Both, because 2 and 0.2 are equal
  5. What is the best next move?
    • Put the quotient's point right above the one in 34.5, then continue.
    • Stop at 2 R4, because the division has worked out.
    • Ignore the point in 34.5 and finish 345 ÷ 15 as a whole number.
    • Slide the point in 15 back to make 1.5 again.
