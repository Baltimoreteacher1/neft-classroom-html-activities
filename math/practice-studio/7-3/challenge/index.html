  2. A submarine is at -150 meters and a bird is flying at +150 meters. A student says the submarine is closer to sea level because -150 is less than 150. Is the student correct? Explain using absolute value.
  3. On a level line, the portal is 0. Rio stands at −9 and Sky stands at 6. How many units farther from the portal is Rio than Sky?

    • Nova is the farthest from the portal.
    • Zed is the farthest, because 5 is greater than −8.
    • Nova and Zed are the same distance, because the signs cancel.
    • Nova is the closest, because negative numbers are less.
  6. A power meter shows a glitch reading of −3.6 and a boost reading of 2.4. The energy cost is the sum of how far each reading is from 0. What is the total energy cost?
