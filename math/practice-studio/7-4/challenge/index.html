Follow the numbers to the truth.Sigue los números hasta la verdad.

A cold case has just been reopened. Every clue is a number, some of them far below zero, and only the right order will crack the lock on the evidence room.

Acaban de reabrir un caso sin resolver. Cada pista es un número, algunos muy por debajo de cero, y solo el orden correcto abrirá la cerradura de la sala de evidencias.

1. Climb the skill ladder

Each rung is harder than the last, and the numbers change every time.

Cada peldaño es más difícil que el anterior y los números cambian cada vez.

  1. 6 fresh problems with new numbers each time (interactive only).

2. Multi-step missions

Real situations that need more than one step: plan, solve, then check that your answer makes sense.

Situaciones reales que necesitan más de un paso: planea, resuelve y revisa que tu respuesta tenga sentido.

  1. Find the Comparison Error
  2. A student says: 'Negative numbers are always less than positive numbers, so -100 is less than 1.' Is this correct? Now the student says: '-100 is less than -1 because 100 is greater than 1.' Is THIS correct? Explain the difference.
  3. Four rooms at the scene were measured: freezer −18°C, attic 31°C, cellar −4°C, study 0°C. Rank them from coldest (1) to warmest (4), and count how many rooms are warmer than each one. Fill in every empty cell.
  4. Five clues are placed on the number line: A at −9, B at −4, C at 0, D at 3 and E at −12. Which list shows the clues in order from least to greatest?
    • D, C, B, A, E
    • C, D, B, A, E
    • E, A, B, C, D
    • B, A, E, C, D
  5. A detective scores six clues: −7, 4, −2, 0, −9 and −5. How many of the scores are greater than −3?
  6. A case ledger lists four balances: Smith −$45, Jones −$12, Park $8 and Lee −$30. Which list orders the balances from greatest to least?
    • $8, −$12, −$30, −$45
    • −$45, −$30, −$12, $8
    • $8, −$45, −$30, −$12
    • −$12, −$30, −$45, $8

3. Build it, move it, test it

Use the tools to build the idea. Then check it.

Usa las herramientas para construir la idea. Luego compruébala.

  1. Decide whether each comparison is true or false. Sort every card.
  2. 2 fresh problems with new numbers each time (interactive only).

4. Detective work

Somebody made a mistake. Find the step, then decide who is right.

Alguien cometió un error. Encuentra el paso y decide quién tiene razón.

  1. Pru orders −9, 5, −2 and 0 from least to greatest. Find the step where her thinking goes wrong.
  2. Quin compares −3 and −8 and writes a statement with a symbol. Find the step where his work goes wrong.
  3. Rae compares 0 and −6 and says which is greater. Find the step where her thinking goes wrong.
  4. Ximena says, "−3 is greater than −7, because −3 is farther right on a number line." Yuri says, "−7 is greater than −3, because 7 is greater than 3." Zoe says, "−3 is less than −7, because negative numbers are less than positive ones." Who is right?
    • Yuri only
    • Ximena only
    • Zoe only
    • Nobody

5. Boss level

Tell us how sure you are, then solve. Finish with a problem of your own.

Dinos qué tan seguro estás y luego resuelve. Termina con un problema tuyo.

  1. Which list shows −7, 3, −10 and 0 in order from least to greatest?
    • −7, −10, 0, 3
    • 3, 0, −7, −10
    • 0, 3, −7, −10
    • −10, −7, 0, 3
  2. Which comparison is true?
    • −2.5 < −2.25
    • −0.3 < −0.8
    • −4 > −3.5
    • −1.2 > 0.1
  3. How many integers are between −4 and 3? Do not count −4 or 3 themselves.
  4. Three points are shown on the number line: P at −7, Q at −2 and R at 4. Which statement is true?
    • Q is less than P.
    • R is less than Q.
    • P is less than Q.
    • P is greater than R.
  5. Four rooms at a scene measure −6°C, 2°C, −11°C and −1°C. How many degrees apart are the coldest room and the warmest room?
  6. Create your own cold case. Write five integers as clues, with at least three negative, and put them in order from least to greatest. Use the symbols < and > in two true comparisons, and explain how a number line helped you.
