4. Detective work

Somebody made a mistake. Find the step, then decide who is right.

Alguien cometió un error. Encuentra el paso y decide quién tiene razón.

  1. A trail guide added two water amounts. One step goes wrong. Tap the first step that is wrong.
  2. A hiker figured out how far is left on a 30 km trail. One step goes wrong. Tap the first step that is wrong.
  3. A camper wanted to give a number two decimal places before subtracting. One step goes wrong. Tap the first step that is wrong.
  4. Ana and Ben added 0.7 + 0.35 for the trail log. Ana wrote 0.7 as 0.70 and got 1.05. Ben added 7 + 35 and wrote 0.42. Who is right?
    • Only Ana: 0.7 is 70 hundredths, and 70 + 35 = 105 hundredths, which is 1.05.
    • Only Ben: 7 + 35 = 42, so the sum is 0.42.
    • Both are right, because 1.05 and 0.42 are two ways to write the sum.
    • Neither: the sum is 0.105.

  4. A guide wants to subtract 1.45 from 3.2. Why is it correct to write 3.2 as 3.20 first?

    • The zero moves the decimal point one place to the right.
  5. A hiker walked three legs: 3.75 km, 2.8 km, and 4.15 km. How many kilometers longer is the longest leg than the shortest leg?
  6. Write your own Summit Trail problem that needs one addition and one subtraction of decimals with different numbers of decimal places. Solve it, show how you lined up the places, and check your answer with an estimate.
