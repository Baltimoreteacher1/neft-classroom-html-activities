Build it right.Constrúyelo bien.

After hours, the workshop's big build begins: gears that must meet, boards that must be marked, and packs that must come out even. Choose the least common multiple, not just any common multiple, and prove it with your lists.

Después del horario, empieza la gran construcción del taller: engranajes que deben encontrarse, tableros que deben marcarse y paquetes que deben salir parejos. Elige el mínimo común múltiplo, no cualquier múltiplo común, y demuéstralo con tus listas.

1. Climb the skill ladder

Each rung is harder than the last, and the numbers change every time.

Cada peldaño es más difícil que el anterior y los números cambian cada vez.

  1. 6 fresh problems with new numbers each time (interactive only).

2. Multi-step missions

Real situations that need more than one step: plan, solve, then check that your answer makes sense.

Situaciones reales que necesitan más de un paso: planea, resuelve y revisa que tu respuesta tenga sentido.

  1. Find Jaylen's Mistake
  2. You are making party favors. Glow sticks come in packs of 10 and bracelets come in packs of 4. What is the fewest of each you can buy so every favor has exactly one glow stick and one bracelet with none left over? Explain how the LCM solves this problem.
  3. A maker drills a hole every 14 cm and marks a line every 21 cm along a 200 cm board, starting at 0 cm. Not counting the starting end, at how many places along the board do a hole and a line land in the same spot?
  4. Bolts come in packs of 15 and brackets come in packs of 6. Pat wants exactly the same number of bolts as brackets, with none left over, and as few packs as possible. How many packs of each should Pat buy?
    • 5 packs of bolts and 2 packs of brackets
    • 6 packs of bolts and 15 packs of brackets
    • 1 pack of bolts and 1 pack of brackets
    • 2 packs of bolts and 5 packs of brackets
  5. Gear A has 16 teeth and gear B has 24 teeth. The gears start with two marked teeth touching. How many full turns does the smaller gear make before the marked teeth touch again?
  6. The table lists where Saw A (every 10 cm) and Saw B (every 25 cm) cut a long board. How many cuts has Saw A made when the two saws first cut the same spot?
    • 2 cuts
    • 5 cuts
    • 7 cuts
    • 50 cuts

3. Build it, move it, test it

Use the tools to build the idea. Then check it.

Usa las herramientas para construir la idea. Luego compruébala.

  1. Sort each number: is it a common multiple of 10 and 15, or not?
  2. 2 fresh problems with new numbers each time (interactive only).

4. Detective work

Somebody made a mistake. Find the step, then decide who is right.

Alguien cometió un error. Encuentra el paso y decide quién tiene razón.

  1. Cleo found the least common multiple of 12 and 15 and got 180. Tap the step where her thinking goes wrong.
  2. Dev found the least common multiple of 8 and 10 and got 10. Tap the step where his thinking goes wrong.
  3. A bracelet has a red bead at every 5th place and a green bead at every 9th place. Both colors are at place 0. Rina says they are next at the same place at place 14. Tap the step where her thinking goes wrong.
  4. Dev says the LCM of 4 and 8 is 32, because 4 × 8 = 32. Hana says the LCM is 8, because 8 is already a multiple of 4. Who is right?
    • Dev: the LCM is always the product of the two numbers.
    • Both are right: 8 and 32 are both common multiples, so either one is the LCM.
    • Neither: the LCM of 4 and 8 is 4.
    • Hana: 8 is a multiple of both 4 and 8, and no smaller number is.

5. Boss level

Tell us how sure you are, then solve. Finish with a problem of your own.

Dinos qué tan seguro estás y luego resuelve. Termina con un problema tuyo.

  1. Which number is a common multiple of 5 and 12 but is NOT their least common multiple?
    • 60
    • 17
    • 120
    • 24
  2. What is the least common multiple of 18 and 24?
  3. I am a common multiple of 4 and 6. I am greater than 30 and less than 40. Which number am I?
    • 36
    • 30
    • 32
    • 40
  4. For which pair of numbers is the least common multiple equal to the product of the two numbers?
    • 10 and 14
    • 8 and 15
    • 6 and 21
    • 12 and 20
  5. A decorative strip repeats a blue tile every 6 tiles and a gold tile every 14 tiles. Both colors are at tile 0. At which tile number do blue and gold first land together again?
  6. Create your own Maker Workshop problem that needs a least common multiple. Solve it by listing multiples, and explain why your answer is the LEAST common multiple and why a bigger common multiple would not answer the question.
