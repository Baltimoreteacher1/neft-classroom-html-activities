Fog rolls over the upper ridge and the trail map is covered in long packing lists. Shorten every list without losing a single item, or the crew will reach camp short on supplies.

La niebla cae sobre la cresta y el mapa del sendero está lleno de listas de equipo muy largas. Acorta cada lista sin perder ni un solo artículo, o el grupo llegará al campamento sin provisiones.

1. Climb the skill ladder

Each rung is harder than the last, and the numbers change every time.

Cada peldaño es más difícil que el anterior y los números cambian cada vez.

  1. 6 fresh problems with new numbers each time (interactive only).

2. Multi-step missions

Real situations that need more than one step: plan, solve, then check that your answer makes sense.

Situaciones reales que necesitan más de un paso: planea, resuelve y revisa que tu respuesta tenga sentido.

  1. Find the Simplification Error
  2. Three students simplified 4x + 3 + 2x + 5 + x differently. Student A got 7x + 8. Student B got 7x² + 8. Student C got 6x + 9. Who is correct? Explain the mistakes the other two students made.
  3. Fill in the table
  4. The trail crew writes each supply list as an expression. For every list, combine the x-terms, combine the constants, then find the value when x = 3. Fill in the empty cells.
  5. Three trail sections have lengths 3x + 4, 2x + 7 and x + 1 kilometers, where x is the number of switchbacks on the trail. The sections are walked end to end. How long is the whole trail when x = 2?
  6. A tape diagram shows four parts of a checkpoint pack: 2x pounds of water, 5 pounds of snacks, 3x pounds of water and 4 pounds of snacks. Which expression is the simplest form of the whole pack's weight?
    • 5x + 9
    • 14x
    • 5x² + 9
    • 6x + 9
  7. A hiking leader packs 3 kits that each weigh (x + 2) pounds. She also carries 4x pounds of tents. The total weight is 3(x + 2) + 4x. When this is simplified to ax + b, what is a + b?

Usa las herramientas para construir la idea. Luego compruébala.

4. Detective work

Somebody made a mistake. Find the step, then decide who is right.

Alguien cometió un error. Encuentra el paso y decide quién tiene razón.

  1. Hana simplified 5n + 8 + 3n. Find the step where her work first goes wrong.
  2. Diego simplified 9p + p + 2. Find the step where his work first goes wrong.
  3. Kofi simplified 12k + 9 − 4k − 5. Find the step where his work first goes wrong.
  4. Three hikers simplified 11a + 8 − 6a. Lena wrote 5a + 8. Omar wrote 17a + 8. Suri wrote 5a − 8. Who simplified correctly?
    • Omar
    • Suri

Tell us how sure you are, then solve. Finish with a problem of your own.

Dinos qué tan seguro estás y luego resuelve. Termina con un problema tuyo.

  1. Which pair of terms can be combined into one term?
    • 7p and 7

  2. Which expression is the simplest form of 14x + 12 − 6x − 9?
    • 20x + 3
    • 8x + 21
    • 8x − 3
    • 8x + 3
  3. The trail crew's load is 3m + 8 + 4m + 2 kilograms, where m is the number of tents. What is the load when m = 5?
  4. Which expression is equivalent to 2(x + 4) + 3x?
    • 5x + 8
    • 5x + 4
    • 13x
    • 6x + 8
  5. Which expression simplifies to 9x + 5?
    • 6x + 3x + 5x
    • 9 + 5x
    • 4x + 5 + 5x
    • 4x + 5 + 5
