2. Multi-step missions

Real situations that need more than one step: plan, solve, then check that your answer makes sense.

Situaciones reales que necesitan más de un paso: planea, resuelve y revisa que tu respuesta tenga sentido.

  1. Find Marcus's Mistake
  2. A store sells 3 pens for $2. Another store sells 9 pens for $6. Are these equivalent ratios? If so, prove it using at least two different methods (simplifying, scaling, or cross-multiplying).
  3. Fill in the table
  4. At the studio's radio show, Maya plays 4 rock songs for every 6 pop songs. Today's long show has 15 pop songs. How many rock songs keep the same ratio?
  5. Ines's mix uses 6 parts guitar to 9 parts vocals. Which new mix sounds exactly the same?
    • 12 parts guitar to 15 parts vocals
    • 9 parts guitar to 6 parts vocals
    • 10 parts guitar to 15 parts vocals
    • 12 parts guitar to 9 parts vocals
  6. Farid's drum pattern plays 12 kick hits for every 20 claps. Complete each row so it stays equivalent to 12 : 20.
  7. Quinn's recording choir has 40 singers. There are 3 sopranos for every 2 altos, and everyone is either a soprano or an alto. How many singers are sopranos?

3. Build it, move it, test it

Use the tools to build the idea. Then check it.

Usa las herramientas para construir la idea. Luego compruébala.

4. Detective work

Somebody made a mistake. Find the step, then decide who is right.

Alguien cometió un error. Encuentra el paso y decide quién tiene razón.

  1. Mina's recording booth has 3 microphones for every 7 headphone sets. She plans a bigger booth with 12 microphones. How many headphone sets keep the same ratio? Tap the first step where her work goes wrong.
  2. Bao simplifies the ratio of 18 trumpets to 24 trombones in the marching band. Tap the first step where his work goes wrong.
  3. A concert sold 5 student tickets for every 8 adult tickets. A second concert sold 9 student tickets for every 12 adult tickets. Rin says the two ratios are equivalent. Tap the first step where her work goes wrong.
  4. An album has 2 hip-hop tracks for every 3 jazz tracks (2:3). Tess says 3:2 is an equivalent ratio because it uses the same two numbers. Omar says the order matters. Who is right?
    • Tess, because the numbers are the same, just in a new order.
    • Omar. 3:2 means 3 hip-hop per 2 jazz, a different album.
    • Both, because a ratio can be written in any order you like.
    • Tess, because 3:2 simplifies to 2:3 when you reduce it.
  5. What is the best next move?
    • Say yes, because 20 and 30 are both bigger than 12 and 18.
    • Subtract: 18 − 12 = 6 and 30 − 20 = 10, so they are not equivalent.
    • Divide 20 and 30 both by 10, then compare it with 2 : 3.
    • Divide only the 20 by 10 to get 2 : 30, then compare it.
  6. 1 fresh problem with new numbers each time (interactive only).

5. Boss level

Tell us how sure you are, then solve. Finish with a problem of your own.

Dinos qué tan seguro estás y luego resuelve. Termina con un problema tuyo.

  1. Zola's merch table sold 28 shirts and 42 posters. Which ratio of shirts to posters is equivalent to 28:42 and in simplest form?
    • 2:3
    • 3:2
    • 14:21
    • 27:41
  2. Dolores prints 35 pages of sheet music for 20 musicians. At the same rate, how many pages does she print for 12 musicians?
  3. Marcus tests whether two knob settings, 6/8 and 9/12, are equivalent with cross products. Which sentence is correct?
    • They are not equivalent, because 6 × 9 = 54 and 8 × 12 = 96.
    • They are not equivalent, because 6 + 12 = 18 and 8 + 9 = 17.
    • They are not equivalent, because 9 is greater than 6.
    • They are equivalent, because 6 × 12 and 8 × 9 both equal 72.
  4. A producer records 150 minutes for every 20 minutes of finished music. How many minutes of recording does Hiro need for 8 minutes of finished music?
  5. Paloma's concert uses 4 spotlights for every 7 floodlights. The stage has 24 spotlights. How many lights are there in all?
  6. Create your own equivalent-ratio problem about a mix, a playlist or a stage. Write a starting ratio and an equivalent ratio, and explain how you know they are equivalent.
