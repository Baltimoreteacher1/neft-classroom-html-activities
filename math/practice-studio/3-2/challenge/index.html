4. Detective work

Somebody made a mistake. Find the step, then decide who is right.

Alguien cometió un error. Encuentra el paso y decide quién tiene razón.

  1. A player found the price of one token. One step goes wrong. Tap the first step that is wrong.
  2. A player compared two packs. One step goes wrong. Tap the first step that is wrong.
  3. A robot rover crawls across a game board. One step goes wrong. Tap the first step that is wrong.
  4. Kai says 10 tokens for $4.00 beats 16 tokens for $6.00, because $4.00 is less. Lena finds the price of one token in each pack. Who is right?
    • Kai: the pack with the lower price is always the better buy.
    • Both are right, because $4.00 is less and $0.375 is less.
    • Neither: the packs cost the same per token.
    • Lena: $4.00 ÷ 10 = $0.40 and $6.00 ÷ 16 = $0.375, so the 16-token pack costs less per token.

5. Boss level

Tell us how sure you are, then solve. Finish with a problem of your own.

Dinos qué tan seguro estás y luego resuelve. Termina con un problema tuyo.

  1. A ride ticket booth sells 9 tickets for $13.50. What is the price of one ticket, in dollars?
  2. A race car in the arcade game travels 255 miles in 1.5 hours at a steady speed. What is the speed in miles per hour?
  3. The snack bar sells 4 cups of popcorn for $3.80 or 6 cups for $5.40. Which is the better buy?
    • 6 cups for $5.40, at $0.90 per cup
    • 4 cups for $3.80, at $0.95 per cup
    • 4 cups for $3.80, because $3.80 is less
    • They cost the same per cup
  4. Game tokens cost $0.35 each at a steady price. How many dollars do 24 tokens cost?
  5. A train travels 150 miles per hour at a constant speed. Which sentence describes this rate correctly?
    • The train goes 150 miles in each 1 minute.
    • The train takes 150 hours to go 1 mile.
    • The train goes 150 miles in each 1 hour.
    • The train goes 150 miles in 150 hours.
  6. Write your own arcade problem about two deals with different numbers of items. Find each unit rate, say which is the better buy, and explain why comparing only the total prices would not be fair.
