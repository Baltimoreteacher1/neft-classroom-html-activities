1. Warm up your thinking

Quick questions that wake up what you already know for this lesson.

Preguntas rápidas para activar lo que ya sabes de esta lección.

  1. Read the unit rate for 5 cups of juice and 4 cups of water: divide 5 ÷ 4. Then predict the unit rate if the water is changed to 5 cups, before you look. Explain what "per 1 cup" means and why you divide.
  2. A bag holds 4 red marbles and 6 blue marbles. Write the ratio of red marbles to blue marbles in simplest form.
    • 2:3
    • 3:2
    • 4:10
    • 10
  3. What does the ratio 3:1 mean in words?
    • The first amount is 3 more than the second
    • There are 4 things altogether
    • For every 3 of the first amount there is 1 of the second
    • The two amounts are equal
  4. A bowl holds 4 apples and 6 oranges. What is the ratio of apples to the TOTAL pieces of fruit?
    • 4:10
    • 4:6
    • 6:10
    • 10:4
  5. Which pair of amounts does the ratio 2:5 compare?
    • Two different quantities
    • A number and its opposite
    • A total and its parts added
    • The same quantity twice
