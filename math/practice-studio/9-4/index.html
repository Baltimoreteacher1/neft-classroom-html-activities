1. Warm up your thinking

Quick questions that wake up what you already know for this lesson.

Preguntas rápidas para activar lo que ya sabes de esta lección.

  1. A table pairs x = 1, 2, 3 with y = 4, 8, 12. Which equation fits?
    • y = x + 3
    • y = 4x
    • y = x + 4
    • y = x ÷ 4
  2. A bike travels 12 miles every hour. Which equation gives distance d after h hours?
    • d = 12h
    • d = 12 + h
    • d = h ÷ 12
    • d = 12 − h
  3. In the equation y = 5x, what is y when x = 6?
    • 5
    • 11
    • 30
    • 56
  4. When you write an equation for a relationship, which variable is usually alone on one side?
    • The dependent variable
    • The independent variable
    • Whichever is larger
    • It does not matter
