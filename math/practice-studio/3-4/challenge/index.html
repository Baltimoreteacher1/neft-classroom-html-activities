It is dinner rush at Corner Kitchen and the order board is a grid of points. Read each graph fast and true: one swapped coordinate sends the wrong food to the wrong table.

Es la hora pico de la cena en Corner Kitchen y el tablero de pedidos es una cuadrícula de puntos. Lee cada gráfica con rapidez y exactitud: una coordenada invertida manda la comida equivocada a la mesa equivocada.

1. Climb the skill ladder

Each rung is harder than the last, and the numbers change every time.

Cada peldaño es más difícil que el anterior y los números cambian cada vez.

  1. 6 fresh problems with new numbers each time (interactive only).

2. Multi-step missions

Real situations that need more than one step: plan, solve, then check that your answer makes sense.

Situaciones reales que necesitan más de un paso: planea, resuelve y revisa que tu respuesta tenga sentido.

  1. Find Jada's Mistake
  2. A student plots the points (1, 3), (2, 6), (3, 9), and (4, 15) from a ratio table. She says they form a straight line because they go up. Is she correct? Explain how to check.
  3. A bakery graphs trays of muffins (x) and muffins baked (y), as shown in the graph. Which ordered pair is also on the line?
    • (5, 20)
    • (30, 5)
    • (5, 30)
    • (4, 28)
  4. The graph shows cups of flour (y) for loaves of bread (x). The points follow a straight line through the origin. How many cups of flour do 9 loaves need?
  5. Each tray holds the same number of eggs. Fill in the missing numbers, using the eggs per tray.
  6. Chef Omar plots cupcakes (x) and dollars (y) for four orders: (1, 3), (2, 5), (3, 7), (4, 9), as shown in the graph. Which statement is true?
    • The points make a straight line, so every pair has the same ratio.
    • The line misses (0, 0), and the ratios 3:1, 5:2, and 7:3 are not equal, so this is not a ratio relationship.
    • It is a ratio relationship, because y goes up by 2 every time.
    • The ratios 1:3, 2:5, 3:7, and 4:9 are all equal.

3. Build it, move it, test it

Use the tools to build the idea. Then check it.

Usa las herramientas para construir la idea. Luego compruébala.

  1. A kitchen uses 5 cups of flour for every 2 loaves, so its line follows y = 2.5 × x. Sort each point by whether it is on that line.
  2. 2 fresh problems with new numbers each time (interactive only).

4. Detective work

Somebody made a mistake. Find the step, then decide who is right.

Alguien cometió un error. Encuentra el paso y decide quién tiene razón.

  1. A cook plotted a point for a tray order. One step goes wrong. Tap the first step that is wrong.
  2. A cook found a point on a line. One step goes wrong. Tap the first step that is wrong.
  3. A cook checked whether four points are on one line through the origin. One step goes wrong. Tap the first step that is wrong.
  4. On a graph of loaves (x) and cups of flour (y), a point is plotted at (4, 10). Ana says it means 10 cups of flour for 4 loaves. Ben says it means 4 cups of flour for 10 loaves. Who is right?
    • Ana, because x is loaves and y is cups, so (4, 10) is 4 loaves and 10 cups.
    • Ben, because the bigger number is always the loaves.
    • Both, because a point can be read either way.
    • Neither, because (4, 10) means 14 things in all.

5. Boss level

Tell us how sure you are, then solve. Finish with a problem of your own.

Dinos qué tan seguro estás y luego resuelve. Termina con un problema tuyo.

  1. A straight line through the origin passes through the point (5, 20) on a graph of batches (x) and cups of milk (y). What is the y-value of the point on the line where x = 8?
  2. A graph shows the ratio 2:3 with x as the first number and y as the second. Which point is NOT on that line?
    • (4, 6)
    • (6, 9)
    • (10, 15)
    • (8, 11)
  3. The graph shows loaves of bread (x) and cups of flour (y). What does the point (1, 3) tell you?
    • One loaf needs 3 cups of flour.
    • Three loaves need 1 cup of flour.
    • The kitchen has 4 items in all.
    • The first point does not mean anything special.
  4. A ratio table gives the points (2, 7), (4, 14), and (6, 21) for trays (x) and pounds of dough (y). On the same line, how many pounds of dough go with 10 trays?
  5. Which situation would NOT make a straight line through (0, 0) when graphed?
    • Tomatoes cost $3 for each kilogram.
    • A cooking class costs $10 to join plus $2 for each lesson.
    • Each loaf needs 3 cups of flour.
    • Each tray holds 12 eggs.
  6. Make up a kitchen recipe with a ratio between two ingredients. Write a ratio table with at least four rows, list the ordered pairs, and explain what shape the points will make when you plot them, and why.
