2. Multi-step missions

Real situations that need more than one step: plan, solve, then check that your answer makes sense.

Situaciones reales que necesitan más de un paso: planea, resuelve y revisa que tu respuesta tenga sentido.

  1. Find the Surface Area Error
  2. Two boxes have the same volume of 60 in³. Box A is 5 × 4 × 3. Box B is 10 × 6 × 1. Which box uses less wrapping paper (has less surface area)? Show your calculations and explain why a more cube-like shape might use less material.
  3. Fill in the table
  4. Corner Kitchen needs 5 pizza boxes for the school fair. Each closed box is 30 cm long, 30 cm wide and 4 cm tall. Cardboard comes in sheets that cover 1,000 cm². How many sheets must the kitchen buy to make all 5 boxes? (Ignore flaps and waste.)
    • 11 sheets
    • 9 sheets
    • 12 sheets
    • 3 sheets
  5. Corner Kitchen lines baking trays with foil. A tray has no lid and is 36 cm long, 24 cm wide and 5 cm tall. Fill in the faces that the foil must cover.
  6. Chef Tomás sketched the faces of a rectangular cereal box. His list shows five face areas: 24, 24, 18, 18 and 12 cm². Ink smudged the sixth. What is the total surface area of the box?
    • 108 cm²

  7. Corner Kitchen makes lunch boxes that are 20 cm long, 15 cm wide and 5 cm tall. Each box is cut from a rectangular sheet of cardboard that is 100 cm by 60 cm. By area alone, how many whole boxes can one sheet make? (Ignore flaps and waste.)

3. Build it, move it, test it

Use the tools to build the idea. Then check it.

Alguien cometió un error. Encuentra el paso y decide quién tiene razón.
