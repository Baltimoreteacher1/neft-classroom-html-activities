surface areaárea de superficie
The total area of all the faces of a solid. It is measured in square units.El área total de todas las caras de un sólido. Se mide en unidades cuadradas.
rectangular prismprisma rectangular
A solid box with six rectangle faces.Un sólido en forma de caja con seis caras rectangulares.
triangular prismprisma triangular
A solid with two triangle ends joined by three rectangles.Un sólido con dos extremos triangulares unidos por tres rectángulos.
lateral facecara lateral
A side face of a solid, not one of its top and bottom bases.Una cara de los lados de un sólido, no una de sus bases de arriba y abajo.
congruentcongruente

2. Multi-step missions

Real situations that need more than one step: plan, solve, then check that your answer makes sense.

Situaciones reales que necesitan más de un paso: planea, resuelve y revisa que tu respuesta tenga sentido.

  1. Find the Surface Area Error
  2. Compare two closed containers: a rectangular prism (8 × 6 × 4 in) and a triangular prism whose ends are right triangles with perpendicular legs 6 in and 8 in and hypotenuse 10 in. The triangular prism is 8 in long. Which uses less protective film to cover every face? Show your work.
  3. Fill in the table
  4. The rooftop garden has 4 identical planter boxes. Each box is 6 ft long, 2 ft wide and 2 ft tall, with no top. The crew paints every outside face of each box. One can of paint covers 50 ft². How many cans must they buy?
    • 4 cans
    • 3 cans
    • 5 cans
    • 1 can
  5. An A-frame greenhouse is a triangular prism 9 ft long. Each triangular end has a base of 12 ft, a height of 8 ft and two sloping sides of 10 ft. Plastic covers the two ends and the two sloping roof panels, but not the ground. How many square feet of plastic does the greenhouse need?
  6. A square planter box has a 3 ft by 3 ft base and no top. The crew needs 57 ft² of sealant to cover its bottom and its four sides. How tall is the planter?
  7. Two identical closed storage boxes are each 4 ft long, 2 ft wide and 2 ft tall. The crew pushes them together end to end so the two 2 ft by 2 ft ends touch, then paints every outside face of the combined shape. How many square feet do they paint?
    • 80 ft²
    • 72 ft²
    • 76 ft²
    • 40 ft²

4. Detective work

Somebody made a mistake. Find the step, then decide who is right.

Alguien cometió un error. Encuentra el paso y decide quién tiene razón.

  1. Lena wants to know how many square feet of paint cover every outside face of a closed planter box that is 9 ft long, 3 ft wide and 2 ft tall. Tap the step where her plan goes wrong.
  2. Marcus finds the surface area of a seed-storage chest that is 11 cm long, 5 cm wide and 2 cm tall. Tap the step where his work first goes wrong.
  3. Tariq finds the surface area of a closed wedge-shaped planter that is 4 in long. Its triangular ends have sides of 5 in, 12 in and 13 in, with a right angle between the 5 in and 12 in sides. Tap the step where his work first goes wrong.
  4. A closed triangular prism is 6 ft long, and its triangular ends have sides of 3 ft, 4 ft and 5 ft. Ana adds the three rectangles: 18 + 24 + 30 = 72 ft². Ben says the rectangle on the bottom sits on the ground, so he adds only two: 18 + 30 = 48 ft². Who is right?
    • Ben, because a face on the ground is not part of the surface area
    • Both, because it depends on how the prism is placed
    • Ana, because all three rectangles are faces of the prism
    • Neither, because the faces of a prism must be triangles
  5. What is the best next move?
    • Double the bottom for a top and a bottom: 2 × 10.
    • Multiply the bottom by the height: 10 × 3.
    • Find all four side faces and add them to the bottom.
    • Find only the front and back faces, 2 × (5 × 3), and stop.

5. Boss level

Tell us how sure you are, then solve. Finish with a problem of your own.

Dinos qué tan seguro estás y luego resuelve. Termina con un problema tuyo.

  1. A closed seed-storage chest is 13 in long, 4 in wide and 3 in tall. What is the surface area of the chest?
  2. A triangular prism has right-triangle ends with legs of 6 ft and 8 ft and a longest side of 10 ft. The prism is 5 ft long. Which expression gives its surface area in square feet?
    • (½ × 6 × 8) + (6 + 8 + 10) × 5
    • 2 × (½ × 6 × 8) + 10 × 5
    • ½ × 6 × 8 × 5
    • 2 × (½ × 6 × 8) + (6 + 8 + 10) × 5
  3. A cube-shaped compost bin has edges of 3 ft and no top. How many square feet of wood does the bin use?
  4. Each of these closed planter boxes holds 64 ft³ of soil. Which one needs the least paint for its outside?
    • 1 ft × 8 ft × 8 ft
    • 2 ft × 4 ft × 8 ft
    • 4 ft × 4 ft × 4 ft
    • They all need the same paint, because they hold the same amount
  5. A wedge-shaped seed packet is a closed triangular prism 10 cm long. Each end is a right triangle with legs of 9 cm and 12 cm and a longest side of 15 cm. What is the surface area of the packet?
  6. Create your own Rooftop Garden problem. Choose a rectangular prism or a triangular prism, give its measurements, and decide which faces get paint or plastic. Find the surface area and explain why you counted those faces.
