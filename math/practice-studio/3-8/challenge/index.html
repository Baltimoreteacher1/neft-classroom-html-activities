Real situations that need more than one step: plan, solve, then check that your answer makes sense.

  2. Two arcade supply companies offer token deals. Company X: 300 tokens for $36. Company Y: 500 tokens for $55. The arcade needs 1,000 tokens. Find the unit rate for each company, determine which is cheaper per token, and calculate the total cost of buying 1,000 tokens from the better deal.

  4. Zainab is ordering screws for build week. Supplier A sells 100 screws for $8.00. Supplier B sells 150 screws for $10.50. The workshop needs 600 screws. How many dollars does Zainab save by buying all 600 from the better supplier instead of the other one?

4. Detective work

Somebody made a mistake. Find the step, then decide who is right.

Alguien cometió un error. Encuentra el paso y decide quién tiene razón.

  1. Dmitri compares two deals for sensor batteries. Tap the first step where his work goes wrong.
  2. Amara compares two spools of wire: Spool A is 20 m for $8 and Spool B is 30 m for $15. Tap the first step where her work goes wrong.
  3. A laser cutter cuts 36 parts in 4 minutes. Kenji finds how many parts it cuts in 10 minutes. Tap the first step where his work goes wrong.
  4. Sander A finishes 90 boards in 6 minutes. Sander B finishes 120 boards in 10 minutes. Dev says B is faster because its unit rate, 12, is lower. Mia says A is faster because it finishes more boards each minute, 15. Who is right?
    • Mia, because for boards per minute a higher rate is better.
    • Dev, because a lower unit rate is always the better one.
    • Neither. Sander B finished more boards in total, so B wins.
    • Neither. They ran different times, so they cannot be compared.
  5. What is the best next move?
    • Multiply $0.75 × 30 and buy A, since A already has a price for 1.
    • Find B's price for 1 bar, $7.00 ÷ 10, and compare it with $0.75.
    • Find the bars in 1 dollar for B, 10 ÷ $7.00, and compare with $0.75.
    • Compare $4.50 with $7.00 and buy A, since it costs less.
  6. 1 fresh problem with new numbers each time (interactive only).

5. Boss level

Tell us how sure you are, then solve. Finish with a problem of your own.

Dinos qué tan seguro estás y luego resuelve. Termina con un problema tuyo.

  1. Wen buys 25 sheets of foam board for $18.00. What is the price of 1 sheet, in dollars?
  2. Takoda compares two cans of paint. Can X holds 0.5 L and costs $6.50. Can Y holds 1.25 L and costs $17.00. Which is the better buy?
    • Can Y, at $13.60 for each liter of paint
    • Can Y, since a bigger can costs less per liter
    • Can X, at $13.00 for each liter of paint
    • Neither, since the cans hold different amounts
  3. A CNC mill uses 3.5 L of coolant every 2 hours. The coolant tank holds 28 L. For how many hours can the mill run?
  4. Isabel's workshop pays $0.35 for each bolt. Isabel has $28 to spend on bolts. How many bolts can she buy?
  5. Ribbon at the workshop costs the same amount for every meter. One price in this ratio table is wrong. Which one?
    • 3 m for $4.50
    • 6 m for $8.50
    • 9 m for $13.50
    • 12 m for $18.00
  6. Create your own better-buy problem for the Maker Workshop. Describe two deals for the same item, find the unit rate of each, and explain which deal is the better buy and by how much per item.
