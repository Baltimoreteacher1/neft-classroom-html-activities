4. Detective work

Somebody made a mistake. Find the step, then decide who is right.

Alguien cometió un error. Encuentra el paso y decide quién tiene razón.

  1. Mateo listed the factors of 36 and wrote 1, 2, 3, 4, 6. Tap the step where his work goes wrong.
  2. Anaya found the greatest common factor of 9 and 15. Tap the step where her thinking goes wrong.
  3. Night guards walk past the vault: Officer Park every 9 days and Officer Reyes every 12 days. Both are there today. Hana says they will both be there again in 3 days. Tap the step where her thinking goes wrong.
  4. Jamal says 5 is a multiple of 20, because 5 goes into 20. Lena says 5 is a factor of 20, and 20 is a multiple of 5. Who is right?
    • Lena is right: 5 divides 20 evenly, so 5 is a factor of 20 and 20 is a multiple of 5.
    • Jamal is right: 5 goes into 20, so 5 is a multiple of 20.
    • Both are right: factor and multiple mean the same thing.
    • Neither is right: 5 does not divide 20 evenly.
