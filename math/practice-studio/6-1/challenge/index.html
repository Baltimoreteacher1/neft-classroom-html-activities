2. Multi-step missions

Real situations that need more than one step: plan, solve, then check that your answer makes sense.

Situaciones reales que necesitan más de un paso: planea, resuelve y revisa que tu respuesta tenga sentido.

  1. Find the Interpretation Error
  2. Dividing 6 by 1/2 gives 12, but dividing 6 by 2 gives 3. How can dividing by a smaller number (1/2) give a bigger answer than dividing by a larger number (2)? Explain using a real-world example.
  3. Spot the Mistake — When the Numerator Is Not 1
  4. The Sound Studio cuts a 6-foot roll of cable into pieces that are each 1/3 foot long. Each patch kit needs 4 pieces. How many complete patch kits can the studio make?
    • 4 kits
    • 18 kits
    • 5 kits
    • 24 kits
  5. The studio records 3/4 of an hour of music and splits it equally into 6 tracks. Two of the tracks are played back to back. How many hours long is that?
  6. The studio is cutting acoustic foam. Fill in each quotient, then check it: multiply the quotient by the divisor to get back the starting number.
  7. A studio engineer used 4 feet of tape. That was 1/5 of the whole roll. How long is the whole roll?
    • 4/5 foot
    • 1/20 foot
    • 9 feet
    • 20 feet

4. Detective work

Somebody made a mistake. Find the step, then decide who is right.

Alguien cometió un error. Encuentra el paso y decide quién tiene razón.

  1. Rosa wants to know how many 1/3-foot pieces fit into a 7-foot cable. Tap the step where her work goes wrong.
  2. Kai finds 5 ÷ 1/6, the number of 1/6-foot pieces in a 5-foot cable. Tap the step where his work first goes wrong.
  3. Mira shares 4/5 of a pan of brownies equally between 2 friends. Tap the step where her work first goes wrong.
  4. Cy says 8 ÷ 4 is greater than 8 ÷ 1/4, because 4 is the bigger divisor. Dee says 8 ÷ 1/4 is greater. Who is right?
    • Dee, because 1/4-size pieces fit many more times into 8
    • Cy, because a bigger divisor gives a bigger quotient
    • Both quotients are equal
    • Neither, because you cannot divide by a fraction

5. Boss level

Tell us how sure you are, then solve. Finish with a problem of your own.

Dinos qué tan seguro estás y luego resuelve. Termina con un problema tuyo.

  1. Which situation is modeled by 9 ÷ 1/3?
    • One third of a 9-foot cable
    • A 9-foot cable shared equally by 3 people
    • How many 1/3-foot pieces can be cut from 9 feet of cable
    • A 9-foot cable with 1/3 foot cut off
  2. The studio has 7/8 of a gallon of paint and pours it equally into 2 cans. How many gallons are in each can?
  3. Which of these is greater than 10?
    • 10 ÷ 2
    • 10 ÷ 1/2
    • 1/2 ÷ 10
    • 10 ÷ 5
  4. A mixing board uses 1/3 of a tube of lubricant on 4 equal sliders. What fraction of the whole tube does each slider get?
  5. 12 ÷ 1/4 = 48. Which multiplication checks this answer?
    • 12 × 4 = 48
    • 48 × 4 = 192
    • 48 × 1/4 = 12
    • 12 × 1/4 = 3
  6. Create your own Sound Studio problem that is solved by dividing a whole number by a unit fraction, or a fraction by a whole number. Write the division, solve it, and explain what the answer means.
