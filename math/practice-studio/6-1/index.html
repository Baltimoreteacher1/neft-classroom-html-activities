  2. What fraction of the bar is shaded?

  3. A whole is cut into equal fourths. How many fourths make 1 whole?
  4. What is 5 × 1/3?

    • 3/5
  5. Which multiplication can you use to check that 12 ÷ 3 = 4?

3. Practice at grade level

Show the full idea. Tables, mistakes to find, and problems that need a few steps.

Muestra la idea completa. Tablas, errores por encontrar y problemas de varios pasos.

  1. Match each division expression to its quotient.
  2. A shelf is 5 feet long. Books are each 1/4 foot wide. How many books fit on the shelf? Which expression represents this?
    • 1/4 ÷ 5 = 1/20 book
    • 5 ÷ 1/4 = 20 books
    • 5 × 1/4 = 1 1/4 books
    • 5 + 1/4 = 5 1/4 books
  3. Match each fraction ÷ whole number expression to its quotient.
  4. A 5/6-mile stretch of the evidence trail is split equally among 3 search teams. How much of a mile does each team walk?
    • 1/18 mile
    • 5/2 miles
    • 5/6 mile
    • 5/18 mile
  5. Spot the Common Mistake
  6. Agent Reyes has used 6 feet of evidence tape, and that is 1/2 of a full roll. How many feet of tape are on a full roll? (6 ÷ 1/2 asks how much is in ONE whole roll.)
    • 3 feet
    • 12 feet
    • 6 1/2 feet
    • 1/12 foot
  7. What is the best next move?
    • Count halves per whole; use 11 wholes.
    • Multiply 11 × 1/2.
    • Divide 11 by 2 to make 2 equal groups.
    • Subtract 1/2 from 11.
