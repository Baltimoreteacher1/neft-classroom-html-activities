Word bankBanco de palabras
mean absolute deviation (MAD)desviación absoluta media (DAM)
The average distance of the data values from the mean. It describes spread.La distancia promedio de los valores a la media. Describe la dispersión.
deviationdesviación
How far a value is from the mean: value − mean. It can be negative.Qué tan lejos está un valor de la media: valor − media. Puede ser negativa.
absolute valuevalor absoluto
The distance of a number from zero. It is never negative.La distancia de un número a cero. Nunca es negativo.
spreaddispersión
How far apart the data values are.Qué tan separados están los valores de los datos.
meanmedia
The sum of the values divided by how many values there are.La suma de los valores dividida entre cuántos valores hay.

  1. 6 fresh problems with new numbers each time (interactive only).

2. Multi-step missions

Real situations that need more than one step: plan, solve, then check that your answer makes sense.

Situaciones reales que necesitan más de un paso: planea, resuelve y revisa que tu respuesta tenga sentido.

  1. Find the MAD Error
  2. Two basketball teams both average 75 points per game. Team A's last 5 scores: 73, 76, 74, 77, 75. Team B's last 5 scores: 60, 90, 65, 85, 75. Calculate the MAD for each team and explain which team a coach would prefer if they need predictable scoring.
  3. Five tracks in the Sound Studio last 12, 15, 9, 18, 11 minutes. Find the mean, the distance of each track from the mean, and the MAD.
  4. Two studio groups practiced for 4 days. Group A played 9, 9, 11 and 11 minutes. Group B played 4, 8, 12 and 16 minutes. Both groups have a mean of 10 minutes. Which statement is true?
    • Group B is more spread out: its MAD is 4 minutes and Group A’s is 1
    • The groups are equally spread out because their means are equal
    • Group A is more spread out: its MAD is 1 and Group B’s is 4
    • Group B is more spread out because 16 is greater than 11
  5. A MAD uses 4 values. Three of the values are 1, 5 and 2 minutes from the mean. The MAD is 3 minutes. How far from the mean is the fourth value?
  6. Each dot is one drum track’s length in minutes. The mean is 6. Which is the best estimate of the MAD?
    • 6
    • 1
    • 4
    • 0

3. Build it, move it, test it

Use the tools to build the idea. Then check it.

Usa las herramientas para construir la idea. Luego compruébala.

  1. Find the MAD of each data set in your head. Then sort the sets by the size of the MAD.
  2. 2 fresh problems with new numbers each time (interactive only).

4. Detective work

Somebody made a mistake. Find the step, then decide who is right.

Alguien cometió un error. Encuentra el paso y decide quién tiene razón.

  1. Imani finds the MAD of the beat counts 4, 10, 7, 11 and 8. The mean is 8.
  2. Jun finds the MAD of the volume settings 1, 2, 3 and 10. The median is 2.5 and the mean is 4.
  3. Cleo finds the MAD of the amp levels 4, 8, 12 and 8. The mean is 8.
  4. A data set has a MAD of 0. Sam says all the values are equal. Rae says the mean must be 0. Who is right?
    • Rae, because MAD and mean are the same number
    • Both, because a mean of 0 makes all values equal
    • Sam, because every distance from the mean must be 0
    • Neither, because a MAD is never 0

5. Boss level

Tell us how sure you are, then solve. Finish with a problem of your own.

Dinos qué tan seguro estás y luego resuelve. Termina con un problema tuyo.

  1. Six speakers play 14, 18, 20, 12, 16, 22 watts. What is the MAD?
  2. Studio X has song lengths with a MAD of 2.5 minutes. Studio Y has song lengths with a MAD of 6 minutes. Which statement is correct?
    • Studio Y’s song lengths are more spread out
    • Studio X’s song lengths are more spread out
    • Studio Y’s songs are longer
    • The two studios have the same spread
  3. A drummer plays 40, 50, 60, 50 beats per measure. What is the MAD?
  4. The MAD of a band’s practice times is 4 minutes. What does that tell you?
    • Every practice time is exactly 4 minutes from the mean
    • The practice times are, on average, about 4 minutes away from the mean
    • The mean practice time is 4 minutes
    • The longest time is 4 minutes more than the shortest
  5. A volume level is 7 units away from a mean of 12. Which values could it be?
    • Only 19
    • −7 or 7
    • Only 5
    • 5 or 19
  6. Create your own problem. Write a data set of 4 or 5 numbers about a Sound Studio, such as song lengths. Find the mean and the MAD, showing each distance. Explain what the MAD tells you about the spread. Use the words mean absolute deviation, deviation, absolute value and spread.
