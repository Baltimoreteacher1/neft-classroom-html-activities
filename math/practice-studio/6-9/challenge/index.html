Cada peldaño es más difícil que el anterior y los números cambian cada vez.

  1. 6 fresh problems with new numbers each time (interactive only).

2. Multi-step missions

Real situations that need more than one step: plan, solve, then check that your answer makes sense.

Situaciones reales que necesitan más de un paso: planea, resuelve y revisa que tu respuesta tenga sentido.

  1. Find the Division Error
  2. A pizza shop has 8 whole pizzas. Each serving is 2/3 of a pizza. How many servings can they make? Write and solve an equation, then explain why the answer is greater than 8.
  3. The rain barrel holds 10 gallons. The garden club keeps 2 gallons in reserve and pours the rest into watering cans that each hold 2/5 gallon. How many full cans can they fill?
  4. A gardener used 9 liters of compost to fill the shaded part of the trench bar, which is 3/5 of the trench. How many liters would fill the whole trench?
    • 5 2/5 liters
    • 45 liters
    • 3 liters
    • 15 liters
  5. The club has 5 meters of hose. Each tomato row needs 5/6 meter of hose. Each herb row needs 1/6 meter of hose. How many more herb rows than tomato rows can the 5 meters supply?
  6. Sofia has 5 cups of chopped cilantro. Each jar of salsa uses 2/3 cup. She fills as many whole jars as she can. How many cups of cilantro are left over?

4. Detective work

Somebody made a mistake. Find the step, then decide who is right.

Alguien cometió un error. Encuentra el paso y decide quién tiene razón.

  1. Tavi divided 12 by 3/8 to find how many 3/8-liter scoops fill 12 liters of soil. Tap the step where his work goes wrong.
  2. Mina worked out how many 5/6-pound seed packs she can make from 4 pounds of seed. Tap the step where her work goes wrong.
  3. How many 2/3-cup scoops are in 6 cups of seed? Ola solved it. Tap the step where her thinking goes wrong.
  4. Kira says 6 ÷ 1/2 must be 3, because dividing always makes a number smaller. Mateo says it is 12, because there are 2 halves in each whole. Who is right?
    • Kira: dividing always makes a number smaller.
    • Mateo: 2 halves fit in each whole; 6 × 2 = 12.
    • Both are right: the answer can be 3 or 12.
    • Neither: the answer is 6, because 6 ÷ 1 is 6.
  5. What is the best next move?
    • Stop here, because 12 is a whole number.
    • Check by multiplying 12 × 6/5.
    • Check: 12 × 5/6 = 10.
    • Check by dividing 12 by 5/6.
  6. 1 fresh problem with new numbers each time (interactive only).

5. Boss level

Tell us how sure you are, then solve. Finish with a problem of your own.

Dinos qué tan seguro estás y luego resuelve. Termina con un problema tuyo.

  1. What is 14 ÷ 7/9?
  2. Without finishing the calculations, which quotient is the greatest?
    • 15 ÷ 5/6
    • 15 ÷ 3/5
    • 15 ÷ 1/4
    • 15 ÷ 3/4
  3. Matías found that 18 ÷ 3/4 = 24. Which multiplication checks his answer?
    • 24 × 3/4 = 18
    • 18 × 3/4 = 13 1/2
    • 24 × 4/3 = 32
    • 18 × 24 = 432
  4. Which expression finds how many 1/6-pound bags of seed can be filled from 4 pounds of seed?
    • 1/6 ÷ 4
    • 4 × 1/6
    • 4 + 1/6
    • 4 ÷ 1/6
  5. Dara has 5 gallons of water. How many FULL 3/8-gallon jugs can she fill?
  6. Create your own Rooftop Garden problem that divides a whole number by a fraction. Solve it with the reciprocal, check it by multiplying, and explain why your answer is greater than the whole number.
