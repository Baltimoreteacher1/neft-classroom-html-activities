A storm rolls over the Rooftop Garden at night. The barrels are filling at different rates, and the graphs on the shed wall are smudged. Rebuild each one and decide which hose to trust.

Una tormenta pasa de noche sobre el Jardín de la Azotea. Los barriles se llenan a ritmos distintos y las gráficas de la pared del cobertizo están borrosas. Reconstruye cada una y decide en cuál manguera confiar.

1. Climb the skill ladder

Each rung is harder than the last, and the numbers change every time.

Cada peldaño es más difícil que el anterior y los números cambian cada vez.

  1. 6 fresh problems with new numbers each time (interactive only).

2. Multi-step missions

Real situations that need more than one step: plan, solve, then check that your answer makes sense.

Situaciones reales que necesitan más de un paso: planea, resuelve y revisa que tu respuesta tenga sentido.

  1. Nakai's savings account has a principal of $750 and earns 3.5% simple interest each year. How much interest does Nakai earn in one year?
    • $3.50
    • $26.25
    • $75.00
    • $262.50
  2. After 10 minutes, the Sandia Peak tram has traveled 12,000 feet. The whole ascent is 18,000 feet. How much farther must it travel to reach the top?
    • 1,200 feet
    • 5,000 feet
    • 6,000 feet
    • 12,000 feet
  3. A second tram travels 900 feet per minute. If both trams are graphed on the same axes (minutes vs. feet), how do the graphs compare?
    • The 900 ft/min line rises more steeply
    • The two lines are identical
    • The Sandia Peak line (1,200 ft/min) rises more steeply than the 900 ft/min line
    • The 900 ft/min line goes downward
  4. Nia's rain barrel fills at a steady rate. The graph shows these (hours, liters) points: (1, 8), (2, 16), (3, 24) and (4, 32). Which ordered pair is also on the graph?
    • (48, 6)
    • (6, 14)
    • (6, 48)
    • (6, 40)
  5. Hose A and hose B each fill a barrel at a steady rate. Hose A's points are (1, 4), (2, 8) and (3, 12). Hose B's points are (1, 7), (2, 14) and (3, 21). After 6 hours, how many more liters has hose B delivered than hose A?
  6. A sprinkler uses 12 liters of water every hour. Fill in each missing number, with hours as x and liters as y.
  7. A drip line delivers 7 liters every hour. On a graph of (hours, liters), which ordered pair does NOT fit this relationship?
    • (6, 40)
    • (3, 21)
    • (5, 35)
    • (9, 63)

3. Build it, move it, test it

Use the tools to build the idea. Then check it.

Usa las herramientas para construir la idea. Luego compruébala.

  1. A graph shows hours on the horizontal axis and liters on the vertical axis. Match each ordered pair to what it means.
  2. 2 fresh problems with new numbers each time (interactive only).

4. Detective work

Somebody made a mistake. Find the step, then decide who is right.

Alguien cometió un error. Encuentra el paso y decide quién tiene razón.

  1. Seed packets cost $4 each. Rosa plots the point for 3 packets on a graph with packets on the horizontal axis and cost on the vertical axis.
  2. A rain gauge collects 5 liters every hour. Kofi wants the point for 3 hours, using the first row: 1 hour, 5 liters.
  3. A garden graph has hours on the horizontal axis and liters on the vertical axis. Each grid line going up is worth 5 liters. Linh reads a point that is 4 hours across and 3 grid lines up.
  4. Barrel A gains 4 liters every hour and barrel B gains 6 liters every hour. Both graphs start at (0, 0) and use the same axes. Amir says B's graph is steeper. Ines says A's graph is steeper because 4 comes first. Who is right?
    • Ines: the rate that is written first is steeper.
    • Both are the same, because both graphs go up.
    • Neither: steepness depends on where the graph starts.
    • Amir: B gains more liters each hour, so its points climb faster.

5. Boss level

Tell us how sure you are, then solve. Finish with a problem of your own.

Dinos qué tan seguro estás y luego resuelve. Termina con un problema tuyo.

  1. On a graph, minutes are on the horizontal axis and liters are on the vertical axis. Which ordered pair shows 12 liters after 3 minutes?
    • (12, 3)
    • (3, 12)
    • (3, 4)
    • (3, 15)
  2. A graph of a steady relationship goes through the origin and the point (4, 28). What is the y-value when x is 9?
  3. The graph shows the points (1, 6), (2, 12), (3, 18) and (4, 24) for liters of water over hours. What is the rate of change?
    • 24 liters per hour
    • 4 liters per hour
    • 1 liter per hour
    • 6 liters per hour
  4. Hose C's graph goes through the origin and the point (5, 35). Hose D adds 2 more liters every hour than hose C, and also starts at the origin. How many liters has hose D delivered after 5 hours?
  5. An empty barrel starts to fill at a steady rate. Why does its graph of (hours, liters) include the point (0, 0)?
    • Every graph has to start at (0, 0).
    • After 0 hours the barrel holds 0 liters, so (0, 0) fits the relationship.
    • The origin is where the axes are labeled.
    • The first hour always has 0 liters.
  6. Choose a garden situation with a steady rate, such as liters per hour or dollars per packet. Give three ordered pairs for your graph, say what one point means in words, and explain what the rate of change tells you.
