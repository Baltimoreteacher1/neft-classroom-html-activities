1. Warm up your thinking

Quick questions that wake up what you already know for this lesson.

Preguntas rápidas para activar lo que ya sabes de esta lección.

  1. Which inequality represents: 'You must be at least 48 inches tall to ride'?
    • h > 48
    • h ≥ 48
    • h < 48
    • h ≤ 48
  2. Which inequality represents: 'A store can hold no more than 75 people'?
    • p < 75
    • p ≥ 75
    • p ≤ 75
    • p > 75
  3. Which inequality represents: 'The speed limit is more than 25 mph'?
    • s ≥ 25
    • s < 25
    • s > 25
    • s = 25
  4. Which inequality represents: 'The temperature is less than 40 degrees'?
    • t > 40
    • t ≤ 40
    • t < 40
    • t = 40
