Night falls on the steep part of the trail. The guides have scribbled equations on the trail signs, and every number has to be found with the right inverse operation before the group camps.

Cae la noche en la parte empinada del sendero. Los guías garabatearon ecuaciones en los letreros, y cada número debe hallarse con la operación inversa correcta antes de que el grupo acampe.

1. Climb the skill ladder

Each rung is harder than the last, and the numbers change every time.

Cada peldaño es más difícil que el anterior y los números cambian cada vez.

  1. 6 fresh problems with new numbers each time (interactive only).

2. Multi-step missions

Real situations that need more than one step: plan, solve, then check that your answer makes sense.

Situaciones reales que necesitan más de un paso: planea, resuelve y revisa que tu respuesta tenga sentido.

  1. Find the Equation Error
  2. A bakery packed muffins into boxes of 8. They made 96 muffins total. Write an equation, solve it, and explain why your answer is reasonable. Then write a different situation that uses division and has the same answer.
  3. Anjali's group carries 9 identical packs of trail mix that weigh 45 pounds in all. She writes 9w = 45, where w is the weight of one pack. What is her step, and what is w?
    • Multiply both sides by 9, so w = 405
    • Subtract 9 from both sides, so w = 36
    • Add 9 to both sides, so w = 54
    • Divide both sides by 9, so w = 5
  4. A guide splits a trail into 6 equal sections. Each section is 2.5 miles long. The equation is t ÷ 6 = 2.5, where t is the length of the whole trail in miles. What is t?
  5. Solve each equation for k. First type the number you use on both sides, then type k.
  6. Two equations are on the trail sign: 4a = 18 and b ÷ 4 = 18. Which pair of solutions is correct?
    • a = 4.5 and b = 72
    • a = 72 and b = 4.5
    • a = 4.5 and b = 4.5
    • a = 72 and b = 72

3. Build it, move it, test it

Use the tools to build the idea. Then check it.

Usa las herramientas para construir la idea. Luego compruébala.

  1. Match each equation to the step that gets the variable alone.
  2. 2 fresh problems with new numbers each time (interactive only).

4. Detective work

Somebody made a mistake. Find the step, then decide who is right.

Alguien cometió un error. Encuentra el paso y decide quién tiene razón.

  1. Luis solves 7x = 56.
  2. Chen solves 8n = 4.
  3. Mika solves d ÷ 6 = 4.
  4. To solve 2.5y = 10, Salma divides both sides by 2.5 and gets y = 4. Ivan says you cannot divide by a decimal, so he multiplies both sides by 2.5 and gets y = 25. Who is right?
    • Ivan, because decimals only work with multiplication.
    • Salma, because dividing by 2.5 undoes multiplying by 2.5.
    • Both, because both answers use 2.5.
    • Neither, because y must be a whole number.

5. Boss level

Tell us how sure you are, then solve. Finish with a problem of your own.

Dinos qué tan seguro estás y luego resuelve. Termina con un problema tuyo.

  1. Which equation has w = 12 as its solution?
    • 3w = 4
    • w ÷ 3 = 36
    • w ÷ 3 = 4
    • 5w = 17
  2. Solve 0.4n = 6.
  3. Which equation is solved by subtracting 6 from both sides?
    • x ÷ 6 = 30
    • x + 6 = 30
    • 6x = 30
    • x − 6 = 30
  4. The summit is 3 times as high as base camp. The summit is 4,800 feet high. With b for the height of base camp in feet, the equation is 3b = 4800. How high is base camp?
  5. Mika solved k ÷ 9 = 7 and got k = 63. Which check proves her answer?
    • 63 ÷ 9 = 7
    • 63 − 9 = 7
    • 63 × 9 = 7
    • 7 ÷ 9 = 63
  6. Write your own trail problem that fits an equation like ax = b or x ÷ a = b. Solve it by using the inverse operation on both sides, and check your answer in the original equation.
