1. Climb the skill ladder

Each rung is harder than the last, and the numbers change every time.

Cada peldaño es más difícil que el anterior y los números cambian cada vez.

  1. 6 fresh problems with new numbers each time (interactive only).

2. Multi-step missions

Real situations that need more than one step: plan, solve, then check that your answer makes sense.

Situaciones reales que necesitan más de un paso: planea, resuelve y revisa que tu respuesta tenga sentido.

  1. Find the Box Plot Error
  2. Two basketball teams played 9 games each. Team A: 40, 42, 44, 46, 48, 50, 52, 54, 56. Team B: 30, 35, 40, 45, 48, 50, 55, 60, 65. Find the five-number summary and IQR for each team. Which team is more consistent? Which has a wider spread in the middle 50%?
  3. Match each box plot feature with what it tells you about the data.
  4. Corner Kitchen timed 9 cakes in the oven. Minutes: 38, 25, 31, 44, 29, 35, 41, 27, 33. Order the times, then fill in the five-number summary and the IQR. Use this rule: Q1 and Q3 are the medians of the lower and upper halves, and the median is left out of both halves when the count is odd.
  5. The box plot shows the prep times of 40 dishes. About how many dishes took longer than 36 minutes?
  6. The box plots show the ticket times, in minutes, for two stations. Both stations have the same median. Which statement is correct?
    • The Grill is slower because its box sits higher
    • The Salad station’s middle half is more spread out: its IQR is 18 minutes and the Grill’s is 8
    • The two stations have equal spread because their medians match
    • The Grill has the larger IQR: 8 minutes
  7. The box plot shows a five-number summary for 8 order times.
    • 3, 5, 6, 8, 8, 12, 14, 15
    • 3, 4, 6, 8, 8, 10, 13, 15
    • 3, 4, 6, 7, 9, 11, 13, 15
    • 3, 4, 5, 7, 9, 12, 12, 15

3. Build it, move it, test it

Use the tools to build the idea. Then check it.

Usa las herramientas para construir la idea. Luego compruébala.

  1. Sort each description by the part of a box plot it describes.
  2. 2 fresh problems with new numbers each time (interactive only).

4. Detective work

Somebody made a mistake. Find the step, then decide who is right.

Alguien cometió un error. Encuentra el paso y decide quién tiene razón.

  1. Yusuf finds Q1 and Q3 for the 7 cooking times 2, 5, 6, 8, 9, 11, 14, which are already in order. Use the rule that the median is left out of both halves when the count is odd.
  2. Mina’s box plot has minimum 6, Q1 14, median 20, Q3 27 and maximum 41. She finds the IQR.
  3. Dev reads a box plot of delivery times. His claim: the long right whisker shows that most of the data are on the right.
  4. These box plots show lunch-rush times for two kitchens. Selam says Kitchen B has the longer box, so it has more spread. Lucía says Kitchen B has times that are all higher but the same spread. Who is right?
    • Lucía, because both boxes have the same length and B is shifted higher
    • Selam, because Kitchen B’s box is farther to the right
    • Neither, because the whiskers are different
    • Both, because Kitchen B is higher and longer

5. Boss level

Tell us how sure you are, then solve. Finish with a problem of your own.

Dinos qué tan seguro estás y luego resuelve. Termina con un problema tuyo.

  1. Ten soups were timed in minutes: 14, 9, 22, 17, 12, 30, 19, 11, 25, 16. What is the IQR? Use this rule: Q1 and Q3 are the medians of the lower and upper halves, and the median is left out of both halves when the count is odd.
  2. The box plot shows the weights of 80 bread loaves. About what fraction of the loaves weigh between the median and the maximum?
    • One quarter
    • One half
    • Three quarters
    • All of them
  3. A box plot of oven temperatures has an IQR of 14 degrees and Q3 = 34. What is Q1?
  4. Which five numbers are needed to draw a box plot?
    • Mean, median, mode, range, IQR
    • Minimum, mean, median, mode, maximum
    • Q1, Q2, Q3, IQR, range
    • Minimum, Q1, median, Q3, maximum
  5. The box plot shows the cooking times of a menu. What is the range of the times?
  6. Create your own problem. Write 9 or 10 cooking times for a kitchen, find the five-number summary and the IQR, and describe what your plot would look like. Use the words box plot, median, quartile and interquartile range.
