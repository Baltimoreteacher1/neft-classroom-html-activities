independent variablevariable independiente
The quantity that is chosen or set. It is the input.La cantidad que se elige o se fija. Es la entrada.
dependent variablevariable dependiente
The quantity that changes in response to the independent variable. It is the output.La cantidad que cambia en respuesta a la variable independiente. Es la salida.
depends ondepende de
One quantity depends on another when changing the first one changes the second. Say it with the responder first: 'The cost depends on the number.'Una cantidad depende de otra cuando cambiar la primera cambia la segunda. Dilo empezando por la que responde: «El costo depende del número».
quantitycantidad

1. Climb the skill ladder

Each rung is harder than the last, and the numbers change every time.

Cada peldaño es más difícil que el anterior y los números cambian cada vez.

  1. 6 fresh problems with new numbers each time (interactive only).

2. Multi-step missions

Real situations that need more than one step: plan, solve, then check that your answer makes sense.

Situaciones reales que necesitan más de un paso: planea, resuelve y revisa que tu respuesta tenga sentido.

  1. Two quantities are related. Which statement is ALWAYS true?
    • The independent variable is always the larger number
    • The independent variable is the input used to describe the relationship
    • The dependent variable is always measured in dollars
    • The independent variable is always listed first in a table
  2. Give an example where the SAME quantity can be independent in one problem and dependent in another.
    • Time is always independent
    • Time: chosen when you pick how long to jog, but measured as a result when you run a set distance
    • Time is always dependent
    • A quantity can never switch roles
  3. The pizza shop spends $1.15 on cheese per pizza. Which weekly total is correct if it makes 200 pizzas?
    • $115
    • $201.15
    • $230
    • $1,150
  4. Chef Imani needs 96 muffins for a catering order. Each tray makes 12 muffins, and she decides how many trays to bake. Which statement names the variables correctly?
    • The number of muffins is independent, because the order says 96.
    • The number of trays is independent, and the number of muffins depends on it.
    • Both are independent, because Imani knows both numbers.
    • The number 12 is the independent variable.
  5. Corner Kitchen serves the same number of bowls from every pot of soup. Use the first row to find the rule, then fill in the missing amounts.
  6. A cook decides how much rice to make, then measures the water it needs. The table shows what he used. Which statement describes the relationship?
    • The rice depends on the water: every cup of water needs 2 cups of rice.
    • The water depends on the rice: there is always 1 more cup of water than rice.
    • Neither depends on the other, because both numbers get bigger.
    • The water depends on the rice: every cup of rice needs 2 cups of water.
  7. Chef Tomás lets a pot of stock simmer. The amount of stock drops by the same amount every 5 minutes, as the table shows. How many liters will be in the pot after 20 minutes?

3. Build it, move it, test it

Use the tools to build the idea. Then check it.

Usa las herramientas para construir la idea. Luego compruébala.

  1. Corner Kitchen sells smoothies for $5 each. A customer decides how many to order. Sort each item: is it the independent variable, the dependent variable, or a fixed amount?
  2. Invent a weather story with two quantities, such as hours and rainfall. Write one question that asks for the independent variable and one that asks for the dependent variable. Solve both with a few input and output pairs.
  3. 4 fresh problems with new numbers each time (interactive only).

4. Detective work

Somebody made a mistake. Find the step, then decide who is right.

Alguien cometió un error. Encuentra el paso y decide quién tiene razón.

  1. Mia is describing a catering order. Corner Kitchen charges $6 for each sandwich, and Mia decides how many sandwiches to order.
  2. Corner Kitchen has 4 ovens. Each oven bakes 9 muffins every hour, and the chef decides how many hours to bake. Hana names the variables.
  3. A table shows batches of jam and jars filled: 1 batch, 8 jars; 2 batches, 16 jars; 3 batches, 24 jars. The cook decides how many batches to make. Ahmed names the variables.
  4. Chef Hana bakes loaves of bread. She decides how many bags of flour to open, and then she counts how many loaves she can make. Leilani says the bags of flour are the independent variable. Dmitri says the loaves are independent, because loaves are what the customers want. Who is right?
    • Leilani: Hana chooses the bags, and the number of loaves depends on them.
    • Dmitri: the quantity the customers care about is independent.
    • Both are right, because the bags and the loaves depend on each other equally.
    • Neither: the size of a flour bag is the independent variable.
  5. What is the best next move?
    • Call the temperature independent, because it is what the scientist wants to find out.
    • Try the sentence "the ___ depends on the ___" both ways and keep the one that makes sense.
    • Say time is not a variable, because the scientist does not change it.
    • Say the two quantities do not affect each other.

5. Boss level

Tell us how sure you are, then solve. Finish with a problem of your own.

Dinos qué tan seguro estás y luego resuelve. Termina con un problema tuyo.

  1. A baker uses 6 apples for each pie and decides how many pies to bake. Which sentence is correct?
    • The number of pies baked depends on the number of apples used.
    • The number 6 depends on the number of pies.
    • The number of apples used depends on the number of pies baked.
    • The apples and the pies do not depend on each other.
  2. A bakery bakes the same number of cookies on every tray, as the table shows. How many trays does the bakery need to bake 144 cookies?
  3. A food truck sells tacos for $3 each and is open 8 hours a day. A customer decides how many tacos to buy. Which two quantities are the variables in the relationship between tacos and cost?
    • The number of tacos bought and the total cost
    • The price of one taco and the number of tacos bought
    • The hours the truck is open and the total cost
    • The price of one taco and the hours the truck is open
  4. A kitchen adds the same number of scoops of spice for each bag of rice, as the table shows. How many scoops go with ONE bag?
  5. Priya tests one oven in two experiments. In Experiment 1 she chooses how many minutes the oven heats and measures its temperature. In Experiment 2 she chooses a temperature and measures how many minutes the oven needs to reach it. Which statement is true?
    • Minutes is independent in both experiments, because time always comes first.
    • Minutes is independent in Experiment 1 and dependent in Experiment 2.
    • Temperature is independent in both experiments, because Priya cares about it.
    • Minutes is dependent in Experiment 1 and independent in Experiment 2.
  6. Think of a relationship from a kitchen, a restaurant or a bakery. Name the two quantities, say which one is independent and which one is dependent, and write the sentence '___ depends on ___'. Then give two examples of an input and its output.
