independent variablevariable independiente
The quantity that is chosen or set. It is the input.La cantidad que se elige o se fija. Es la entrada.
dependent variablevariable dependiente
The quantity that changes in response to the independent variable. It is the output.La cantidad que cambia en respuesta a la variable independiente. Es la salida.
depends ondepende de
One quantity depends on another when changing the first one changes the second. Say it with the responder first: 'The cost depends on the number.'Una cantidad depende de otra cuando cambiar la primera cambia la segunda. Dilo empezando por la que responde: «El costo depende del número».
quantitycantidad
Something that can be counted or measured, like 12 muffins or 5 liters.Algo que se puede contar o medir, como 12 muffins o 5 litros.

A list of matched input and output pairs, one pair in each row.Una lista de pares de entrada y salida que se corresponden, un par en cada fila.

Corner Kitchen is open, and every order hides a relationship. The number of trays you bake changes how many muffins come out. Decide which quantity you choose and which quantity responds before the next order comes in.

Corner Kitchen está abierta, y cada pedido esconde una relación. El número de bandejas que horneas cambia cuántos muffins salen. Decide cuál cantidad eliges y cuál cantidad responde antes de que llegue el siguiente pedido.

Preguntas rápidas para activar lo que ya sabes de esta lección.

    • 4
    • 2
  2. Each batch of dough needs 3 cups of flour. Fill in the missing amounts.
  3. Two bags hold 10 apples. Three bags hold 15 apples. Every bag holds the same number of apples. How many apples are in ONE bag?

2. Practice with support

Start here. Use the hints whenever you need them; every wrong answer tells you why.

Empieza aquí. Usa las pistas cuando las necesites; cada respuesta incorrecta te dice por qué.

  1. Akela chooses how many hours of sunlight each tray gets. Which is the INDEPENDENT variable?
    • Plant height
    • Hours of sunlight
    • Number of trays
    • Number of seeds
  2. In Akela's experiment, which is the DEPENDENT variable?
    • Hours of sunlight
    • The kind of seed
    • The size of the tray
    • Plant height
  3. Claire burned 123 Calories on a 30-minute jog. Which quantity is subject to choice?
    • Calories she burns
    • Her heart rate
    • Minutes she jogs
    • The weather
  4. Which sentence is written in the correct order?
    • Minutes jogged depends on Calories burned.
    • Calories burned depends on Calories burned.
    • Neither quantity depends on the other.
    • Calories burned depends on minutes jogged.
  5. A tray that got 2 hours of sun grew to 4 cm. A tray that got 6 hours grew to 12 cm. As sunlight increases, plant height…
    • decreases
    • stays the same
    • increases
    • cannot be determined
  6. You buy tickets at $45 each. Which quantity depends on the other?
    • Total cost depends on number of tickets
    • Number of tickets depends on total cost
    • The price per ticket depends on the total cost
    • Nothing depends on anything here
  7. Sort each quantity: is it the independent variable (the input you choose) or the dependent variable (the output that responds)?

3. Practice at grade level

Show the full idea. Tables, mistakes to find, and problems that need a few steps.

Muestra la idea completa. Tablas, errores por encontrar y problemas de varios pasos.

  1. A gym charges $24.95 per month. Which pair correctly names the variables?
    • Independent: months; Dependent: total cost
    • Independent: total cost; Dependent: months
    • Independent: $24.95; Dependent: months
    • Independent: months; Dependent: $24.95
  2. Claire jogs 30 minutes and burns 123 Calories. At the same rate, how many Calories would a 90-minute jog burn?
    • 246
    • 300
    • 369
    • 410
  3. Which of these pairs is NOT a dependent relationship?
    • Number of pizzas sold and weekly cost of cheese
    • Number of pizzas sold and number of employees the shop has
    • Hours jogged and Calories burned
    • Tickets bought and total cost
  4. A plant grows 2 cm for every hour of daily sunlight. Which table row is correct?
    • 4 hours to 8 cm
    • 4 hours to 6 cm
    • 4 hours to 2 cm
    • 4 hours to 4 cm
  5. Which question would you ask to find the DEPENDENT variable?
    • Which quantity responds when the other one changes?
    • Which quantity is larger?
    • Which quantity has units?
    • Which quantity comes first alphabetically?
  6. Claire decides to walk instead of jog for the same 30 minutes. What happens to the two variables?
    • Calories burned becomes the independent variable
    • Neither is a variable any more
    • Time stays the independent variable; Calories burned still depends on it
    • Time becomes the dependent variable
  7. If Claire runs exactly 3 miles and records how long it takes, which is the independent variable now?
    • Time it takes
    • Her speed
    • Distance (3 miles)
    • Calories burned
