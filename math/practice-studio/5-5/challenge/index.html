4. Detective work

Somebody made a mistake. Find the step, then decide who is right.

Alguien cometió un error. Encuentra el paso y decide quién tiene razón.

  1. An evidence box is 5 in long, 3 in wide and 4 in tall. Carla finds its volume. Tap the first step where her work goes wrong.
  2. A crate is 4 ft long, 3 ft wide and 5 ft tall. Hugo finds its volume. Tap the first step where his work goes wrong.
  3. A case box is 11 in long, 5 in wide and 4 in tall. Jules finds its volume. Tap the first step where his work goes wrong.
  4. Box P is 2 in by 6 in by 6 in. Box Q is 3 in by 4 in by 6 in. Tomás says Box P holds more because it has two long edges. Lin says they hold the same amount. Who is right?
    • Tomás, because longer edges mean more volume.
    • Neither. Box Q holds more because it has more different edges.
    • Neither. Different shapes can never hold the same amount.
    • Lin. Both boxes have a volume of 72 in³.
