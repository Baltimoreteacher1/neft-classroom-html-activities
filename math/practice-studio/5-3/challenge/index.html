4. Detective work

Somebody made a mistake. Find the step, then decide who is right.

Alguien cometió un error. Encuentra el paso y decide quién tiene razón.

  1. A reef ramp is a trapezoid with bases of 9 m and 15 m, a height of 4 m and a sloping side of 5 m. Rosa finds its area. Tap the first step where her work goes wrong.
  2. A trapezoid window for the reef lab has bases of 12 ft and 8 ft and a height of 7 ft. Kai finds its area. Tap the first step where his work goes wrong.
  3. A trapezoid has an area of 60 m², a height of 6 m and one base of 8 m. Noa finds the other base. Tap the first step where her work goes wrong.
  4. A trapezoid has bases of 4 m and 10 m and a height of 5 m. Lola says you can multiply the average of the two bases by the height to get the area. Pablo says that only works for rectangles. Who is right?
    • Pablo. Averages only work for rectangles.
    • Neither. You must multiply the two bases together.
    • Lola, but only if the trapezoid has a right angle.
    • Lola. The average of the bases is 7, and 7 × 5 = 35, which matches ½ × (4 + 10) × 5.
