  3. Team A's salaries have a range of $14M and an IQR of $7M. Team B's have a range of $7M and an IQR of $2.5M. Both teams have the same median salary. What does this tell a player?
    • Team B pays more overall
    • Team B's salaries cluster much more tightly around the median
    • Team A has more players
    • Team B's highest salary is greater
  4. Find the error
  5. Class A's test scores are shown on a dot plot. Most dots stack between 80 and 90, and a few dots trail off to the left at 52, 58, and 61. Which word describes the SHAPE of this distribution?
    • Skewed left — the long tail points toward the lower scores
    • Skewed right — the long tail points toward the higher scores
    • Symmetric — the left and right sides match
    • Uniform — every score appears about equally often
  6. Class B's nine retest scores are 72, 76, 78, 80, 80, 80, 82, 84, 88. Which statement describes the center, spread, AND shape of this distribution?

    • Center about 16, range 80, roughly symmetric
    • Center about 80, range 16, skewed right because 88 is the greatest score
  7. Both classes have a median score of 81. Class A's scores make a symmetric dot plot. Class B's scores pile up between 78 and 86, with one score of 52. Which statement about Class B is true?

    • Class B's distribution is skewed right because 52 is far away from the other scores
    • Class B's distribution is skewed left: the single low score of 52 makes a tail toward the lower scores
