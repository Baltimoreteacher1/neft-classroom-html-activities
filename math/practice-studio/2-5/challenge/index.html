  2. Two students both scored 80 on a make-up test — one joins Class A (range 28, IQR 13) and one joins Class B (range 20, IQR 8). Both classes have a median of 81. Predict how each class's IQR changes, and explain your reasoning.
  3. The rooftop garden measured 11 sunflowers. Heights in centimeters: 48, 62, 55, 71, 39, 66, 58, 44, 80, 52, 60. Fill in each measure. Use this rule: Q1 and Q3 are the medians of the lower and upper halves, and the median is left out of both halves when the count is odd.
  4. The box plots show the weights, in grams, of tomatoes from two garden beds. Both beds have a median of 60 grams. Which statement is true?
    • Bed A has both the larger range and the larger IQR
    • Bed B has both the larger range and the larger IQR
    • Bed B has the larger range (80 grams), but Bed A has the larger IQR (16 grams)
    • The two beds are equally spread out because their medians match
  5. A garden crew measured 8 bean pods (cm): 12, 14, 15, 15, 17, 18, 19, 21. Then they found a 9th pod that is 45 cm long. Fill in the range and the IQR before and after the new pod. Use this rule: Q1 and Q3 are the medians of the lower and upper halves, and the median is left out of both halves when the count is odd.
  6. A garden bed’s data have a minimum of 6, Q1 = 18, an IQR of 14 and a range of 40. What is the maximum value?

4. Detective work

Somebody made a mistake. Find the step, then decide who is right.

Alguien cometió un error. Encuentra el paso y decide quién tiene razón.

  1. Ayesha has a data set with minimum 22, Q1 31, median 40, Q3 47 and maximum 60. She finds the range and the IQR.
  2. Bao finds the range of the seed counts 14, 9, 22, 6, 18, written in the order the pods were opened.
  3. Greta compares two herb beds. Bed 1: 10, 15, 20, 25, 30. Bed 2: 18, 19, 20, 21, 22.
  4. Snail lengths in millimeters are 12, 13, 13, 14, 15, 15, 16, 48. Hugo says the range, 36, best shows how spread out the typical snails are. Noa says the IQR, 2.5, is better because the 48 is far from the rest. Who is right? Use this rule: Q1 and Q3 are the medians of the lower and upper halves, and the median is left out of both halves when the count is odd.
    • Hugo, because the range uses every value
    • Hugo, because the range is always the smaller measure
    • Both are equally good because both use subtraction
    • Noa, because the IQR ignores the extreme 48

5. Boss level

Tell us how sure you are, then solve. Finish with a problem of your own.

Dinos qué tan seguro estás y luego resuelve. Termina con un problema tuyo.

  1. Seven pepper plants made 27, 14, 33, 9, 21, 38, 16 peppers. What is the range?
  2. Ten tomato plants have these ripe-tomato counts: 5, 8, 9, 11, 12, 14, 15, 19, 22, 30. They are already in order. What is the IQR? Use this rule: Q1 and Q3 are the medians of the lower and upper halves, and the median is left out of both halves when the count is odd.
  3. The box plots show the heights of plants from two seed packets. Which packet gives more even plants, with a smaller IQR?
    • Packet Y: its IQR is 6, compared with 20
    • Packet X: its range is 40
    • Neither, because both medians are 30
    • Packet X: its IQR is 20, the larger number
  4. Can the IQR of a data set be larger than its range?
    • Yes, if the data have an extreme value
    • No, because the middle half is only part of the whole spread
    • Yes, because Q3 + Q1 is bigger than the maximum
    • Yes, when the median is large
  5. A set of data has a range of 28 and a maximum of 45. What is the minimum?
  6. Create your own problem. Write two data sets of 7 or 8 numbers about two garden beds that have the same median but different spread. Find the range and the IQR of each, and explain which bed is more consistent. Use the words range, quartile, interquartile range and vary.
