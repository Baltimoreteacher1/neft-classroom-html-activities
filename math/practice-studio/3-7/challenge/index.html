4. Detective work

Somebody made a mistake. Find the step, then decide who is right.

Alguien cometió un error. Encuentra el paso y decide quién tiene razón.

  1. Aisha changes a 33-pound battery to kilograms. She uses 1 kg ≈ 2.2 lb. Tap the first step where her work goes wrong.
  2. Mateo needs a cable at least 30 cm long. The only cable in the bin is labeled 12 in. Use 1 in = 2.54 cm. Tap the first step that goes wrong.
  3. Ravi changes 8 liters of paint to quarts with the label 1 L ≈ 1.06 qt. Tap the first step that goes wrong.
  4. A test rig weighs 150 lb. Kofi changes it to kilograms. He multiplies by 0.45 (1 lb ≈ 0.45 kg) and gets 67.5 kg. Lena divides by 2.2 (1 kg ≈ 2.2 lb) and gets about 68.2 kg. Who is right?
    • Only Kofi. Multiplying is the only correct way.
    • Only Lena. Dividing is the only correct way.
    • Both. Each uses a rounded factor, so the answers are close but not identical.
    • Neither. The answers are different, so one of them must be a mistake.

5. Boss level

Tell us how sure you are, then solve. Finish with a problem of your own.

Dinos qué tan seguro estás y luego resuelve. Termina con un problema tuyo.

  1. A courier robot must drive 12 km to a partner lab in Canada. Use 1 km ≈ 0.6 mi. About how far is that in miles?
    • 7.2 mi
    • 12.6 mi
    • 20 mi
    • 72 mi
  2. Robot Lab's chart says 1 in = 2.54 cm is exact. The factors 1 mi ≈ 1.6 km, 1 kg ≈ 2.2 lb and 1 L ≈ 1.06 qt are rounded. Which sentence may use an equals sign?
    • 5 mi = 8 km
    • 5 kg = 11 lb
    • 5 in = 12.7 cm
    • 5 L = 5.3 qt
  3. Camila can run either test course at the lab. One course is 5 mi long and the other is 8 km long. Use 1 mi ≈ 1.6 km. Which course is longer?
    • The 8 km course, because 8 is greater than 5.
    • They are about the same length.
    • The 5 mi course, because a mile is a longer unit.
    • They cannot be compared because the units are different.
  4. A water tank at the lab holds 53 qt. Each robot test uses 2 L. Use 1 L ≈ 1.06 qt. How many full tests can the tank supply?
  5. A robot arm can lift at most 10 kg. In a lab test, a scale reads 24.2 lb for the load on the arm. Use 1 kg ≈ 2.2 lb. By how many kilograms is the load over the arm's limit?
