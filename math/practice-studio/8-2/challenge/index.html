The stadium lights are on and the stats crew has a stack of tricky scoreboards. The head coach wants every missing number solved, balanced on both sides, and checked before the game.

Las luces del estadio están encendidas y el equipo de estadísticas tiene una pila de marcadores difíciles. El entrenador quiere cada número faltante resuelto, equilibrado en los dos lados y comprobado antes del juego.

1. Climb the skill ladder

Each rung is harder than the last, and the numbers change every time.

Cada peldaño es más difícil que el anterior y los números cambian cada vez.

  1. 6 fresh problems with new numbers each time (interactive only).

2. Multi-step missions

Real situations that need more than one step: plan, solve, then check that your answer makes sense.

Situaciones reales que necesitan más de un paso: planea, resuelve y revisa que tu respuesta tenga sentido.

  1. Find the Equation Error
  2. A detective solved x + 25 = 60 and got x = 35. Another detective solved y − 25 = 60 and got y = 35. Are both correct? Explain using inverse operations and check each answer.
  3. Theo's team has scored 28 points and needs 50 to win. He writes 28 + p = 50, where p is the points still needed. Which step solves it, and what is p?
    • Add 28 to both sides, so p = 78
    • Subtract 50 from both sides, so p = −22
    • Subtract 28 from both sides, so p = 22
    • No step is needed, so p = 50
  4. Kwame's best long jump last season was 4.1 meters, which is 0.35 meters shorter than his best jump j this season. The equation is j − 0.35 = 4.1. How many meters is his jump this season?
  5. Solve each stat equation. Type the value of s.
  6. Two equations are on the coach's board: a + 9 = 30 and b − 9 = 30. Which statement about their solutions is true?
    • a = 21 and b = 39
    • a = 39 and b = 21
    • a = 21 and b = 21
    • a = 39 and b = 39

3. Build it, move it, test it

Use the tools to build the idea. Then check it.

Usa las herramientas para construir la idea. Luego compruébala.

  1. Match each equation to the first step that gets the variable alone.
  2. 2 fresh problems with new numbers each time (interactive only).

4. Detective work

Somebody made a mistake. Find the step, then decide who is right.

Alguien cometió un error. Encuentra el paso y decide quién tiene razón.

  1. Wen solves y − 9 = 24.
  2. Diego solves n + 12 = 35.
  3. Aaliyah solves p + 2.4 = 9.1.
  4. Beatriz and Hamza both found a solution for y − 6 = 14. Beatriz got y = 20 and checked 20 − 6 = 14. Hamza got y = 8 and checked 8 + 6 = 14. Whose check proves the solution?
    • Hamza's, because 8 + 6 = 14 is true.
    • Beatriz's, because 20 − 6 = 14 uses the original equation.
    • Both, because both checks end in 14.
    • Neither, because a check cannot prove a solution.

5. Boss level

Tell us how sure you are, then solve. Finish with a problem of your own.

Dinos qué tan seguro estás y luego resuelve. Termina con un problema tuyo.

  1. Which equation has m = 18 as its solution?
    • m + 7 = 11
    • m + 7 = 18
    • m − 7 = 11
    • m − 7 = 25
  2. Solve q + 3.6 = 10.2.
  3. Pita's relay team must swim 36 laps in all. They have done d laps and 15 laps are left. Which equation fits the story and gives the right value of d?
    • d + 15 = 36, so d = 21
    • d − 15 = 36, so d = 51
    • d + 15 = 36, so d = 51
    • d + 15 = 36, so d = 15
  4. Emre's team lost 9 yards on a play and is now at the 34-yard line. The equation is y − 9 = 34, where y is the line where the play started. What was y?
  5. Which equation has the greatest solution?
    • w + 4 = 20
    • w − 4 = 20
    • w + 14 = 20
    • w − 14 = 20
  6. Write your own sports problem that fits an equation like x + a = b or x − a = b. Solve it with an inverse operation on both sides, then check your answer in the original equation.
