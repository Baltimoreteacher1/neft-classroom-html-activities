  1. 6 fresh problems with new numbers each time (interactive only).

2. Multi-step missions

Real situations that need more than one step: plan, solve, then check that your answer makes sense.

Situaciones reales que necesitan más de un paso: planea, resuelve y revisa que tu respuesta tenga sentido.

  1. Find the Solving Error
  2. A detective has a budget of at most $100 for supplies. She already spent $37 on gloves. Write an inequality for the remaining amount r she can spend, solve it, graph it, and name three possible values for r.
  3. Zara needs at least 150 points to unlock Level 3, and she has 90. Let p be the extra points she still needs, so 90 + p ≥ 150. Which answer solves the inequality and describes its graph?
    • p ≥ 240; closed circle at 240, shaded right
    • p > 60; open circle at 60, shaded right
    • p ≥ 60; closed circle at 60, shaded right
    • p ≤ 60; closed circle at 60, shaded left
  4. Solve each inequality. Type the boundary number. Then type the whole-number solution asked for, the least or the greatest.
  5. Bola has 36 tickets. Each play of the claw game costs 4 tickets. The inequality 4p ≤ 36 shows the number of plays p he can afford. What is the greatest whole number of plays?
  6. Hiro solved k − 7 < 5 and drew this graph. What mistake did he make?
    • He shaded toward larger numbers, but the solution is smaller than the boundary.
    • He should have used a closed circle.
    • He should have put the boundary at 2.
    • He should have written the inequality as k > 12.

Use the tools to build the idea. Then check it.

4. Detective work

Somebody made a mistake. Find the step, then decide who is right.

Alguien cometió un error. Encuentra el paso y decide quién tiene razón.

  1. Mina solves and graphs x + 5 > 12.
  2. Tobias solves x − 6 ≥ 10.
  3. Ayesha graphs the solution of x ≥ 5.
  4. Giselle and Oscar solve 5x > 30. Giselle says x > 6 with an open circle at 6. Oscar says x ≥ 6 with a closed circle at 6. Who is right?
    • Oscar, because the boundary is always a solution.
    • Giselle, because dividing keeps the symbol > and 6 is not a solution.
    • Both, because the boundary is 6 in each.
    • Neither, because dividing by 5 flips the symbol to <.

5. Boss level

Tell us how sure you are, then solve. Finish with a problem of your own.

Dinos qué tan seguro estás y luego resuelve. Termina con un problema tuyo.

  1. Solve x + 3.5 ≥ 8. What is the least value x can be?
  2. Which inequality has the solution x > 9?
    • x + 4 > 5
    • 4x > 5
    • x ÷ 4 > 5
    • x − 4 > 5
  3. Which inequality has this solution graph?
    • x − 3 ≥ 11
    • x + 3 ≥ 11
    • 3x ≥ 11
    • x ÷ 3 ≥ 11
  4. Felix has $30 to spend on tokens that cost $2 each. The inequality 2t ≤ 30 shows how many tokens t he can buy. Which statement is true?
    • He can buy at least 15 tokens.
    • He can buy at most 60 tokens.
    • He can buy at most 15 tokens.
    • He must buy exactly 15 tokens.
  5. The whole numbers from 0 to 10 are tested in the inequality x − 2 < 5. How many of them are solutions?
  6. Make up an arcade problem that can be written as a one-step inequality, such as tokens, tickets or points. Solve it, say how you would graph the solution, and name one value that is a solution.
