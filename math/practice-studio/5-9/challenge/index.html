  2. A regular hexagon and a regular pentagon are each divided into congruent central triangles. In both polygons, each central triangle has area 15 sq ft. Which polygon has greater total area? Explain why the triangle count matters. The two polygons have different side lengths.

  4. Robot Lab covers hexagonal sensor plates with film. Each plate is a regular hexagon with sides of 20 cm. Split from the center, each of its 6 triangles has a height of 17.3 cm (rounded). The lab covers 4 plates, and each sheet of film covers 400 cm². How many sheets does the lab need?

    • 21 sheets
  5. Robot Lab is comparing three regular-polygon floor plates. Fill in the area of one triangle and the area of each plate, in square feet. Use the given heights; they are rounded, so areas with decimals are estimates.
  6. A regular pentagon pad is split from its center into 5 identical triangles. Each triangle has a base of 10 ft, and the whole pad has an area of 172.5 ft². What is the height of each triangle?
  7. A regular hexagon charging pad has a perimeter of 48 ft. Split from the center, each triangle has a height of 6.9 ft (rounded). What is the approximate area of the pad?

4. Detective work

Somebody made a mistake. Find the step, then decide who is right.

Alguien cometió un error. Encuentra el paso y decide quién tiene razón.

  1. Ivo finds the area of a regular hexagon whose center triangles have a base of 12 in and a height of 10.4 in (rounded). Tap the step where his work first goes wrong.
  2. Noor finds the area of a regular octagon whose 8 center triangles each have a base of 8 cm and a height of 9.7 cm (rounded). Tap the step where her work first goes wrong.
  3. Diego finds the area of a regular hexagon whose center triangles have a base of 9 m and a height of 7.8 m (rounded). Tap the step where his work first goes wrong.
  4. A regular hexagon has sides of 6 ft. Li says to use 6 ft as the height of each center triangle. Omar says the height is the distance from the center straight to a side, which is 5.2 ft (rounded). Who is right?
    • Li, because the side is the longest length in the triangle
    • Both, because either length works for the area
    • Omar, because a triangle's height is measured at a right angle to its base
    • Neither, because a hexagon cannot be split into triangles

5. Boss level

Tell us how sure you are, then solve. Finish with a problem of your own.

  1. A regular pentagon is split from its center into 5 identical triangles. Each triangle has a base of 14 cm and a height of 9.6 cm (rounded). What is the approximate area of the pentagon?
  2. A regular polygon floor has an area of 144 ft² and splits into identical triangles of 18 ft² each. Which polygon is it?
    • A regular hexagon
    • A regular decagon
    • A regular pentagon
    • A regular octagon
  3. A regular decagon robot pad has 10 center triangles, each with a base of 4 ft and a height of 6.2 ft (rounded). Special coating costs $5 per square foot. How much does it cost to coat the whole pad?
  4. Regular hexagon plate A has 6 center triangles of 14 ft² each. Regular pentagon plate B has 5 center triangles of 17 ft² each. Which plate has the greater area, and by how much?
    • Plate A, because it has more triangles
    • Plate B, by 1 ft²
    • Plate B, by 3 ft²
    • They have the same area
  5. A regular hexagon has center triangles with a base of 6 ft and a height of 5.2 ft. Which expression gives the area of the hexagon in square feet?
    • 6 × (½ × 6 × 5.2)
    • ½ × 6 × 5.2
    • 6 + ½ × 6 × 5.2
    • 6 × 6 × 5.2
