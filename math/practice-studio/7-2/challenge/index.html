A storm has scrambled the survey lines on the deep reef. Every fraction and decimal must go back to its exact spot, or the rescue boats will search in the wrong water.

Una tormenta revolvió las líneas de estudio del arrecife profundo. Cada fracción y cada decimal debe volver a su lugar exacto, o los botes de rescate buscarán en el agua equivocada.

1. Climb the skill ladder

Each rung is harder than the last, and the numbers change every time.

Cada peldaño es más difícil que el anterior y los números cambian cada vez.

  1. 6 fresh problems with new numbers each time (interactive only).

2. Multi-step missions

Real situations that need more than one step: plan, solve, then check that your answer makes sense.

Situaciones reales que necesitan más de un paso: planea, resuelve y revisa que tu respuesta tenga sentido.

  1. Find the Plotting Error
  2. Name three different rational numbers between -1 and 0. Write each as both a fraction and a decimal. Explain how you know they are between -1 and 0.
  3. On a number line, point P is located at -2.5. Which statement describes its location best?
    • At -3
    • At -2
    • Halfway between -2 and -3
    • Halfway between 2 and 3
  4. Four depth sensors are shown on the survey line, where 0 is the water surface and each tick is 0.5. Which sensor reads −1 1/2?
    • Sensor A
    • Sensor B
    • Sensor C
    • Sensor D
  5. A sea turtle swims at −2 3/4 meters. A fish swims at the opposite reading. Write the fish's reading as a decimal.
  6. The survey crew records each reading as a fraction. Write it as a decimal, then write the integer just to its left on a number line. Fill in every empty cell.
  7. The number line from 0 to 1 is split into equal parts, as shown. Point P is the third mark to the right of 0. Which number is point P?
    • 3/8
    • 3/5
    • 0.3
    • 5/8

3. Build it, move it, test it

Use the tools to build the idea. Then check it.

Usa las herramientas para construir la idea. Luego compruébala.

  1. Which two integers does each reading fall between? Sort every reading into its home interval.
  2. 2 fresh problems with new numbers each time (interactive only).

4. Detective work

Somebody made a mistake. Find the step, then decide who is right.

Alguien cometió un error. Encuentra el paso y decide quién tiene razón.

  1. Pia wants to plot 3/4 on a line from 0 to 1 that is split into 4 equal parts. Find the step where her thinking goes wrong.
  2. Leo wants the decimal for −7/4 so he can place it. Find the step where his work goes wrong.
  3. Dara wants the opposite of −2 1/2. Find the step where her thinking goes wrong.
  4. Cam says, "0.5 and 1/2 are at different points, because one is a decimal and one is a fraction." Dee says, "−0.5 is the opposite of 1/2." Eli says, "0.50 is farther right than 0.5." Who is right?
    • Cam only
    • Dee only
    • Eli only
    • Cam and Eli

5. Boss level

Tell us how sure you are, then solve. Finish with a problem of your own.

Dinos qué tan seguro estás y luego resuelve. Termina con un problema tuyo.

  1. Between which two integers is −3.4 located?
    • −3 and −2
    • 3 and 4
    • −5 and −4
    • −4 and −3
  2. A buoy reads 3/8 meters above the surface. A second buoy reads the opposite of the first buoy's reading. Write the second reading as a decimal.
  3. Four coral markers are shown on the survey line, where each tick is 0.25. Which marker is at −1 3/4?
    • Marker A
    • Marker C
    • Marker B
    • Marker D
  4. Which pair of numbers names the same point on a number line?
    • −1/4 and −0.25
    • −1/4 and −0.4
    • 1/4 and −0.25
    • −1/4 and −2.5
  5. Write 2 3/5 as a decimal.
  6. Create your own reef survey. Choose three readings: a negative fraction, a negative decimal and a positive mixed number. For each, say which two integers it is between and explain how you would place it on a number line.
