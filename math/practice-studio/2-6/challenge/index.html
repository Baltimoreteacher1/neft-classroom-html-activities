La estación pasa al modo nocturno y las alarmas parpadean. Un registro de carga tiene dígitos borrosos, y solo un navegante cuidadoso puede reconstruir cada división y ver dónde se perdió un cero.

1. Climb the skill ladder

Each rung is harder than the last, and the numbers change every time.

Cada peldaño es más difícil que el anterior y los números cambian cada vez.

  1. 6 fresh problems with new numbers each time (interactive only).

2. Multi-step missions

Real situations that need more than one step: plan, solve, then check that your answer makes sense.

Situaciones reales que necesitan más de un paso: planea, resuelve y revisa que tu respuesta tenga sentido.

  1. Find Sonia's Mistake
  2. The station must load 3,360 ration packs equally into 14 storage pods. Solve it with long division. Name each step — divide, multiply, subtract, bring down — and check your answer by multiplying.
  3. Orbit Station has 6,327 food packets to share equally among 3 storage modules. How many packets does each module get?
  4. The lab has 487 sample tubes. Each rack holds 12 tubes. How many racks are needed to hold every tube?
  5. A crew has 275 meal pouches for a 12-day mission. They want the same number of pouches each day. Which statement is correct?
    • They can use 22 pouches each day, with 11 pouches left over
    • They can use 23 pouches each day, with none left over
    • They can use 11 pouches each day, with 22 pouches left over
    • They can use 27 pouches each day, with 5 pouches left over
  6. Run the long-division cycle for 1,404 ÷ 12 = 117. For each step, fill in the quotient digit, the product with 12, and what is left after subtracting.

3. Build it, move it, test it

Use the tools to build the idea. Then check it.

Usa las herramientas para construir la idea. Luego compruébala.

  1. Long division repeats four steps. Sort each action into the step it belongs to.
  2. 2 fresh problems with new numbers each time (interactive only).

4. Detective work

Somebody made a mistake. Find the step, then decide who is right.

Alguien cometió un error. Encuentra el paso y decide quién tiene razón.

  1. Farah divides 4,208 ÷ 4 with long division.
  2. Nikhil divides 150 ÷ 7 by guessing the quotient and then subtracting.
  3. Dmitri divides 1,248 ÷ 8 and gets 181.
  4. A shuttle seats 6 trainees. There are 89 trainees. Kwame says 14 shuttles are enough because 89 ÷ 6 = 14 R5. Mei-Ling says 15 shuttles are needed. Who is right?
    • Kwame, because the quotient is 14
    • Mei-Ling, because the 5 left over trainees need another shuttle
    • Both, because 14 and 15 are close
    • Neither, because 89 ÷ 6 should be 15 R−1

5. Boss level

Tell us how sure you are, then solve. Finish with a problem of your own.

Dinos qué tan seguro estás y luego resuelve. Termina con un problema tuyo.

  1. A solar array has 1,764 cells arranged in 21 equal rows. How many cells are in each row?
  2. What is 2,009 ÷ 17?
    • 118 R3
    • 117 R20
    • 18 R3
    • 118 R17
  3. Which is the best estimate of 4,187 ÷ 21?
    • About 20
    • About 2,000
    • About 200
    • About 4,000
  4. Which quotient is correct for 2,436 ÷ 12?
    • 23
    • 213
    • 230
    • 203
  5. A division has a divisor of 14, a quotient of 61 and a remainder of 5. What is the dividend?
