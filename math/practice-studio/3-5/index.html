1. Warm up your thinking

Quick questions that wake up what you already know for this lesson.

Preguntas rápidas para activar lo que ya sabes de esta lección.

  1. A rooftop planter uses 12 liters of water for 8 plants. How many liters is that for each plant?
  2. Two garden timers repeat every 4 days and every 6 days. What is the least common multiple of 4 and 6?
  3. A fertilizer recipe is 3 scoops to 5 liters. Scale it so there are 35 liters. How many scoops go with 35 liters?
  4. Two seed mixes have 0.4 and 0.375 grams of seed in each gram of soil. Which mix has more seed in each gram of soil?
