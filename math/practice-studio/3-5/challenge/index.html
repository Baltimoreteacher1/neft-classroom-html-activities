Frost is coming to the Rooftop Garden, and the gardeners must pick the strongest mix tonight. Compare every pair of ratios fairly, because a wrong pick could cost the whole crop.

Se acerca una helada a Rooftop Garden y los jardineros deben elegir esta noche la mezcla más fuerte. Compara cada par de razones con justicia, porque una mala elección podría costar toda la cosecha.

1. Climb the skill ladder

Each rung is harder than the last, and the numbers change every time.

Cada peldaño es más difícil que el anterior y los números cambian cada vez.

  1. 6 fresh problems with new numbers each time (interactive only).

2. Multi-step missions

Real situations that need more than one step: plan, solve, then check that your answer makes sense.

Situaciones reales que necesitan más de un paso: planea, resuelve y revisa que tu respuesta tenga sentido.

  1. Find Kai's Mistake
  2. Store A sells 5 notebooks for $12. Store B sells 8 notebooks for $20. Without calculating, a student says Store B must be cheaper because you get more notebooks. Is the student's reasoning valid? Find the unit rates to prove your answer.
  3. Two garden beds use different soil mixes, shown in the table. Which bed has the richer mix, with more compost for the same amount of soil?
    • Bed A: 15 compost per 24 soil beats 14.
    • Bed B, because 7 buckets is more than 5.
    • Bed B, because it has more soil.
    • The beds are the same.
  4. Fertilizer Mix X has 9 grams in 6 liters of water. Fertilizer Mix Y has 14 grams in 8 liters of water. How many more grams per liter does the stronger mix have?
  5. Three watering plans use different amounts for different numbers of plants. Scale each plan to 24 plants and fill in the liters of water.
  6. Mix P has 3 parts fertilizer for every 5 parts water. Mix Q has 9 parts fertilizer for every 14 parts water. Which statement is correct?
    • Mix P is stronger: 42 fertilizer per 70 water beats 45.
    • Mix Q is stronger: 45 fertilizer per 70 water beats 42.
    • The mixes are equal, because both are about 3 to 5.
    • Mix Q is stronger, because 14 water is more than 5 water.

4. Detective work

Somebody made a mistake. Find the step, then decide who is right.

Alguien cometió un error. Encuentra el paso y decide quién tiene razón.

  1. A gardener compared watering plans. One step goes wrong. Tap the first step that is wrong.
  2. A gardener compared fertilizer strength as fertilizer to water. One step goes wrong. Tap the first step that is wrong.
  3. A gardener compared seed density in two plots. One step goes wrong. Tap the first step that is wrong.
  4. Kai says 3:4 and 5:6 are equal, because in each ratio the second number is 1 more than the first. Lena scales both to 12. Who is right?
    • Kai: the difference is the same, so the ratios are the same.
    • Both, because 12 is a common multiple.
    • Lena, but 3:4 is greater.
    • Lena: 3:4 is 9:12, 5:6 is 10:12, so they are different and 5:6 is greater.

5. Boss level

Tell us how sure you are, then solve. Finish with a problem of your own.

Dinos qué tan seguro estás y luego resuelve. Termina con un problema tuyo.

  1. A garden grew 27 tomatoes on 6 plants. How many tomatoes is that for each plant?
  2. Which ratio is greater, 5:8 or 3:5?
    • 3:5, because 3:5 is greater than 5:8 when you scale to 40.
    • 5:8, because 25:40 is greater than 24:40.
    • 3:5, because 5 is less than 8.
    • They are equal, because 8 − 5 = 3 and 5 − 3 = 2 are both small.
  3. Soil Mix A is 3 compost to 4 soil. Soil Mix B is 5 compost to 7 soil. Scale both to 28 soil. How many more compost does Mix A have than Mix B?
  4. Three stores sell seed packets. Store X: 6 packets for $4.50. Store Y: 10 packets for $7.20. Store Z: 8 packets for $6.40. Which store has the lowest price per packet?
    • Store X, at $0.75 per packet
    • Store Z, at $0.80 per packet
    • Store Y, at $0.72 per packet
    • Store X, because $4.50 is the lowest total
  5. Mix P uses 3 cups of fertilizer for every 8 cups of water. Mix Q uses 9 cups of fertilizer. How many cups of water make Mix Q exactly as strong as Mix P?
  6. Write your own garden comparison with two plans that use different amounts. Compare them using unit rates or by scaling to a common amount, say which is greater, and explain why comparing the raw numbers would be unfair.
