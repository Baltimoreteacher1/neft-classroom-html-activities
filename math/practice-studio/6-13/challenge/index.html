Cada peldaño es más difícil que el anterior y los números cambian cada vez.

  1. 6 fresh problems with new numbers each time (interactive only).

2. Multi-step missions

Real situations that need more than one step: plan, solve, then check that your answer makes sense.

Situaciones reales que necesitan más de un paso: planea, resuelve y revisa que tu respuesta tenga sentido.

  1. Find Marcus's Mistake
  2. Choose 24, 36, or 60. Show two factor trees with different first factor pairs. Finish both trees with prime factors, then explain how you checked that both products equal your chosen number.
  3. Every loop here is built from the primes 2, 3 and 5. Fill the empty cells. The last column counts every prime factor, repeats included.
  4. A sample pack's label is smudged. It reads 2² × 3 × ? × 5, and the pack plays 420 beats. The missing number is prime. What is it?
  5. A mixing preset lasts 2² × 3 × 5² beats. A loop syncs with the preset only if its length divides the preset's beats evenly. Which loop length can sync?
    • 8
    • 9
    • 14
    • 50
  6. Sam is mounting 120 drum pads on a wall in a rectangle, with no gaps and no extra pads. Rows × columns must equal 120. A 4 × 30 rectangle and a 30 × 4 rectangle are the same one turned sideways, and a single row of 120 counts. How many different rectangles can Sam build?

4. Detective work

Somebody made a mistake. Find the step, then decide who is right.

Alguien cometió un error. Encuentra el paso y decide quién tiene razón.

  1. Ruben built a factor tree for 150. Find the step where his thinking first goes wrong.
  2. Amara factored 200 correctly, then checked her answer. Find the step where the check goes wrong.
  3. Kenji factored 180 and wrote only the different primes. Find the step where his thinking goes wrong.
  4. Three producers factored 126. Dev wrote 2 × 63. Mei wrote 2 × 3 × 3 × 7. Priya wrote 2 × 3² × 7. Who has the complete prime factorization of 126?
    • Only Mei
    • Only Priya
    • Mei and Priya
    • Dev, Mei and Priya

5. Boss level

Tell us how sure you are, then solve. Finish with a problem of your own.

Dinos qué tan seguro estás y luego resuelve. Termina con un problema tuyo.

  1. Which expression is the complete prime factorization of 168?
    • 2³ × 3 × 7
    • 2 × 3 × 7
    • 2² × 6 × 7
    • 2³ × 21
  2. A loop is 2³ × 3² × 7 beats long. How many beats is that?
  3. Which of these numbers is prime?
    • 49
    • 51
    • 91
    • 89
  4. Every complete factor tree for 396 ends in the same prime leaves, however it starts. How many leaves does a complete tree for 396 have? Count every leaf, repeats included.
  5. Ilse says, "Since 102 = 2 × 3 × 17, the number 102 cannot be divided evenly by 9." Is Ilse right?
    • No. 3 is a factor of 102, so 9 must be a factor too.
    • Yes. 9 = 3 × 3 needs two 3s, and 102 has only one 3.
    • Yes. 9 does not appear in 2 × 3 × 17, so it cannot be a factor.
    • Not enough information. You must multiply 2 × 3 × 17 out first.
  6. Create your own problem. Pick a composite number between 100 and 200. Build two factor trees for it, each starting with a different pair of factors. Write the prime factorization with exponents, check it by multiplying, and explain how you know each tree is finished.
