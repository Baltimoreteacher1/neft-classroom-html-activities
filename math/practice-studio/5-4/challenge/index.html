  2. A T-shaped room can be decomposed in two different ways. Show both ways and prove they give the same total area. Use a T-shape where the top is 14 ft × 4 ft and the stem is 6 ft × 10 ft.

4. Detective work

Somebody made a mistake. Find the step, then decide who is right.

Alguien cometió un error. Encuentra el paso y decide quién tiene razón.

  1. A clubhouse wall is an 18 ft by 11 ft rectangle with a triangle on top that has a base of 18 ft and a height of 6 ft. Dana finds the total area. Tap the first step where her work goes wrong.
  2. A 16 ft by 9 ft game room has a 4 ft by 5 ft supply closet cut out of one corner. Eli finds the usable floor area. Tap the first step where his work goes wrong.
  3. An arcade stage is a 10 ft by 8 ft rectangle with a triangle on its 10 ft side. The triangle has a base of 10 ft and a height of 4 ft. Fay finds the area. Tap the first step where her work goes wrong.
  4. A floor is an L-shape drawn on a grid where each square is 1 ft. Mo cuts it into a 10 by 4 rectangle and a 4 by 6 rectangle. Ana cuts it into a 4 by 10 rectangle and a 6 by 4 rectangle. Who gets the correct area?
    • Only Mo, because he cut the bottom first
    • Only Ana, because her rectangles are taller
    • Both. Each way gives 64 ft².
    • Neither, because their areas are different

5. Boss level

Tell us how sure you are, then solve. Finish with a problem of your own.

Dinos qué tan seguro estás y luego resuelve. Termina con un problema tuyo.

  1. An L-shaped arcade lounge is made of a 14 ft by 6 ft rectangle and a 5 ft by 4 ft rectangle joined to it. What is the total area, in square feet?
  2. A 16 m by 12 m courtyard has a square fountain, 4 m on each side, cut out of it. What is the area left for visitors?
    • 16 m²
    • 192 m²
    • 208 m²
    • 176 m²
  3. A hexagon-shaped prize table is a regular hexagon. It splits into 6 identical triangles. Each triangle has a base of 10 cm and a height of 9 cm. What is the area of its top surface, in square centimeters?
  4. A game booth front is a 10 ft by 8 ft rectangle with a triangular roof on top. The triangle's base is 10 ft. The whole front has an area of 140 ft². What is the height of the triangular roof, in feet?
  5. A 12 ft by 8 ft arcade room has a 4 ft by 3 ft notch cut out of one corner. Which expression gives the floor area?
    • 12 × 8 + 4 × 3
    • 12 × 8 − 4 × 3
    • (12 − 4) × (8 − 3)
    • 12 × 8 × 4 × 3
  6. Create your own Pixel Arcade composite figure. Describe a floor or wall made of two or three simple shapes, find its area, and explain whether you add or subtract each piece.
