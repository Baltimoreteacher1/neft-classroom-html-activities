2. Multi-step missions

Real situations that need more than one step: plan, solve, then check that your answer makes sense.

Situaciones reales que necesitan más de un paso: planea, resuelve y revisa que tu respuesta tenga sentido.

  1. Find Aliya's Mistake
  2. A bag of marbles has 8 red, 6 blue, and 4 green marbles. Write three different ratios using these quantities: one part-to-part ratio, one part-to-whole ratio, and explain the difference.
  3. The bar graph shows the fish in a rescue tank. What is the ratio of gobies to all the fish in the tank, in simplest form?
    • 3:15
    • 6:1
    • 1:6
    • 3:9
  4. A coral nursery plants staghorn and brain coral in the ratio 5:3. A tray holds 40 fragments in all. How many fragments are staghorn?
  5. Reef shrimp are fed with clownfish in the ratio 2:3 in every tank. Fill in the missing numbers so every row keeps the ratio 2 clownfish to 3 shrimp.
  6. A reef survey counted 12 sea turtles and 8 rays. Which statement is correct?
    • The ratio of rays to all the animals is 8:20.
    • The ratio of turtles to rays is 8:12.
    • The ratio of turtles to all the animals is 12:8.
    • The ratio of turtles to rays is 12:20.

4. Detective work

Somebody made a mistake. Find the step, then decide who is right.

Alguien cometió un error. Encuentra el paso y decide quién tiene razón.

  1. A diver wrote a ratio for the tank. One step goes wrong. Tap the first step that is wrong.
  2. A volunteer wrote a part-to-whole ratio. One step goes wrong. Tap the first step that is wrong.
  3. A student wrote 12:18 in simplest form. One step goes wrong. Tap the first step that is wrong.
  4. A tank has 6 turtles and 4 rays. Mia says the ratio of turtles to rays is 6:4, which is the same as 3:2. Omar says it is 2, because there are 2 more turtles. Who is right?
    • Only Omar: the ratio is how many more turtles there are.
    • Only Mia: a ratio compares the two amounts, and 6:4 matches 3:2.
    • Both are right, because 2 is the difference in 3:2.
    • Neither: the ratio is 4:6.

5. Boss level

Tell us how sure you are, then solve. Finish with a problem of your own.

Dinos qué tan seguro estás y luego resuelve. Termina con un problema tuyo.

  1. A reef tank has 14 clownfish and 21 tangs. Which ratio is equivalent to the ratio of clownfish to tangs?
    • 2:3
    • 3:2
    • 7:14
    • 14:35
  2. Of the 36 fish in a reef tank, the ratio of parrotfish to all fish is 1:4. How many parrotfish are in the tank?
  3. A tank holds 5 red fish and 3 blue fish. Which ratio compares the blue fish to ALL the fish?
    • 3:5
    • 8:3
    • 3:8
    • 5:8
  4. Divers and boat crew work in the ratio 3:2. There are 12 divers. How many people are on the boat crew?
  5. A tank has 12 fish, and 4 of them are damselfish. Which statement is true?
    • The ratio of damselfish to other fish is 4:12.
    • The ratio of damselfish to all fish is 1:2.
    • The ratio of other fish to damselfish is 1:2.
    • For every 1 damselfish there are 2 other fish.
  6. Write your own Reef Rescue story with two groups. Give one part-to-part ratio and one part-to-whole ratio, write each in colon notation, and explain how they are different.
