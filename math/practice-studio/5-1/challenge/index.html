4. Detective work

Somebody made a mistake. Find the step, then decide who is right.

Alguien cometió un error. Encuentra el paso y decide quién tiene razón.

  1. A trail map shows a parallelogram with base 15 ft, slanted side 13 ft and height 12 ft. Gabi finds its area. Tap the first step where her work goes wrong.
  2. A tent floor is a parallelogram with base 7 ft and height 4 ft. Hamza finds the area. Tap the first step where his work goes wrong.
  3. A parallelogram has an area of 72 m² and a base of 8 m. Pilar finds its height. Tap the first step where her work goes wrong.
  4. A parallelogram has sides of 12 ft and 8 ft. Its height to the 12-ft side is 6 ft, and its height to the 8-ft side is 9 ft. Luis says you must use the longer side as the base. Aiko says any side works if you use the matching height. Who is right?
    • Luis. Only the longer side can be the base.
    • Luis. Using the 8-ft side gives a smaller area.
    • Aiko. The areas are different, so use the biggest.
    • Aiko. Both choices give the same area, 72 ft².

  3. A tent floor is a parallelogram with a base of 6 ft and a height of 4 ft. A bigger tent has a base 3 times as long and the same height. What is the area of the bigger tent?

  5. Camp pad 1 is a parallelogram with a base of 11 m, a height of 6 m and a slanted side of 12 m. Camp pad 2 is a parallelogram with a base of 8 m, a height of 8 m and a slanted side of 10 m. Which pad is larger?
    • Pad 1, by 2 m²
    • Pad 1, by 52 m²
    • Pad 2, because its base and height are equal
    • They have equal areas
