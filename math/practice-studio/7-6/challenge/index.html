Grow your skills.Haz crecer tus habilidades.

The garden has overgrown, and the old plan is hidden under the vines. Work out the distances between the beds from their coordinates before the next planting season.

El jardín creció sin control y el plano viejo quedó escondido entre las enredaderas. Calcula las distancias entre las parcelas a partir de sus coordenadas antes de la próxima temporada de siembra.

1. Climb the skill ladder

Each rung is harder than the last, and the numbers change every time.

Cada peldaño es más difícil que el anterior y los números cambian cada vez.

  1. 6 fresh problems with new numbers each time (interactive only).

2. Multi-step missions

Real situations that need more than one step: plan, solve, then check that your answer makes sense.

Situaciones reales que necesitan más de un paso: planea, resuelve y revisa que tu respuesta tenga sentido.

  1. Find the Distance Error
  2. A rectangle has vertices at (-4, -2), (3, -2), (3, 5), and (-4, 5). Find the length, width, perimeter, and area of the rectangle. Show your work using absolute value.
  3. A rectangle has corners (1, 2), (1, 5), (5, 5), and (5, 2). What is its perimeter?
    • 7 units
    • 10 units
    • 12 units
    • 14 units
  4. On the garden grid, each unit is 1 foot. A hose runs from the tomato bed straight across to the herb bed, then straight down to the pepper bed. How many feet of hose are needed in all?
  5. The gardener measures between pairs of beds that share a row or a column. Write the distance between each pair, and write 1 if the changing coordinates are on the same side of 0 or 2 if they are on opposite sides. Fill in every empty cell.
  6. A rectangular garden bed has corners at (−3, −2), (4, −2), (4, 3) and (−3, 3), as shown on the grid. Each unit is 1 foot. How many feet of fence go around the bed?
    • 24 feet
    • 12 feet
    • 35 feet
    • 4 feet
  7. A garden path runs along the row y = 2 from x = −7 to x = 4. The gardener plants one bush at every whole-number x-value on the path, including both end points. How many bushes does she plant?

3. Build it, move it, test it

Use the tools to build the idea. Then check it.

Usa las herramientas para construir la idea. Luego compruébala.

  1. Each pair of garden stakes shares a row or a column. Sort the pairs by how you find the distance.
  2. 2 fresh problems with new numbers each time (interactive only).

4. Detective work

Somebody made a mistake. Find the step, then decide who is right.

Alguien cometió un error. Encuentra el paso y decide quién tiene razón.

  1. Pablo finds the distance between (−8, 1) and (5, 1). Find the step where his work goes wrong.
  2. Sofía finds the distance between (−4, −2) and (−4, −8). Find the step where her work goes wrong.
  3. Teo finds the distance between (3, −2) and (3, 5). Find the step where his work goes wrong.
  4. Three gardeners find the distance from (−3, 4) to (−3, −6). Ula says 10 units, because |4| + |−6| = 10. Van says 2 units, because 6 − 4 = 2. Wes says −10 units, because the point is below the other. Who is right?
    • Van
    • Wes
    • Ula and Wes
    • Ula

5. Boss level

Tell us how sure you are, then solve. Finish with a problem of your own.

Dinos qué tan seguro estás y luego resuelve. Termina con un problema tuyo.

  1. What is the distance between (−9, 5) and (4, 5)?
    • 5 units
    • 13 units
    • 9 units
    • 4 units
  2. How many units apart are (7, −12) and (7, −4)?
  3. Four plant stakes are on the grid: P at (−3, 4), Q at (5, 4), R at (−3, −5) and S at (2, −5). Which pair of stakes is exactly 8 units apart?
    • P and R
    • R and S
    • P and Q
    • Q and S
  4. A rectangular herb bed has corners at (−6, −3), (2, −3), (2, 4) and (−6, 4). Each unit is 1 foot. What is the area of the bed in square feet?
  5. Two scarecrows stand on the same vertical line, 11 units apart. One scarecrow is at (5, −4). Which point could be the other scarecrow?
    • (5, 7)
    • (5, 11)
    • (16, −4)
    • (5, −7)
  6. Create your own garden bed. Choose four corners of a rectangle that cross at least one axis. Use absolute value to find the length of each side, then find the perimeter, and explain when you add and when you subtract.
