It is championship night at Home Field. The broadcast crew will read your numbers on live TV, and a misleading average could fool thousands of fans. Choose the measures that keep the broadcast honest.

Es la noche del campeonato en Home Field. El equipo de transmisión leerá tus números en vivo, y un promedio engañoso podría confundir a miles de aficionados. Elige las medidas que mantengan honesta la transmisión.

Cada peldaño es más difícil que el anterior y los números cambian cada vez.

  1. 6 fresh problems with new numbers each time (interactive only).

2. Multi-step missions

Real situations that need more than one step: plan, solve, then check that your answer makes sense.

Situaciones reales que necesitan más de un paso: planea, resuelve y revisa que tu respuesta tenga sentido.

  1. Find the Reasoning Error
  2. A local newspaper reports that the average home price in a neighborhood is $450,000. The actual prices are: $180K, $200K, $190K, $210K, $195K, $1,725K. Is the reporter's claim misleading? Calculate the mean and median and explain which better represents a 'typical' home price.
  3. Sort each real-world situation into whether the mean or median is more commonly reported.
  4. The Home Field cycling club logged the length of its last 9 rides, shown in the dot plot. The newsletter wants one number for a typical ride. Which statement is correct?
    • The mean is 26 km and the median is 21 km. Use the mean, because it counts every ride.
    • The mean is 26 km and the median is 21 km. Use the median, because the 70 km ride pulls the mean up.
    • Without the 70 km ride, the mean is 20.5 km and the median is 21 km. Either works, so leave the long ride out.
    • The mean is 26 km and the median is 21 km. Use the range, 52 km, because it shows the biggest ride.
  5. A center's rebounds in 8 games are shown in the dot plot. Which pair of measures should the stats sheet report, and what are their values?
    • Mean 8.5 and MAD 4.625
    • Median 6 and MAD 4.625
    • Mean 8.5 and IQR 2.5
    • Median 6 and IQR 2.5
  6. A swim team's practice minutes for two weeks are listed in the first column. In Week 2 the team added one marathon session. Fill in each mean, each median, and the gap between them (mean − median).
  7. The equipment manager recorded how many water bottles the team used in each of its last 8 games, shown in the dot plot. She must order enough for the next 10 games, and a hot day like the 49-bottle game can happen again. Which order makes the most sense?
    • 250 bottles: the mean is 25, and a total for many games must count the hot day too.
    • 220 bottles: the median is 22, the typical game, times 10 games.
    • 490 bottles: order for the biggest game, 49, every time.
    • 200 bottles: that was the total for the last 8 games.

4. Detective work

Somebody made a mistake. Find the step, then decide who is right.

Alguien cometió un error. Encuentra el paso y decide quién tiene razón.

  1. An analyst reported a goalkeeper's saves. One step in the report goes wrong. Tap the first step that is wrong.
  2. A tennis coach tried to find the median serve speed. One step goes wrong. Tap the first step that is wrong.
  3. A runner's coach wrote this report. One step goes wrong. Tap the first step that is wrong.
  4. After a sprint drill, two analysts reported the spread of the recovery times in the dot plot. Ana reported "median 15, range 34." Ben reported "median 15, IQR 3.5." Who chose the better spread measure?
    • Ana, because the range uses the smallest and largest times, so it shows all the data.
    • Both are fine, because both reported the same median.
    • Ben, because the 46 stretches the range, but the IQR describes the middle half of the times.
    • Neither, because skewed data needs the mean and MAD for spread.

5. Boss level

Tell us how sure you are, then solve. Finish with a problem of your own.

Dinos qué tan seguro estás y luego resuelve. Termina con un problema tuyo.

  1. Nine athletes took the Home Field fitness test. The dot plot shows how many push-ups each one completed. The mean is 16 push-ups. Which statement is correct?
    • The median is 16, the same as the mean, so either measure works.
    • The median is 19, but the mean is better because it uses all nine scores.
    • The median is 19, and it is the better measure because the two low scores pull the mean down.
    • The median is 18, the 4th score, because 9 ÷ 2 = 4.5 rounds down.
  2. The league reports attendance with the median and IQR. Attendance at 9 home games, in hundreds of fans, is shown in the dot plot. What is the IQR?
  3. Two relay teams' 100 m times (seconds) are shown in the box plots. Which statement is best supported by the median and the IQR?
    • The Rams are faster, because their quickest time is 10.9 s.
    • Both teams have the same typical time (median 12.2 s), but the Hawks are more consistent (IQR 0.8 s against 1.6 s).
    • The Rams are more consistent, because their box is wider.
    • The teams are equally consistent, because they have the same median.
  4. Home Field's account gained 10, 12, 13, 15, and 40 hundred followers after five games. A viral video then changes the last number to 400. Which statement is true?
    • Only the mean changes: it jumps from 18 to 90, while the median stays 13.
    • Only the median changes, because it is the middle value.
    • Both change by the same amount.
    • Neither changes, because 40 and 400 are both the biggest number.
  5. A swimmer's 100 m practice times are shown in the dot plot. The 75 s lap happened when her goggles slipped. The coach reports the median. Which explanation is complete and correct?
    • The median is better because it is the middle number.
    • The 75 s outlier pulls the mean up to about 61.7 s, higher than six of the seven laps, so the median is a better typical time.
    • The 75 s outlier pulls the median up, so the median is not typical.
    • The data is skewed, so the mean cannot be found.
  6. Make up a data set of 6 to 8 numbers from a sport you like, so that the median is a better center than the mean. Write the numbers, find the mean and the median, and explain which one you would report and why.
