4. Detective work

Somebody made a mistake. Find the step, then decide who is right.

Alguien cometió un error. Encuentra el paso y decide quién tiene razón.

  1. Lucia converts 48 ounces of chalk to pounds with 1 lb = 16 oz. Tap the first step where her work goes wrong.
  2. Kwame changes a 2-yard rope to inches. He knows 1 ft = 12 in and 1 yd = 3 ft. Tap the first step where his work goes wrong.
  3. Nia changes the 3.4-kilometer cross-country course to meters with 1 km = 1,000 m. Tap the first step where her work goes wrong.
  4. A sign says the discus throw went 300 cm. Ana says to find meters you multiply by 100. Ben says you divide by 100. Who is right?
    • Ana, because you always multiply by the factor.
    • Ben, because a meter is bigger than a centimeter, so there are fewer meters.
    • Both, because the answer is the same either way.
    • Neither, because you add 100.
