2. Multi-step missions

Real situations that need more than one step: plan, solve, then check that your answer makes sense.

Situaciones reales que necesitan más de un paso: planea, resuelve y revisa que tu respuesta tenga sentido.

  2. A recipe says you need 5 feet of ribbon, but the store only sells ribbon in inches. Another recipe says you need 2 yards of fabric, but your ruler measures in feet. Convert both measurements and explain the steps you used. Which conversion factor did you use each time?
  3. Coach Adaeze lays out a long jump runway that is 40 meters long. She puts a marker every 250 centimeters, starting at the beginning. How many 250-centimeter sections does the runway have?
  4. The team cooler holds 2 gallons of water. Each of 12 players drinks 2 cups. Use 1 gal = 4 qt and 1 qt = 4 cups. How much water is left in the cooler?
    • 0.5 gallon
    • 1.5 gallons
    • 6 gallons
    • 8 gallons
  5. One lap of the Home Field track is 400 meters. Complete each row. Use 1 km = 1,000 m. Type numbers only.
  6. The weight room has 6 bars that each weigh 45 lb. Selim must write the total weight in ounces on a shipping label. Use 1 lb = 16 oz. How many ounces is that?

3. Build it, move it, test it

4. Detective work

Somebody made a mistake. Find the step, then decide who is right.

Alguien cometió un error. Encuentra el paso y decide quién tiene razón.

  1. Lucia converts 48 ounces of chalk to pounds with 1 lb = 16 oz. Tap the first step where her work goes wrong.
  2. Kwame changes a 2-yard rope to inches. He knows 1 ft = 12 in and 1 yd = 3 ft. Tap the first step where his work goes wrong.
  3. In 1999 NASA lost the Mars Climate Orbiter because one team's numbers were in pound-force seconds (lbf·s) and the other team's software expected newton-seconds (N·s). Use 1 lbf ≈ 4.45 N. Here is a made-up check of an engine push of 100 lbf·s. Tap the first step where the work goes wrong.
  4. A sign says the discus throw went 300 cm. Ana says to find meters you multiply by 100. Ben says you divide by 100. Who is right?
    • Ana, because you always multiply when you convert units.
    • Ben: a meter is bigger, so there are fewer meters.
    • Both, because each one uses the factor 100 to convert.
    • Neither, because you add 100 to change the unit.
  5. What is the best next move?

5. Boss level

Tell us how sure you are, then solve. Finish with a problem of your own.

Dinos qué tan seguro estás y luego resuelve. Termina con un problema tuyo.

  1. A cycling route at Home Field is 7.5 km long. Use 1 km = 1,000 m. How many meters is the route?
  2. Three jumpers cleared these heights: Ravi 2 yd, Sol 5 ft, Tam 70 in. Use 1 yd = 3 ft and 1 ft = 12 in. Who jumped the highest?
    • Sol, with 5 feet
    • Tam, with 70 inches
    • Ravi, with 2 yards
    • All three are equal
  3. Water bottles at Home Field hold 1 pint. Use 1 gal = 8 pt. How many pints are in 3 gallons?
  4. A shot put weighs 4,500 g. The scoreboard shows weights in kilograms. Use 1 kg = 1,000 g. How many kilograms is the shot put?
  5. Coach Dara lines up 12 relay batons end to end. Each baton is 30 cm long. Use 1 m = 100 cm. How long is the line?
    • 0.36 m
    • 36 m
    • 360 m
    • 3.6 m
  6. Create your own Home Field conversion problem with two steps, such as yards to feet to inches. Show each conversion factor and each step, and explain how you know your answer is reasonable.
