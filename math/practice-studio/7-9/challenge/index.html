reflectionreflexión
A flip of a point or figure across a line, like a mirror. The image is the same distance from the line, on the other side.Un volteo de un punto o una figura sobre una línea, como un espejo. La imagen queda a la misma distancia de la línea, del otro lado.
x-axiseje x
The horizontal number line. Reflecting across it changes the sign of y: (x, y) → (x, −y).La recta numérica horizontal. Reflejar sobre ella cambia el signo de y: (x, y) → (x, −y).
y-axiseje y
The vertical number line. Reflecting across it changes the sign of x: (x, y) → (−x, y).La recta numérica vertical. Reflejar sobre ella cambia el signo de x: (x, y) → (−x, y).
symmetrysimetría
A figure has symmetry when one side is the mirror image of the other side.Una figura tiene simetría cuando un lado es la imagen en espejo del otro lado.
perpendicularperpendicular
Two lines that meet at a square corner (90°). A point and its image are joined by a segment perpendicular to the axis.Dos líneas que se cruzan formando una esquina cuadrada (90°). Un punto y su imagen se unen con un segmento perpendicular al eje.

Build it right.Constrúyelo bien.

Late-night rush in the Maker Workshop: a client wants a sign that is perfectly symmetrical, and the wood is too expensive to waste. Check every mirror point before the laser fires.

Trabajo urgente de noche en el Taller de Creadores: un cliente quiere un letrero perfectamente simétrico, y la madera es demasiado cara para desperdiciarla. Revisa cada punto del espejo antes de que dispare el láser.

1. Climb the skill ladder

Each rung is harder than the last, and the numbers change every time.

Cada peldaño es más difícil que el anterior y los números cambian cada vez.

  1. 6 fresh problems with new numbers each time (interactive only).

2. Multi-step missions

Real situations that need more than one step: plan, solve, then check that your answer makes sense.

Situaciones reales que necesitan más de un paso: planea, resuelve y revisa que tu respuesta tenga sentido.

  1. Find the Reflection Error
  2. A triangle has vertices at A(1, 2), B(4, 2), and C(4, 6). If you reflect the entire triangle over the y-axis, what are the new vertices? What do you notice about the size and shape of the reflected triangle compared to the original?
  3. Five holes are marked on a workshop panel: A(−5, 2), B(5, −2), C(−2, 5), D(5, 2) and E(2, −5). Which two holes are reflections of each other across the y-axis?
    • B and D
    • A and D
    • A and B
    • A and E
  4. A rectangular tile has vertices (2, 1), (7, 1), (7, 4) and (2, 4). A copy of the tile is made by reflecting it across the y-axis. How many units apart are the two tiles at their closest edges?
  5. Complete the laser's reflection log. Each row shows a point, the axis it is reflected across, and its image. Fill in every empty cell.
  6. A bracket ends at (6, −9) after two reflections: first across the x-axis, then across the y-axis. Where did the bracket start?
    • (−6, 9)
    • (6, 9)
    • (−6, −9)
    • (6, −9)

3. Build it, move it, test it

Use the tools to build the idea. Then check it.

Usa las herramientas para construir la idea. Luego compruébala.

  1. Point P is at (4, 3). Sort each point by how it is related to P.
  2. 2 fresh problems with new numbers each time (interactive only).

4. Detective work

Somebody made a mistake. Find the step, then decide who is right.

Alguien cometió un error. Encuentra el paso y decide quién tiene razón.

  1. Dmitri reflects the point (−4, 9) across the x-axis.
  2. Lena reflects the point (6, −5) across the y-axis.
  3. Kai reflects a bolt at (3, 8) across the y-axis.
  4. Noor, Mateo and Lin reflect the point (5, 0) across the x-axis. Noor says the reflection is (5, 0). Mateo says it is (−5, 0). Lin says it is (0, 5). Who is right?
    • Mateo: every reflection changes one of the signs.
    • Lin: the two coordinates trade places.
    • Nobody: a point on an axis has no reflection.
    • Noor: the point is already on the x-axis, so it stays where it is.

5. Boss level

Tell us how sure you are, then solve. Finish with a problem of your own.

Dinos qué tan seguro estás y luego resuelve. Termina con un problema tuyo.

  1. Point P is in Quadrant II. P is reflected across the x-axis. In which quadrant is the reflection of P?
    • Quadrant I
    • Quadrant II
    • Quadrant III
    • Quadrant IV
  2. A rectangle has vertices (−7, 2), (−3, 2), (−3, 5) and (−7, 5). It is reflected across the x-axis. What is the y-coordinate of the TOP edge of the image?
  3. Triangle JKL has vertices J(2, 1), K(6, 1) and L(2, 5). Which list gives the vertices J′, K′ and L′ of its reflection across the y-axis?
    • J′(2, −1), K′(6, −1), L′(2, −5)
    • J′(−2, −1), K′(−6, −1), L′(−2, −5)
    • J′(−2, 1), K′(6, 1), L′(2, 5)
    • J′(−2, 1), K′(−6, 1), L′(−2, 5)
  4. A point in Quadrant I and its reflection across the y-axis are 14 units apart. The point is 3 units above the x-axis. What are the coordinates of the point?
    • (7, 3)
    • (14, 3)
    • (3, 7)
    • (−7, 3)
  5. A kite on the grid has vertices (0, 6), (3, 2), (0, −2) and (−3, 2). Which statement is true?
    • It is symmetric across the x-axis, but not across the y-axis.
    • It is symmetric across the y-axis, but not across the x-axis.
    • It is symmetric across both axes.
    • It is not symmetric across either axis.
  6. Choose a point that is not on either axis. Reflect it across the x-axis, across the y-axis, and across both axes. List the three images, then explain what stays the same and what changes each time, using the words reflection and axis.
