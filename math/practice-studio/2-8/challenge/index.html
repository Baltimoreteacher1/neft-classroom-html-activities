Build it right.Constrúyelo bien.

After hours the workbenches go quiet and the lamps stay on. One job log has a number far out of line, and only a careful maker can find the mean that stays honest.

Después del horario, los bancos de trabajo quedan en silencio y las lámparas siguen encendidas. Un registro tiene un número muy fuera de lugar, y solo un creador atento puede hallar la media que sigue siendo honesta.

1. Climb the skill ladder

Each rung is harder than the last, and the numbers change every time.

Cada peldaño es más difícil que el anterior y los números cambian cada vez.

  1. 6 fresh problems with new numbers each time (interactive only).

2. Multi-step missions

Real situations that need more than one step: plan, solve, then check that your answer makes sense.

Situaciones reales que necesitan más de un paso: planea, resuelve y revisa que tu respuesta tenga sentido.

  1. Sofia realized she made an error: she actually scored 12 points in Game 8, not 10. Her other games are unchanged (12, 20, 16, 11, 17, 14, 15, and 18). How does this change the number of points she needs in Game 10 to average 15 points per game? Show how you know.
  2. Fill in the table
  3. A data set has a mean of 20. Every value in the data set is then increased by 4. What is the new mean?
    • 5
    • 20
    • 24
    • 80
  4. Six makers logged their weekly bench time in hours: 3.5, 4, 2.5, 5, 4.5, 3. What is the mean number of hours?
  5. A maker sold 12, 15, 9, 14 bracelets in the first four weeks. How many must she sell in week 5 for the mean of the five weeks to be 13?
  6. Each dot is one workbench’s count of finished clamps. If the dots were weights on a beam, where would the beam balance?
    • 4
    • 6
    • 5
    • 9
  7. A shop timed 5 sanding jobs in minutes: 8, 9, 10, 9, 8. Then the last job turns out to take 38 minutes instead of 8. Fill in the total and the mean before and after the change.

3. Build it, move it, test it

Use the tools to build the idea. Then check it.

Usa las herramientas para construir la idea. Luego compruébala.

  1. Sort each statement: does it describe the mean or the median?
  2. 2 fresh problems with new numbers each time (interactive only).

4. Detective work

Somebody made a mistake. Find the step, then decide who is right.

Alguien cometió un error. Encuentra el paso y decide quién tiene razón.

  1. Pilar finds the mean of the sawing times 14, 9, 11 and 6 minutes.
  2. Tevita finds the mean number of nails used on four jobs: 4, 0, 6 and 2.
  3. Anneke finds the mean of the glue amounts 2, 3, 3, 4 and 18 grams.
  4. Ola wants a mean of 80 on four quality checks. Her first three scores are 70, 85 and 90. Luis says she needs 80 on the last check. Sia says she needs 75. Who is right?
    • Sia, because the four scores must total 4 × 80 = 320
    • Luis, because the last score should match the target mean
    • Neither, because she needs 95
    • Both, because 75 and 80 are close

5. Boss level

Tell us how sure you are, then solve. Finish with a problem of your own.

Dinos qué tan seguro estás y luego resuelve. Termina con un problema tuyo.

  1. Eight pieces of cedar have lengths of 3.4, 5.1, 4.8, 6.2, 4.5, 5, 3.9, 5.7 meters. What is the mean length?
  2. The mean of 5 numbers is 12. What is the sum of the 5 numbers?
    • 60
    • 17
    • 2.4
    • 12
  3. Six drill bits have a mean length of 8.5 centimeters. Five of them are 7, 9, 8, 10, 6 centimeters. How long is the sixth?
  4. A shop sells boards that cost $12, $14, $13, $15 and $96. Which statement is true when the $96 board is included?
    • The mean is the middle price, $14
    • The mean is less than four of the five prices
    • The mean is greater than four of the five prices
    • The mean is greater than all five prices
  5. A student says the mean of 4, 7, 9 and 12 is 32. How can you tell without calculating that she is wrong?
    • The mean must be an even number
    • The mean must be between the least and greatest values, 4 and 12
    • The mean must be one of the numbers in the list
    • The mean must be less than 4
  6. Create your own problem. Write 5 or 6 numbers about a workshop, such as nails used or minutes sanding. Find the mean, then explain the mean as the fair share and as the balance point. Use the words mean, average, balance point and data set.
