After a storm, the harbor office finds several shipping sheets with mistakes in them. Find the wrong step, fix the order of operations, and rewrite each bill correctly.

Después de una tormenta, la oficina del puerto encuentra varias hojas de envío con errores. Encuentra el paso equivocado, corrige el orden de las operaciones y reescribe cada factura correctamente.

1. Climb the skill ladder

Each rung is harder than the last, and the numbers change every time.

Cada peldaño es más difícil que el anterior y los números cambian cada vez.

  1. 6 fresh problems with new numbers each time (interactive only).

2. Multi-step missions

Real situations that need more than one step: plan, solve, then check that your answer makes sense.

Situaciones reales que necesitan más de un paso: planea, resuelve y revisa que tu respuesta tenga sentido.

  1. Find the error
  2. Seven friends split the bill for a pizza party. They order 4 pizzas at 3² dollars each, plus a one-time $6 delivery fee. Write ONE numerical expression for what each friend owes, evaluate it, and explain why the grouping symbols matter.
  3. Fill in the table
  4. A Harbor Works ship carries 4 stacks with 3² containers in each stack, plus 6 loose containers on the deck. Which expression gives the total number of containers?
    • (4 × 3)² + 6
    • 4 × 3² + 6
    • 4 × 3 × 2 + 6
    • 4 + 3² + 6
  5. The harbor charges a dock fee of 5² dollars, plus a crane fee of $12 for each of 4 containers, minus a discount of 2³ dollars. Write one expression and evaluate it. What is the total fee in dollars?
  6. Evaluate two expressions in order, one step at a time. Fill in the result of each step.
  7. Five dockworkers share the cost of 3 pallets that cost 2³ dollars each, plus a $16 rope fee. Which expression gives what each dockworker pays?
    • 3 × 2³ + 16 ÷ 5
    • (3 × 2³ + 16) × 5
    • (3 × 2 × 3 + 16) ÷ 5
    • (3 × 2³ + 16) ÷ 5

3. Build it, move it, test it

Use the tools to build the idea. Then check it.

Usa las herramientas para construir la idea. Luego compruébala.

  1. Evaluate each expression in the correct order. Is its value equal to 20?
  2. Match each expression to its value.
  3. Evaluate 5 + 3² × 2 − 4. Tap its value.

4. Detective work

Somebody made a mistake. Find the step, then decide who is right.

Alguien cometió un error. Encuentra el paso y decide quién tiene razón.

  1. Tavi evaluates 6 + 2 × 3². Tap the step where his work first goes wrong.
  2. Sade evaluates 3 × 4². Tap the step where her work first goes wrong.
  3. Pia writes an expression for 6 crates that each hold 2³ items, plus 5 extra items, and evaluates it. Tap the step where her work first goes wrong.
  4. Ana says (8 − 2)² and 8 − 2² have the same value, because they use the same numbers and symbols. Ben says they are different. Who is right?
    • Ana, because the same numbers always give the same value
    • Ben, because 8 − 2² equals 36
    • Ben, because the parentheses change which operation comes first
    • Neither, because both equal 4

5. Boss level

Tell us how sure you are, then solve. Finish with a problem of your own.

Dinos qué tan seguro estás y luego resuelve. Termina con un problema tuyo.

  1. Evaluate 5 × 2³ − 4².
  2. A crane lifts 3 loads of 2⁴ kilograms each and then a 5 kilogram hook. Which expression gives the total weight lifted?
    • 3 × 2⁴ + 5
    • 3 × 2 × 4 + 5
    • (3 × 2)⁴ + 5
    • 3 × 2⁴ × 5
  3. Evaluate (18 − 6) ÷ 2² + 3².
  4. Which expression has the greatest value?
    • 2 + 3 × 4²
    • (2 + 3) × 4²
    • (2 + 3 × 4)²
    • 2 + (3 × 4)²
  5. A ship has 5 deck rows with 2² containers in each, and 3 hold rows with 3² containers in each. Write one expression and evaluate it. How many containers are on the ship?
  6. Create your own Harbor Works problem with a power. Tell a story, write ONE numerical expression that includes a power, evaluate it step by step, and explain why the order of operations matters.
