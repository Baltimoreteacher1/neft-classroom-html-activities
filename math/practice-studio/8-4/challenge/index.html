Keep the port running.Mantén el puerto en marcha.

A storm rolls in and the harbor master hands you a stack of damaged signs. Rebuild every rule from clues, and be careful about whether the limit itself is allowed.

Llega una tormenta y el capitán del puerto te entrega una pila de letreros dañados. Reconstruye cada regla con pistas y ten cuidado con si el propio límite está permitido.

1. Climb the skill ladder

Each rung is harder than the last, and the numbers change every time.

Cada peldaño es más difícil que el anterior y los números cambian cada vez.

  1. 6 fresh problems with new numbers each time (interactive only).

2. Multi-step missions

Real situations that need more than one step: plan, solve, then check that your answer makes sense.

Situaciones reales que necesitan más de un paso: planea, resuelve y revisa que tu respuesta tenga sentido.

  1. Find the Inequality Error
  2. Write two different real-world situations: one that uses > and one that uses ≥. Explain why the situations need different symbols even though both mean 'bigger.'
  3. The graph shows every weight a harbor crane can lift, in tons. Which inequality describes it, with w for the weight?
    • w > 12
    • w ≥ 12
    • w < 12
    • w ≤ 12
  4. A cargo hold can carry no more than 500 crates, and 230 crates are already loaded. Let c be the number of additional crates. Which inequality shows the limit?
    • c + 230 < 500
    • c + 230 ≤ 500
    • c + 230 ≥ 500
    • c ≤ 500
  5. Each row is a harbor rule. Type the boundary number. Then type 1 if the boundary itself is allowed, or 0 if it is not.
  6. Which situation can be written as x ≥ 30?
    • A ship must carry at least 30 crates.
    • The elevator allows no more than 30 people.
    • Fewer than 30 ships dock today.
    • More than 30 sailors are on shore.

3. Build it, move it, test it

Use the tools to build the idea. Then check it.

Usa las herramientas para construir la idea. Luego compruébala.

  1. Sort each phrase by the symbol it needs.
  2. 2 fresh problems with new numbers each time (interactive only).

4. Detective work

Somebody made a mistake. Find the step, then decide who is right.

Alguien cometió un error. Encuentra el paso y decide quién tiene razón.

  1. Imani writes an inequality for 'Harbor passes are for sailors at least 16 years old'.
  2. Pavel writes an inequality for 'The cargo hold holds at most 40 crates'.
  3. Declan writes an inequality for 'The number of boats is more than 7'.
  4. A sign on the dock says 'Maximum load 9 tons'. Marcus writes w ≤ 9. Lupe writes w < 9. Who is right?
    • Lupe, because the load must be below the maximum.
    • Both, because 9 is the boundary in each.
    • Marcus, because 9 tons is allowed and smaller loads are too.
    • Neither, because the inequality should be w ≥ 9.

5. Boss level

Tell us how sure you are, then solve. Finish with a problem of your own.

Dinos qué tan seguro estás y luego resuelve. Termina con un problema tuyo.

  1. Which phrase matches y < 15?
    • y is at most 15
    • y is fewer than 15
    • y is at least 15
    • y is no fewer than 15
  2. Which inequality represents 'No fewer than 6 sailors must be on deck'?
    • s ≤ 6
    • s < 6
    • s > 6
    • s ≥ 6
  3. A ferry may carry at most 9 cars. The number of cars c can be 0, 1, 2 and so on, in whole numbers. How many different values of c are allowed?
  4. Which value of p makes p ≥ 50 true?
    • p = 40
    • p = 49.9
    • p = 50
    • p = 5
  5. Which phrase needs a strict symbol, < or >, with no equal line?
    • at most 20 boats
    • fewer than 20 boats
    • no more than 20 boats
    • 20 boats or fewer
  6. Make up a harbor rule that uses a limit, such as a weight, a number of people or a fee. Write it in words and as an inequality, then say whether the boundary number is allowed.
