1. Climb the skill ladder

Each rung is harder than the last, and the numbers change every time.

Cada peldaño es más difícil que el anterior y los números cambian cada vez.

  1. 6 fresh problems with new numbers each time (interactive only).

2. Multi-step missions

Real situations that need more than one step: plan, solve, then check that your answer makes sense.

Situaciones reales que necesitan más de un paso: planea, resuelve y revisa que tu respuesta tenga sentido.

  1. Find the Stats Error
  2. A basketball player scored 15, 18, 16, 42, and 14 points in 5 games. Find the mean and median. Which better represents the player's typical game? Explain how the outlier affects each measure.
  3. Fill in the table
  4. Case 14: detectives timed 9 witnesses walking to the scene. Their times in minutes were 12, 7, 15, 9, 21, 8, 11, 14, 10. What is the median time?
  5. The lab measured 6 footprints at the scene. Their lengths in centimeters were 24, 27, 22, 30, 25, 26. What is the median length?
  6. A suspect’s six alibi checks took 31, 28, 33, 30, 29, 95 minutes. One check, 95, was far longer than the rest. Which statement best describes a typical check time?
    • Median 30.5 min: one long check shifts it little.
    • The mean, 41 minutes, because it uses every time
    • The largest time, 95 minutes, because it stands out
    • The median, 30 minutes, the lower middle value
  7. The team has five clue scores: 8, 14, 5, 11, 20. A sixth clue arrives with a score of 17. What is the median of all six scores?

3. Build it, move it, test it

Use the tools to build the idea. Then check it.

Usa las herramientas para construir la idea. Luego compruébala.

  1. Find the median of each data set in your head. Then sort the sets: is the median one of the numbers in the set, or a number between two of them?
  2. Write your own set of 8 numbers, in any order, with a median of 14. Show them in order and circle the two middle numbers. Then replace your greatest number with a much larger outlier and tell whether the median changes.
  3. 4 fresh problems with new numbers each time (interactive only).

4. Detective work

Somebody made a mistake. Find the step, then decide who is right.

Alguien cometió un error. Encuentra el paso y decide quién tiene razón.

  1. Hiro finds the median of the clue counts 18, 5, 9, 20, 12 collected from five rooms.
  2. Rosa finds the median of the fingerprint counts 6, 11, 15, 22, 28, 30, already in order.
  3. Sven finds the median of the tip counts 4, 6, 7, 9, 12, 13, 20, already in order.
  4. The clue scores are 3, 8, 11, 20, 40. Andre says that if 40 is changed to 400, the median changes to 400. Wen says the median stays the same. Who is right?
    • Wen, because the middle value is still 11
    • Andre, because the largest value is in the median
    • Both, because the median depends on every value
    • Neither, because the median becomes 20
  5. What is the best next move?
    • Average the two middle scores.
    • Add the two middle scores and use that sum.
    • Take the 3rd score, 18, as the median.
    • Add all six scores and divide by 6.
  6. 1 fresh problem with new numbers each time (interactive only).

5. Boss level

Tell us how sure you are, then solve. Finish with a problem of your own.

Dinos qué tan seguro estás y luego resuelve. Termina con un problema tuyo.

  1. Seven witnesses gave their ages: 23, 31, 18, 35, 27, 20, 29. What is the median age?
  2. In an ordered list of 10 evidence weights, the 5th weight is 18 grams and the 6th weight is 25 grams. What is the median?
    • 18 grams
    • 21.5 grams
    • 25 grams
    • 43 grams
  3. Each dot is one tip the hotline received per hour. What is the median number of tips?
    • 3
    • 15
    • 8
    • 5
  4. Four numbers in order are 10, 14, x and 30. Their median is 17. What is x?
  5. The median time to solve a puzzle in a group of detectives is 12 minutes. Which statement must be true?
    • Most detectives took exactly 12 minutes
    • All the times add up to 12 times the number of detectives
    • At least half took 12 min or less; half took 12 min or more.
    • No detective took more than 12 minutes
  6. Create your own problem. Write a data set of 7 or 8 numbers about a case, for example witness ages or clue counts. Find the median and explain what it tells you about a typical value. Use the words order, middle, typical and half.
