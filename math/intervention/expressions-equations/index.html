Differentiation

Level 1 — Support

Level 1 (support): substitute carefully, one step at a time, and circle the operation that 'undoes' the equation.

Level 2 — Stretch

Level 2 (stretch): do the ★ word-problem-to-equation translations and check each solution by substituting back.
