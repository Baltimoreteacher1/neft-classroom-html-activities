Area, Surface Area & Volume

Find area, surface area, and volume of real shapes — triangles, prisms, and composite figures.

Learning objective

I can find the area of triangles, parallelograms, trapezoids, and composite figures, and the surface area and volume of rectangular prisms.

🎯 Builds 6.G.A.1–4 📚 4 lessons ⏱ 30 min

Core question

How can I model, solve, and explain area, Surface Area & Volume so another student understands my thinking?

Evidence of mastery

Score 80% or higher, correct one missed item in Smart Review, and write a complete explanation using at least one vocabulary word.

Key vocabulary

  • Area — The amount of flat space a shape covers.
  • Base and height — A chosen side and the straight-up distance to the opposite point.
  • Parallelogram — A four-sided shape with two pairs of parallel sides.
  • Trapezoid — A four-sided shape with exactly one pair of parallel sides.
  • Volume — The amount of space inside a solid, in cubic units.
  • Surface area — The total area of all the faces of a solid.

Worked examples

Study these three examples — easy to challenge — then head to Practice.

Example 1Warm-up

Find the area of a triangle with base 8 cm and height 5 cm.

  1. Write the triangle area formula: A = ½ × base × height.
  2. Put in the numbers: A = ½ × 8 × 5.
  3. Multiply the base and height: 8 × 5 = 40.
  4. Take half: ½ × 40 = 20.
Answer: 20 cm²
Example 2On level

Find the area of a trapezoid with parallel sides 6 m and 10 m and a height of 4 m.

  1. Write the trapezoid formula: A = (base₁ + base₂) ÷ 2 × height.
  2. Add the two parallel sides: 6 + 10 = 16.
  3. Divide by 2 to get the average width: 16 ÷ 2 = 8.
  4. Multiply by the height: 8 × 4 = 32.
Answer: 32 m²
Example 3Challenge

A gift box is 5 in by 4 in by 3 in. Find its surface area, then its volume.

  1. For surface area, find the area of the three different faces: 5×4 = 20, 5×3 = 15, and 4×3 = 12.
  2. Add those three faces: 20 + 15 + 12 = 47.
  3. Double it because each face has a matching opposite face: 2 × 47 = 94 in².
  4. For volume, multiply all three edges: 5 × 4 × 3 = 60 in³.
Answer: Surface area = 94 in², Volume = 60 in³
