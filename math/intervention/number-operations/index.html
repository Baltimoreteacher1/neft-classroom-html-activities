🔢 Builds 6.NS.B.2–3

Whole-Number Operations & Fluency

Rebuild fast, accurate multi-digit multiplication and long division — the engine behind every Grade 6 unit.

Learning objective

I can multiply and divide multi-digit whole numbers, estimate to check my work, and use the order of operations to solve real-world problems.

🎯 Builds 6.NS.B.2–3 📚 4 lessons ⏱ 30 min

Key vocabulary

  • Product — The answer when you multiply two numbers.
  • Quotient — The answer when you divide one number by another.
  • Remainder — The amount left over after dividing evenly.
  • Estimate — A close guess made by rounding numbers first.
  • Order of operations — The rules for which step to do first.
  • Partial product — A piece of a multiplication you add up later.

Materials

  • Pencil and lined paper
  • Place-value grid or graph paper
  • Multiplication facts chart

Worked examples

Study these three examples — easy to challenge — then head to Practice.

Example 1Warm-up

Multiply 34 × 6.

  1. Break 34 into 30 + 4.
  2. Multiply each part: 6 × 30 = 180 and 6 × 4 = 24.
  3. Add the partial products: 180 + 24 = 204.
Answer: 204
Example 2On level

Divide 487 ÷ 6 and write the remainder.

  1. 6 goes into 48 eight times: 6 × 8 = 48, leaving 7.
  2. Bring down the 7 to make 07.
  3. 6 goes into 7 one time: 6 × 1 = 6, leaving a remainder of 1.
  4. The quotient is 81 with remainder 1.
Answer: 81 R1
Example 3Challenge

Evaluate 4 + 3 × (10 − 6)² using order of operations.

  1. Do parentheses first: 10 − 6 = 4.
  2. Apply the exponent: 4² = 16.
  3. Multiply: 3 × 16 = 48.
  4. Add last: 4 + 48 = 52.
Answer: 52

🔢 Whole-Number Operations & Fluency Set B · Application

Name: ____________________ Date: __________
A box holds 24 crayons. How many crayons are in 15 boxes?
A baker has 156 muffins packed 12 to a tray. How many full trays does she fill?
Tickets cost $8 each. Maria buys 45 tickets and pays a $30 service fee. What is her total cost?
A school orders 500 pencils packed 7 to a pouch. How many full pouches, and how many pencils are left over?
A class reads 36 books each month for 24 months. How many books did they read in all?
A field trip has 720 students riding buses that hold 15 each. How many buses are needed?
A store splits 2,476 marbles equally into 8 jars. How many marbles per jar, and how many are left over?
Estimate the cost of 28 shirts that sell for $412 each by rounding to the nearest ten and hundred.
A pizza shop sold 19 pizzas at a fair. Each pizza was cut into 64 small bites. How many bites in all?
A team scored points using 4 + 18 ÷ 3 × 2. How many points did they score?
A library has 1,250 books to place on shelves that hold 25 books each. How many shelves are filled?

👪 Family Letter Whole-Number Operations & Fluency

Dear Family,

This week your student is working on whole-number operations & fluency. The goal is: I can multiply and divide multi-digit whole numbers, estimate to check my work, and use the order of operations to solve real-world problems.

Words to know at home:

  • Product — The answer when you multiply two numbers.
  • Quotient — The answer when you divide one number by another.
  • Remainder — The amount left over after dividing evenly.
  • Estimate — A close guess made by rounding numbers first.
  • Order of operations — The rules for which step to do first.
  • Partial product — A piece of a multiplication you add up later.

How to help: ask your student to teach you one example out loud, look for these ideas in everyday life (shopping, cooking, time, money), and praise effort and clear explanations — not just right answers.
