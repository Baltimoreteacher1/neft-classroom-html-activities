Statistics & Data

Ask statistical questions and summarize data with center, spread, and graphs — making sense of numbers.

Learning objective

I can write statistical questions and find and compare the mean, median, mode, range, and mean absolute deviation of a data set, and read dot plots, histograms, and box plots.

🎯 Builds 6.SP.A–B 📚 4 lessons ⏱ 30 min

Core question

How can I model, solve, and explain statistics & Data so another student understands my thinking?

Evidence of mastery

Score 80% or higher, correct one missed item in Smart Review, and write a complete explanation using at least one vocabulary word.

Key vocabulary

  • Statistical question — A question with answers that vary, not just one answer.
  • Mean — The average; add all values, then divide by how many.
  • Median — The middle value when data is put in order.
  • Mode — The value that shows up most often.
  • Range — The largest value minus the smallest value.
  • Mean absolute deviation — The average distance each value is from the mean.

Worked examples

Study these three examples — easy to challenge — then head to Practice.

Example 1Warm-up

Find the mean of 4, 8, 6, 2, and 5.

  1. Add all the values: 4 + 8 + 6 + 2 + 5 = 25.
  2. Count how many values there are: 5.
  3. Divide the sum by the count: 25 / 5 = 5.
Answer: Mean = 5
Example 2On level

Find the median of 7, 3, 9, 5, 3, and 11.

  1. Put the numbers in order: 3, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11.
  2. There are 6 numbers (even), so find the two middle ones: 5 and 7.
  3. Average the two middle numbers: (5 + 7) / 2 = 6.
Answer: Median = 6
Example 3Challenge

Find the mean absolute deviation (MAD) of 5, 8, 8, 11, and 13.

  1. Find the mean: (5 + 8 + 8 + 11 + 13) / 5 = 45 / 5 = 9.
  2. Find each value's distance from 9: 4, 1, 1, 2, 4.
  3. Add the distances: 4 + 1 + 1 + 2 + 4 = 12.
  4. Divide by the number of values: 12 / 5 = 2.4.
Answer: MAD = 2.4

📈 Statistics & Data Set B · Application

Name: ____________________ Date: __________
A class took a survey. Which is a statistical question: 'What is my age?' or 'What are the ages of students in this school?' Explain by naming the better one.
Liam's five quiz scores are 8, 6, 10, 9, and 7. What is his mean score?
A bakery sold 12, 15, 9, 12, and 17 muffins over five days. What is the median number sold?
Daily high temperatures were 70, 72, 72, 75, and 81 degrees. What is the mode?
The fastest runner finished in 52 seconds and the slowest in 78 seconds. What is the range of times?
A dot plot shows pets owned: 0 (3 dots), 1 (5 dots), 2 (2 dots). How many students were surveyed?
A histogram shows hours of sleep: 6-7 hrs (4 people), 7-8 hrs (9 people), 8-9 hrs (5 people). How many people slept at least 7 hours?
A box plot of game scores has minimum 8 and maximum 32. What is the range?
Ben scored 18, 22, 20, and 20 points in four games. What was his mean points per game?
Five plants grew 5, 7, 7, 9, and 12 cm. The mean is 8 cm. What is the mean absolute deviation?
A team's mean score over 5 games was 14. What was the team's total score?
★ Three of Ava's test scores are 78, 85, and 92. What must she score on the fourth test to have a mean of 85?

  5. 26 seconds
  6. 10 students
  7. 14 people
  8. 24
