Percents & Proportions

Convert fractions, decimals, and percents and solve real percent problems — discounts, tax, and tips.

Learning objective

I can find the percent of a number, change between fractions, decimals, and percents, find the whole, and solve discount, tax, and tip problems.

🎯 Builds 6.AT.A.3c 📚 4 lessons ⏱ 30 min

Core question

How can I model, solve, and explain percents & Proportions so another student understands my thinking?

Concept spine

Understand the situation, represent it, choose the strategy, then prove the answer.

Evidence of mastery

Score 80% or higher, correct one missed item in Smart Review, and write a complete explanation using at least one vocabulary word.

Key vocabulary

  • Percent — A part out of 100; the symbol is %.
  • Percent of a number — Multiplying a percent by a number to find a part.
  • Discount — An amount taken off the regular price.
  • Sales tax — Extra money added to a price, set by the government.
  • Tip — Extra money you give for good service.
  • The whole — The total amount that a part comes from.

Worked examples

Study these three examples — easy to challenge — then head to Practice.

Example 1Warm-up

Find 20% of 45.

  1. Change the percent to a decimal: 20% = 0.20.
  2. Multiply the decimal by the number: 0.20 times 45.
  3. 0.20 times 45 equals 9.
Answer: 9
Example 2On level

A $50 game is 20% off. What is the sale price?

  1. Find the discount: 20% of 50 = 0.20 times 50 = $10.
  2. Subtract the discount from the regular price: 50 minus 10.
  3. The sale price is $40.
Answer: $40
Example 3Challenge

A $40 dinner has a 15% tip and 6% tax added. What is the total cost?

  1. Find the tip: 15% of 40 = 0.15 times 40 = $6.
  2. Find the tax: 6% of 40 = 0.06 times 40 = $2.40.
  3. Add everything together: 40 + 6 + 2.40 = $48.40.
Answer: $48.40

Error clinic

Use this as a quick conference script after a missed diagnostic, a worksheet error, or a low post-quiz score.

Likely misconception

Students may answer procedurally without explaining why the method works for percents & Proportions.

Teacher move

Look for accurate use of % of a number, conversions, find the whole, and discounts and a clear explanation.

Student self-check

Can I show the problem with a model, name the operation or relationship, and explain why my answer is reasonable?

Two-minute conference

  1. Ask the student to point to the exact step where the answer changed.
  2. Have the student restate the problem using one vocabulary word from this topic.
  3. Rebuild one simpler example together, then ask the student to solve a parallel problem alone.

Performance task

Students apply percents & Proportions in a short constructed-response task. This gives publishers, teachers, and families evidence beyond multiple choice.

Scenario

Percents & Proportions in the real world

Create a realistic situation where someone must use % of a number, conversions, find the whole, and discounts. Solve it two ways: first with a model or diagram, then with numbers or symbols. Finish by explaining why the answer is reasonable.

Student deliverables

  • A labeled model, diagram, table, or number line.
  • A complete solution with units or labels.
  • A written explanation using at least one vocabulary word.
  • A revised answer after checking for the common misconception.
