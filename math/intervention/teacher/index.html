Teacher edition overview

Implementation guide for a publishable intervention block

This guide is built to support tutoring, pull-out intervention, stations, after-school programs, and independent recovery without changing the core routine from topic to topic.

Planning

Before instruction

Assign the pre-quiz, preview vocabulary, choose the pathway, and prepare the concrete model for the topic.

Instruction

During instruction

Open the larger concept, teach the focused mini-lesson students need, then release students into Practice, Smart Review, fluency, and print work.

Evidence

After instruction

Collect exit tickets, performance tasks, and post-quiz data to decide whether students need reteach, practice, or extension.

Equity

Access for all learners

Use read-aloud, discourse frames, family letters, vocabulary routines, and Talk-Write-Revise supports every cycle.

How to run it

The four-part routine

Each unit is a self-paced station with an explicit concept opening and smaller mini-lessons inside the broader topic. Reserve 2–4 short sessions per unit.

Step 1

Assign the Pre-Quiz

Use the student Google Form to baseline. The teacher form auto-grades with the key.

Step 2

Teach the mini-lesson

Open the concept spine, pick the mini-lesson students need, and use the model before practice.

Step 3

Send home

Print the family letter and exit ticket; assign Level 1 or Level 2 supports as needed.

Step 4

Post-Quiz & review

Re-assess with the post-quiz to measure growth and regroup.

A

Reteach group

Pre-quiz below 50% or diagnostic below 50%. Start with Concept Lab, worked examples, and a teacher-led model.

B

Guided practice group

Scores from 50% to 79%. Assign Practice, Smart Review, Error Clinic, and Worksheet A before the post-quiz.

C

Extension group

Scores 80%+. Use Fluency, Game, Worksheet B challenge, and the transfer prompt to keep growth moving.

Pacing & standards correlation

12-unit scope & sequence

Suggested order, the Grade 6 standard each unit builds toward, and approximate time.

#UnitStandardStudents will…Pacing
1 🔢Whole-Number Operations & Fluency 6.NS.B.2–3 multiply and divide multi-digit whole numbers, estimate to check my work, and use the order of operations to solve real-world problems. 4 × 30m
2 🧱Factors, Multiples & Primes 6.NS.B.4 find factors, multiples, GCF, LCM, and prime factorizations, and use the GCF with the distributive property. 4 × 30m
3 🍕Fraction Sense 6.NS.A.1 find equivalent fractions, write fractions in simplest form, compare fractions, add and subtract fractions with unlike denominators, and multiply and divide fractions. 4 × 30m
4 💯Decimals & Place Value 6.NS.B.3 read, compare, and round decimals and add, subtract, multiply, and divide decimals to solve money and measurement problems. 4 × 30m
5 ⚖️Ratios & Rates 6.RP.A.1–2 describe ratios using ratio language, make equivalent ratios with tables, find unit rates, and compare prices to find the better buy. 4 × 30m
6 📊Percents & Proportions 6.RP.A.3c find the percent of a number, change between fractions, decimals, and percents, find the whole, and solve discount, tax, and tip problems. 4 × 30m
7 🌡️Integers & the Number Line 6.NS.C.5–7 use, compare, order, and add and subtract integers, and find absolute value and opposites, to solve real-world problems. 4 × 30m
8 🧭The Coordinate Plane 6.NS.C.6,8 plot ordered pairs in all four quadrants, name quadrants and axes, find the distance between two points, and reflect points across the axes. 4 × 30m
9 🧮Expressions & Properties 6.EE.A.1–4 use exponents, evaluate expressions, write expressions from words, combine like terms, and use the distributive property. 4 × 30m
10 ➗One-Step Equations & Inequalities 6.EE.B.5–8 solve one-step equations using all four operations, check my solutions, write and graph simple inequalities, and tell apart dependent and independent variables. 4 × 30m
11 📐Area, Surface Area & Volume 6.G.A.1–4 find the area of triangles, parallelograms, trapezoids, and composite figures, and the surface area and volume of rectangular prisms. 4 × 30m
12 📈Statistics & Data 6.SP.A–B write statistical questions and find and compare the mean, median, mode, range, and mean absolute deviation of a data set, and read dot plots, histograms, and box plots. 4 × 30m
Small-group playbook

What to reteach when students miss it

This table turns results into teacher action: identify the misconception, choose the model, and require a complete math-talk explanation.

UnitLikely gapBest modelDiscourse frame
🔢Whole-Number Operations & Fluency Students often know the procedure but lose place value, skip a remainder, or stop checking whether the answer is reasonable. Build the operation with arrays, factor rainbows, or place-value boxes before recording an algorithm. My estimate was ___, so my exact answer is reasonable because ___.
🧱Factors, Multiples & Primes Students often know the procedure but lose place value, skip a remainder, or stop checking whether the answer is reasonable. Build the operation with arrays, factor rainbows, or place-value boxes before recording an algorithm. My estimate was ___, so my exact answer is reasonable because ___.
🍕Fraction Sense Students may compare digits instead of values, treat denominators as whole-number size, or forget that decimals and fractions can represent the same amount. Use area models, number lines, and place-value charts so students see the size of each quantity. These two values are equivalent because I can show them as ___ and ___.
