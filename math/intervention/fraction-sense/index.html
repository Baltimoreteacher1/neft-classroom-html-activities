Differentiation

Level 1 — Support

Level 1 (support): use the fraction bars in the activity and keep a multiplication chart handy for common denominators.

Level 2 — Stretch

Level 2 (stretch): do the ★ mixed-number problems and explain in words why 'divide by a fraction' makes a bigger number.
