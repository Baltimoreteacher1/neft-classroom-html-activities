Stretch: Writing and Graphing Inequalities

I can write an inequality from a real situation and identify which numbers are solutions.

🧠 The Challenge

inequalityat least (≥)at most (≤)solution setopen / closed circle

An inequality has many solutions. "At least 18" → x ≥ 18 uses a closed circle (18 included). "More than 18" → x > 18 uses an open circle.

👀 Worked Example

"You must be at least 48 inches to ride." Let h = height: h ≥ 48. So 48, 50, 60 all work; 47 does not.
