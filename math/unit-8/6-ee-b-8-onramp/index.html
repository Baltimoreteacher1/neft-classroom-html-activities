Inequalities Like x > 5: From Words to Symbols

I can write an inequality (x > c or x < c) from a real-life sentence.

📘 Word Bank + Sentence Frames

greater than (>)less than (<)at least (≥)at most (≤)variable x

  • "more than / greater than" → >  ·  "fewer than / less than" → <
  • "at least" → ≥  ·  "at most / no more than" → ≤
  • Frame: "Let x = ____. The inequality is x ____ ____."

👀 See It: A Worked Example

"You must be more than 5 years old to ride." Let x = your age.

WordsInequality
more than 5x > 5

On a number line, x > 5 uses an open circle at 5 (5 is not included) with the arrow going right.
