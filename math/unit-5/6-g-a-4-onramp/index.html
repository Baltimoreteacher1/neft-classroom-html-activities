Nets & Surface Area: Unfold the Box, Add the Faces

I can use a net to find the surface area of a 3-D figure by adding up its faces.

📘 Word Bank + Sentence Frames

net (unfolded shape)facesurface areaarea = L × W

  • A net is a 3-D shape "unfolded" flat so you can see every face.
  • Surface area = the area of all the faces added together.
  • Frame: "There are ____ faces. I add their areas: ____ = ____ square units."

👀 See It: A Worked Example
