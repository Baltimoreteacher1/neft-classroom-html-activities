Step 1 — Bedroom Floor Area
Calculate the area of your rectangular bedroom
Your job: Enter the length and width of your main rectangular bedroom area, then calculate the area using the formula: Area = length × width (l × w).
🟢 Level 1 Tip: Try friendly whole numbers like a length of 12 ft and a width of 10 ft.
LEVEL 2 CHALLENGE
If you double the length of your bedroom, what happens to the area? Explain using your numbers.
Need a hint?

To find the area of a rectangle, multiply the length by the width.
Example: Length = 12 ft, Width = 10 ft. Area = 12 × 10 = 120 sq ft.

📐
Step 2 — Triangle Reading Nook
Calculate the area of a triangular nook or alcove
Your job: Enter the base and height of your custom triangular reading nook. Then calculate the area using: Area = ½ × base × height (½ × b × h).
🟢 Level 1 Tip: Try numbers like a base of 6 ft and a height of 4 ft.
LEVEL 2 CHALLENGE
If you wanted the nook area to be exactly 16 sq ft, and the height was 4 ft, what would the base have to be? Show your equation.
Need a hint?

To find the area of a triangle, multiply the base by the height, then divide by 2 (or multiply by ½).
Example: Base = 6 ft, Height = 4 ft. Area = ½ × 6 × 4 = 3 × 4 = 12 sq ft.

💵
Step 3 — Flooring & Costs
Sum the composite floor areas and calculate flooring costs
Your job: We will add the rectangular bedroom area and triangular nook area together. Select your flooring type, set your price per sq ft, and calculate the total cost.
🟢 Level 1 Tip: Choose carpet ($3.50/sq ft) for a soft feel, or hardwood ($9.00/sq ft) for a modern look.
LEVEL 2 CHALLENGE
If you have a strict budget of $1,000 for bedroom flooring, does your choice fit? Calculate your remaining budget or how much extra you need.
