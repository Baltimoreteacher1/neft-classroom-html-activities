Step 1 — Room Floor Area
Calculate the total area of your rectangular floor plan
Your job: Plan the dimensions of your room's floor. Multiply the length by the width to calculate the total floor area in square feet.
🟢 Level 1 tip: Try friendly round numbers like 15 ft length and 12 ft width.
LEVEL 2 CHALLENGE — L-Shaped Composite Floor

If your room has an L-shaped floor plan with a main area of 12 ft by 15 ft and a closet extension of 4 ft by 5 ft, what is the total area? Explain how you split the shape and computed the sum.

Need a hint?

Area of a rectangle = Length × Width.
Example: A room that is 10 ft long and 10 ft wide has an area of 10 × 10 = 100 square feet.

🎨
Step 2 — Wall Paint Coverage
Find wall area, subtract window/door areas, and compute gallons of paint
Your job: Calculate the area of the 4 walls of your room. Find the total perimeter of the floor, multiply it by the wall height, and then subtract any windows or doors. Finally, find how many whole gallons of paint you need (1 gallon covers ~350 sq ft).
🟢 Level 1 tip: Try standard room metrics: Height = 8 ft, Window Area = 30 sq ft, Door Area = 20 sq ft.
LEVEL 2 CHALLENGE — Two Coats of Paint

If you want to apply two complete coats of paint to all walls, how would your calculations change? How many total whole gallons would you need to buy?

Need a hint?

1. Find gross wall area: 2 × (Length + Width) × Height.
2. Subtract window + door area to find Net Area.
3. Divide Net Area by 350, then round UP to find whole gallons to buy.

💵
Step 3 — Paint & Flooring Cost
Choose materials, calculate total cost, and compare with average budgets
Your job: Select your flooring type and paint quality. Calculate the total cost and check where your makeover falls compared to typical real-world ranges.
LEVEL 2 CHALLENGE — Budget Strategy ($1,500 limit)

If you have a strict limit of $1,500 for the makeover, what materials would you choose? Explain your optimization strategy.

Need a hint?

Flooring Cost = Floor Area × Flooring Price.
Paint Cost = Paint Gallons × Paint Price.
Total Cost = Flooring Cost + Paint Cost.

Compare your room makeover plan with a classmate
Your job: Ask a partner for their measurements and costs. Write their answers below, then press Compare to see the mathematical differences.
LEVEL 2 CHALLENGE — Cost Efficiency Reflection

Calculate your total cost per square foot of floor space (Total Cost ÷ Floor Area). Compare it to your partner's cost per square foot. What does this tell you about the efficiency of your material choices?

Write your pitch, check the list, and review the rubric
