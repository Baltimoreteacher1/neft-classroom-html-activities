Finding Side Lengths by Counting Units

I can find the length of a horizontal or vertical side by counting units between points.

📘 Word Bank + Sentence Frames

vertexhorizontalverticalside length

  • Horizontal side: subtract the x-values. Vertical side: subtract the y-values.
  • Frame: "From (____,____) to (____,____) the length is ____ units."

👀 See It

From (1, 2) to (6, 2): same y, so length = 6 − 1 = 5 units (a horizontal side).
