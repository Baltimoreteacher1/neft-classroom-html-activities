Fraction to Percent Scale denominator to 100 or divide numerator by denominator: ⅗ × 20/20 = 60/100 = 60%.
Decimal to Percent Multiply by 100 (shift decimal point 2 places right): 0.38 → 38%. 0.07 → 7%.
Percent to Fraction Put over 100 and simplify: 45% = 45/100 = 9/20.
Decimal to Fraction Read the place value: 0.6 = 6/10 = 3/5. 0.08 = 8/100 = 2/25.
