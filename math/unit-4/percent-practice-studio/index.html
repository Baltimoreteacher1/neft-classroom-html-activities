Percent (%) Means “per hundred” or “out of 100.” Example: 40% = 40/100 = 0.40.
100% = 1 Whole Less than 100% is less than 1 whole. Greater than 100% (like 150%) is more than 1 whole!
Shaded + Unshaded = 100% If 62 squares are shaded (62%), then 100 − 62 = 38 squares (38%) are NOT shaded.
Less Than 1% (like 0.5%) 1 square = 1%. Splitting 1 square in half gives 0.5% = 0.5/100 = 1/200!
