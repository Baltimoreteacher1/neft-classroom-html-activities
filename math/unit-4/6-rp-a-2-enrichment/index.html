Stretch: Which Is the Better Deal?

I can find and compare unit rates to decide which option is the better buy.

🧠 The Challenge

To compare deals fairly, put them on the same footing: the unit rate (price for one). Then the smaller unit price wins.

Example: Store A: 6 apples for $3 → $0.50 each. Store B: 10 apples for $4 → $0.40 each. Store B is the better deal.

✏️ Your Turn

0 of 3 checks complete

1. Brand X: 4 bars for $2.00. Brand Y: 6 bars for $2.40. Which is cheaper per bar?

2. Car A goes 180 mi on 6 gal. Car B goes 200 mi on 8 gal. Which gets more miles per gallon?

3. Which is the better unit rate for buying juice (lower price per liter)?
