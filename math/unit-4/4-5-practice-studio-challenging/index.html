Tab 1: Finding the Whole with Percents >100% and Micro-Percents (<1%)

When the percent is over 100%, the whole will be smaller than the part! When percent is <1%, the whole is massive.

Cuando el porcentaje supera el 100%, el total es menor que la parte. Cuando es menor a 1%, el total es enorme.
★ 140 is 175% of 80  |  6 is 0.75% of 800

Tab 2: The Toy Store Puzzle Problem (Combined Groups)

Solve compound multi-category inventory problems where two groups combined represent a known percent of the total.

Resuelve el problema de la juguetería donde dos grupos combinados forman un porcentaje del inventario total.
🧩 120 puzzles sold = 35% + 25% = 60% of inventory

Tab 3: Reverse Discounts & Net Take-Home Pay

Working backward from sale prices: if you pay $48 after a 20% discount, $48 represents 80% of the original price!

Calcula el precio original a partir del precio de oferta: si descuentan el 20%, pagas el 80% del total.
🏷 Sale Price = (100% - Discount%) × Original
