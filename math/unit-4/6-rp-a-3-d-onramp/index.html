Changing Units Using a Conversion Ratio

I can convert between units using a conversion ratio, like feet to inches.

📘 Word Bank + Sentence Frames

unitconversion ratiomultiplyequal measures

  • A conversion ratio links two units (1 ft = 12 in).
  • Multiply by the ratio to change units.

👀 See It

3 ft = 3 × 12 in = 36 in
