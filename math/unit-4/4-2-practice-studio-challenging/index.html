1. Values Beyond 100% and Less Than 1%

Percents greater than 100% represent more than one whole. Percents less than 1% represent tiny fractions of a single hundredth.

Porcentajes mayores que 100% representan más de un entero. Porcentajes menores que 1% son fracciones diminutas de un centésimo.

⚡ Key Insight: 150% = 1.5 = 1 ½  |  0.5% = 0.005 = 1/200  |  225% = 2.25 = 2 ¼

2. Eighths, Sixteenths, and Tough Denominators

When the denominator does not scale directly to 100 with whole numbers, use long division (numerator ÷ denominator) to find the decimal and percent.

Cuando el denominador no escala limpiamente a 100, divide numerador entre denominador.
