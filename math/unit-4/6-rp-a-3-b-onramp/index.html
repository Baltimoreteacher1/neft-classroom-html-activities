Using Unit Rates: Price and Speed

I can solve problems using a unit rate, like price per item or miles per hour.

📘 Word Bank + Sentence Frames

unit rateperprice per itemmiles per hour

  • Once you know the rate for one, multiply to find the rate for many.
  • Frame: "One ____ is ____, so ____ of them is ____ × ____ = ____."

👀 See It

1 notebook = $3  →  5 notebooks = 5 × $3 = $15

A car at 60 miles per hour travels 60 × 3 = 180 miles in 3 hours.
