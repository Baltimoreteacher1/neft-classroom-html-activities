What Is a Unit Rate? How Much for Just ONE

I can find a unit rate — the amount for exactly one of something.

📘 Word Bank + Sentence Frames

rateunit rateperdividefor one

"Per" means "for one."
  • To find a unit rate, divide so the second number becomes 1.
  • Frame: "That is ____ per one ____."

👀 See It: A Worked Example

3 apples cost $6. How much is one apple?

$6 ÷ 3 apples = $2 per apple

The unit rate is $2 per 1 apple. We made the second amount equal to 1.
