Key Words
Learn these 4 math words before you begin
📊
Percent
A ratio or fraction that compares a number to 100. Means "per hundred."
25% = 25/100 = 0.25
🏷️
Discount
The amount of money subtracted from the original price during a sale.
Save $5.00 on a $20.00 item
⚖️
Unit Rate
The price for exactly one item, used to compare different bulk bundle deals.
$2.50 per individual candle
🧾
Sales Tax
An extra charge added to purchases, calculated as a percentage of the subtotal.
6% tax adds $0.60 to a $10 sale

Need a discount calculation hint?

To find a sale price, calculate the discount amount first and subtract it:
- 10% off: Multiply price by 0.10. (e.g. 10% of $20.00 = $2.00 off. Sale: $18.00)
- 20% off: Multiply price by 0.20. (e.g. 20% of $20.00 = $4.00 off. Sale: $16.00)
- 25% off: Multiply price by 0.25, or divide by 4. (e.g. 25% of $20.00 = $5.00 off. Sale: $15.00)

Need a unit rate hint?

Divide the total price by the quantity to find the unit rate (cost per single item).
Formula: Total Cost ÷ Quantity = Unit Rate
Example: $7.50 ÷ 3 items = $2.50 per item. Compare this to the other rate; the lower rate is the better value!

Step 3 — Sales Tax
Add standard tax and compare other regional tax rates
Your job: Enter a purchase subtotal (usually a customer's cart total). Find the standard 6% sales tax, then select different states to see how regional tax variations impact the total cost.
🟢 Level 1 support: A 6% tax rate means you add $0.06 of tax for every $1.00 spent. For $50.00, the tax is 50 × 0.06 = $3.00.
LEVEL 2 CHALLENGE — Stacked Discount & Tax (6.RP.3c)

A customer buys a $50.00 original price item. First, apply a 20% discount. Next, calculate and add 6% sales tax on the discounted price to get the final checkout total.

Need a tax helper hint?

To calculate tax, write the percent as a decimal and multiply by your subtotal:
- 6% tax = Subtotal × 0.06.
- Add this tax to your subtotal to get the final total: Total = Subtotal + Tax.
