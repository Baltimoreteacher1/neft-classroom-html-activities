Key Words
Learn these 4 shopping math terms before you begin
🏷️
Percent Off
A discount computed as a percentage of the original price, subtracted from the total.
20% off $50 saves you $10
⚖️
Unit Rate
The cost of exactly one unit of an item (like per ounce, per pound, or per item).
$3.00 for 6 ounces = $0.50 per ounce
🛍️
Better Buy
The choice with the lower unit rate (cheapest per unit).
$0.10/oz is a better buy than $0.12/oz
🧾
Sales Tax
An extra percent added to the purchase price that goes to local government.
8% tax on $10 adds $0.80

Step 1 — Find the Better Deal
Compare 2 packages by finding the unit rate
Your job: Compare two packages of the same product to find which is the cheaper buy. Enter the name, price, and size for both items, then calculate their unit rates.
🟢 Level 1 tip: Try comparing friendly numbers like 12 oz for $3.60 (Item A) and 16 oz for $4.48 (Item B).

Need a hint?

To find the unit rate, divide the Total Price by the Total Size. Whichever is lower is the Better Buy!
Example: $3.60 ÷ 12 oz = $0.30 per oz. $4.48 ÷ 16 oz = $0.28 per oz. Option B is cheaper per unit.

Step 2 — Percent Savings
Find how much you save and what you pay after a discount
Your job: Pick an item you want to buy, enter its original price, and see what you save with a percent discount!
🟢 Level 1 tip: Try an original price of $60.00 with a 25% discount.
Need a hint?

1. Convert the percent to a decimal (e.g. 25% = 0.25).
2. Multiply: Original Price × Decimal = Savings.
3. Subtract: Original Price - Savings = Sale Price.
Example: $60 × 0.25 = $15 saved. $60 - $15 = $45 to pay.

LEVEL 2 CHALLENGE — Multi-Step Discount

If you have an additional member coupon for 10% off the sale price, what is the final price you pay?

Step 3 — Total Bill with Tax
Total your items, calculate sales tax, and check budget compatibility
Your job: Enter the prices of 3 food items in your shopping cart. Add a local sales tax rate to find your final bill. Then compare it to real average weekly grocery data!
🟢 Level 1 tip: Try values like $14.50, $28.00, and $17.50 with a 6% tax rate.

🌎 Real-World Comparison

Here is the average weekly cost of groceries per person in the US. Compare your bill to these benchmarks.

Need a hint?

1. Add the three item prices to find the subtotal.
2. Multiply: Subtotal × (Tax % ÷ 100) to find the tax amount.
3. Add: Subtotal + Tax Amount = Total Bill.
Example: $60 subtotal with 6% tax. Tax = $60 × 0.06 = $3.60. Total = $60 + $3.60 = $63.60.
