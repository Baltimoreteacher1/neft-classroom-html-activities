Percent of a Number: "Per 100"

I can find a percent of a quantity, like 25% of 80.

📘 Word Bank + Sentence Frames

percent (%)per 100of = multiplydecimal form

"Percent" means "per 100."
  • 25% = 25 out of 100 = 0.25.
  • "% of a number" → change % to a decimal, then multiply.
  • Frame: "____% of ____ = ____ × ____ = ____."

👀 See It

25% of 80 = 0.25 × 80 = 20

Half of 80 is 40 (50%), and 25% is half of that — 20. The math agrees!
