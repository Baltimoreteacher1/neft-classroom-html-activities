Stretch: Reading Position as an Inequality

I can interpret an inequality between two numbers as their relative position on a number line.

🧠 The Challenge

greater than (>)less than (<)to the rightto the leftnumber line

On a horizontal number line, the number farther right is always greater. So −3 > −7 because −3 sits to the right of −7. The statement and the picture must agree.

👀 Worked Example

Is −2 > −5? Plot both: −2 is right of −5, so yes, −2 > −5. The inequality −9 < −4 means −9 is to the left of −4.
