Stretch: Choosing the Sign and the Zero

I can use positive and negative numbers to model real situations and explain what zero represents.

🧠 The Challenge

positive / negativeopposite quantitieszero pointelevation / temperature / money

Signed numbers describe quantities with opposite directions. You choose where zero is (sea level, freezing, break-even), then one direction is positive and the other negative.

👀 Worked Example

A submarine 40 m below sea level is −40 (zero = sea level). A $25 deposit is +25; a $25 charge is −25. A gain of 5 then a loss of 5 returns you to 0.
