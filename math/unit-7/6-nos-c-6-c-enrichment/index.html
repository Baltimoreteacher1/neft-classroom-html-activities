Stretch: Opposites on the Number Line

I can find the opposite of a number and the opposite of the opposite, and explain why –(–a) = a.

🧠 The Challenge

oppositenegativeopposite of the oppositenumber line

The opposite of a number is the same distance from 0 on the other side. The opposite of −7 is 7. The opposite of the opposite brings you back: −(−7) = 7, and −(−(−3)) = −3.

👀 Worked Example

Opposite of −8 is 8. −(−5) = 5. The opposite of the opposite of −12 is −12 (two flips return the start).
