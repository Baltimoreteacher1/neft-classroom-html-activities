Signs Tell You the Quadrant

I can use the signs of the coordinates to find the quadrant, and reflect a point across an axis.

📘 Word Bank + Sentence Frames

coordinate (x, y)quadrantreflectx-axis / y-axis

  • (+, +) = top-right, (−, +) = top-left, (−, −) = bottom-left, (+, −) = bottom-right.
  • Reflecting across the x-axis flips the sign of y: (3, 2) → (3, −2).
  • Reflecting across the y-axis flips the sign of x: (3, 2) → (−3, 2).
