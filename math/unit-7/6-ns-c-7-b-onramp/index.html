Ordering Numbers in Real Life

I can write and explain an ordering statement (like −3 > −7) using a real situation.

📘 Word Bank + Sentence Frames

warmer / colder higher / lower owe (debt) greater than (>) less than (<)

Connect the words to the math:
  • Warmer / higher / more money → the greater number (>).
  • Colder / lower / more debt → the lesser number (<).
  • "Because ____ is ____ (colder/lower), ____ < ____."

👀 See It: A Worked Example

Two mornings in January:

Monday−2°C
Tuesday−5°C

Tuesday (−5°C) is colder than Monday (−2°C). Because Tuesday is colder, −5 < −2. In words: "−5 is less than −2."
