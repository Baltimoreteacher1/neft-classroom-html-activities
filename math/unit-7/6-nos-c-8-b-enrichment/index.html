Stretch: Ordering Negatives in Context

I can write and explain ordering statements about temperature, elevation, and money using inequalities.

🧠 The Challenge

temperatureelevationdebtorderinequality

Context tells you which way is "more." Warmer temperature, higher elevation, and a smaller debt are all the greater (rightmost) value. −5°C is warmer than −10°C, so −5 > −10.

👀 Worked Example

City A is −8°C and City B is −3°C. Since −3 > −8, City B is warmer. An elevation of −50 ft is higher than −200 ft because −50 > −200.
