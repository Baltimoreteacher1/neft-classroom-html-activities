Four Quadrants: Reading and Plotting Points

I can name the quadrant of a point and read its coordinates in all four quadrants.

📘 Word Bank + Sentence Frames

quadrantordered pair (x, y)originaxis

  • Quadrants go counter-clockwise: I (+,+), II (−,+), III (−,−), IV (+,−).
  • Frame: "(____, ____) is in Quadrant ____."

👀 See It

(−4, 3): x is negative (left), y is positive (up) → top-left → Quadrant II.
