The Opposite of the Opposite

I can find the opposite of a number and understand that the opposite of an opposite is the original number.

📘 Word Bank + Sentence Frames

oppositenegative sign−(−x)number line

  • The opposite of a number is the same distance from 0 on the other side.
  • −(−5) = 5: the opposite of the opposite returns the original.

👀 See It

Opposite of 7 is −7. Opposite of −7 is 7. So −(−7) = 7.
