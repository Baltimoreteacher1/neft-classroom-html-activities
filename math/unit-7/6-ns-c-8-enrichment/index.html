Stretch: Finding Distance Between Points

I can graph points in all four quadrants and find the distance between two points that share a coordinate.

🧠 The Big Idea

When two points are on the same horizontal or vertical line (they share one coordinate), the distance between them is the difference of the other coordinate — which is the same as |a − b|.

Example: (−3, 2) and (4, 2) share y = 2. Distance = |4 − (−3)| = 7 units (3 left of zero + 4 right of zero).
