Which Number Is Greater? Reading Position on the Number Line

I can use a number line to tell which of two numbers is greater — and explain why.

📘 Word Bank + Sentence Frames

greater than (>) less than (<) to the right to the left number line negative number

The big idea: On a number line, the number farther to the right is always greater.
  • "____ is to the right of ____, so ____ > ____."
  • "____ is to the left of ____, so ____ < ____."

👀 See It: A Worked Example

Compare −3 and −7. Find both on the line:

−8
−7
−5
−3
−1

−3 is to the right of −7, so −3 > −7. Even though 7 looks "bigger," −7 is colder / lower / smaller because it sits farther left.
