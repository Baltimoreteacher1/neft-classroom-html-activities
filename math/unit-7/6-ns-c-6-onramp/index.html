Numbers on the Number Line: Negatives & Opposites

I can place positive and negative numbers on a number line and name opposites.

📘 Word Bank + Sentence Frames

positive (+)negative (−)zerooppositenumber line

  • Zero is the middle. Positives go right; negatives go left.
  • Opposites are the same distance from 0, on opposite sides. (3 and −3.)
  • Frame: "The opposite of ____ is ____, because both are ____ steps from 0."

👀 See It: A Worked Example

−4
−2
0
2
4

−2 (left) and 2 (right) are both 2 steps from 0. They are opposites.
