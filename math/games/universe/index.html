The Math Universe

Real games where understanding the mathematics is how you win. Rewind any decision with the Time Machine, open a second reality to see what one change does, and teach Ori, an apprentice who starts out with the mistakes students make.

    How the games show learning

    Every mathematical decision is recorded: the expression you set, the ratio you chose, what the world did with it, and whether you revised it. Speed is never measured.

    Teachers see accuracy, revisions, predictions, explanations, the supports used, and whether Ori passed a task it had never seen, which only happens after the student has shown the idea.
