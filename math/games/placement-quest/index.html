The Lost Compass

Eleven stations. One scattered compass. Pilot your survey ship across reefs, frozen outposts, and floating cities to chart a route home.

Answer 11 questions, one from each Grade 6 math topic. There is no timer. It takes about 10 minutes.

  • At the end you get your Level and a return trip for anything you missed.

Explore for compass fragments and rescue stranded scouts. Each station opens one math challenge. Exploration rewards are separate from your placement result.
