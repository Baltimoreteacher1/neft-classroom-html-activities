    How to play

    1. Battle Mr. Neft Monster. Every right answer hits him. Every mistake lets him hit back. No timer, so take your time.
    2. World 1 is the warm-up. Tap the operation that comes next, type its answer and press ZAP. Repeat until one number is left.
    3. Worlds 2 and 3 are full problems. Work out the whole expression on paper, then type the final answer. Stuck after two tries? Break it down into steps for half points.
    4. Power-ups. Clean answers build a combo. Every 4 in a row earns a 🔦 Spotlight that shows what is next. A 3-star battle earns a 🛡️ Shield that blocks one hit.
    5. Remember: in 34 the exponent says use 3 as a factor 4 times: 3 × 3 × 3 × 3 = 81, not 3 × 4.
