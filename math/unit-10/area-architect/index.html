Vocabulary
Area

The amount of surface inside a 2D shape, measured in square units (ft², m², etc.).

Composite Figure

A shape made of two or more basic shapes joined together. Decompose it to find the total area.

Base (b)

Any side of a shape used as a reference for measuring height. For a triangle, h is perpendicular to b.

Height (h)

The perpendicular distance from the base to the opposite vertex or parallel side.

Triangle

A polygon with 3 sides. Area = ½ × base × height.

Trapezoid

A quadrilateral with exactly one pair of parallel sides. Area = ½(b₁ + b₂)h.

Parallelogram

A quadrilateral with two pairs of parallel sides. Area = base × height (not slant side!).

Engineer Tips

💡 Decompose complex shapes into simpler ones.


📐 Height is always perpendicular to the base — never a slanted side.


🔢 Double-check your units: always write unit² in your answer.


🏗 Real CAD tools use the same formulas you're practicing here!
