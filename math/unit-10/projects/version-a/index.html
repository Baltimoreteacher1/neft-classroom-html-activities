Key Words
Learn these 5 math terms before you begin
🧊
Volume
The amount of space inside a 3D box, measured in unit cubes.
Volume = l × w × h
📐
Surface Area
The total area of all 6 outer rectangular faces added together.
SA = 2lw + 2lh + 2wh
🗺️
Net
A flat 2D pattern that folds up to make a 3D solid box shape.
6 rectangular faces
📦
Rectangular Prism
A 3D box shape with 6 flat rectangular faces.
Shipping container
⬜

A flat rectangular side of a box. A closed box has 6 faces.

Step 1 — Calculate Box Volume
Measure your package and find its capacity
Your job: Enter the length, width, and height of your shipping package below. The blueprint will automatically draw your 3D package design.
🟢 Level 1 support: Try clean whole numbers, like 8" length, 5" width, and 4" height.
LEVEL 2 CHALLENGE
Measure a real delivery box or cereal box. Decimals (like 7.5 or 3.25) are encouraged!
Need a hint?

Volume measures how much 3D space is inside the box. Multiply Length × Width × Height.
Example: A box with Length = 6", Width = 4", Height = 3" has a volume of 6 × 4 × 3 = 72 cubic inches.

Step 2 — Surface Area from Net
Calculate the area of all faces to find cardboard usage
Your job: Look at your custom unfolded 2D net. Multiply the correct sides to calculate the area of each rectangular face, then enter the areas below.
LEVEL 2 CHALLENGE — Box Lid Configuration
🟢 Level 1 support: Opposite faces are identical in a rectangular prism! Top = Bottom, Front = Back, Left = Right.
Need a hint?

Surface area is the sum of the areas of all flat rectangles. Find the area of each face (length × width) and add them all up.
Closed Box SA = 2(lw) + 2(lh) + 2(wh)
