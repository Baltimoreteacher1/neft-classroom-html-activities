Key Words
Learn these 5 math words before you begin
🧊
Volume
The amount of 3-D space inside a solid shape, measured in unit cubes.
V = l × w × h
📐
Surface Area
The total area of the outside faces of a shape. Our open tank has only 5 faces.
SA = lw + 2lh + 2wh
📦
Rectangular Prism
A box shape with 6 flat faces. Our custom tank is open on top, showing 5 faces.
A standard fish tank
🗺️
Net
A flat 2-D shape pattern that folds up to make a 3-D box prism.
Unfolded tank blueprint
⬜
Cubic Unit
A 1×1×1 cube (like 1 cubic inch) used to measure space capacity.
1 cubic inch (in³)

Step 1 — Aquarium Volume
Enter dimensions and calculate how much water the tank holds
Your job: Pick where your aquarium will sit. Measure the space, then decide the dimensions (length, width, and height) in inches. Calculate the total volume in cubic inches, and convert it to gallons.
🟢 Level 1 tip: Try friendly whole numbers like 20 inches length, 10 inches width, and 12 inches height.
LEVEL 2 CHALLENGE
Feel free to use decimal dimensions (like 20.5 inches length or 12.25 inches height) for custom precision!
Need a hint?

To find the volume of a rectangular prism, multiply length × width × height ($V = l \times w \times h$). Since 1 gallon of water fills exactly 231 cubic inches, divide your total volume by 231 to find the volume in gallons.
Example: 20 in × 10 in × 12 in = 2,400 cubic inches. Then 2,400 ÷ 231 ≈ 10.4 gallons.

Step 2 — Glass Surface Area
Calculate the total glass surface area for 5 faces (open top)
Your job: Since the tank has an open top, we only need glass for 5 faces (the bottom, the front, the back, and the two sides). Calculate the surface area of your tank in square inches.
🟢 Level 1 tip: Find the area of the bottom, front/back, and sides separately, then add them together. Do not include the top!
LEVEL 2 CHALLENGE — Distributive Property (6.G.4)
Write an expression showing how you can group the calculations using the distributive property: $lw + 2h(l + w)$.
Need a hint?

Compute the area of the 5 rectangles on the net:
- Bottom face: Length × Width ($l \times w$)
- Front & Back faces: 2 × Length × Height ($2lh$)
- Left & Right faces: 2 × Width × Height ($2wh$)
Sum: $SA = lw + 2lh + 2wh$.
