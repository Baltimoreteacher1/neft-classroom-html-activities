Independent & Dependent Variables

I can tell which variable you choose (independent) and which one results from it (dependent).

📘 Word Bank + Sentence Frames

independent (you choose)dependent (the result)depends oninput / output

  • Independent = the input you control (often x).
  • Dependent = the output that depends on the input (often y).
  • Frame: "____ depends on ____, so ____ is the dependent variable."

👀 See It: A Worked Example

You earn $8 for each hour you work: y = 8x.

Hours (x) — you choose123
Pay (y) — the result$8$16$24

Your pay depends on the hours. So x (hours) is independent and y (pay) is dependent.
