Key Words
Learn these 5 vocabulary terms before you begin
🎮
Independent Variable (x)
The input variable that changes independently. In this lab, it is the number of weeks.
x = Time (Weeks)
📊
Dependent Variable (y)
The output variable that depends on the independent variable. In this lab, it is total subscribers.
y = Total Subscribers
⚡
Constant of Proportionality (k)
The constant rate at which the variables are related. Here, the weekly subscriber growth rate.
k = New Subs / Week
📐
Equation
A mathematical sentence showing y is equal to the rate k multiplied by the input x.
y = kx (e.g. y = 15x)
📍
Coordinate Point
An ordered pair (x, y) representing a week and the corresponding total subscribers.
Point: (3, 45)

LEVEL 2 CHALLENGE

Need a hint?

Remember that the **Independent Variable (x)** changes on its own (Time in Weeks), and the **Dependent Variable (y)** depends on the input (Total Subscribers). The equation is written as **y = kx** where **k** is your weekly rate.
