Stretch: Two Operations in One Expression

I can translate a two-step verbal description into an algebraic expression.

🧠 The Challenge

expressiontwice / triplemore than / less thanorder of words

Two-step phrases combine operations. "3 more than twice a number" = 2n + 3. Be careful: "less than" reverses order — "5 less than a number" = n − 5, not 5 − n.

👀 Worked Example

"Twice a number, decreased by 4" → 2n − 4. "Three more than half a number" → (n ÷ 2) + 3.
