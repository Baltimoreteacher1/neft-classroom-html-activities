Distributing: Multiply Across the Parentheses

I can use the distributive property to expand a simple expression like 2(x + 3).

📘 Word Bank + Sentence Frames

distributetermparenthesesequivalent

  • Multiply the outside number by each term inside.
  • Frame: "____(____ + ____) = ____ + ____."

👀 See It

2(x + 3) = (2 × x) + (2 × 3) = 2x + 6
