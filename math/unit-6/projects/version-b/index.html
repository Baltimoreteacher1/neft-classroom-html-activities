Step 1 — Subscription Revenue
Write and evaluate revenue expressions for standard and premium tiers
Your job: Plan the monthly pricing structure for standard subscribers (s) and premium subscribers (p). Enter your prices and user counts below to evaluate the expression: as + bp.
🟢 Level 1 tip: Try standard price $3.00, premium price $8.00. For subscribers, use 100 standard and 50 premium.
LEVEL 2 CHALLENGE
Use decimal pricing values (like $3.99 and $8.99). You will identify coefficients and variables.
Need a hint?

Multiply standard price by standard subscribers (as), then multiply premium price by premium subscribers (bp), and add the two amounts together.
Example: ($3.00 × 100) + ($8.00 × 50) = $300.00 + $400.00 = $700.00.

Step 2 — Server Cost Exponent
Model cloud hosting costs that double monthly using exponents
Your job: Your app hosting costs double every month. The cost is modeled by: C × 2^m, where C is the starting base cost multiplier and m is the month. Evaluate costs for Month 1 through Month 5.
🟢 Level 1 tip: Keep the base multiplier at $1.00. Exponents mean repeated multiplication: 2^3 = 2 × 2 × 2 = $8.00.
LEVEL 2 CHALLENGE
Change the starting cost multiplier C to another number (e.g. $5.00) and evaluate how hosting costs grow.
Need a hint?

An exponent shows how many times a base is multiplied by itself. 2^5 = 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 32. Multiply this by your multiplier C.
Example: For C = $1.50 and month 5: $1.50 × 32 = $48.00.
