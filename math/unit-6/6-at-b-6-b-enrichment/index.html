Stretch: Terms, Coefficients, and Constants

I can identify the terms, coefficients, and constant in a multi-term expression.

🧠 The Challenge

termcoefficientconstantfactor

In 7x + 4y + 9 there are three terms (7x, 4y, 9). The coefficients are 7 and 4. The constant is 9. A coefficient is the number multiplied by a variable.

👀 Worked Example

For 7x + 4y + 9: terms are 7x, 4y, 9; coefficient of x is 7; coefficient of y is 4; constant is 9.
