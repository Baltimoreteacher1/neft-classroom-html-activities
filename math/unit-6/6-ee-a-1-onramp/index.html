What Exponents Mean

I can evaluate a number with a whole-number exponent, like 2³ = 8.

📘 Word Bank + Sentence Frames

baseexponentpowersquared / cubed

  • base^exponent: multiply the base by itself that many times.
  • 2³ = 2 × 2 × 2. (NOT 2 × 3.)
  • Frame: "____ to the ____ power means ____ × ____ × …"

👀 See It

2³ = 2 × 2 × 2 = 8  ·  5² = 5 × 5 = 25
