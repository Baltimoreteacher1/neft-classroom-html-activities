How students are placed

  • Catch-Up — the same misconception 3 or more times in the window, or under 50% correct on 4+ graded answers.
  • Small Group 1 — a named misconception once or twice.
  • Small Group 2 — no named misconception, 50–79% correct on 4+ graded answers.
  • Whole group — everyone else with evidence. Each student is placed once.

Placement runs entirely in this browser from existing lesson evidence. Nothing is written back. Your judgement overrides it — move anyone you know better.
