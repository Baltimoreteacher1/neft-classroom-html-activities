Groups from Evidence

Small groups composed from the errors students actually made — not from a score band. Each group is one specific misconception, routed to the small-group lesson where that error keeps happening, and measured against last week to show whether it is clearing.

Teacher key

Student names come from the teacher-gated progress API. Enter your teacher key once — it stays on this device.
