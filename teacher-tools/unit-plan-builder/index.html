What happens after you download

  1. Open Canvas in Chrome and sign in to your course.
  2. Open Claude in Chrome (the assistant that can act in your browser).
  3. Drop in the file you just downloaded and say: "Deploy this Unit Plan to Canvas."
  4. Claude shows you a summary, you confirm, and it creates everything with the right dates. You never share a password — you stay signed in yourself.
