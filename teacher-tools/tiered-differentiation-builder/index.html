Lesson 1-1: Concept of Ratios & Rates

Objective: Students will model and express ratio relationships using visual tape diagrams, colon notation, and fractional comparisons.

🔵 Tier 1: Support Group

Focus: Conceptual foundation, step-by-step visual models, sentence frames.

  • Scaffolded Graphic Organizer with visual prompts
  • Inline SVG Tape Diagrams with pre-chunked terms
  • Sentence frames for verbalizing ratio comparisons

🟢 Tier 2: On-Level Core

Focus: Standard grade-level practice & error analysis.

  • Independent problem solving in multiple representations
  • "Spot the Mistake" misconception analysis
  • Self-check rubric against Grade 6 standards

🟣 Tier 3: Challenge Extension

Focus: DOK 3 application, open-ended modeling, mathematical defense.

  • Real-world multi-step problem solving
  • Written mathematical defense prompt
  • Creative extension challenge card

👩‍🏫 Small Group Rotation & Facilitation Guide

Station 1 (Direct Teacher Group - Tier 1):

Guide students through the SVG visual model. Use concrete counters or ratio bars. Prompt with: "For every ___ blue blocks, how many yellow blocks do we draw?"

Station 2 (Collaborative Pair Work - Tier 2):

Students work in pairs on problem sets and exchange error-analysis cards to critique each other's reasoning.

Station 3 (Independent Challenge Lab - Tier 3):

Students solve real-world optimization scenarios and construct a written defense card explaining their strategy.
