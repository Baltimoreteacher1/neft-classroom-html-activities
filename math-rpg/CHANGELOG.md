# Number Realm — Changelog

A record of how the Grade 6 math RPG campaign was built.

## v3 — Deeper campaign
- 9 new problem generators (ratio tables, percent word problems, order of
  operations, inequality solutions, dependent/independent variables, opposites,
  coordinate reflections, statistical questions, dot-plot reading) — 48 topics.
- 6 new advanced chapters (Units 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9); 42 chapters across 12 realms.
- Difficulty tiers (I/II/III) that scale with hero level; bosses/weekly bump it.
- Weekly Challenge (per-ISO-week gauntlet) alongside the Daily Quest.
- Hero onboarding (name + avatar) and a Read-Aloud button (TTS) for ESOL/a11y.

## v2 — Term-long campaign
- One persistent hero across all realms (level, XP, gold, HP, inventory).
- Shop + battle items; level-gated abilities (Focus, Power Strike, Second Wind,
  Scholar's Mercy).
- Daily Quest with a return streak.
- Per-standard mastery tracking + Hero Codex; 13 achievements.
- SVG math diagrams (coordinate plane, figures, prisms, nets, number lines,
  dot plots).
- Unit 0 "Gateway Gardens" readiness realm + gated Saga Finale ("The Null").

## v1 — First release
- A story-driven RPG for each Grade 6 unit: world map, monster battles driven by
  standards-aligned problems, Socratic AI Sage hints (with offline fallback),
  XP/leveling, per-unit boss, and full Save/Resume integration.
