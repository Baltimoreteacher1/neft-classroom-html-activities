Differentiated Classroom Architecture

Three support pathways. One shared intellectual goal.

Students never get watered-down mysteries. Whether a learner chooses Guided, Developing, or Independent support, they investigate the same case, examine the same evidence, and write to the same story audience.

Open Teacher Hub & Link BuilderFamily Guide (EN / ES)
Pathway 1

Guided

High-support entry with visible sentence frames, focused reading excerpt, and core word bank.

  • Focused 1-paragraph reading
  • Visible sentence frames
  • Curated word bank
Pathway 2

Developing

Balanced challenge with complete story texts and on-demand starters when needed.

  • Complete 2-paragraph source
  • On-demand sentence starters
  • Core vocabulary bank
Pathway 3

Independent

Extended academic synthesis with a second corroborating source and unprompted drafting.

  • Primary + secondary source
  • Unscaffolded drafting
  • Extended synthesis stretch prompt
