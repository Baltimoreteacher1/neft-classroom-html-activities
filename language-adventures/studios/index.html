Ten Language & Discourse Studios

These sit on the lesson a teacher already has, the person a child can interview, and two answers to the same prompt. Practice evidence stays on this device. WIDA and ACCESS scores are produced by those programs, not by this page.

En español, las instrucciones de cada estudio cambian con el botón Español. La tarea en inglés permanece en inglés.

Studio 01Educator, the night before
Tomorrow's lessonLa lección de mañanaPaste the text you are already teaching. Leave with one language use, six words, a frame that fades, and a four-minute talk.
Mounts: Teacher Hub, StudiosOpen
Studio 02Student
Same prompt, two datesLa misma pregunta, dos fechasAnswer one prompt in September and again in March. Hear or read what showed up later. No combined number.
Mounts: Passport, StudiosOpen
Studio 03Student with a family member
Family as sourceLa familia como fuenteInterview someone at home. Their account is source number one. The school writing cites them by name.
Mounts: Families, StudiosOpen
Studio 04Class, one shared screen
Pair talkConversación en parejaTwo students talk. The studio reads the second turn: build-on, challenge, restatement, or not yet. The map has no score.
Mounts: Teacher Hub, StudiosOpen
Studio 05Class
One question, four ways inUna pregunta, cuatro entradasThe class publishes one explanation. Each student contributes the move they can make today. Roles are ways in, not levels.
Mounts: Teacher Hub, StudiosOpen
Studio 06Student
Name the missNombra el errorAfter a wrong listening or reading choice, say whether the miss was the idea, the question wording, or a home-language pattern.
Mounts: Five minutes, StudiosOpen
Studio 07Educator and student
Transfer mapsMapas de transferenciaA home-language map of sentence and listening supports. Speaking feedback checks the idea. Accent is left alone.
Mounts: Teacher Hub, StudiosOpen
Studio 08Student
Academic word matrixMatriz morfológicaCombine prefixes, Latin roots, and suffixes in an interactive matrix, inspect part-of-speech shifts, and deploy derived words in context.
Mounts: Five minutes, Word Forge, StudiosOpen
Studio 09Student
Register & precision polisherPulidor de registro y precisiónTransform informal conversational drafts into audience-aware academic writing by replacing highlighted vague words with exact evidence.
Mounts: Communication Lab, Five minutes, StudiosOpen
Studio 010Student
Data & visual evidence benchBanco de datos y evidencia visualInspect interactive bar charts from Science City, Media Detectives, and Diplomacy Chronicles, test a claim against numbers, and write a verified proof.
Mounts: Media Detectives, Science City, StudiosOpen
