Register & Precision Polisher

Every writer starts with rough, conversational ideas. Practice upgrading an informal draft for a real decision-making audience by cutting highlighted vague words and inserting verifiable evidence.

Audience & Source Evidence

Target Audience: the Library Board

October sign-in logs show 32 students used the makerspace every Tuesday to build science models, and student stewards clean the tables by 4:20 p.m.

Informal First Take (Contains Vague Filler):

“Hey guys, please keep the makerspace open because a bunch of kids do cool stuff there every week and we clean up our mess fast.”

Vague words to cut:guys (still in draft)bunch (still in draft)kids (still in draft)cool (still in draft)stuff (still in draft)mess (still in draft)

Live Register & Precision Check

Still contains informal or vague wording (guys, bunch, kids, cool, stuff, mess). Replace those with exact domain terms.

  • Address the Library Board formally
  • Replace 'a bunch of kids' with 32 students
  • Replace 'cool stuff' with science models or projects
  • Use a causal connector (because, since, so that)
