Five minutes · speaking

Tell the route

Give a two-step direction using place words.

Grades 3-5 · about 5 minutes · not a test score

A new student is at the library door. Tell them how to get to the round rug and then the word wall.

  • Say the first place.
  • Say the second place.
  • Use next or then.

Optional starter: First, walk to ___. Then ___.
