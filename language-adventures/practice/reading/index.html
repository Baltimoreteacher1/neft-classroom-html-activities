The early bus note

Find one stated detail and explain why it matters.

Grades 3-5 · about 5 minutes · not a test score

Tomorrow the museum bus leaves at 8:10, not 8:30. Ms. Alvarez will lock the classroom at 8:00. Bring your badge. If you arrive at the usual time, the bus will already be gone.
