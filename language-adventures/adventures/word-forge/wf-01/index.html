The Word Forge · Case 01Key Use: ExplainELD-LA~18 min

The Broken Aqueduct Arch

The keystone over the pressroom canal is cracked. Forge the exact word parts and expand the repair spec so the arch holds. The path branches. What you ask, and what you do with the evidence, changes the ending.

Forgekeeper Anika Shah, printer Bodhi Nguyen, and apprentice Leo Martins stand beneath the cracked stone arch that carries water to the print foundry. To cast the replacement keystone, students must listen to Anika's inspection report, read the mason's specification sheet, build precise morphological words from prefixes, roots, and suffixes, and write an expanded repair order.

Communicative Objective

Students use prefixes, roots, and suffixes to construct precise academic words and expand short sentences to explain a structural repair.

Target Academic Vocabulary (6 Terms)

rebuild: to build something again after it was damaged (prefix re- + root build)
inspection: the act of looking closely at something to check its condition (inspect + -ion)
unstable: not steady or safe; likely to shift or fall (prefix un- + stable)
supportive: holding weight or giving strength to a structure (support + -ive)
precisely: in an exact and accurate way (precise + -ly)
connector: a word or piece that joins two parts together (connect + -or)
