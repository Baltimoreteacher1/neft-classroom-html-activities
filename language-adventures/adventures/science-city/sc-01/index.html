Science City · Case 01Key Use: ExplainELD-SC~20 min

Silt in the River Garden

The floating lettuce beds are wilting at noon. Trace the cause before you replace the wrong valve. The path branches. What you ask, and what you do with the evidence, changes the ending.

Student researcher Amina Yusuf joins Dr. Priya Raman and engineer Mateo Cruz at Canal Basin Three. The lettuce leaves in the river garden are drooping even though the water pump is running. Amina must listen to the sensor readout, inspect the intake filter log, and explain what the mesh filter can and cannot fix.

Communicative Objective

Students explain a cause-and-effect chain in an environmental system and state the physical limit of an engineering solution.

Target Academic Vocabulary (6 Terms)

sediment: fine sand or clay carried by moving water
filter: a device with small openings that traps solid particles
dissolved: mixed completely into a liquid so it cannot be seen as pieces
intake: the opening where water enters a pipe or pump
oxygen: a gas in water and air that plant roots need to stay healthy
cause: the reason something happens in a system
