The Canal Lock Ledger

Two historical accounts describe the day the stone canal lock opened in 1828. Compare what both witnesses saw. The path branches. What you ask, and what you do with the evidence, changes the ending.

Portal guide Imani Brooks, historian Rafael Ortega, and scribe Ellis Ward use their fictional archive lens to study two real-style primary sources from an 1828 canal town: the lockkeeper's toll ledger and a mill worker's letter. Students recount the chronology, compare perspectives, and keep the fictional portal frame separate from the historical evidence.

Communicative Objective

Students recount a historical sequence and compare two primary accounts while keeping the fictional portal frame separate from historical fact.

Bench: Dual-Account Comparator

Target Academic Vocabulary (6 Terms)

chronology: the order in which historical events happened in time
account: a report or description of an event written by someone who was there
perspective: how a person sees an event based on their role and experience
ledger: a book where weights, tolls, and times are recorded
primary: created at the time of the historical event by a witness
corroborate: to confirm a detail using a second independent source
