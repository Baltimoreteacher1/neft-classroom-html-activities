The Language Nexus · Case 01Key Use: ArgueELD-SC~22 min

The Archive and the River Pipe

Floodwater is seeping toward the lower archive vault. Combine physical evidence and cause-and-effect science in one coordinated brief. The path branches. What you ask, and what you do with the evidence, changes the ending.

Keeper Soren Abebe calls Detective Omar Voss (from The Mystery Files) and Dr. Priya Raman (from Science City) to the glass observatory of the Language Nexus. Water is pooling outside Vault B, and a rumor claims someone left the street door open in the rain. Students must transfer observational rigor from Harbor City and cause-and-effect reasoning from Science City to prove the water comes from a clogged cooling-pipe screen—and recommend an immediate fix.

Communicative Objective

Students integrate observational evidence and scientific cause-and-effect reasoning to refute a false rumor and justify a targeted repair.

Target Academic Vocabulary (6 Terms)

transfer: to carry a skill or language move from one subject or world into a new problem
corroborate: to support a conclusion with two different kinds of proof
condensation: water droplets that form when warm humid air cools against a surface
overflow: water that spills over the top of a tray or basin when the drain screen is blocked
observation: a fact noticed directly with senses or instruments before making a claim
synthesis: combining evidence from more than one source into one clear argument
