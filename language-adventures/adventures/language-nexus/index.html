    The Archive and the River Pipe

    Floodwater is seeping toward the lower archive vault. Combine physical evidence and cause-and-effect science in one coordinated brief.

    Keeper Soren Abebe calls Detective Omar Voss (from The Mystery Files) and Dr. Priya Raman (from Science City) to the glass observatory of the Language Nexus. Water is pooling outside Vault B, and a rumor claims someone left the street door open in the rain. Students must transfer observational rigor from Harbor City and cause-and-effect reasoning from Science City to prove the water comes from a clogged cooling-pipe screen—and recommend an immediate fix.

    Communicative Objective

    Students integrate observational evidence and scientific cause-and-effect reasoning to refute a false rumor and justify a targeted repair.

    Interactive Mechanics & Vocabulary

    Bench Tool: Syntax Studio (2 story forks). Words: transfer, corroborate, condensation, overflow, observation, synthesis.
