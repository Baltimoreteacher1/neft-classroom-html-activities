The Shared Cistern Schedule

The rainwater cistern drops every afternoon. Listen to both sides and propose a fair schedule that protects the seedlings and the bakery. The path branches. What you ask, and what you do with the evidence, changes the ending.

Envoy Hana Sato and mediator Caleb Brooks meet community gardener Rosa Delgado and baker Tariq Darwish at the courtyard cistern. Rosa needs morning water before the sun scorches the tomato seedlings; the courtyard bakery needs noon water to scrub the stone ovens. Students compare both viewpoints, ask a clarifying question, and write a fair compromise.

Communicative Objective

Students acknowledge two competing community perspectives and justify a compromise schedule using shared evidence and respectful disagreement.

Target Academic Vocabulary (6 Terms)

compromise: a fair agreement where each side adjusts part of its request
priority: something that must happen first because it is urgent
schedule: a plan that lists what happens at specific times
acknowledge: to show that you heard and understood another person's point
conserve: to use a limited resource carefully so it does not run out
proposal: a clear plan offered for others to consider and vote on
