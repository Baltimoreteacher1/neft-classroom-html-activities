The Cropped Photo on Sparkline

A viral post claims the library courtyard is flooding. Check the full frame and the numbers before you publish a correction. The path branches. What you ask, and what you do with the evidence, changes the ending.

Editor Noor El-Sayed, producer Jordan Hale, and fact-checker Aya Mensah examine a trending post on the school feed Sparkline. A tightly cropped photo of a puddle beside three water buckets claims the library courtyard is closed by a burst pipe. Students inspect the uncropped photo log, check the maintenance meter numbers, and write a verified correction.

Communicative Objective

Students evaluate a viral claim against quantitative and visual evidence and write a correction that states only what the evidence proves.

Target Academic Vocabulary (6 Terms)

claim: a statement that someone says is true, which still needs proof
cropped: trimmed around the edges so part of the original picture is hidden
context: the surrounding facts and details that show what really happened
verify: to check whether a statement is true using reliable evidence
rumor: an unverified story passed around without solid proof
correction: a published update that replaces an error with verified facts
