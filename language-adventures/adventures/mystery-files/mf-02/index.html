The Salt-Stained Ledger

Harbor shipping ledger #12 came back with a crust of sea salt on page eight. Reconstruct the timeline before you blame the dock clerk. The path branches. What you ask, and what you do with the evidence, changes the ending.

The morning after the lantern case, Archive Keeper Jun Park discovers a white crust of dried harbor spray across page eight of the 1912 Wharf Ledger. Someone carried the book outside during the 5:30 p.m. squall and returned it before 6:00 p.m. Nia and Detective Voss must listen to the checkout log, inspect the weather slip, order the timeline, and write an evidence-based sequence report.

Communicative Objective

Students sequence an investigative timeline from multiple timestamps and argue which window of time accounts for the salt damage.

Target Academic Vocabulary (6 Terms)

timeline: an ordered record of events showing what happened first, next, and last
residue: a thin layer of salt or grit left behind after water dries
interval: the period of time between two recorded events
verify: to confirm a time or detail using a written record
conclude: to reach a reasoned judgment supported by the timeline
alibi: proof that a person was somewhere else when an event happened
