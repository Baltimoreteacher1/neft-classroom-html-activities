Three Minutes for the Library Board

You have ninety seconds at the microphone to save the after-school makerspace hour. Cut the vague words and expand your proof. The path branches. What you ask, and what you do with the evidence, changes the ending.

Coach Elena Vasquez, sound designer Kwame Diallo, and presenter Riley Chen are in Studio Four preparing for tonight's Library Board hearing. The board is considering cutting the Tuesday makerspace hour unless students show concrete attendance data and a clear plan. Students listen to Riley's rehearsal, inspect the attendance log, expand a kernel sentence with a causal clause, and deliver a polished oral and written brief.

Communicative Objective

Students revise vague claims into audience-aware arguments supported by attendance data and expanded clauses.

Target Academic Vocabulary (6 Terms)

audience: the specific group of listeners or readers you are addressing
rehearse: to practice a talk aloud so you can hear what needs improvement
specific: exact and detailed rather than general or vague
attendance: the number of people present at a scheduled session
transition: a linking word or phrase that guides a listener from one idea to the next
revise: to improve a draft by adding evidence, cutting vague words, or reordering ideas
