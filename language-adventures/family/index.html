For families

You can open an adventure with your child. No account is required. Work stays on this device.

En español, para orientar. La tarea del estudiante sigue en inglés.

Estas aventuras practican inglés: escuchar, hablar, leer y escribir. El español de esta página explica cómo entrar. No reemplaza la tarea en inglés. Elijan grados 3–5 o 6–8, luego un nivel de apoyo. El nivel de apoyo no es una calificación de WIDA.

What each domain is doing

  • Listening. A character speaks. The transcript is optional support, and using it is labeled as support.
  • Speaking. Your child records on this device, listens back, and can record again. If the microphone is blocked, a typed rehearsal is available and is not called independent speaking.
  • Reading. They use a document from the story and point to evidence.
  • Writing. They plan, draft, read feedback, and revise. The feedback names what is present and what is missing.
