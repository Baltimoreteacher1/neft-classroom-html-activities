Four Language Domains

What each domain is doing

  • Listening.

    A character speaks. The transcript is optional support, and using it is labeled as support.

  • Speaking.

    Your child records on this device, listens back, and can record again. If the microphone is blocked, a typed rehearsal is available and is not called independent speaking.

  • Reading.

    They use a document from the story and point to evidence.

  • Writing.

    They plan, draft, read feedback, and revise. The feedback names what is present and what is missing.

The Language Page

An illustrated academic English studio for grades 3–8. Students listen, read primary story documents, build language structures, record oral responses, and revise written drafts.
