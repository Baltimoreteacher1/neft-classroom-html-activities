# Research Scope: EduWonderLab Culminating Project Award Readiness

## Primary Question
What concrete upgrades would make the culminating projects at eduwonderlab.com/curriculum competitive for credible awards in education innovation, mathematics teaching, multilingual/ESOL mathematics instruction, and forward-thinking learning design?

## Sub-Questions
1. What do the existing culminating projects currently offer, and where are their strongest differentiators and gaps?
2. What evidence, documentation, accessibility, equity, and implementation criteria recur across credible education-innovation awards?
3. What upgrades would distinguish the projects specifically in mathematics teaching and multilingual/ESOL mathematics teaching?
4. What product, research, and dissemination package would turn strong classroom activities into award-ready exemplars?
5. Which upgrades are highest priority by likely award impact and implementation effort?

## Source Types
Hybrid: live website, local curriculum source, official award criteria, and authoritative professional/research standards.

## Depth
Standard — approximately 10 source checks per research angle.

## Output Format
Technical report with a prioritized upgrade list and award-category mapping.

## Parallel Agents
3 agents, angles: current curriculum/product audit; award criteria and evidence; mathematics plus multilingual learner pedagogy.
