EduWonderLab · Guided demonstrations

Judge Mode

Six short walkthroughs — three to five minutes each — showing what each product actually does, step by step, with the underlying evidence record visible at each step.

Everything here is simulated

These demonstrations use three invented learners and invented work. No roster is loaded. Nothing is read from or written to any real student's saved progress. There is no randomness anywhere, so every run shows the same numbers and no step can fail unexpectedly. Every demonstration record is labelled synthetic and every learner id begins with demo:.

The numbers illustrate the shape of the system's output. They are not classroom results and are not evidence of efficacy.

Choose a walkthrough

Each demonstration links out to the real surface at every step, so you can leave the tour and use the product directly at any point.

Scope of this portfolio

These six products are connected views of one platform, not six separate applications. They share a curriculum registry, an evidence interface, a support profile, and a design system.

Other experiences on this site — including Monster Math Academy — are outside the scope of this portfolio and are intentionally not presented here. They remain fully available through the curriculum hub.
