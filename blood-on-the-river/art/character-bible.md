## Cultural-accuracy guardrails

- The Powhatan are an **Eastern Woodland (Tidewater Virginia)** people. Depict longhouses, woven-reed-mat walls, deerskin clothing, shell (roanoke) and copper ornaments, a roach hairstyle, dugout canoes, and the tall mixed hardwood forest of coastal Virginia.
- **Never** use Plains/Western stereotypes: no feathered war bonnets, no tipis, no horses, no buckskin fringe jackets, no Western desert backdrops.
- Keep all figures dignified and respectful; avoid caricature.
