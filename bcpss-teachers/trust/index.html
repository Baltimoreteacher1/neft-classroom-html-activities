Privacy & Trust Commitments

How the BCPSS K–8 Curriculum & Language Bridge protects student privacy by design, keeps student work on-device, and limits aggregate reporting.

Draft: pending legal review D8 Lock · No Student Work Sent to Models D14 Min Cell Size: 10

1. Alias-Only Student Privacy

Classroom tools use neutral, non-identifying aliases (such as nature-themed labels) instead of real student identities.

  • No personal identifiers: never enter student full names, initials, student ID numbers, email addresses, or dates of birth.
  • No protected classifications: never enter IEP, 504, SPED, or individual language-proficiency level codes into the tool.
  • No student accounts: students do not create logins, passwords, or cloud profiles.

2. D8 Lock: No Student Writing or Audio Leaves the Device

Under our D8 privacy lock, student writing and student audio are never transmitted to any AI model and are never stored server-side.

Hard boundary (D8): Zero student writing, zero student audio recordings, and zero voice transcripts are sent to language models, third-party APIs, or remote databases.

3. On-Device Storage Only

Draft responses, selected scaffolds, and classroom preferences are saved locally in the browser on the device being used.

  • Teachers and students can clear local browser storage at any time to remove saved drafts immediately.
  • No third-party advertising trackers, behavioral analytics, or external CDN fonts are loaded.
  • All stylesheets, scripts, and web fonts are served strictly same-origin.

4. Anonymous Aggregate Reporting (D14 Minimum Cell Size 10)

Optional usage counts track only aggregate curriculum activity (unit ID, proficiency band selected, and tool action) with no teacher names, student names, emails, or IP addresses.

  • D14 suppression rule: any aggregate group with fewer than 10 observations is automatically suppressed and displayed as <10 (suppressed).
  • No row-level export: aggregate summaries never return individual student or alias rows.
