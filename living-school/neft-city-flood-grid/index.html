    Welcome to the team! A huge storm is flooding Neft City. Our 8 sensors report how high or low each spot is compared to sea level, and each sensor has a spot on the city grid. Help us read the data and place a new pump.

    🎯 Your Mission Goals

    • 1 Order the elevations from least to greatest.
    • 2 Plot the sensors on the coordinate plane.
    • 3 Reflect a point across an axis.
    • 4 Find distance using absolute value.
