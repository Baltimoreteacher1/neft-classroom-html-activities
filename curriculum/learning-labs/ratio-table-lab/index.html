    About this lab

    Grade 6 · Lesson 3.4
    Determine equivalent ratios using graphs

    I can graph the values from a ratio table as points on the coordinate plane, and explain my graph using the words coordinate plane, ordered pair, origin, and proportional.

    Eight stations

    • Learn — one ratio in words, a table, and a graph; key vocabulary.
    • Explore · Build — grow and complete the soccer-ball ratio table.
    • Strategies — multiply, divide, add columns, and use the unit rate.
    • Graph — plot columns as ordered pairs, find the origin, read the line, name the unit rate point.
    • Compare — decide which tables show equivalent ratios.
    • Check — find and repair a ratio-table mistake.
    • Practice — new contexts at three levels, with hints and worked answers.

    The soccer-ball context, six-ball relationship, 10-bag application, and 4-bag check come from Session 1 of the Reveal Math Lesson 3.4 deck. The sundae and recipe practice contexts follow the lesson's Explore and Practice. Activities and hints are adapted for this interactive lab.
