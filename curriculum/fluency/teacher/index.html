A routine for responsive teaching

Check → Diagnose → Practice → Revisit
1

The Day Before · 1 Minute

Open tomorrow's lesson card. Note whether the four prerequisite skills stem from elementary grades (Gr 2–5) or spiral from earlier Grade 6 units. Identify the foundational hurdle before instruction begins.

2

First 2 Minutes of Class

Press Project on the lesson card. Display the quick check on your projector or smartboard. Students write answers on individual whiteboards. Use forms A, B, or C across class periods.

3

Right After · Decide

Open the Answer & Teaching Notes. If the room missed it, run the scripted 4–6 min reteach. If a few students struggle, assign Practice Studio (Foundation / Connect / Extend) or run the Partner Lab.

Listen for the strategy, not just the answer. Use a quick check as one piece of evidence. Ask students to explain or model their thinking before choosing support. Accurate, flexible reasoning matters more than speed.
