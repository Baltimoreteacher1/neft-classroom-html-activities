Start Here: What does “per 1” mean?

A unit rate compares a quantity to exactly 1 of another quantity. The same idea works for dollars per item, miles per hour, words per minute, and more.

Goal: understand the pattern before you play
RateCompares two quantities with different units.
Unit rateA rate with a second quantity of 1.
Unit priceThe cost for 1 item or 1 unit.
Better buyThe option with the lower unit price when the products are comparable.
Worked example
A café sells 6 fruit smoothies for $9.00. What is the cost per smoothie?
Write as a rate
$9.006 smoothies
→
Divide both parts by 6
$1.501 smoothie
Notice: $9.00 ÷ 6 = $1.50 and 6 ÷ 6 = 1. The unit rate is $1.50 per smoothie.
