Thank you for doing this with your student. Here is what to expect.Gracias por acompañar a su estudiante. Esto es lo que pueden esperar.

Unit 4 is about percents: a percent is a ratio out of 100. Students read percent tape diagrams, move between fractions, decimals and percents, find a percent of a number, and work backward to find the whole. The test also asks them to explain their thinking with a table, a tape diagram, or a double number line.La Unidad 4 trata sobre los porcentajes: un porcentaje es una razón sobre 100. Los estudiantes leen diagramas de cinta de porcentajes, pasan entre fracciones, decimales y porcentajes, hallan un porcentaje de un número y trabajan hacia atrás para hallar el entero. El examen también les pide explicar su razonamiento con una tabla, un diagrama de cinta o una recta numérica doble.

You do not need to know the math to help. If a part feels hard, that is useful — it tells you what to practice together before test day, and it is a good thing to tell me about.Usted no necesita ser un experto en matemáticas para ayudar. Si algo resulta difícil, eso es valioso: les indica exactamente qué repasar juntos antes del examen. La próxima vez cuéntemelo en clase.

👀 What to listen forQué escuchar
  • “Percent” means “out of 100.” 85% = 85 out of 100.«Por ciento» significa «de cada 100». 85% = 85 de cada 100.
  • A percent over 100% means MORE than the original amount (250% = 2.5 times).Un porcentaje mayor que 100% significa MÁS que la cantidad original (250% = 2.5 veces).
  • Percent of a number: the answer is smaller than the number when the percent is under 100%.Porcentaje de un número: la respuesta es menor que el número cuando el porcentaje es menor que 100%.
  • To find the whole, divide the part by the percent. The whole is bigger than the part.Para hallar el entero, divida la parte entre el porcentaje. El entero es mayor que la parte.
  • Do not average percents from groups of different sizes. Combine the counts first.No promedie porcentajes de grupos de distinto tamaño. Combine primero las cantidades.

Unit 4 Test Quick Family Guide · Guía rápida para la familia

1. Percent Sense (Sentido del porcentaje)

Percent = out of 100. 17 out of 20 = 85 out of 100 = 85%.

Equivalent forms: 38% = 38/100 = 19/50 = 0.38. Over 100%: 250% = 2.5 times.

Tape diagram: 100% ÷ number of boxes = percent per box.

2. Percent of a Number (% de un número)

• 10% → divide by 10   • 50% → half   • 25% → a fourth

• Build it: 45% of 140 = 40% (56) + 5% (7) = 63.

• Or use the decimal: 0.45 × 140 = 63.

3. Find the Whole (Hallar el entero)

Part ÷ percent (as a decimal). The whole is bigger than the part.

• 8% of ? = 12 → 1% = 12 ÷ 8 = 1.5 → 100% = 150.

• 600% of ? = 144 → 144 ÷ 6 = 24.

Combine data first: (9 + 21) ÷ (10 + 30) = 75%. Never average percents.

4. Four Questions to Ask at Home

  • “Is this number the part or the whole?”
  • “What is 10% (or 50%) of the whole? Can we build from that?”
  • “Does the answer make sense — bigger or smaller than where we started?”
  • “Can you explain it with a table or a tape diagram?”
