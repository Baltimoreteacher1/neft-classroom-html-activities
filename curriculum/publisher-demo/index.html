01 Working feature

Start with a question students can picture.

In Understand Ratios, students compare quantities from a recipe, name the order of the comparison, and distinguish part-to-part from part-to-whole. The lesson moves through vocabulary, launch, learning, exploration, practice, connection, and reflection.

Course and content

The curriculum map and canonical lesson configs are served from the same repository. This sample lesson is Grade 6 Understand Ratios, standard 6.AT.1. Course sequence and published resources are documented in the course brief.

Instruction and access

The flagship lesson includes concept vocabulary, a real-world launch, worked learning, an exploration, structured practice, connection, and reflection. Student pages use existing keyboard, reading-support, save/resume, and language-support systems where enabled.

Data and privacy

This public tour uses only an explicitly synthetic aggregate example. Protected teacher surfaces retain their existing authentication. The demonstration does not collect a learner name, responses, or classroom records.

What this does not establish

This walkthrough is not an independent standards audit of every lesson, a validated mastery or predictive model, a district efficacy study, or certification of every screen against WCAG 2.2 AA. Those require separate documented review and broader coverage.
