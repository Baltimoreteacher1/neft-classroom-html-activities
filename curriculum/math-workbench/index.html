How to use the Math Workbench

Everything you need to work out a problem — all in one place.

  • ✏️Write anywhere. Pick Pen and write numbers, symbols, or notes right on the board. Use Highlighter to mark, and Erase to remove pen marks.
  • ✥Move & edit. With Move, drag anything around. Tap an item to select it — a tiny toolbar lets you delete, duplicate, resize, or recolor it.
  • 7Stamp numbers & symbols. Open Numbers or Symbols and tap to drop draggable tiles. Line them up to build problems.
  • ➗Set up problems. Use Fraction, Long division, and Line to add the bars that separate numbers when you add, subtract, multiply, or divide. Use Cross to cross out digits when you regroup or cancel.
  • 📐Drop in shapes. Add 2-D shapes and 3-D figures, then label them and write your measurements.
  • 📚Formula bank. Open a topic at the bottom and tap any formula to drop a reminder card onto your board.
  • ⤓Keep your work. It saves automatically on this device. Use Save / Print to make a PDF or paper copy.
