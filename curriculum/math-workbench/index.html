How to use the Math Workbench

Pick a guided template, show your model, and explain your thinking — all in one place.

  • ◆Start with Guided Practice. Choose an equation, ratio, geometry, coordinate, data, or word-problem workspace. Each one drops the model and writing frames you need.
  • ✏️Write anywhere. Pick Pen and write numbers, symbols, or notes right on the board. Use Highlighter to mark, and Erase to remove pen marks.
  • ✥Move & edit. With Move, drag anything around. Tap an item to select it — a tiny toolbar lets you bring it to the front, send it to the back, resize, recolor, duplicate (Ctrl+D), or delete it.
  • ▦Align & reuse. Turn on Align in the top bar and items snap to the grid so columns line up neatly. Use the ⧉ button on the page tabs to copy the whole page and try a problem again.
  • 7Stamp numbers & symbols. Open Numbers or Symbols and tap to drop draggable tiles. Line them up to build problems.
  • ➗Set up problems. Use Fraction, Long division, and Line to add the bars that separate numbers when you add, subtract, multiply, or divide. Use Cross to cross out digits when you regroup or cancel.
  • 📐Drop in shapes. Add 2-D shapes and 3-D figures, then label them and write your measurements.
  • 📚Formula bank. Open a topic at the bottom and tap any formula to drop a reminder card onto your board. Tap the 🔊 on a card to hear it read aloud.
  • ⤓Keep your work. It saves automatically on this device. Use Save / Print to make a PDF or paper copy.
