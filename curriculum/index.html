The Forge — Build the Fix

Not a worksheet — a real lesson on the same engine every other lesson here uses, with vocabulary pop-ups, a three-step hint ladder, wrong answers that name the specific error, Spanish throughout, and an exit ticket. Every forged lesson passes the same quality gates before it is allowed to exist.

Class Game

The Class Boss

A weekly boss whose attacks are the mistakes your class is actually making.

One shared health bar for the whole class. Every correct answer lands a hit; every wrong answer earns the coaching line for that exact error instead of a scolding. No timers, no lives, nothing to lose. Put it on the board in projector mode and let the room work as one.

Fight the Boss Projector Mode

For Students

Teach the Machine

Explain the math to a learner that is confused in exactly the way your class is confused.

The machine holds a specific wrong idea and asks real questions until your explanation actually addresses it. A visible checklist shows what it still does not get, so students can see their own reasoning improving. Scored on the explanation, never on the answer. Available in English and Spanish.

Start Teaching

For Families

Weekly Family Broadcast

A one-minute, bilingual recap of what your child actually did this week.

Built from your own child's work, not a class newsletter: what they did, where they grew, what they are still building, and one five-minute activity you can do at the kitchen table tonight. English and Spanish, equally. Opens with your child's save code.
