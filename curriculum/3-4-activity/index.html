Tab 1 · Learn it

From a ratio table to a straight line

Chef Reyes uses 2 ounces of chocolate sauce for every 1 sundae. Watch how a ratio table turns into ordered pairs, and how the ordered pairs make a straight line on the coordinate plane.

Step by step: build the table, then graph it

Press Next step. Each column of the table becomes one point.

Three things every equivalent-ratio graph shows

📏 A straight lineThe points line up. Each step right goes up by the same amount.
🎯 It starts at the originThe line goes through (0, 0). Zero sundaes need zero ounces of sauce.
1️⃣ The unit rate pointThe point (1, r) shows the amount for one. For sundaes it is (1, 2): 2 ounces for 1 sundae.
