Math Notes

This is what your notebook page looks like — every lesson, same three sections, top to bottom. You write it by hand. The screen tells you when.

  1. at the start of the lesson

    1. Math Words

    A new page, headed with the lesson number and the date. Under it, two columns: the math word on the left, what it means on the right, in your own words.

  2. while we learn it together

    2. Today's Math

    One box holding the rule, formula, or model the lesson is built on — copied exactly as it appears on the screen.

  3. while you practice

    3. My Work

    The problems, worked on paper. Every problem numbered, every step visible — the answer on its own is not the work.
