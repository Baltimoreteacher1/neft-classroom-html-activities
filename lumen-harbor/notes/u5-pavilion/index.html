The Pavilion's Two Questions

The Geometry Architects · Lessons 5.9–5.10 · about 12 minutes

Lesson titles are the published EduWonderLab Grade 6 names from the fluency index and learning labs at eduwonderlab.com. Objectives in each chapter restate those published focuses. They are not quotations from the McGraw-Hill Reveal Math teacher edition, which is not in this repository. The public units page names the matching Reveal Math homework packet ranges.

Lessons

  • 5.9 Area of Regular Polygons
  • 5.10 Volume of Rectangular Prisms

Objectives

  • Find the area of a regular polygon using perimeter and apothem.
  • Find the volume of a rectangular prism and keep that result distinct from area.

Representations

Regular polygon, Rectangular prism

Vocabulary

  • Regular polygon. A polygon with all sides equal and all angles equal.
  • Apothem. The perpendicular distance from the center of a regular polygon to a side.
  • Square units and cubic units. Area covers a region. Volume fills a space. They are not interchangeable.

Misconceptions the chapter watches for

  • Multiplying perimeter by apothem and forgetting to divide by 2.
  • Using a side length as the apothem.
  • Labeling a volume with square units because the floor nearby was an area.

Differentiation
