The Floor That Counted Twice

The Geometry Architects · Lessons 5.3–5.4 · about 13 minutes

Lesson titles are the published EduWonderLab Grade 6 names from the fluency index and learning labs at eduwonderlab.com. Objectives in each chapter restate those published focuses. They are not quotations from the McGraw-Hill Reveal Math teacher edition, which is not in this repository. The public units page names the matching Reveal Math homework packet ranges.

Lessons

  • 5.3 Determine the Area of Trapezoids
  • 5.4 Apply Area Concepts to Solve Problems

Objectives

  • Find the area of a trapezoid from both bases and the height.
  • Find a composite area by decomposing a figure without double-counting.

Representations

Trapezoid, Composite area

Vocabulary

  • Trapezoid. A quadrilateral with exactly one pair of parallel sides, its two bases.
  • Composite figure. A shape made from simpler shapes.
  • Decompose. Break a figure into pieces whose areas you can add.

Misconceptions the chapter watches for

  • Adding the bases without averaging them.
  • Using a non-height side in the trapezoid formula.
  • Counting a shared region in both pieces of a composite figure.

Differentiation
