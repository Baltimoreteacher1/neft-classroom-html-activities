The Question Under the Headline

The Data Files · Lessons 2.1–2.2 · about 12 minutes

Lesson titles are the published EduWonderLab Grade 6 names from the fluency index and learning labs at eduwonderlab.com. Objectives in each chapter restate those published focuses. They are not quotations from the McGraw-Hill Reveal Math teacher edition, which is not in this repository. The public units page names the matching Reveal Math homework packet ranges.

Lessons

  • 2.1 Understand Statistical Questions
  • 2.2 Represent and Describe Data in a Histogram

Objectives

  • Distinguish a statistical question, which expects varied answers, from a question with one determined answer.
  • Organize numerical data into equal-width bins and describe what a histogram does and does not show.

Representations

Data list, Histogram

Vocabulary

  • Statistical question. A question that expects answers to vary, so one response is not enough.
  • Histogram. A display that counts how many data values fall in each equal interval.
  • Bin. One interval on a histogram, such as 20 through 29.

Misconceptions the chapter watches for

  • Treating a single-answer question as statistical.
  • Placing a boundary value in two bins or in neither bin.
  • Reading the tallest bin as if it were most of the data.
