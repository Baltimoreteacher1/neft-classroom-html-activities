The Gate Expression

The Expression Code · Lessons 6.5–6.6 · about 13 minutes

Lesson titles are the published EduWonderLab Grade 6 names from the fluency index and learning labs at eduwonderlab.com. Objectives in each chapter restate those published focuses. They are not quotations from the McGraw-Hill Reveal Math teacher edition, which is not in this repository. The public units page names the matching Reveal Math homework packet ranges.

Lessons

  • 6.5 Write and Evaluate Algebraic Expressions
  • 6.6 Identify Equivalent Algebraic Expressions

Objectives

  • Write and evaluate an algebraic expression for a situation.
  • Identify equivalent expressions by structure, not by a single lucky value.

Representations

Algebraic expressions, Substitution

Vocabulary

  • Variable. A letter that stands for a number.
  • Evaluate. Substitute a value and compute.
  • Equivalent expressions. Expressions that have the same value for every allowed substitution, because of their structure.

Misconceptions the chapter watches for

  • Adding the coefficient to the variable's value.
  • Dropping parentheses when a factor applies to a sum.
  • Calling two expressions equivalent because they match at one convenient input.

Differentiation

Supported Explorer keeps the same story and Grade 6 goals, with shorter lines, vocabulary glosses, and fuller hints. Independent Investigator uses leaner prompts and harder or multi-step decisions. Switching pathways keeps the scene and keeps solved work for the pathway where it was solved.

The opening scene is a choose-your-own-adventure fork. The two branches change the next panel and then rejoin, so a reader cannot skip a challenge by picking a path. Previous follows the path they actually took.
