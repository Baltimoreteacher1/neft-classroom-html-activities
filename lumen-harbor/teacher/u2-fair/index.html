Fair Share at the Night Desk

Lessons

  • 2.8 Describe Data Using the Mean
  • 2.9 Describe Data by Mean Absolute Deviation

Objectives

  • Interpret the mean as a fair share and compute it.
  • Compute and interpret the mean absolute deviation.

Representations

Dot list, Fair-share mean, MAD

Vocabulary

  • Mean. The fair share: the total divided equally among the observations.
  • Deviation. How far one value is from the mean.
  • Mean absolute deviation. The average distance of the values from the mean.

Misconceptions the chapter watches for

  • Finding a median and calling it the mean.
  • Dropping the absolute value so deviations cancel to zero.
  • Treating a larger MAD as more consistent.
