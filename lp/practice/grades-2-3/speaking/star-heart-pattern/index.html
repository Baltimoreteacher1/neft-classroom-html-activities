Finish the shape pattern

Students name and count shapes in a repeating pattern, explain what comes next, and judge a classmate's claim about the ninth shape, using math words like repeats and every third.

Grades 2–3SpeakingELD-MAInform10 min

Your recording stays on this device. It is never uploaded.

  1. Task 1 of 3 · Levels 1–2 · Inform

    Look at the shapes in a row. Say the shapes you see. How many stars are there?

    Words: star, heart, one, two, three, four.

    Think: 15 seconds · Speak: about 25 seconds

    Hear sample answers

    Level 1 sample

    Star. Heart. Four.

    Discourse: names the shapes and gives a number. Sentence: no sentences yet. Word: shape names and a counting word.

    Level 3 sample

    I see star and heart. It have four star.

    Discourse: names the shapes, then answers the question. Sentence: simple sentences with errors (have, star without -s). Word: uses the correct count.

    Level 5 sample

    I see stars and hearts in a row. There are four stars and two hearts, and the stars come in pairs.

    Discourse: describes the row, counts both shapes and notices how they are grouped. Sentence: correct plurals and a sentence joined with and. Word: math words like in a row and pairs.

    Check yourself

  2. Task 2 of 3 · Levels 3–4 · Explain

    What shape comes next in the pattern? Tell the shape and explain how you know.

    Use the words: next, repeats, after, because.

    Think: 20 seconds · Speak: about 45 seconds

    Hear sample answers

    Level 2 sample

    Star. Star, star, heart. Again.

    Discourse: gives the answer and says the pattern. Sentence: words and phrases only. Word: again shows the idea of repeating.

    Level 4 sample

    Next is a star. Because it go star, star, heart and then again. The heart is the end so star come after.

    Discourse: answer, then a reason. Sentence: uses because and so, with verb errors (go, come). Word: uses next, end and after.

    Level 6 sample

    The next shape is a star. The pattern repeats star, star, heart, and the last shape was a heart. That means the pattern starts over, so the next shape is a star, then another star, and then a heart again.

    Discourse: answer, the rule of the pattern and a conclusion. Sentence: links ideas with that means and so. Word: math words like repeats, pattern and starts over.

    Check yourself

  3. Task 3 of 3 · Level 5 · Argue

    Jae says the 9th shape in this pattern is a star. Do you agree or disagree with Jae? Explain your thinking.

    I agree / I disagree because ___. Every ___ shape is a ___.

    Think: 30 seconds · Speak: about 60 seconds

    Hear sample answers

    Level 2 sample

    No. Heart. Three, six, nine.

    Discourse: disagrees and gives the counting as proof. Sentence: words only. Word: number words show the thinking.

    Level 4 sample

    I disagree with Jae. I count the shapes and number 9 is a heart. Because the heart is every three shape.

    Discourse: position, then how the student checked, then a rule. Sentence: complete sentences with a stand-alone because and an error in three shape. Word: uses disagree and count.

    Level 6 sample

    I disagree with Jae. The pattern has three shapes in each part: star, star, heart. So a heart is always the 3rd, 6th and 9th shape. That means the 9th shape is a heart, not a star. Jae might have stopped counting at 8, and the 8th shape is a star.

    Discourse: position, the rule, proof and a guess about why Jae made a mistake. Sentence: uses so, that means and might have. Word: exact words like 3rd, 6th, 9th and always.

    Check yourself
