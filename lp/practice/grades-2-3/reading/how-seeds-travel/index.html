How Seeds Travel

Plants cannot walk, but their seeds can still go on a trip. A seed needs space, sun and water to grow. If every seed fell right under its parent plant, the young plants would be too . So seeds have different ways to travel.

Some seeds travel with the wind. A dandelion seed has soft white hairs. The hairs work like a tiny . The wind lifts the seed and carries it far away.

Other seeds travel with animals. Some seeds have little . The hooks stick to the of a dog or a rabbit. Later, the seed falls off in a new place. Birds also help. They eat berries, and the seeds inside drop to the ground far from the bush.

Some seeds travel on water. A coconut is a very big seed. It can in the ocean for many weeks. When it lands on a beach, it can grow into a new tree.

Word help
crowded
too close together, with no space (amontonado: muy juntos, sin espacio)
parachute
a big piece of cloth that helps something fall slowly (paracaídas: una tela grande que ayuda a algo a caer despacio)
hooks
small bent parts that catch and hold things (ganchos: partes pequeñas y dobladas que agarran cosas)
fur
the soft hair on an animal (pelaje: el pelo suave de un animal)
float
to stay on top of the water (flotar: quedarse encima del agua)
