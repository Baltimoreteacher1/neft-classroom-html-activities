Counting our class books

Students read a short math explanation with a data table and practice reading the table, following steps to add and using numbers as evidence.

Grades 2–3ReadingELD-MAExplain14 min

The Book Chart

Room 8 has a reading . The class wants to read 50 books in four weeks. Each Friday, a student counts the books on the class list. Then the helper writes the number in a .

Here are the numbers. In Week 1, the class read 12 books. In Week 2, they read 15 books. In Week 3, many students were sick, so the class read only 9 books. In Week 4, they read 18 books.

To find the , Ms. Rahman showed the class how to add. First, add 12 and 15 to get 27. Next, add 9 to get 36. Last, add 18. The total is 54 books.

Did the class meet the goal? Yes! The goal was 50 books, and they read 54. That is 4 more books than the goal. The class with a picnic in the park.

Word help
goal
something you want to do and work hard for (meta: algo que quieres lograr y por lo que trabajas)
helper
a person who helps with a job (ayudante: una persona que ayuda con un trabajo)
table
numbers or words put in rows and columns (tabla: números o palabras en filas y columnas)
total
the number you get when you add everything (total: el número que sale cuando sumas todo)
celebrated
did something fun because of a happy event (celebró: hizo algo divertido por algo bueno que pasó)
