Lina and the quiet drum

Students read an original short story and practice finding details, putting events in order and understanding figurative language and character feelings.

Grades 2–3ReadingELD-LANarrate12 min

The Quiet Drum

Lina had a small drum from her grandmother. Every morning she tapped it softly on the . Tap, tap, tap. The sound was like rain on a roof.

One day, the class planned a music show. Mr. Osei asked, "Who wants to play an ?" Lina's hands felt cold. Her heart was a jumping frog. She wanted to play, but she was .

At home, Lina told her grandmother about the show. Grandmother smiled. "Play the song you play every morning," she said. "Close your eyes and think of the porch."

On the day of the show, Lina sat on a chair in front of everyone. She closed her eyes. She tapped the drum. Tap, tap, tap. Soon her clapped along with her . When she opened her eyes, everyone was smiling.

After the show, Lina ran to her grandmother. "They liked the rain song!" she said. Grandmother hugged her. "The drum was never quiet," she said. "It was waiting for you."

Word help
porch
the open part with a roof at the front of a house (porche: la parte abierta con techo enfrente de una casa)
instrument
a thing you use to make music (instrumento: una cosa que usas para hacer música)
nervous
a little scared or worried (nerviosa: un poco asustada o preocupada)
classmates
the other students in your class (compañeros de clase: los otros estudiantes de tu clase)
beat
the steady sound in music that you can clap to (ritmo: el sonido constante de la música que puedes seguir con aplausos)
