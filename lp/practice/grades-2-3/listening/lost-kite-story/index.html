Grades 2–3 · Listening

Talking about a story

Students listen to a short story and a class talk about it, then answer questions about setting, the order of events, problem and solution, and the lesson, using time words.

Grades 2–3ListeningELD-LANarrate10 min

The Lost Kite

A teacher reads a short story aloud, and then a student talks about it.

This browser cannot read aloud. Read the words below, or ask a teacher to read them.

Show the words

Mrs. Okafor: Listen to our story. It is called The Lost Kite.

Mrs. Okafor: One windy day, Rosa flew her red kite in the park. Suddenly, the string broke. The kite flew up into a tall tree.

Mrs. Okafor: Rosa felt sad. First, she tried to jump, but the tree was too tall. Next, she asked her big brother Tomas for help.

Mrs. Okafor: Tomas got a long stick from home. He gently pulled the kite down. At the end, Rosa smiled and said, thank you!

Sami: I think the problem is the kite got stuck. Rosa solved it by asking for help.

Mrs. Okafor: Yes, Sami. What lesson does the story teach?

Sami: It teaches that it is okay to ask for help when a problem is too big.
