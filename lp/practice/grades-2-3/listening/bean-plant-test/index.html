Grades 2–3 · Listening

Which bean plant grew?

Students listen to a class talk about two bean plants and answer questions that move from details to explaining why the plants grew differently, using because and so.

Grades 2–3ListeningELD-SCExplain10 min

The Bean Plant Test

A teacher and a student talk about a plant investigation in class.

This browser cannot read aloud. Read the words below, or ask a teacher to read them.

Show the words

Mr. Diaz: Good morning, class. Two weeks ago, we planted 2 bean seeds. We put them in the same kind of cup with the same soil.

Mr. Diaz: First, we put cup A by the sunny window. Next, we put cup B in the dark closet. We gave both cups the same water every day.

Amara: Today cup A has a tall green plant. It has 6 leaves.

Amara: Cup B has a short plant. It is yellow, and it has only 2 leaves.

Mr. Diaz: Good observing, Amara. Why do you think the plants are different?

Amara: I think cup A grew better because it got sunlight. Both plants got water, so water is not the difference.

Mr. Diaz: Yes. Plants need light to make food. Tomorrow, we will move cup B to the window. Then we will watch to see if it turns green.

  1. Question 1 of 6 · Levels 1–2

    What did the class plant?

  2. Question 2 of 6 · Levels 1–2

    Where did the class put cup A?

  3. Question 3 of 6 · Levels 3–4

    What was the same for both cups?

  4. Question 4 of 6 · Levels 3–4

    How is the plant in cup B different from the plant in cup A?

  5. Question 5 of 6 · Level 5

    Why does Amara say water is NOT the reason the plants are different?

  6. Question 6 of 6 · Level 5

    Why will the class move cup B to the window tomorrow?
