Which fundraiser makes more sense?

Students read a student editorial that uses costs, profit and rates to argue for a fundraiser, and practice reading data, identifying a claim and evaluating evidence.

Grades 6–8ReadingELD-MAArgue20 min

Plant Sale or Car Wash? Let the Numbers Decide

Our seventh-grade class needs to raise money for a trip to the science museum in March. At last week's class meeting, students suggested two fundraisers: a plant sale and a car wash. Both ideas are popular, and both could work. However, before we vote, I believe we should compare the numbers carefully. When I did the math, the plant sale came out as the smarter choice.

For the plant sale, we would buy 60 small vegetable plants from a local farm for $90. We would sell each plant for $4. If we sell all 60 plants, we would collect $240. After we subtract the $90 we spent, our would be $150. Mr. Haddad that the sale would need about 15 hours in total.

For the car wash, such as soap, sponges and buckets would cost about $40. We would charge $8 per car. Last year, a similar car wash at our school washed 30 cars, so we could expect about $240 in sales and a profit of $200. That is $50 more than the plant sale. However, a car wash needs many helpers. Eight students would have to work for five hours each, which adds up to 40 volunteer hours.

Here is the key point. If we divide profit by volunteer hours, the plant sale earns $10 for every hour of work, while the car wash earns only $5 per hour. In other words, the plant sale earns twice as much for each hour students give. Those saved hours could be used to study for exams or to plan the trip itself.

Weather is another . A car wash depends on a sunny day. If it rains and only half as many cars arrive, the profit would drop to $80. Plants, on the other hand, can be sold indoors in the school lobby.

Some classmates argue that the car wash is more fun, and that matters. Still, the main purpose of a fundraiser is to raise money . For these reasons, I urge everyone to vote for the plant sale. Written by Priya N., Grade 7 class representative.

Word help
profit
the money left after you pay your costs (ganancia: el dinero que queda después de pagar los gastos)
estimates
makes a careful guess about an amount (calcula: hace un cálculo aproximado de una cantidad)
volunteer
done for free, by choice (voluntario: hecho gratis, porque uno quiere)
supplies
things you need to do a job (materiales: cosas que necesitas para hacer un trabajo)
risk
a chance that something bad could happen (riesgo: la posibilidad de que pase algo malo)
efficiently
without wasting time, work or money (de manera eficiente: sin perder tiempo, trabajo ni dinero)
