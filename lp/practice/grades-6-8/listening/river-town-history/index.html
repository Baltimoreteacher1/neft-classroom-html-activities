How a river town grew

Students listen to a class read three historical maps of a fictional town and answer questions about chronology, population change and cause and effect, focusing on time words and causal language for narrating history.

Grades 6–8ListeningELD-SSNarrate15 min

Three Maps of Millbrook

A social studies teacher and two students compare three maps of a fictional town.

This browser cannot read aloud. Read the words below, or ask a teacher to read them.

Show the words

Ms. Petrova: Today we will trace the history of a fictional town called Millbrook using 3 maps. Each map shows the town at a different time. Listen for how the town changed and why.

Ms. Petrova: The first map is from 1850. Millbrook was a small farming village on the east bank of the Silver River. About 300 people lived there. Families settled near the river because they needed water for crops and animals.

Aiyana: On the second map, from 1900, I notice a railroad line and a large mill next to the river. The population grew to 4,000 people.

Ms. Petrova: That is correct. The railroad arrived in 1885. After that, farmers could ship their crops to cities quickly. The mill also created jobs, so many new workers moved to Millbrook.

Kwame: The third map is from today. The mill is gone, and there is a park along the river instead. Also, the town spread to the west bank, and there is a highway.

Ms. Petrova: Good observation. The mill closed in 1970, and many people lost their jobs. For a while, the population went down. However, in the 1990s, the town turned the old mill land into a riverside park and a community college.

Aiyana: So the river was important in every time period, but people used it in different ways. First, it was for farming. Then, it powered the mill. Now, it is a place for people to relax.

Ms. Petrova: Exactly. Your task is to write a short history of Millbrook. Use time words, such as first, later and finally, and explain the causes of each change.

  1. Question 1 of 6 · Levels 1–2

    What does the class use to learn about Millbrook?

  2. Question 2 of 6 · Levels 1–2

    What was Millbrook like in 1850?

  3. Question 3 of 6 · Levels 3–4

    According to Ms. Petrova, what happened after the railroad arrived in 1885?

  4. Question 4 of 6 · Levels 3–4

    How did the population of Millbrook change between 1850 and 1900?

  5. Question 5 of 6 · Level 5

    Which statement correctly describes a cause-and-effect relationship in Millbrook's history?

  6. Question 6 of 6 · Level 5

    What is Aiyana's main idea about the river?
