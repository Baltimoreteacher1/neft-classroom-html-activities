Speaking

Look, think, and record your answer. It stays on your device. Each set has easier and harder parts, so everyone can start.

A map of Maple Street

Students name places on a neighborhood map, give walking directions with direction words, and argue for the best place for a new crosswalk using evidence from the map.

Our field trip vote

Students read a bar graph of class votes, compare amounts and explain how they found the difference, and judge two classmates' claims about what most students want, using numbers as evidence.

The kite in the tree

Students describe a story picture, retell a short story in order with time words, and explain how the main character's feelings change using details from the pictures.
