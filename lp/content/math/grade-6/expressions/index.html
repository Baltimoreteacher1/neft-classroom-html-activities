Writing, reading and evaluating expressions

Partners translate word phrases into expressions and back again, untangle reversed phrases like 7 less than n, and evaluate expressions for given values of the variable.

Make this a game Edit the activity

Standards

CCSS
6.EE.A.2: Write, read, and evaluate expressions in which letters stand for numbers.
WIDA ELD Standard
ELD-MA: Language for Mathematics
Key Language Use
Explain

Language demands

Discourse

  • Translate between words and symbols in both directions and explain how each word or phrase maps to a symbol or operation.
  • Read an expression aloud so a listener can write it correctly, using pauses or the words the quantity to show parentheses.
  • Explain what a part of an expression means in the story (14 in 14t is the cost for one friend) using the names term, coefficient, sum and product.

Sentence

  • Phrases that reverse the written order: 7 less than n is n − 7; subtract y from 20 is 20 − y.
  • Grouping in speech: three times the sum of x and 4 is 3(x + 4), but three times x, plus 4 is 3x + 4.
  • Evaluation sentences: When n is 20, the expression n − 7 equals 13.

Word / phrase

  • Operation words: sum, difference, product, quotient, more than, less than, times, twice.
  • Less versus less than: 7 less n is unusual, but 7 less than n is n − 7; the word than flips the order.
  • Evaluate (e-value-ate: find the value) and the multiple-meaning word term (a school term, a word, a part of an expression).

Objectives

Content
Students will write, read and evaluate expressions in which letters stand for numbers, and identify parts of an expression using mathematical terms.
Language
Students will explain how word phrases map to expressions, including reversed phrases with less than and from, and state evaluations with When ___ is ___, the expression equals ___.

Sentence frames by proficiency band

Levels 1–2

  • ___ more than n is n + ___.
  • When n is ___, the answer is ___.
  • The variable is ___. (Word bank: plus, minus, times, less than, more than)

Levels 3–4

  • The phrase ___ means ___ because the word ___ tells me to ___.
  • When ___ is ___, the expression ___ equals ___.
  • In this expression, ___ is the coefficient and it means ___.

Levels 5–6

  • The order is reversed in ___ because than / from signals that ___ comes first.
  • Without parentheses, ___ would mean ___, so the grouping changes the value from ___ to ___.
  • The term ___ represents ___ in the story, while the constant ___ represents ___.

Activity: Say It, Write It, Check It

15 minutes · partners

  1. Write four word phrases on the board as Column A: 7 less than n, the product of 5 and m, subtract y from 20, three times the sum of x and 4. Keep the matching expressions (n − 7, 5m, 20 − y, 3(x + 4)) for step 3.
  2. Partner B turns away from the board. Partner A reads a phrase aloud; Partner B writes the expression without looking, then explains which word told them the operation and the order. Switch after each phrase.
  3. Write the four expressions on the board in mixed order as Column B. Partner B reads one aloud while Partner A, turned away, writes it. Compare with the board; fix any grouping error by saying where the pause belongs.
  4. Partners solve the problems below. For each evaluation, say the full sentence (When t is 4, ...) before writing the value.
  5. Close by having one pair explain why 7 less than n is not 7 − n.

Problems

  1. Write an expression for 7 less than n. Then evaluate it when n = 20.
    Answer
    13 (the expression is n − 7)
  2. Evaluate 3(x + 4) when x = 6.
    Answer
    30
  3. A movie ticket costs $9 and popcorn costs $5. Write an expression for the total cost when t friends each buy a ticket and popcorn, and evaluate it for t = 4.
    Answer
    $56, from the expression (9 + 5)t, or 14t

Five-minute warm-up

Write two phrases side by side: 5 more than a number and 5 less than a number. Partners pick a number and act both phrases out with fingers or counters, then write each as an expression with n. Ask: which phrase can be written in the same order it is spoken, and which one flips? Collect the signal words more than, less than, from.
