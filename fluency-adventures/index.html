Three minutes of practice, or a path through nine worlds.

Fluency Lab keeps short skill practice in front. The adventures are choose-your-own-adventure stories: two routes, two plans, and two endings. One plan is a detour you can repair.

Worlds

Number Kingdom

Hosted by Bram

Lantern bridges, ten-gates, and a market that counts by thousands. You choose which gate to open.

River Expedition

Hosted by Lumen

Camp, river, and trail. Joining, separating, and regrouping decide whether the crew makes camp.

Multiplication City

Hosted by Mayor Gear

Equal groups, arrays, and factors keep the city stocked. A remainder changes what the warehouse can ship.

Fraction Islands

Hosted by Isla

Equal parts, number lines, and fair shares. The same amount can wear two fraction names.

Decimal Depths

Hosted by Nere

Tenths and hundredths in the dark water. Place value, not the number of digits, decides which depth is deeper.

Measurement Quest

Hosted by Quinn

Clocks, lengths, area, and volume. The unit you choose is part of the answer.

Geometry Architects

Hosted by Vela

Attributes, angles, symmetry, and moves on the grid. A shape keeps its name when you turn it.

Ratio Realm

Hosted by Rho

Pairs that scale together, percents, and a city that grows by a factor rather than by a lucky add.

Algebra Nexus

Hosted by Nyx

Integers, equations, and a grid that can light all nine worlds. Four annex missions go further and are labeled as extensions.
