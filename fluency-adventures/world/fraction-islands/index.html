Isla

Fraction Islands

Equal parts, number lines, and fair shares. The same amount can wear two fraction names.

Gates you can reach in any order: Harbors, Reefs, Ferries, Pools, Shores, Bridges, Wind.

  • 1. The Halved Harbor

    Harbors

    A dock must be split into equal shares. You choose halves or fourths, then whether the pieces stay equal.

    Grades 1, 2, 3 · partition

  • 2. Unit Fraction Reef

    Reefs

    One equal part of the reef must be marked. You choose the denominator, then whether to shade one part or the whole.

    Grades 3 · unit-fractions

  • 3. Number Line Ferry

    Ferries

    The ferry stops on equal jumps from 0 to 1. You choose the jump size, then whether to count spaces or tick marks.

    Grades 3, 4 · fraction-line

  • 4. Twin Tide Pools

    Pools

    Two pools look different and hold the same water. You choose which fraction to rewrite, then whether to scale both terms or only the numerator.

    Grades 3, 4 · equivalent-fractions

  • 5. Which Island Is Larger

    Shores

    Two islands claim to be the larger share of the same whole. You choose a comparison, then whether to use the same whole or the flashier digits.

    Grades 3, 4 · compare-fractions

  • 6. The Shared Picnic

    Shores

    Two dishes share a denominator. You choose which to add, then whether to add numerators only or denominators too.

    Grades 4 · add-like-fractions

  • 7. Unlike Shores

    Bridges

    Two shores use different denominators. You choose a common piece size, then whether to rewrite both fractions or add too soon.

    Grades 5 · add-unlike-fractions

  • 8. The Scaling Wind

    Wind

    A wind takes a fraction of a sail, then a fraction of what remains. You choose to multiply or to divide, and whether 'of' means multiply.

    Grades 4, 5 · multiply-fractions
