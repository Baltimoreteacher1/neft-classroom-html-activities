Measurement Quest

Clocks, lengths, area, and volume. The unit you choose is part of the answer.

Gates you can reach in any order: Tools, Clocks, Cisterns, Plans, Locks.

  • 1. The Footbridge Ruler

    Tools

    A footbridge must be measured from zero. You choose a plank, then whether to start at zero or at one.

    Grades 1, 2 · measure-length

  • 2. Clocktower Start

    Clocks

    The quest starts when the clock matches the plan. You choose morning or afternoon faces, then whether to set both hands.

    Grades 1, 2, 3 · tell-time

  • 3. The Elapsed Trail

    Clocks

    A hike has a start and an end. You choose the trail, then whether to count the minutes or subtract the clock faces like ordinary numbers.

    Grades 3, 4 · elapsed-time

  • 4. Capacity Cisterns

    Cisterns

    Two cisterns must be compared in the same unit. You choose which pair, then whether to convert before comparing or to trust the bigger number.

    Grades 3 · capacity

  • 5. Blueprint Perimeter

    Plans

    A fence is the distance around. You choose a garden, then whether to add the sides or multiply for area.

    Grades 3, 4 · perimeter

  • 6. Floor of the Hall

    Plans

    The hall floor is a rectangle of tiles. You choose a room, then whether to cover it with squares or measure the walk around it.

    Grades 3, 4 · area-rect

  • 7. Unit Conversion Locks

    Locks

    Locks open when a measure is rewritten in another unit. You choose to go to a smaller unit or a larger one.

    Grades 4, 5 · convert-units

  • 8. The Cistern Volume

    Cisterns

    A cistern is a rectangular prism of unit cubes. You choose to count layers, then whether one layer is the whole volume.

    Grades 5 · volume
