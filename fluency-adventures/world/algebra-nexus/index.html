Nyx

Algebra Nexus

Integers, equations, and a grid that can light all nine worlds. Four annex missions go further and are labeled as extensions.

Gates you can reach in any order: Lines, Chips, Loom, Gates, Locks, Reactors, Currents, Grid, Annex.

  • 1. Integer Frost

    Lines

    Frost covers a number line that runs below zero. You choose a side of zero, then whether farther left means smaller.

    Grades 6 · integers

  • 2. Chip Balance

    Chips

    Positive and negative chips must show a target value. You choose a charge, then whether opposites cancel.

    Grades 6, 7 · integer-ops

  • 3. Expression Loom

    Loom

    The loom evaluates an expression when a variable is replaced. You choose a thread, then substitute before you calculate.

    Grades 6 · expressions

  • 4. Order Gates

    Gates

    Gates open only if the expression's structure is respected. You choose a pattern, then whether multiplication outranks a casual left-to-right reading.

    Grades 5, 6 · order-ops

  • 5. One-Step Locks

    Locks

    A lock is a one-step equation on a balance. You choose to undo addition or multiplication, and you do the same to both sides.

    Grades 6 · equations

  • 6. Two-Step Reactor

    Reactors

    The reactor is a two-step equation. You undo addition before division, or the core stays unbalanced.

    Grades 7 · two-step-equations

  • 7. Inequality Currents

    Currents

    A current stays safe for many values, not one. You find the boundary and the direction.

    Grades 7 · inequalities

  • 8. The Nine-World Grid

    Grid

    A grid of lights from every world is an ordered pair, then a linear rule. You plot the point, then decide whether the rule holds for every row.

    Grades 6, 7, 8 · coordinates

  • 9. Exponent Conduits

    Extension

    Conduits repeat a factor. You choose a base, then whether an exponent means repeated multiplication.

    Grades 6, 8 · exponents

  • 10. Scientific Beacons

    Extension

    Beacons write a number as a coefficient from 1 up to 10 times a power of ten. You choose a beacon, then keep the value the same.

    Grades 8 · scientific-notation

  • 11. Chance Engine

    Extension

    A chance engine spins equal outcomes. You choose a spinner, then write favorable over all outcomes.

    Grades 7 · probability

  • 12. Signal Plots

    Extension

    Signals from the nine worlds arrive as a short list. You choose a list, then find the median rather than the mean.

    Grades 6 · data
