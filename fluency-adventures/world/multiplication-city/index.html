Mayor Gear

Multiplication City

Equal groups, arrays, and factors keep the city stocked. A remainder changes what the warehouse can ship.

Gates you can reach in any order: Foundry, Windows, Belts, Locks, Harbor, Cranes.

  • 1. Gear Baskets

    Foundry

    The city runs on equal baskets of bolts. You choose a machine, then whether to keep the groups equal or dump them into one pile.

    Grades 2, 3 · equal-groups

  • 2. Window Arrays

    Windows

    A dark apartment block is an array of windows. You choose a facade, then whether to fill a rectangle or scatter the lights.

    Grades 3 · arrays

  • 3. The Fact Foundry

    Foundry

    The foundry stamps facts through 10. You choose a forge, then whether to recall with an array beside you or guess a nearby sum.

    Grades 3, 4 · mult-facts

  • 4. Belt of Tens

    Belts

    A belt multiplies loads by 10 or 100. You choose a belt, then whether to shift places or stick a zero on the wrong side.

    Grades 3, 4 · mult-tens

  • 5. Factor Locks

    Locks

    A lock opens for the factors of a number. You choose a door, then whether to test by division or by adding until you get tired.

    Grades 4 · factors

  • 6. Cargo Shares

    Harbor

    A ship must share crates into equal holds. You choose a deck, then whether to deal them out or subtract once and call it division.

    Grades 3, 4 · division

  • 7. Remainder Warehouse

    Harbor

    Some crates will not make another full group. You choose a warehouse, then whether to report the remainder or force it into a group that is not equal.

    Grades 4 · remainders

  • 8. The Multi-Digit Crane

    Cranes

    The crane lifts a rectangular load. You choose how to see the partial products, then whether to add them or multiply only one digit and hope.

    Grades 4, 5 · multi-digit-mult
