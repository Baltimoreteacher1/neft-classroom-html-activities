Vela

Geometry Architects

Attributes, angles, symmetry, and moves on the grid. A shape keeps its name when you turn it.

Gates you can reach in any order: Quarry, Arches, Scaffolds, Gallery, Courts, Roofs, Windows, Spires.

  • 1. Shape Quarry

    Quarry

    The quarry sorts stones by sides and corners. You choose an attribute, then whether turning a stone changes its name.

    Grades K, 1, 2 · shape-attributes

  • 2. Compose the Arch

    Arches

    An arch is a plan of filled cells. You choose a silhouette, then whether to match the plan or only the number of blocks.

    Grades 1, 2 · compose-shapes

  • 3. Angle Scaffold

    Scaffolds

    A scaffold needs an exact angle. You choose the measure, then whether a square corner is 90 degrees.

    Grades 4 · angles

  • 4. Symmetry Gallery

    Gallery

    A gallery asks which fold matches a figure onto itself. You choose a figure, then test the fold.

    Grades 4 · symmetry

  • 5. Quadrilateral Court

    Courts

    The court sorts four-sided figures. You choose a rule, then whether a square counts as a rectangle.

    Grades 4, 5 · classify-quads

  • 6. Triangle Roofs

    Roofs

    A roof is a triangle. You choose a gable, then whether to take half the surrounding rectangle.

    Grades 6 · area-triangle

  • 7. The Round Window

    Windows

    A round window needs circumference or area. You choose which measure, then whether to swap the formulas.

    Grades 7 · circles

  • 8. Slide, Turn, Flip

    Spires

    The spire's plan is a point that must move. You choose a transformation, then check a right triangle with the Pythagorean theorem.

    Grades 8 · transformations
