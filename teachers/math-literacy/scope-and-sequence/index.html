Fluency goal by grade

GradeFluency goal
KindergartenFluently add and subtract within 5, and know the partners of 10.
Grade 1Fluently add and subtract within 10, and add and subtract within 20 using strategies.
Grade 2Fluently add and subtract within 20 from memory, and add and subtract within 100.
Grade 3Fluently multiply and divide within 100 and know all products of two one-digit numbers from memory.
Grade 4Fluently add and subtract multi-digit whole numbers, and multiply up to a four-digit number by a one-digit number.
Grade 5Fluently multiply multi-digit whole numbers using the standard algorithm, and add, subtract, multiply and divide decimals.
Grade 6Fluently divide multi-digit numbers and compute with multi-digit decimals using the standard algorithm.
Grade 7Fluently add, subtract, multiply and divide rational numbers, and solve two-step equations.
Grade 8Fluently solve linear equations in one variable and compute with integer exponents and square roots.
