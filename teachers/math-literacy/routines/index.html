Six routines, used all year

Teach each routine once, then reuse it in every unit and every grade. All of them use paper and pencil and take no preparation.

Three Reads

Every word problem

  1. Read 1: Read the whole problem. Retell it to a partner without looking.
  2. Read 2: Read again. Circle each number and underline what it counts. Cross out any number you do not need.
  3. Read 3: Read the question again. Say what you are looking for, then plan how to find it.

Sentence frames:

  • The story is about ___.
  • The numbers tell ___ and ___.
  • I need to find ___.

Word Wall in Four Moves

Each unit's five words

  1. Say it: students say the word and the Spanish word together.
  2. See it: read the meaning and the example.
  3. Use it: write one sentence with the word on the line provided.
  4. Show it: mark "I can use this word" only after saying a sentence to a partner.

Sentence frames:

  • ___ means ___.
  • An example of ___ is ___.
  • I can use ___ when ___.

Math Talk Moves

Math talk and write, any time

  1. Say it: one student explains their thinking using the frame.
  2. Revoice: a partner restates it. "So you are saying ___."
  3. Agree or add: "I agree because ___." or "I want to add ___."
  4. Write it: both students write one sentence from the conversation.

Sentence frames:

  • I think ___ because ___.
  • So you are saying ___.
  • I agree with ___ because ___.
  • I would like to add ___.

Notice and Wonder the Question

Warm-up before a new unit

  1. Show one problem with the question covered.
  2. Students write one thing they notice and one thing they wonder.
  3. Uncover the question. Did anyone wonder it?

Sentence frames:

  • I notice ___.
  • I wonder ___.
  • This reminds me of ___.

Untimed Fluency Round

Fact and skill fluency sets

  1. Solve all eight with pencil. No clock.
  2. Check with a partner or on the page. Circle any miss.
  3. Say the strategy for one problem out loud.
  4. Next time, aim to miss fewer, not to finish sooner.

Sentence frames:

  • I used the strategy ___.
  • I know ___ because ___.
  • A fact I am working on is ___.

Explain and Check

End of each unit

  1. Pick your hardest problem. Solve it a different way.
  2. Do the two answers agree? Say why or why not.
  3. Rate yourself 1 to 4 and tell your partner one reason.

Sentence frames:

  • My two answers are the same because ___.
  • I changed my answer because ___.
  • I can teach ___.

One reading move for each unit

Every grade teaches the same six reading moves in the same order, one per unit, so students meet each move again in the next grade with harder text.

UnitReading moveWhat students do
Unit 1Find the questionRead the last sentence first. Say the question in your own words.
Unit 2Numbers and unitsCircle each number. Underline what it counts.
Unit 3Retell itClose your eyes or look away. Tell a partner what happened in the story.
Unit 4Spot extra informationCross out any number the question does not need.
Unit 5Draw or label itMake a quick picture or diagram. Label every part with its unit.
Unit 6Explain and checkSay why your answer makes sense. Check it with a different method.
