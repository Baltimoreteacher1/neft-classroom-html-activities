Family Math Studio art kit

Original SVG: one icon sprite, ten unit scenes, spot illustrations, six hook cards, the celebration set, ten math model kinds and the brand marks. Hook cards and models show English and Spanish side by side. Shapes carry meaning; hue is decoration.

Hook cards

Celebration

Math models

Lesson data to SVG, renderModel({kind, data, alt}, {lang}). Every model has a computed text alternative. Part, whole, unknown (dashed, with a question mark), given, answer and error differ by shape as well as colour.
