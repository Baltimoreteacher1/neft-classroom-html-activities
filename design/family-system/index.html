Math models: one grammar

Same stroke, same radius, 15-unit labels. Part, whole, unknown, given and answer differ by outline, not by color alone.

  • Part: solid outline
  • Whole: thick outline
  • Unknown: dashed and ?
  • Given: gray
  • Answer: green and ✓
