Services

Choose the level of support that fits your celebration. Every service is flexible and can be combined into a custom plan.

Full Event Planning

Start-to-finish planning support, from first concept to the final moments of your celebration.

Best for Clients who want guidance through every step.

02

Partial Planning

Targeted help to finish what you started — we step in where you need us most.

Best for Clients who began planning but need help completing details.

Day-of Coordination

Budget Planning

Custom Theme Planning

Corporate Events

Family Celebrations
