Vowels — Nikud (נִקּוּד)

Hebrew vowels are little dots and lines. Here they are on the letter בּ (Bet). Tap to hear the sound!

Each vowel changes how the letter sounds. Try saying them out loud: ba, be, bee, bo, boo!
