🛡️ Word Defender

Read the definition at the top, then tap the matching literary device before the falling word reaches the bottom. Reach 150 points with 3 lives.

How to play — Word Defender

  • A definition shows at the top of the screen.
  • Three device shields sit at the bottom. Tap the one that matches the definition.
  • Correct = +25 points and the word is destroyed. Wrong (or a word that lands) = lose a life.
  • Reach 150 points to win. Your high score is saved.
