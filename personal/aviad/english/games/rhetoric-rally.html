⚖️ Rhetoric Rally

Read each persuasive line and sort it into the right rhetorical appeal: ethos, pathos, or logos.

How to play — Rhetoric Rally

  • Ethos = credibility / character (who the speaker is).
  • Pathos = emotion (how it makes you feel).
  • Logos = logic / data (facts and reasoning).
  • Tap the appeal that fits each line. 10 lines per round; the reason is shown after each.
