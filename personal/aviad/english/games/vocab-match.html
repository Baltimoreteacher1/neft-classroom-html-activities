🧩 Vocab Match

Clear the board by matching each academic word to its definition. Fewer misses means a higher score.

How to play — Vocab Match

  • Tap a word tile, then tap the definition tile that matches it.
  • A correct pair turns green and locks. A wrong pair shakes and counts as a miss.
  • Clear all 8 pairs. Tap New round for a fresh shuffle.
