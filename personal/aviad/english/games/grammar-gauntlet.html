⚡ Grammar Gauntlet

Each sentence has exactly one error. Tap the wrong word before the timer runs out. Speed and accuracy both count.

How to play — Grammar Gauntlet

  • Read the sentence. Exactly one word is grammatically wrong.
  • Tap that word before the timer bar empties.
  • Right = +1 and the fix is shown. Run out of time and the answer is revealed.
  • 10 sentences per round. Beat your high score!
