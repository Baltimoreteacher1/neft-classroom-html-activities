Unit 5, Lesson 8: Determine Surface Area of Pyramids

What students are learning

Key idea: The surface area of a pyramid is the area of its bottom plus the areas of its triangle sides. A square pyramid with a 6 in base edge and 5 in slant height: 6 × 6 = 36, each triangle is ½ × 6 × 5 = 15, so 36 + 4 × 15 = 96 square inches.

The surface area of a pyramid is the area of its bottom plus the areas of its triangle sides. A square pyramid with a 6 in base edge and 5 in slant height: 6 × 6 = 36, each triangle is ½ × 6 × 5 = 15, so 36 + 4 × 15 = 96 square inches.

  • Surface Area of Pyramids — The total area of a pyramid's base and triangular faces. Formula: SA = B + lateral area.
  • Pyramid — A solid with a flat bottom and triangle sides that meet at one point on top.
  • Slant height — The height of a side triangle, measured along its slanted face.
  • Lateral face — A triangle side of a pyramid, not the bottom.
  • Base — The flat bottom of a pyramid.
  • Apex — The point at the top of a pyramid where the sides meet.
  • Lateral area — The total area of just the side triangles, not the bottom.
  • Surface area — The total area of every face of a solid, base included.

How we did it in class today

  1. My square pyramid has a 6 in. × 6 in. base and a slant height of 8 in.
  2. First the base area: 6 × 6 = 36.
  3. Now one triangular face: ½ × 6 × 8 = 24. A square pyramid has 4 of them, so 4 × 24 = 96.
  4. I add the base and the side faces: 36 + 96 = 132.
  5. So the total surface area is 132 in². I used the slant height (8) for the triangles, not the pyramid's height.

If they get stuck...

Watch out for this: A common mistake in Surface Area of Pyramids is finding the four triangular lateral faces correctly (½ × base edge × slant height, times 4) and then stopping — forgetting to add the base area at the end. This leaves the total surface area too small by exactly the base's area. Before you submit, ask: 'Did I add the base area to the four lateral faces, or did I stop after the sides?'

Try asking: "Can you show me how you got that? Did we miss a step?"
