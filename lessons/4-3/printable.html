🚀 Launch

Margaret checked the cloud storage on her phone: 6 GB total, 4 GB used. Approximately what percent of her storage is used — and of the used storage, approximately what percent are photos?

Approximately — the problem is asking for an estimate

The word approximately tells you an exact answer is not required. Benchmarks, rounding and compatible numbers get you close, fast.

Key idea: Estimating Percents of Numbers. 1. Use benchmark percents: 10% (move decimal left 1), 1% (move left 2), 50% (half), 25% (quarter). 2. Combine benchmarks (e.g. 15% = 10% + 5%; 75% = 50% + 25%). 3. Round numbers to compatible values for quick mental estimates

Watch me set up the double number line

  • The whole is 6 GB — that is 100%.
  • I mark the bottom line in benchmark jumps of 25%: 0%, 25%, 50%, 75%, 100%.
  • Equivalent ratios give the top line: 25% of 6 GB is 1.5 GB, so the top reads 0, 1.5, 3, 4.5, 6.
  • 4 GB used lands between 4.5 (75%) and 3 (50%), close to 4.5 — so about 70% of the storage is used.

Let's estimate the metal recycling together

  • The Green-Team recycled 216 pounds of material; 8.6% was metal.
  • Round both: 8.6% is about 10%, and 216 is about 220.
  • 10% of 220 = 0.1 × 220 = 22. About 22 pounds of metal.
  • Compatible numbers work too: 10% of 200 = 20. Both estimates agree — around 20 pounds.

Now you try

  • Estimate 24% of 81 using a benchmark. Say which benchmark you chose and why.
  • Then check: is your estimate a little high or a little low?
