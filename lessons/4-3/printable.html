🚀 Launch

The arcade just posted its monthly leaderboard! Some players crushed the target score — one player hit 150% of the goal! Meanwhile, a rare bonus round gives only a 0.5% point boost. You need to understand what these unusual percents really mean to read the leaderboard correctly.

How can a percent be more than the whole, or smaller than a tiny piece?

100% means one whole. A percent greater than 100% is more than the whole. A percent less than 1% is a very tiny part.

Key idea: To change any percent to a decimal, divide by 100 (move the decimal point two places to the left).

Watch me

  • A player hit 150% of the goal. I want this as a decimal.
  • I divide by 100: 150 ÷ 100 = 1.5.
  • Since 1.5 is more than 1, this player beat the whole goal.
  • Now a tiny boost of 0.5%: 0.5 ÷ 100 = 0.005, much less than 1.

Let's try together

  • Let's write 200% as a decimal. What is 200 ÷ 100? (2.0)
  • Is 2.0 more or less than one whole? (More — it is twice the whole.)
  • Now try 0.25%: what is 0.25 ÷ 100? (0.0025)
