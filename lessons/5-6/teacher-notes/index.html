Warm-up

Now a smaller box: 4 long, 3 wide, 2 tall. How many pairs of faces does it have?

Teaching notes

Key idea: Representing 3D Solids with Nets. 1. A net is a 2D flat pattern that folds into a 3D solid without overlapping. 2. Rectangular prism net has 6 rectangular faces in 3 congruent opposite pairs. 3. Surface Area is the total sum of the areas of all flat faces on the net

I do:

  1. My box is 8 in. long, 5 in. wide, and 3 in. tall. I unfold it into a net and see 3 pairs of faces.
  2. Top and bottom: each is 8 × 5 = 40, and there are 2 of them, so 2 × 40 = 80.
  3. Front and back: each is 8 × 3 = 24, so 2 × 24 = 48.
  4. Left and right: each is 5 × 3 = 15, so 2 × 15 = 30.
  5. I add all the pairs: 80 + 48 + 30 = 158. The surface area is 158 in².

You do: In the table below, find the area of each face pair, then add them for the total surface area. Write your answers in square inches (in²).

Common misconception

A common mistake in Surface Area Using Nets is finding the area of only one face from each of the 3 matching pairs and forgetting to double it, instead of counting all 6 faces. For a box that is 9 in × 4 in × 5 in, a student might add just one face from each pair: (9×4) + (9×5) + (4×5) = 36 + 45 + 20 = 101 in², but the net actually has two of each face, so the correct total is 2(36) + 2(45) + 2(20) = 72 + 90 + 40 = 202 in² — exactly double. Before you submit, ask: 'Did I count both matching faces in each pair, or only one from each pair?'

Language & learning supports

  • Pre-teach: Surface Area Using Nets, Surface area, Net, Face, Two-dimensional, Edge (Spanish + visuals are in the lesson vocabulary).
  • Offer the sentence frames: “I know ___ because ___.” “First, I ___. Then, I ___.” “The answer is ___, so ___.”
  • Chunk the I-do steps; let students restate each step to a partner before moving on.
  • Provide the worked example as a reference while they practice.

Exit ticket

A rectangular prism has l = 9 in, w = 5 in, h = 3 in. What is the surface area?

  • 174 in² ✓ correct
  • 135 in²
  • 174 in³
  • 87 in²

SA = 2(9×5) + 2(9×3) + 2(5×3) = 90 + 54 + 30 = 174 in². Surface area uses square units (in²).
