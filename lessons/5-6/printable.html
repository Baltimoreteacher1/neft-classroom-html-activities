🚀 Launch

Your class is designing custom wrapping paper for time capsule boxes. To figure out how much paper you need, you have to find the total surface area of each box. The trick? Unfold the box into a net so you can see every face laid flat! One time capsule box is 6 inches long, 4 inches wide, and 2 inches tall. How much wrapping paper covers the whole box?

How do we find the surface area of a box?

Surface area is the total area of all the flat faces of a solid. A net unfolds the box flat so you can see and add all 6 faces. The answer uses square units.

Key idea: Representing 3D Solids with Nets. 1. A net is a 2D flat pattern that folds into a 3D solid without overlapping. 2. Rectangular prism net has 6 rectangular faces in 3 congruent opposite pairs. 3. Surface Area is the total sum of the areas of all flat faces on the net

Watch me

GIVEN DATA / MODEL
  • My box is 8 in. long, 5 in. wide, and 3 in. tall. I unfold it into a net and see 3 pairs of faces.
  • Top and bottom: each is 8 × 5 = 40, and there are 2 of them, so 2 × 40 = 80.
  • Front and back: each is 8 × 3 = 24, so 2 × 24 = 48.
  • Left and right: each is 5 × 3 = 15, so 2 × 15 = 30.
  • I add all the pairs: 80 + 48 + 30 = 158. The surface area is 158 in².
