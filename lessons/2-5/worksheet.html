    A ribbon is 4/5 of a yard long. Each bow uses 1/10 of a yard. Which equation finds how many bows can be made?

    1. A4/5 ÷ 1/10
    2. B1/10 ÷ 4/5
    3. C4/5 × 1/10
    4. D4/5 + 1/10

    The total (4/5 yard) is divided into groups of 1/10 yard each. The equation is 4/5 ÷ 1/10 = 4/5 × 10/1 = 40/5 = 8 bows.

    Watch for: A common mistake in Fraction Division Problem Solving is skipping the key idea: "Total goes first (dividend), group size goes second (divisor): total ÷ group size. Then check your answer by multiplying it back." — always check your work against this rule before you submit.
