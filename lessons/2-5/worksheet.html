  1. 1

    A ribbon is 4/5 of a yard long. Each bow uses 1/10 of a yard. Which equation finds how many bows can be made?

    1. A4/5 ÷ 1/10
    2. B1/10 ÷ 4/5
    3. C4/5 × 1/10
    4. D4/5 + 1/10

    The total (4/5 yard) is divided into groups of 1/10 yard each. The equation is 4/5 ÷ 1/10 = 4/5 × 10/1 = 40/5 = 8 bows.

  2. 2

    A detective has 1/2 gallon of solution. Each test uses 1/8 gallon. How many tests can be run?

    1. A4
    2. B1/16
    3. C16
    4. D1/4

    1/2 ÷ 1/8 = 1/2 × 8/1 = 8/2 = 4 tests.

  3. 3

    A trail is 3/4 mile long. A jogger runs laps of 1/4 mile each. How many laps does the jogger complete?

    1. A3 laps
    2. B1/3 lap
    3. C4 laps
    4. D3/16 lap

    3/4 ÷ 1/4 = 3/4 × 4/1 = 12/4 = 3 laps. This is reasonable: three 1/4-mile pieces fit into 3/4 mile.

    1. A5
