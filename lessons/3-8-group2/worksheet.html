6.AT.2 Version B — Answer Key

3.8 Small Group · Group 2

Answer Key

Name: Date:
  1. 1

    A machine dispenses 24 tokens in 3 minutes. At this rate, how many tokens does it dispense in 10 minutes?

    1. A80 tokens
    2. B60 tokens
    3. C72 tokens
    4. D240 tokens

    Unit rate: 24 ÷ 3 = 8 tokens per minute. In 10 minutes: 8 × 10 = 80 tokens.

    Watch for: A common mistake in Solve Problems with Unit Rates is comparing the total prices instead of the cost per item — the deal with the smaller total can still cost more per unit. Always reduce each option to its unit rate before you decide, then pick the lower one.

  2. 2

    A factory produces 360 widgets in 8 hours. At the same rate, how many widgets are produced in a 12-hour shift?

    1. A540 widgets
    2. B480 widgets
    3. C360 widgets
    4. D720 widgets

    Unit rate: 360 ÷ 8 = 45 widgets per hour. In 12 hours: 45 × 12 = 540 widgets.

    Watch for: A common mistake in Solve Problems with Unit Rates is comparing the total prices instead of the cost per item — the deal with the smaller total can still cost more per unit. Always reduce each option to its unit rate before you decide, then pick the lower one.

  3. 3

    Spot the Common Mistake

    1. 1ReadA vending machine dispenses 24 tokens in 3 minutes. How many tokens per minute is that?
    2. 2AttemptA student writes: 3 ÷ 24 = 0.125, so the machine dispenses 0.125 tokens per minute.
    3. 3CheckThe student divided minutes by tokens, which gives minutes per token, not tokens per minute.

    Which step has the mistake? Explain it and write the correct work.

    The mistake is in Step 2. Correct work: For tokens per minute, divide the tokens by the minutes: 24 ÷ 3 = 8 tokens per minute. The student divided in the wrong order, so they got the rate upside down. The attempt divided minutes by tokens, which answers a different question — how many minutes each token takes. The phrase "tokens per minute" puts tokens first, so the division is 24 ÷ 3 = 8 tokens per minute.

  4. 4

    Find the Unit Rate Error

    1. 1Which is the better buy: 5 lb for $8.75 or 3 lb for $5.10?Compare two deals
    2. 2Find unit rate for Deal 15 ÷ $8.75 = 0.57 lb per dollar
    3. 3Find unit rate for Deal 23 ÷ $5.10 = 0.59 lb per dollar
    4. 4Compare0.59 > 0.57, so Deal 2 is the better buy

    Which step has the mistake? Explain it and write the correct work.

    The mistake is in Step 2. Correct work: To find cost per pound, divide price by pounds: $8.75 ÷ 5 = $1.75 per lb, $5.10 ÷ 3 = $1.70 per lb. Deal 2 ($1.70/lb) is still the better buy — but the correct unit rates are dollars per pound, not pounds per dollar. The first unit-rate step divided pounds by dollars, which gives pounds per dollar instead of the cost per pound the question asks about. Dividing price by weight gives $8.75 ÷ 5 = $1.75 per pound and $5.10 ÷ 3 = $1.70 per pound, so Deal 2 is cheaper — the same choice, now backed by the rate that was actually requested.
