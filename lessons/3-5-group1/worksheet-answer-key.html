    Spot the Common Mistake

    1. 1Read the problemChef Priya makes hot chocolate with 2 tbsp cocoa for 4 oz milk. Chef Owen makes hot chocolate with 3 tbsp cocoa for 8 oz milk. Whose hot chocolate is more chocolatey?
    2. 2Compare the cocoaChef Owen has 3 tbsp cocoa. Chef Priya has 2 tbsp cocoa.
    3. 3Draw a conclusionSince 3 is greater than 2, Chef Owen's hot chocolate must be more chocolatey.
    4. 4ConclusionChef Owen's hot chocolate is more chocolatey.

    Which step has the mistake? Explain it and write the correct work.

    The mistake is in Step 3. Correct work: You cannot compare 2 tbsp and 3 tbsp directly because the milk amounts are different (4 oz vs. 8 oz). Find the unit rate instead: Chef Priya = 2 ÷ 4 = 0.5 tbsp per oz; Chef Owen = 3 ÷ 8 = 0.375 tbsp per oz. Since 0.5 > 0.375, Chef Priya's hot chocolate is actually more chocolatey — the opposite of what comparing raw cocoa amounts suggested. The conclusion compared 3 tbsp against 2 tbsp even though the milk amounts differ (8 oz vs. 4 oz), and cocoa amounts only tell you something when the milk matches. The unit rates settle it: Chef Priya has 2 ÷ 4 = 0.5 tbsp of cocoa per ounce and Chef Owen has 3 ÷ 8 = 0.375, so Chef Priya's hot chocolate is the more chocolatey one.

  6. 6

    Level 2 Extension — Chef Omar's Glaze Comparison

    1. 1Read the problemChef Omar compares two glazes: 3 oz syrup for 8 oz water and 5 oz syrup for 16 oz water. Which is sweeter per ounce of water?
    2. 2Look at the syrupGlaze 1 has 3 oz syrup. Glaze 2 has 5 oz syrup.
    3. 3Compare the syrup amountsSince 5 > 3, Glaze 2 must have more syrup per ounce of water.
    4. 4ConclusionGlaze 2 is sweeter per ounce of water.

    Which step has the mistake? Explain it and write the correct work.

    The mistake is in Step 3. Correct work: You cannot just compare the syrup amounts because the water amounts are different (8 oz vs 16 oz). Find the unit rate: Glaze 1 = 3 ÷ 8 = 0.375 oz syrup per oz water; Glaze 2 = 5 ÷ 16 = 0.3125 oz syrup per oz water. Since 0.375 > 0.3125, Glaze 1 is actually sweeter per ounce — the 'bigger syrup number wins' shortcut gives the WRONG glaze. Comparing 5 oz of syrup to 3 oz proves nothing while the water amounts differ (12 oz vs. 8 oz). The unit rates decide it: Glaze 1 is 3 ÷ 8 = 0.375 oz of syrup per ounce of water and Glaze 2 is 5 ÷ 12 ≈ 0.417, so Glaze 2 is sweeter — but the unit rates are what prove it, not the raw syrup amounts.
