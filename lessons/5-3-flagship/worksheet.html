6.G.1 Version A — Answer Key

Area of Triangles

Answer Key

Name: Date:
  1. 1

    What is the area of a triangle with base 10 cm and height 6 cm?

    1. A30 sq cm
    2. B60 sq cm
    3. C16 sq cm
    4. D30 cm

    A = ½ × b × h = ½ × 10 × 6 = 30 square centimeters.

  2. 2

    A triangle has an area of 24 sq ft and a base of 8 ft. What is the height?

    1. A6 ft
    2. B3 ft
    3. C16 ft
    4. D12 ft

    24 = ½ × 8 × h → 24 = 4h → h = 6 ft.

  3. 3

    What is the area of a triangle with base 14 ft and height 6 ft?

    1. A42 sq ft
    2. B84 sq ft
    3. C20 sq ft
    4. D42 ft

    A = ½ × 14 × 6 = ½ × 84 = 42 sq ft.

  4. 4

    A triangular window pane has a base of 9 in and a height of 4 in. What is its area?

    1. A18 sq in
    2. B36 sq in
    3. C13 sq in
    4. D18 in

    A = ½ × 9 × 4 = ½ × 36 = 18 square inches.

  5. 5

    Which formula gives the area of a triangle with base b and height h?

    1. AA = ½ × b × h
    2. BA = b × h
    3. CA = b + h
    4. DA = 2 × b × h

    A triangle covers exactly half of a rectangle with the same base and height, so A = ½ × b × h.
