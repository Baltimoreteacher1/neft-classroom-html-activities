Unit 5, Lesson 4: Apply Area Concepts to Solve Problems

Standard 6.GR.1

What students are learning Qué aprenden

Key idea: A tricky shape can be split into simple shapes like rectangles and triangles. Find the area of each piece, then add them. If a piece is cut out, subtract it instead. An L-shaped room made of a 9 × 6 and a 7 × 3 rectangle has area 54 + 21 = 75 square feet.
Idea clave: Una figura complicada se puede dividir en figuras simples como rectángulos y triángulos. Halla el área de cada pieza y luego suma. Si se recorta una pieza, réstala. Un cuarto en forma de L formado por un rectángulo de 9 × 6 y otro de 7 × 3 tiene un área de 54 + 21 = 75 pies cuadrados.

Try at home Para hacer en casa

Draw an L-shaped room on paper: a 6 by 4 rectangle with a 2 by 2 square cut from one corner. Find its area two ways: split it into two rectangles and add, or find the big rectangle and subtract the cut-out square. Do both answers match?

Dibuja en papel un cuarto en forma de L: un rectángulo de 6 por 4 con un cuadrado de 2 por 2 recortado en una esquina. Halla su área de dos maneras: divídelo en dos rectángulos y suma, o halla el rectángulo grande y resta el cuadrado recortado. ¿Coinciden las dos respuestas?

Talk about it:

Which pieces did you add, and which piece did you subtract?

¿Cuáles piezas sumaste y cuál pieza restaste?

How we did it in class today Cómo lo hicimos hoy en clase

  1. My L-shaped room splits into two rectangles: one is 12 ft by 8 ft, the other is 6 ft by 5 ft.
  2. First rectangle: A = length × width = 12 × 8 = 96 square feet.
  3. Second rectangle: A = 6 × 5 = 30 square feet.
  4. The pieces are joined, so I add: 96 + 30 = 126.
  5. So the total area is 126 square feet.
  6. A regular polygon splits the same way: a stop sign is a regular octagon, so I cut it into 8 congruent triangles that meet at the centre.
  7. One triangle has a base of 14 9/10 inches and a height of 18 inches, so A = 1/2 × 14.9 × 18 = 134.1 square inches.
  8. There are 8 congruent triangles, so I multiply instead of adding eight times: 134.1 × 8 = 1,072.8 square inches.

If they get stuck... Si se traban...

Watch out for this: Adding a piece that was cut out. A cut-out piece is taken away, not added.
Atención: Sumar una pieza que fue recortada. Una pieza recortada se quita, no se suma.
