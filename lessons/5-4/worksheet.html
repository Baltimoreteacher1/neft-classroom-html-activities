  1. 1

    A regular hexagon is divided into 6 equal triangles from the center. Each triangle has a base of 6 ft and a height of 5.2 ft. What is the area of one triangle?

    1. A15.6 sq ft
    2. B31.2 sq ft
    3. C11.2 sq ft
    4. D15.6 ft

    A = ½ × b × h = ½ × 6 × 5.2 = 15.6 square feet.

    Watch for: A common mistake in Area of Regular Polygons is stopping after finding the area of ONE triangle and reporting that as the total area — or adding the number of triangles instead of multiplying. Remember: total area = (one triangle's area) × (number of sides), not (one triangle's area) + (number of sides).

  2. 2

    Using the triangle from the previous question, what is the total area of the hexagonal skylight?

    1. A93.6 sq ft
    2. B15.6 sq ft
    3. C187.2 sq ft
    4. D62.4 sq ft

    Total area = 6 triangles × 15.6 sq ft = 93.6 square feet.

    Watch for: A common mistake in Area of Regular Polygons is stopping after finding the area of ONE triangle and reporting that as the total area — or adding the number of triangles instead of multiplying. Remember: total area = (one triangle's area) × (number of sides), not (one triangle's area) + (number of sides).

  3. 3

    A regular hexagon is split into 6 triangles, each with base 4 cm and height 3.5 cm. What is the total area?

    1. A42 sq cm
    2. B21 sq cm
    3. C84 sq cm
    4. D14 sq cm

    Each triangle: ½ × 4 × 3.5 = 7 sq cm. Total: 6 × 7 = 42 sq cm.

    Watch for: A common mistake in Area of Regular Polygons is stopping after finding the area of ONE triangle and reporting that as the total area — or adding the number of triangles instead of multiplying. Remember: total area = (one triangle's area) × (number of sides), not (one triangle's area) + (number of sides).
