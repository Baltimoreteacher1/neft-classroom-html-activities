  1. 1

    (Lesson 6.10) What is 1 1/2 ÷ 3/4?

    1. A2
    2. B1 1/8
    3. C3/4
    4. D9/8

    1 1/2 = 3/2. Then 3/2 ÷ 3/4 = 3/2 × 4/3 = 12/6 = 2.

    Watch for: A common mistake in Fraction Division Problem Solving is reversing which fraction is the dividend and which is the divisor when setting up the equation from a word problem. For example: a pitcher holds 3/4 cup of punch, and each serving is 1/8 cup — how many servings? The correct setup is total ÷ group size = 3/4 ÷ 1/8 = 3/4 × 8/1 = 24/4 = 6 servings. A student who reverses the setup calculates 1/8 ÷ 3/4 = 1/8 × 4/3 = 4/24 = 1/6, an answer smaller than 1 that can't possibly be a serving count. Before you submit, check: does my answer make sense as a NUMBER OF GROUPS? If the setup gives a fraction less than 1 for a 'how many' question, you likely swapped the dividend and divisor.

    (Lesson 6.10) Find the Mixed Number Error

    1. 1Problem2 1/3 ÷ 1/2
    2. 2Convert2 1/3 = 6/3
    3. 3Keep, Change, Flip6/3 × 2/1
    4. 4Multiply12/3 = 4

    Which step has the mistake? Explain it and write the correct work.

    The mistake is in Step 2. Correct work: 2 1/3 = 7/3 (not 6/3). Multiply: 2 × 3 = 6, then ADD the numerator 1 to get 7. So 7/3 × 2/1 = 14/3 = 4 2/3. The Convert step is wrong: the student wrote 2 1/3 = 6/3, multiplying 2 × 3 but never adding the numerator. The correct conversion is (2 × 3 + 1)/3 = 7/3, so 7/3 × 2/1 = 14/3 = 4 2/3.

  2. 2

    (Lesson 6.11) Find the Problem-Solving Error

    1. 1ProblemA tank has 3/4 gallon of water. Each bucket holds 1/6 gallon. How many buckets can be filled?
    2. 2Student's equation1/6 ÷ 3/4
    3. 3Solve1/6 × 4/3 = 4/18 = 2/9
    4. 4Answer2/9 buckets

    Which step has the mistake? Explain it and write the correct work.

    The mistake is in Step 2. Correct work: The equation should be 3/4 ÷ 1/6 (total ÷ group size). Then 3/4 × 6/1 = 18/4 = 4 1/2 buckets. The dividend and divisor are swapped. The 3/4 gallon is the total being split up and 1/6 gallon is one bucket, so the equation must be 3/4 ÷ 1/6 = 3/4 × 6/1 = 18/4 = 4 1/2 buckets. An answer of 2/9 of a bucket is a warning sign, since a 1/6-gallon bucket clearly fits inside 3/4 gallon more than once.
