RateSpanish: Tasa A ratio comparing two amounts with different units, like miles per hour. $12 for 4 games is a rate
Unit RateSpanish: Tasa unitaria A rate for just 1 of something, like cost for 1 item. $3 per 1 game
PerSpanish: Por For each one. Example: 5 dollars per book. 60 miles per hour means 60 miles for every 1 hour
RatioSpanish: Razón A way to compare two amounts, like 3 to 2. 5 to 3 or 5:3
Unit priceSpanish: Precio unitario A unit rate that gives the cost of exactly 1 item, like $0.60 per game. $3 for 5 games → $0.60 per 1 game

Watch outA common mistake

Two essential things to watch: First, always divide Top by Bottom (Cost ÷ Amount) so you find dollars per item. If you flip it upside down (3 ÷ 15), you find items per dollar instead. Second, the rate is originally over 1 (5/1 = $5 for 1 ticket), but we do NOT keep the 1 in everyday writing — we drop the 1 and write '$5 per ticket'. Finally, never decide from the total price alone: $15 for 3 tickets is $5 each, while $20 for 5 tickets is $4 each, making $20 the better value.
