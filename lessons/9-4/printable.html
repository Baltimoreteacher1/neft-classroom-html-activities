How do you place rational numbers on a number line?

A rational number is any number you can write as a fraction. This includes fractions, decimals, and integers. Many rational numbers fall between two whole numbers.

Key idea: To plot a rational number, find the two whole numbers it falls between, then split the space into equal parts.

Watch me

  • I will plot -2.5 on the number line.
  • First, the whole numbers it falls between: -2.5 is between -2 and -3.
  • The .5 means halfway, so I split the space between -2 and -3 in half.
  • I place my dot exactly halfway between -2 and -3. That is -2.5.

Let's try together

  • Now let's plot 0.75. Which two whole numbers is it between? It is between 0 and 1.
  • What does 0.75 mean? It is 3/4 of the way from 0 to 1.
  • So we split 0 to 1 into 4 equal parts and place the dot at the 3rd mark.

Now it's your turn

  • In the explore, plot each treasure clue like -2.5, 0.75, and -1.5 at its exact spot on the number line.
