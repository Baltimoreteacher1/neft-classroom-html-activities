Rates and Unit Rates (Tasas y tasas unitarias)Rate (Tasa)Unit Rate (Tasa unitaria)Per (Por)Ratio (Razón)Unit price (Precio unitario)Better Buy (Mejor compra)Divide (Dividir)

✏️ Worked Example

  1. Booth A charges $3 for 5 games. I want the cost for just 1 game.
  2. I divide the cost by the games: $3 ÷ 5 = $0.60.
  3. So Booth A costs $0.60 per game. That is the unit rate.
  4. Because this unit rate is money for one item, it has its own name: it is the unit price.
  5. Now I can compare this booth's unit price fairly with any other booth's.
