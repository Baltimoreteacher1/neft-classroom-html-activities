Remember3.2 Small Group · Group 1

To find a unit rate, divide the total cost by the number of items. A unit rate that gives a cost is called a unit price, and the lower unit price is the better buy. A rate compares two amounts with different units. A unit rate tells you the amount for just 1 of something, like the cost for 1 game. We'll walk through a worked example, try one together, then you'll try a few with hints right there when you need them.

    Token packs: Pack A is 20 tokens for $5.00, Pack B is 35 tokens for $7.00, and Pack C is 50 tokens for $12.50. Which pack is the best deal per token?

    1. APack B
    2. BPack A
    3. CPack C
    4. DThey are all the same

    Hint The 'best deal' is the pack where ONE token costs the least — not the pack with the most tokens.

    1. ARunner B
    2. BRunner A
    3. CSame rate

    Hint Each runner has laps and minutes. 'More laps per minute' means you need each runner's laps-per-minute rate first.

  3. 3Circle the letter of the best answer. Show how you know.

    2. B30 points per game
    3. C93 points per game
    4. D270 points per game

    Hint A unit rate tells you the amount for exactly one of something — here, for one game.

    1. APulse, at 15 points per minute

  5. 5Circle the letter of the best answer. Show how you know.

    Machine A: 150 coins for 5 plays. Machine B: 168 coins for 8 plays. Which is the better value (fewer coins per play)?

    1. AMachine A, at 30 coins per play
    2. BMachine B, at 21 coins per play
    3. CThey are the same value
    4. DMachine A, because it costs fewer coins total

    Hint "Better value" here means fewer coins for each single play, so the smaller unit rate wins.
