Grade 6 Mathematics · Unit 9: Relationships Between Two VariablesStandard 6.AT.11

Lesson 9.39.3 Small Group · Group 1

Group 1 · Answer KeySupported practice

Learning target With my small group, I can write an equation from a table or a graph to model the relationship between the dependent and independent variables — one step at a time, with support.

  1. 1Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    A bike rents for $8 per hour. Let h = hours and c = total cost. Write an equation for the total cost.

    1. 1Which quantity depends on the other: hours or total cost?The total cost depends on the hours.
    2. 2What is the cost in the 1-hour column?$8
    3. 3What do you multiply 1 hour by to get h hours?h
    4. 4Multiply the cost by h too. Complete: c = ___h.8
    Final answer:The equation is c = 8h.
  2. 2Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    A graph shows the cost of movie tickets. It goes through (1, 9), (2, 18) and (4, 36). Let t = tickets and c = total cost. Write an equation. Then find the cost of 6 tickets.

    1. 1Read the point (1, 9). What does 1 ticket cost?$9
    2. 2Write the equation for the total cost.c = 9t
    3. 3Use the equation. What do 6 tickets cost?$54
    Final answer:The equation is c = 9t. Six tickets cost 9 × 6 = 54 dollars.
  3. 3Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    A table shows 1 book costs $4, 2 books cost $8 and 3 books cost $12. Let b = books and c = total cost. Complete the equation: c = ___b.

    Hint How much does 1 book cost? That number goes in the blank.

    Final answer:4

    Why: Write the 1-book column in the table: (1, 4) Scale 1 book by b: 1 × b = b Do the same to the cost: c = 4 × b = 4b

  4. 4Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    A music plan costs $12.99 for 1 month, $25.98 for 2 months and $38.97 for 3 months. Let m = months and c = total cost. Complete the equation: c = ___m.

    Hint Subtract one row's cost from the next row's cost. That is how much each month adds.

    Final answer:12.99

    Why: Write the 1-month column in the table: (1, 12.99) Scale 1 month by m: 1 × m = m Do the same to the cost: c = 12.99 × m = 12.99m

  5. 5Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    Ana earns $11 for each hour she babysits. Let h = hours and e = money earned. Write an equation. How much does she earn in 5 hours?

    Hint Say it in words first: money earned = $11 times the hours. Then put 5 in for h.

    Final answer:$55

    Why: Write the known ratio in the table: (1, 11) Scale 1 hour by h to write the equation: e = 11h Find the scale factor: 5 ÷ 1 = 5 Do the same to the dollars: 11 × 5 = 55

  6. 6Circle the letter of the best answer. Show how you know.

    A table shows 1 hour costs $6, 2 hours cost $12 and 3 hours cost $18. Leo writes c = 6 + h. Which equation is correct?

    1. Ac = 6h
    2. Bc = 6 + h
    3. Ch = 6c

    Hint Test each equation with 2 hours. Which one gives $12?

    Answer: A — c = 6h

    Why: Write the 1-hour column in the table: (1, 6) Scale by 2: do the same to the cost: 6 × 2 = 12 Scale by h instead: 6 × h = 6h Test c = 6 + h with 2 hours: 6 + 2 = 8

    Watch for: Writing the equation with addition — c = 24.95 + m — instead of multiplication. The fee is charged EVERY month, so the total is the rate times the number of months; always test the equation against a row of the table.
