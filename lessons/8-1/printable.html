Shanice is building a door from planks cut out of one wide board. How can Shanice determine the number of planks needed?

How do I turn a word clue into an equation?

An equation is a math sentence with an equal sign (=). To write one, I pick a letter (a variable) for the unknown number and match the words to math symbols.

Key idea: Equations & Solutions. Formula: Left Expression = Right Expression. 1. An equation is a mathematical sentence stating two expressions are equal. 2. A solution is a value for the variable that makes the equation true. 3. Check: Substitute the value into the equation and verify that both sides balance

Watch me

  • Clue: 'A number of stolen gems plus 8 more equals 20 total.'
  • The unknown is the number of gems. I let n stand for it.
  • 'Plus 8 more' means + 8. 'Equals 20 total' means = 20.
  • So my equation is n + 8 = 20.

Let's try together

  • New clue: 'A number plus 5 equals 9.' What is the unknown?
  • We let n be the unknown. What does 'plus 5' tell us to write?
  • What does 'equals 9' tell us? Put it together: n + 5 = 9.

Your turn — test a solution

  • Here is the equation from the clue: n + 5 = 9. A detective guesses n = 3.
  • Substitute the guess: replace n with 3 to get 3 + 5 = 9.
  • Is that statement true? 3 + 5 is 8, and 8 does not equal 9, so 3 is NOT a solution.
  • Revise the guess and test again until the statement is true. Which value makes it true, and how do you know it is the solution?
