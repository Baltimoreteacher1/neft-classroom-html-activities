    What is 6 ÷ 1/3?

    1. A18
    2. B2
    3. C6/3
    4. D3

    6 ÷ 1/3 = 6 × 3/1 = 18. There are 18 thirds in 6 wholes.

    Watch for: A common mistake in Divide Whole Numbers by Fractions is doing 'Keep, Change' but skipping the 'Flip' — students multiply by the divisor as it is instead of by its reciprocal. For example, for 6 ÷ 3/4, a student might write 6 × 3/4 = 4 1/2 and stop there, but the divisor 3/4 must be flipped to 4/3 first: 6 × 4/3 = 24/3 = 8. Before you submit, check: did I flip the second fraction (the divisor) upside down before multiplying?

    Spot the Common Mistake

    1. 1Problem4 ÷ 1/5
    2. 2Keep, Change, FlipKept the 4 and changed ÷ to ×, but forgot to flip: 4 × 1/5
    3. 3Multiply4 × 1/5 = 4/5

    Which step has the mistake? Explain it and write the correct work.

    The mistake is in Step 2. Correct work: Flip the divisor 1/5 to its reciprocal 5/1 before multiplying: 4 × 5/1 = 20. The mistake was multiplying by 1/5 instead of flipping it to 5. (The answer should be larger than 4, not smaller.) The Keep, Change, Flip step changed the ÷ to × but never flipped the divisor, so it multiplied by 1/5 instead of by 5/1. The reciprocal of 1/5 is 5, so 4 ÷ 1/5 = 4 × 5 = 20. That size makes sense too: 4 wholes contain twenty 1/5-size pieces, so the answer has to be bigger than 4, not the 4/5 that was written.
