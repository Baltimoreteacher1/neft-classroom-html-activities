Learning target I can find the surface area of a solid by using its net, explain why the method works, and use it on a problem I have not seen before.

Part 1Understand the ideamodels and meaning
  1. 1Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    A toy chest is 3 ft long, 2 ft wide, and 2.5 ft tall. Paint costs $3 for each square foot. What does it cost to paint every face of the chest?

    Final answer:$111

    Why: Find each pair of faces: 2 × (3 × 2) = 12; 2 × (3 × 2.5) = 15; 2 × (2 × 2.5) = 10 Add the three pairs: 12 + 15 + 10 = 37 Multiply by the cost: 37 × $3 = $111

Part 2Apply itreal-world problems
  1. 2Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    A cube has a surface area of 150 square centimeters. How long is each edge?

    1. 1How many faces does a cube have? Are they the same size?6 faces, all congruent squares
    2. 2What is the area of one face?25
    3. 3Which edge length makes a square with area 25?5 cm
    Final answer:Each edge is 5 cm. Check: 6 × (5 × 5) = 150.
  2. 3Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    A box is 5 in long and 4 in wide. Its surface area is 112 square inches. How tall is the box?

    1. 1What is the area of the top and bottom together?40
    2. 2How much area is left for the four side faces?72
    3. 3The four sides unfold into one rectangle 18 in long. How tall is the box?4 in
    4. 4Why is that long rectangle 18 in long?The four side faces go around the base: 5 + 4 + 5 + 4 = 18, the perimeter of the base.
    Final answer:The box is 4 in tall. Check: 40 + 40 + 32 = 112.
  3. 4Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    A box is 4.5 cm long, 2 cm wide, and 3 cm tall. Find its surface area.

    Final answer:57 square centimeters

    Why: Find each pair of faces: 2 × (4.5 × 2) = 18; 2 × (4.5 × 3) = 27; 2 × (2 × 3) = 12 Add the three pairs: 18 + 27 + 12 = 57

  4. 5Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    A cube-shaped box needs 216 square inches of paper to cover it with no overlap. How long is each edge?

    Final answer:6 in

    Why: Find the area of one face: 216 ÷ 6 = 36 Find the side of that square: 6 × 6 = 36 Check with the surface area: 6 × 36 = 216

  5. 6Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    Kim says boxes with the same volume need the same paper. Box A is 3 in × 3 in × 4 in. Box B is 9 in × 4 in × 1 in. Both hold 36 cubic inches. How much more paper does B need?

    Final answer:32 square inches

    Why: Find the surface area of Box A: 2 × 9 + 2 × 12 + 2 × 12 = 66 Find the surface area of Box B: 2 × 36 + 2 × 9 + 2 × 4 = 98 Subtract: 98 − 66 = 32
