🚀 Launch

Captain Vega left a treasure map, but instead of an 'X marks the spot,' she hid the directions as ordered pairs! To find the treasure, you need to plot each point on the coordinate plane and connect them in order. The first clue says: Start at (2, 1), then go to (5, 4), then (8, 1).

How do you plot a point on the coordinate plane?

An ordered pair (x, y) is two numbers that tell you exactly where a point goes. The first number moves you right or left. The second number moves you up or down.

Key idea: Coordinate Plane: Plotting Points. Formula: Ordered Pair: (x, y) = (Horizontal, Vertical). 1. Always start at the Origin (0, 0). 2. x-coordinate: Move right for positive (+), left for negative (-). 3. y-coordinate: Move up for positive (+), down for negative (-)

Watch me

  • I will plot the point (5, 4). The 5 is the x-coordinate, and the 4 is the y-coordinate.
  • I start at the origin, which is (0, 0).
  • First I move 5 units to the right along the x-axis.
  • Then I move 4 units up. I place my dot. That is the point (5, 4).

Let's try together

  • Now let's plot (2, 1). Where do we start? At the origin (0, 0).
  • How far do we move right? The first number is 2, so we move 2 right.
  • How far do we move up? The second number is 1, so we move 1 up and place the dot.
