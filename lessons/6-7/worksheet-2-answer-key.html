SECTION 3 MATHEMATICAL WRITING & ERROR ANALYSIS [Reasoning & Critique]
  1. 6 ERROR ANALYSIS

    Find the error

    1. 1Problem:List the factors of 16.
    2. 2Step 1:16 = 1 × 16, 2 × 8, 4 × 4
    3. 3Step 2:So the factors are 2, 4, and 8.

    Which step contains the error? Explain the mathematical misconception and write the correct calculation below.

    Key Analysis: Mistake is in Step 2. Correct Work: Step 2 drops 1 and 16. Every factor pair contributes both of its numbers, so the full list is 1, 2, 4, 8, 16.. Misconception: 1 and the number itself are always factors. Leaving them out is what makes a GCF come out too small.
