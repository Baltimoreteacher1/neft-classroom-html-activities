✏️ Worked Example

  1. Problem: Find the LCM of 6 and 8.
  2. Step 1: Multiples of 6: 6, 12, 18, 24, 30, 36, 42, 48
  3. Step 2: Multiples of 8: 8, 16, 24, 32, 40, 48
  4. Step 3: Both lists contain 48, so the LCM is 48.
