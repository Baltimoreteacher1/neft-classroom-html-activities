          What is the surface area of a rectangular prism with l = 5 cm, w = 4 cm, h = 3 cm?

          1. A94 cm²
          2. B60 cm²
          3. C94 cm³
          4. D47 cm²

          SA = 2(5×4) + 2(5×3) + 2(4×3) = 40 + 30 + 24 = 94 cm².

          Watch for: A common mistake in Surface Area of Prisms is confusing surface area with volume — multiplying l × w × h (which finds the space INSIDE the prism, in cubic units) instead of adding the areas of all the faces (SA = 2lw + 2lh + 2wh, in square units). A second common slip is forgetting to double one face pair, like writing 2(l×w) + 2(l×h) + (w×h) instead of + 2(w×h) — always count all 6 faces (or all 5 for a triangular prism) before you submit.
