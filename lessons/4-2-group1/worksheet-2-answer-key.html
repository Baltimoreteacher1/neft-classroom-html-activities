Part 1Apply itreal-world problems
  1. 1Circle the letter of the best answer. Show how you know.

    A game shows your score as 7/8 complete. What percent is that?

    1. A75%
    2. B78%
    3. C87.5%
    4. D88%

    Hint Try the out-of-100 move first: is there a whole number you can multiply 8 by to get exactly 100?

    Answer: C — 87.5%

    Why: 8 does not scale to 100 by a whole number, so divide: 7 ÷ 8 = 0.875, and 0.875 × 100 = 87.5%. That is 87.5 out of 100.

    Watch for: A common mistake in Relate Fractions, Decimals, and Percents is treating the numerator and denominator as the percent's digits — turning 3/5 into "35%" by just reading the 3 and the 5. Percent means out of 100, so the fix is to build an equivalent fraction with denominator 100: 5 × 20 = 100, so multiply the top by 20 too — 3/5 = 60/100 = 60%. A second version of this mistake is scaling only ONE part of the fraction (writing 3/5 = 3/100 or 60/5). Whatever you multiply the denominator by, you must multiply the numerator by the same number. When a denominator will not reach 100 evenly (8, 3, 7), divide the numerator by the denominator instead and then multiply by 100.

Part 2Explain and correctreasoning
  1. 4Find the mistake. Explain it, then show the correct work.

    Spot the Common Mistake

    1. 1Convert 3/5 to a percent3/5
    2. 2Read the digits as the percentThe numerator is 3 and the denominator is 5, so the student writes 35%.
    3. 3Final answer3/5 = 35%

    Which step has the mistake? Explain what went wrong, then show the correct work.

    Hint Do the digits 3 and 5 tell you how many parts out of 100?

    Mistake: Step 2. The two digits of a fraction are not the two digits of a percent. Percent means out of 100, so rewrite 3/5 with a denominator of 100: multiply the bottom by 20 to reach 100, and the top by that same 20. That gives 3/5 = 60/100 = 60%, not 35%.

    Correct work: You cannot just read the digits. Percent means out of 100, so build the equivalent fraction: 5 × 20 = 100, and 3 × 20 = 60. So 3/5 = 60/100 = 0.6 = 60%, not 35%.

  2. 5Find the mistake. Explain it, then show the correct work.

    Level 2 Extension — Find the Decimal-to-Percent Mistake

    1. 1Convert 0.4 to a percent0.4
    2. 2Move the decimal point0.4 → 0.04
    3. 3Write the percent0.04 = 4%
    4. 4Final answer0.4 = 4%

    Which step has the mistake? Explain what went wrong, then show the correct work.

    Hint Which way does the decimal move when you go from a decimal to a percent — left or right?

    Mistake: Step 2. Going from a decimal to a percent means multiplying by 100, which moves the decimal point two places to the RIGHT. Moving it left divides instead, which is why 0.4 turned into 0.04. Done correctly, 0.4 × 100 = 40, so 0.4 = 40%.

    Correct work: To change a decimal to a percent, multiply by 100 (move the decimal two places RIGHT): 0.4 × 100 = 40. So 0.4 = 40%, not 4%.

  3. 6Find the mistake. Explain it, then show the correct work.

    Find the Conversion Error

    1. 1Convert 2/3 to a percent2/3
    2. 2Divide numerator by denominator2 ÷ 3 = 0.67
    3. 3Move decimal two places0.67 → 67%
    4. 4Final answer2/3 = 67%

    Which step has the mistake? Explain what went wrong, then show the correct work.

    Hint 2 ÷ 3 doesn't terminate — is 0.67 the exact decimal?

    Mistake: Step 2. The division is set up the right way, but 2 ÷ 3 does not end — it gives 0.666... repeating. Writing 0.67 rounds the value, so the percent it produces (67%) is rounded too. The exact answer is 66.7% or 66⅔%.

    Correct work: 2 ÷ 3 = 0.666... (repeating). The exact answer is 66.7% or 66⅔%.
