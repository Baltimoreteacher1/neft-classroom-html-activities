  1. 1

    Convert 3/5 to a percent.

    1. A60%
    2. B35%
    3. C53%
    4. D65%

    5 × 20 = 100, so 3 × 20 = 60. That makes 3/5 = 60/100 = 0.6 = 60%.

    Watch for: A common mistake in Relate Fractions, Decimals, and Percents is treating the numerator and denominator as the percent's digits — turning 3/5 into "35%" by just reading the 3 and the 5. Percent means out of 100, so the fix is to build an equivalent fraction with denominator 100: 5 × 20 = 100, so multiply the top by 20 too — 3/5 = 60/100 = 60%. A second version of this mistake is scaling only ONE part of the fraction (writing 3/5 = 3/100 or 60/5). Whatever you multiply the denominator by, you must multiply the numerator by the same number. When a denominator will not reach 100 evenly (8, 3, 7), divide the numerator by the denominator instead and then multiply by 100.

    Spot the Common Mistake

    1. 1Convert 3/5 to a percent3/5
    2. 2Read the digits as the percentThe numerator is 3 and the denominator is 5, so the student writes 35%.
    3. 3Final answer3/5 = 35%

    Which step has the mistake? Explain it and write the correct work.

    The mistake is in Step 2. Correct work: You cannot just read the digits. Percent means out of 100, so build the equivalent fraction: 5 × 20 = 100, and 3 × 20 = 60. So 3/5 = 60/100 = 0.6 = 60%, not 35%. The two digits of a fraction are not the two digits of a percent. Percent means out of 100, so rewrite 3/5 with a denominator of 100: multiply the bottom by 20 to reach 100, and the top by that same 20. That gives 3/5 = 60/100 = 60%, not 35%.

  2. 2

    A game shows your score as 7/8 complete. What percent is that?

    1. A87.5%
    2. B78%
    3. C75%
    4. D88%

    8 does not scale to 100 by a whole number, so divide: 7 ÷ 8 = 0.875, and 0.875 × 100 = 87.5%. That is 87.5 out of 100.

  5. 5

    Level 2 Extension — Find the Decimal-to-Percent Mistake

    1. 1Convert 0.4 to a percent0.4
    2. 2Move the decimal point0.4 → 0.04
    3. 3Write the percent0.04 = 4%
    4. 4Final answer0.4 = 4%

    Which step has the mistake? Explain it and write the correct work.

    The mistake is in Step 2. Correct work: To change a decimal to a percent, multiply by 100 (move the decimal two places RIGHT): 0.4 × 100 = 40. So 0.4 = 40%, not 4%. Going from a decimal to a percent means multiplying by 100, which moves the decimal point two places to the RIGHT. Moving it left divides instead, which is why 0.4 turned into 0.04. Done correctly, 0.4 × 100 = 40, so 0.4 = 40%.

  6. 6

    Find the Conversion Error

    1. 1Convert 2/3 to a percent2/3
    2. 2Divide numerator by denominator2 ÷ 3 = 0.67
    3. 3Move decimal two places0.67 → 67%
    4. 4Final answer2/3 = 67%

    Which step has the mistake? Explain it and write the correct work.

    The mistake is in Step 2. Correct work: 2 ÷ 3 = 0.666... (repeating). The exact answer is 66.7% or 66⅔%. The division is set up the right way, but 2 ÷ 3 does not end — it gives 0.666... repeating. Writing 0.67 rounds the value, so the percent it produces (67%) is rounded too. The exact answer is 66.7% or 66⅔%.
