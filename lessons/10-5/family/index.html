Vocabulary you might hear

  • repetition — The same object, shape, or form repeated multiple times.
    Español: repetición — El mismo objeto, figura o forma repetido varias veces.
  • pattern — A set of objects or shapes that are repeated and arranged in a regular order.
    Español: patrón — Un conjunto de objetos o figuras que se repiten y se organizan en un orden regular.
  • pattern unit — The smallest chunk of a pattern that repeats.
    Español: unidad del patrón — La parte más pequeña de un patrón que se repite.
  • rhythm — A design element created by varying the placement, size, orientation, or order of the same objects or shapes.
    Español: ritmo — Un elemento de diseño creado al variar la posición, el tamaño, la orientación o el orden de los mismos objetos o figuras.
  • predictability — Being able to know what comes next because the design follows a rule.
    Español: previsibilidad — Poder saber qué viene después porque el diseño sigue una regla.

How we did it in class today

  1. A border repeats: pink circle, purple square, blue triangle, pink circle, purple square, blue triangle, ...
  2. The pattern unit is 3 shapes long: circle, square, triangle.
  3. To predict shape number 12, I divide: 12 ÷ 3 = 4 with no remainder, so shape 12 ends a complete unit — it is the blue triangle.
  4. The next three shapes after any complete unit will be a pink circle, a purple square, and a blue triangle.

If they get stuck...

Watch out for this: Calling every repeated design a 'pattern.' One identical shape repeated is repetition; a pattern needs a unit of shapes repeating in a regular order; and rhythm varies the shapes' size and order, so it is neither.

Try asking: "Can you show me how you got that? Did we miss a step?"
