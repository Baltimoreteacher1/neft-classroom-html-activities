Lateral face

Problem: A square pyramid has a base edge of 5 in and a slant height of 7 in. What is the surface area?

  1. 95 in²
  2. 70 in²
  3. 95 in³
  4. 120 in²
  1. Step 1 Base: 5 × 5 = 25 in².
  2. Step 2 Each lateral face: ½ × 5 × 7 = 17.5 in².
  3. Step 3 Four faces: 4 × 17.5 = 70 in².
  4. Step 4 SA = 25 + 70 = 95 in².

✅ Answer: A. 95 in²

Answer Key & Teacher Guide

  1. Notes 1: Pyramid
  2. Notes 2: Slant height
  3. Notes 3: Lateral face
  4. Notes 4: Base
  5. Notes 5: Apex
  6. Notes 6: Lateral area
  7. We Try (notes): A. 4 — A square pyramid has a square base and 4 triangular lateral faces — one for each side of the square.
  8. You Try (notes): A. 24 in² — Area of a triangle = ½ × base × height = ½ × 8 × 6 = 24 in².
  9. Try It 1: A. 20 in² — Area of a triangle = ½ × base × height = ½ × 8 × 5 = 20 in². You use the slant height (5) as the triangle's height, not the pyramid's vertical height.
  10. Try It 2: A. 81 in² — The base is a square, so base area = side × side = 9 × 9 = 81 in². You would still add the four triangular faces to get the full surface area.
  11. Exit Ticket: A. 96 in² — Base: 6 × 6 = 36 in². Each lateral face: ½ × 6 × 5 = 15 in². Four faces: 4 × 15 = 60 in². SA = 36 + 60 = 96 in². Surface area uses square units (in²).
