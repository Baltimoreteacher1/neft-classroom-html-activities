✏️ Worked Example

  1. Lesson 2.1 — Understand Statistical Questions: If you expect the answers to vary, it is a statistical question.
  2. Lesson 2.3 — Describe the Data Using the Median: The median is the middle value once the data is in order. With an even count, it is the mean of the two middle values — and it is the measure that an unusually high or low value cannot drag around.
  3. Lesson 2.9 — Describe Data by Mean Absolute Deviation: MAD = the average of how far each number is from the mean (always a positive distance).

    3. 3Find the MADSum of absolute deviations = 4 + 1 + 2 + 3 = 10. MAD = 10 ÷ 3 ≈ 3.33
