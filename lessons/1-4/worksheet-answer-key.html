    An egg muffin is about 60 calories. A plate holds 4 egg muffins. About how many calories are on the plate?

    1. AAbout 240
    2. BAbout 64
    3. CAbout 15
    4. DAbout 600

    4 muffins at about 60 calories each is 60 × 4 = 240 calories.

    Watch for: Reporting volume without cubic units — saying the garden needs '48 feet' of soil, which is a length, or '48 square feet', which is an area.

  2. 2

    A large cereal box is 8 in long, 3 in wide, and 10 in tall. What is its volume?

    1. A240 cubic inches
    2. B21 cubic inches
    3. C80 cubic inches
    4. D240 square inches

    Volume of a rectangular prism is length × width × height: 8 × 3 × 10 = 240 cubic inches.

    Watch for: Reporting volume without cubic units — saying the garden needs '48 feet' of soil, which is a length, or '48 square feet', which is an area.

  3. 3

    Each jumbo box holds 400 cubic inches. How much do TWO jumbo boxes hold?

    1. A800 cubic inches
    2. B402 cubic inches
    3. C200 cubic inches
    4. D4,000 cubic inches

    Two boxes of 400 cubic inches each is 400 × 2 = 800 cubic inches.

      Three large boxes hold 240 cubic inches each. Two jumbo boxes hold 400 cubic inches each. Which is the correct volume comparison?

      1. A720 cubic inches vs. 800 cubic inches — the jumbo option holds more
      2. B720 cubic inches vs. 800 cubic inches — the large option holds more
      3. C240 cubic inches vs. 400 cubic inches — the jumbo option holds more
      4. D480 cubic inches vs. 800 cubic inches — the jumbo option holds more

      240 × 3 = 720 and 400 × 2 = 800; since 800 > 720, the two jumbo boxes hold more in total.

      Watch for: Reporting volume without cubic units — saying the garden needs '48 feet' of soil, which is a length, or '48 square feet', which is an area.

      1. A48 cubic feet
      2. B24 cubic feet
      3. C19 cubic feet
      4. D96 cubic feet

      First bed: 4 × 3 × 2 = 24 cubic feet. Second bed: 6 × 2 × 2 = 24 cubic feet. Together: 24 + 24 = 48 cubic feet.

      A student claims: “Greater box volume ALWAYS means more cereal inside.” Which is the best counterexample?

      1. AA jumbo box with more volume that is sold half-filled with air
      2. BA large box that costs more than a jumbo box
      3. CTwo jumbo boxes that are easier to store than three large boxes

      A counterexample must break the claim itself: a bigger box holding less cereal (more air) shows greater volume does not guarantee more cereal.

      The garden needs 48 cubic feet of soil. One decomposition used two prisms of 24 cubic feet each. Which of these is ANOTHER decomposition of the same garden volume?

      1. AA 4 × 4 × 2 prism and a 4 × 2 × 2 prism
      2. BA 4 × 4 × 2 prism and a 4 × 4 × 2 prism

      4. DA 6 × 4 × 2 prism and a 2 × 2 × 2 prism

      4 × 4 × 2 = 32 and 4 × 2 × 2 = 16, and 32 + 16 = 48 cubic feet — the same total by a different split.
