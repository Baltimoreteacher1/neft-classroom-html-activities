📖 What we're learning tonightQué aprendemos hoy

English

Tonight we build a math argument. We use volume — the space inside a box — to defend a recommendation with numbers instead of opinions.

Key words
  • argument
  • conjecture
  • counterexample
  • volume
  • cubic unit
Español

Esta noche construimos un argumento matemático. Usamos el volumen —el espacio dentro de una caja— para defender una recomendación con números y no con opiniones.

Palabras clave
  • argumento
  • conjetura
  • contraejemplo
  • volumen
  • unidad cúbica

🎯 The big ideaLa idea principal

1Real example: a large box is 8 inches long, 3 inches wide, and 10 inches tall
2Its volume is 8 × 3 × 10 = 240 cubic inches
3A jumbo box is 10 × 4 × 10 = 400 cubic inches

Follow the picture pathSigan la ruta visual

  1. 👀StartEmpieza
  2. 🧩Show itMuéstralo
  3. ✏️SolveResuelve
  4. ✅CheckRevisa

In one sentence: Volume = length × width × height, measured in cubic units. Compare the totals, then say which choice is better and why.

En una frase: Volumen = largo × ancho × alto, medido en unidades cúbicas. Compara los totales y luego di cuál opción es mejor y por qué.

  1. 👀
    StartEmpiezaStep 1Paso 1

    Real example: a large box is 8 inches long, 3 inches wide, and 10 inches tall.

    Ejemplo real: una caja grande mide 8 pulgadas de largo, 3 de ancho y 10 de alto.

  2. 🧩
    Show itMuéstraloStep 2Paso 2

    Its volume is 8 × 3 × 10 = 240 cubic inches.

    Su volumen es 8 × 3 × 10 = 240 pulgadas cúbicas.

    More detailMás detalle

    Three of those boxes hold 240 × 3 = 720 cubic inches.

    Tres cajas así contienen 240 × 3 = 720 pulgadas cúbicas.

  3. ✏️
    SolveResuelveStep 3Paso 3

    A jumbo box is 10 × 4 × 10 = 400 cubic inches.

    Una caja jumbo mide 10 × 4 × 10 = 400 pulgadas cúbicas.

    More detailMás detalle

    Two of those hold 400 × 2 = 800 cubic inches.

    Dos de esas contienen 400 × 2 = 800 pulgadas cúbicas.

  4. ✅
    CheckRevisaStep 4Paso 4

    800 is more than 720, so the argument is: "Two jumbo boxes hold more, because 800 cubic inches > 720 cubic inches.".

    800 es más que 720, así que el argumento es: "Dos cajas jumbo contienen más, porque 800 pulgadas cúbicas > 720 pulgadas cúbicas".

  • 👀

    Listen for "argument" (argumento) — a claim backed by numbers, not a guess.

    Escucha la palabra "argumento": una afirmación respaldada por números, no una suposición.

  • 📦

    Volume is measured in CUBIC units (cubic inches, cubic cm), because it fills space in three directions.

    El volumen se mide en unidades CÚBICAS (pulgadas cúbicas, cm cúbicos), porque llena el espacio en tres direcciones.

  • 🗣️

    Ask "Why?" after every answer. The reason is the part being graded tonight.

    Pregunta "¿Por qué?" después de cada respuesta. La razón es lo que se evalúa esta noche.

📚 Words to knowPalabras clave

Tap a card to flip. Use these words when you talk about the math together. Toquen una tarjeta para voltearla. Usen estas palabras cuando hablen de la matemática juntos.

argument

argumento

💡 “Two jumbo boxes hold more, because 400 × 2 = 800 > 720.”
TapToca ➔

A chain of reasons — equations, drawings, or words — used to defend your thinking.

Una cadena de razones — ecuaciones, dibujos o palabras — que usas para defender tu pensamiento.

📌 “Two jumbo boxes hold more, because 400 × 2 = 800 > 720.”

conjecture

conjetura

💡 “A bigger box ALWAYS holds more cereal” — believed, not yet proven.
TapToca ➔

A mathematical statement you believe is true but have not yet proven.

Una afirmación matemática que crees verdadera pero que aún no has probado.

📌 “A bigger box ALWAYS holds more cereal” — believed, not yet proven.

counterexample

contraejemplo

💡 One jumbo box sold half full of air breaks “bigger always holds more”.
TapToca ➔

A single example that shows a statement is not always true.

Un solo ejemplo que muestra que una afirmación no siempre es verdadera.

📌 One jumbo box sold half full of air breaks “bigger always holds more”.

volume

volumen

💡 8 in × 3 in × 10 in = 240 cubic inches of cereal space.
TapToca ➔

The amount of space a solid figure takes up, measured in cubic units.

La cantidad de espacio que ocupa una figura sólida, medida en unidades cúbicas.

📌 8 in × 3 in × 10 in = 240 cubic inches of cereal space.

cubic unit

unidad cúbica

💡 One cube, 1 unit on every edge — the block volume is counted in.
TapToca ➔

A cube with edges 1 unit long — the unit used to measure volume.

Un cubo cuyas aristas miden 1 unidad — la unidad que se usa para medir el volumen.

📌 One cube, 1 unit on every edge — the block volume is counted in.

🤝 Try this togetherInténtenlo juntos

Find two boxes at home. Measure each one's length, width, and height.

Busquen dos cajas en casa. Midan el largo, el ancho y el alto de cada una.

Work side by side. You ask questions; your student does the thinking. Trabajen juntos. Tú haces preguntas; tu estudiante piensa.

  1. Step 1Paso 1

    Multiply the three measurements for the first box.

    Multipliquen las tres medidas de la primera caja.

  2. Step 2Paso 2

    Do the same for the second box.

    Hagan lo mismo con la segunda caja.

  3. Step 3Paso 3

    Say which box holds more, and finish the sentence "because…"

    Digan cuál caja contiene más y terminen la frase "porque…"

📈 Practice ladder — easiest first 📈 Escalera de práctica — del más fácil al más difícil

    An egg muffin is about 60 calories. A plate holds 4 egg muffins. About how many calories are on the plate?

    1. About 240
    2. About 64
    3. About 15
    4. About 600
    👁️ Show answer👁️ Ver respuesta

    A. About 240

  2. ★ Start easyEmpieza fácil

    A large cereal box is 8 in long, 3 in wide, and 10 in tall. What is its volume?

    1. 240 cubic inches
    2. 21 cubic inches
    3. 80 cubic inches
    4. 240 square inches
    👁️ Show answer👁️ Ver respuesta

    A. 240 cubic inches

  3. ★★ Keep goingSigan

    Three large boxes hold 240 cubic inches each. Two jumbo boxes hold 400 cubic inches each. Which is the correct volume comparison?

    1. 720 cubic inches vs. 800 cubic inches — the jumbo option holds more
    2. 720 cubic inches vs. 800 cubic inches — the large option holds more
    3. 240 cubic inches vs. 400 cubic inches — the jumbo option holds more
    4. 480 cubic inches vs. 800 cubic inches — the jumbo option holds more
    👁️ Show answer👁️ Ver respuesta

    A. 720 cubic inches vs. 800 cubic inches — the jumbo option holds more

  4. ★★ Keep goingSigan

    One garden bed is 4 ft long, 3 ft wide, and 2 ft deep. A second bed is 6 ft long, 2 ft wide, and 2 ft deep. How much soil fills BOTH beds?

    1. 48 cubic feet
    2. 24 cubic feet
    3. 19 cubic feet
    4. 96 cubic feet
    👁️ Show answer👁️ Ver respuesta

    A. 48 cubic feet

  5. ★★★ ChallengeReto

    A student claims: “Greater box volume ALWAYS means more cereal inside.” Which is the best counterexample?

    1. A jumbo box with more volume that is sold half-filled with air
    2. A large box that costs more than a jumbo box
    3. Two jumbo boxes that are easier to store than three large boxes
    4. A box of a different cereal brand
    👁️ Show answer👁️ Ver respuesta

    A. A jumbo box with more volume that is sold half-filled with air
