6.NS.2 Version A — Answer Key

Divide Multi-Digit Numbers

Answer Key

Name: Date:
  1. 1

    What is 936 ÷ 12?

    1. A78
    2. B87
    3. C68
    4. D76

    12 × 78 = 936. You can verify: 12 × 70 = 840, 12 × 8 = 96, 840 + 96 = 936.

  2. 2

    What is 2,485 ÷ 5?

    1. A497
    2. B487
    3. C507
    4. D496

    5 × 497 = 2,485. Check: 5 × 400 = 2,000, 5 × 90 = 450, 5 × 7 = 35. 2,000 + 450 + 35 = 2,485.

  3. 3

    What is 756 ÷ 9?

    1. A84
    2. B83
    3. C94
    4. D86

    9 × 84 = 756. Check: 9 × 80 = 720, 9 × 4 = 36, 720 + 36 = 756.

  4. 4

    ProblemFirst ChunkRemainingSecond ChunkQuotient
    384 ÷ 88 × 40 = 320384 − 320 = 648 × 8 = 6448
    672 ÷ 66 × 100 = 600672 − 600 = 726 × 12 = 72112
  5. 5

    What is 1,125 ÷ 9?

    1. A125
    2. B115
    3. C135
    4. D124

    9 × 125 = 1,125. Check: 9 × 100 = 900, 9 × 25 = 225, 900 + 225 = 1,125.
