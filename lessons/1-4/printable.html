How do you divide big numbers?

Dividing means splitting a total into equal groups. Partial quotients let you divide a big number by taking away easy chunks until nothing is left.

Key idea: Subtract easy chunks of the divisor, then add up how many you took out to get the quotient.

Watch me

  • I want 1,344 ÷ 12. The dividend is 1,344 and the divisor is 12.
  • First I take a big easy chunk: 12 × 100 = 1,200. I subtract: 1,344 − 1,200 = 144.
  • Now I divide 144: 12 × 12 = 144. I subtract: 144 − 144 = 0, so nothing is left.
  • I add my chunks: 100 + 12 = 112. So 1,344 ÷ 12 = 112.
  • I check by multiplying: 12 × 112 = 1,344. Correct, and the remainder is 0.

Let's try together

  • Let's do 384 ÷ 8. What easy chunk of 8 can we take from 384? (8 × 40 = 320.)
  • Subtract: 384 − 320 = 64. How many 8s are in 64? (8 × 8 = 64.)
  • Add the chunks: 40 + 8 = 48. So 384 ÷ 8 = 48. Check: 8 × 48 = 384.

Now it's your turn

  • Next, you will sort the steps that solve 1,344 ÷ 12 and then divide on your own.
  • Remember: take away easy chunks, then add how many you used.
