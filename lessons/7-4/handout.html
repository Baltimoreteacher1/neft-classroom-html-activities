Compare and Order Integers and Rational Numbers

6.NOS.8 · Unit 7 · Lesson 4

Name   Period

Objectives / Objetivos

Content Objective: I can compare and order integers using a number line.

Language Objective: I can explain my reasoning using the words compare, order, greater than, and less than.

Key Idea / Idea clave

Comparing & Ordering Rational Numbers. Formula: Left is Less (<) | Right is Greater (>). 1. Plot or visualize numbers on a horizontal number line. 2. Numbers further to the left are always less than numbers to the right. 3. For negatives: A negative number with a larger absolute value is smaller (-10 < -2)

I Notice / Observo

  • Which treasure is closest to the surface?
  • Is -7 greater than or less than -2?

I Wonder / Me pregunto

  • Why does a bigger negative number mean a deeper location?
