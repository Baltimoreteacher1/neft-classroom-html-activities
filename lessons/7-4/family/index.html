A quick guide for families and for any student who missed class.

What students are learning

To compare integers means to see which one is greater or less. On a number line, numbers get bigger as you go right and smaller as you go left.

Key idea: Comparing & Ordering Rational Numbers. Formula: Left is Less (<) | Right is Greater (>). 1. Plot or visualize numbers on a horizontal number line. 2. Numbers further to the left are always less than numbers to the right. 3. For negatives: A negative number with a larger absolute value is smaller (-10 < -2)

Key words

  • Compare and Order Integers — Use a number line to decide which integers are greater or less and place them in order.
    Español: Comparar y ordenar enteros — Usar una recta numérica para decidir cuáles enteros son mayores o menores y colocarlos en orden.
  • Number line — A straight line where numbers are placed in order; numbers get smaller to the left and larger to the right.
    Español: Recta numérica — Una recta donde los números se colocan en orden; los números son menores a la izquierda y mayores a la derecha.
  • Compare — To see if a number is bigger, smaller, or equal to another.
    Español: Comparar — Ver si un número es mayor, menor o igual que otro.
  • Order — To arrange numbers by size, from least to greatest or greatest to least.
    Español: Ordenar — Acomodar números por tamaño, de menor a mayor o de mayor a menor.
  • Greater than — The > sign, showing one number is bigger.
    Español: Mayor que — El signo >, que muestra que un número es mayor.
  • Less than — The < sign, showing one number is smaller.
    Español: Menor que — El signo <, que muestra que un número es menor.
  • Negative integer — A whole number smaller than zero, like -5.
    Español: Entero negativo — Un número entero menor que cero, como -5.
  • Integer — Whole numbers and their opposites, like -2, -1, 0, 1, 2.
    Español: Número entero — Números enteros y sus opuestos, como -2, -1, 0, 1, 2.

Example

  1. I will compare -7 and -5. They are both negative, so I look at the number line.
  2. -7 is 7 steps left of zero. -5 is only 5 steps left of zero.
  3. -7 is farther to the left, so -7 is the smaller number.
  4. I write -7 < -5. The open side of the symbol points to the bigger number, -5.

Common mistake

A common mistake in Compare and Order Integers is thinking that a negative number with a bigger digit is the bigger value — for example, believing -15 is greater than -8 because 15 is greater than 8. It's the opposite for negatives: the number farther to the left on the number line (farther from zero) is always the LESSER value, so -15 < -8. Before you submit, check your comparison against the number line, not just the digits.
