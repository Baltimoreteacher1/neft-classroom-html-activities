      6.SP.2 Version A — Answer Key

      Shape of Data Distributions

      Answer Key

      Name: Date:
      1. 1

        A histogram of basketball players' ages shows: most players are 22–28, with a long tail of older players up to age 40. What is the shape of this distribution?

        1. ASkewed right
        2. BSymmetric
        3. CSkewed left
        4. DUniform

        Most data is on the left (younger ages) with a tail stretching right (older ages). This is skewed right.

      2. 2

        A data set is symmetric. What is likely true about the mean and median?

        1. AThey are approximately equal
        2. BThe mean is much higher than the median
        3. CThe median is much higher than the mean
        4. DThey are always exactly the same

        In a symmetric distribution, the mean and median are approximately equal because the data is balanced on both sides.

      3. 3

        Write the letter of the matching answer next to each.

        1. A
        2. A
        3. A
        4. A
        • A
        • B
        • C
        • D
      4. 4

        Which word describes the direction of the TAIL, not the peak?

        1. ASkew — the direction of the long tail tells you the skew direction
        2. BSkew — the direction of the peak tells you the skew direction
        3. CSkew — the direction the median points
        4. DSkew — the direction of the mode

        The skew direction is named after the TAIL. If the tail stretches to the right, it is skewed right, even though the peak is on the left.
