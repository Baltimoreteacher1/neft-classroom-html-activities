              1. 1DataPlayer salaries (millions): 1, 1, 2, 2, 2, 3, 3, 40.

The single 40-million salary is an extreme value in the right tail that pulls the mean up to about 6.75 million, far above what most players earn. The median is 2 million, which truly represents a typical salary. Use the median whenever data is skewed.
