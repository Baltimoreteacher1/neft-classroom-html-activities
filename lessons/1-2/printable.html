🎯 Learning Goals

THE BIG HOOK · WHY THIS MATTERS

Doing math is exploring: you make sense of a problem, plan a strategy, check your progress, and try new paths when you hit a dead end. Today's problems — walking distances and skyscraper heights — reward thinkers, not just calculators. Try this one: a new building will be 3/4 as tall as a tower that is 400 meters tall. Plan a strategy to find how tall the new building will be.

Content: I can make sense of a problem, plan a strategy, and use fractions of a whole number to compare quantities.

Language: I can explain my plan using the words we know, we don't know, and my strategy is ___ .

Key idea: Math Practices: Sense Making & Strategy Planning. 1. Identify known quantities and the unknown question. 2. Choose a strategy (draw a model, write an equation, or build a table). 3. Evaluate progress and pivot to an alternate approach if needed

Watch me estimate the peas

GIVEN DATA / MODEL
  • A bowl is full of peas — far too many to count one at a time. About how many peas are in the bowl?
  • I do not count every pea — I look for a group I CAN count, like one small cluster of about 10.
  • Then I ask how many of those clusters would cover the whole bowl. I count about 12.
  • 12 clusters of about 10 is 12 × 10 = 120, so my estimate is about 120 peas — a reasoned answer, close enough to be useful.
