🚀 Launch

Mission Control needs to organize 24 food packs and 36 water containers into equal groups for the crew pods. Each pod must get the same number of food packs AND the same number of water containers. What is the largest number of pods they can fill equally?

What is the greatest common factor (GCF)?

A factor is a number that divides another number evenly, with nothing left over. The greatest common factor is the biggest factor that two numbers share.

Key idea: The GCF is the largest number that divides BOTH numbers with no remainder.

Watch me

  • I want the GCF of 24 and 36. First I list the factors of 24: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24.
  • Next I list the factors of 36: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 9, 12, 18, 36.
  • Now I circle the factors in BOTH lists: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12. These are the common factors.
  • The biggest one is 12, so the GCF of 24 and 36 is 12. I check: 24 ÷ 12 = 2 and 36 ÷ 12 = 3. Both are even, so 12 works.

Let's try together

  • Let's find the GCF of 8 and 12. What are the factors of 8? (1, 2, 4, 8.)
  • What are the factors of 12? (1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12.) Which numbers are in both lists? (1, 2, 4.)
  • Which shared factor is the largest? (4.) So the GCF of 8 and 12 is 4.

Now it's your turn

  • Next, you will sort numbers to find the common factors of two numbers and choose the greatest one.
  • Remember: pick the LARGEST factor the two numbers share.
