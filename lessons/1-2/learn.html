What is the greatest common factor (GCF)?

A factor is a number that divides another number evenly, with nothing left over. The greatest common factor is the biggest factor that two numbers share.

📊 Common factors of 24 and 36
1234612
The greatest is 12 → GCF of 24 and 36

👀 Example 1 — Watch me solve it

Read each step. This is the teacher thinking out loud.

  1. 1I want the GCF of 24 and 36. First I list the factors of 24: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24.
  2. 2Next I list the factors of 36: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 9, 12, 18, 36.
  3. 3Now I circle the factors in BOTH lists: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12. These are the common factors.
  4. 4The biggest one is 12, so the GCF of 24 and 36 is 12. I check: 24 ÷ 12 = 2 and 36 ÷ 12 = 3. Both are even, so 12 works.

📝 Notes to remember

💡 Key ideaThe GCF is the largest number that divides BOTH numbers with no remainder.

🔑 Words to use

  • FactorA number that divides another number with nothing left over.
  • Greatest Common FactorThe biggest number that divides two or more numbers evenly.
  • Common factorA number that divides two or more numbers evenly.
  • DivisibleA number you can divide evenly, with nothing left over.

🤝 Example 2 — Work with me

We solve this one together. Fill in each step as we go.

  1. 1 Let's find the GCF of 8 and 12. What are the factors of 8? (1, 2, 4, 8.)
  2. 2 What are the factors of 12? (1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12.) Which numbers are in both lists? (1, 2, 4.)
  3. 3 Which shared factor is the largest? (4.) So the GCF of 8 and 12 is 4.
