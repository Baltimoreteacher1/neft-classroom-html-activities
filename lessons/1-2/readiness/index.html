Words to Know First

These are words you'll need to already know to follow this lesson — not the new words the lesson teaches. Review each one, its meaning, and the example so the new lesson makes sense from the start.

✖️

multiply

Level 1 To add the same number many times to find a total.

Example: 6 × 7 means add 6 seven times to get 42.

🧩

factor pair

Level 1 Two numbers you multiply together to make another number.

Example: 3 and 4 are a factor pair of 12 because 3 × 4 = 12.

➗

divide evenly

Level 1 To split a number into equal groups with nothing left over.

Example: 4 divides 12 evenly because 12 ÷ 4 = 3.

📝

list

Level 1 To write numbers out in order, one after another.

Example: I can list the parts of 8: 1, 2, 4, 8.
