Lesson 5.9Area of Regular Polygons

Version A · Answer KeySupported practice

Learning target I can find the area of a regular polygon by decomposing it into triangles.

Start here pageNow you try — model solution

  1. First find one triangle: ½ × 4 × 3. What is 4 × 3? Yes, 12, and half of 12 is 6 sq cm.
  2. A pentagon has 5 sides, so how many triangles? Yes, 5.
  3. Multiply: 5 × 6 = 30, so the total area is 30 square centimeters.
Part 1Understand the ideamodels and meaning
  1. 1Write the name of the correct group on each line.

    Each regular polygon below is cut into congruent triangles by drawing a line from the center to every corner. Sort by how many triangles it makes.

    Groups: More than 5 triangles 5 or fewer triangles
    • More than 5 trianglesRegular hexagon
    • More than 5 trianglesRegular octagon
    • 5 or fewer trianglesEquilateral triangle
    • 5 or fewer trianglesSquare
    • 5 or fewer trianglesRegular pentagon

    Hint Picture it: one spoke from the center to each corner. Each triangle sits on one side.

    Why: A regular polygon splits into one triangle per side, so the triangle count is just the number of sides. The hexagon (6 sides) and the octagon (8 sides) are the only ones above 5; the equilateral triangle (3), the square (4), and the pentagon (exactly 5) all belong in the '5 or fewer' group.

Part 2Apply itreal-world problems
  1. 2Circle the letter of the best answer. Show how you know.

    A regular hexagon is divided into 6 equal triangles from the center. Each triangle has a base of 6 ft and a height of 5.2 ft. What is the area of one triangle? Use the given heights; nonsquare heights are rounded, so their areas are estimates.

    1. A11.2 sq ft
    2. B15.6 sq ft
    3. C15.6 ft
    4. D31.2 sq ft

    Hint Focus on just ONE of the 6 triangles: its base is 6 ft and its height is 5.2 ft.

    Answer: B — 15.6 sq ft

    Why: A = ½ × b × h = ½ × 6 × 5.2 = 15.6 square feet.

    Watch for: A common mistake in Area of Regular Polygons is stopping after finding the area of ONE triangle and reporting that as the total area — or adding the number of triangles instead of multiplying. Remember: total area = (one triangle's area) × (number of sides), not (one triangle's area) + (number of sides).

    Spot the Common Mistake (use rounded heights)

    1. 1Decompose the octagonA regular octagon = 8 congruent triangles
    2. 2Identify one trianglebase = 3 ft, height = 4 ft
    3. 3Find the area of one triangleA = ½ × 3 × 4 = 6 sq ft
    4. 4Find total areaTotal area = 5 × 6 = 30 sq ft

    Which step has the mistake? Explain what went wrong, then show the correct work.

    Hint Look at Step 4. How many sides does an octagon actually have?

    Mistake: Step 4. The area of one triangle, 6 sq ft, is right, but the final step multiplied by 5 when an octagon decomposes into 8 triangles. Multiplying by the true number of sides gives Total = 8 × 6 = 48 sq ft.

    Correct work: An octagon has 8 sides, not 5. Total area = 8 × 6 = 48 sq ft — multiply one triangle's area by the actual number of sides (triangles), not a random number.

    Spot the Common Mistake (use rounded heights)

    1. 1Decompose the pentagonA regular pentagon = 5 congruent triangles
    2. 2Identify one trianglebase = 6 ft, height = 4 ft

    Mistake: Step 3. In the step that finds one triangle's area, the student used 6 × 4 = 24 and left out the ½. A triangle is half of the rectangle with the same base and height, so A = ½ × 6 × 4 = 12 sq ft and the pentagon's total is 5 × 12 = 60 sq ft.

Part 2Explain and correctreasoning
  1. 2Find the mistake. Explain it, then show the correct work.

    Find the Area Error (use rounded heights)

    1. 1Decompose the hexagonA regular hexagon = 6 triangles
    2. 2Find area of one triangleb = 5 m, h = 4.3 m
    3. 3Calculate one triangleA = ½ × 5 × 4.3 = 10.75 sq m
    4. 4Find total areaTotal = 10.75 + 6 = 16.75 sq m

    Which step has the mistake? Explain what went wrong, then show the correct work.

    Mistake: Step 4. One triangle at 10.75 sq m is correct, but the last step added the 6 instead of using it as a count. Six congruent triangles means six copies of that area, so Total = 10.75 × 6 = 64.5 sq m.

    Correct work: To find total area, multiply (not add): Total = 10.75 × 6 = 64.5 sq m. Six equal triangles means 6 times one triangle's area.

  2. 3Answer in complete sentences.

    A regular hexagon and a regular pentagon are each divided into congruent central triangles. In both polygons, each central triangle has area 15 sq ft. Which polygon has greater total area? Explain why the triangle count matters. The two polygons have different side lengths.

    Look for: hexagon, pentagon, triangles, multiply, sides, decompose, area

    Why: The hexagon has area 6 × 15 = 90 sq ft. The pentagon has area 5 × 15 = 75 sq ft. The hexagon has 15 sq ft more because it has one additional triangle of that area. Equal triangle areas do not require equal bases and heights.
