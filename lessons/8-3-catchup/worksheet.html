✏️ Worked Example

  1. Lesson 8.1 — Understand Equations and Their Solutions: An equation says two amounts are equal. A value is a SOLUTION when substituting it makes that statement true — so any guess can be checked, not just solved for.
  2. Lesson 8.2 — Write and Solve Equations Using Addition or Subtraction: Undo the operation with its inverse, and do it to both sides to keep the equation balanced.
  3. Lesson 8.3 — Write and Solve Equations Using Multiplication or Division: Divide to undo multiplying, and multiply to undo dividing — always on both sides.
