Lesson 6-4: Write and Evaluate Numerical Expressions with Exponents

Question 1Two-part question

Part A — correct answer: C

Evaluate the numerical expression 3 × 4² − 8.

  • A16
  • B24
  • C40
  • D136

Following the order of operations, evaluate the power first: 4² = 16. Then multiply: 3 × 16 = 48. Then subtract: 48 − 8 = 40.

  • A: Only the power was evaluated. Continue: multiply by 3, then subtract 8.
  • B: The exponent was treated as a multiplier, 4 times 2. Four squared is 16.
  • D: The multiplication happened before the power. Powers come first: evaluate 4 squared, then multiply.

Part B — correct answer: B

Which reasoning shows why your answer to Part A is correct?

  • AMultiply 3 × 4 first, then square the product: 12² = 144, and 144 − 8 = 136.
  • BEvaluate the power first: 4² = 16. Then multiply: 3 × 16 = 48. Then subtract: 48 − 8 = 40.
  • CRead 4² as 4 × 2 = 8, then multiply 3 × 8 = 24 and subtract: 24 − 8 = 16.
  • DSubtract first because 16 and 8 are next to each other: 16 − 8 = 8, then 3 × 8 = 24.

The order of operations puts exponents before multiplication, and multiplication before subtraction. Each other path breaks that order.

Question 2Written response · 2 points

Type II Reasoning: Subtracting Too Soon

Devon evaluated 20 − 2 × 3². His work was: 3² = 9, then 20 − 2 = 18, then 18 × 9 = 162. He wrote that the value of the expression is 162.

Explain Devon's misconception and give the correct value of the expression.

Model answer: Devon evaluated the power correctly but then subtracted before multiplying. In PEMDAS, multiplication comes before subtraction: 3² = 9, then 2 × 9 = 18, then 20 − 18 = 2. The value is 2.

2 pointsStudent explains that the order of operations requires multiplying 2 × 9 before subtracting from 20, and gives the value 2.
1 pointStudent gives the value 2 but does not explain that multiplication comes before subtraction.
0 pointsStudent agrees with Devon that the value is 162 or gives an unrelated response.
