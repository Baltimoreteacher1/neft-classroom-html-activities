How it worksWorked exampleevaluating with grouping and a power

These numbers are not on your problems. The steps are. Follow them with your own numbers.

Find the value of 4 + 3 × (9 − 7)^2.

  1. Grouping first. (9 − 7) = 2, so now 4 + 3 × 2^2
  2. Powers next. 2^2 = 4, so now 4 + 3 × 4
  3. Multiply and divide, left to right. 3 × 4 = 12, so now 4 + 12
  4. Add and subtract, left to right. 4 + 12 = 16

Answer: The value of the expression is 16.

Watch outA common mistake

A common mistake with powers is multiplying the base by the exponent: reading 3² as 3 × 2 = 6 instead of 3 × 3 = 9. A second is evaluating strictly left to right, so 3 + 4 × 2 becomes 14 instead of 11. The order of operations exists so one expression has exactly one value: grouping symbols, then powers, then × and ÷ left to right, then + and − left to right.
