Understand Statistical Questions

6.DS.1 · Unit 2 · Lesson 1

Name   Period

Objectives / Objetivos

Content Objective: I can tell the difference between a statistical question and a non-statistical question.

Language Objective: I can explain my reasoning using the words statistical question, data, variability, and survey.

Key Idea / Idea clave

Statistical Questions & Variability. 1. Statistical Question: Expects answers that vary across people, trials, or items. 2. Non-Statistical Question: Has one single fixed answer. 3. Data Collection: Gather varied data points to create a distribution

I Notice / Observo

  • Which questions could have many different answers?
  • Which questions have only one exact answer?

I Wonder / Me pregunto

  • Why would a coach care about questions with variability?

Vocabulary / Vocabulario

Term / TérminoDefinition / Definición
Statistical Questions and Data
Preguntas estadísticas y datos		A statistical question expects varied answers that can be collected as data.
Una pregunta estadística espera respuestas variadas que se pueden recopilar como datos.
Statistical Question
Pregunta estadística		A question answered with data that are expected to vary.
Una pregunta que se responde con datos que se espera que varíen.
Non-statistical Question
Pregunta no estadística		A question that does not anticipate variability in the data used to answer it. It may ask for one fact or measurement.
Una pregunta que no anticipa variabilidad en los datos usados para responderla. Puede pedir un solo dato o una medición.
Data
Datos		Facts and numbers you collect, like answers to a survey.
Datos y números que recoges, como respuestas a una encuesta.
Variability
Variabilidad		How spread out the numbers are.
Qué tan separados están los números.
Survey
Encuesta		Collecting facts by asking people questions.
Recoger datos haciendo preguntas a las personas.
