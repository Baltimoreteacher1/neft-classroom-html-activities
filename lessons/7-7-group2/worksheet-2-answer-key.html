Lesson 7.77.7 Small Group · Group 2

Group 2 · Set B · Answer KeySecond challenge form · new problems, same idea

Learning target I can draw polygons from their vertex coordinates on the coordinate plane, and use coordinates to find side lengths and solve perimeter and area problems, explain what each part stands for, and build one for a situation I have not seen before.

  1. 1Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    A square has vertices (−1, 2), (3, 2), (3, −2) and (−1, −2). Each grid unit is 10 feet. What is the perimeter in feet?

    Final answer:160 feet
  2. 2Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    Three vertices of a rectangle are (−4, 2), (4, 2) and (4, −3). What is the area of the rectangle?

    Final answer:40 square units

    Why: Find the side across: 4 + 4 = 8 Find the side up and down: 2 + 3 = 5 Multiply for the area: 8 × 5 = 40

  3. 3Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    A school draws its playground on a grid. The corners are (−20, 10), (25, 10), (25, −10) and (−20, −10). Each unit is 1 meter. How many meters of fence go all the way around?

    Final answer:130 meters

    Why: Find the two side lengths: 20 + 25 = 45; 10 + 10 = 20 Add all four sides: 2 × 45 + 2 × 20 = 90 + 40 = 130

  4. 4Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    A polygon has vertices (−4, 3), (2, 3), (2, 0), (5, 0), (5, −2) and (−4, −2). Find its area.

    Final answer:36 square units

    Why: Find the top rectangle: 6 × 3 = 18 Find the bottom rectangle: 9 × 2 = 18 Add the two areas: 18 + 18 = 36
