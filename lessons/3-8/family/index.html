Unit 3, Lesson 8: Solve Problems with Unit Rates

Standard 6.AT.2

What students are learning Qué aprenden

Key idea: A unit rate is the cost for just 1 item. Find it by dividing the total cost by the number of items. Compare unit rates to find the best deal, and multiply the unit rate by how many you want to find the total cost.
Idea clave: Una tasa unitaria es el costo de solo 1 artículo. Hállala dividiendo el costo total entre el número de artículos. Compara las tasas unitarias para hallar la mejor oferta, y multiplica la tasa unitaria por cuántos quieres para hallar el costo total.

How we did it in class today Cómo lo hicimos hoy en clase

  1. Fun Supply Co. sells 500 tokens for $60. I want the cost for 1 token.
  2. I divide the price by the tokens: $60 ÷ 500 = $0.12.
  3. So this deal is $0.12 per token. That is the unit rate.
  4. Now I can compare it fairly to a deal with a different number of tokens.

If they get stuck... Si se traban...

Watch out for this: A common mistake in Solve Problems with Unit Rates is comparing the total prices instead of the cost per item — the deal with the smaller total can still cost more per unit. Always reduce each option to its unit rate before you decide, then pick the lower one.
