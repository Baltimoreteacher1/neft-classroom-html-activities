A quick guide for families and for any student who missed class.

What students are learning

A unit rate is the cost for just 1 item, like the price per token. When packages have different sizes, the unit rate lets you compare them fairly.

Key idea: Solving Problems with Unit Rates. Formula: Unit Price = Cost ÷ Quantity | Total Cost = Unit Price × Quantity. 1. Find the unit rate by dividing total cost by quantity. 2. Compare unit rates to determine the best deal. 3. Multiply unit rate by desired quantity to calculate total cost

Key words

  • Unit Rate Problem Solving — Find an amount per 1 unit and use it to compare choices or solve a problem.
    Español: Resolución de problemas con tasas unitarias — Hallar una cantidad por 1 unidad y usarla para comparar opciones o resolver un problema.
  • Unit Rate — A rate for just 1 of something, like cost for 1 item.
    Español: Tasa unitaria — Una tasa para solo 1 de algo, como el precio de 1 artículo.
  • Better Buy — The choice that costs less for each item.
    Español: Mejor compra — La opción que cuesta menos por cada artículo.
  • Comparison — Looking at two or more amounts to see how they are related.
    Español: Comparación — Mirar dos o más cantidades para ver cómo se relacionan.
  • Per Unit — For each single item.
    Español: Por unidad — Por cada artículo.
  • Rate — A ratio comparing two amounts with different units, like miles per hour.
    Español: Tasa — Una razón que compara dos cantidades con unidades distintas, como millas por hora.
  • Proportion — A math sentence saying two ratios are equal. It helps find a missing number.
    Español: Proporción — Una oración matemática que dice que dos razones son iguales. Ayuda a hallar un número que falta.

Example

  1. Fun Supply Co. sells 500 tokens for $60. I want the cost for 1 token.
  2. I divide the price by the tokens: $60 ÷ 500 = $0.12.
  3. So this deal is $0.12 per token. That is the unit rate.
  4. Now I can compare it fairly to a deal with a different number of tokens.

Practice at home

  1. A car travels 195 miles on 6 gallons of gas. What is the unit rate in miles per gallon?

Common mistake

A common mistake in Solve Problems with Unit Rates is comparing the total prices instead of the cost per item — the deal with the smaller total can still cost more per unit. Always reduce each option to its unit rate before you decide, then pick the lower one.
