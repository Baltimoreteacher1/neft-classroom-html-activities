Lesson 9.1Explore Relationships Between Two Variables

Learning target I can recognize when two quantities change together, and identify which one is the independent variable and which is the dependent variable.

  1. 1Circle the letter of the best answer. Show how you know.

    A ticket costs $8. Which equation represents "8 tickets cost $56"?

    1. A8t = 56
    2. Bt + 8 = 56
    3. Ct − 8 = 56
    4. Dt ÷ 8 = 56

    Answer: A — 8t = 56

    Why: Each of the 8 tickets costs t, so 8 × t = 56. The total is built by multiplying, not by adding.

    Watch for: Naming the variables backward — calling the responding quantity 'independent' because it is the one the question asks about.

  2. 2Circle the letter of the best answer. Show how you know.

    Solve: 4x = 32

    1. Ax = 8
    2. Bx = 28
    3. Cx = 36
    4. Dx = 128

    Answer: A — x = 8

    Why: x is multiplied by 4, so divide both sides by 4: 32 ÷ 4 = 8.

    Watch for: Naming the variables backward — calling the responding quantity 'independent' because it is the one the question asks about.

  3. 3Circle the letter of the best answer. Show how you know.

    Maya needs at least 20 points to win a prize. Which inequality shows this?

    1. Ap ≥ 20
    2. Bp > 20
    3. Cp ≤ 20
    4. Dp < 20

    Answer: A — p ≥ 20

    Why: "At least 20" includes 20 itself, so the symbol must allow equal to: p ≥ 20.

    Watch for: Naming the variables backward — calling the responding quantity 'independent' because it is the one the question asks about.

  4. 4Circle the letter of the best answer. Show how you know.

    Solve: x + 7 = 19

    1. Ax = 7
    2. Bx = 12
    3. Cx = 19
    4. Dx = 26

    Answer: B — x = 12

    Why: 7 is added to x, so subtract 7 from both sides: 19 − 7 = 12.

    Watch for: Naming the variables backward — calling the responding quantity 'independent' because it is the one the question asks about.

  1. 1Circle the letter of the best answer. Show how you know.

    At a local pizza shop a customer can order up to three toppings on a large pizza. The shop has 6 employees. The shop spends $1.15 on cheese and $1.95 on sauce for each pizza it makes. The number of pizzas made each week determines other costs at the shop. Which variable is the independent variable in this relationship?

    1. Athe cost of cheese used
    2. Bthe number of pizzas made
    3. Cthe number of employees
    4. Dthe number of toppings allowed

    Answer: B — the number of pizzas made

    Why: The number of pizzas is independent because it is the value that determines the other costs. Cost of cheese is dependent because it changes as a result of pizzas made; the number of employees and toppings allowed are fixed limits, not quantities that change with pizza count.

    Watch for: Naming the variables backward — calling the responding quantity 'independent' because it is the one the question asks about.

  2. 2Circle the letter of the best answer. Show how you know.

    At a local pizza shop a customer can order up to three toppings on a large pizza. The shop has 6 employees. The shop spends $1.15 on cheese and $1.95 on sauce for each pizza it makes. Which two quantities have a dependent relationship in this scenario?

    1. Anumber of employees and number of pizzas made
    2. Bcost of cheese and number of pizzas made
    3. Cnumber of toppings allowed and cost of sauce
    4. Dnumber of employees and number of toppings allowed

    Answer: B — cost of cheese and number of pizzas made

    Why: The cost of cheese rises as more pizzas are made ($1.15 per pizza), so it depends on the number of pizzas. The number of employees (6) and the topping limit (3) are fixed values that stay the same no matter how many pizzas are made, so they are not part of a dependent relationship here.

    Watch for: Naming the variables backward — calling the responding quantity 'independent' because it is the one the question asks about.

  3. 3Circle the letter of the best answer. Show how you know.

    At a local pizza shop a customer can order up to three toppings on a large pizza. The shop has 6 employees. The shop spends $1.15 on cheese and $1.95 on sauce for each pizza it makes. The shop makes 20 pizzas in a week. Which of these does NOT depend on the number of pizzas sold?

    1. Athe total cost of cheese
    2. Bthe number of employees
    3. Cthe total cost of sauce
    4. Dthe total spent on cheese and sauce combined

    Answer: B — the number of employees

    Why: Staff count (6 employees) is fixed and stays the same regardless of pizza volume. Cheese cost, sauce cost, and their combined total all scale up as more pizzas are made, so those three DO depend on the number of pizzas.

    Watch for: Naming the variables backward — calling the responding quantity 'independent' because it is the one the question asks about.

  4. 4Circle the letter of the best answer. Show how you know.

    A dog walker charges by the hour. Which sentence is correct?

    1. AThe number of hours depends on the total charge, so the charge is the independent variable.
    2. BBoth quantities are independent variables.
    3. CThe total charge depends on the number of hours, so hours is the independent variable.
    4. DNeither quantity depends on the other.

    Answer: C — The total charge depends on the number of hours, so hours is the independent variable.

    Why: The walker chooses how many hours to work; the money that results responds to that choice. Hours is subject to choice, so hours is independent and the total charge is dependent.

    Watch for: Naming the variables backward — calling the responding quantity 'independent' because it is the one the question asks about.
