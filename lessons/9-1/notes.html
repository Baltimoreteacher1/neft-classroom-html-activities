  4. (0, 3)

Answer Key & Teacher Guide

  1. Notes 1: Coordinate plane
  2. Notes 2: Ordered pair
  3. Notes 3: Origin
  4. Notes 4: Quadrant
  5. Notes 5: Reflection
  6. Notes 6: Integer
  7. We Try (notes): A. (0, 0) — The origin is where the x-axis and y-axis cross, at (0, 0).
  8. You Try (notes): A. Move 6 units to the right — The first number (x-coordinate) tells you horizontal movement. 6 means go right 6 units from the origin.
  9. Try It 1: A. Move 4 units to the right along the x-axis — The first number in (x, y) is the x-coordinate, so 4 means move 4 units to the right along the x-axis before moving up.
  10. Try It 2: A. (3, 8) — Across (3) is the x-coordinate and up (8) is the y-coordinate, so the Coconut Grove is at (3, 8).
  11. Exit Ticket: A. (6, 3) — Right 6 = x-coordinate of 6, Up 3 = y-coordinate of 3. The ordered pair is (6, 3).
