Warm-up

Teaching notes

Key idea: Independent & Dependent Variables. 1. Independent Variable (x): The input / cause that is chosen or changes freely. 2. Dependent Variable (y): The output / effect that depends on the independent variable. 3. Sentence check: 'The (dependent) depends on the (independent)'

I do:

  1. The two quantities are hours of sunlight per day and average plant height after 3 weeks.
  2. Akela CHOSE where to put each tray, so she chose the sunlight. Sunlight is subject to choice — that makes it the independent variable.
  3. She did not choose how tall the plants got. Height responded. That makes height the dependent variable.
  4. Say it as a sentence: plant height depends on sunlight.

You do: For each pair below, decide which quantity is subject to choice. Then finish the sentence: ___ depends on ___ .

Common misconception

Naming the variables backward — calling the responding quantity 'independent' because it is the one the question asks about.

Language & learning supports

  • Pre-teach: independent variable, dependent variable, depends on, quantity, relationship (Spanish + visuals are in the lesson vocabulary).
  • Offer the sentence frames: “I know ___ because ___.” “First, I ___. Then, I ___.” “The answer is ___, so ___.”
  • Chunk the I-do steps; let students restate each step to a partner before moving on.
  • Provide the worked example as a reference while they practice.

Exit ticket

A dog walker charges by the hour. Which sentence is correct?

  • The total charge depends on the number of hours, so hours is the independent variable. ✓ correct
  • The number of hours depends on the total charge, so the charge is the independent variable.
  • Both quantities are independent variables.
  • Neither quantity depends on the other.

The walker chooses how many hours to work; the money that results responds to that choice. Hours is subject to choice, so hours is independent and the total charge is dependent.

Reteach

Re-run the I-do example with smaller numbers and have students narrate each step using the key idea.
