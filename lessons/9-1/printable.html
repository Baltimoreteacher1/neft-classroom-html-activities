📚 Vocabulary

WordWhat it meansExample
independent variable
variable independiente		The quantity that is subject to choice. It does not depend on the other quantity.
La cantidad que se elige. No depende de la otra cantidad.		The number of tickets you decide to buy — You choose it; nothing in the problem forces the value.
dependent variable
variable dependiente		The quantity that changes in response to the independent variable.
La cantidad que cambia en respuesta a la variable independiente.		Calories burned during a jog of the length you chose — It moves because the minutes moved.
depends on
depende de		One quantity depends on another when changing the first causes the second to change.
Una cantidad depende de otra cuando cambiar la primera hace cambiar la segunda.		Pages printed depends on minutes running — The printer's output responds to the time you let it run.
quantity
cantidad		Something that can be counted or measured.
Algo que se puede contar o medir.		6 hours of canoe rental — A number together with what it measures.
relationship
relación		How two quantities change together.
Cómo dos cantidades cambian juntas.		Tickets bought and total cost — Change one and the other changes with it.

🚀 Launch

Akela planted three trays of bean seeds for a science experiment. Each tray sat in a different spot, so each got a different amount of sunlight per day. After three weeks she measured the average plant height in every tray. Two quantities in that experiment are related: change the sunlight and the height changes with it. One of them Akela chose, and the other one answered back. Today you will name which quantity is the independent one and which depends on it, and you will find pairs like this in a jogging tracker and a pizza shop.

Which quantity is doing the changing — and which is responding?

When two quantities are related, one of them depends on the other. Naming which is which is the whole job of this lesson.

Key idea: Independent & Dependent Variables. 1. Independent Variable (x): The input / cause that is chosen or changes freely. 2. Dependent Variable (y): The output / effect that depends on the independent variable. 3. Sentence check: 'The (dependent) depends on the (independent)'

Watch me sort Akela's experiment

  • The two quantities are hours of sunlight per day and average plant height after 3 weeks.
  • Akela CHOSE where to put each tray, so she chose the sunlight. Sunlight is subject to choice — that makes it the independent variable.
  • She did not choose how tall the plants got. Height responded. That makes height the dependent variable.
  • Say it as a sentence: plant height depends on sunlight.

Let's try Claire's fitness tracker together

  • Claire burned 123 Calories on a 30-minute jog and 246 Calories on a 60-minute jog.
  • Which did Claire choose — the minutes, or the Calories?
  • She chose how long to jog. So time is independent, and Calories burned is dependent.
  • Sentence frame: ___ depends on ___ .
