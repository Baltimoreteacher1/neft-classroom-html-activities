Question 7Two-part question

Part A

What is the value of 2 1/4 ÷ 3/8?

Fill in the bubble next to the one best answer.

  • A1/6
  • B2 2/3
  • C6
  • D27/32

Part B

Answer Part A before Part B — Part B asks about your reasoning.

Which reasoning shows why your answer to Part A is correct?

  • ADivide only the fraction parts, 1/4 ÷ 3/8 = 2/3, then put the whole number 2 back in front to get 2 2/3.
  • BChange 2 1/4 to 9/4. Keep 9/4, change ÷ to ×, flip 3/8 to 8/3: 9/4 × 8/3 = 72/12 = 6.
  • CChange 2 1/4 to 9/4, then multiply straight across without flipping: 9/4 × 3/8 = 27/32.
  • DChange 2 1/4 to 9/4, then flip the first fraction instead of the second: 4/9 × 3/8 = 1/6.
