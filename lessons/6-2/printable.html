Divide Fractions
Dividir fracciones		Multiply the first fraction by the reciprocal of the second fraction.
Multiplicar la primera fracción por el recíproco de la segunda fracción.		3/4 ÷ 1/8 = 3/4 × 8/1 — Only the divisor flipped.
Dividend
Dividendo		The total number being divided into equal groups (the number inside the division bracket).
El número total que se divide en partes iguales (el número dentro de la casilla de división).		The 3/4 in 3/4 ÷ 1/8 — The total being split.
Divisor
Divisor		The number of equal groups you are dividing into (the number outside the division bracket).
El número de partes iguales en las que divides (el número fuera de la casilla de división).		The 1/8 in 3/4 ÷ 1/8 — The size of each group.
Reciprocal
Recíproco		A fraction turned upside down.
Una fracción volteada de arriba abajo.		4/3 for 3/4 — The fraction turned upside down.
Quotient
Cociente		The answer when you divide.
La respuesta cuando divides.		The 6 in 3/4 ÷ 1/8 = 6 — The result of dividing.
Simplify
Simplificar		To write a fraction with smaller numbers that names the same amount, like 2/4 = 1/2.
Escribir una fracción con números más pequeños que nombra la misma cantidad, como 2/4 = 1/2.		2/4 written as 1/2 — Same value, smaller parts.
Mixed number
Número mixto		A whole number and a fraction written together, like 2 1/2. It must be rewritten as an improper fraction before dividing.
Un número entero y una fracción escritos juntos, como 2 1/2. Debe reescribirse como fracción impropia antes de dividir.		2 1/2 — A whole part and a fraction part.
Improper fraction
Fracción impropia		A fraction whose numerator is greater than or equal to its denominator, like 5/2.
Una fracción cuyo numerador es mayor o igual que su denominador, como 5/2.		5/2 — The top is larger than the bottom.

🚀 Launch

Agent Rivera is dividing a 3/4-pound evidence bag into portions that each weigh 1/8 pound. She needs to know exactly how many portions she can make for testing. Can you solve the case?

How do I divide one fraction by another fraction?

When you divide a fraction by a fraction, you keep the first fraction, change ÷ to ×, and flip the second fraction (the divisor) to its reciprocal. Then multiply and simplify.

Key idea: Dividing Fractions by Fractions. Formula: a/b ÷ c/d = a/b × d/c. 1. Name the question: 3/4 ÷ 1/8 asks how many 1/8s fit in 3/4. 2. Use common denominators: 3/4 = 6/8, and 6 eighths ÷ 1 eighth = 6. 3. Shortcut: multiply by the reciprocal of the divisor, 3/4 × 8/1 = 24/4 = 6, then check by multiplying: 6 × 1/8 = 6/8 = 3/4

Watch me

GIVEN DATA / MODEL
  • Agent Rivera has 3/4 pound and each portion is 1/8 pound. I want 3/4 ÷ 1/8.
  • It asks how many 1/8-pound portions fit in 3/4 pound. I rename 3/4 in eighths: 3/4 = 6/8.
  • 6 eighths ÷ 1 eighth = 6, the same way 6 apples ÷ 1 apple = 6. That is the common-denominator method.
  • The reciprocal shortcut gives the same answer: 3/4 × 8/1 = 24/4 = 6, because each pound holds 8 eighths. Check: 6 × 1/8 = 6/8 = 3/4. She can make 6 portions.
  • One more move to know: mixed number → improper fraction: multiply the bottom by the whole number, add the top, keep the same bottom. 3 1/2 → (2 × 3) + 1 = 7 → 7/2.

Let's try together

GIVEN DATA / MODEL
  • Let's try 1/2 ÷ 1/4. How many 1/4s fit in 1/2? Rename 1/2 as 2/4.
  • 2 fourths ÷ 1 fourth = 2. The reciprocal shortcut agrees: 1/2 × 4/1 = 4/2 = 2.
  • Check by multiplying: 2 × 1/4 = 2/4 = 1/2. So 1/2 ÷ 1/4 = 2.

Your turn — a mixed number this time

GIVEN DATA / MODEL
  • Try 2 1/2 ÷ 1/4. The first number is a mixed number, so it cannot be flipped or multiplied as it stands.
  • Rewrite it as an improper fraction: 2 1/2 = 5/2, because two wholes are 4 halves plus 1 more half.
  • Now multiply by the reciprocal of the divisor 1/4: 5/2 × 4/1 = 20/2.
  • Simplify: 20/2 = 10. So 2 1/2 ÷ 1/4 = 10. Check by multiplying: 10 × 1/4 = 10/4 = 2 1/2 — ten quarter-pieces really do fill two and a half wholes.
