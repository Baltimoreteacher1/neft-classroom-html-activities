    A ribbon is 4/5 of a yard long. Each bow uses 1/10 of a yard. Which equation finds how many bows can be made?

    1. A4/5 ÷ 1/10
    2. B1/10 ÷ 4/5
    3. C4/5 × 1/10
    4. D4/5 + 1/10

    The total (4/5 yard) is divided into groups of 1/10 yard each. The equation is 4/5 ÷ 1/10 = 4/5 × 10/1 = 40/5 = 8 bows.

    Watch for: A common mistake in Fraction Division Problem Solving is reversing which fraction is the dividend and which is the divisor when setting up the equation from a word problem. For example: a pitcher holds 3/4 cup of punch, and each serving is 1/8 cup — how many servings? The correct setup is total ÷ group size = 3/4 ÷ 1/8 = 3/4 × 8/1 = 24/4 = 6 servings. A student who reverses the setup calculates 1/8 ÷ 3/4 = 1/8 × 4/3 = 4/24 = 1/6, an answer smaller than 1 that can't possibly be a serving count.     Spot the Common Mistake

    1. 1ProblemA detective has 4/5 gallon of cleaning fluid. Each spray bottle holds 1/5 gallon. How many bottles can she fill?
    2. 2Student's equation1/5 ÷ 4/5
    3. 3Solve1/5 × 5/4 = 5/20 = 1/4
    4. 4Answer1/4 of a bottle

    Which step has the mistake? Explain it and write the correct work.

    The mistake is in Step 2. Correct work: The total amount (4/5 gallon) must be the dividend and each bottle (1/5 gallon) the divisor, so the equation is 4/5 ÷ 1/5, not 1/5 ÷ 4/5. Solve: 4/5 × 5/1 = 20/5 = 4 bottles. In a division story the total comes first and the size of one group comes second. The 4/5 gallon is the total and 1/5 gallon is one bottle, so the equation is 4/5 ÷ 1/5, not 1/5 ÷ 4/5. Solving gives 4/5 × 5/1 = 20/5 = 4 bottles, which fits, because 4/5 is exactly four times 1/5.
