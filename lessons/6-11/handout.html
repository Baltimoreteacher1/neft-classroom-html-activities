Fraction Division Problem Solving

6.NOS.1 · Unit 6 · Lesson 11

Name   Period

Objectives / Objetivos

Content Objective: I can solve real-world problems by writing and solving fraction division equations.

Language Objective: I can explain my reasoning using the words model, equation, solution, and reasonableness.

Key Idea / Idea clave

Fraction Division Word Problems. Formula: Total Amount ÷ Group Size = Number of Groups. 1. Identify the Dividend (total amount being split or shared). 2. Identify the Divisor (size of each group or part). 3. Divide: Total ÷ Size = Number of Groups (or Total ÷ Groups = Size per Group)

I Notice / Observo

  • What is the total amount of fingerprint dust?
  • How much does each test use?

I Wonder / Me pregunto

  • How do you decide which number is the dividend and which is the divisor?
