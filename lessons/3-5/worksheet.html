  1. Read the problem: Compare 3:5 and 4:7. Which ratio is greater?
  2. Find common denominator: LCD of 5 and 7 is 35
  3. Scale the ratios: 3:5 = 21:35 and 4:7 = 28:35
  4. Conclusion: Since 21 < 28, the ratio 3:5 is greater.

6.RP.3 Version A — Answer Key

Compare Ratios

Answer Key

Name: Date:
  1. 1

    Chef A uses 2 cups of cheese for every 8 crackers. Chef B uses 3 cups of cheese for every 9 crackers. Who uses more cheese per cracker?

    1. AChef B
    2. BChef A
    3. CThey use the same amount
    4. DCannot determine

    Chef A: 2÷8 = 0.25 cups per cracker. Chef B: 3÷9 ≈ 0.33 cups per cracker. Chef B uses more cheese per cracker.

  2. 2

    Which ratio is greater: 3:4 or 5:8?

    1. A3:4
    2. B5:8
    3. CThey are equal
    4. DCannot determine

    Convert to the same denominator: 3:4 = 6:8. Since 6:8 > 5:8, the ratio 3:4 is greater.

  3. 3

    Which lemonade recipe is more lemony? Recipe A: 2 lemons for 6 cups of water. Recipe B: 3 lemons for 7 cups of water.

    1. ARecipe B
    2. BRecipe A
    3. CThey are the same
    4. DCannot determine

    Recipe A: 2÷6 ≈ 0.33 lemons per cup. Recipe B: 3÷7 ≈ 0.43 lemons per cup. Since 0.43 > 0.33, Recipe B is more lemony.

  4. 4

    Which is the better deal: 4 apples for $3 or 6 apples for $5?

    1. A4 apples for $3
    2. B6 apples for $5
    3. CThey cost the same
    4. DCannot determine

    Deal 1: $3÷4 = $0.75 per apple. Deal 2: $5÷6 ≈ $0.83 per apple. $0.75 < $0.83, so 4 apples for $3 is the better deal.

    Juice B10 oz for 4 servings
