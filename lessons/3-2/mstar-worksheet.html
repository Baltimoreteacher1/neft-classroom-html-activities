Part B — Test-Format Questions

These match the state test's formats: two-part questions, select-all, and written responses.

Question 7Two-part question

Part A

Booth A charges $3 for 5 games. What is the unit rate in dollars per game?

Fill in the bubble next to the one best answer.

  • A$0.30 per game
  • B$0.60 per game
  • C$1.67 per game
  • D$15.00 per game

Part B

Answer Part A before Part B — Part B asks about your reasoning.

Booth B charges $5 for 8 games. Which booth is the better deal, and why?

  • ABooth B, because $5 buys more games than $3.
  • BBooth A, because 3 is less than 5.
  • CThey are equal, because both ratios simplify to the same value.
  • DBooth A, because $0.60 per game is less than $0.625 per game.
Question 8Written response · 2 points

Type II Reasoning: Dividing the Wrong Way

Marisol wanted the cost per pound of apples. The sign read '4 pounds for $6.' She wrote: 'Unit rate = 4 ÷ 6 = about $0.67 per pound.'

Explain Marisol's misconception and give the correct unit rate.

Write your answer in complete sentences. Show or explain your mathematical thinking.

Part C — Show Your Thinking

Answer in writing, the way the state test's constructed responses work.

Question 9Written response · 2 points

An arcade sells tokens in three packs: 25 tokens for $5, 60 tokens for $10.80, and 100 tokens for $19. A student says the 100-token pack is always the best deal because you get the most tokens. Is the student correct? Find all three unit rates and explain.

Write your answer in complete sentences. Show or explain your mathematical thinking.

When you finish, check your reasoning with your teacher or family. Your teacher has the scoring guide for the written responses.
