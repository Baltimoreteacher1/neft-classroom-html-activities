🚀 Launch

You're the stats analyst for the school basketball team. Coach needs a report on the team's scoring: in the last 7 games they scored 42, 38, 55, 41, 38, 44, and 40 points. Which average best represents the team's typical performance?

What are mean, median, and mode?

Mean, median, and mode are three ways to find the center, or "typical" value, of a data set. The mean is the average, the median is the middle, and the mode is the most common value.

Key idea: Mean = add then divide, median = the middle of the ordered numbers, mode = the value that shows up most.

Watch me

  • My small data set: 2, 4, 4, 6, 9. There are 5 numbers.
  • Mean: add them, 2 + 4 + 4 + 6 + 9 = 25, then divide by 5. So the mean = 25 ÷ 5 = 5.
  • Median: the numbers are already in order. The middle (3rd) value is 4, so the median = 4.
  • Mode: the number 4 appears twice, more than any other, so the mode = 4.

Let's try together

  • New data set, in order: 3, 7, 7, 10, 13. First, what is the mean? Add all five, then divide by 5.
  • Next, what is the median? Which value is right in the middle?
  • Last, what is the mode? Which number appears the most times?

Now it's your turn

  • Next, you will order the team's game scores and find the mean, median, and mode yourself.
  • Then you will decide which one best shows a typical game.
