Start hereWords for this lesson

Read each word before you begin. Say it out loud.

WordWhat it meansExample
estimateSpanish: estimación A reasoned answer that is close enough to be useful, found without exact computation. about 22 pounds
benchmark percentSpanish: porcentaje de referencia A friendly percent — 1%, 10%, 25%, 50%, 75%, 100% — used as a landmark for estimating. 10%, 25%, 50%
compatible numbersSpanish: números compatibles Numbers close to the originals that are easy to compute with mentally. 216 pounds → 200 pounds
double number lineSpanish: recta numérica doble Two parallel number lines that pair amounts with their percents at the same positions. 0 · 25% · 50% · 75% · 100% above; 0 · 1.5 · 3 · 4.5 · 6 GB below.
the wholeSpanish: el todo The quantity that counts as 100% in a problem — and it can change between questions. “Of the USED storage” makes 4 GB the whole — not the 6 GB total.
round Change a number to a nearby friendly one. 8.6% → 10%

How it worksWorked exampleestimating a percent of an amount

These numbers are not on your problems. The steps are. Follow them with your own numbers.

A school collected 412 cans. About 18.5% of them were soup cans. About how many soup cans is that?

  1. Round the percent: 18.5% is close to the benchmark twenty percent.
  2. Round the amount: 412 cans is close to the friendly number 400 cans.
  3. Ten percent of 400 cans is 40 cans, and twenty percent is twice that.
  4. The percent was rounded up and the total down, so the estimate runs a bit high.

Answer: About 80 of the cans were soup cans.

Watch outA common mistake

Keeping the same whole for every question — when the problem says 'of the used storage,' the whole changes from 6 GB to 4 GB, and estimates anchored to the old whole are wrong before any arithmetic happens.
