    Spot the Common Mistake

    1. 1Find the prime factorization of 20Start: 20
    2. 2First split20 = 4 × 5
    3. 3Check each factor5 is prime, so that branch is done.
    4. 4Final answer20 = 4 × 5

    Which step has the mistake? Explain it and write the correct work.

    The mistake is in Step 4. Correct work: 4 is not prime because 4 = 2 × 2, so the tree needs one more split. The complete prime factorization is 20 = 2 × 2 × 5. The factor tree stopped one split too early. The check tested only the 5 and left the 4 alone, but 4 = 2 × 2 is composite, so 20 = 4 × 5 is not a prime factorization. Splitting that 4 gives the complete answer, 20 = 2 × 2 × 5.

  6. 6

    Level 2 Extension — Fix the Broken Factor Tree

    1. 1Find prime factorization of 96Start: 96
    2. 2First split96 = 8 × 12
    3. 3Break down 88 = 2 × 2 × 2
    4. 4Break down 1212 = 2 × 6
    5. 5Final answer96 = 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 6

    Which step has the mistake? Explain it and write the correct work.

    The mistake is in Step 4. Correct work: 12 = 2 × 6, but 6 is not prime. 6 = 2 × 3, so 12 = 2 × 2 × 3. The complete prime factorization is 96 = 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 3 = 2⁵ × 3. The 12 branch stopped while 6 was still composite: 6 = 2 × 3, so 12 = 2 × 2 × 3, not 2 × 6. Finishing that branch gives 96 = 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 3 = 2⁵ × 3.
