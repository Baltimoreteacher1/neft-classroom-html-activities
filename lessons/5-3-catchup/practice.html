Where we left off

Our goal: I can show I am caught up on Lessons 5.1–5.3 by using each lesson's big idea in mixed practice.

The big idea: 5.1: Area of a parallelogram = base × height, where the height is the straight-up (perpendicular) distance, NOT the slanted side. • 5.2: Area of a triangle = ½ × base × height. A triangle is exactly half of a rectangle with the same base and height. • 5.3: Area of a trapezoid = ½ × (base 1 + base 2) × height. We add the two bases first, then take half.

Model to copy — The Big Ideas (one per lesson)

  3. Lesson 5.3 — Determine the Area of Trapezoids: Area of a trapezoid = ½ × (base 1 + base 2) × height. We add the two bases first, then take half.
