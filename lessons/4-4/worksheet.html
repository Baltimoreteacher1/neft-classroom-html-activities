Answer Key

Name: Date:
  1. 1

    What is 20% of 150?

    1. A30
    2. B20
    3. C35
    4. D75

    20% = 0.20. Multiply: 0.20 × 150 = 30.

  2. 2

    What is 50% of 84?

    1. A42
    2. B50
    3. C34
    4. D168

    50% = 0.50. Multiply: 0.50 × 84 = 42. (Or simply divide by 2: 84 ÷ 2 = 42.)

  3. 3

    What is 10% of 320?

    1. A32
    2. B3.2
    3. C320
    4. D64

    10% = 0.10. Multiply: 0.10 × 320 = 32. (Or move the decimal one place left.)

  4. 4

    What percent of 50 is 15?

    1. A30%
    2. B15%
    3. C35%
    4. D50%

    15 ÷ 50 = 0.30 = 30%.
