Learning target I can find the percent of a number using an equation or a model.

  1. 1Circle the letter of the best answer. Show how you know.

    Which benchmark percent is closest to 9%?

    1. A1%
    2. B10%
    3. C25%
    4. D50%

    Answer: B — 10%

    Why: 9% is only 1 away from the benchmark 10%.

    Watch for: A common mistake in Find the Percent of a Number is multiplying by the percent number itself instead of its decimal form — for example, finding 30% of 80 by computing 30 × 80 = 2,400 instead of 0.30 × 80 = 24. Always divide the percent by 100 to get the decimal, then multiply that decimal by the base.

    Estimate 24% of 80 using a benchmark percent.

    1. Aabout 8
    2. Babout 20
    3. Cabout 24
    4. Dabout 40

    Answer: B — about 20

    Why: 24% is close to 25%, and 25% of 80 is 80 ÷ 4 = 20.

    Answer: C — about $8

    Why: Round $39.75 to $40. 10% of $40 is $4, so 20% is about $8.

Part 1Understand the ideamodels and meaning
  1. 1Write the letter of the matching item on each line.

    Match each percent-of-a-number tip to its result.

    1. A20% of 70
    2. B50% of 46
    3. C10% of 90
    4. D30% of 200
    • A14
    • B23
    • C9
    • D60

    Why: Each tip is the percent written as a decimal times its base: 0.20 × 70 = 14, 0.50 × 46 = 23, 0.10 × 90 = 9, and 0.30 × 200 = 60. Friendly shortcuts confirm them fast — 50% is just half, and 10% slides the decimal one place left.

  2. 2Write the name of the correct group on each line.

    Each arcade game shows a percent of a number. Sort each one into the correct result bin.

    Groups: Equals 12 Equals 30
    • Equals 1225% of 48
    • Equals 1240% of 30
    • Equals 3075% of 40
    • Equals 3010% of 300

    Why: Working out each card sorts it: 0.25 × 48 = 12 and 0.40 × 30 = 12 land in the Equals 12 bin, while 0.75 × 40 = 30 and 0.10 × 300 = 30 land in Equals 30. Notice that '40% of 30' and '75% of 40' use the same two numbers but give different results, so you have to compute rather than pattern-match.

Part 2Apply itreal-world problems
  1. 3Circle the letter of the best answer. Show how you know.

    The arcade is donating 12% of Saturday's ticket sales to charity. They sold 1,500 tickets on Saturday. The manager says they will donate 180 tickets. A student says it should be 18 tickets. Check with a benchmark: what is 10% of 1,500?

    1. A1.5
    2. B15
    3. C100
    4. D150

    Answer: D — 150

    Why: 10% of 1,500 is 150. Since 12% is a bit more than 10%, the answer must be a bit more than 150 — so 180 makes sense and 18 cannot.

    Watch for: A common mistake in Find the Percent of a Number is multiplying by the percent number itself instead of its decimal form — for example, finding 30% of 80 by computing 30 × 80 = 2,400 instead of 0.30 × 80 = 24. Always divide the percent by 100 to get the decimal, then multiply that decimal by the base.
