🚀 Launch

It's Discount Day at the arcade! Different games are offering percent-off deals on ticket prices. The claw machine is 25% off its usual 60-ticket price, and the racing game is 40% off 80 tickets. You need to calculate the discounts to figure out how many tickets each game actually costs!

How do I find the percent of a number?

The base is the number right after the word "of" — and that word "of" is telling you to multiply. The part is the amount you end up with.

Key idea: To find the part, change the percent to a decimal, then multiply: part = percent × base.

Watch me

  • The claw machine is 25% off its 60-ticket price. I want the discount.
  • First I change 25% to a decimal: 25 ÷ 100 = 0.25.
  • Then I multiply by the base: 0.25 × 60 = 15.
  • So the discount is 15 tickets. The part is 15.

Let's try together

  • Let's find 40% of 80. What is 40% as a decimal? (0.40)
  • Now multiply: 0.40 × 80 = ?
  • What is the part? (32)

Now it's your turn

  • In the table, change each percent to a decimal, then multiply by the base.
