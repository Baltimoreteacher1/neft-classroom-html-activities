      Spot the Common Mistake

      1. 1Equation6x = 42
      2. 2Choose operationTo undo it, I will multiply both sides by 6.
      3. 3Apply to both sides6x × 6 = 42 × 6
      4. 4Simplifyx = 252

      Which step has the mistake? Explain it and write the correct work.

      The mistake is in Step 2. Correct work: x is multiplied by 6, so the inverse is DIVISION. Divide both sides by 6: x = 42 ÷ 6 = 7. Check: 6 × 7 = 42 ✓ The operation chosen was the same one, not the inverse. Since x is multiplied by 6, dividing both sides by 6 is what isolates it: x = 42 ÷ 6 = 7, and the check 6 × 7 = 42 works. Multiplying both sides by 6 again buried the variable deeper and produced 252.

      Level 2 Extension - The Vault That Won't Open

      1. 1Equation4x = 32
      2. 2Identify inverseInverse of multiplication is multiplication
      3. 3Apply to both sides4x x 4 = 32 x 4
      4. 4Simplifyx = 128

      Which step has the mistake? Explain it and write the correct work.

      The mistake is in Step 2. Correct work: The inverse of multiplication is DIVISION. Divide both sides by 4: x = 32 / 4 = 8. Check: 4 x 8 = 32. The inverse of multiplication is division, not more multiplication. Dividing both sides of 4x = 32 by 4 gives x = 8, and the check 4 × 8 = 32 works. Multiplying both sides by 4 instead pushed the answer up to 128, which is 16 times too big.
