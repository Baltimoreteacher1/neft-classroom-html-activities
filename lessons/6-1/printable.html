What does it mean to divide by a fraction?

Dividing by a fraction asks one question: how many of that small piece fit inside the whole amount? For 3 ÷ 1/4, we ask how many 1/4-foot pieces fit into 3 feet.

Key idea: Dividing Whole Numbers & Fractions. 1. Put a 1 under the whole number: 3 ÷ 1/4 = 3/1 ÷ 1/4. 2. Keep, Change, Flip: 3/1 ÷ 1/4 = 3/1 × 4/1. 3. Multiply across: 3/1 × 4/1 = 12/1 = 12

Watch me

  • I want to cut a 3-foot strip of tape into 1/4-foot pieces, so I write 3 ÷ 1/4.
  • The 3 is a whole number, so I put a 1 under it. Now both numbers are fractions: 3/1 ÷ 1/4.
  • Keep, Change, Flip. I keep 3/1, I change ÷ to ×, and I flip 1/4 to 4/1. That gives 3/1 × 4/1.
  • I multiply across: 3 × 4 = 12 on top and 1 × 1 = 1 on the bottom, so 3 ÷ 1/4 = 3/1 × 4/1 = 12 pieces.
  • One more move to know: mixed number → improper fraction: multiply the bottom by the whole number, add the top, keep the same bottom. 3 1/2 → (2 × 3) + 1 = 7 → 7/2.

Let's do one together — the same problem the bar model showed

  • Let's try 2 ÷ 1/3. The bar model counted 6 thirds inside 2 wholes — now we get there with the steps.
  • Step 1: put a 1 under the whole number. 2 becomes 2/1, so we have 2/1 ÷ 1/3.
  • Step 2: Keep, Change, Flip. Keep 2/1, change ÷ to ×, flip 1/3 to 3/1. That gives 2/1 × 3/1.
  • Step 3: multiply across. 2 × 3 = 6 on top and 1 × 1 = 1 on the bottom, so 2 ÷ 1/3 = 6 — exactly what we counted on the bars.

Your turn — same three steps, the other direction

  • Now try 1/2 ÷ 4. This time the whole number is the second number instead of the first.
  • Step 1 does not change: put a 1 under the whole number. The 4 becomes 4/1, so you have 1/2 ÷ 4/1.
  • Step 2: Keep, Change, Flip. Keep 1/2, change ÷ to ×, flip 4/1 to 1/4. Multiply across.
  • You should get 1/8. Notice you used the same three steps as before — only the whole number moved.
  • One more: the top number does not have to be a 1. A unit fraction like 1/2 and a fraction like 3/4 use the same three steps. For 3/4 ÷ 2, write the 2 as 2/1, flip it to 1/2, and multiply: 3/4 × 1/2 = 3/8.
