    Every story cuts a whole amount into equal small pieces, so the total is the dividend and the piece size is the divisor. Put a 1 under each whole number and flip the divisor: 9/1 × 3/1 = 27 steps, 7/1 × 4/1 = 28 zones, 10/1 × 2/1 = 20 jars, 6/1 × 6/1 = 36 samples, and 8/1 × 5/1 = 40 clips. Pon un 1 debajo de cada número entero y voltea el divisor: 9/1 × 3/1 = 27 pasos, 7/1 × 4/1 = 28 zonas, 10/1 × 2/1 = 20 frascos, 6/1 × 6/1 = 36 muestras y 8/1 × 5/1 = 40 clips. Fíjate en que el cable de 6 pies cortado en muestras de 1/6 de pie da 36, no 1: multiplicas por el divisor volteado, no por que coincida con el entero.

    The first three cards name a piece size — 1/4-foot pieces, 1/3-yard steps, 1/5-meter clips — and ask how many of those pieces fit, so they are "how many groups" questions. The last three name how many shares there are — 3 agents, 4 case files, 5 suspects — and ask how much each one gets, so they are "how big is each share" questions. Both are division, and both solve the same way: write the whole number over 1, then Keep, Change, Flip.Las primeras tres tarjetas nombran un tamaño de pieza —piezas de 1/4 de pie, pasos de 1/3 de yarda, clips de 1/5 de metro— y preguntan cuántas de esas piezas caben, así que son preguntas de "cuántos grupos". Las últimas tres nombran cuántas partes hay —3 agentes, 4 expedientes, 5 sospechosos— y preguntan cuánto le toca a cada una, así que son preguntas de "qué tan grande es cada parte".     • Quotient GREATER Than Dividend: 5 ÷ 1/3, 8 ÷ 1/2, 6 ÷ 1/4Cociente MAYOR que el dividendo: 5 ÷ 1/3 = 15 (mayor que 5), 8 ÷ 1/2 = 16 (mayor que 8), 6 ÷ 1/4 = 24 (mayor que 6)
    • Quotient LESS Than Dividend: 4 ÷ 2, 10 ÷ 5, 12 ÷ 3Cociente MENOR que el dividendo: 4 ÷ 2 = 2 (menor que 4), 10 ÷ 5 = 2 (menor que 10), 12 ÷ 3 = 4 (menor que 12)

    Put a 1 under each whole number and flip the divisor.
