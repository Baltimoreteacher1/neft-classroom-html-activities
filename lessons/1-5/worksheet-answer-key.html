    Each penny has a value of $0.01. What is the value of 100 pennies?

    1. A$1.00
    2. B$0.10
    3. C$10.00
    4. D$100.00

    100 × $0.01 = $1.00 — one hundred hundredths make one whole dollar.

    Watch for: Reporting only the points from the streak (20 or 30) and forgetting to add the points each player had before the streak began.

  2. 2

    Each quarter has a value of $0.25. Following the pattern, what is the value of 100 quarters?

    1. A$25.00
    2. B$2.50
    3. C$250.00
    4. D$100.25

    100 × $0.25 = $25.00 — the same pattern as 100 pennies making $1.00 and 100 nickels making $5.00.

    Watch for: Reporting only the points from the streak (20 or 30) and forgetting to add the points each player had before the streak began.

  3. 3

    Player A scores a two-pointer on every shot. What is Player A's pattern rule?

    1. AAdd 2
    2. BAdd 3
    3. CMultiply by 2
    4. DAdd 18

    Each number in Player A's points is 2 greater than the number before it, so the rule is to add 2.

    Watch for: Reporting only the points from the streak (20 or 30) and forgetting to add the points each player had before the streak began.

  4. 4

    Player B had 6 points, then made 10 three-pointers in a row. How many points does Player B have now?

    1. A36
    2. B16
    3. C30
    4. D9

    Ten three-pointers add 10 × 3 = 30 points, and 6 + 30 = 36 points.

    Watch for: Reporting only the points from the streak (20 or 30) and forgetting to add the points each player had before the streak began.

    If both players kept their streaks going, after how many MORE shots would Player B tie Player A?

    1. A2 more shots
    2. B1 more shot
    3. CThey can never tie
    4. D10 more shots

    The gap is 38 − 36 = 2 and shrinks by 1 each shot (3 − 2 = 1). After 2 more shots: Player A has 38 + 4 = 42 and Player B has 36 + 6 = 42.

    Watch for: Reporting only the points from the streak (20 or 30) and forgetting to add the points each player had before the streak began.

  2. 2

    A sei whale measures 4.5 meters at birth and grows about 2.5 centimeters each day. About how many days until it reaches 15 meters?

    1. AAbout 420 days
    2. BAbout 600 days
    3. CAbout 42 days
    4. DAbout 4.2 days

    It must grow 15 − 4.5 = 10.5 meters = 1,050 centimeters. At 2.5 cm per day: 1,050 ÷ 2.5 = 420 days.

    Watch for: Reporting only the points from the streak (20 or 30) and forgetting to add the points each player had before the streak began.
