  1. 1Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    Each bike has 2 wheels. Bikes are x and wheels are y. What is the y-value of the point for 4 bikes?

    Hint Multiply the bikes by 2.

    Final answer:8

    Why: Write 1 bike and 2 wheels in the table. Find the scale factor for the bikes: 4 ÷ 1 = 4 Multiply the wheels by the same factor: 2 × 4 = 8 Write the ordered pair: (4, 8)

  2. 2Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    Each pack has 4 pencils. Packs are x and pencils are y. The point for 3 packs is (3, ?). What is the missing number?

    Hint The missing number is y. Multiply the packs by 4.

    Final answer:12

    Why: Write 1 pack and 4 pencils in the table. Find the scale factor for the packs: 3 ÷ 1 = 3 Multiply the pencils by the same factor: 4 × 3 = 12 Write the ordered pair: (3, 12)

  3. 3Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    Shop A sells 2 cookies for $4. Shop B sells 3 cookies for $9. What is the lower price for 1 cookie?

    Hint Divide each cost by the number of cookies.

    Final answer:$2

    Why: Write each shop in its own table: 2 : $4; 3 : $9 Divide each table down to 1 cookie: $4 ÷ 2 = $2; $9 ÷ 3 = $3 Choose the lower price: $2 < $3

  4. 4Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    Drink A uses 1 cup of juice for 2 cups of water. Drink B uses 2 cups of juice for 3 cups of water. Scale both to 6 cups of water. Which drink has more juice?

    Hint With the same water, the drink with more juice wins.

    Final answer:Drink B

    Why: Find each scale factor to 6 cups: 6 ÷ 2 = 3; 6 ÷ 3 = 2 Scale both drinks to 6 cups: 1 × 3 = 3 → 3:6; 2 × 2 = 4 → 4:6 Compare the juice: 4 > 3

  5. 5Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    How many inches are in 2 feet?

    Hint Each foot is 12 inches. Find 2 × 12.

    Final answer:24 inches

    Why: Write 1 foot and 12 inches in the table. Find the scale factor for the feet: 2 ÷ 1 = 2 Multiply by the same factor: 12 × 2 = 24

  6. 6Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    There are 1,000 meters in 1 kilometer. How many meters are in 6 kilometers?

    Hint Meters are smaller than kilometers, so multiply by 1,000.

    Final answer:6,000 meters

    Why: Write 1 kilometer and 1,000 meters in the table. Find the scale factor for the kilometers: 6 ÷ 1 = 6 Multiply by the same factor: 1,000 × 6 = 6,000

  7. 7Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    Use 1 kilometer ≈ 0.6 mile. About how many miles is 60 kilometers?

    Hint Find the scale factor from 1 to 60. Multiply 0.6 by the same factor.

    Final answer:36 miles

    Why: Write the conversion fact in the table: 1 km : 0.6 mi Find the scale factor from 1 to 60: 60 ÷ 1 = 60 Multiply the miles by the same factor: 0.6 × 60 = 36

  8. 8Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    Use 1 kilogram ≈ 2.2 pounds. About how many pounds is 3 kilograms?

    Hint Find the scale factor from 1 to 3. Multiply 2.2 by the same factor.

    Final answer:6.6 pounds

    Why: Write the conversion fact in the table: 1 kg : 2.2 lb Find the scale factor from 1 to 3: 3 ÷ 1 = 3 Multiply the pounds by the same factor: 2.2 × 3 = 6.6

  9. 9Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    6 apples cost $3. What is the cost for 1 apple?

    Hint Divide the price by the number of apples: $3 ÷ 6.

    Final answer:$0.50

    Why: Write the deal in the table: 6 apples : $3 Find the factor from 6 to 1: 6 ÷ 6 = 1 Divide the cost by the same number: $3 ÷ 6 = $0.50

  10. 10Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    Shop A sells 2 pens for $3. Shop B sells 5 pens for $6. Which shop has the lower cost for 1 pen?

    Hint Find the cost for 1 pen at each shop. Then compare.

    Final answer:Shop B

    Why: Write both deals in the table. Scale each deal down to 1 pen: $3 ÷ 2 = $1.50; $6 ÷ 5 = $1.20 Compare the unit prices: $1.20 < $1.50

  11. 11Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    A ratio is 1:4. Multiply both numbers by 3. The new ratio is 3 : ?. What is the missing number?

    Hint Multiply the second number by 3: 4 × 3.

    Final answer:12

    Why: Write the ratio in the table: 1 : 4 Find the scale factor: 3 ÷ 1 = 3 Multiply the second number by 3: 4 × 3 = 12

  12. 12Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    A ratio is 3:2. Use a scale factor of 5. The new ratio is ? : 10. What is the missing number?

    Hint Multiply the first number by 5: 3 × 5.

    Final answer:15

    Why: Write the ratio in the table: 3 : 2 Find the scale factor: 10 ÷ 2 = 5 Multiply the first number by 5: 3 × 5 = 15

  13. 13Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    There are 3 feet in 1 yard. How many feet are in 7 yards?

    Hint The scale factor from 1 yard to 7 yards is 7. Multiply the feet by 7.

    Final answer:21 feet

    Why: Write the conversion fact in the table: 1 yd : 3 ft Find the scale factor: 7 ÷ 1 = 7 Multiply the feet by the same factor: 3 × 7 = 21

  14. 14Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    There are 100 centimeters in 1 meter. How many centimeters are in 6 meters?

    Hint The scale factor from 1 meter to 6 meters is 6. Multiply the centimeters by 6.

    Final answer:600 centimeters

    Why: Write the conversion fact in the table: 1 m : 100 cm Find the scale factor: 6 ÷ 1 = 6 Multiply the centimeters by the same factor: 100 × 6 = 600
