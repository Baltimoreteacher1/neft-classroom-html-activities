  1. 1Circle the letter of the best answer. Show how you know.

    (Lesson 3.4) A ratio table shows (2, 6), (4, 12), and (6, 18). Which ordered pair comes next if the pattern continues?

    1. A(7, 20)
    2. B(8, 24)
    3. C(8, 22)
    4. D(10, 24)

    Hint Watch each coordinate separately: x goes 2, 4, 6, … and y goes 6, 12, 18, …

    Answer: B — (8, 24)

    Why: The pattern adds 2 to x and 6 to y each time. After (6, 18): x = 6+2 = 8, y = 18+6 = 24. The next point is (8, 24).

    Watch for: A common mistake in Convert Measurement Units is applying the conversion factor in the wrong direction — for example, converting 4 feet to inches by dividing (4 ÷ 12 = 0.33 inches) instead of multiplying (4 × 12 = 48 inches), or converting 80 ounces to pounds by multiplying (80 × 16 = 1,280 pounds) instead of dividing (80 ÷ 16 = 5 pounds). Before you answer, ask: am I changing to a SMALLER unit (there will be MORE of them, so multiply) or a BIGGER unit (there will be FEWER of them, so divide)?

    (Lesson 3.4) When you graph equivalent ratios, the points will always form what shape?

    1. AA curved line
    2. BA zigzag
    3. CA circle
    4. DA straight line through the origin

    Hint Think about what happened when you plotted equivalent-ratio points earlier in this lesson.

    Answer: D — A straight line through the origin

    Why: Equivalent ratios are proportional, so their graph is always a straight line that passes through the origin (0, 0).

    (Lesson 3.5) Chef A uses 2 cups of cheese for every 8 crackers. Chef B uses 3 cups of cheese for every 9 crackers. Who uses more cheese per cracker?

    1. AChef B
    2. BChef A
    3. CThey use the same amount
    4. DCannot determine

    Hint You can't compare 2 cups and 3 cups directly — the chefs use different numbers of crackers.

    Answer: A — Chef B

    Why: Chef A: 2÷8 = 0.25 cups per cracker. Chef B: 3÷9 ≈ 0.33 cups per cracker. Chef B uses more cheese per cracker.

    (Lesson 3.5) Which ratio is greater: 3:4 or 5:8?

    1. A5:8
    2. BThey are equal
    3. C3:4
    4. DCannot determine

    Hint The second numbers (4 and 8) don't match, so the ratios aren't ready to compare yet.

    Answer: C — 3:4

    Why: Convert to the same denominator: 3:4 = 6:8. Since 6:8 > 5:8, the ratio 3:4 is greater.

    (Lesson 3.6) There are 12 inches in 1 foot. How many inches are in 5 feet?

    1. A7 inches
    2. B17 inches
    3. C50 inches
    4. D60 inches

    Hint Write the unit ratio first: 12 inches : 1 foot.

    Answer: D — 60 inches

    Why: Inches are smaller than feet, so multiply by the unit ratio: 5 × 12 = 60 inches.
