Name: Date:
  1. 1

    Point A is at (-3, 5). It is reflected over the x-axis to A', then A' is reflected over the y-axis to A''. What are the coordinates of A''?

    1. A(3, -5)
    2. B(-3, -5)
    3. C(3, 5)
    4. D(-3, 5)

    Over x-axis: (-3, 5) → (-3, -5). Then over y-axis: (-3, -5) → (3, -5). Both coordinates changed sign.

    Watch for: A common mistake in Reflect Points Across Axes is flipping the sign of the wrong coordinate — changing the x-coordinate when reflecting over the x-axis, instead of the y-coordinate (or vice versa for the y-axis). For example, to reflect (3, -5) over the x-axis, a student might flip the x-coordinate and write (-3, -5), but reflecting over the x-axis only changes the sign of the y-coordinate: (3, -5) becomes (3, 5). Before you submit, ask: 'Did I flip the sign of the coordinate that matches the axis I'm reflecting over — y for the x-axis, x for the y-axis?'

  2. 2

    Spot the Common Mistake

    1. 1ProblemReflect the point (-3, -8) over the x-axis.
    2. 2AttemptBoth coordinates are negative, so I will flip the x-coordinate: -3 becomes 3. The answer is (3, -8).
    3. 3CheckFolding over the horizontal x-axis should send the point up across the axis, but this answer kept it below the axis.

    Which step has the mistake? Explain it and write the correct work.

    The mistake is in Step 2. Correct work: Reflecting over the x-axis changes the y-coordinate, not the x-coordinate. The rule is (x, y) -> (x, -y). For (-3, -8), keep x as -3 and flip y: -8 becomes 8. The correct reflection is (-3, 8), not (3, -8). The negative signs do not change which coordinate flips — the axis does. The Attempt step flips the x-coordinate because both numbers are negative, but the signs of the coordinates never decide which one flips — the axis does. Over the x-axis the rule is (x, y) → (x, -y), so (-3, -8) reflects to (-3, 8), moving up across the horizontal axis.

  3. 3

    Find the Reflection Error

    1. 1ProblemReflect (-3, 5) over the y-axis
    2. 2Student thinkingThe y-axis is vertical, so I change the y-coordinate
    3. 3Student answer(-3, -5)

    Which step has the mistake? Explain it and write the correct work.

    The mistake is in Step 2. Correct work: Reflecting over the y-axis changes the x-coordinate, not the y-coordinate. The correct reflection of (-3, 5) over the y-axis is (3, 5). The student confused which coordinate changes for each axis. The student's thinking step says the vertical y-axis changes the y-coordinate, but the axis name tells you which coordinate stays the same. Over the y-axis the rule is (x, y) → (-x, y), so (-3, 5) reflects to (3, 5), not (-3, -5).
