🎯 The big ideaLa idea principal

In one sentence: Break the figure into shapes you already know, find each area, then combine. The decision that matters is per piece: a piece that is PART of the figure gets added, and a piece that has been CUT OUT of it gets subtracted.

En una frase: Divide la figura en formas que ya conoces, halla cada área, y luego combínalas. La decisión que importa es pieza por pieza: una pieza que es PARTE de la figura se suma, y una pieza que fue RECORTADA se resta.

⚠️
Watch out:Cuidado con este error común:

Adding every piece, even the ones cut out. Parts of the figure are added; holes are subtracted. Sumar todas las piezas, incluso las recortadas. Las partes de la figura se suman; los huecos se restan.

      10. Level up 1Sube de nivel 1

        Add areas when a piece is attached to or built onto the main shape, like a triangular roof added to a rectangular wall -- the total figure includes that piece.
