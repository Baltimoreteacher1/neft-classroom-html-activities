    • 2 MULTIPLE CHOICE

      Convert 3/5 to a percent.

      1. A60%
      2. B35%
      3. C53%
      4. D65%

      💡 Solution Rationale: 5 × 20 = 100, so 3 × 20 = 60. That makes 3/5 = 60/100 = 0.6 = 60%.

      ⚠️ Watch for (Misconception): A common mistake in Relate Fractions, Decimals, and Percents is treating the numerator and denominator as the percent's digits — turning 3/5 into "35%" by just reading the 3 and the 5. Percent means out of 100, so the fix is to build an equivalent fraction with denominator 100: 5 × 20 = 100, so multiply the top by 20 too — 3/5 = 60/100 = 60%. A second version of this mistake is scaling only ONE part of the fraction (writing 3/5 = 3/100 or 60/5). Whatever you multiply the denominator by, you must multiply the numerator by the same number. When a denominator will not reach 100 evenly (8, 3, 7), divide the numerator by the denominator instead and then multiply by 100.

      • 6 ERROR ANALYSIS

        Spot the Common Mistake

        1. 1Convert 3/5 to a percent:3/5
        2. 2Read the digits as the percent:The numerator is 3 and the denominator is 5, so the student writes 35%.
        3. 3Final answer:3/5 = 35%

        Which step contains the error? Explain the mathematical misconception and write the correct calculation below.

        Key Analysis: Mistake is in Step 2. Correct Work: You cannot just read the digits. Percent means out of 100, so build the equivalent fraction: 5 × 20 = 100, and 3 × 20 = 60. So 3/5 = 60/100 = 0.6 = 60%, not 35%.. Misconception: The two digits of a fraction are not the two digits of a percent. Percent means out of 100, so rewrite 3/5 with a denominator of 100: multiply the bottom by 20 to reach 100, and the top by that same 20. That gives 3/5 = 60/100 = 60%, not 35%.

        • 5 ERROR ANALYSIS

          Spot the Common Mistake

          1. 1Convert 5/8 to a percent:5/8
          2. 2Divide the bottom by the top:8 ÷ 5 = 1.6
          3. 3Multiply by 100:1.6 × 100 = 160%
          4. 4Final answer:5/8 = 160%

          Which step contains the error? Explain the mathematical misconception and write the correct calculation below.

          Key Analysis: Mistake is in Step 2. Correct Work: The student divided in the wrong order. To turn a fraction into a decimal, divide the TOP by the bottom: 5 ÷ 8 = 0.625, then × 100 → 62.5%. So 5/8 = 0.625 = 62.5%. (A check: 5/8 is less than 1, so the percent must be under 100%, never 160%.). Misconception: The fraction bar means numerator ÷ denominator, so this division is upside down. It should be 5 ÷ 8 = 0.625, and 0.625 × 100 = 62.5%. A quick check catches it: 5/8 is less than one whole, so its percent must be under 100% — 160% was impossible.

        1. 1 ERROR ANALYSIS

          Find the Conversion Error

          1. 1Convert 2/3 to a percent:2/3
          2. 2Divide numerator by denominator:2 ÷ 3 = 0.67
          3. 3Move decimal two places:0.67 → 67%
          4. 4Final answer:2/3 = 67%

          Which step contains the error? Explain the mathematical misconception and write the correct calculation below.

          Key Analysis: Mistake is in Step 2. Correct Work: 2 ÷ 3 = 0.666... (repeating). The exact answer is 66.7% or 66⅔%.. Misconception: The division is set up the right way, but 2 ÷ 3 does not end — it gives 0.666... repeating. Writing 0.67 rounds the value, so the percent it produces (67%) is rounded too. The exact answer is 66.7% or 66⅔%.
        2. 2 OPEN RESPONSE

          A student says 3/8 is greater than 40% because 3 and 8 are bigger numbers than 4 and 0. Is the student correct? Convert both values to the same form and explain.

          💡 Exemplar Response: Key Terms to Look For: not correct, 0.375, 37.5%, less than, convert, compare Explanation: Bigger-looking digits do not mean a bigger value — the two amounts have to be in the same form before you compare them. Converting the fraction gives 3 ÷ 8 = 0.375, which is 37.5%. Since 37.5% is less than 40%, the student is not correct: 3/8 is the smaller value.
