Question 1Two-part question

Part A — correct answer: A

A concert ticket costs 8 dollars. The website also charges one 5 dollar service fee for the whole order. Let t represent the number of tickets bought in one order. Which expression gives the total cost in dollars?

  • A8t + 5
  • B8 + 5t
  • C8(t + 5)
  • D13t

The variable t is the number of tickets. Each ticket costs 8 dollars, so the ticket cost is 8t. The 5 dollar fee is charged once, so it is a constant added on: 8t + 5.

  • B: This charges the fee on every ticket and the ticket price only once. The 8 dollars is per ticket, so it pairs with t.
  • C: This multiplies the fee by the ticket price, charging 8 dollars on the fee as well. The fee is added once.
  • D: This charges 13 dollars per ticket, applying the one-time fee to every ticket.

Part B — correct answer: B

Which reasoning shows why your answer to Part A is correct?

  • AThe 5 dollar fee is paid for each ticket, so 5 is the coefficient: 8 + 5t.
  • BThe 8 is the coefficient because it is paid for each ticket, and the 5 is a constant because it is paid one time. This gives 8t + 5, so an order of 6 tickets costs 8 × 6 + 5 = 53 dollars.
  • CAdd the fee to the number of tickets first, then multiply by the ticket price: 8(t + 5).
  • DCombine 8 and 5 into a single coefficient because both are numbers: 13t.

A per-item price becomes the coefficient of the variable and a one-time charge becomes a constant. Substituting t = 6 into 8t + 5 gives 53 dollars.

Question 2Select all that apply — correct: A, B, C, D

Select ALL statements that are true about the algebraic expression 7n + 4.

  • AThe variable is n.
  • BThe coefficient of the variable term is 7.
  • CThe constant is 4.
  • DWhen n = 3, the expression has a value of 25.
  • EWhen n = 3, the expression has a value of 33.
  • FThe term 7n means 7 plus n.

In 7n + 4 the variable is n, its coefficient is 7, and 4 is the constant. Substituting n = 3 gives 7 × 3 + 4 = 21 + 4 = 25. A value of 33 comes from combining 7 and 4 into 11 first, which is not allowed because 7n and 4 are not like terms. The term 7n means 7 times n.
