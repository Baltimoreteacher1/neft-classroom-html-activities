Part A — Skills Check

One point each.

Question 1Selected response — correct: A

Which expression represents 'the product of 6 and a number n'?

  • A6n
  • B6 + n
  • Cn − 6
  • Dn ÷ 6

'Product' means multiplication, so the product of 6 and n is 6n.

Question 2Selected response — correct: A

Which expression represents '9 less than a number y'?

  • Ay − 9
  • B9 − y
  • Cy + 9
  • D9y

'Less than' means subtract from the number, so it's y − 9.

Question 3Selected response — correct: A

Which expression represents 'the sum of a number m and 15'?

  • Am + 15
  • Bm − 15
  • C15m
  • Dm ÷ 15

'Sum' means addition, so the sum of m and 15 is m + 15.

Question 4Selected response — correct: B

Evaluate 4.25s + 23.50 when s = 10.

  • A$47.75
  • B$66.00
  • C$66.75
  • D$277.50

Substitute s = 10: 4.25 × 10 + 23.50. Multiply first: 42.50, then add: 42.50 + 23.50 = $66.00.

Question 5Selected response — correct: A

Which key word tells you to multiply?

  • AProduct
  • BSum
  • CDifference
  • DQuotient

'Product' means multiplication. 'Sum' means addition, 'difference' means subtraction, and 'quotient' means division.

Question 6Selected response — correct: C

The expression 8n + 15 gives the cost of n tickets plus a fee. What is the cost for 6 tickets?

  • A$29
  • B$48
  • C$63
  • D$138

Substitute n = 6: 8 × 6 + 15 = 48 + 15 = $63.

Part B — Test-Format Questions

Scoring notes and distractor rationales below each item.

Question 7Two-part question

Part A — correct answer: A

A concert ticket costs 8 dollars. The website also charges one 5 dollar service fee for the whole order. Let t represent the number of tickets bought in one order. Which expression gives the total cost in dollars?

  • A8t + 5
  • B8 + 5t
  • C8(t + 5)
  • D13t

The variable t is the number of tickets. Each ticket costs 8 dollars, so the ticket cost is 8t. The 5 dollar fee is charged once, so it is a constant added on: 8t + 5.

  • B: This charges the fee on every ticket and the ticket price only once. The 8 dollars is per ticket, so it pairs with t.
  • C: This multiplies the fee by the ticket price, charging 8 dollars on the fee as well. The fee is added once.
  • D: This charges 13 dollars per ticket, applying the one-time fee to every ticket.

Part B — correct answer: B

Which reasoning shows why your answer to Part A is correct?

  • AThe 5 dollar fee is paid for each ticket, so 5 is the coefficient: 8 + 5t.
  • BThe 8 is the coefficient because it is paid for each ticket, and the 5 is a constant because it is paid one time. This gives 8t + 5, so an order of 6 tickets costs 8 × 6 + 5 = 53 dollars.
  • CAdd the fee to the number of tickets first, then multiply by the ticket price: 8(t + 5).
  • DCombine 8 and 5 into a single coefficient because both are numbers: 13t.

A per-item price becomes the coefficient of the variable and a one-time charge becomes a constant. Substituting t = 6 into 8t + 5 gives 53 dollars.

Question 8Select all that apply — correct: A, B, C, D

Select ALL statements that are true about the algebraic expression 7n + 4.

  • AThe variable is n.
  • BThe coefficient of the variable term is 7.
  • CThe constant is 4.
  • DWhen n = 3, the expression has a value of 25.
  • EWhen n = 3, the expression has a value of 33.
  • FThe term 7n means 7 plus n.

In 7n + 4 the variable is n, its coefficient is 7, and 4 is the constant. Substituting n = 3 gives 7 × 3 + 4 = 21 + 4 = 25. A value of 33 comes from combining 7 and 4 into 11 first, which is not allowed because 7n and 4 are not like terms. The term 7n means 7 times n.

Part C — Show Your Thinking

Model answer and scoring guidance.

Question 9Written response · 2 points

Field day needs one $4.25 item per student plus a one-time $23.50 shipping fee. Write the expression, then find the cost for 30 students AND for 60 students. Does doubling the students double the cost? Explain.

Model answer: The expression is 4.25s + 23.50. For s = 30: 4.25 × 30 + 23.50 = 127.50 + 23.50 = $151.00. For s = 60: 4.25 × 60 + 23.50 = 255.00 + 23.50 = $278.50. Doubling the students does NOT double the cost. Twice $151.00 would be $302.00, but the actual cost is $278.50 — a difference of exactly $23.50. The reason is that only the 4.25s term depends on the number of students; the shipping fee is a constant charged once no matter how many students come, so it is not doubled along with everything else.

Award 2 points for a complete explanation with correct mathematics, 1 point for a correct answer with thin reasoning, 0 for an unrelated response.
