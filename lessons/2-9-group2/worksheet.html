    Spot the Common Mistake

    1. 1Data setA swimmer's lap times: 2, 5, 8, 9. Mean = (2 + 5 + 8 + 9) ÷ 4 = 24 ÷ 4 = 6.
    2. 2Find each absolute deviation|2 − 6| = 4, |5 − 6| = 1, |8 − 6| = 2, |9 − 6| = 3
    3. 3Find the MADSum of absolute deviations = 4 + 1 + 2 + 3 = 10. MAD = 10 ÷ 3 ≈ 3.33
