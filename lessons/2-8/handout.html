Describe Data Using the Mean

6.DS.4 · Unit 2 · Lesson 8

Name   Period

Objectives / Objetivos

Content Objective: I can determine the mean of a data set, use a target mean to find a missing value, and explain the mean as the fair share and the balance point of the data.

Language Objective: I can explain my reasoning using the words mean, average, measure of center, balance point, and data set.

Key Idea / Idea clave

Measures of Center: Mean. Formula: Mean = (Sum of all values) ÷ (Total number of values). 1. Add all data values together to find the total sum. 2. Divide the sum by the count of data values. 3. Concept: The mean represents the fair share or balance point of the data

I Notice / Observo

  • How many days of earnings did Miguel record?
  • What is the total amount Miguel earned?

I Wonder / Me pregunto

  • If Miguel shared the $60 equally across the 4 days, how much would each day be?

Vocabulary / Vocabulario

Term / TérminoDefinition / Definición
Mean
Media		The average of a data set. Add all the values, then divide by how many values there are.
El promedio de un conjunto de datos. Suma todos los valores y divide entre cuántos valores hay.
Average
Promedio		Another word for the mean — the amount each value would be if the total were shared equally.
Otra palabra para la media: la cantidad que tendría cada valor si el total se repartiera en partes iguales.
Measure of center
Medida de centro		A single number used to describe the middle, or typical value, of a whole data set.
Un solo número que se usa para describir el centro, o valor típico, de todo un conjunto de datos.
Balance point
Punto de equilibrio		The value where the total distance of the data above it equals the total distance below it. The mean is the balance point.
El valor donde la distancia total de los datos por encima es igual a la distancia total por debajo. La media es el punto de equilibrio.
Data set
Conjunto de datos		The whole collection of values that were gathered or measured.
Toda la colección de valores que se recopilaron o midieron.
Target mean
Media objetivo		The mean a person is trying to reach.
La media que una persona intenta alcanzar.
