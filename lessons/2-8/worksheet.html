      Add the values: 4 + 6 + 8 + 10 = 28. There are 4 values, so the mean is 28 ÷ 4 = 7.

      Watch for: The most common mistake with the mean is adding correctly and then dividing by the wrong number — or not dividing at all. Students report the total (for 8, 10, 6, 12 they say the mean is 36) or they divide by a count that does not match the data, such as dividing a 4-value total by 5, or skipping a zero when counting the values.         The Mean Mistake (forgot to divide)

        1. 1Data setPoints scored in 4 games: 8, 10, 6, 12
        2. 2GoalFind the MEAN number of points per game.
        3. 3Add the values8 + 10 + 6 + 12 = 36
        4. 4AnswerMean = 36 points

        Which step has the mistake? Explain it and write the correct work.

        The mistake is in Step 4. Correct work: Adding is only half of finding a mean. After the total (36) you must DIVIDE by how many values there are: 36 ÷ 4 = 9. The mean is 9 points per game, not 36. The sum 8 + 10 + 6 + 12 = 36 is correct, but the answer stops there. Dividing by the 4 values gives 36 ÷ 4 = 9. A mean of 36 is also impossible here, because no single game reached 36 points — a mean must fall between the smallest and largest value.

        The Missing Zero

        1. 1Data setGoals allowed in 4 games: 0, 3, 2, 7
        2. 2GoalFind the MEAN number of goals allowed per game.
