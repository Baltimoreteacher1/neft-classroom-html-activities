Read each word before you begin. Say it out loud.

WordWhat it meansExample
repetitionSpanish: repetición The same object, shape, or form repeated multiple times. The same tile, over and over, all the way along the border.
patternSpanish: patrón A set of objects or shapes that are repeated and arranged in a regular order. Circle, square, triangle, circle, square, triangle …
pattern unitSpanish: unidad del patrón The smallest chunk of a pattern that repeats. circle · square · triangle — the chunk that starts over.
rhythmSpanish: ritmo A design element created by varying the placement, size, orientation, or order of the same objects or shapes. The same circles, but sizes and spacing change as your eye travels.
predictabilitySpanish: previsibilidad Being able to know what comes next because the design follows a rule. Shape 25 must be a circle — the unit repeats every 3.

How it worksWorked example

These numbers are not on your problems. The steps are. Follow them with your own numbers.

A border repeats: pink circle, purple square, blue triangle, pink circle, purple square, blue triangle, and so on. What is shape number 12?

  1. Find the pattern unit: circle, square, triangle → 3 shapes
  2. Divide the shape number by the unit length: 12 ÷ 3 = 4, remainder 0
  3. Use the remainder to name the shape: remainder 0 → last shape → triangle
  4. Shape number 12 is the blue triangle.

Watch outA common mistake

Calling every repeated design a 'pattern.' One identical shape repeated is repetition; a pattern needs a unit of shapes repeating in a regular order; and rhythm varies the shapes' size and order, so it is neither.
