How do unit rates solve rate problems?

A rate compares two amounts with different units, like dollars and pounds. A unit rate is the amount for just 1. You find it by dividing, and you can use it to find any amount.

Key idea: Find the unit rate by dividing, then multiply it to find the amount you need.

Watch me

  • Team 1 knows that 3 pounds of chicken cost $18.
  • To find the unit rate, I divide the cost by the pounds: 18 divided by 3 = $6 per pound.
  • Now I can find any amount. For 5 pounds, I multiply: 6 x 5 = $30.
  • For 8 pounds, I multiply: 6 x 8 = $48.
  • The unit rate $6 per pound is the shortcut for every amount.

Let's try together

  • A car drives 100 miles in 2 hours. What is the unit rate in miles per hour?
  • Divide: 100 divided by 2 = 50 miles per hour.
  • How far in 4 hours? Multiply: 50 x 4 = 200 miles.

Now it's your turn

  • Next you will complete the chicken cost table and find the unit rate.
  • Divide to find the cost of 1 pound, then use it for other amounts.
