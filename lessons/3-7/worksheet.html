✏️ Worked Example

  1. Read the problem: A store sells 6 oranges for $4.50. What is the cost of 10 oranges?
  2. Find the unit rate: $4.50 ÷ 6 = $0.75 per orange
  3. Multiply for 10 oranges: $0.75 + 10 = $10.75
  4. Answer: 10 oranges cost $10.75
