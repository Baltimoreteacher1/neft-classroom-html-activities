Area of a Triangle

Área de un triángulo

💡 A = ½ × b × h; if b = 8 and h = 5, then A = ½ × 8 × 5 = 20 square units
TapToca ➔

The space inside a triangle. A triangle is half of a rectangle with the same base and height, so A = ½ × b × h.
