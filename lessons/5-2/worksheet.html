Answer Key

Name: Date:
  1. 1

    What is the area of a trapezoid with bases 6 cm and 10 cm, and height 4 cm?

    1. A32 sq cm
    2. B64 sq cm
    3. C24 sq cm
    4. D40 sq cm

    A = ½ × (b₁ + b₂) × h = ½ × (6 + 10) × 4 = ½ × 16 × 4 = 32 sq cm.

    Watch for: A common mistake in Area of Trapezoids is skipping the key idea: "Area of a trapezoid = ½ × (base 1 + base 2) × height. We add the two bases first, then take half." — always check your work against this rule before you submit.

  2. 2

    A trapezoid has an area of 45 sq ft, bases of 7 ft and 11 ft. What is the height?

    1. A5 ft
    2. B9 ft
    3. C3 ft
    4. D10 ft

    A = ½ × (b₁ + b₂) × h → 45 = ½ × 18 × h → 45 = 9h → h = 5 ft.

    Watch for: A common mistake in Area of Trapezoids is skipping the key idea: "Area of a trapezoid = ½ × (base 1 + base 2) × height. We add the two bases first, then take half." — always check your work against this rule before you submit.

  3. 3

    What is the first step when finding the area of a trapezoid?

    1. AAdd the two bases together
    2. BMultiply the two bases
    3. CSubtract the shorter base from the longer base
    4. DDivide the height by 2

    The formula is A = ½ × (b₁ + b₂) × h. The first step is to add the two parallel bases.

    Watch for: A common mistake in Area of Trapezoids is skipping the key idea: "Area of a trapezoid = ½ × (base 1 + base 2) × height. We add the two bases first, then take half." — always check your work against this rule before you submit.

  4. 4

    A trapezoid has bases of 5 in and 9 in, and a height of 6 in. What is its area?

    1. A42 sq in
    2. B84 sq in
    3. C27 sq in
    4. D30 sq in

    A = ½ × (5 + 9) × 6 = ½ × 14 × 6 = ½ × 84 = 42 sq in.

    Watch for: A common mistake in Area of Trapezoids is skipping the key idea: "Area of a trapezoid = ½ × (base 1 + base 2) × height. We add the two bases first, then take half." — always check your work against this rule before you submit.
