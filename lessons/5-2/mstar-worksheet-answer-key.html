Part A — Skills Check

One point each.

Question 1Selected response — correct: B

What is the area of a triangle with base 10 cm and height 6 cm?

  • A16 sq cm
  • B30 sq cm
  • C30 cm
  • D60 sq cm

A = ½ × b × h = ½ × 10 × 6 = 30 square centimeters.

Question 2Selected response — correct: B

A triangle has an area of 24 sq ft and a base of 8 ft. What is the height?

  • A3 ft
  • B6 ft
  • C12 ft
  • D16 ft

A = ½ × b × h → 24 = ½ × 8 × h → 24 = 4h → h = 6 ft.

Question 3Selected response — correct: A

Why do we divide by 2 when finding the area of a triangle?

  • AA triangle is exactly half of a rectangle with the same base and height
  • BTriangles have 2 equal sides
  • CThe base is always twice the height
  • DWe always divide area formulas by 2

A triangle with base b and height h fits inside a rectangle with the same base and height. The triangle covers exactly half the rectangle, so A = ½ × b × h.

Question 4Selected response — correct: B

What is the area of a triangle with base 14 ft and height 6 ft?

  • A20 sq ft
  • B42 sq ft
  • C42 ft
  • D84 sq ft

A = ½ × 14 × 6 = ½ × 84 = 42 sq ft.

Question 5Selected response — correct: B

A triangular window in the firm's lobby has a base of 10 ft and a height of 6 ft. What is its area?

  • A16 sq ft
  • B30 sq ft
  • C32 sq ft
  • D60 sq ft

A = ½ × base × height = ½ × 10 × 6 = ½ × 60 = 30 sq ft. The triangle is half of the 10 × 6 = 60 rectangle it fits inside.

Question 6Selected response — correct: B

A triangular roof panel on the blueprint has a base of 7 ft and a height of 4 ft. What is its area?

  • A11 sq ft
  • B14 sq ft
  • C22 sq ft
  • D28 sq ft

A = ½ × base × height = ½ × 7 × 4 = ½ × 28 = 14 sq ft. Multiply base × height first (28), then take half (14).

Part B — Test-Format Questions

Scoring notes and distractor rationales below each item.

Question 7Two-part question

Part A — correct answer: C

A triangular garden bed has a base of 14 meters and a perpendicular height of 6 meters. What is its area?

  • A10 square meters
  • B20 square meters
  • C42 square meters
  • D84 square meters

Area = ½ × base × height = ½ × 14 × 6 = 42 square meters.

  • A: That halves the sum of 14 and 6. Multiply first: half of 14 times 6.
  • B: That adds 14 and 6. Multiply the two measurements, then halve.
  • D: That is base times height with no halving, which gives the PARALLELOGRAM area. A triangle is half of it.

Part B — correct answer: A

Which statement explains why the area formula for a triangle includes the factor ½?

  • AA triangle is exactly half of a parallelogram with the same base and height.
  • BTriangles always have three sides, so you divide by three and round.
  • CThe height of a triangle is always half of its base.
  • DHalf of any shape's area is its perimeter.

Two identical triangles fit together to form a parallelogram with the same base and height, so one triangle is half of b · h.

Question 8Written response · 2 points

Type II Reasoning: The Missing One-Half

Dev found the area of a triangle with a base of 10 cm and a height of 7 cm. He wrote: 'Area = 10 × 7 = 70 square centimeters.'

Explain Dev's misconception and give the correct area.

Model answer: Area = ½ × 10 × 7 = 35 square centimeters. Dev used b · h, which is the area of a parallelogram; a triangle is half of that parallelogram.

2 pointsStudent identifies that Dev used the parallelogram formula and omitted the factor of ½, and gives 35 square centimeters.
1 pointStudent gives 35 square centimeters but does not explain why the ½ is needed.
0 pointsStudent agrees with Dev or gives an unrelated response.

Part C — Show Your Thinking

Model answer and scoring guidance.

Question 9Written response · 2 points

A rectangle is 12 ft by 8 ft. A diagonal line cuts it into two triangles. What is the area of each triangle? Explain how the triangle area formula relates to the rectangle area formula.

Model answer: The rectangle's area is 12 × 8 = 96 sq ft, and one diagonal cuts it into two identical triangles, so each triangle is 96 ÷ 2 = 48 sq ft. The formula says the same thing: each triangle keeps the rectangle's base of 12 and height of 8, and A = ½ × 12 × 8 = 48 sq ft. The ½ in the triangle formula IS that diagonal cut.

Award 2 points for a complete explanation with correct mathematics, 1 point for a correct answer with thin reasoning, 0 for an unrelated response.
