Lesson 4.54.5 Small Group · Group 1

Group 1 · Answer KeySupported practice

Learning target With my small group, I can determine the whole when I know a part and the percent that part represents — one step at a time, with support.

  1. 1Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    Jin paid $28 for a backpack on sale. That was 70% of the original price. What was the original price?

    1. 1Match the part with its percent.$28 is 70% of the price.
    2. 270% is 7 groups of 10%. Find 10%: 28 ÷ 7.$4
    3. 3100% is 10 groups of 10%. What is the original price?$40
    Final answer:The original price was $40. Check: 0.7 × 40 = 28.
  2. 2Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    A team has played 15 games. That is 30% of its season. How many games are in the whole season?

    1. 1Which number is the part? Which is the percent?The part is 15 games. The percent is 30%.
    2. 2Write an equation. Use s for the season.0.3 × s = 15
    3. 3Divide 15 by 0.3. How many games are in the season?50
    4. 4Check: is the whole more than the part?Yes. 50 games is more than 15 games.
    Final answer:There are 50 games in the season.
  3. 3Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    8 is 50% of what number?

    Hint 50% means half. If half is 8, what is the whole?

    Final answer:16

    Why: Write the percent as a decimal: 50 ÷ 100 = 0.5 Divide the part by the decimal: 8 ÷ 0.5 = 16

  4. 4Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    $45 is 75% of the original price. What was the original price?

    Hint Write 75% as a decimal. Then divide the part, $45, by it.

    Final answer:$60

    Why: Write the percent as a decimal: 75 ÷ 100 = 0.75 Divide the part by the decimal: 45 ÷ 0.75 = 60 Check by multiplying back: 0.75 × 60 = 45

  5. 5Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    Ty has saved $36. That is 20% of the cost of a bike. How much does the bike cost?

    Hint $36 is the part. The cost of the bike is the whole. 20% is 1 of 5 equal parts.

    Final answer:$180

    Why: Write an equation for the cost: 0.2 × c = 36 Divide both sides by 0.2: c = 36 ÷ 0.2 = 180 Check: 5 parts of $36: 5 × 36 = 180

  6. 6Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    9 is 30% of a number. Ella writes 0.3 × 9 = 2.7 and says the number is 2.7. Find the correct number.

    Hint 9 is the part. Should the whole be bigger or smaller than 9?

    Final answer:30

    Why: Divide the part by the decimal: 9 ÷ 0.3 = 30 Check by multiplying back: 0.3 × 30 = 9
