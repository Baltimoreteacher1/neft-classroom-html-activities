Lesson 3.4Interpreting Points on Ratio Graphs

Apply Day · Version A · Answer KeySupported practice

Learning target I can graph the values from a ratio table as points on the coordinate plane.

  1. 1Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    Each bike has 2 wheels. Bikes are x and wheels are y. What is the y-value of the point for 4 bikes?

    Hint Multiply the bikes by 2.

    Final answer:8

    Why: Multiply the bikes by 2: 4 × 2 = 8 Write the ordered pair: (4, 8)

  2. 2Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    Each pack has 4 pencils. Packs are x and pencils are y. The point for 3 packs is (3, ?). What is the missing number?

    Hint The missing number is y. Multiply the packs by 4.

    Final answer:12

    Why: Multiply the packs by 4: 3 × 4 = 12 Write the ordered pair: (3, 12)

  3. 3Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    Each table seats 6 students. Tables are x and students are y. What is the y-value of the point for 4 tables?

    Hint Multiply the tables by 6.

    Final answer:24

    Why: Multiply the tables by 6: 4 × 6 = 24 Write the ordered pair: (4, 24)

Learning target I can graph the values from a ratio table as points on the coordinate plane.

  1. 1Circle the letter of the best answer. Show how you know.

    A recipe uses 4 cups of flour for every 1 cup of sugar. Sugar is x and flour is y. Which point is NOT on the graph?

    1. A(3, 7)
    2. B(2, 8)
    3. C(3, 12)
    4. D(0, 0)

    Answer: A — (3, 7)

    Why: Find y for x = 3: 3 × 4 = 12 Compare with the point (3, 7): 7 ≠ 12

  2. 2Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    The points (1, 6) and (2, 12) are on a graph of equivalent ratios. What is the y-value when x = 5?

    Final answer:30

    Why: Read y for x = 1: (1, 6) → y is 6 times x Multiply 5 by 6: 5 × 6 = 30

  3. 3Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    Each pack has 3 juice boxes. Packs are x and juice boxes are y. What is the y-value of the point for 4 packs?

    Final answer:12

    Why: Multiply the packs by 3: 4 × 3 = 12 Write the ordered pair: (4, 12)

Lesson 3.4Interpreting Points on Ratio Graphs

Apply Day · Challenge · Answer KeyExtension

Learning target I can graph the values from a ratio table as points on the coordinate plane.

  1. 1Circle the letter of the best answer. Show how you know.

    A ratio table pairs 1 hour with 45 miles, 2 hours with 90 miles, and 3 hours with 135 miles. Which ordered pair belongs on the graph of this relationship?

    1. A(4, 180)
    2. B(4, 45)
    3. C(4, 140)
    4. D(180, 4)

    Answer: A — (4, 180)

    Why: The relationship is 45 miles per hour, so 4 hours pairs with 4 × 45 = 180 miles, giving the point (4, 180).

  2. 2Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    A graph of equivalent ratios goes through (0, 0) and (6, 4). Lee says the point with y = 10 is (12, 10), because he added 6 to both numbers. What is the correct x-value?

    Final answer:15

    Why: Find how x compares to y: 6 ÷ 4 = 1.5 Multiply y = 10 by 1.5: 10 × 1.5 = 15 Write the point: (15, 10)

  3. 3Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    The points (3, 6) and (5, 10) are on a graph of equivalent ratios. What is the y-value when x = 9?

    Final answer:18

    Why: Find y for x = 1: 6 ÷ 3 = 2 Multiply 9 by 2: 9 × 2 = 18
