How we did it in class today

  1. Producer A wrote 3x + 2x + 10 and Producer B wrote 5x + 10. Are they equivalent?
  2. I test x = 4. For A: 3(4) + 2(4) + 10 = 12 + 8 + 10 = 30.
  3. For B: 5(4) + 10 = 20 + 10 = 30.
  4. Both give 30, and 3x + 2x is just 5x, so they are equivalent.

If they get stuck...

Watch out for this: A common mistake in Equivalent Expressions is adding the exponents instead of the coefficients when combining like terms — writing 3x + 2x as 5x² instead of 5x. Remember: 3x means 3 groups of x and 2x means 2 more groups of x, so you add the coefficients (3 + 2 = 5) and keep the variable's power the same (x¹, not x²).

Try asking: "Can you show me how you got that? Did we miss a step?"
