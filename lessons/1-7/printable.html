🚀 Launch

The space station has 18.9 kilograms of a rare mineral sample that must be divided equally among 6.3-kilogram storage pods. How many pods can be completely filled?

How do you divide with decimals?

Dividing splits a total into equal groups. When you divide by a decimal, you first make the divisor (the number you divide by) a whole number, so the division is easier.

Key idea: Move the decimal point the same number of places in BOTH the divisor and the dividend; the answer stays the same.

Watch me

  • I want 18.9 ÷ 6.3. The divisor is 6.3, and I want it to be a whole number.
  • I move the decimal point 1 place right in the divisor: 6.3 becomes 63.
  • I must move the dividend the same 1 place right: 18.9 becomes 189. Now the problem is 189 ÷ 63.
  • I divide: 189 ÷ 63 = 3. So 18.9 ÷ 6.3 = 3.
  • I check by multiplying: 6.3 × 3 = 18.9. Correct — 3 pods can be filled.

Let's try together

  • Let's do 8.4 ÷ 2.1. How many places do we move to make 2.1 a whole number? (1 place, so 2.1 becomes 21.)
  • Move the dividend the same way: 8.4 becomes 84. What is the new problem? (84 ÷ 21.)
  • Divide: 84 ÷ 21 = 4. So 8.4 ÷ 2.1 = 4. Check: 2.1 × 4 = 8.4.

Now it's your turn

  • Next, you will rewrite decimal divisions as whole-number divisions and solve them on your own.
  • Remember: move the point the same number of places in both numbers.
