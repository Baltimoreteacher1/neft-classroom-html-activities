How does reflection change the coordinates?

How do you reflect a point across an axis?

A reflection is a flip of a point over a line, like a mirror image. The axis you flip over decides which coordinate changes its sign.

Key idea: Reflect over the x-axis: the y-coordinate changes sign. Reflect over the y-axis: the x-coordinate changes sign.

Watch me

  • I will reflect (3, 2) over the y-axis.
  • The y-axis is the vertical line, so the point flips left or right. That changes the x-coordinate.
  • The x changes sign: 3 becomes -3. The y stays the same: 2 stays 2.
  • So the reflection of (3, 2) over the y-axis is (-3, 2).

Let's try together

  • Now let's reflect (4, -5) over the x-axis. Which coordinate changes over the x-axis? The y-coordinate.
  • What does -5 become when it changes sign? It becomes 5.
  • The x stays 4, so the reflection is what point? It is (4, 5).
