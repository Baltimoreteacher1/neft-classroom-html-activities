    (Lesson 5.6) Spot the Mistake — One Pair Was Not Doubled

    1. 1ReadA time capsule box from the net is l = 7 in, w = 5 in, h = 4 in. Find the surface area.
    2. 2Find each face pairTop/Bottom: 7×5 = 35, Front/Back: 7×4 = 28, Left/Right: 5×4 = 20
    3. 3Apply the formulaSA = 2(35) + 2(28) + 20 = 70 + 56 + 20 = 146 in² (left/right pair not doubled)
    4. 4AnswerSurface Area = 146 in²

    Which step has the mistake? Explain it and write the correct work.
