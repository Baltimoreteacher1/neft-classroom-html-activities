  1. Start at 100. What do you add to reach 105.76? (5.76, so 105.76 = 100 + 5.76)
  2. Start at 105. What do you add to reach 105.76? (0.76, so 105.76 = 105 + 0.76)
  3. Start at 110. How far down is 105.76? (4.24, so 105.76 = 110 − 4.24)
  4. Half of 105.76 is 52.88. Double is 211.52. Write two more ways. (105.76 = 52.88 × 2 and 105.76 = 211.52 ÷ 2)
  1. 1Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    A jar is full of marbles. There are too many to count one by one. One handful has about 15 marbles. About 6 handfuls fill the jar. About how many marbles are in the jar?

    1. 1How many marbles are in one handful?about 15
    2. 2How many handfuls fill the jar?about 6
    3. 3Multiply the handfuls by the marbles in each: 6 × 15.90
    Final answer:About 90 marbles are in the jar.
  2. 2Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    Decompose 54.38 in three ways. Each way must equal exactly 54.38.

    1. 1Start at 50. What do you add to reach 54.38?4.38
    2. 2Start at 60. How far down is 54.38?5.62
    3. 3Half of 54.38 is 27.19. Write 54.38 as a multiplication.54.38 = 27.19 × 2
    Final answer:54.38 = 50 + 4.38 = 60 − 5.62 = 27.19 × 2. Three ways, one number.
  3. 3Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    A box is full of paper clips. One small pile has about 25 clips. About 4 piles fill the box. About how many clips are in the box?

    Hint Count one pile. Then multiply by the number of piles.

    Final answer:About 100 clips

    Why: Count one small group: about 25 clips Multiply by the number of groups: 4 × 25 = 100

  4. 4Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    Write 208.45 as 210 minus a number. What is the number?

    Hint Find how far 208.45 is from 210.

    Final answer:1.55

    Why: Find the distance up to 210: 210 − 208.45 = 1.55 Check that it rebuilds 208.45: 210 − 1.55 = 208.45

  5. 5Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    A field has too many flowers to count. One square of the field has about 30 flowers. About 9 squares cover the field. About how many flowers are in the field?

    Hint One square is the small group you can count. How many squares are there?

    Final answer:About 270 flowers

    Why: Count one small group: about 30 flowers Multiply by the number of squares: 9 × 30 = 270

  6. 6Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    Rosa counts one group of about 10 beans. About 14 groups fill the jar. She says there are about 24 beans. What is a better estimate?

    Hint Rosa added. Is 14 groups of 10 the same as 14 + 10?

    Final answer:About 140 beans

    Why: See Rosa's mistake: 14 + 10 = 24 Multiply the groups by the group size: 14 × 10 = 140
