  1. Write the ratio of apples to oranges. (4:1)
  2. Fill the Total row. Write the ratio of apples to all the fruit. (4:5)
  3. Both ratios start with 4. Why is the second number different? (4:1 compares a part to a part. 4:5 compares a part to the whole bowl.)
  1. 1Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    A smoothie uses 2 cups of mango for every 5 cups of yogurt. Jen makes 28 cups of smoothie. How many cups of mango does she use?

    1. 1One batch has 2 cups of mango and 5 of yogurt. How many cups of smoothie?7
    2. 2What scale factor takes 7 cups to 28 cups?4
    3. 3Multiply the mango by 4. How many cups of mango?8
    Final answer:She uses 8 cups of mango and 20 cups of yogurt. Check: 8 + 20 = 28.
  2. 2Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    The ratio of boys to girls in a chorus is 4:5. There are 20 girls. How many students are in the chorus?

    1. 1What scale factor takes 5 girls to 20 girls?4
    2. 2Multiply the 4 boys by 4. How many boys?16
    3. 3Multiply all 9 students by 4. How many students?36
    4. 4Why is the answer not 4 + 5 = 9 students?9 is only the first column. The real chorus is 4 times that column.
    Final answer:There are 36 students: 16 boys and 20 girls.
  3. 3Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    A paint mix uses red paint and blue paint in a ratio of 1:3. A can holds 24 cups of the mix. How many cups of blue paint are in it?

    Final answer:18

    Why: Fill in the first column: one batch: 1 + 3 = 4 Find the scale factor: 24 ÷ 4 = 6 Multiply the blue paint by 6: 3 × 6 = 18

  4. 4Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    Chef Reyes uses 3 cups of apple juice for every 2 cups of sparkling water. She used 21 cups of apple juice. How many cups of fruit drink did she make?

    Final answer:35

    Why: Fill in the first column: one batch: 3 + 2 = 5 Find the scale factor: 21 ÷ 3 = 7 Multiply the whole drink by 7: 5 × 7 = 35

  5. 5Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    A school club has 40 members. The ratio of 6th graders to 7th graders is 3:5. How many more 7th graders than 6th graders are in the club?

    Final answer:10

    Why: Fill in the members for the first column: 3 + 5 = 8 Find the scale factor: 40 ÷ 8 = 5 Multiply both grades by 5: 3 × 5 = 15; 5 × 5 = 25 Subtract: 25 − 15 = 10

  6. 6Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    A trail mix uses 2 cups of nuts for every 3 cups of raisins. Leo makes 20 cups of trail mix. He says 20 ÷ 2 = 10, so he needs 10 cups of nuts. How many cups of nuts does he really need?

    Final answer:8

    Why: Fill in the first column: one batch: 2 + 3 = 5 Find the scale factor: 20 ÷ 5 = 4 Multiply the nuts by 4: 2 × 4 = 8
