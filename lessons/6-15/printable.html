🚀 Launch

The music studio is placing supply orders for three rehearsal rooms. Room A needs 4 microphones and 3 stands. Room B needs 2 microphones and 5 stands. Room C needs 6 microphones and 1 stand. Instead of ordering separately, you can combine: (4 + 2 + 6) microphones and (3 + 5 + 1) stands = 12 microphones and 9 stands. That's simplifying by combining like items!

How do we simplify by combining like terms?

Like terms are terms with the same variable raised to the same exponent, like 2x and 5x. To simplify an expression, you add or subtract the like terms to make it shorter.

Key idea: Combining Like Terms. 1. Like Terms: Terms that share the exact same variable part and exponent (e.g. 4x and 2x). 2. Add or subtract the coefficients of like terms; keep the variable unchanged. 3. Constants (numbers without variables) combine together separately

Watch me

  • The rooms need microphones and stands: (4 + 2 + 6) microphones and (3 + 5 + 1) stands.
  • Microphones are like items, so I add: 4 + 2 + 6 = 12 microphones.
  • Stands are like items, so I add: 3 + 5 + 1 = 9 stands.
  • In algebra this is the same as 4m + 2m + 6m + 3s + 5s + 1s = 12m + 9s.

Let's try together

  • Let's simplify 5x + 3x. Are these like terms?
  • Yes, both have x, so we add the numbers in front: 5 + 3 = 8.
  • So 5x + 3x = 8x.

Now it's your turn

  • Sort the terms into like terms and not-like terms in the activity.
  • Remember: only terms with the same variable can be combined.
