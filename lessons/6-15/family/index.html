A quick guide for families and for any student who missed class.

What students are learning

Like terms are terms with the same variable, like 2x and 5x. To simplify an expression, you add or subtract the like terms to make it shorter.

Key idea: Combining Like Terms. 1. Like Terms: Terms that share the exact same variable part and exponent (e.g. 4x and 2x). 2. Add or subtract the coefficients of like terms; keep the variable unchanged. 3. Constants (numbers without variables) combine together separately

Key words

  • Simplify Algebraic Expressions — Rewrite an expression in a shorter equivalent form by combining like terms and using properties.
    Español: Simplificar expresiones algebraicas — Reescribir una expresión en una forma equivalente más corta combinando términos semejantes y usando propiedades.
  • Like Terms — Terms with the same letter raised to the same power, like 2x and 5x.
    Español: Términos semejantes — Términos con la misma letra elevada a la misma potencia, como 2x y 5x.
  • Combine — To add or subtract terms with the same letter into one.
    Español: Combinar — Sumar o restar términos con la misma letra en uno solo.
  • Simplify — To rewrite an expression in a shorter form that is still equal to the original.
    Español: Simplificar — Reescribir una expresión en una forma más corta que sigue siendo igual a la original.
  • Coefficient — The number in front of a letter, like the 3 in 3x.
    Español: Coeficiente — El número frente a una letra, como el 3 en 3x.
  • Term — A single number, variable, or product of them, separated by + or − signs.
    Español: Término — Un solo número, variable o producto de ellos, separado por signos + o −.
  • Distributive property — Multiplying a number by everything inside the parentheses: a(b + c) = ab + ac.
    Español: Propiedad distributiva — Multiplicar un número por todo lo que está dentro del paréntesis: a(b + c) = ab + ac.

Example

  1. The rooms need microphones and stands: (4 + 2 + 6) microphones and (3 + 5 + 1) stands.
  2. Microphones are like items, so I add: 4 + 2 + 6 = 12 microphones.
  3. Stands are like items, so I add: 3 + 5 + 1 = 9 stands.
  4. In algebra this is the same as 4m + 2m + 6m + 3s + 5s + 1s = 12m + 9s.

Practice at home

Match each expression to its simplified form.

  1. 8x + 3x + 1
  2. 5a − 2a + 7
  3. 4n + n + 6

Answer check

  1. 8x + 3x + 1 → 11x + 1
  2. 5a − 2a + 7 → 3a + 7
  3. 4n + n + 6 → 5n + 6

Common mistake

A common mistake in Simplify Algebraic Expressions is sweeping a constant into the variable terms — for example, simplifying 6x + 4 + 2x as 12x by adding all three numbers (6 + 4 + 2 = 12) instead of recognizing that 4 has no variable and cannot combine with 6x and 2x. The correct simplified expression is 8x + 4 (combine 6x + 2x = 8x, and let the 4 stay on its own). Before you submit, check that every term you combined has the exact same variable part.

How families can help at home

  • Have them say why each step works, not just the answer.
