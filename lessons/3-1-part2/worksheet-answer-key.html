Learning target I can write and describe a ratio that compares two quantities.

  1. 1Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    A pencil box has 9 pencils and 2 erasers. The ratio of erasers to pencils is ? : 9. What is the missing number?

    Hint Erasers come first in this ratio.

    Final answer:2

    Why: Read the counts from the table: erasers : pencils Write them in order: 2:9

  2. 2Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    A vase has 5 red flowers and 4 yellow flowers. The ratio of red flowers to all the flowers is 5 to ?. What is the missing number?

    Hint All the flowers means red and yellow together.

    Final answer:9

    Why: Add to fill the Total row: 5 + 4 = 9 Write red to the total, in order: 5:9

  3. 3Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    A team has 7 players on the field and 4 players on the bench. The ratio of bench players to all the players is 4 to ?. What is the missing number?

    Hint All the players means the field and the bench together.

    Final answer:11

    Why: Add to fill the Total row: 7 + 4 = 11 Write bench players to the total, in order: 4:11

Learning target I can write and describe a ratio that compares two quantities.

  1. 1Circle the letter of the best answer. Show how you know.

    Lily has 3 cats and 4 dogs. Ben says the ratio of dogs to cats is 3:4. Which ratio of dogs to cats is correct?

    1. A4:3
    2. B3:4
    3. C4:7
    4. D3:7

    Answer: A — 4:3

    Why: Read the counts from the table: dogs : cats Write them in order: 4:3

  2. 2Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    A paint mix uses red paint and blue paint in a ratio of 1:3. A can holds 24 cups of the mix. How many cups of blue paint are in it?

    Final answer:18

    Why: Fill in the first column: one batch: 1 + 3 = 4 Find the scale factor: 24 ÷ 4 = 6 Multiply the blue paint by 6: 3 × 6 = 18

  3. 3Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    A plate has 6 cookies and 1 muffin. The ratio of cookies to all the treats is 6 to ?. What is the missing number?

    Final answer:7

    Why: Add to fill the Total row: 6 + 1 = 7 Write cookies to the total, in order: 6:7

Lesson 3.1Ratios with Tape Diagrams

Apply Day · Challenge · Answer KeyExtension

Learning target I can write and describe a ratio that compares two quantities.

  1. 1Circle the letter of the best answer. Show how you know.

    A class has 12 sixth graders and 18 seventh graders. What is the ratio of sixth graders to the TOTAL number of students, written in simplest form?

    1. A2 : 5
    2. B2 : 3
    3. C3 : 5
    4. D12 : 18

    Answer: A — 2 : 5

    Why: The total is 12 + 18 = 30. The ratio 12 : 30 divides by 6 to give 2 : 5.

  2. 2Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    A trail mix uses 2 cups of nuts for every 3 cups of raisins. Leo makes 20 cups of trail mix. He says 20 ÷ 2 = 10, so he needs 10 cups of nuts. How many cups of nuts does he really need?

    Final answer:8

    Why: Fill in the first column: one batch: 2 + 3 = 5 Find the scale factor: 20 ÷ 5 = 4 Multiply the nuts by 4: 2 × 4 = 8

  3. 3Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    A punch uses 1 cup of lemon juice for every 4 cups of water. Tom makes 30 cups of punch. How many cups of water does he use?

    Final answer:24

    Why: Fill in the first column: one batch: 1 + 4 = 5 Find the scale factor: 30 ÷ 5 = 6 Multiply the water by 6: 4 × 6 = 24
