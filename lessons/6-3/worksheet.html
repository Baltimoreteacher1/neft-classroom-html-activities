How it worksWorked examplewriting repeated multiplication as a power

These numbers are not on your problems. The steps are. Follow them with your own numbers.

Write 9 × 9 × 9 × 9 as a power. Then name its base and its exponent.

  1. Find the repeated factor. The factor is 9.
  2. Count how many times it is used. It is used 4 times.
  3. Write the base, then the exponent. 9 ^ 4
  4. Read it out loud. 9 to the 4th power

Answer: 9 × 9 × 9 × 9 = 9^4. The base is 9 and the exponent is 4.

Watch outA common mistake

A common mistake in Powers and Exponents is multiplying the base by the exponent instead of using the base as a repeated factor — for example, thinking 2³ = 2 × 3 = 6 instead of 2³ = 2 × 2 × 2 = 8. Before you submit, rewrite the power as repeated multiplication first, then multiply step by step.
