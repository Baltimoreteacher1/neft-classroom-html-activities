  1. 1

    (Lesson 9.1) Akela chooses how many hours of sunlight each tray gets. Which is the INDEPENDENT variable?

    1. AHours of sunlight
    2. BPlant height
    3. CNumber of trays
    4. DNumber of seeds

    Akela chooses the sunlight, so sunlight is subject to choice — that makes it independent.

    Watch for: Writing the equation with addition — c = 24.95 + m — instead of multiplication. The fee is charged EVERY month, so the total is the rate times the number of months; always test the equation against a row of the table.

  2. 2

    (Lesson 9.1) In Akela's experiment, which is the DEPENDENT variable?

    1. APlant height
    2. BHours of sunlight
    3. CThe kind of seed
    4. DThe size of the tray

    Height responds to how much sunlight the tray received, so height is the dependent variable.

    Watch for: Writing the equation with addition — c = 24.95 + m — instead of multiplication. The fee is charged EVERY month, so the total is the rate times the number of months; always test the equation against a row of the table.

  3. 3

    (Lesson 9.2) Football tickets cost $45 each. What is the total cost of 3 tickets?

    1. A$135
    2. B$48
    3. C$90
    4. D$45

    The total cost is 45 times the number of tickets, and 3 × 45 = 135.

    Watch for: Writing the equation with addition — c = 24.95 + m — instead of multiplication. The fee is charged EVERY month, so the total is the rate times the number of months; always test the equation against a row of the table.

  4. 4

    (Lesson 9.2) Which ordered pair belongs on the ticket graph for 2 tickets?

    1. A(2, 90)
    2. B(90, 2)
    3. C(2, 45)
    4. D(2, 47)

    The number of tickets goes first, and 2 tickets cost 2 × 45 = 90 dollars, so the point is (2, 90).

    Watch for: Writing the equation with addition — c = 24.95 + m — instead of multiplication. The fee is charged EVERY month, so the total is the rate times the number of months; always test the equation against a row of the table.
