Percent
Porcentaje		A ratio that compares a number to 100. The word means “per hundred,” and the symbol is %.
Una razón que compara un número con 100. La palabra significa “por cada cien” y su símbolo es %.		9 out of 100 students walk to school → 9% — The comparison is already out of 100.
Per hundred
Por cada cien		The meaning hidden inside the word percent: for every group of 100. It is why every percent can be written as a fraction over 100.
El significado dentro de la palabra porcentaje: por cada grupo de 100. Por eso todo porcentaje se puede escribir como fracción sobre 100.		3 out of every 10 rewritten as 30 out of 100 — It restates the ratio for a group of 100.
Decimal grid
Cuadrícula decimal		A 10-by-10 grid of 100 squares. Shading squares shows a percent directly, one square per percent.
Una cuadrícula de 10 por 10 con 100 cuadros. Sombrear cuadros muestra un porcentaje directamente, un cuadro por cada uno por ciento.		A 100-square grid with 65 squares shaded — Each square is one percent, so the shading reads directly as 65%.
Part-to-whole
Parte a todo		A comparison of one part against the whole amount. In a percent, the whole is always 100%.
Una comparación de una parte con el total. En un porcentaje, el total siempre es 100 %.		18 students out of a class of 60 — The second number is the whole the part came from.
Greater than 100%
Mayor que 100 %		More than one whole. A percent above 100 means the amount is larger than the thing it is being compared to.
Más de un entero. Un porcentaje mayor que 100 significa que la cantidad es mayor que aquello con lo que se compara.		150% of the gray kitten's weight — One whole plus another half — more than the whole.

🚀 Launch

The arcade posts every result as a percent of the weekly goal. A percent compares a number to 100 — so 40% means 40 out of every 100. Some players land under the goal, and some blow past it: a score of 150% means one and a half times the whole goal. Your job is to read and model each result as a comparison to 100.

What a percent really says

Percent means per hundred. Any part-to-whole comparison becomes a percent by rewriting it as an equivalent ratio whose second term is 100. A decimal grid makes that visible: 100 squares, one for each percent.

Key idea: Understanding Percent. Formula: Percent = Parts per 100 = Ratio of a number to 100. 1. Percent means 'per hundred' (% = /100). 2. On a 100-grid, each square represents 1% = 0.01 = 1/100. 3. Fraction to Percent: Scale fraction to a denominator of 100

Watch me turn a vote into a percent

  • Students voted on a mascot, and 4 out of every 10 voters chose the tiger. I want to know how many that is out of 100.
  • "4 out of every 10" is a ratio, so I write an equivalent ratio with 100 as the second term. Multiplying both terms by 10 gives 40 out of 100.
  • On a decimal grid of 100 squares, I shade 40 squares — one square for each percent.
  • 40 out of 100 is written 40%. So 40% of the students voted for the tiger.

Let's do one together

  • Shanice's gray kitten weighs 4 ounces. The orange kitten weighs 1.5 times as much. What percent of the gray kitten's weight is the orange kitten's weight?
  • The gray kitten is the whole, so its weight is 100%.
  • Draw a tape diagram: one full tape is 100%. The orange kitten needs 1.5 tapes — one whole tape plus half of another.
  • One whole is 100% and half a whole is 50%, so 100% + 50% = 150%. The orange kitten weighs 150% of the gray kitten's weight — more than one whole, which matches it being the heavier kitten.

Your turn

  • In the same vote, 23 out of every 100 voters chose the eagle.
  • Write that comparison as a percent, and say how you would shade it on a decimal grid.
  • Now a player scores twice the weekly goal. Is that percent more or less than 100%?
  • Explain how you know, using the words whole and per hundred.
