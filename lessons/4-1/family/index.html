Unit 4, Lesson 1: Understand Percent6.AT.4

A quick guide for families and for any student who missed class.

What students are learning

Percent means per hundred. Any part-to-whole comparison becomes a percent by rewriting it as an equivalent ratio whose second term is 100. A decimal grid makes that visible: 100 squares, one for each percent.

Key idea: Understanding Percent. Formula: Percent = Parts per 100 = Ratio of a number to 100. 1. Percent means 'per hundred' (% = /100). 2. On a 100-grid, each square represents 1% = 0.01 = 1/100. 3. Fraction to Percent: Scale fraction to a denominator of 100

Key words

  • Understand Percent — A percent is a ratio that compares a number to 100, so every percent answers the question “how many out of 100?”
    Español: Comprender el porcentaje — Un porcentaje es una razón que compara un número con 100, así que todo porcentaje responde “¿cuántos de cada 100?”
  • Percent — A ratio that compares a number to 100. The word means “per hundred,” and the symbol is %.
    Español: Porcentaje — Una razón que compara un número con 100. La palabra significa “por cada cien” y su símbolo es %.
  • Per hundred — The meaning hidden inside the word percent: for every group of 100. It is why every percent can be written as a fraction over 100.
    Español: Por cada cien — El significado dentro de la palabra porcentaje: por cada grupo de 100. Por eso todo porcentaje se puede escribir como fracción sobre 100.
  • Decimal grid — A 10-by-10 grid of 100 squares. Shading squares shows a percent directly, one square per percent.
    Español: Cuadrícula decimal — Una cuadrícula de 10 por 10 con 100 cuadros. Sombrear cuadros muestra un porcentaje directamente, un cuadro por cada uno por ciento.
  • Part-to-whole — A comparison of one part against the whole amount. In a percent, the whole is always 100%.
    Español: Parte a todo — Una comparación de una parte con el total. En un porcentaje, el total siempre es 100 %.
  • Greater than 100% — More than one whole. A percent above 100 means the amount is larger than the thing it is being compared to.
    Español: Mayor que 100 % — Más de un entero. Un porcentaje mayor que 100 significa que la cantidad es mayor que aquello con lo que se compara.

Example

  1. Students voted on a mascot, and 4 out of every 10 voters chose the tiger. I want to know how many that is out of 100.
  2. "4 out of every 10" is a ratio, so I write an equivalent ratio with 100 as the second term. Multiplying both terms by 10 gives 40 out of 100.
  3. On a decimal grid of 100 squares, I shade 40 squares — one square for each percent.
  4. 40 out of 100 is written 40%. So 40% of the students voted for the tiger.

Practice at home

  1. Four out of every ten voters chose the tiger. What percent chose the tiger, and how do you know?

Answer check

  1. Four out of every ten voters chose the tiger. What percent chose the tiger, and how do you know? → 40%, because 4 out of 10 equals 40 out of 100

Common mistake

A common mistake with percent is scaling only one term of the ratio: reading 7 out of 10 as 7%. A percent compares to 100, so BOTH terms must be multiplied by the same factor — 10 × 10 = 100, so 7 × 10 = 70, giving 70%. Reading it as 7% describes seats that are nearly empty when they are nearly full. A second mistake is treating a percent as an amount: 150% of a 200-point goal is 1.5 × 200 = 300 points, not 150 points.

How families can help at home

  • Ask your student to teach you the example above in their own words.
  • Have them say why each step works, not just the answer.
  • Use the sentence starters below so they explain their thinking in full sentences.
