Problem: Which is the better buy: 6 bouncy balls for $4.50 or 10 bouncy balls for $8.00?

  1. 6 for $4.50 — $0.75 each
  2. 10 for $8.00 — $0.80 each
  3. They cost the same per ball
  4. Not enough information
  1. Step 1 $4.50 ÷ 6 = $0.75 each.
  2. Step 2 $8.00 ÷ 10 = $0.80 each.
  3. Step 3 $0.75 < $0.80, so 6 for $4.50 is the better buy.

✅ Answer: A. 6 for $4.50 — $0.75 each

  3. Notes 3: Comparison
  4. Notes 4: Per Unit

  7. We Try (notes): A. 3 ounces per minute — 15 ÷ 5 = 3 ounces per minute.
  8. You Try (notes): A. 105 pages — Unit rate: 42 ÷ 6 = 7 pages per minute. In 15 minutes: 7 × 15 = 105 pages.
  9. Try It 1: A. 50 miles per hour — 150 ÷ 3 = 50 miles per hour.
  10. Try It 2: A. 15 tickets for $3.00 — $0.20 each — 8 for $2.00: $2.00 ÷ 8 = $0.25 each. 15 for $3.00: $3.00 ÷ 15 = $0.20 each. $0.20 < $0.25, so 15 for $3.00 is cheaper per ticket.
  11. Exit Ticket: A. 32.5 miles per gallon — 195 ÷ 6 = 32.5 miles per gallon.
