🚀 Launch

You're the new supply manager for the arcade! You need to order tokens, prizes, and snacks from different suppliers. Each supplier offers a different package deal. Your job is to find the unit rate for each deal and figure out which supplier gives you the best value for every item you need to order.

How do I use unit rates to find the better buy?

A unit rate is the cost for just 1 item, like the price per token. When packages have different sizes, the unit rate lets you compare them fairly.

Key idea: Divide the price by the number of items to get the cost per unit. The lower cost per unit is the better buy.

Watch me

  • Fun Supply Co. sells 500 tokens for $60. I want the cost for 1 token.
  • I divide the price by the tokens: $60 ÷ 500 = $0.12.
  • So this deal is $0.12 per token. That is the unit rate.
  • Now I can compare it fairly to a deal with a different number of tokens.

Let's try together

  • Arcade Depot sells 350 tokens for $38.50. What do we divide?
  • We divide price by tokens: $38.50 ÷ 350 = ?
  • What is the cost per token, and is it lower than $0.12? ($0.11 — yes, the better buy.)

Now it's your turn

  • In the table, find the unit rate for each supplier by dividing price by quantity.
  • Mark the option with the lower unit rate as the better buy.
