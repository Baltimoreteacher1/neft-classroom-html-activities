    Which of the following is a prime number?

    1. A17
    2. B15
    3. C21
    4. D9

    17 has exactly two factors: 1 and 17. 15 = 3 × 5, 21 = 3 × 7, and 9 = 3 × 3, so they are all composite.

    Watch for: A common mistake in Prime Factorization is stopping the factor tree too soon — leaving a composite number like 4, 6, 8, or 9 in the final answer instead of continuing to split it. For example, writing 20 = 4 × 5 as the prime factorization (4 is composite) instead of finishing with 20 = 2 × 2 × 5.
