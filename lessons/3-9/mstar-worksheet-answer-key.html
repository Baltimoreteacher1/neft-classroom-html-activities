Lesson 3-9: Equivalent Ratios

Question 1Two-part question

Part A — correct answer: B

Solve the proportion 4/10 = x/25 for x.

  • A8
  • B10
  • C19
  • D62.5

The scale factor from 10 to 25 is 2.5, so x = 4 × 2.5 = 10. Cross products confirm it: 4 × 25 = 100 and 10 × 10 = 100.

  • A: That doubles the 4. The denominator went from 10 to 25, a factor of 2.5 rather than 2.
  • C: That adds the 15 that was added to the denominator. Proportions scale by multiplying.
  • D: That scales the 25 instead of the 4. Apply the factor 2.5 to the numerator.

Part B — correct answer: B

How do cross products show that two ratios are equivalent?

  • AIf a + d equals b + c, the ratios are equivalent.
  • BIf a/b = c/d, then a × d equals b × c.
  • CCross products work only when all four numbers are whole.
  • DCross products compare the two numerators only.

Multiplying both sides of a/b = c/d by b·d gives a·d = b·c, so equal cross products mean the ratios are equivalent.

Question 2Select all that apply — correct: A, B, D

Select ALL pairs of ratios that are equivalent:

  • A2/3 and 8/12
  • B5/8 and 10/16
  • C3/4 and 4/5
  • D6/9 and 2/3
  • E7/10 and 14/21

A (2×12 = 24 = 3×8), B (5×16 = 80 = 8×10), and D (6×3 = 18 = 9×2) are equivalent. C fails: 3×5 = 15 but 4×4 = 16. E fails: 7×21 = 147 but 10×14 = 140.
