Volume with Whole Number Edges

6.G.2 · Unit 10 · Lesson 1

Name   Period

Objectives / Objetivos

Content Objective: I can find the volume of a rectangular prism with whole-number edges using length × width × height.

Language Objective: I can explain my work using the words volume, rectangular prism, cubic units, and edge.

Key Idea / Idea clave

To find the volume of a box, multiply all three edges: V = length × width × height.

I Notice / Observo

  • What three measurements do you need to find the volume of a box?
  • What does volume tell us about a container?

I Wonder / Me pregunto

  • If two containers have the same volume, must they have the same shape?

Vocabulary / Vocabulario

Term / TérminoDefinition / Definición
Volume
Volumen		How much space is inside a solid shape.
Cuánto espacio hay dentro de una figura sólida.
Rectangular prism
Prisma rectangular		A solid box shape with six flat rectangle sides.
Una figura sólida en forma de caja con seis lados rectangulares.
Cubic units
Unidades cúbicas		The units used to measure space inside, like cubic inches.
Las unidades para medir el espacio interior, como pulgadas cúbicas.
Length, width, height
Largo, ancho, altura		How long, how wide, and how tall a box is.
Qué tan largo, ancho y alto es una caja.
Net
Plantilla (desarrollo plano)		A flat shape that folds up into a solid.
Una figura plana que se dobla y forma un sólido.
Edge
Arista		The line where two flat sides of a solid meet.
La línea donde se unen dos lados planos de un sólido.
