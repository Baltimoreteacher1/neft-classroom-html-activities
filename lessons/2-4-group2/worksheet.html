    The Quartile-Is-One-Value Mistake

    1. 1Data (ordered)Eagles' game scores: 4, 8, 10, 14, 18, 22, 26, 30 (8 values)
    2. 2MedianWith 8 values, Median = (14 + 18) ÷ 2 = 16 ✓
    3. 3Q1Lower half = 4, 8, 10, 14 → Q1 = 10
    4. 4Q3Upper half = 18, 22, 26, 30 → Q3 = (22 + 26) ÷ 2 = 24

    Which step has the mistake? Explain it and write the correct work.
