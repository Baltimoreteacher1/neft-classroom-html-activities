The big idea: 5.4: Break the figure into simple shapes. ADD the areas when shapes are joined; SUBTRACT when a piece is cut out. • 5.5: To find the volume of a box, multiply all three edges: V = length × width × height. • 5.6: A box has 3 pairs of matching faces, so add up all 6 faces to get the surface area. • 5.7: No matter the prism, find the area of every face and add them all together. • 5.8: Surface area of a pyramid = base area + the area of all the triangular lateral faces. • 5.9: A regular polygon splits into the same number of triangles as it has sides, so total area = (one triangle's area) × (number of sides). • 5.10: A fractional edge does not change the rule: still multiply length × width × height.

  4. Lesson 5.7 — Determine Surface Area of Prisms: No matter the prism, find the area of every face and add them all together.
  5. Lesson 5.8 — Determine Surface Area of Pyramids: Surface area of a pyramid = base area + the area of all the triangular lateral faces.
  6. Lesson 5.9 — Area of Regular Polygons: A regular polygon splits into the same number of triangles as it has sides, so total area = (one triangle's area) × (number of sides).
