Part B — Test-Format Questions

Scoring notes and distractor rationales below each item.

Question 7Two-part question

Part A — correct answer: D

What is the value of 6 ÷ 3/4?

  • A2
  • B2/9
  • C4 1/2
  • D8

Write 6 as 6/1 and multiply by the reciprocal of 3/4: 6/1 × 4/3. Cross-cancel the 6 and the 3 to get 2/1 × 4/1 = 8.

  • A: That divides 6 by the 3 alone and stops. The divisor is 3/4, so multiply by 4/3.
  • B: Both fractions were flipped. Keep 6/1 as it is and flip only the divisor.
  • C: That multiplies 6 by 3/4. Flip the divisor to 4/3 before multiplying.

Part B — correct answer: B

Which reasoning shows why your answer to Part A is correct?

  • AFlip the whole number instead of the divisor: 1/6 × 4/3 = 4/18 = 2/9.
  • BWrite 6 as 6/1 and multiply by the reciprocal of the divisor: 6/1 × 4/3. Cross-cancel the common factor 3 to get 2/1 × 4/1 = 8.
  • CMultiply straight across without using the reciprocal: 6/1 × 3/4 = 18/4 = 4 1/2.
  • DDivide 6 by the numerator of the divisor and ignore the denominator: 6 ÷ 3 = 2.

Division by a fraction is rewritten as multiplication by the reciprocal of the divisor. Cross-cancelling the shared factor 3 before multiplying gives 8.

Question 8Select all that apply — correct: A, B, C, E

Select ALL statements that are true about the expression 10 ÷ 2/5.

  • AThe reciprocal of the divisor 2/5 is 5/2.
  • BThe expression can be rewritten as 10/1 × 5/2.
  • CThe quotient is 25.
  • DThe quotient is 4.
  • EThe quotient is greater than 10 because the divisor is less than 1.
  • FThe reciprocal of 2/5 is 2/5.

Write 10 as 10/1 and multiply by the reciprocal 5/2: 10/1 × 5/2 = 50/2 = 25. A quotient of 4 comes from multiplying by 2/5 instead of its reciprocal. Dividing by a number less than 1 gives a quotient larger than the dividend.

Part C — Show Your Thinking

Model answer and scoring guidance.

Question 9Written response · 2 points

A pizza shop has 8 whole pizzas. Each serving is 2/3 of a pizza. How many servings can they make? Write and solve an equation, then explain why the answer is greater than 8.

Model answer: The question asks how many 2/3-pizza servings fit in 8 pizzas, so the equation is 8 ÷ 2/3. Keep, change, flip gives 8 × 3/2 = 24/2 = 12 servings. The count beats 8 because each serving is smaller than a whole pizza, so every pizza makes more than one serving.

Award 2 points for a complete explanation with correct mathematics, 1 point for a correct answer with thin reasoning, 0 for an unrelated response.
