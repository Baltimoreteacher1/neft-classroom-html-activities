Divide Multi-Digit Numbers Using an Algorithm

6.NOS.2 · Unit 2 · Lesson 6

Name   Period

Objectives / Objetivos

Content Objective: I can divide multi-digit whole numbers and explain the meaning of the quotient and remainder.

Language Objective: I can explain my division using the words dividend, divisor, quotient, and remainder.

Key Idea / Idea clave

Algorithm: A step-by-step set of rules or instructions to solve a math problem. Long Division Algorithm: D (Divide), M (Multiply), S (Subtract), B (Bring Down). Formula: Divisor ) Dividend = Quotient R Remainder. 1. D - Divide: write quotient digit above the bar. 2. M - Multiply: digit × divisor. 3. S - Subtract: find the difference. 4. B - Bring down: next digit from dividend. 5. Repeat: continue until no digits remain (leftover = remainder). Example: 1,344 ÷ 12 = 112

I Notice / Observo

  • What total amount needs to be divided?
  • How many groups are the supplies being split into?

I Wonder / Me pregunto

  • Will each section get the same amount with none left over?
