Divide Multi-Digit Numbers Using an Algorithm

6.NOS.2 · Unit 2 · Lesson 6

Name   Period

Objectives / Objetivos

Content Objective: I can divide multi-digit whole numbers and explain the meaning of the quotient and remainder.

Language Objective: I can explain my division using the words dividend, divisor, quotient, and remainder.

Key Idea / Idea clave

Multi-Digit Long Division Algorithm. Formula: Divisor ) Dividend = Quotient R Remainder. 1. Divide: write quotient digit above the bar. 2. Multiply: digit × divisor. 3. Subtract: find the difference. 4. Bring down: next digit from dividend. 5. Repeat: continue until no digits remain (leftover = remainder). Example: 1,344 ÷ 12 = 112

I Notice / Observo

  • What total amount needs to be divided?
  • How many groups are the supplies being split into?

I Wonder / Me pregunto

  • Will each section get the same amount with none left over?

  4. Find Sonia's Mistake
    1. Step 1: Problem — 1,536 ÷ 16
    2. Step 2: Divide — 16 does not fit into 1 or 15, so start with 153. 153 ÷ 16 → 9, written above the 3
    3. Step 3: Multiply — 9 × 16 = 144, written under the 153
    4. Step 4: Subtract — 153 − 144 = 19
    5. Step 5: Bring down — Bring down the 6 to make 196
    6. Step 6: Final answer — Quotient = 9 with 196 left over
