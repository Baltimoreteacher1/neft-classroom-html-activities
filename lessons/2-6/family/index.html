Unit 2, Lesson 6: Divide Multi-Digit Numbers Using an Algorithm

Standard 6.NOS.2

What students are learning Qué aprenden

Key idea: Long division splits a number into equal groups, one digit at a time. Repeat four steps — divide, multiply, subtract, bring down — until there are no digits left. Anything left over at the end is the remainder.
Idea clave: La división larga reparte un número en grupos iguales, un dígito a la vez. Repite cuatro pasos — divide, multiplica, resta, baja — hasta que no queden dígitos. Lo que sobre al final es el residuo.

Try at home Para hacer en casa

Write 288 ÷ 12 on paper. Take away easy chunks of 12: 12 × 10 = 120, then subtract. Keep taking away chunks until nothing is left. Add the chunks to get the quotient, then check by multiplying it by 12.

Escribe 288 ÷ 12 en papel. Quita trozos fáciles de 12: 12 × 10 = 120 y resta. Sigue quitando trozos hasta que no quede nada. Suma los trozos para obtener el cociente y comprueba multiplicándolo por 12.

How we did it in class today Cómo lo hicimos hoy en clase

  1. I want 1,344 ÷ 12. The dividend is 1,344 and the divisor is 12, so 12 goes outside the bracket and 1,344 goes inside.
  2. DIVIDE: 12 does not fit into 1, so I look at 13. How many 12s fit into 13? One. I write 1 above the 3.
  3. MULTIPLY: 1 × 12 = 12. I write 12 underneath the 13.
  4. SUBTRACT: 13 − 12 = 1. That is what is left so far.
  5. BRING DOWN: I bring the next digit, 4, down next to the 1 to make 14. Now I repeat the cycle.
  6. REPEAT: 14 ÷ 12 → 1 (written above the 4), 1 × 12 = 12, 14 − 12 = 2, and I bring down the last 4 to make 24.
  7. REPEAT: 24 ÷ 12 → 2 (written above the last 4), 2 × 12 = 24, 24 − 24 = 0. Nothing is left to bring down, so the remainder is 0.
  8. The digits I wrote are 1, 1, 2, so 1,344 ÷ 12 = 112. I check by multiplying: 12 × 112 = 1,344.

Watch out for this: Thinking the quotient is just the first chunk you took away. After the first subtraction you must keep dividing what is left, then add every chunk.
Atención: Pensar que el cociente es solo el primer trozo que quitaste. Después de la primera resta debes seguir dividiendo lo que queda y luego sumar todos los trozos.
