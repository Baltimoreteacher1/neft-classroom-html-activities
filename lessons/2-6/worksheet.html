📕 Mathematical Word Bank & Spanish Cognates / Banco de Palabras
Divide Multi-Digit Numbers (Dividir números de varias cifras) — Separate a multi-digit number into equal groups to find a quotient.
Dividend (Dividendo) — The number you are splitting up in a division problem.
Divisor (Divisor) — The number you split by in a division problem.
Quotient (Cociente) — The answer when you divide.
Remainder (Residuo) — What is left over when a number does not divide evenly.
Long division (División larga) — A method that repeats four steps — divide, multiply, subtract, bring down — until there are no digits left to bring down.
Bring down (Bajar el dígito) — The fourth step of long division: move the next digit of the dividend down next to what is left, then start the cycle again.

    Spot the Mistake in 1,134 ÷ 9

    1. 1Problem:Divide 1,134 ÷ 9 with long division. Dividend = 1,134, divisor = 9.
    2. 2Divide, Multiply, Subtract:9 does not fit into 1, so start with 11. 11 ÷ 9 → 1. 1 × 9 = 9, and 11 − 9 = 2
    3. 3Bring down, repeat:Bring down the 3 to make 23. 23 ÷ 9 → 2. 2 × 9 = 18, and 23 − 18 = 5
    4. 4Final answer:Quotient = 12, remainder = 5
