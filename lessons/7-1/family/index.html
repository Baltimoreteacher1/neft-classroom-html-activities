Unit 7, Lesson 1: Explore Integers and Their Opposites6.NOS.6

A quick guide for families and for any student who missed class.

What students are learning

Counting numbers, their opposites, and 0 are integers. A negative integer is less than zero and is written with a − sign. Opposites are the same distance from 0, but on opposite sides of the number line.

Key idea: Integers & Opposites. 1. Integers: Positive whole numbers, negative whole numbers, and zero (..., -2, -1, 0, 1, 2, ...). 2. Opposites: Numbers that are the same distance from 0 in opposite directions (e.g. -5 and 5). 3. The opposite of the opposite of a number is the number itself: -(-a) = a

Key words

  • Integer — The counting numbers, their opposites, and 0.
    Español: Número entero — Los números para contar, sus opuestos y el 0.
  • Opposite — A number that is the same distance from 0 as another number, but on the other side of 0.
    Español: Opuesto — Un número que está a la misma distancia de 0 que otro número, pero del otro lado del 0.
  • Negative number — A number less than 0, written with a − sign. On a number line it sits to the left of 0.
    Español: Número negativo — Un número menor que 0, escrito con un signo −. En la recta numérica queda a la izquierda del 0.
  • Positive number — A number greater than 0. On a number line it sits to the right of 0.
    Español: Número positivo — Un número mayor que 0. En la recta numérica queda a la derecha del 0.
  • Number line — A straight line where numbers are placed in order. It extends to the left of 0 to show values less than 0.
    Español: Recta numérica — Una recta donde los números se colocan en orden. Se extiende a la izquierda del 0 para mostrar valores menores que 0.

Example

  1. Badwater Basin is 86 meters below sea level, and sea level is defined as 0.
  2. Below 0 means a negative number, so I write the elevation as −86 meters.
  3. On a number line, I count 86 units to the LEFT of 0 and mark that point.
  4. The opposite of −86 is 86. Both are 86 units from 0, just on different sides.

Common mistake

The most common mistake in this lesson is thinking that the opposite of a negative integer is still negative — for example, saying the opposite of -7 is -7, or that the opposite of -12 is -24. Opposites are the same DISTANCE from 0 but on OPPOSITE sides of 0, so taking an opposite always changes the direction and never changes the distance: the opposite of -7 is 7 and the opposite of -12 is 12. The one exception is 0, which is 0 units from itself and is therefore its own opposite. Before you submit, count the units from 0 and check that your answer is on the other side.
