  7. We Try (notes): A. n − 7 = 18 — 'Seven less than a number' means start with the number and subtract 7: n − 7 = 18.
  8. You Try (notes): A. 3n = 21 — 'Three times' means multiply by 3, so the equation is 3n = 21.
