🚀 Launch

The lowest elevation in North America is Badwater Basin in Death Valley: 86 meters below sea level. When we measure elevation, sea level is defined as 0. So 86 meters below sea level cannot be written as plain 86 — that would put it ABOVE the water. Numbers less than zero are negative numbers, written with a − sign, so the elevation of Badwater Basin is −86 meters. On a number line, −86 is 86 units to the LEFT of 0, while 86 is 86 units to the RIGHT of 0. Those two numbers are opposites.

How do you use integers to describe quantities above and below zero?

Counting numbers, their opposites, and 0 are integers. A negative integer is less than zero and is written with a − sign. Opposites are the same distance from 0, but on opposite sides of the number line.

Key idea: Integers & Opposites. 1. Integers: Positive whole numbers, negative whole numbers, and zero (..., -2, -1, 0, 1, 2, ...). 2. Opposites: Numbers that are the same distance from 0 in opposite directions (e.g. -5 and 5). 3. The opposite of the opposite of a number is the number itself: -(-a) = a

Watch me

  • Badwater Basin is 86 meters below sea level, and sea level is defined as 0.
  • Below 0 means a negative number, so I write the elevation as −86 meters.
  • On a number line, I count 86 units to the LEFT of 0 and mark that point.
  • The opposite of −86 is 86. Both are 86 units from 0, just on different sides.

Let's try together

  • One day in January the temperature in Toronto was 9°C and in Reykjavik it was −9°C.
  • Which one is to the left of 0 on the number line? The negative sign tells us: −9.
  • How many units from 0 is 9? How many units from 0 is −9? Both are 9 units.
  • So 9 and −9 are opposites — same distance from 0, opposite sides.
