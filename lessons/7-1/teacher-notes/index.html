Warm-up

One day in January the temperature in Toronto was 9°C and in Reykjavik it was −9°C.

Teaching notes

Key idea: Integers & Opposites. 1. Integers: Positive whole numbers, negative whole numbers, and zero (..., -2, -1, 0, 1, 2, ...). 2. Opposites: Numbers that are the same distance from 0 in opposite directions (e.g. -5 and 5). 3. The opposite of the opposite of a number is the number itself: -(-a) = a

I do:

  1. Badwater Basin is 86 meters below sea level, and sea level is defined as 0.
  2. Below 0 means a negative number, so I write the elevation as −86 meters.
  3. On a number line, I count 86 units to the LEFT of 0 and mark that point.
  4. The opposite of −86 is 86. Both are 86 units from 0, just on different sides.

You do: In the explore, decide whether each real-world quantity is above 0, below 0, or exactly at 0, then name its opposite.

Common misconception

The most common mistake in this lesson is thinking that the opposite of a negative integer is still negative — for example, saying the opposite of -7 is -7, or that the opposite of -12 is -24. Opposites are the same DISTANCE from 0 but on OPPOSITE sides of 0, so taking an opposite always changes the direction and never changes the distance: the opposite of -7 is 7 and the opposite of -12 is 12. The one exception is 0, which is 0 units from itself and is therefore its own opposite. Before you submit, count the units from 0 and check that your answer is on the other side.

Language & learning supports

Exit ticket

Which situation is best represented by the integer -15?

  • A diver 15 meters below the surface of the water ✓ correct
  • A hill 15 meters above sea level
  • A deposit of $15 into a bank account
  • A football play with a 15-yard gain

A negative integer represents a quantity below the chosen 0. With the water surface as 0, a diver 15 meters below it is -15.

Reteach

Re-run the I-do example with smaller numbers and have students narrate each step using the key idea.

Extension

To make a first down, a football team must gain at least 10 yards from where they started, a spot called the line of scrimmage. The line of scrimmage is 0: a gain moves the team above 0 and a loss moves the team below 0. During one series, a team had a 10-yard loss, a 4-yard gain, a 3-yard loss, and a 21-yard gain.
