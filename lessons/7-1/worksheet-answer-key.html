  1. 1 MULTIPLE CHOICE

    Badwater Basin is 86 meters below sea level, and sea level is 0. Which integer represents that elevation?

    1. A-86
    2. B86
    3. C0
    4. D-68

    💡 Solution Rationale: Below sea level means less than 0, so the elevation is written with a negative sign: -86 meters.

    ⚠️ Watch for (Misconception): The most common mistake in this lesson is thinking that the opposite of a negative integer is still negative — for example, saying the opposite of -7 is -7, or that the opposite of -12 is -24. Opposites are the same DISTANCE from 0 but on OPPOSITE sides of 0, so taking an opposite always changes the direction and never changes the distance: the opposite of -7 is 7 and the opposite of -12 is 12. The one exception is 0, which is 0 units from itself and is therefore its own opposite. Before you submit, count the units from 0 and check that your answer is on the other side.

      Spot the Mistake: The Opposite of -7

      1. 1Problem:Name the opposite of -7.
      2. 2Student thinking:The number starts out negative, and taking the opposite of a negative number keeps it negative, so the answer stays on the left side of 0.
      3. 3Student answer:The opposite of -7 is -7.

      Key Analysis: Mistake is in Step 2. Correct Work: Opposites are the same distance from 0 but on OPPOSITE sides of 0. -7 sits 7 units to the left of 0, so its opposite must sit 7 units to the RIGHT of 0. That number is 7. The only integer that is its own opposite is 0, because 0 is zero units from itself.. Misconception: The "Student thinking" step invents a rule that opposites keep their sign. The definition says the two numbers sit on opposite sides of 0, so taking an opposite always changes the direction: from the left side to the right side. The distance never changes, which is why -7 and 7 are both 7 units from 0.

    5. 5 ERROR ANALYSIS

      Spot the Mistake: Reykjavik's Temperature Swing

      1. 1Problem:At 6 a.m. in Reykjavik the temperature was -12°C. At 2 p.m. it was the opposite of the 6 a.m. temperature. What was the temperature at 2 p.m.?
      2. 2Student thinking:The opposite of a temperature should still be a cold temperature, so I keep the negative sign and just make the number bigger. That gives a temperature farther from 0.
      3. 3Student answer:The temperature at 2 p.m. was -24°C.

      Which step contains the error? Explain the mathematical misconception and write the correct calculation below.

      Key Analysis: Mistake is in Step 2. Correct Work: An opposite never changes the distance from 0 — it changes only the side. -12 is 12 units to the left of 0, so its opposite is 12 units to the RIGHT of 0, which is 12°C. The 2 p.m. temperature was 12°C. (If you then take the opposite again for 11 p.m., you come back to -12°C.). Misconception: The "Student thinking" step changes the distance from 0 instead of the side, so it produces a number twice as far from zero rather than its mirror image. The definition of opposite fixes the distance at 12 units and moves the point across 0, giving 12°C.

    4. 4 OPEN RESPONSE

      Describe two different real-world situations that use negative integers. For each one, tell exactly what 0 means in that situation, write one negative integer it could produce, and explain what the opposite of that integer would mean in the same situation.

      💡 Exemplar Response: Key Terms to Look For: integer, opposite, negative, positive, zero, sea level, temperature, below, above Explanation: Every situation that uses negative integers has a chosen zero. With elevation, 0 is sea level, so -30 meters means 30 meters below the surface and its opposite, 30, means 30 meters above it. With temperature, 0 is zero degrees, so -5°C is five degrees below zero and its opposite, 5°C, is five degrees above. In both cases the opposite is the same distance from 0, just measured in the other direction, which is what makes it the mirror image of the original quantity.
