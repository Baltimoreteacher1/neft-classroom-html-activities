Answer Key & Teacher Guide

  1. Notes 1: Composite Figure
  2. Notes 2: Decompose
  3. Notes 3: Add
  4. Notes 4: Subtract
  5. Notes 5: Formula
  6. We Try (notes): A. 130 sq ft — Patio = 15 × 10 = 150 sq ft. Cutout = 5 × 4 = 20 sq ft. Remaining = 150 − 20 = 130 sq ft.
  7. You Try (notes): A. 23 sq units — 5 × 3 = 15. 2 × 4 = 8. Total = 15 + 8 = 23 sq units.
  8. Try It 1: A. 75 sq ft — First rectangle = 9 x 6 = 54 sq ft. Second rectangle = 7 x 3 = 21 sq ft. Joined L-shape, so add: 54 + 21 = 75 sq ft.
  9. Try It 2: A. 172 sq ft — Large rectangle = 16 x 12 = 192 sq ft. Cutout closet = 5 x 4 = 20 sq ft. A piece is removed, so subtract: 192 - 20 = 172 sq ft.
  10. Exit Ticket: A. 75 sq ft — Area 1 = 9 x 7 = 63 sq ft. Area 2 = 3 x 4 = 12 sq ft. Total = 63 + 12 = 75 square feet.
