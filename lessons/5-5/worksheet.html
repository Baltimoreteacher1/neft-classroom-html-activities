6.GR.1 Level 0 — Answer Key

Area of Composite Figures

Answer Key

Name: Date:
  1. 1

    An L-shaped room is made of two rectangles: 10 ft × 6 ft and 4 ft × 3 ft. What is the total area?

    1. A72 sq ft
    2. B60 sq ft
    3. C12 sq ft
    4. D72 ft

    Area 1 = 10 × 6 = 60 sq ft. Area 2 = 4 × 3 = 12 sq ft. Total = 60 + 12 = 72 sq ft.

    Watch for: A common mistake in Area of Composite Figures is adding every piece even when a piece has been cut out of the figure. For a 10 ft by 8 ft rectangle with a 3 ft by 2 ft rectangular notch cut from one corner, students compute the full rectangle 10 × 8 = 80 sq ft and stop, or they add the notch instead of removing it (80 + 6 = 86 sq ft) instead of subtracting it: 80 − (3 × 2) = 80 − 6 = 74 sq ft. Before submitting, check whether each piece is part of the figure (add it) or missing from the figure (subtract it).

  2. 2

    A rectangular patio is 15 ft × 10 ft with a 5 ft × 4 ft rectangular flower bed cut out. What is the remaining area?

    1. A130 sq ft
    2. B150 sq ft
    3. C20 sq ft
    4. D170 sq ft

    Patio = 15 × 10 = 150 sq ft. Cutout = 5 × 4 = 20 sq ft. Remaining = 150 − 20 = 130 sq ft.

    Watch for: A common mistake in Area of Composite Figures is adding every piece even when a piece has been cut out of the figure.   3. 3

    Find the area of an L-shape made of a 5×3 rectangle and a 2×4 rectangle.

    1. A23 sq units
    2. B15 sq units
    3. C8 sq units
    4. D20 sq units

    5 × 3 = 15. 2 × 4 = 8. Total = 15 + 8 = 23 sq units.

For a 10 ft by 8 ft rectangle with a 3 ft by 2 ft rectangular notch cut from one corner, students compute the full rectangle 10 × 8 = 80 sq ft and stop, or they add the notch instead of removing it (80 + 6 = 86 sq ft) instead of subtracting it: 80 − (3 × 2) = 80 − 6 = 74 sq ft.     When should you subtract areas instead of adding them to find the area of a composite figure?

    1. AWhen a piece is cut out or removed from a larger shape
    2. BWhen the shapes overlap
    3. CWhen one shape is larger than the other
    4. DAlways subtract — never add

    Subtract when a piece is removed from a larger shape (like a window cut from a wall). Add when two shapes are joined together (like an L-shape).

Both rectangle areas are correct — 8 × 5 = 40 and 4 × 3 = 12 — but the last step multiplies them. Multiplying is how you get the area of one rectangle from its length and width; combining two joined pieces means adding, so the total is 40 + 12 = 52 sq ft.
