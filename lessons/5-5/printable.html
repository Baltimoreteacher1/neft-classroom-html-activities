Your team is building time capsule containers to store class memories. Each container is a rectangular prism, and you need to figure out how much each one can hold. That means calculating the volume using whole number dimensions!

How do we find the volume of a box?

Volume is how much space is inside a 3D shape. For a rectangular prism (a box), volume = length × width × height, measured in cubic units.

Key idea: Volume of Rectangular Prisms (Whole Numbers). Formula: V = Length × Width × Height = Base Area × Height (V = l · w · h = B · h). 1. Identify the length, width, and height of the box. 2. Multiply base area (l × w) by height (h). 3. Label answer in cubic units (e.g. in³, cm³, ft³)

Watch me

  • My time capsule box is 6 long, 4 wide, and 3 tall. I write the formula: V = length × width × height.
  • I put in the numbers: V = 6 × 4 × 3.
  • First I multiply two of them: 6 × 4 = 24.
  • Then I multiply by the last one: 24 × 3 = 72.
  • So the volume is 72 cubic units. I use cubic units because volume fills space in 3 directions.

Let's try together

  • Now a smaller box: 5 long, 2 wide, 2 tall. What formula do we use?
  • Let's put in the numbers: V = 5 × 2 × 2. What is 5 × 2?
  • We got 10. Now multiply 10 × 2. What is the volume?
  • Yes, V = 20 cubic units. Don't forget the cubic units!
