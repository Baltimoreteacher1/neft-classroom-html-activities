    Watch for: A common mistake in Divide Mixed Numbers is dividing the whole-number parts and fraction parts separately instead of converting first — for example, treating 4 1/2 ÷ 1 1/2 as (4 ÷ 1) + (1/2 ÷ 1/2) = 5. Always convert each mixed number to one improper fraction first, then divide using Keep, Change, Flip: 4 1/2 ÷ 1 1/2 = 9/2 ÷ 3/2 = 9/2 × 2/3 = 3.

  6. 6

    Spot the Common Mistake

    1. 1Problem1 1/2 ÷ 3/4
    2. 2Convert1 1/2 = 3/2
    3. 3Keep, Change, Flip3/2 × 3/4
    4. 4Multiply9/8 = 1 1/8

    Which step has the mistake? Explain it and write the correct work.

    The mistake is in Step 3. Correct work: The student forgot to flip the divisor. Keep, Change, Flip means 3/4 becomes 4/3. So 3/2 ÷ 3/4 = 3/2 × 4/3 = 12/6 = 2, not 1 1/8. The conversion 1 1/2 = 3/2 is fine, but at the Keep, Change, Flip step the student changed the sign to × without flipping the divisor, writing 3/2 × 3/4. Dividing by 3/4 means multiplying by its reciprocal 4/3, so 3/2 × 4/3 = 12/6 = 2, not 1 1/8.

  1. 1

    Find the Mixed Number Error

    1. 1Problem2 1/3 ÷ 1/2
    2. 2Convert2 1/3 = 6/3
    3. 3Keep, Change, Flip6/3 × 2/1
    4. 4Multiply12/3 = 4

    Which step has the mistake? Explain it and write the correct work.

    The mistake is in Step 2. Correct work: 2 1/3 = 7/3 (not 6/3). Multiply: 2 × 3 = 6, then ADD the numerator 1 to get 7. So 7/3 × 2/1 = 14/3 = 4 2/3. The Convert step is wrong: the student wrote 2 1/3 = 6/3, multiplying 2 × 3 but never adding the numerator. The correct conversion is (2 × 3 + 1)/3 = 7/3, so 7/3 × 2/1 = 14/3 = 4 2/3.

  2. 2

    A detective has a rope that is 5 1/4 feet long. She needs to cut it into pieces that are each 3/4 foot for tying evidence tags. How many pieces can she cut? Write the equation, show each step (convert, keep-change-flip, multiply, simplify), and verify your answer.

    Look for: convert, 21/4, reciprocal, 4/3, multiply, 7, verify.

  3. 3

    Find Liam's Mistake

    1. 1Problem3 1/2 ÷ 1 1/4
    2. 2Convert first number3 1/2 = 7/2
    3. 3Convert second number1 1/4 = 5/4
    4. 4Divide7/2 ÷ 5/4 = 7/2 × 5/4 = 35/8 = 4 3/8

    Which step has the mistake? Explain it and write the correct work.

    The mistake is in Step 4. Correct work: Liam forgot to flip the second fraction. 7/2 ÷ 5/4 = 7/2 × 4/5 = 28/10 = 14/5 = 2 4/5. Both conversions are correct, but in the Divide step Liam changed ÷ to × and left the divisor as 5/4. Flipping it gives 7/2 × 4/5 = 28/10 = 2 4/5, which makes sense because 3 1/2 is only a little more than twice 1 1/4.
