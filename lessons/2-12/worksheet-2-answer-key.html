Part 1Practice the skillshow every step
  1. 1Circle the letter of the best answer. Show how you know.

    What is 3.2 + 4.5?

    1. A7.5
    2. B7.7
    3. C8.7
    4. D77

    Answer: B — 7.7

    Why: Line up the decimal points: 3.2 + 4.5 = 7.7. No regrouping is needed.

    Watch for: A common mistake in Multiply Decimals is counting decimal places from only one factor instead of adding the decimal places from BOTH factors. For example, in 0.4 × 0.06, a student multiplies 4 × 6 = 24, then places the decimal point using just one factor's place count (1 place), writing 0.24 instead of the correct answer: 0.4 has 1 decimal place and 0.06 has 2 decimal places, for a total of 3, so 24 becomes 0.024. Before you submit, count the decimal places in EACH factor separately and add them together — that total is how many decimal places belong in your final answer.

Part 3Explain and correctreasoning
  1. 6Find the mistake. Explain it, then show the correct work.

    Level 2 Extension — Find the Cashier's Mistake

    1. 1Order2 burrito bowls at $6.45 each: 2 × 6.45
    2. 2Multiply as whole numbers2 × 645 = 1,290
    3. 3Count decimal places6.45 has 2 decimal places → 2 total
    4. 4Place the decimal point129.0, so the total is $129.00

    Which step has the mistake? Explain what went wrong, then show the correct work.

    Mistake: Step 4. The digits 2 × 645 = 1,290 and the count of 2 decimal places are both right, so only the placement is wrong. Counting 2 places from the right end of 1,290 puts the point between the 2 and the 9, giving $12.90. Two bowls at about $6 each should cost around $13, so $129.00 is ten times too big.

    Correct work: The whole-number product 1,290 and the count of 2 decimal places are both correct. The mistake is placing the decimal point: move it 2 places from the right in 1,290 to get 12.90, so the total is $12.90 — not $129.00.
