Multiplicar decimales		Find the product of decimals, then use place value to place the decimal point.

Product
Producto		The answer when you multiply.
La respuesta cuando multiplicas.		42 from 6 × 7 — It is the result of multiplying.
Decimal point
Punto decimal		The dot that splits the whole number from the part after it.
El punto que separa el número entero de la parte que sigue.		The dot in 78.5 separating 78 from 5 tenths — It marks where the whole number ends.
Estimate
Estimar		A close answer you get by rounding first.
Una respuesta cercana que obtienes al redondear primero.		8.6% of 216 ≈ 10% of 200 = 20 — Rounded to friendly numbers, close enough to be useful.
Place value
Valor posicional		What a digit is worth based on where it sits in a number.
Lo que vale un dígito según el lugar que ocupa en el número.		The 5 in 12.5 meaning five tenths — Its worth comes from the column it sits in.

Cifras decimales		How many digits come after the decimal point.
Cuántos dígitos hay después del punto decimal.		0.4 × 0.2 = 0.08 — Both factors have one decimal place, so the product has two.

How do you multiply decimals?

To multiply decimals, you multiply the numbers as if there were no decimal points. Then you count the decimal places and put the point back in the answer.

Key idea: Multiplying Decimals. 1. Multiply the numbers as if they were whole numbers (ignore decimals during multiplication). 2. Count the total number of decimal places in both factors. 3. Place the decimal point in the product so it has that same total number of decimal places

Watch me

GIVEN DATA / MODEL
  • I want 4.5 × 12.60, the cost of 4.5 meters at $12.60 each.
  • First I multiply without points, like whole numbers: 45 × 1260 = 56,700.
  • Now I count decimal places: 4.5 has 1, and 12.60 has 2, so 1 + 2 = 3 decimal places.
  • I put the point 3 places from the right in 56,700: that gives 56.700, or $56.70.
  • I check with an estimate: 5 × $13 = $65, and $56.70 is close, so my answer is reasonable.

GIVEN DATA / MODEL
  • Let's multiply 0.4 × 0.7. First multiply like whole numbers: what is 4 × 7? (28.)
  • Now count decimal places: 0.4 has 1 and 0.7 has 1, so that is 2 places total.
  • Put the point 2 places from the right in 28: 0.28. So 0.4 × 0.7 = 0.28.
