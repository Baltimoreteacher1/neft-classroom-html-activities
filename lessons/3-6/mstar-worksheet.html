Part B — Test-Format Questions

These match the state test's formats: two-part questions, select-all, and written responses.

Question 7Two-part question

Part A

A rope measures 7 feet. Using 1 foot = 12 inches, how long is the rope in inches?

Fill in the bubble next to the one best answer.

  • A0.58 inches
  • B7 inches
  • C19 inches
  • D84 inches

Part B

Answer Part A before Part B — Part B asks about your reasoning.

How do you know the answer to Part A should be a LARGER number than 7?

  • AConverting always makes numbers larger.
  • BMultiplying always makes numbers larger.
  • CAn inch is smaller than a foot, so it takes more inches to measure the same length.
  • DFeet and inches are in different systems.
Question 8Written response · 2 points

Type II Reasoning: Converting the Wrong Direction

Tariq converted 240 centimeters to meters. Knowing 1 meter = 100 centimeters, he wrote: '240 × 100 = 24,000 meters.'

Explain Tariq's misconception and give the correct conversion.

Write your answer in complete sentences. Show or explain your mathematical thinking.

Part C — Show Your Thinking

Answer in writing, the way the state test's constructed responses work.

Question 9Written response · 2 points

Michael's mother is setting four fence posts. Each post needs 250 ounces of concrete mix, one bag holds 80 ounces, and each bag needs 1 quart of water. How many bags and how many cups of water does she need? Show your conversions.

Write your answer in complete sentences. Show or explain your mathematical thinking.
