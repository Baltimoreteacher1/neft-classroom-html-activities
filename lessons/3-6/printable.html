The students need to use ratio reasoning to scale the recipe so every guest gets the same delicious flavor.

How do we use ratio reasoning to solve problems?

Ratio reasoning means using a scale factor or a proportion to find a missing amount. A proportion is two equal ratios. You can check it by cross-multiplying.

Key idea: Find the scale factor, then multiply both parts; cross-multiply to check a proportion.

Watch me

  • Chef Reyes's recipe serves 8 people and uses 5 cups of tomatoes. The banquet has 120 guests.
  • I find the scale factor: 120 divided by 8 = 15.
  • I multiply the tomatoes by 15: 5 x 15 = 75 cups of tomatoes.
  • To check, I can cross-multiply 5/8 = 75/120: 5 x 120 = 600 and 8 x 75 = 600.
  • The cross products match, so 75 cups is correct.

Let's try together

  • A recipe uses 2 cups of rice for every 6 servings. We need 18 servings.
  • Find the scale factor: 18 divided by 6 = 3.
  • Multiply the rice by 3: 2 x 3 = 6 cups of rice.

Now it's your turn

  • Next you will sort ratio pairs into equivalent and not-equivalent.
  • Use cross-multiplying to prove which pairs are true proportions.
