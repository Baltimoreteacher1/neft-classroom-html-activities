6.RP.3 Version A — Answer Key

Use Ratio Reasoning

Answer Key

Name: Date:
  1. 1

    A recipe uses 4 cups of broth for every 10 servings. How many cups of broth are needed for 30 servings?

    1. A12
    2. B8
    3. C14
    4. D40

    Scale factor: 30 ÷ 10 = 3. Multiply broth by 3: 4 × 3 = 12 cups.

  2. 2

    Are the ratios 6:9 and 2:3 equivalent?

    1. AYes, both simplify to 2:3
    2. BNo, 6 ≠ 2
    3. CYes, because 6 + 9 = 15
    4. DNo, because 9 - 6 ≠ 3 - 2

    Divide 6:9 by 3 to get 2:3. Since both ratios simplify to 2:3, they are equivalent.

  3. 3

    If 3 pounds of apples cost $6, how much do 7 pounds cost?

    1. A$14
    2. B$13
    3. C$10
    4. D$21

    Unit rate: $6 ÷ 3 = $2 per pound. For 7 pounds: $2 × 7 = $14.

  4. 4

    Solve the proportion: 2/5 = x/20. What is x?

    1. A8
    2. B10
    3. C4
    4. D12

    Scale factor: 20 ÷ 5 = 4. Multiply: 2 × 4 = 8. So x = 8.
