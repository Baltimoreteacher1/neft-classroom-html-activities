    2. 2Multiply as whole numbers3 × 6 = 18
    3. 3Count decimal places0.3 has 1 decimal place and 0.6 has 1 decimal place → 1 total
    4. 4Place the decimal pointMove the point 1 place in 18 to get 1.8

    Which step has the mistake? Explain it and write the correct work.

    The mistake is in Step 3. Correct work: The whole-number product 3 × 6 = 18 is correct, but the decimal-place count is wrong. 0.3 has 1 place and 0.6 has 1 place, so 1 + 1 = 2 total places — not 1. Move the point 2 places from the right in 18 to get 0.18. So 0.3 × 0.6 = 0.18.
