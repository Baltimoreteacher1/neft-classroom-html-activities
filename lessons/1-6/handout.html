Multiply Decimals

6.NOS.3 · Unit 1 · Lesson 6

Name   Period

Objectives / Objetivos

Content Objective: I can multiply decimals and place the decimal point correctly in the product.

Language Objective: I can explain my work using the words product, decimal point, estimate, and decimal places.

Key Idea / Idea clave

Multiply like whole numbers, then the product has the SAME total of decimal places as both factors combined.

I Notice / Observo

  • What two quantities are being multiplied?
  • How many total decimal places do the two factors have?

I Wonder / Me pregunto

  • How can estimating help you check if your answer is reasonable?

Vocabulary / Vocabulario

Term / TérminoDefinition / Definición
Product
Producto		The answer when you multiply.
La respuesta cuando multiplicas.
Decimal point
Punto decimal		The dot that splits the whole number from the part after it.
El punto que separa el número entero de la parte que sigue.
Estimate
Estimar		A close answer you get by rounding first.
Una respuesta cercana que obtienes al redondear primero.
Place value
Valor posicional		What a digit is worth based on where it sits in a number.
Lo que vale un dígito según el lugar que ocupa en el número.
Decimal places
Cifras decimales		How many digits come after the decimal point.
Cuántos dígitos hay después del punto decimal.
