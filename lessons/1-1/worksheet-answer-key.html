    About how many people can ride the Ferris wheel at one time if it has 20 cars that each hold about 4 people?

    1. AAbout 80
    2. BAbout 24
    3. CAbout 5
    4. DAbout 400

    Which of these is a correct way to decompose 78.5?

    1. A70 + 8.5
    2. B70 + 8.05
    3. C78 + 5
    4. D7 + 8.5

    70 + 8.5 = 78.5. Decomposing means the parts must add back to the original number.

    Watch for: Believing that 'doing math' only counts when it happens on paper in a classroom.

    1. A80 − 1.5
    2. B80 − 2.5
