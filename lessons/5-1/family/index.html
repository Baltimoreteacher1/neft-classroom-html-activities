Unit 5, Lesson 1: Area of Parallelograms6.GR.1

A quick guide for families and for any student who missed class.

What students are learning

A parallelogram is a four-sided shape with two pairs of parallel sides, like a leaning rectangle. Its area is how much flat space is inside it.

Key idea: Area of a parallelogram = base × height, where the height is the straight-up (perpendicular) distance, NOT the slanted side.

Key words

  • Area of Parallelograms — The space inside a parallelogram. Formula: A = b × h, using the perpendicular height.
    Español: Área de paralelogramos — El espacio dentro de un paralelogramo. Fórmula: A = b × h, usando la altura perpendicular.
  • Slanted side — A side that leans instead of going straight up and down. It is not the perpendicular height.
    Español: Lado inclinado — Un lado que está inclinado en vez de subir y bajar verticalmente. No es la altura perpendicular.
  • Parallelogram — A four-sided shape with two pairs of parallel sides.
    Español: Paralelogramo — Una figura de cuatro lados con dos pares de lados paralelos.
  • Base — Any side of a parallelogram can be the base. Once you pick a base, the height must be measured perpendicular (at a 90° angle) to it.
    Español: Base — Cualquier lado de un paralelogramo puede ser la base. Una vez que eliges la base, la altura debe medirse perpendicular (en un ángulo de 90°) a ella.
  • Height — The straight-up distance from the base to the top.
    Español: Altura — La distancia recta hacia arriba desde la base hasta la parte de arriba.
  • Area — How much space is inside a flat shape.
    Español: Área — Cuánto espacio hay dentro de una figura plana.
  • Composite figure — A shape made by putting two or more simple shapes together.
    Español: Figura compuesta — Una figura formada al juntar dos o más figuras simples.
  • Formula — A math rule written with symbols.
    Español: Fórmula — Una regla matemática escrita con símbolos.

Example

  1. I have a parallelogram with a base of 14 feet and a height of 9 feet.
  2. I write the formula: A = base × height.
  3. I put in the numbers: A = 14 × 9.
  4. I multiply: 14 × 9 = 126.
  5. So the area is 126 square feet. I used the height of 9, not the slanted side.

Practice at home

Match each shape to its area.

  1. Triangle 8×6
  2. Parallelogram 5×4
  3. Rectangle 7×3

Answer check

  1. Triangle 8×6 → 24 sq units
  2. Parallelogram 5×4 → 20 sq units
  3. Rectangle 7×3 → 21 sq units

Common mistake

The most common mistake in Area of Parallelograms is multiplying the base by the slanted side instead of the perpendicular height. For a parallelogram with a base of 16 ft, a perpendicular height of 9 ft, and a slanted side of 11 ft, students compute 16 × 11 = 176 square feet instead of the correct 16 × 9 = 144 square feet. The height must be the straight-up (perpendicular) distance between the base and its opposite side, not the length of the slanted side, even though the slanted side is often the number that 'looks' like it belongs in the formula.

How families can help at home

  • Ask your student to teach you the example above in their own words.
  • Have them say why each step works, not just the answer.
