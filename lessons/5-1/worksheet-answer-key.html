Part 2Apply itreal-world problems
  1. 2Circle the letter of the best answer. Show how you know.

    What is the area of a parallelogram with base 10 cm and height 7 cm?

    1. A17 sq cm
    2. B34 sq cm
    3. C70 sq cm
    4. D70 cm

    Hint You know both the base (10 cm) and the height (7 cm) — this is a direct use of the parallelogram area formula.

    Answer: C — 70 sq cm

    Why: A = b × h = 10 × 7 = 70 square centimeters.

    Watch for: The most common mistake in Area of Parallelograms is multiplying the base by the slanted side instead of the perpendicular height. For a parallelogram with a base of 16 ft, a perpendicular height of 9 ft, and a slanted side of 11 ft, students compute 16 × 11 = 176 square feet instead of the correct 16 × 9 = 144 square feet. The height must be the straight-up (perpendicular) distance between the base and its opposite side, not the length of the slanted side, even though the slanted side is often the number that 'looks' like it belongs in the formula.

  2. 3Circle the letter of the best answer. Show how you know.

    A parallelogram has an area of 54 sq ft and a base of 9 ft. What is the height?

    1. A6 ft
    2. B27 ft
    3. C45 ft
    4. D63 ft

    Hint This time you're given the area (54 sq ft) and the base (9 ft) — the height is the missing piece.

    Answer: A — 6 ft

    Why: A = b × h → 54 = 9 × h → h = 54 ÷ 9 = 6 ft.

Part 3Explain and correctreasoning
  1. 6Find the mistake. Explain it, then show the correct work.

    Spot the Common Mistake

    1. 1Read the parallelogrambase = 7 ft, height = 4 ft
    2. 2Write the formulaA = base × height
    3. 3Substitute the valuesA = 7 + 4
    4. 4Calculate the areaA = 11 sq ft

    Which step has the mistake? Explain what went wrong, then show the correct work.

    Hint Look at the substitution step. Did the student add the base and height, or multiply them?

    Mistake: Step 3. The formula line is written correctly, but the substitution step turns A = base × height into A = 7 + 4. Area counts the squares that cover the shape, so you multiply: A = 7 × 4 = 28 sq ft.

    Correct work: Area uses multiplication, not addition. The formula is A = base × height, so A = 7 × 4 = 28 sq ft.

Part 2Explain and correctreasoning
  1. 5Find the mistake. Explain it, then show the correct work.

    Spot the Common Mistake

    1. 1Read the parallelogrambase = 9 cm, slanted side = 10 cm, height = 6 cm
    2. 2Write the formulaA = base × height
    3. 3Substitute the valuesA = 9 × 10
    4. 4Calculate the areaA = 90 sq cm

    Which step has the mistake? Explain what went wrong, then show the correct work.

    Mistake: Step 3. The substitution step uses 10 cm, which is the slanted side, instead of the 6 cm height listed in the first step. Only the perpendicular height belongs in A = base × height, so A = 9 × 6 = 54 sq cm.

    Correct work: The height is 6 cm, not the slanted side of 10 cm. Substitute the perpendicular height: A = 9 × 6 = 54 sq cm. Only the straight-up height belongs in A = base × height.

  1. 1Plot and label each point on the grid.

    Plot the parallelogram and find its area.

    Answer: A, B, C, D

    Why: Plotting A(0,0), B(10,0), C(12,6), D(2,6) makes a parallelogram whose bottom side is 10 units long and whose top sits 6 units up, so A = 10 × 6 = 60 square units. It equals the 10 × 6 rectangle because sliding the slanted triangle from one end to the other turns the parallelogram into that rectangle without gaining or losing area.

Part 2Explain and correctreasoning
  1. 2Find the mistake. Explain it, then show the correct work.

    Find the Area Error

    1. 1Identify base and heightb = 11 m, slant side = 8 m, h = 6 m
    2. 2Write the formulaA = b × h
    3. 3Substitute valuesA = 11 × 8
    4. 4CalculateA = 88 sq m

    Which step has the mistake? Explain what went wrong, then show the correct work.

    Mistake: Step 3. The Substitute step plugs in the 8 m slant side for h, but the first step already labeled the height as 6 m. The slant side is longer than the true straight-up distance, so the correct area is A = 11 × 6 = 66 sq m.

    Correct work: The height is 6 m, not the slant side of 8 m. A = 11 × 6 = 66 sq m. Always use the perpendicular height, not the slanted side.

  2. 3Answer in complete sentences.

    A parallelogram has a base of 12 cm. If you double the height, the area doubles too. Explain why this happens using the formula, and give a specific example with numbers.

    Look for: formula, base, height, double, multiply, proportional

    Why: In A = b × h the base of 12 cm is a fixed factor, so the area is just 12 times whatever the height is. With h = 5, A = 12 × 5 = 60 sq cm; doubling to h = 10 gives A = 12 × 10 = 120 sq cm, exactly double, because doubling one factor doubles the product.
