6.G.1 Version A — Answer Key

Area of Parallelograms

Answer Key

Name: Date:
  1. 1

    What is the area of a parallelogram with base 10 cm and height 7 cm?

    1. A70 sq cm
    2. B34 sq cm
    3. C17 sq cm
    4. D70 cm

    A = b × h = 10 × 7 = 70 square centimeters.

  2. 2

    A parallelogram has an area of 54 sq ft and a base of 9 ft. What is the height?

    1. A6 ft
    2. B45 ft
    3. C63 ft
    4. D27 ft

    A = b × h → 54 = 9 × h → h = 54 ÷ 9 = 6 ft.

  3. 3

    What is the area of a parallelogram with base 9 cm and height 5 cm?

    1. A45 sq cm
    2. B14 sq cm
    3. C22.5 sq cm
    4. D90 sq cm

    A = b × h = 9 × 5 = 45 square centimeters.

  4. 4

    Which measurement is the height of a parallelogram?

    1. AThe perpendicular distance between the base and the opposite side
    2. BThe length of the slanted side
    3. CThe perimeter divided by 4
    4. DThe longest side

    The height is always the perpendicular (straight up-and-down) distance between the base and the opposite side, not the slanted side.
