    A car travels 55.5 miles per hour for 2.4 hours. How far does it go?

    1. A133.2 miles
    2. B13.32 miles
    3. C1,332 miles
    4. D57.9 miles

    555 × 24 = 13,320. Total decimal places: 1 + 1 = 2. Answer: 133.20 = 133.2 miles.

    Watch for: A common mistake in Multiply Decimals is counting decimal places from only one factor instead of adding the decimal places from BOTH factors. For example, in 0.4 × 0.06, a student multiplies 4 × 6 = 24, then places the decimal point using just one factor's place count (1 place), writing 0.24 instead of the correct answer: 0.4 has 1 decimal place and 0.06 has 2 decimal places, for a total of 3, so 24 becomes 0.024. Before you submit, count the decimal places in EACH factor separately and add them together — that total is how many decimal places belong in your final answer.

  2. 2

    Spot the Common Mistake — Placing the Decimal Point

    1. 1Problem1.5 × 2.4
    2. 2Multiply as whole numbers15 × 24 = 360
    3. 3Count decimal places1.5 has 1 decimal place and 2.4 has 1 decimal place → 2 total
    4. 4Place the decimal pointMove the point 2 places in 360 to get 0.36

    Which step has the mistake? Explain it and write the correct work.

    The mistake is in Step 4. Correct work: The whole-number product 15 × 24 = 360 and the count of 2 decimal places are both correct. The mistake is in placing the point: moving it 2 places from the right in 360 gives 3.60, not 0.36. So 1.5 × 2.4 = 3.6. An estimate of 2 × 2 = 4 confirms 3.6 is reasonable, while 0.36 is far too small.
