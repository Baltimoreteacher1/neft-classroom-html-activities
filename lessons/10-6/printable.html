🚀 Launch

Throughout this school year, you have seen math used in many different activities and used it to solve different kinds of problems. Today you look back at the answers you wrote to the questions in Lesson 1-1 — and see how far your math story has come.

Your math story, one year later

In Lesson 1-1 you estimated riders on a Ferris wheel, decomposed 78.5, and wrote your math story. The question now is not 'what is math?' but 'how have I changed as a doer of math?'

Key idea: Mathematical Reflection & Portfolio Culmination. 1. Synthesize grade-level mastery across Number Systems, Ratios, Expressions, Geometry, and Statistics. 2. Justify problem-solving strategies using multiple representations (equations, graphs, models). 3. Reflect on mathematical growth and communicate solutions with precision and confidence

Watch me reread my September work

  • In September I estimated the Ferris wheel: 20 cars with about 4 riders each is 20 × 4 = 80 riders — 'about 80.'
  • Now I can go further with this year's math: at 6 turns an hour, that wheel moves about 80 × 6 = 480 riders per hour.
  • Same wheel, same me — but my toolbox grew. That difference IS my math story.

Let's revisit 78.5 with this year's tools

  • In September we decomposed 78.5 as 70 + 8.5 and 80 − 1.5.
  • This year we learned new ways to say the same number: 78.5 = 157 ÷ 2.
  • And with percents: 50% of 157 = 78.5.
  • Find one more way to write 78.5 using something you learned this year.

Now you try

  • Look back at the answers you wrote in Lesson 1-1.
  • Mark one answer you would write differently today, and tell a classmate what changed.
