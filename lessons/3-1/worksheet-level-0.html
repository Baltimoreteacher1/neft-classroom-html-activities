RatioSpanish: Razón A way to compare two amounts, like 3 to 2. 3 to 5 ■■■ □□□□□
ComparisonSpanish: Comparación Looking at two or more amounts to see how they are related. 5 eggs vs. 3 eggs — which recipe uses more?
Part-to-partSpanish: Parte a parte A ratio comparing one part of a group to another part. apples to oranges
Part-to-wholeSpanish: Parte a todo A ratio comparing one part to the whole group. apples to all the fruit
Colon notationSpanish: Notación con dos puntos Writing a ratio with two dots between the numbers, like 3:2. 3 to 2 can be written as 3:2
Tape diagramSpanish: Diagrama de cintas A bar cut into equal parts that shows how two amounts in a ratio compare, and how they make up the whole. 3 equal parts of apple juice beside 2 equal parts of sparkling water — 5 parts of fruit drink in all.

How it worksWorked example

These numbers are not on your problems. The steps are. Follow them with your own numbers.

  1. Count each group. pens = 10 pencils = 4
  2. Write them in the order the question asks — pens FIRST.
  3. Write all three forms: 10 to 4 · 10 : 4 · 10/4
  4. Simplify: divide both terms by 2. 10 : 4 → 5 : 2

Answer: The ratio of pens to pencils is 5 : 2 in simplest form.

Watch outA common mistake

A common mistake in Understand Ratios is writing the ratio in the wrong order: when asked for the ratio of apple juice to sparkling water in a recipe that uses 3 cups of apple juice for every 2 cups of sparkling water, some students write 2:3 (sparkling water to apple juice) because they list the ingredients in whatever order comes to mind rather than the order the question asks for. The two ratios describe different drinks — 3:2 means 3 cups of juice for every 2 cups of water, while 2:3 would mean only 2 cups of juice for every 3 cups of water. Always match the order of your ratio to the order named in the question: "apple juice to sparkling water" means the apple juice number comes first, giving 3:2.

    A recipe uses 4 cups of milk and 1 cup of cream. What is the ratio of milk to cream?

    1. A1:4
    2. B4:1
    3. C4:5
    4. D5:1
    Steps to try
    1. Re-read the question: it asks for milk to cream, in that exact order.
    2. In a ratio, the amount named first goes first. Find the milk amount and the cream amount before you write anything.
    3. Underline the two amounts: milk = 4 cups, cream = 1 cup. Write them in the same order the question names them.
    Show your work
  2. 2Circle the letter of the best answer. Show how you know.

    Chef Reyes uses 6 strawberries and 2 bananas in a smoothie. What is the part-to-whole ratio of bananas to total fruit?

    1. A2:8
    2. B2:6
    3. C6:2
    4. D8:2
    Steps to try
    1. Notice the word 'total' — this is a part-to-WHOLE ratio, not banana-to-strawberry.
    2. First find the total fruit by adding strawberries and bananas. Then compare bananas to that total.
    3. Add 6 + 2 to get the total fruit. Then write the bananas' number first, followed by that total.
    Show your work
  3. 3Circle the letter of the best answer. Show how you know.

    In a class of 30 students, 18 are girls. What is the part-to-part ratio of boys to girls?

    1. A12:18
    2. B12:30
    3. C18:12
    4. D18:30
    Steps to try
    1. The problem tells you girls (18) and the whole class (30) — but it asks about BOYS to girls.
    2. Find the number of boys first by subtracting the girls from the class total. Then write boys first, girls second.
    3. Subtract 30 − 18 to find the boys. Put that number first in the ratio, then 18 for the girls.
    Show your work
  4. 4Circle the letter of the best answer. Show how you know.

    A pet store has 5 dogs and 9 cats. What is the ratio of dogs to cats?

    1. A5:9
    2. B5:14
    3. C9:5
    4. D14:5
    Steps to try
    1. Re-read: the question asks for dogs to cats, in that order.
    2. A ratio only uses the two amounts being compared — don't add them together. Match the order to the words.
    3. Dogs are named first, so their count (5) starts the ratio. Then write the cats' count after the colon.
    Show your work
