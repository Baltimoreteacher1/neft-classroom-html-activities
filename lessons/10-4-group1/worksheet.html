MPP.4 Level 0 — Answer Key

10.4 Small Group · Group 1

Answer Key

Name: Date:
  1. 1

    On the Penny Farthing, what is the ratio of pedal rotations to front-wheel rotations?

    1. A1 to 1
    2. B1 to 2
    3. C2 to 1
    4. D1 to 4

    The pedals are attached directly to the front wheel, so each pedal rotation rotates the wheel one time — a 1 to 1 ratio.

    Watch for: Thinking a rear gear with MORE teeth makes the wheel spin more — it is the opposite: the 32-tooth gear gives only 1.5 rotations per pedal rotation, while the 12-tooth gear gives 4.

  2. 2

    How far does the Penny Farthing travel with each pedal rotation?

    1. AThe length of the perimeter of the front wheel
    2. BThe height of the rider
    3. CThe length of the pedal
    4. DIt does not move a fixed distance

    One wheel rotation rolls the bike forward exactly the perimeter of the wheel, and one pedal rotation makes one wheel rotation.

    Watch for: Thinking a rear gear with MORE teeth makes the wheel spin more — it is the opposite: the 32-tooth gear gives only 1.5 rotations per pedal rotation, while the 12-tooth gear gives 4.

  3. 3

    Why was the front wheel of the Penny Farthing so large?

    1. ASo the bicycle travels a greater distance with one pedal rotation
    2. BSo the bicycle is easier to stop
    3. CSo the rider sits closer to the ground
    4. DSo the rear wheel spins faster

    A bigger wheel has a longer perimeter, so each pedal rotation carries the rider farther.

    Watch for: Thinking a rear gear with MORE teeth makes the wheel spin more — it is the opposite: the 32-tooth gear gives only 1.5 rotations per pedal rotation, while the 12-tooth gear gives 4.

  1. 1

    The pedal gear turns 48 teeth per rotation. The largest rear gear needs 32 teeth for one full rotation. How many rotations does the rear wheel make for each pedal rotation?

    1. A1.5 rotations
    2. B16 rotations
    3. C0.5 rotations
    4. D2 rotations

    48 ÷ 32 = 1.5, so the rear wheel makes 1.5 rotations for each pedal rotation.

    Watch for: Thinking a rear gear with MORE teeth makes the wheel spin more — it is the opposite: the 32-tooth gear gives only 1.5 rotations per pedal rotation, while the 12-tooth gear gives 4.

  2. 2

    The smallest rear gear has 12 teeth. How many rotations will the rear wheel make for each rotation of the 48-tooth pedal gear?

    1. A4 rotations
    2. B36 rotations
    3. C3 rotations
    4. D0.25 rotations

    48 ÷ 12 = 4, so the rear wheel makes 4 rotations for each pedal rotation.

    Watch for: Thinking a rear gear with MORE teeth makes the wheel spin more — it is the opposite: the 32-tooth gear gives only 1.5 rotations per pedal rotation, while the 12-tooth gear gives 4.

  3. 3

    You are about to ride up a steep hill. Which rear gear should the chain be on, and why?

    1. AThe 32-tooth gear, because 1.5 rotations per pedal makes pedaling easier at slow speeds
    2. BThe 12-tooth gear, because 4 rotations per pedal is always better
    3. CThe 32-tooth gear, because it makes the bike go faster
    4. DIt does not matter which gear you use on a hill

    The larger gear turns the wheel fewer times per pedal rotation, which makes it easier to pedal at slower speeds or go uphill.

    Watch for: Thinking a rear gear with MORE teeth makes the wheel spin more — it is the opposite: the 32-tooth gear gives only 1.5 rotations per pedal rotation, while the 12-tooth gear gives 4.
