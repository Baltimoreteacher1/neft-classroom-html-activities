    Sort each pair: does the LCM equal the product of the two numbers, or is the LCM less than the product?

    Categories: equals-product · less-than-product

    • 3 and 7 → LCM = 21 = 3 × 7
    • 5 and 11 → LCM = 55 = 5 × 11
    • 2 and 9 → LCM = 18 = 2 × 9
    • 4 and 6 → LCM = 12 < 4 × 6 = 24
    • 6 and 8 → LCM = 24 < 6 × 8 = 48
    • 10 and 15 → LCM = 30 < 10 × 15 = 150
