  1. 1

    Spot the Common Mistake

    1. 1Convert 2 yards of rope to feet2 yards → feet
    2. 2Use the conversion factor1 yard = 3 feet
    3. 3Operate2 ÷ 3 = 0.67 feet
    4. 4Final answer2 yards ≈ 0.67 feet

    Which step has the mistake? Explain it and write the correct work.

    The mistake is in Step 3. Correct work: Feet are smaller than yards, so you multiply (not divide): 2 × 3 = 6 feet. Changing to a smaller unit always gives a bigger number, not a tiny decimal. The Operate step divided when it should have multiplied. Feet are smaller than yards, so each yard becomes 3 feet and 2 × 3 = 6 feet. The answer 0.67 feet is a warning sign by itself — a 2-yard rope cannot end up shorter than 1 foot.

    Level 2 Extension — Multiply or Divide?

    1. 1Convert 4 feet of side panel to inches4 feet → inches
    2. 2Use the conversion factor1 foot = 12 inches
    3. 3Operate4 ÷ 12 = 0.33 inches
    4. 4Final answer4 feet ≈ 0.33 inches

    Which step has the mistake? Explain it and write the correct work.

    The mistake is in Step 3. Correct work: Inches are smaller than feet, so you multiply (not divide): 4 × 12 = 48 inches. The answer should be larger, not smaller, when changing to a smaller unit. The Operate step divided when the conversion calls for multiplying. Inches are smaller than feet, so each of the 4 feet becomes 12 inches: 4 × 12 = 48 inches. A side panel measuring 0.33 inches would be smaller than a fingertip, which is another sign the operation was flipped.
