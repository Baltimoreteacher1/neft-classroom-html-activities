  2. 2Circle the letter of the best answer. Show how you know.

    A triangular prism has triangular bases with base 6 in and height 4 in. The prism is 10 in long and the two slant sides of the triangle are each 5 in. What is the surface area?

    1. A124 in²
    2. B160 in²
    3. C184 in²
    4. D184 in³

    Answer: C — 184 in²

    Why: Triangular bases: 2 × (½ × 6 × 4) = 24 in². Three rectangular faces: 6(10) + 5(10) + 5(10) = 60 + 50 + 50 = 160 in². SA = 24 + 160 = 184 in².

  1. 1Circle the letter of the best answer. Show how you know.

    A rectangular prism measures 6 cm long, 4 cm wide, and 2 cm tall. What is its surface area?

    1. A24 square centimeters
    2. B44 square centimeters
    3. C48 square centimeters
    4. D88 square centimeters

    Answer: D — 88 square centimeters

    Why: 2(6×4) + 2(6×2) + 2(4×2) = 48 + 24 + 16 = 88 square centimeters.

  2. 2Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    Find the surface area of a rectangular prism measuring 12 by 3 by 5.

    1. 1Add one of each face pair: 36 + 60 + 15 = ___.111
    2. 2Double the three-face sum: ___.222
    3. 3Label the answer in ___ units.square
    Final answer:222

    Why: Add one of each face pair: 36 + 60 + 15 = 111. Double the three-face sum: 222. Label the answer in square units.

Part 2Explain and correctreasoning
  1. 3Answer in complete sentences.

    A company ships products in two container options: a rectangular prism (8 × 6 × 4 in) or a triangular prism (triangle base 8 in, triangle height 6 in, prism length 8 in, slant sides 6.3 in each). Both must be wrapped in protective film. Which shape uses LESS film? Show your work.

    Look for: surface area, rectangular, triangular, compare, less, film, face, area

    Why: Film covers the outside, so compare surface areas. The rectangular prism gives 2(8×6) + 2(8×4) + 2(6×4) = 96 + 64 + 48 = 208 in², while the triangular prism gives two triangles 2(½ × 8 × 6) = 48 plus three rectangles 8×8 = 64 and two of 6.3×8 = 50.4, for 212.8 in². The rectangular container uses less film, by about 4.8 in².
