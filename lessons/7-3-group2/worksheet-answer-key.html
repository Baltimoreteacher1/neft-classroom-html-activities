Group 2 · Answer KeyChallenge practice

Learning target I can find the absolute value of a rational number — an integer, fraction, or decimal — as its distance from zero, explain why the method works, and use it on a problem I have not seen before.

Part 1Understand the ideamodels and meaning
  1. 1Mark and label each value on the number line.

    Plot these integers and circle pairs that have the same absolute value: -6, 6, -2, 2, -4, 4

    Answer: A at -6; B at 6; C at -2; D at 2; E at -4; F at 4

    Why: Each pair of points is a mirror image across zero: A (-6) and B (6) are both 6 units away, C (-2) and D (2) are both 2 units away, and E (-4) and F (4) are both 4 units away. Opposite integers always share the same absolute value because they are the same distance from zero in different directions.

  2. 2Complete the table. Show how you found each value.

    Complete the table with each integer's absolute value and its opposite.

    IntegerAbsolute ValueOpposite
    -7
    4
    -12
    0
    9

    Why: The two columns ask for different things: absolute value drops the sign (|-7| = 7, |-12| = 12, |4| = 4, |9| = 9) while the opposite flips the sign to the other side of zero (-7 → 7, 4 → -4, -12 → 12, 9 → -9). Zero is the one integer where both columns are the same, because 0 is its own opposite and sits at distance 0.

Part 2Apply itreal-world problems
  1. 3Circle the letter of the best answer. Show how you know.

    On a frosty morning at the mountain cache, the thermometer reads -8 degrees. What is the absolute value of -8?

    1. A-8
    2. B0
    3. C8
    4. D16

    Answer: C — 8

    Why: Absolute value is the distance from zero, so |-8| = 8.

    Watch for: A common mistake in Integers and Absolute Value is judging size by the digits alone instead of position on the number line — students think -8 is 'more' than -3 because 8 > 3, when really -8 sits farther left (colder, deeper, or lower) and is actually the SMALLER integer. Absolute value measures distance from zero and is always positive or zero, but that distance does not decide which integer is greater — only left-to-right order on the number line does. Before you submit, ask: am I comparing distance from zero (absolute value) or position on the number line (greater/less than)?

  2. 4Circle the letter of the best answer. Show how you know.

    Two treasure sites report their temperatures: -5 degrees F at the Ice Cave and -2 degrees F at the Frozen Dock. Which site is COLDER?

    1. A-5 degrees F (Ice Cave)
    2. B-2 degrees F (Frozen Dock)
    3. CThey are the same
    4. DNeither is below zero

    Answer: A — -5 degrees F (Ice Cave)

    Why: On a number line, -5 is to the left of -2, so -5 is the smaller (colder) temperature.

    Watch for: A common mistake in Integers and Absolute Value is judging size by the digits alone instead of position on the number line — students think -8 is 'more' than -3 because 8 > 3, when really -8 sits farther left (colder, deeper, or lower) and is actually the SMALLER integer.   3. 5Circle the letter of the best answer. Show how you know.

    Captain Vega compares |-12| (a deep cave) and |7| (a hilltop). Which absolute value is GREATER?

    1. A|7| because 7 is positive
    2. BThey are equal
    3. C|-12| = -12, so |7| is greater
    4. D|-12| because 12 > 7

    Answer: D — |-12| because 12 > 7

    Why: |-12| = 12 and |7| = 7. Since 12 > 7, the absolute value of -12 is greater.

    Watch for: A common mistake in Integers and Absolute Value is judging size by the digits alone instead of position on the number line — students think -8 is 'more' than -3 because 8 > 3, when really -8 sits farther left (colder, deeper, or lower) and is actually the SMALLER integer. Part 3Explain and correctreasoning
  1. 6Answer in complete sentences.

    A submarine is at -150 meters and a bird is flying at +150 meters. A student says the submarine is closer to sea level because -150 is less than 150. Is the student correct? Explain using absolute value.

    Look for: absolute value, distance, sea level, zero, equal, negative, less than, position

    Why: The student is not correct. Absolute value measures distance from sea level, and |-150| = 150 while |150| = 150, so the submarine and the bird are exactly the same distance from 0. The comparison -150 < 150 describes position — below versus above sea level — not closeness to it.
