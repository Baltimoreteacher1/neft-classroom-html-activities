🚀 Launch

Detective Park discovered that 3 identical locked boxes contain a total of 21 pieces of evidence. She writes the equation 3x = 21 to find how many pieces are in each box. How can she solve for x?

How do I solve a multiplication or division equation?

When a variable is multiplied or divided by a number, I use the inverse operation to get it alone. Division undoes multiplication, and multiplication undoes division.

Key idea: Solving Multiplication & Division Equations. Formula: ax = b → x = b/a | x/a = b → x = a · b. 1. Identify the operation applied to the variable. 2. Use the inverse operation: Divide to undo multiplication; multiply to undo division. 3. Balance: Apply the inverse operation to both sides to isolate the variable

Watch me

  • Solve 3x = 21. This means 3 times x equals 21.
  • The inverse of multiplying by 3 is dividing by 3.
  • I divide both sides by 3: 3x ÷ 3 = 21 ÷ 3.
  • That leaves x = 7. Check: 3 × 7 = 21. True!

Let's try together

  • Solve x ÷ 4 = 9. Is x multiplied or divided?
  • It is divided by 4, so the inverse is multiplying both sides by 4.
  • x = 9 × 4 = 36. Check: 36 ÷ 4 = 9. True?

Now it's your turn

  • Fill in the table to solve 3x = 21 and the other equations, showing your inverse operation.
  • Then finish the practice problems on your own.
