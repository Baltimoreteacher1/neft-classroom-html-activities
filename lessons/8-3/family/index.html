Unit 8, Lesson 3: Write and Solve Equations Using Multiplication or Division

Standard 6.AT.8

What students are learning Qué aprenden

Key idea: To solve a multiplication or division equation, undo it with the opposite step on both sides. For 4x = 20, divide both sides by 4: x = 5. For x ÷ 3 = 6, multiply both sides by 3: x = 18.
Idea clave: Para resolver una ecuación de multiplicación o división, deshazla con el paso contrario en los dos lados. Para 4x = 20, divide los dos lados entre 4: x = 5. Para x ÷ 3 = 6, multiplica los dos lados por 3: x = 18.

Try at home Para hacer en casa

Gather 12 small objects, such as beans or coins. Say 3 equal groups hold 12 in all, so 3g = 12 where g is the number in each group. Share the objects into 3 equal piles to find g, then write 12 ÷ 3 = 4 and check that 3 × 4 = 12.

Reúne 12 objetos pequeños, como frijoles o monedas. Di que 3 grupos iguales tienen 12 en total, así que 3g = 12, donde g es la cantidad en cada grupo. Reparte los objetos en 3 montones iguales para hallar g, escribe 12 ÷ 3 = 4 y comprueba que 3 × 4 = 12.

Talk about it:

What operation undoes multiplying by 3? What did you do to both sides?

¿Qué operación deshace multiplicar por 3? ¿Qué hiciste a ambos lados?

How we did it in class today Cómo lo hicimos hoy en clase

  1. Solve 3x = 21. This means 3 times x equals 21.
  2. The inverse of multiplying by 3 is dividing by 3.
  3. I divide both sides by 3: 3x ÷ 3 = 21 ÷ 3.
  4. That leaves x = 7. Check: 3 × 7 = 21. True!
