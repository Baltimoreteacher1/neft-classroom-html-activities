🎯 The big ideaLa idea principal

In one sentence: Total ÷ group size = number of groups. The whole amount you are splitting goes first; the size of one piece goes second. If a "how many" question gives you an answer smaller than 1, the two were swapped.

Watch out:Cuidado con este error común:

Putting the group size first, like writing 1/8 ÷ 3/4 for "how many 1/8-cup servings are in 3/4 cup." That gives an answer under 1, which cannot be right for a "how many" question like this one.
