✏️ Worked Example

  1. Read the problem: A smoothie stand makes 18 smoothies in 6 minutes. How many smoothies can it make in 10 minutes at the same rate?
  2. Find the unit rate: 6 minutes ÷ 18 smoothies = 0.33 minute per smoothie
  3. Find smoothies in 10 minutes: 0.33 × 10 = about 3.3 smoothies
  4. Answer: About 3 smoothies in 10 minutes
