Unit 7, Lesson 6: Determine Distance on the Coordinate Plane6.NOS.9

A quick guide for families and for any student who missed class.

What students are learning

Distance is how far apart two points are, and it is never negative. When two points share a row or a column, you can find the distance using their coordinates.

Key idea: Distance on the Coordinate Plane. Formula: Distance = |x1 − x2| (horizontal) or |y1 − y2| (vertical). 1. Same quadrant / Same sign: Subtract absolute values of differing coordinates. 2. Different quadrants / Opposite signs: Add absolute values of differing coordinates. 3. Distance represents length, so it is always positive

Key words

  • Distance on the Coordinate Plane — The positive space between points, found from the absolute difference of coordinates on a horizontal or vertical line.
    Español: Distancia en el plano de coordenadas — El espacio positivo entre puntos, que se halla con la diferencia absoluta de las coordenadas en una línea horizontal o vertical.
  • Distance — How far apart two points are. It is never negative.
    Español: Distancia — Qué tan separados están dos puntos. Nunca es negativo.
  • Absolute value — How far a number is from zero. It is never negative.
    Español: Valor absoluto — Qué tan lejos está un número de cero. Nunca es negativo.
  • Horizontal distance — How far apart two points are going left or right.
    Español: Distancia horizontal — Qué tan separados están dos puntos yendo a la izquierda o a la derecha.
  • Vertical distance — How far apart two points are going up or down.
    Español: Distancia vertical — Qué tan separados están dos puntos yendo hacia arriba o hacia abajo.
  • Coordinate plane — A grid with a line going across and a line going up to plot points.
    Español: Plano cartesiano — Una cuadrícula con una línea horizontal y una vertical para marcar puntos.
  • Integer — Whole numbers and their opposites, like -2, -1, 0, 1, 2.
    Español: Número entero — Números enteros y sus opuestos, como -2, -1, 0, 1, 2.

Example

  1. I will find the distance from (-3, 2) to (4, 2). Both have y = 2, so they sit on the same horizontal line.
  2. The points are on opposite sides of zero, so I add the absolute values.
  3. |-3| = 3 and |4| = 4.
  4. I add: 3 + 4 = 7. The distance between them is 7 units.

Common mistake

A common mistake in Distance on the Coordinate Plane is subtracting the coordinates as if both points were on the same side of zero, instead of adding their absolute values when the points are on opposite sides. For example, to find the distance between (-6, 3) and (2, 3), a student might subtract 6 − 2 = 4, but since -6 and 2 are on opposite sides of zero, the absolute values must be added instead: |-6| + |2| = 6 + 2 = 8. The correct distance is 8 units, not 4. Before you submit, ask: 'Are these two points on opposite sides of zero — and if so, did I add the absolute values instead of subtracting?'
