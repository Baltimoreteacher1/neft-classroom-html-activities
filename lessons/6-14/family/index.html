Unit 6, Lesson 14: The Distributive Property6.AT.7

A quick guide for families and for any student who missed class.

What students are learning

The distributive property means you can multiply a number by everything inside the parentheses, one piece at a time: a(b + c) = ab + ac.

Key idea: The distributive property says the number outside the parentheses multiplies every number inside. 3(15 + 5) = 3 × 15 + 3 × 5 = 45 + 15 = 60. With a letter: 7(x + 3) = 7x + 21.
Idea clave: La propiedad distributiva dice que el número fuera del paréntesis multiplica a cada número de adentro. 3(15 + 5) = 3 × 15 + 3 × 5 = 45 + 15 = 60. Con una letra: 7(x + 3) = 7x + 21.

How we did it in class today

  1. Each bundle has a ticket ($15) and a snack ($5). For 3 bundles I can write 3(15 + 5).
  2. Way 1: add inside first. 15 + 5 = 20, then 3 × 20 = 60.
  3. Way 2: distribute the 3 to each part. 3 × 15 + 3 × 5.
  4. That is 45 + 15 = 60.
  5. Both ways give $60, so 3(15 + 5) = 3 × 15 + 3 × 5.

If they get stuck...

Watch out for this: The most common mistake in The Distributive Property is multiplying the outside factor by only the FIRST term inside the parentheses and forgetting the second — for example, writing 3(x + 4) = 3x + 4 instead of 3x + 12. Before you submit, check: did the outside number reach BOTH terms inside the parentheses?

What to ask: "Can you show me how you got that? Did we miss a step?"
