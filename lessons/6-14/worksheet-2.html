RememberThe Distributive Property

The Distributive Property. Formula: a(b + c) = ab + ac | a(b − c) = ab − ac The distributive property means you can multiply a number by everything inside the parentheses, one piece at a time: a(b + c) = ab + ac.

  1. Expand: Multiply the outside factor by each term inside parentheses.
  2. Factor: Find the GCF of terms and pull it outside: ab + ac = a(b + c).
  3. Visual: Represent as the area of partitioned rectangles
