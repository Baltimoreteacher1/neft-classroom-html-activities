  1. 1

    What does it mean that a butterfly is symmetric?

    1. AThe two sides of the butterfly look like mirror images
    2. BThe butterfly has exactly two wings
    3. CThe butterfly's wings are very colorful
    4. DThe butterfly is larger than most insects

    Symmetric means one side is a mirror image of the other — that is exactly what we see across the butterfly's body.

    Watch for: Thinking a figure lacks bilateral symmetry just because its line of symmetry is not vertical — the mirror line can run in any direction, and the test is whether the two sides match across it.

  2. 2

    In plants and animals, reflective symmetry is called:

    1. Abilateral symmetry
    2. Brotational symmetry
    3. Ca mirror image
    4. Dbalance

    The lesson says it directly: all butterflies have reflective symmetry, and in plants and animals this is called bilateral symmetry.

    Watch for: Thinking a figure lacks bilateral symmetry just because its line of symmetry is not vertical — the mirror line can run in any direction, and the test is whether the two sides match across it.

  3. 3

    A plant grows in layers, and each layer is made up of 2 leaves. How many leaves does the plant have after growing 4 layers?

    1. A8 leaves
    2. B6 leaves
    3. C16 leaves
    4. D4 leaves

    4 layers × 2 leaves per layer = 8 leaves.

    Watch for: Thinking a figure lacks bilateral symmetry just because its line of symmetry is not vertical — the mirror line can run in any direction, and the test is whether the two sides match across it.
