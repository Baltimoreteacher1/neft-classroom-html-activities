Question 7Two-part question

Part A — correct answer: C

Find 18.9 ÷ 6.3 using the decimal division algorithm.

  • A0.3
  • B2.8
  • C3
  • D30

Slide both decimal points one place right: 189 ÷ 63 = 3. Multiplying both numbers by 10 leaves the quotient unchanged.

  • A: The quotient was divided by 10 after the points were slid. Moving BOTH points the same direction leaves the answer unchanged.
  • B: This division comes out even: 63 x 3 = 189, so the quotient is a whole number.
  • D: The quotient was multiplied by 10 after the points were slid. 189 divided by 63 is exactly 3.

Part B — correct answer: D

Why does sliding BOTH decimal points the same number of places leave the quotient unchanged?

  • AThe decimal point does not affect division.
  • BOnly the divisor matters in a division problem.
  • CThe quotient always doubles when you slide a point.
  • DMultiplying the dividend and the divisor by the same power of ten produces an equivalent division.

18.9 ÷ 6.3 and 189 ÷ 63 are equivalent because both numbers were multiplied by 10 — the same scaling on both terms of a ratio keeps its value.

Question 8Written response · 2 points

Type II Reasoning: Moving Only One Point

Bianca computed 7.5 ÷ 0.5. She slid the divisor's point to make it 5, but left the dividend as 7.5, and wrote '7.5 ÷ 5 = 1.5.'

Explain Bianca's misconception and give the correct quotient.

Model answer: 75 ÷ 5 = 15. Bianca multiplied only the divisor by 10, which changes the problem. Both numbers must be scaled by the same factor, so 7.5 becomes 75. A check confirms it: 0.5 × 15 = 7.5.

2 pointsStudent explains that both decimal points must slide the same number of places to keep the division equivalent, computes 75 ÷ 5 = 15, and notes that 1.5 is ten times too small.
1 pointStudent gives 15 without explaining why both points must move.
0 pointsStudent agrees with Bianca or gives an unrelated response.

Model answer and scoring guidance.

Question 9Written response · 2 points

A gas station sells fuel at $3.45 per gallon. A customer pays $27.60 total. How many gallons did the customer buy? Set up the decimal division, show how to convert it to whole-number division, and solve.

Model answer: Total cost divided by price per gallon gives the number of gallons, so the setup is 27.60 ÷ 3.45. The divisor has two decimal places, so multiplying both numbers by 100 gives 2,760 ÷ 345 = 8 gallons, and 8 × $3.45 = $27.60 confirms it.

Award 2 points for a complete explanation with correct mathematics, 1 point for a correct answer with thin reasoning, 0 for an unrelated response.
