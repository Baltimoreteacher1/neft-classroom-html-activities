🚀 Launch

The space station has 18.9 kilograms of a rare mineral sample that must be divided equally among 6.3-kilogram storage pods. How many pods can be completely filled?

How do you divide with decimals?

Dividing splits a total into equal groups. When you divide by a decimal, first move the point to make the divisor a whole number — then run the same long-division cycle: divide, multiply, subtract, bring down.

Key idea: Move the decimal point the same number of places in BOTH the divisor and the dividend, put the point in the quotient straight above it, then repeat Divide → Multiply → Subtract → Bring Down.

Watch me

  • I want 18.9 ÷ 6.3. The divisor is 6.3, and long division needs a whole-number divisor.
  • STEP 0 — MOVE THE POINT: I move the decimal point 1 place right in the divisor, so 6.3 becomes 63.
  • I must move the dividend the same 1 place right: 18.9 becomes 189, and the point in the quotient goes straight above its new spot. The problem is now 189 ÷ 63.
  • DIVIDE: 63 does not fit into 1 or 18, so my first working number is 189. How many 63s fit into 189? Three. I write 3 above the 9.
  • MULTIPLY: 3 × 63 = 189. I write it underneath.
  • SUBTRACT: 189 − 189 = 0.
  • BRING DOWN: there are no digits left to bring down, so the cycle is finished and the remainder is 0. 18.9 ÷ 6.3 = 3.
  • I check by multiplying: 6.3 × 3 = 18.9. Correct — 3 pods can be filled.

Let's try together

  • Let's do 8.4 ÷ 2.1. STEP 0 — MOVE THE POINT: how many places make 2.1 a whole number? (1 place, so 2.1 becomes 21.)
  • Move the dividend the same 1 place: 8.4 becomes 84, and the quotient's point goes straight above. What is the new problem? (84 ÷ 21.)
  • DIVIDE: 84 ÷ 21 → 4. MULTIPLY: 4 × 21 = 84. SUBTRACT: 84 − 84 = 0. BRING DOWN: nothing is left, so 8.4 ÷ 2.1 = 4. Check: 2.1 × 4 = 8.4.

Now it's your turn

  • Next, you will move the point so the divisor is a whole number, then run the divide, multiply, subtract, bring down cycle on your own.
  • Remember: move the point in both numbers, put the point in the quotient, then divide, multiply, subtract, bring down.
