Learning target With my small group, I can solve one-step addition and subtraction equations using inverse operations — one step at a time, with support.

Part 1Understand the ideamodels and meaning
  1. 1Mark and label each value on the number line.

    Use the number line to solve x + 8 = 20. Start at 20 and count back 8 to find x.

    Hint Start at 20 on the number line. Since x + 8 = 20, subtract 8 to find x.

    Answer: x at 12

    Why: The equation x + 8 = 20 says that starting at x and moving 8 to the right lands on 20, so going backward from 20 undoes the move. Counting back 8 ticks from 20 gives 12, so the marker belongs on 12. Checking on the line, 12 + 8 does land on 20.

  2. 2Decide whether each pair balances. Check Yes or No, then explain.

    Solve: m − 14 = 32

    Left sideRight sideBalanced?

    Hint The inverse of subtracting 14 is adding 14.

    Why: In m − 14 = 32 the variable has 14 taken away from it, so add 14 to both sides to undo that: m = 32 + 14 = 46. Checking keeps the scale balanced, since 46 − 14 = 32.

Part 2Apply itreal-world problems
  1. 3Circle the letter of the best answer. Show how you know.

    Solve: x + 9 = 15

    1. Ax = 6
    2. Bx = 9
    3. Cx = 15
    4. Dx = 24

    Hint x has 9 added to it. To get x alone, you need to UNDO that addition.

    Answer: A — x = 6

    Why: Subtract 9 from both sides: x = 15 − 9 = 6. Check: 6 + 9 = 15 ✓

    Watch for: A common mistake is applying the same operation instead of the inverse — for example, solving x − 7 = 15 by subtracting 7 from both sides (getting the wrong x = 8) instead of adding 7 to both sides (the correct x = 22). Another common mistake is doing the inverse operation to only one side, like solving x + 6 = 14 by subtracting 6 from the left but forgetting the right side. Before you submit, name the operation shown in the equation, choose its inverse, apply it to BOTH sides, and check by substituting your answer back into the original equation.

    1. Ay = 7

    Answer: C — y = 27

    Why: Add 7 to both sides: y = 20 + 7 = 27. Check: 27 − 7 = 20 ✓
