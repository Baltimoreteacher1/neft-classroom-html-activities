6.AT.3 Version B — Answer Key

3.6 Small Group · Group 2

Answer Key

Name: Date:
  1. 1

    A chef uses the proportion 3/7 = x/28 to scale a recipe. What is x?

    1. A12
    2. B14
    3. C9
    4. D21

    Scale factor: 28 ÷ 7 = 4. Multiply: 3 × 4 = 12. So x = 12.

    Watch for: A common mistake in Use Ratio Reasoning is scaling by adding instead of multiplying: since the banquet has 120 − 8 = 112 more guests than the recipe serves, some students add 112 to the ingredient amount instead of finding the scale factor. For Chef Reyes's recipe that serves 8 people with 5 cups of tomatoes, scaling up to 120 guests this way gives 5 + 112 = 117 cups of tomatoes — a wildly wrong answer. Ratios scale multiplicatively, not additively: find the scale factor first (120 ÷ 8 = 15), then multiply the ingredient by that same scale factor: 5 × 15 = 75 cups of tomatoes. Before you submit, check that you multiplied every part of the ratio by the same scale factor instead of adding the same amount to each.

  2. 2

    Spot the Common Mistake

    1. 1Set up the proportionA recipe uses 4 cups of sugar for every 9 cups of flour: 4/9 = x/27.
    2. 2Find the scale factor27 ÷ 9 = 3.
    3. 3Solve for x4 + 3 = 7, so x = 7 cups of sugar.
    4. 4AnswerUse 7 cups of sugar.

    Which step has the mistake? Explain it and write the correct work.

    The mistake is in Step 3. Correct work: Multiply by the scale factor instead of adding it: 4 × 3 = 12 cups of sugar. To keep ratios equivalent you multiply both parts by the scale factor — you never add it. The solving step added the scale factor instead of multiplying by it. Because 27 is 9 × 3, the sugar has to grow the same way: 4 × 3 = 12 cups. Adding would break the relationship, since 7/27 is not equivalent to 4/9 but 12/27 is.

  3. 3

    Find Priya's Mistake

    1. 1Set up the proportion5/8 = x/32
    2. 2Find the scale factor32 ÷ 8 = 4
    3. 3Solve for x5 + 4 = 9
    4. 4Answerx = 9

    Which step has the mistake? Explain it and write the correct work.

    The mistake is in Step 3. Correct work: 5 × 4 = 20 (multiply by the scale factor, don't add it) In the solving step Priya added the scale factor to 5 instead of multiplying by it. Since 32 is 8 × 4, the top of the fraction scales the same way: 5 × 4 = 20, so x = 20 and 20/32 simplifies right back to 5/8.

A ratio stays equivalent only when both parts are scaled the same way, so the eggs become 2 × 3 = 6 and the recipe reads 21 cups of flour to 6 eggs.
