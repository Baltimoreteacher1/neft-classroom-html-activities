    A fence is 27 feet long. How many yards long is it?

    Final answer:9 yards

    Why: Write the conversion fact in the table: 1 yd : 3 ft Find the scale factor: 27 ÷ 3 = 9 Multiply the yards by the same factor: 1 × 9 = 9

  4. 4Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    A board is 90 inches long. How many feet long is it? Write your answer as a decimal.

    Final answer:7.5 feet

    Why: Write the conversion fact in the table: 1 ft : 12 in Find the scale factor: 90 ÷ 12 = 7.5 Multiply the feet by the same factor: 1 × 7.5 = 7.5 Check with the leftover inches: 7 × 12 = 84; 90 − 84 = 6

    One lap around a running track is 1,320 feet. How many yards is one lap?

    Final answer:440 yards

    Why: Write the conversion fact in the table: 1 yd : 3 ft Find the scale factor: 1,320 ÷ 3 = 440 Multiply the yards by the same factor: 1 × 440 = 440

    Dee changes 6 yards to inches. She multiplies 6 × 12 and gets 72 inches. What is the correct number of inches?

    Final answer:216 inches

    Why: Find the inches in 1 yard: 3 × 12 = 36 Find the scale factor: 6 ÷ 1 = 6 Multiply the feet and inches by 6: 3 × 6 = 18; 36 × 6 = 216
