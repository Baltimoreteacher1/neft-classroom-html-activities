📕 Mathematical Word Bank & Spanish Cognates / Banco de Palabras
Rates and Unit Rates (Tasas y tasas unitarias) — A rate compares different units; a unit rate compares a quantity to 1 unit.
Rate (Tasa) — A ratio comparing two amounts with different units, like miles per hour.
Unit Rate (Tasa unitaria) — A rate for just 1 of something, like cost for 1 item.
Per (Por) — For each one. Example: 5 dollars per book.
Ratio (Razón) — A way to compare two amounts, like 3 to 2.
Unit price (Precio unitario) — A unit rate that gives the cost of exactly 1 item, like $0.60 per game.
Better Buy (Mejor compra) — The choice that costs less for each item.
Divide (Dividir) — Splitting a total into equal groups.
