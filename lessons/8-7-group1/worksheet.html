Equations and Inequalities Problem SolvingModelEquationInequalityReasonablenessVariableConstraint

✏️ Worked Example

  1. Clue: Solve the keypad inequality 3x ≤ 18 to find how many bars x fit.
  2. Step 1: 3x ≤ 18
  3. Step 2: Divide only the left side by 3: 3x ÷ 3 ≤ 18
  4. Step 3: x ≤ 18
  5. Answer: At most 18 bars fit.

🧩 Extra support: use the word bank and the worked example. A sentence starter is given under each problem.

    1. Ap + 12 = 45
    2. Bp − 12 = 45
    3. C12p = 45

    1. A3 + h > 8
    2. B3 + h = 8
    3. Ch > 3
    4. D8 + h > 3

    Which model is correct for: 'A number divided by 6 equals 7'?
