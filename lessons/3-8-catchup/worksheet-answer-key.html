  1. 1Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    6 apples cost $3. What is the cost for 1 apple?

    Hint Divide the price by the number of apples: $3 ÷ 6.

    Final answer:$0.50

    Why: Write the deal in the table: 6 apples : $3 Find the factor from 6 to 1: 6 ÷ 6 = 1 Divide the cost by the same number: $3 ÷ 6 = $0.50

  2. 2Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    Shop A sells 2 pens for $3. Shop B sells 5 pens for $6. Which shop has the lower cost for 1 pen?

    Hint Find the cost for 1 pen at each shop. Then compare.

    Final answer:Shop B

    Why: Write both deals in the table. Scale each deal down to 1 pen: $3 ÷ 2 = $1.50; $6 ÷ 5 = $1.20 Compare the unit prices: $1.20 < $1.50

  3. 3Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    There are 3 feet in 1 yard. How many feet are in 7 yards?

    Hint The scale factor from 1 yard to 7 yards is 7. Multiply the feet by 7.

    Final answer:21 feet

    Why: Write the conversion fact in the table: 1 yd : 3 ft Find the scale factor: 7 ÷ 1 = 7 Multiply the feet by the same factor: 3 × 7 = 21

  4. 4Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    There are 100 centimeters in 1 meter. How many centimeters are in 6 meters?

    Hint The scale factor from 1 meter to 6 meters is 6. Multiply the centimeters by 6.

    Final answer:600 centimeters

    Why: Write the conversion fact in the table: 1 m : 100 cm Find the scale factor: 6 ÷ 1 = 6 Multiply the centimeters by the same factor: 100 × 6 = 600

  5. 5Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    Find 50% of 26.

    Hint Put 100% and 26 in the first column. What do you divide 100 by to get 50?

    Final answer:13

    Why: Write 100% and the whole in the first column: 100% : 26 Find the scale factor from 100% to 50%: 100 ÷ 50 = 2 Divide the whole by 2 too: 26 ÷ 2 = 13

    Why: Write 100% and the whole in the first column: 100% : 20 Find the scale factor from 100% to 10%: 100 ÷ 10 = 10 Divide the whole by 10 too: 20 ÷ 10 = 2

    $5 is 50% of what amount?

    Hint Put 50% and 5 in the first column. What scale factor takes 50% to 100%?

    Final answer:$10

    Why: Write the part and its percent in the first column: 50% : 5 Find the scale factor from 50% to 100%: 100 ÷ 50 = 2 Multiply the part by 2 too: 5 × 2 = 10
