🚀 Launch

Your architecture firm is designing a trapezoidal window for a modern building. The glass supplier charges by the square foot, so the team must calculate the exact area. The window has a top edge of 4 feet, a bottom edge of 8 feet, and a height of 5 feet.

How do we find the area of a trapezoid?

A trapezoid is a four-sided shape with one pair of parallel sides. The two parallel sides are called the bases, and they have different lengths.

Key idea: Area of Trapezoids. Formula: Area = ½ × (b1 + b2) × h. 1. A trapezoid is a four-sided shape with one pair of parallel bases. 2. Add the two bases together: (b1 + b2). 3. Multiply by the perpendicular height, then take half

Watch me

  • My trapezoid window has a top base of 4 feet, a bottom base of 8 feet, and a height of 5 feet.
  • I write the formula: A = ½ × (b1 + b2) × h.
  • I add the bases first: 4 + 8 = 12.
  • I multiply by the height: 12 × 5 = 60.
  • I take half: ½ × 60 = 30. So the area is 30 square feet.

Let's try together

  • Now a smaller trapezoid: bases 3 cm and 5 cm, height 2 cm.
  • First, what is 3 + 5? Yes, 8.
  • Next, what is 8 × 2? Yes, 16.
  • Now take half: ½ × 16 = 8, so the area is 8 square centimeters.

Now it's your turn

  • Next you will plot the window on a grid and find its area using the formula.
  • Remember the order: add the bases, multiply by the height, then take half.
