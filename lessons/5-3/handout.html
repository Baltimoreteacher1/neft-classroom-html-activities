Determine the Area of Trapezoids

6.GR.1 · Unit 5 · Lesson 3

Name   Period

Objectives / Objetivos

Content Objective: I can find the area of a trapezoid using the formula A = ½(b1 + b2) × h.

Language Objective: I can explain my steps using the words trapezoid, base, height, and area.

Key Idea / Idea clave

Area of Trapezoids. Formula: Area = ½ × (b1 + b2) × h. 1. A trapezoid is a four-sided shape with one pair of parallel bases. 2. Add the two bases together: (b1 + b2). 3. Multiply by the perpendicular height, then take half

I Notice / Observo

  • What shape is the window?
  • How many parallel sides does it have?

I Wonder / Me pregunto

  • Why does the formula use two bases instead of one?
