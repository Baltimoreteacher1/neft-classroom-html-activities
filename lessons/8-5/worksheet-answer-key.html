  1. 1 MULTIPLE CHOICE

    Which graph represents x < 5?

    1. AOpen circle at 5, shaded left
    2. BClosed circle at 5, shaded left
    3. COpen circle at 5, shaded right
    4. DClosed circle at 5, shaded right

    💡 Solution Rationale: x < 5 means 5 is NOT included (open circle) and values less than 5 are shaded (left).

    ⚠️ Watch for (Misconception): A common mistake in Graph Inequalities is treating ≥ and ≤ the same as > and < and drawing the wrong circle. For example, students often graph x ≥ 6 with an OPEN circle (as if it were x > 6), which wrongly excludes 6 even though 6 itself makes x ≥ 6 true. A second common mistake is choosing the right circle but shading the wrong direction — for x < 9 students sometimes shade right instead of left. Always check the symbol twice: once for the circle (does it have an 'or equal to' line?) and once for the shading direction (does the symbol point toward larger or smaller numbers?).

SECTION 3 MATHEMATICAL WRITING & ERROR ANALYSIS [Reasoning & Critique]
  1. 6 ERROR ANALYSIS

    Spot the Common Mistake

    1. 1Read:Graph n ≥ 8 on the number line.
    2. 2Circle choice:Open circle at 8
    3. 3Shade direction:Shade right
    4. 4Final graph:Open circle at 8, shaded right

    Which step contains the error? Explain the mathematical misconception and write the correct calculation below.

    Key Analysis: Mistake is in Step 2. Correct Work: n ≥ 8 uses ≥, which means 8 IS part of the solution set. The circle must be CLOSED (filled in), not open — the shading to the right is correct. Final graph: closed circle at 8, shaded right.. Misconception: The shading to the right is fine, but the circle choice is wrong: n ≥ 8 includes 8, because 8 ≥ 8 is true. A boundary that is part of the solution set gets a closed, filled-in circle, so the graph should be a closed circle at 8 shaded right.

'Fewer than 9 clues' means values smaller than 9, so the shading runs left, giving an open circle at 9 shaded left.

  1. 2 ERROR ANALYSIS

    Find the Graphing Error

    1. 1Inequality:x > 6
    2. 2Circle type:Closed circle at 6
    3. 3Shade direction:Shade right
    4. 4Graph:Closed circle at 6, shaded right

    Which step contains the error? Explain the mathematical misconception and write the correct calculation below.

    Key Analysis: Mistake is in Step 2. Correct Work: x > 6 uses a strict inequality (no 'equal to'), so the circle should be OPEN, not closed. Open circle at 6, shaded right.. Misconception: The shading right matches x > 6, but the circle type step used a closed circle. Since > is strict, 6 > 6 is false and 6 is not a solution, so the graph needs an open circle at 6 shaded right.
  2. 3 OPEN RESPONSE

    A theme park has two rides. Ride A requires height h > 48 inches. Ride B requires height h ≥ 48 inches. A child is exactly 48 inches tall. Which ride can they go on? Explain using number line graphs for both inequalities.

    💡 Exemplar Response: Key Terms to Look For: open, closed, true, false, included, not included, boundary Explanation: The child is exactly on the boundary, so test 48 in each rule. For Ride A, 48 > 48 is false, so the graph has an open circle at 48 and the child cannot ride. For Ride B, 48 ≥ 48 is true, so the graph has a closed circle at 48 and the child can ride B.
