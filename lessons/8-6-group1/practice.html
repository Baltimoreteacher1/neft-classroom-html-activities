Where we left off

Our goal: With my small group, I can solve an inequality and graph its solution set on a number line — one step at a time, with support.

The big idea: Solve with inverse operations, then graph the solution set: open circle for < or >, closed for ≤ or ≥.

Model to copy — Watch me

  1. Solve x + 3 > 53. The inverse of adding 3 is subtracting 3.
  2. I subtract 3 from both sides: x + 3 − 3 > 53 − 3, so x > 50.
  3. The symbol > does not include 50, so I use an open circle at 50.
  4. 'Greater than' shades right. Check: x = 60 gives 60 + 3 = 63 > 53. True!
