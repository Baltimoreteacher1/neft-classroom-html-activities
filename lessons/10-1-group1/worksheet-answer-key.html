Part 1Understand the ideamodels and meaning
  1. 1Circle the letter of the best answer. Show how you know.

    A planter is 5 feet long, 2 feet wide and 3 feet deep. Ana says she needs 10 cubic feet of soil. Which statement is correct?

    1. AAna forgot the depth. She needs 30 cubic feet.
    2. BAna is right. She needs 10 cubic feet.
    3. CAna should add: 5 + 2 + 3 = 10 cubic feet.

    Hint Ana used only two of the three measures. Which one did she leave out?

    Answer: A — Ana forgot the depth. She needs 30 cubic feet.

    Why: See what Ana found: 5 × 2 = 10 Multiply by the depth too: 5 × 2 × 3 = 30

    Watch for: Believing math only 'counts' when it happens in a classroom — and walking past the calculating, measuring, and projecting inside jobs, hobbies, and chores every day.

Part 2Apply itreal-world problems
  1. 2Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    A chef plans 25 plates of pasta. Each plate uses 3 ounces of cheese. How many ounces of cheese should the chef order?

    1. 1Write the known ratio. How much cheese goes on 1 plate?3 ounces
    2. 2What do you multiply 1 plate by to get 25 plates?25
    3. 3Multiply the ounces by 25 too. How many ounces?75 ounces
    Final answer:The chef should order 75 ounces of cheese.
  2. 3Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    A sandbox is 5 feet long and 4 feet wide. The sand must be 2 feet deep. Find the area of the sandbox and the volume of sand to buy.

    1. 1What is the area of the bottom of the sandbox?20 square feet
    2. 2What is the volume of the sand?40 cubic feet
    3. 3Which number tells how much sand to buy: 20 or 40?40. Sand fills space, so you buy the volume.
    Final answer:The area is 5 × 4 = 20 square feet. The volume is 5 × 4 × 2 = 40 cubic feet of sand.
  3. 4Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    A cook puts 4 meatballs in each bowl of soup. She makes 12 bowls. How many meatballs does she need?

    Hint Each bowl has the same amount. Multiply the bowls by the meatballs in one bowl.

    Final answer:48 meatballs

    Why: Write the known ratio in the table: 1 bowl → 4 meatballs Find the scale factor from 1 bowl to 12: 12 ÷ 1 = 12 Multiply the meatballs by 12 too: 4 × 12 = 48

  4. 5Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    A planter is 8 feet long and 3 feet wide. What is its area?

    Hint Area covers flat space. Multiply the length by the width.

    Final answer:24 square feet

    Why: Multiply length × width: 8 × 3 = 24 Write the unit for area: 24 sq ft

  5. 6Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    Mia fills a planter that is 7 feet long, 2 feet wide and 2 feet deep. How many cubic feet of soil does she need?

    Hint Soil fills space, so find the volume: length × width × depth.

    Final answer:28 cubic feet

    Why: Find the area of the bottom: 7 × 2 = 14 Multiply by the depth: 14 × 2 = 28
