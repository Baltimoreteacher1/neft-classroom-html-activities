    A planter is 4 feet long and 2 feet wide. What is its area?

    1. A8 square feet
    2. B6 square feet
    3. C12 square feet
    4. D2 square feet

    Area of a rectangle is length × width: 4 × 2 = 8 square feet.

    4. D4 cubic feet

    Volume is length × width × depth: 4 × 2 × 1 = 8 cubic feet of soil.

    4. D2 gallons

      4. D5,840 gallons

    5. 5

      A household of 5 saves 2,920 gallons per person per year. A household of 4 does the same. How much MORE does the household of 5 save than the household of 4?

      1. A2,920 gallons
      2. B14,600 gallons
      3. C11,680 gallons
      4. D1 gallon

      5 people save 5 × 2,920 = 14,600 gallons and 4 people save 4 × 2,920 = 11,680 gallons. The difference is 14,600 − 11,680 = 2,920 gallons — exactly one more person's savings.
