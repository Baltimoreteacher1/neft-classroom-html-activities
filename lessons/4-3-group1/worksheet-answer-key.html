    Margaret's phone has 6 GB of storage and 4 GB is used. About what percent is used?

    1. AAbout 70%
    2. BAbout 40%
    3. CAbout 25%
    4. DAbout 150%

    4 out of 6 is 2/3, which is about 67%, so 'about 70%' is the reasonable estimate.
