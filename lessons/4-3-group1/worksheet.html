So 120% = 1.2, which makes sense because it is only a little more than one whole (100%). In the Attempt step the student counted digits instead of dividing by 100, which shifted the decimal only one place. Because 120% means 120 out of 100, the decimal is 120 ÷ 100 = 1.2 — just a little more than one whole, which fits a percent just above 100%.
