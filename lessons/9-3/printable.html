🚀 Launch

Miguel is interested in joining a recreation center. Recreation World charges the monthly fee shown on the flyer. How can Miguel determine the cost of being a member of Recreation World for any number of months?

From a pattern in the table to an equation

A table can only list some values. An equation captures the whole pattern at once, so it works for any number of months or hours.

Key idea: Writing Two-Variable Equations. Formula: y = kx | Dependent = (Unit Rate) × Independent. 1. Identify the independent variable (x) and dependent variable (y). 2. Find the constant unit rate (k = y ÷ x). 3. Write the equation relating the two variables: y = kx (or y = x + c)

Watch me write Miguel's equation

GIVEN DATA / MODEL
  • The quantities are the number of months of membership and the total cost. The total cost depends on the number of months.
  • In the table, each month adds $24.95: 1 month is $24.95, 2 months are $49.90, 3 months are $74.85.
  • The pattern in words: the total cost is $24.95 times the number of months.
  • Define the variables — let m = number of months and c = total cost in dollars — and write the equation: c = 24.95m.
  • Now ANY number of months works: for 10 months, c = 24.95 × 10 = 249.50.
STUDENT THINKING & SENTENCE FRAME

Because ___ , therefore ___ .

Let's write the canoe equation together

GIVEN DATA / MODEL
  • A canoe rents for $15 per hour. Which quantity is independent — hours or total cost?
  • The hours of rental is independent; the total cost depends on it.
  • Say the pattern in words: the total cost equals $15 times the number of hours.
  • Let h = hours rented and c = total cost. What equation matches? c = 15h.
  • Check it: a 3-hour rental is 15 × 3 = 45 dollars, and a 5-hour rental is 15 × 5 = 75 dollars.
