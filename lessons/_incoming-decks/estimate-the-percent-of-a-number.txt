[slide 43] A local middle school has organized a Green-Team to set up and manage recycling at the school. The team decided to collect paper, metal, glass, and plastic to recycle. In one month, the team collected 216 total pounds of recycled materials, in the percents shown. About how many pounds of metal were recycled that month?
What do you know about the amount of metal recycled that month?
[slide 44] Reveal: 
Of the 216 pounds of recycled materials, 8.6% was metal.
[slide 45] Of the 216 pounds of recycled materials, 8.6% was metal. About how many pounds of metal were recycled that month? 
We need to estimate 8.6% of 216. 

What strategies have you used before to estimate?
[slide 46] Reveal: 
We can use rounding or compatible numbers to estimate.
[slide 47] Let’s use rounding to estimate 8.6% of 216. 
8.6% of the recycling collected was metal. This is about 10%. 
216 pounds of material were recycled. This is about 220. 
So, we can estimate using 10% of 220. 
How can you write 10% as a decimal?
[slide 48] Reveal: 
We can write 10% as 0.10 or 0.1.
[slide 49] Let’s represent the percentage as a decimal.

10% of 220 or 0.1 of 220 

How can you find 0.1 of 220?
[slide 50] Reveal:
