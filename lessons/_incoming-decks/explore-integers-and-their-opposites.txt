[slide 42] Summarize: Integers
[slide 42] A negative integer is an integer less than zero and is written with a − sign. Integers include the counting numbers, 0 (zero), and the opposites, or negatives, of the counting numbers. ​
[slide 42] The opposite of a number is the same distance from 0 on the opposite side of the number line.
[slide 42] 6 and −6 are opposites.
[slide 43] Apply: Football Plays
[slide 43] To make a first down, a football team must make at least a 10-yard gain from where they originally started, called the line of scrimmage. During a series of plays, a football team had a 10-yard loss, a 4-yard gain, a 3-yard loss, and a 21-yard gain.
[slide 43] Question : Did the football team make a first down? How far are they from the line of scrimmage?

[slide 44] I can use integers to represent quantities in everyday life and explain the meaning of zero within each real-world context.
