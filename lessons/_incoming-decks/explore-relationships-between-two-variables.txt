[slide 43] Summarize: Explore Relationships Between Two Quantities
[slide 43] When two quantities are related, one quantity is represented by an independent variable and the other by a dependent variable. The independent variable does not depend upon the other quantity. It is subject to choice. The dependent variable changes in response to changes of the independent variable.

[slide 45] I can recognize when two quantities relate to each other, i.e., as one quantity changes independently, the other quantity changes in a related way.
