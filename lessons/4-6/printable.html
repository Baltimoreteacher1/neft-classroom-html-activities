🚀 Launch

You're building a brand-new arcade cabinet! The blueprints use feet, but the parts catalog lists measurements in inches. The wiring instructions use yards, and the weight limits are in pounds — but the shipping labels use ounces. You need to convert between units to make sure everything fits and ships correctly.

How do I change one measurement unit into another?

A unit is a standard amount used to measure, like a foot or an inch. A conversion factor is a ratio that relates two units, like 1 foot = 12 inches.

Key idea: Multiply when changing to a smaller unit, and divide when changing to a bigger unit.

Watch me

  • The cabinet is 5 feet tall. I want to know how many inches that is.
  • My conversion factor is 1 foot = 12 inches.
  • Inches are smaller than feet, so I multiply: 5 × 12 = 60.
  • So 5 feet = 60 inches.

Let's try together

  • A wire is 4 yards long. The factor is 1 yard = 3 feet.
  • Feet are smaller than yards, so do we multiply or divide? (Multiply)
  • What is 4 × 3 feet? (12 feet)
  • Now a metric example: a power cable is 3 meters long. The factor is 1 meter = 100 centimeters.
  • Centimeters are smaller than meters, so we multiply: 3 × 100 = 300 centimeters.

Now it's your turn

  • In the table, use the conversion factor to fill in each converted measurement.
  • Decide each time: are you going to a smaller unit (multiply) or a bigger unit (divide)?
