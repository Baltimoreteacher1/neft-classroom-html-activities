Words to Know First

These are words you'll need to already know to follow this lesson — not the new words the lesson teaches. Review each one, its meaning, and the example so the new lesson makes sense from the start.

✖️

multiply

Level 1 To add the same number many times to find a total.

Example: 3 × 12 = 36.

➗

divide

Level 1 To split a total into equal groups.

Example: 400 ÷ 100 = 4.

🔟

multiply by 10 or 100

Level 1 To make a number 10 or 100 times bigger.

Example: 4 × 10 = 40 and 3 × 100 = 300.

📏

length

Level 1 How long something is, measured in units like inches or meters.

Example: A pencil's length might be 7 inches.
