    The mistake is in Step 2. Correct work: The student subtracted 5 - 3, but the first x-coordinate is -3, not 3. The correct calculation is |5 - (-3)| = |5 + 3| = 8 units. When points span opposite sides of an axis, you must add the absolute values: |-3| + |5| = 3 + 5 = 8. The subtraction step used 3 in place of -3, so the negative sign disappeared. Keeping it gives |5 - (-3)| = |5 + 3| = 8 units, which is the same as adding the absolute values |-3| + |5| = 3 + 5 = 8 because the points straddle the y-axis.

    (Lesson 7.7) Maria is choosing between two store spaces on a grid where each unit is 1 foot and each grid line is 5 feet apart. Space A is a rectangle 25 ft by 40 ft; Space B is 30 ft by 30 ft. Which has more floor area?

    1. ASpace A — 1,000 sq ft vs 900 sq ft
    2. BSpace B — 900 sq ft vs 850 sq ft
    3. CThey are equal
    4. DSpace B — because a square always beats a rectangle

    A: 25 × 40 = 1,000 sq ft. B: 30 × 30 = 900 sq ft. Space A has 100 more square feet.

    Watch for: A common mistake in Reflect Points Across Axes is flipping the sign of the wrong coordinate — changing the x-coordinate when reflecting over the x-axis, instead of the y-coordinate (or vice versa for the y-axis). For example, to reflect (3, -5) over the x-axis, a student might flip the x-coordinate and write (-3, -5), but reflecting over the x-axis only changes the sign of the y-coordinate: (3, -5) becomes (3, 5). Before you submit, ask: 'Did I flip the sign of the coordinate that matches the axis I'm reflecting over — y for the x-axis, x for the y-axis?'

    (Lesson 7.8) Find the Quadrant Error

    1. 1ProblemIdentify the quadrant for (-3, -5)
    2. 2Student thinkingThe x is -3, so I go left 3. The y is -5, so I go up 5.
    3. 3Student answer(-3, -5) is in Quadrant II

    Which step has the mistake? Explain it and write the correct work.

    The mistake is in Step 2. Correct work: A negative y-coordinate means DOWN, not up. Go left 3 and DOWN 5. The point (-3, -5) is in Quadrant III (both negative), not Quadrant II. A negative y-coordinate means down, not up. From the origin you go left 3 and DOWN 5, landing in the bottom-left region. Both coordinates are negative, and (-, -) is Quadrant III, not Quadrant II.

    (Lesson 7.9) Find the Reflection Error

    1. 1ProblemReflect (-3, 5) over the y-axis
    2. 2Student thinkingThe y-axis is vertical, so I change the y-coordinate
    3. 3Student answer(-3, -5)

    Which step has the mistake? Explain it and write the correct work.

    The mistake is in Step 2. Correct work: Reflecting over the y-axis changes the x-coordinate, not the y-coordinate. The correct reflection of (-3, 5) over the y-axis is (3, 5). The student confused which coordinate changes for each axis. The student's thinking step says the vertical y-axis changes the y-coordinate, but the axis name tells you which coordinate stays the same. Over the y-axis the rule is (x, y) → (-x, y), so (-3, 5) reflects to (3, 5), not (-3, -5).
