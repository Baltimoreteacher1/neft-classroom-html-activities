6.NS.1 Version A — Answer Key

Divide Fractions

Answer Key

Name: Date:
  1. 1

    What is the reciprocal of 3/5?

    1. A5/3
    2. B3/5
    3. C5/5
    4. D1/3

    To find the reciprocal, flip the fraction. The reciprocal of 3/5 is 5/3.

  2. 2

    What is 1/2 ÷ 1/4?

    1. A2
    2. B1/8
    3. C4
    4. D1/2

    1/2 ÷ 1/4 = 1/2 × 4/1 = 4/2 = 2. Two 1/4-size pieces fit into 1/2.

  3. 3

    What is 3/4 ÷ 1/8?

    1. A6
    2. B3/32
    3. C8/3
    4. D24

    3/4 ÷ 1/8 = 3/4 × 8/1 = 24/4 = 6. Six 1/8-size pieces fit into 3/4.

  4. 4

    Sort each division: will the quotient be GREATER than 1 or LESS than 1?

    Categories: greater · less

    • greater3/4 ÷ 1/4 = 3 (greater)
    • greater5/6 ÷ 1/3 = 5/2 (greater)
    • greater2/3 ÷ 1/6 = 4 (greater)
    • less1/4 ÷ 1/2 = 1/2 (less)
    • less1/3 ÷ 2/3 = 1/2 (less)
    • less2/5 ÷ 4/5 = 1/2 (less)
  5. 5

    What is 2/3 ÷ 1/3?

    1. A2
    2. B2/9
    3. C1/2
    4. D6

    2/3 ÷ 1/3 = 2/3 × 3/1 = 6/3 = 2. Two 1/3-size pieces fit into 2/3.
