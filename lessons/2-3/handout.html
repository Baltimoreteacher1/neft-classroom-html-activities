Describe the Data Using the Median

6.DS.4 · Unit 2 · Lesson 3

Name   Period

Objectives / Objetivos

Content Objective: I can find the median of a data set and use it to describe what is typical.

Language Objective: I can explain the median using the words order, middle, typical, and half.

Key Idea / Idea clave

Measures of Center: Median. Formula: Median = Middle value in an ordered set. 1. Order all data values from least to greatest. 2. Odd count: The median is the exact center number. 3. Even count: The median is the mean of the two middle numbers

I Notice / Observo

  • What are the highest and lowest scores?
  • Do any scores repeat?

I Wonder / Me pregunto

  • Will the mean and median be the same number?
