WordWhat it meansExample
Ratio Tables
Tablas de razones		Tables that organize pairs of equivalent ratios.
Tablas que organizan pares de razones equivalentes.		Rows 1:4, 2:8, 3:12 — Each row scales both parts equally.
Ratio table
Tabla de razones		A table of equal ratios that shows a pattern.
Una tabla de razones iguales que muestra un patrón.		juice 2, 4, 6 beside water 3, 6, 9 — Every column holds the same ratio.
Equivalent ratio
Razón equivalente		Two ratios that mean the same thing, like 1:2 and 2:4.
Dos razones que significan lo mismo, como 1:2 y 2:4.		1:2 and 2:4 — Both parts doubled.
Scale factor
Factor de escala		The number you multiply both parts of a ratio by to get an equal ratio.
El número por el que multiplicas ambas partes de una razón para obtener una razón igual.		The 4 that turns 3:8 into 12:32 — Both parts multiplied by it.
Pattern
Patrón		A rule that numbers follow again and again.
Una regla que los números siguen una y otra vez.		2, 4, 6, 8 — add 2 each time — The rule repeats predictably.
Additive pattern
Patrón aditivo		Building new rows of a ratio table by adding each column's own constant amount, so the two columns grow together.
Formar nuevas filas de una tabla de razones sumando a cada columna su propia cantidad constante, para que las dos columnas crezcan juntas.		Going down a ratio table by +2 and +3 together — Each column grows by its own constant.
Equivalent ratios
Razones equivalentes		Ratios that use different numbers but compare the same way.
Razones que usan números diferentes pero comparan de la misma manera.		2 : 1 and 6 : 3 — Both terms of 2 : 1 were multiplied by 3.
Simplify
Simplificar		Divide both terms of a ratio by the same number.
Dividir ambos términos de una razón entre el mismo número.		Changing 8 : 20 to 2 : 5 — Both terms were divided by 4.
Greatest common factor
Máximo común divisor		The largest number that divides both terms evenly.
El mayor número que divide a ambos términos exactamente.		4, for 8 and 20 — 4 divides both, and no larger number does.

How do ratio tables work?

A ratio table lists equal ratios in rows. To make a new row, you multiply BOTH numbers by the same amount, called the scale factor.

Key idea: Equivalent Ratios & Ratio Tables. 1. Multiply or divide both terms in a ratio by the same non-zero scale factor. 2. In a ratio table, rows and columns maintain the same proportional unit rate. 3. Use additive or multiplicative patterns to find missing values in the table

Watch me

GIVEN DATA / MODEL
  • The pancake recipe is 2 cups of mix for every 3 cups of milk, so the ratio is 2:3.
  • To make 2 batches, my scale factor is 2.
  • I multiply both numbers by 2: 2 x 2 = 4 cups of mix, and 3 x 2 = 6 cups of milk.
  • Now my row is 4:6, which is the same ratio as 2:3.
  • I do NOT add 2 to each number; I multiply both by the same factor.
