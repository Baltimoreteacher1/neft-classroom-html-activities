  1. 1

    A recipe uses 3 eggs for every 5 cups of flour. How many eggs are needed for 15 cups of flour?

    1. A9
    2. B6
    3. C10
    4. D12

    The scale factor is 15 ÷ 5 = 3. Multiply the eggs by 3: 3 × 3 = 9 eggs.

    Watch for: A common mistake in Ratio Tables is scaling only ONE part of the ratio, or adding the scale factor to a part instead of multiplying by it — for example, turning 2:3 into 6:3 (multiplying only the first number by 3 and forgetting the second) or into 4:5 (adding the scale factor 2 to the second number instead of multiplying, since 3+2=5 but 3×2=6). Before you submit, multiply BOTH numbers by the same scale factor and check that your new ratio simplifies back to the original.

    3. C12:15
