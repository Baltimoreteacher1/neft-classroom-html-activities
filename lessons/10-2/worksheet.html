    What is the volume of a rectangular prism with l = 4 in, w = 3 in, h = 5 in?

    1. A60 in³
    2. B12 in³
    3. C60 in²
    4. D24 in³

    V = l × w × h = 4 × 3 × 5 = 60 cubic inches.

    Watch for: A common mistake in Volume of Rectangular Prisms is rounding a fractional edge length to the nearest whole number before multiplying, instead of using the exact fraction or decimal. For a box that is 2 ft × 1.5 ft × 1 ft, a student might round 1.5 down to 1 and compute 2 × 1 × 1 = 2 ft³, but the correct volume uses the exact edge length: 2 × 1.5 × 1 = 3 ft³ — a full cubic foot larger. Before you submit, ask: 'Did I multiply using the exact fractional or decimal edge length, or did I round it off first?'
