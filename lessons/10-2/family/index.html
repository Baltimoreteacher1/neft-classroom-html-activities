Unit 10, Lesson 2: Math is BeautyMPP.7

A quick guide for families and for any student who missed class.

What students are learning

Look closely at a butterfly's wings, then at how a plant grows its leaves. The same mathematical idea keeps appearing.

Key idea: Symmetry, Geometry & Design. 1. Line of Symmetry: Divides a figure into two congruent mirror-image halves. 2. Rotational Symmetry: Shape rotates onto itself in less than a full 360° turn. 3. Geometric transformations preserve angle measures and side lengths

Key words

  • symmetric — Having two halves that match — one side is a mirror image of the other.
    Español: simétrico — Que tiene dos mitades que coinciden: un lado es la imagen reflejada del otro.
  • mirror image — A copy of a figure flipped across a line, so the two halves face each other.
    Español: imagen reflejada — Una copia de una figura volteada al otro lado de una línea, de modo que las dos mitades se miran entre sí.
  • bilateral symmetry — Reflective symmetry in plants and animals — the two sides of the body mirror each other.
    Español: simetría bilateral — La simetría reflectiva en plantas y animales: los dos lados del cuerpo se reflejan entre sí.
  • line of symmetry — The line you can draw through a figure so each side is a reflection of the other.
    Español: eje de simetría — La línea que puedes trazar por una figura de modo que cada lado sea el reflejo del otro.
  • balance — An even, steady arrangement — neither side feels heavier than the other.
    Español: equilibrio — Una disposición pareja y estable: ningún lado se siente más pesado que el otro.

Example

  1. I look at the two sides of the butterfly, and they look like mirror images — the butterfly is symmetric.
  2. All butterflies have reflective symmetry. In plants and animals, this is called bilateral symmetry.
  3. To show it, I draw a line down the middle of the butterfly's body: everything on the left is reflected on the right.

Common mistake

Thinking a figure lacks bilateral symmetry just because its line of symmetry is not vertical — the mirror line can run in any direction, and the test is whether the two sides match across it.
