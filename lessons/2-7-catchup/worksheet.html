    (Lesson 2.6) Spot the Mistake in 2,058 ÷ 14

    1. 1ProblemDivide 2,058 ÷ 14 with long division. Dividend = 2,058, divisor = 14.
    2. 2Divide, Multiply, Subtract14 does not fit into 2, so start with 20. 20 ÷ 14 → 1. 1 × 14 = 14, and 20 − 14 = 6
    3. 3Bring down, repeatBring down the 5 to make 65. 65 ÷ 14 → 4. 4 × 14 = 56, and 65 − 56 = 9
    4. 4Bring down, repeatBring down the 8 to make 98. 98 ÷ 14 → 8. 8 × 14 = 112, and 98 − 112 = −14
    5. 5Final answerQuotient = 148

    Which step has the mistake? Explain it and write the correct work.
