Volume of Rectangular Prisms

6.GR.2 · Unit 5 · Lesson 10

Name   Period

Objectives / Objetivos

Content Objective: I can find the volume of a rectangular prism, including ones with fractional edge lengths, using base area × height.

Language Objective: I can explain my work using the words volume, rectangular prism, dimensions, and base area.

Key Idea / Idea clave

Volume of Rectangular Prisms with Fractional Edges. Formula: V = l × w × h = B × h. 1. Convert mixed numbers to improper fractions before multiplying. 2. Multiply numerators across, multiply denominators across: V = (l · w · h). 3. Simplify the fraction and label with cubic units

I Notice / Observo

  • What are the three measurements of the time capsule?
  • What does volume measure — length, area, or space inside?

I Wonder / Me pregunto

  • What if we made the capsule taller but narrower — would the volume change?

Practice Preview / Vista previa de práctica

  1. What is the volume of a rectangular prism with l = 4 in, w = 3 in, h = 5 in?
    1. 60 in³
    2. 12 in³
    3. 60 in²
    4. 24 in³
  2. A shipping container is 8 ft long, 4 ft wide, and 3 ft tall. It is half full of boxes.
  3. Find the missing dimension or volume for each container.
  4. Find the Volume Error
    1. Step 1: Dimensions — l = 5 m, w = 3 m, h = 2.5 m
    2. Step 2: Formula — V = l × w × h
    3. Step 3: Calculate — V = 5 × 3 × 2.5 = 5 × 5.5 = 27.5 m³
    4. Step 4: Answer — Volume = 27.5 m³

    Which step has the mistake? Explain it and write the correct work.
