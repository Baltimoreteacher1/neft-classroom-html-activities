🚀 Launch

Welcome to the arcade prize shop! You've been saving up tickets and now it's time to spend them. Some prizes are on sale with discounts, but the shop also charges a 5% ticket tax on purchases. Plus, there's a 10% markup on limited-edition prizes. Can you figure out the final ticket cost for each prize?

How do I find a price after a discount and then a tax?

A discount lowers a price. A tax, tip, or markup raises a price. Each one is a percent of the price.

Key idea: Take the discount off first to get the new lower price, then add the tax on that new price.

Watch me

  • A bear costs 200 tickets, 25% off, then a 5% tax is added.
  • First the discount: 0.25 × 200 = 50 off, so the price is 200 − 50 = 150.
  • Then the tax on the new price: 0.05 × 150 = 7.5.
  • I add it on: 150 + 7.5 = 157.5 tickets is the final price.

Let's try together

  • A keychain costs 80 tickets with 10% off, then 5% tax.
  • What is 10% of 80, and what is the price after the discount? (8 off, so 72)
  • Now find 5% tax on 72 and add it. What is the final price? (3.6 tax, total 75.6)

Now it's your turn

  • In the table, find the discount, the price after discount, the tax, and the final price.
  • Always take the discount first, then add the tax.
