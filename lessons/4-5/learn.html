How do I find a price after a discount and then a tax?

A discount lowers a price. A tax, tip, or markup raises a price. Each one is a percent of the price.

Key idea

Take the discount off first to get the new lower price, then add the tax on that new price.

🌍Apply itUse the math in a real situation

Book Fair

Students were asked which night of the book fair they planned to attend. The results of the survey are shown. Twenty of the students who planned to attend on Wednesday attended on Thursday instead. Twenty-five percent of the students who planned to attend on Thursday attended on Friday instead. Which day, Thursday or Friday, had a greater actual attendance? How many more students attended that day?

Show your work

My answer

Check my answer
✅ Sample answer: The survey shows 70 students planned for Wednesday, 112 for Thursday, and 65 for Friday. 20% of the 70 Wednesday planners moved to Thursday: 0.20 × 70 = 14 students. 25% of the 112 Thursday planners moved to Friday: 0.25 × 112 = 28 students. Actual Thursday attendance = 112 − 28 + 14 = 98. Actual Friday attendance = 65 + 28 = 93. Thursday had greater actual attendance, by 98 − 93 = 5 students.
