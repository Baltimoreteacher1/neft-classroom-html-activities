Use Percent to Solve Problems

6.AT.4 · Unit 4 · Lesson 5

Name   Period

Objectives / Objetivos

Content Objective: I can solve problems involving discounts, markups, tax, and tips using percents.

Language Objective: I can explain my solution using the words discount, markup, tax, tip, and percent.

Key Idea / Idea clave

Take the discount off first to get the new lower price, then add the tax on that new price.

I Notice / Observo

  • What happens to the price when there is a discount?
  • What happens to the price when tax is added?

I Wonder / Me pregunto

  • Does it matter whether you apply the discount or tax first?

Vocabulary / Vocabulario

Term / TérminoDefinition / Definición
Percent Problem Solving
Resolución de problemas con porcentajes		Use a percent to find a part, a whole, or a percent change in a real situation.
Usar un porcentaje para hallar una parte, un total o un cambio porcentual en una situación real.
Discount
Descuento		Money taken off the first price to make it cheaper.
Dinero que se quita del precio original para que sea más barato.
Markup
Recargo (margen)		The extra amount a store adds to what it paid, found by taking a percent of that cost. Cost + markup = selling price.
La cantidad extra que una tienda suma a lo que pagó, hallada tomando un porcentaje de ese costo. Costo + recargo = precio de venta.
Tax
Impuesto		Extra money added to the price of a purchase for the government, found by taking a percent of the price. Price + tax = total.
Dinero extra que se suma al precio de una compra para el gobierno, hallado tomando un porcentaje del precio. Precio + impuesto = total.
Tip
Propina		Extra money you add for good service, found by taking a percent of the bill. Bill + tip = total.
Dinero extra que agregas por un buen servicio, hallado tomando un porcentaje de la cuenta. Cuenta + propina = total.
Percent
Porcentaje		A way to compare a number to 100, shown with the % sign.
Una manera de comparar un número con 100, con el signo %.

Practice Preview / Vista previa de práctica

  1. A prize costs 120 tickets and is 25% off. What is the sale price?
    1. 90 tickets
    2. 95 tickets
    3. 100 tickets
    4. 30 tickets
  2. Solve each percent word problem.
  3. A store marks up a $30 item by 40%, then a customer gets 10% off. What is the final price?
    1. $37.80
    2. $39.00
    3. $42.00
    4. $36.00
  4. Find the Discount Error
    1. Step 1: A prize costs 160 tickets at 25% off. Find the sale price. — 160 tickets, 25% off
    2. Step 2: Find 25% of 160 — 25 × 160 = 4,000
    3. Step 3: Subtract discount — 160 − 4,000 = −3,840
    4. Step 4: Final answer — Sale price is −3,840 tickets

    Which step has the mistake? Explain it and write the correct work.
