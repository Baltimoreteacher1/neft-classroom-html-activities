THE UNIT RATE RECIPE

One denominator. One clear comparison.

A rate compares two quantities. A unit rate compares a quantity to exactly 1 of another quantity—like dollars per ounce or miles per hour.

  1. 01Write the rate as a fraction.
  2. 02Divide both parts by the bottom number.
  3. 03State unit rate as a fraction over 1.
  4. 04Compare unit rates for the best buy.
EXAMPLE
$68 notebooks = $6 ÷ 88 ÷ 8 = $0.751 notebook =$0.75per notebook

How unit rates work

1

Write the rate

2

Divide

The fraction bar tells you to divide the top number by the bottom number.

3

Name “per 1”

Your quotient tells the cost for exactly one item, ounce, sheet, or other unit.

4

Compare fairly

When units match, the lower unit price is the best buy.
