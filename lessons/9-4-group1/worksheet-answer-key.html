Lesson 9.49.4 Small Group · Group 1

Group 1 · Answer KeySupported practice

Learning target With my small group, I can write an equation to represent the relationship between two variable quantities and use it to solve a real problem — one step at a time, with support.

  1. 1Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    A bakery makes 1.5 cakes every hour. It gets an order for 60 cakes. Let h = hours and c = cakes. How many hours does the bakery need? It works 8 hours a day. How many days is that?

    1. 1Write an equation for the cakes made in h hours.c = 1.5h
    2. 2Substitute 60 for c. What equation do you get?1.5h = 60
    3. 3Divide both sides by 1.5. How many hours?40 hours
    4. 4The bakery works 8 hours a day. How many days is that?5 days
    Final answer:The bakery needs 40 hours. That is 40 ÷ 8 = 5 days.
  2. 2Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    A club sells bracelets for $3 each. Their goal is $100. Let b = bracelets sold and t = total raised. How many bracelets must they sell?

    1. 1Write 1 bracelet → $3. What scale factor takes $3 to $100?3b = 100, so 100 ÷ 3 ≈ 33.3. It is not whole.
    2. 2How much money do 33 bracelets raise?$99
    3. 3What is the least number of bracelets that reaches $100?34 bracelets
    Final answer:They must sell at least 34 bracelets. 33 bracelets raise only $99.
  3. 3Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    A machine fills 5 boxes every minute. Let m = minutes and b = boxes. How many minutes does it take to fill 45 boxes?

    Hint The equation is b = 5m. Put in 45 for b. Then divide both sides by 5.

    Final answer:9 minutes

    Why: Write the rate in the table: 1 min → 5 boxes Find the scale factor: 45 ÷ 5 = 9 Do the same to the minutes: 1 × 9 = 9

  4. 4Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    A factory makes 3.5 bikes every hour. Let h = hours and b = bikes. How many hours does the factory need to make 280 bikes?

    Hint Write b = 3.5h. Put in 280 for b. Which operation undoes multiplying by 3.5?

    Final answer:80 hours

    Why: Write the rate in the table: 1 h → 3.5 bikes Find the scale factor: 280 ÷ 3.5 = 80 Do the same to the hours: 1 × 80 = 80

  5. 5Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    A school earns $8 for each plant it sells. The goal is $150. Let p = plants sold. What is the least number of plants they must sell to reach the goal?

    Hint Solve 8p = 150. The answer is not whole. Check the whole numbers on each side of it.

    Final answer:19 plants

    Why: Find the scale factor: 150 ÷ 8 = 18.75 Check 18 plants: 18 × 8 = 144 Round up to reach the goal: 19 × 8 = 152

  6. 6Circle the letter of the best answer. Show how you know.

    Kim has $20. Notebooks cost $3 each. She solves 3n = 20 and gets n ≈ 6.7. She says she can buy 7 notebooks. Is she right?

    1. ANo. 7 notebooks cost $21, so she can buy 6.
    2. BYes. 6.7 rounds up to 7.
    3. CNo. She can buy 6.7 notebooks.

    Hint Find the cost of 7 notebooks. Can Kim pay for them with $20?

    Answer: A — No. 7 notebooks cost $21, so she can buy 6.

    Why: Write the rate in the table: 1 notebook → $3 Check 7 notebooks: 7 × 3 = 21 Check 6 notebooks: 6 × 3 = 18

    Watch for: Adding the rate instead of multiplying — writing 2.5 + 8 = 10.5 cars for an 8-hour shift instead of 2.5 × 8 = 20. The rate applies to EVERY hour, so the total is always rate × amount.
