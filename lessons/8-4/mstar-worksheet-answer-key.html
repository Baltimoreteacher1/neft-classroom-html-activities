Question 1Two-part question

Part A — correct answer: A

A fair ride requires each rider to be at least 48 inches tall. Let h be a rider's height in inches. Which inequality represents the heights that are allowed to ride?

  • Ah ≥ 48
  • Bh > 48
  • Ch ≤ 48
  • Dh < 48

The phrase 'at least 48 inches' means 48 inches is allowed and any greater height is allowed too. That is greater than or equal to, so the inequality is h ≥ 48.

  • B: This shuts out a rider who is exactly 48 inches tall. 'At least' includes the 48.
  • C: This admits only riders 48 inches and SHORTER. Taller riders are allowed too.
  • D: This admits only riders under 48 inches, which is the opposite of the rule.

Part B — correct answer: C

Which explanation justifies the symbol chosen above?

  • AThe words 'at least' mean a rider must be taller than 48 inches, so 48 inches is not allowed.
  • BThe words 'at least' point to the smallest numbers, so riders must be shorter than 48 inches.
  • CThe words 'at least' include the boundary value, so a rider who is exactly 48 inches tall may ride.
  • DThe words 'at least' and 'at most' describe the same group of heights, so either symbol works.

'At least' sets a minimum that is part of the group, which is why the symbol carries the bar underneath. A rider who measures exactly 48 inches meets the rule, so h ≥ 48 rather than h > 48.

Question 2Written response · 2 points

Type II Reasoning: Reversing 'No More Than'

Priya read this situation: 'A backpack can hold no more than 15 pounds.' She let w stand for the number of pounds in the backpack and wrote the inequality w ≥ 15.

Explain Priya's misconception and give the correct inequality.

Model answer: w ≤ 15. 'No more than 15 pounds' means the weight may be 15 pounds or anything below 15 pounds, so the correct inequality is w ≤ 15. Priya's inequality w ≥ 15 describes weights of 15 pounds and heavier, which is the opposite group. She kept the boundary number but pointed the symbol the wrong way.

2 pointsStudent explains that 'no more than' sets a maximum the weight cannot pass, so the symbol must be less than or equal to, and writes w ≤ 15.
1 pointStudent writes w ≤ 15 but does not explain why 'no more than' sets a maximum.
0 pointsStudent agrees with Priya or gives an unrelated response.
