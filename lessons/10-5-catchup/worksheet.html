  1. 1

    (Lesson 10.4) What is the surface area of a rectangular prism with l = 5 cm, w = 4 cm, h = 3 cm?

    1. A94 cm²
    2. B60 cm²
    3. C94 cm³
    4. D47 cm²

    SA = 2(5×4) + 2(5×3) + 2(4×3) = 40 + 30 + 24 = 94 cm².

    Watch for: A common mistake in Surface Area of Pyramids is finding the four triangular lateral faces correctly (½ × base edge × slant height, times 4) and then stopping — forgetting to add the base area at the end. This leaves the total surface area too small by exactly the base's area. Before you submit, ask: 'Did I add the base area to the four lateral faces, or did I stop after the sides?'

    (Lesson 10.4) How many faces does a triangular prism have?

    1. A5
    2. B4
    3. C6
    4. D3

    A triangular prism has 5 faces: 2 triangular bases and 3 rectangular lateral faces.

    Watch for: A common mistake in Surface Area of Pyramids is finding the four triangular lateral faces correctly (½ × base edge × slant height, times 4) and then stopping — forgetting to add the base area at the end. This leaves the total surface area too small by exactly the base's area. Before you submit, ask: 'Did I add the base area to the four lateral faces, or did I stop after the sides?'

    (Lesson 10.5) A square pyramid has a base edge of 5 in and a slant height of 7 in. What is the surface area?

    1. A95 in²
    2. B70 in²
    3. C95 in³
    4. D120 in²

    Base: 5 × 5 = 25 in². Each lateral face: ½ × 5 × 7 = 17.5 in². Four faces: 4 × 17.5 = 70 in². SA = 25 + 70 = 95 in².

    Watch for: A common mistake in Surface Area of Pyramids is finding the four triangular lateral faces correctly (½ × base edge × slant height, times 4) and then stopping — forgetting to add the base area at the end. This leaves the total surface area too small by exactly the base's area. Before you submit, ask: 'Did I add the base area to the four lateral faces, or did I stop after the sides?'

  4. 4

    (Lesson 10.5) How many lateral (triangular) faces does a square pyramid have?

    1. A4
    2. B3
    3. C5
    4. D6

    A square pyramid has a square base and 4 triangular lateral faces — one for each side of the square.

    Watch for: A common mistake in Surface Area of Pyramids is finding the four triangular lateral faces correctly (½ × base edge × slant height, times 4) and then stopping — forgetting to add the base area at the end. This leaves the total surface area too small by exactly the base's area. Before you submit, ask: 'Did I add the base area to the four lateral faces, or did I stop after the sides?'

  1. 1

    (Lesson 10.4) A triangular prism has triangular bases with base 6 in and height 4 in. The prism is 10 in long and the two slant sides of the triangle are each 5 in. What is the surface area?

    1. A184 in²
    2. B160 in²
    3. C124 in²
    4. D184 in³

    Triangular bases: 2 × (½ × 6 × 4) = 24 in². Three rectangular faces: 6(10) + 5(10) + 5(10) = 60 + 50 + 50 = 160 in². SA = 24 + 160 = 184 in².

    Watch for: A common mistake in Surface Area of Pyramids is finding the four triangular lateral faces correctly (½ × base edge × slant height, times 4) and then stopping — forgetting to add the base area at the end. This leaves the total surface area too small by exactly the base's area. Before you submit, ask: 'Did I add the base area to the four lateral faces, or did I stop after the sides?'

  2. 2

    (Lesson 10.4) Spot the Common Mistake — Surface Area or Volume?

    1. 1DimensionsRectangular prism time capsule: l = 7 in, w = 4 in, h = 3 in. Find the surface area.
    2. 2FormulaSurface area adds all 6 faces: SA = 2lw + 2lh + 2wh.
    3. 3CalculateThe student multiplied the three dimensions: 7 × 4 × 3 = 84, so they wrote SA = 84 in².

    Which step has the mistake? Explain it and write the correct work.

    The mistake is in Step 3. Correct work: Multiplying l × w × h gives the VOLUME (84 in³), not surface area. Add all six faces: SA = 2(7×4) + 2(7×3) + 2(4×3) = 56 + 42 + 24 = 122 in².

  3. 3

    (Lesson 10.5) A square pyramid has a base area of 49 cm² and a total lateral area of 84 cm². What is the surface area?

    1. A133 cm²
    2. B84 cm²
    3. C49 cm²
    4. D133 cm³

    SA = Base Area + Lateral Area = 49 + 84 = 133 cm².

    Watch for: A common mistake in Surface Area of Pyramids is finding the four triangular lateral faces correctly (½ × base edge × slant height, times 4) and then stopping — forgetting to add the base area at the end. This leaves the total surface area too small by exactly the base's area. Before you submit, ask: 'Did I add the base area to the four lateral faces, or did I stop after the sides?'

  4. 4

    (Lesson 10.5) Did You Halve the Triangle?

    1. 1BoxSquare pyramid: base edge = 8 in, slant height = 10 in
    2. 2Base Area8 × 8 = 64 in²
    3. 3One Triangular Face8 × 10 = 80 in² (forgot the ½)
    4. 4AnswerSA = 64 + 4 × 80 = 64 + 320 = 384 in²

    Which step has the mistake? Explain it and write the correct work.

    The mistake is in Step 3. Correct work: A triangle's area is ½ × base × height, so each face is ½ × 8 × 10 = 40 in², not 80 in². The student left out the ½. Four faces: 4 × 40 = 160 in². Correct SA = 64 + 160 = 224 in².

    2. 2FormulaSA = 2lw + 2lh + 2wh

    1. 1DimensionsSquare pyramid: base edge = 10 in, slant height = 8 in

    4. 4AnswerSA = 100 + 120 = 220 in²

    Which step has the mistake? Explain it and write the correct work.

    The mistake is in Step 3. Correct work: A square pyramid has 4 lateral faces, not 3. Total lateral = 40 × 4 = 160 in². Correct: SA = 100 + 160 = 260 in².
