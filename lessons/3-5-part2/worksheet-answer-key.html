Learning target I can compare ratios by finding unit rates or using equivalent ratios.

  1. 1Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    Shop A sells 2 cookies for $4. Shop B sells 3 cookies for $9. What is the lower price for 1 cookie?

    Hint Divide each cost by the number of cookies.

    Final answer:$2

    Why: Find each price for 1 cookie: $4 ÷ 2 = $2; $9 ÷ 3 = $3 Choose the lower price: $2 < $3

  2. 2Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    Drink A uses 1 cup of juice for 2 cups of water. Drink B uses 2 cups of juice for 3 cups of water. Scale both to 6 cups of water. Which drink has more juice?

    Hint With the same water, the drink with more juice wins.

    Final answer:Drink B

    Why: Scale both drinks to 6 cups: 1 × 3 = 3 → 3:6; 2 × 2 = 4 → 4:6 Compare the juice: 4 > 3

  3. 3Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    Bag A has 4 oranges for $2. Bag B has 6 oranges for $4. Which bag costs less for 1 orange?

    Hint Divide each cost by the number of oranges. The lower price wins.

    Final answer:Bag A

    Why: Find each price for 1 orange: $2 ÷ 4 = $0.50; $4 ÷ 6 ≈ $0.67 Choose the lower price: $0.50 < $0.67

    2. BBox B — $0.50 per bar
    3. CBox B — it has more bars
    4. DThey cost the same per bar

    Answer: A — Box A — $0.40 per bar

    Why: Find each price for 1 bar: $4 ÷ 10 = $0.40; $6 ÷ 12 = $0.50 Choose the lower price: $0.40 < $0.50

  2. 2Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    Drink A uses 3 scoops of powder for 5 cups of water. Drink B uses 5 scoops for 8 cups of water. Scale both to 40 cups of water. For 40 cups of water, how many scoops does the stronger drink use?

    Final answer:25

    Why: Scale Drink A by 8: 3 × 8 = 24 → 24:40 Scale Drink B by 5: 5 × 5 = 25 → 25:40 Pick the larger number of scoops: 25 > 24

  3. 3Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    Store A sells 3 pens for $3.60. Store B sells 5 pens for $5.50. What is the lower price for 1 pen?

    Final answer:$1.10

    Why: Find each price for 1 pen: $3.60 ÷ 3 = $1.20; $5.50 ÷ 5 = $1.10 Choose the lower price: $1.10 < $1.20

    Ty says the ratio 7:10 is greater than 3:4, because 7 > 3. Which statement is true?

    1. A3:4 is greater
    2. B7:10 is greater
    3. CThey are equal

    Answer: A — 3:4 is greater

Part 2Apply itreal-world problems
  1. 2Circle the letter of the best answer. Show how you know.

    Car A travels 180 miles on 6 gallons. Car B travels 225 miles on 9 gallons. Which car gets better gas mileage?

    1. ACar B, at 25 miles per gallon
    2. BCar B, because it traveled farther
    3. CThey are equal
    4. DCar A, at 30 miles per gallon

    Answer: D — Car A, at 30 miles per gallon

    Why: Car A: 180 ÷ 6 = 30 miles per gallon. Car B: 225 ÷ 9 = 25 miles per gallon. 30 > 25, so Car A gets better mileage.

  2. 3Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    Tea A uses 2 spoons of honey for 9 cups of tea. Tea B uses 3 spoons of honey for 12 cups of tea. Which tea is sweeter?

    Final answer:Tea B

    Why: Scale Tea A by 4: 2 × 4 = 8 → 8:36 Scale Tea B by 3: 3 × 3 = 9 → 9:36 Compare the honey: 9 > 8
