Lesson 3.5Comparing Rates with Benchmarks

Apply Day · Set B · Answer KeySecond form · new problems, same idea

Learning target I can compare ratios by finding unit rates or using equivalent ratios.

  1. 1Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    Pack A has 2 apples for $1. Pack B has 5 apples for $3. What is the lower price for 1 apple?

    Final answer:$0.50

    Why: Write each pack in its own table: 2 : $1; 5 : $3 Divide each table down to 1 apple: $1 ÷ 2 = $0.50; $3 ÷ 5 = $0.60 Choose the lower price: $0.50 < $0.60

  2. 2Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    Chef Reyes uses 2 tablespoons of cocoa for 3 ounces of milk. Chef Tran uses 3 tablespoons for 5 ounces of milk. Scale both to 15 ounces of milk. Whose cocoa is more chocolatey?

    Final answer:Chef Reyes

    Why: Find each scale factor to 15 ounces: 15 ÷ 3 = 5; 15 ÷ 5 = 3 Scale Chef Reyes's recipe by 5: 2 × 5 = 10 → 10:15 Scale Chef Tran's recipe by 3: 3 × 3 = 9 → 9:15 Compare the cocoa: 10 > 9

  3. 3Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    Mix A uses 4 cups of juice for 10 cups of water. Mix B uses 6 cups of juice for ? cups of water. The two mixes taste the same. Find the missing number.

    Final answer:15

    Why: Write Mix A, 4 : 10, in the table. Divide both numbers by 2: 4 ÷ 2 = 2; 10 ÷ 2 = 5 Multiply both numbers by 3: 2 × 3 = 6; 5 × 3 = 15

  4. 4Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    Paint A mixes 1 cup of red with 2 cups of white. Paint B mixes 2 cups of red with 5 cups of white. Which paint is more red?

    Final answer:Paint A

    Why: Find each scale factor to 10 cups: 10 ÷ 2 = 5; 10 ÷ 5 = 2 Scale Paint A by 5: 1 × 5 = 5 → 5:10 Scale Paint B by 2: 2 × 2 = 4 → 4:10 Compare the red: 5 > 4

  5. 5Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    Bird A flies 54 miles in 3 hours. Bird B flies 75 miles in 5 hours. How many more miles per hour does the faster bird fly?

    Final answer:3

    Why: Write each bird in its own table: 3 : 54; 5 : 75 Divide each table down to 1 hour: 54 ÷ 3 = 18; 75 ÷ 5 = 15 Subtract the unit rates: 18 − 15 = 3

  6. 6Complete each step, then write the final answer.

    Shop A sells 6 pens for $4.50. Shop B sells 8 pens for $5.60. Shop C sells 10 pens for $7.20. What is the lowest price for 1 pen?

    Final answer:$0.70

    Why: Write each shop in its own table: 6 : $4.50; 8 : $5.60; 10 : $7.20 Divide each table down to 1 pen: $4.50 ÷ 6 = $0.75; $5.60 ÷ 8 = $0.70; $7.20 ÷ 10 = $0.72 Choose the lowest price: $0.70 < $0.72 < $0.75

  7. 7Circle the letter of the best answer. Show how you know.

    Chef A uses 2 cups of cheese for every 8 crackers. Chef B uses 3 cups of cheese for every 9 crackers. Who uses more cheese per cracker?

    1. AChef A
    2. BChef B
    3. CThey use the same amount
    4. DCannot determine

    Answer: B — Chef B

    Why: Chef A: 2÷8 = 0.25 cups per cracker. Chef B: 3÷9 ≈ 0.33 cups per cracker. Chef B uses more cheese per cracker.

  8. 8Circle the letter of the best answer. Show how you know.

    Which ratio is greater: 3:4 or 5:8?

    1. A5:8
    2. B3:4
    3. CThey are equal
    4. DCannot determine

    Answer: B — 3:4

    Why: Convert to the same denominator: 3:4 = 6:8. Since 6:8 > 5:8, the ratio 3:4 is greater.

  9. 9Circle the letter of the best answer. Show how you know.

    Which lemonade recipe is more lemony? Recipe A: 2 lemons for 6 cups of water. Recipe B: 3 lemons for 7 cups of water.

    1. ARecipe B
    2. BRecipe A
    3. CThey are the same
    4. DCannot determine

    Answer: A — Recipe B

    Why: Recipe A: 2÷6 ≈ 0.33 lemons per cup. Recipe B: 3÷7 ≈ 0.43 lemons per cup. Since 0.43 > 0.33, Recipe B is more lemony.
