Learning target I can use a table and a graph to determine and analyze the relationship between two variable quantities.

  1. 1Circle the letter of the best answer. Show how you know.

    Football tickets cost $45 each. What is the total cost of 3 tickets?

    1. A$45
    2. B$48
    3. C$90
    4. D$135

    Hint What is the cost of ONE ticket?

    Answer: D — $135

    Why: The total cost is 45 times the number of tickets, and 3 × 45 = 135.

    Watch for: Reversing the coordinates of a point — writing (cost, tickets) instead of (tickets, cost). The independent variable always comes first in the ordered pair.

  2. 2Circle the letter of the best answer. Show how you know.

    Which ordered pair belongs on the ticket graph for 2 tickets?

    1. A(2, 90)
    2. B(2, 45)
    3. C(2, 47)
    4. D(90, 2)

    Hint Which quantity goes first in the ordered pair?

    Answer: A — (2, 90)

    Why: The number of tickets goes first, and 2 tickets cost 2 × 45 = 90 dollars, so the point is (2, 90).

    Watch for: Reversing the coordinates of a point — writing (cost, tickets) instead of (tickets, cost). The independent variable always comes first in the ordered pair.

    Hint Which TWO quantities change in this situation?

    Answer: B — Number of tickets and total cost

Challenge · Answer KeyExtension

Learning target I can use a table and a graph to determine and analyze the relationship between two variable quantities.

Part 1Understand the ideamodels and meaning
  1. 1Plot and label each point on the grid.

    Plot the ticket-cost relationship: one point for each number of tickets.

    Answer: (1, 45), (2, 90), (3, 135), (4, 180)

    Why: The four points (1, 45), (2, 90), (3, 135), (4, 180) line up on a straight path from the origin: each extra ticket adds exactly $45. That constant climb IS the rate of change, and seeing it in the lined-up points is what 'analyzing a graph' means in this lesson.

Part 2Apply itreal-world problems
  1. 2Circle the letter of the best answer. Show how you know.

    Nakai's savings account has a principal of $750 and earns 3.5% simple interest each year. How much interest does Nakai earn in one year?

    1. A$3.50
    2. B$26.25
    3. C$75.00
    4. D$262.50

    Answer: B — $26.25

    Why: One year of interest is 3.5% of $750: 0.035 × 750 = 26.25.

    Watch for: Reversing the coordinates of a point — writing (cost, tickets) instead of (tickets, cost). The independent variable always comes first in the ordered pair.

  2. 3Circle the letter of the best answer. Show how you know.

    2. B(4, 26.25)
    3. C(4, 78.75)
    4. D(4, 750)

    Answer: A — (4, 105)

    Why: After 4 years the interest earned is 4 × 26.25 = 105 dollars, so the point is (4, 105).

    Watch for: Reversing the coordinates of a point — writing (cost, tickets) instead of (tickets, cost). The independent variable always comes first in the ordered pair.

  3. 4Circle the letter of the best answer. Show how you know.

    3. C6,000 feet
    4. D12,000 feet

    Answer: C — 6,000 feet

    Why: The remaining distance is 18,000 − 12,000 = 6,000 feet — which matches the last 5 minutes at 1,200 feet per minute.

    Watch for: Reversing the coordinates of a point — writing (cost, tickets) instead of (tickets, cost). The independent variable always comes first in the ordered pair.

    Answer: C — The Sandia Peak line (1,200 ft/min) rises more steeply than the 900 ft/min line

    Why: In one minute, the Sandia tram climbs 1,200 feet on the graph while the other climbs only 900 — a faster rate makes a steeper line.
