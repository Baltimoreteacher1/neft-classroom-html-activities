    Football tickets cost $45 each. What is the total cost of 3 tickets?

    1. A$135
    2. B$48
    3. C$90
    4. D$45

    The total cost is 45 times the number of tickets, and 3 × 45 = 135.

    Watch for: Reversing the coordinates of a point — writing (cost, tickets) instead of (tickets, cost). The independent variable always comes first in the ordered pair.

  2. 2

    Which ordered pair belongs on the ticket graph for 2 tickets?

    1. A(2, 90)
    2. B(90, 2)
    3. C(2, 45)
    4. D(2, 47)

    The number of tickets goes first, and 2 tickets cost 2 × 45 = 90 dollars, so the point is (2, 90).

    Watch for: Reversing the coordinates of a point — writing (cost, tickets) instead of (tickets, cost). The independent variable always comes first in the ordered pair.

    1. ANumber of tickets and total cost
    2. BNumber of tickets and price of one ticket
    3. CTotal cost and $45
    4. DNumber of games and number of tickets

    Each axis is labeled with one quantity from the problem — one axis shows the number of tickets and the other shows the total cost.

    Watch for: Reversing the coordinates of a point — writing (cost, tickets) instead of (tickets, cost). The independent variable always comes first in the ordered pair.

      Nakai's savings account has a principal of $750 and earns 3.5% simple interest each year. How much interest does Nakai earn in one year?

      1. A$26.25
      2. B$262.50
      3. C$3.50
      4. D$75.00

      One year of interest is 3.5% of $750: 0.035 × 750 = 26.25.

      Watch for: Reversing the coordinates of a point — writing (cost, tickets) instead of (tickets, cost). The independent variable always comes first in the ordered pair.

      Nakai earns $26.25 in simple interest every year. On the graph of years vs. interest earned, which point represents 4 years?

      1. A(4, 105)
      2. B(4, 26.25)
      3. C(4, 78.75)
      4. D(4, 750)

      After 4 years the interest earned is 4 × 26.25 = 105 dollars, so the point is (4, 105).

      Watch for: Reversing the coordinates of a point — writing (cost, tickets) instead of (tickets, cost). The independent variable always comes first in the ordered pair.

      1. A6,000 feet
      2. B12,000 feet
      3. C5,000 feet
      4. D1,200 feet

      The remaining distance is 18,000 − 12,000 = 6,000 feet — which matches the last 5 minutes at 1,200 feet per minute.

      Watch for: Reversing the coordinates of a point — writing (cost, tickets) instead of (tickets, cost). The independent variable always comes first in the ordered pair.

      2. BThe 900 ft/min line rises more steeply
      3. CThe two lines are identical
      4. DThe 900 ft/min line goes downward

      In one minute, the Sandia tram climbs 1,200 feet on the graph while the other climbs only 900 — a faster rate makes a steeper line.

      Watch for: Reversing the coordinates of a point — writing (cost, tickets) instead of (tickets, cost). The independent variable always comes first in the ordered pair.
