  1. 1

    A rectangular prism has l = 6 in, w = 4 in, h = 2 in. What is its surface area?

    1. A88 in²
    2. B48 in²
    3. C88 in³
    4. D44 in²

    SA = 2(6×4) + 2(6×2) + 2(4×2) = 48 + 24 + 16 = 88 in².

    Watch for: A common mistake in Surface Area Using Nets is finding the area of only one face from each of the 3 matching pairs and forgetting to double it, instead of counting all 6 faces. For a box that is 9 in × 4 in × 5 in, a student might add just one face from each pair: (9×4) + (9×5) + (4×5) = 36 + 45 + 20 = 101 in², but the net actually has two of each face, so the correct total is 2(36) + 2(45) + 2(20) = 72 + 90 + 40 = 202 in² — exactly double. Before you submit, ask: 'Did I count both matching faces in each pair, or only one from each pair?'

  4. 4

    Spot the Mistake — A Counted Face Was Skipped

    1. 1ReadA time capsule box from the net is l = 6 in, w = 4 in, h = 2 in. Find the surface area.
    2. 2Label the facesTop/Bottom: 6×4 = 24, Front/Back: 6×2 = 12, Left/Right: 4×2 = 8
    3. 3Add the facesSA = 24 + 24 + 12 + 8 + 8 = 76 in² (only one front/back face counted)

    Which step has the mistake? Explain it and write the correct work.

    The mistake is in Step 3. Correct work: The student only counted one 12 in² front/back face. The net shows both a front AND a back, so both 12 in² faces must be added. Correct: SA = 24 + 24 + 12 + 12 + 8 + 8 = 88 in². The three face areas in the middle step are correct, but the addition line includes only one of the two 12 in² faces. The net shows a front AND a back, so both belong in the sum. Counting all six faces gives SA = 24 + 24 + 12 + 12 + 8 + 8 = 88 in², not 76 in².

  6. 6

    Spot the Mistake — One Pair Was Not Doubled

    1. 1ReadA time capsule box from the net is l = 7 in, w = 5 in, h = 4 in. Find the surface area.
    2. 2Find each face pairTop/Bottom: 7×5 = 35, Front/Back: 7×4 = 28, Left/Right: 5×4 = 20
    3. 3Apply the formulaSA = 2(35) + 2(28) + 20 = 70 + 56 + 20 = 146 in² (left/right pair not doubled)
    4. 4AnswerSurface Area = 146 in²

    Which step has the mistake? Explain it and write the correct work.

    The mistake is in Step 3. Correct work: The student doubled the first two pairs but forgot to double the left/right pair. There are two 20 in² faces, so it should be 2(20) = 40, not 20. Correct: SA = 70 + 56 + 40 = 166 in². The three face areas are right, but the formula line doubles only the first two pairs. The left and right faces are each 5 × 4 = 20 in², so that pair contributes 2(20) = 40 in², not 20 in². The correct total is SA = 70 + 56 + 40 = 166 in².

    Level 2 Extension — The Missing Back Face

    1. 1Box from the netA time capsule box: l = 8 in, w = 3 in, h = 2 in
    2. 2Label the facesTop/Bottom: 8×3 = 24, Front/Back: 8×2 = 16, Left/Right: 3×2 = 6
    3. 3Add the facesSA = 24 + 24 + 16 + 6 + 6 = 76 in² (only one front face counted)
    4. 4AnswerSurface Area = 76 in²

    Which step has the mistake? Explain it and write the correct work.

Correct: SA = 24 + 24 + 16 + 16 + 6 + 6 = 92 in². The three face areas are correct, but the addition line uses only one 8×2 = 16 in² face.   5. 5

    Find the Surface Area Error

    1. 1Dimensionsl = 10 in, w = 4 in, h = 3 in
    2. 2FormulaSA = 2lw + 2lh + 2wh
    3. 3CalculateSA = 2(10×4) + 2(10×3) + (4×3) = 80 + 60 + 12 = 152 in²
    4. 4AnswerSurface Area = 152 in²

    Which step has the mistake? Explain it and write the correct work.

    The mistake is in Step 3. Correct work: The student forgot to multiply the last face pair by 2. It should be 2(4×3) = 24, not 12. Correct: SA = 80 + 60 + 24 = 164 in². The formula SA = 2lw + 2lh + 2wh is written correctly, but in the calculation the last face pair loses its 2. The left and right faces are each 4 × 3 = 12 in², so they contribute 2(12) = 24 in². The correct total is SA = 80 + 60 + 24 = 164 in².
