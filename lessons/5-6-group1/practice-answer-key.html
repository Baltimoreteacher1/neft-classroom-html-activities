Where we left off

Our goal: With my small group, I can find the surface area of a solid by using its net — one step at a time, with support.

The big idea: A box has 3 pairs of matching faces, so add up all 6 faces to get the surface area.

Model to copy — Watch me

  1. My box is 8 in. long, 5 in. wide, and 3 in. tall. I unfold it into a net and see 3 pairs of faces.
  2. Top and bottom: each is 8 × 5 = 40, and there are 2 of them, so 2 × 40 = 80.
  3. Front and back: each is 8 × 3 = 24, so 2 × 24 = 48.
  4. Left and right: each is 5 × 3 = 15, so 2 × 15 = 30.
  5. I add all the pairs: 80 + 48 + 30 = 158. The surface area is 158 in².

  4. 4

    Warm restartHow is volume different from area?

    1. AVolume uses square units; area uses cubic units
    2. BVolume measures 3D space (cubic units); area measures 2D surface (square units)
    3. CVolume only applies to cubes; area applies to all shapes
    4. DThere is no difference

    Volume measures the space inside a 3D shape (cubic units like in³). Area measures a flat 2D surface (square units like in²).

Explain the thinking

  1. 9

    Find the mistakeAnother student made this exact mistake on this lesson. Explain why it is wrong, then write what they should have done.

    The mistake: A common mistake in Surface Area Using Nets is finding the area of only one face from each of the 3 matching pairs and forgetting to double it, instead of counting all 6 faces. For a box that is 9 in × 4 in × 5 in, a student might add just one face from each pair: (9×4) + (9×5) + (4×5) = 36 + 45 + 20 = 101 in², but the net actually has two of each face, so the correct total is 2(36) + 2(45) + 2(20) = 72 + 90 + 40 = 202 in² — exactly double. Before you submit, ask: 'Did I count both matching faces in each pair, or only one from each pair?'

    The full note above is the teacher's — the student sheet shows only the misconception, not the correction. A correct answer names the step that went wrong, states the rule it broke, and shows the fixed work.

    Watch for: a student who repeats the mistake back without naming why it is wrong.

    The faces come in ___ pairs because opposite faces are ___.

    Opposite faces match in size, so the six faces form three equal pairs.
