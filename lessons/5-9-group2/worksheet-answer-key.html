  3. Multiply: 5 × 6 = 30, so the total area is 30 square centimeters.
  4. Now prove it: say why that move had to work at all — not just that it did.
  5. Sentence frame — convince a skeptic: "My method works because ___ . It would stop working if ___ ."

Part 2Apply itreal-world problems
  1. 3Circle the letter of the best answer. Show how you know.

    A regular pentagonal tile splits into 5 congruent triangles. Each triangle has a base of 4 in and a height of 6 in. What is the total area of the tile?

    1. A12 in²
    2. B24 in²
    3. C60 in²
    4. D120 in²

    Answer: C — 60 in²

    Why: One triangle = ½ × 4 × 6 = 12 in². A pentagon has 5 triangles, so total = 5 × 12 = 60 in².

    Watch for: A common mistake in Area of Regular Polygons is stopping after finding the area of ONE triangle and reporting that as the total area — or adding the number of triangles instead of multiplying. Remember: total area = (one triangle's area) × (number of sides), not (one triangle's area) + (number of sides).

  2. 4Circle the letter of the best answer. Show how you know.

    An octagon tile splits into equal triangles, each with base 6 ft and height 5 ft. How many triangles, and what is the total area?

    1. A15 sq ft
    2. B90 sq ft
    3. C105 sq ft
    4. D120 sq ft

    Answer: D — 120 sq ft

    Why: An octagon has 8 sides, so it splits into 8 triangles. One triangle = ½ × 6 × 5 = 15 sq ft. Total = 8 × 15 = 120 sq ft.

    Watch for: A common mistake in Area of Regular Polygons is stopping after finding the area of ONE triangle and reporting that as the total area — or adding the number of triangles instead of multiplying. Remember: total area = (one triangle's area) × (number of sides), not (one triangle's area) + (number of sides).

    Level 2 Extension — Find the Tile Area Error

    1. 1Decompose the tileA regular hexagonal tile = 6 congruent triangles
    2. 2Identify one trianglebase = 8 in, height = 3.5 in
    3. 3Find the area of one triangleA = ½ × 8 × 3.5 = 14 sq in

    Which step has the mistake? Explain what went wrong, then show the correct work.

    Mistake: Step 4. The final step reported 14 sq in, which is only ONE of the hexagon's six congruent triangles. Multiplying by the triangle count finishes the job: Total = 6 × 14 = 84 sq in.

    Correct work: The student stopped after finding ONE triangle. A hexagon has 6 congruent triangles, so multiply by 6: Total = 6 × 14 = 84 sq in.
