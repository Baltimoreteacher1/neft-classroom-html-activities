OSAMR Sort

Seven real AI moves. Where does each one land?

What you do: as a group, place all seven cards on the OSAMR scale.

What you produce: your group's sorted board, the one card you argued about, and a takeaway everyone posts at the end.

How long: about 15 minutes. No writing until the last two steps.

How it works

  1. Pick your group number below.
  2. Sort the seven cards. Read what AI actually did, then tap one letter for each card.
  3. Name the card you argued about and say why in one line.
  4. Post your takeaway. Every group's line drops onto the room board at once.

There is no answer key. The point is where the room disagrees — the board marks every card groups split on, and those are the ones we talk about.

Not a ranking. The goal is the honest letter, not the highest one.
