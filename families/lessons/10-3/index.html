Surface Area Using Nets

I can find the surface area of a solid by using its net.

Speaking and writing goal: I can explain my work using the words surface area, net, face, and two-dimensional.

Interactive LessonGuided NotesPractice HandoutHomework PracticeStudent HelpCheck Understanding

What Your Child Is Learning

In this lesson, students practice how to find the surface area of a solid by using its net. They learn to show their thinking with numbers, pictures, words, or a model. The goal is not just getting an answer. Students should be able to explain why the answer makes sense.

Why This Matters

Geometry helps with building, decorating, packing, maps, art, and design. Students use it when they plan space, compare shapes, or figure out how much material a project needs.

What It May Look Like In Class

Your child may use the interactive lesson, guided notes, examples from the board, partner talk, and short practice problems. They may be asked to solve, draw a model, label important numbers, and explain their reasoning in a sentence.

How You Can Help At Home

You do not need to teach a new method. Ask your child to read the problem out loud, circle the important numbers, and explain what they tried first. Helpful questions include: What do you know? What are you trying to find? Does your answer make sense?

Apoyo para familias en español

Qué está aprendiendo su hijo/a

En esta lección, su hijo/a practica medir y resolver problemas de geometría. Puede usar dibujos, números, palabras o modelos para mostrar su pensamiento. Lo más importante es que pueda explicar por qué su respuesta tiene sentido.

Por qué es importante

La geometría se usa para construir, decorar, empacar, leer mapas y diseñar. Ayuda a planear espacios y calcular materiales.

Cómo puede ayudar en casa

No necesita ser experto/a en matemáticas. Pídale a su hijo/a que lea el problema en voz alta, marque los números importantes y explique su primer paso. Puede preguntar: ¿Qué sabes? ¿Qué necesitas encontrar? ¿Tu respuesta tiene sentido?

Key Vocabulary

Surface area Área de superficie

The total area of all the flat sides of a solid.

Net Plantilla (desarrollo plano)

A flat shape that folds up into a solid.

Example: Six connected rectangles that fold into a box

Face Cara

One flat side of a solid shape.

Two-dimensional Bidimensional

A flat shape with length and width, but no thickness.

Edge Arista

The line where two flat sides of a solid meet.

Try It Together

Pick one or two problems. Let your child explain the first step before opening the answer.

  1. A rectangle is 7 ft by 4 ft. What is its area?
    Show answer

    28 square feet

  2. A parallelogram has base 9 cm and height 3 cm. What is its area?
    Show answer

    27 square centimeters

  3. Name two units that could measure area.
    Show answer

    Square inches, square feet, square centimeters, or similar

  4. Why is labeling units helpful?
    Show answer

    It shows what the number measures and helps catch mistakes.
