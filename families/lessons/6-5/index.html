Write and Evaluate Algebraic Expressions

I can write an algebraic expression for a real situation and evaluate it for a given value of the variable.

Speaking and writing goal: I can explain my expression using the words variable, term, coefficient, constant, substitute, and evaluate.

What Your Child Is Learning

In this lesson, students practice how to write an algebraic expression for a real situation and evaluate it for a given value of the variable. They learn to show their thinking with numbers, pictures, words, or a model. The goal is not just getting an answer. Students should be able to explain why the answer makes sense.

Why This Matters

Expressions and equations help students describe patterns and missing numbers. This matters for planning, budgeting, games, coding, and solving everyday questions step by step.

What It May Look Like In Class

Your child may use the interactive lesson, guided notes, examples from the board, partner talk, and short practice problems. They may be asked to solve, draw a model, label important numbers, and explain their reasoning in a sentence.

How You Can Help At Home

You do not need to teach a new method. Ask your child to read the problem out loud, circle the important numbers, and explain what they tried first. Helpful questions include: What do you know? What are you trying to find? Does your answer make sense?

Apoyo para familias en español

Qué está aprendiendo su hijo/a

En esta lección, su hijo/a practica usar letras, números y reglas para resolver problemas. Puede usar dibujos, números, palabras o modelos para mostrar su pensamiento. Lo más importante es que pueda explicar por qué su respuesta tiene sentido.

Por qué es importante

Las expresiones y ecuaciones ayudan a describir patrones y números desconocidos. Son útiles para planear, presupuestar y resolver problemas paso a paso.

Cómo puede ayudar en casa

No necesita ser experto/a en matemáticas. Pídale a su hijo/a que lea el problema en voz alta, marque los números importantes y explique su primer paso. Puede preguntar: ¿Qué sabes? ¿Qué necesitas encontrar? ¿Tu respuesta tiene sentido?

Key Vocabulary

Write Algebraic Expressions Escribir expresiones algebraicas

Translate words or a situation into numbers, variables, and operation symbols.

Example: “8 fewer than a number” as n − 8

Variable Variable

A letter that stands for a number that is unknown or can change.

Example: The n in 8n + 15

Algebraic Expression Expresión algebraica

A math phrase that has at least one letter.

Example: 8n + 15

Coefficient Coeficiente

The number in front of a letter, like the 3 in 3x.

Example: The 7 in 7n

Constant Constante

A fixed number in an expression or equation that does not change.

Example: The 5 in 3x + 5

Like terms Términos semejantes

Terms with the same letter raised to the same power, like 2x and 5x.

Example: 2x and 5x
