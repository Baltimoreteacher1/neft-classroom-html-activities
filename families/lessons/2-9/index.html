Unit 2: Fraction Detective Agency Lesson 2-9 6.DS.6c

Describe Data by Mean Absolute Deviation

I can find the mean absolute deviation (MAD) to describe how spread out data is.

Speaking and writing goal: I can explain my work using the words mean absolute deviation, deviation, absolute value, and spread.

Interactive LessonGuided NotesPractice HandoutHomework PracticeStudent HelpCheck Understanding

What Your Child Is Learning

In this lesson, students practice how to find the mean absolute deviation (mad) to describe how spread out data is. They learn to show their thinking with numbers, pictures, words, or a model. The goal is not just getting an answer. Students should be able to explain why the answer makes sense.

Why This Matters

Data helps people make decisions. Students use these skills to read sports stats, compare survey results, notice patterns, and decide what a graph is really saying.

What It May Look Like In Class

Your child may use the interactive lesson, guided notes, examples from the board, partner talk, and short practice problems. They may be asked to solve, draw a model, label important numbers, and explain their reasoning in a sentence.

How You Can Help At Home

You do not need to teach a new method. Ask your child to read the problem out loud, circle the important numbers, and explain what they tried first. Helpful questions include: What do you know? What are you trying to find? Does your answer make sense?

Key Vocabulary

Mean Absolute Deviation Desviación media absoluta

The average distance of data values from the mean; it describes spread.

Example: (3 + 0 + 3) ÷ 3 = 2

Deviation Desviación

How far a number is from the mean.

Example: 7 being 3 away from a mean of 10

Absolute Value Valor absoluto

How far a number is from zero. It is never negative.

Example: The absolute value of −4 is 4

Spread Dispersión

How far apart the numbers are.

Example: Values running 5 to 25

Data distribution Distribución de datos

How the data looks: where it sits and how spread out it is.

Example: “Most scores cluster near 85, with one down at 68”

Variability Variabilidad

How spread out the numbers are.

Example: Scores running from 68 to 94

Try It Together

Pick one or two problems. Let your child explain the first step before opening the answer.

  1. Find the mean of 4, 6, 8, and 10.
    Show answer

    7

  2. Find the median of 3, 5, 9, 12, and 20.
    Show answer

    9

  3. A dot plot has four dots above 6. What does that mean?
    Show answer

    The value 6 appears four times.

  4. Name one question data could help answer.
    Show answer

    Answers vary, such as which lunch is most popular.
