# The Language Page: data inventory (from code and live network behavior)

Date: 2026-11-20. Scope: `https://thelanguagepage.com/access-practice-lab/` as loaded inside the app, plus this branch's
`native-app/` layer. Not legal advice; nothing here asserts COPPA, FERPA, Maryland SDPA, GDPR or App Store compliance.
Items marked LEGAL REVIEW need a person qualified to decide.

How each fact was obtained:

- **Measured**: Playwright on the live site, fresh browser profile, `context.route` logging every request (method, host,
  path, body keys, body text). Non-GET requests to the site were answered locally so nothing was written to production.
  Flows: home idle, Speaking activity with a fake microphone (record, stop, play back), Writing activity (typed text),
  Mr. Neft question helper (typed question), Save/Resume panel, family trip page.
- **Read**: taken from the cited code, not run.
- **Not run**: needs a class code, a camera flow that the site blocks, or a production write.

## 5. This branch's app layer adds

Nothing that sends data. `native-app/*.js` has no `fetch`, XHR, beacon or WebSocket (pinned by
`tools/access-lab-native-app.test.mjs`), no cookie, no IndexedDB. It writes one localStorage key,
`ewl-lp-reminder-v1` ("on" or "off"), and reads the lab's `accessPracticeLab:v1:prefs`. The share button hands only
`https://thelanguagepage.com/` and a one-line text to the phone's share sheet. Language changes go through the lab's own
control. `family-app/home-link.js` adds no request.

## 7. Legal-review list (decisions, not findings)

1. App Store label answers for Audio Data, Other User Content, Usage Data and Product Interaction (sections 2 and 6).
2. Whether Cloudflare Web Analytics inside a children's education app needs consent, a disclosure on the lab's own policy page, or removal for the app host.
3. Under-13 users: the app has no age gate and no parental step; the lab policy cites COPPA, FERPA and the Maryland Student Data Privacy Act as practices, not compliance.
4. Speech recognition in a WebView, which may send audio to Apple or Google.
5. Which privacy policy URL and which support contact the stores should hold.
6. AI processors for the Mr. Neft helper (Gemini, Anthropic, Cloudflare Workers AI): agreements and retention are not kept with the code.
