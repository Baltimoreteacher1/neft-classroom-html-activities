# Units 2–9 content review — October 2, 2026

This review inspected all 323 lesson configurations in Units 2–9: 72 canonical lessons and 251 related sessions, small groups, catch-up lessons, and supplements. It corrected or enriched 145 configurations (44 canonical and 101 related configurations). The review preserved lesson IDs, standards, routes, existing item counts, answer indices, and existing array lengths. Deliberate numeric changes corrected a mean target from 20 to 19.2 and refined number-line increments and snapping so the intended values can be entered. Optional `studentPrint` metadata now defines explicit paper directions and givens for 43 task copies, representing 25 distinct canonical tasks.

## Coverage and limits

Every canonical lesson's exit-ticket stem, choices, keyed answer, explanation, and Apply problem/model answer were read. The mathematical calculations and logic in those 72 exit tickets and 72 Apply problems were checked; six source figures were opened to resolve specific visual discrepancies. This was not a page-by-page visual inspection of every publisher deck or every exported PDF/DOCX. Not every diagram-dependent value in the remaining Apply problems was independently compared with its source image.

All 323 configurations were parsed and passed through the existing exact-arithmetic validator. It proved 4,438 checks, reported no failures, and explicitly skipped 599 undecidable cases. These are check counts, not counts of distinct questions. In particular, the arithmetic gate performs **zero decidable checks in Unit 7**. Its success cannot substitute for reviewing the geometry, interpretation, or graph interactions.

## Verification

- `node scripts/validate-math.mjs`: full repository, 5,276 proved checks, zero failures, 645 explicit skips. A scoped call to its exported `validateConfig` produced the Unit 2–9 table above.
- `node scripts/validate-distractors.mjs`: 5,531 multiple-choice items, 2,291 value-checked, zero failures; 44 existing warnings, 3,155 undecidable cases, and 85 form-selection cases remain separately reported.
- `npm run validate:es-parity`: 3,036 small-group practice items structurally complete in both languages.
- `npm run validate:es-concept-intro`, `validate:es-reflect`, and `validate:es-explore`: passed their positional-array and translation-completeness checks. These are not a claim of professional linguistic certification.
- `npm run validate:small-groups` and `npm run validate:part-two`: passed.
- Six inspected source figures: 2-1 Notice/Wonder; 2-4 and 2-5 Apply; 5-3, 5-6, and 7-7 Apply. Source assets were not altered.
- Thirty-four independent exact-arithmetic/domain assertions passed across the worked-answer and paper-contract review. The new parking-lot and shop-space areas were recomputed by the shoelace formula, independently of the written decomposition. Currency used decimal half-up rounding; an initial binary-floating-point assertion at a half-cent boundary was replaced with exact decimal arithmetic.
- Full config diff comparison confirmed unchanged identity fields, original array lengths, and answer indices. Changes to existing nontext fields are limited to the documented mean correction and tick/snap settings; optional `studentPrint` objects are new metadata.
- `node tools/curriculum-content-baseline.mjs`: passed after reviewing and accepting 30 intentional fingerprint changes. This baseline does not fingerprint every Apply answer, nested sort card, number-line target, or paper-metadata object; it is one protection layer, not a proof of all content accuracy.

The paper contract was authored from each actual problem. `prompt` supplies the complete student task; `pointTasks` supplies either a known coordinate or a task to derive one, never both. The `workspace` model requests student drawing space instead of a solved graphic. Volume tasks keep their dimensions while omitting computed volume; surface-area tasks retain their stated totals and known face areas; balance comparisons retain their unevaluated expressions; reflections keep original coordinates but omit image coordinates; the ticket graph retains the $45 unit price and required ticket counts without supplying calculated costs. Existing mathematical targets and worked explanations remain the grading/key source. The resource-quality renderer and regression tests consume this explicit contract.

The old `scripts/validate-lesson-quality.mjs` reports 248 failures and 292 warnings on the starting repository. Many failures apply a three-tier practice schema to second-session configurations that intentionally use `groupLevels`; its output must not be summarized as 248 broken lessons. The validator was not weakened or modified in this review. The active small-group and second-session validators passed.

## Generated resources and delivery boundary

The source of these corrections is `lessons/<id>/config.json`. The parent task coordinates regeneration and verifies the resulting files. Relevant generators are:

- `scripts/generate-notes.mjs`, `scripts/generate-slides.mjs`, and `scripts/generate-homework-html.mjs`: scope with `NEFT_LESSON_SCOPE`.
- `scripts/generate-worksheets.mjs` and `scripts/generate-sg-practice.mjs`: exact lesson IDs as positional arguments, including appropriate variants.
- `scripts/generate-lesson-support-pages.mjs`: canonical lesson IDs; preserves explicit hand-edited locks.
- `scripts/generate-docx.mjs` and `scripts/generate-pdf.mjs`: canonical IDs as positional arguments. PDF generation must be checked for actual output because absence of a browser can produce a successful exit without PDFs.
- Handout and printable generation is owned by the resource-quality review.

Re-running a small-group authoring generator with `--replace` is unnecessary and can overwrite authored variant work. The corrected variant configurations are already the source of truth. All work in this report is local; it does not claim deployment or a complete visual review of every binary download.
