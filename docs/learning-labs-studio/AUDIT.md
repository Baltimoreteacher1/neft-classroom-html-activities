# Learning Labs audit (2026-11-13)

## Scope and method

What was actually examined, so the findings can be judged against it:

- **All 46 labs, measured in a real browser** at 1366×768 (Chromebook) and 390×844 (phone) on the Mission and Investigate tabs: where the model and its first control sit, words above them, touch-target size, horizontal overflow, console errors (`tools/audit-learning-labs-first-screen.mjs`; `first-screen-baseline.*`, `first-screen-final.*`).
- **Five labs walked through all six tabs** on the live site at both sizes, with screenshots (`baseline/`): The Power Plant, Recipe Remix Radio, Shape-Shift Garden, The Balance Bureau, The Missing Whole Bureau.
- **The shared engine read in code**: `shared/app.mjs` (shell), `activities.mjs` (Learn / Investigate / Practice / Create), `model.mjs`, `math.mjs`, `models/` (21 registered models), `nextgen.mjs`.
- **Existing gates run**: `validate:learning-labs` (46 labs, 84 lessons, 1,426 practice items, 20 independent math fixtures) and `validate:learning-labs-browser` both passed at baseline.

**Not done.** Every practice item, hint and game goal of every lab was not read by hand. No human review (Grade 6 educator, accessibility reviewer, multilingual specialist) has taken place. No screen reader was run. No device other than headless Chromium at the two sizes was used. No field performance data exists.

## Usability risks (not fixed)

- **Phone first screen (updated 2026-11-13).** The first control is now on-screen in 41 of 46 labs. Data Newsroom, Middle Ground, Consistency Cup, Fair Share Festival and Outlier Investigation retain full-width data plots and place the controls below them. The Picture and control column are side by side for non-data models. The Pocket Reference button is at the lower-left on phone Investigate screens, away from the control column. See `first-screen-before.*` and `first-screen-phone-final.*` for the measured before/after. Targets remain at least 44px; no overflow or console errors were measured.
- **Wrap Lab Chromebook check (updated 2026-11-13).** The control is now on-screen in all 46 labs. Wrap Lab uses a compact model picture/control panel at Chromebook width; the question and the math are unchanged.
- **Always-on widgets** that remain by design: "Repair Byte's Mathematical Logic" (Learn and Practice, a per-kind bug catalog) and "Morning Lab Sync Code" (Create, classroom sync). They are not unrelated to the mathematics but have not been reviewed for load.
- **Spanish (updated 2026-11-13).** The five current Bridges and the Recorder now follow the saved English/Spanish language state. Spanish text is shown in `lang="es"` lines beside English. No native Spanish review has occurred; the translations need review by a fluent speaker.
- The curriculum shell's EN/ES/hub pills are 22–35px wide and 24–28px tall: they meet WCAG 2.2 AA's 24px minimum but not the 44px target. They belong to the shell, not the labs.

## Findings by scorecard dimension

No numerical scores are given: a score for each of 46 labs would need the hand review that has not happened.

| Dimension                                  | What was observed                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Mathematical accuracy and conceptual depth | The registered models are mathematically sound and gated by 20 independent fixtures and 500 property checks. The percent model draws a double number line; the ratio model draws batches and a pitcher-color lens. Depth was limited by what was presented, not by the models. |
| Clarity and progression                    | Defects 1–2.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Meaningful interaction                     | Direct manipulation exists on the models. Defect 7: no prediction record.                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Innovation and exploration                 | Up/down/same prediction and a target challenge existed; nothing connected representations or kept experiments.                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Visual design                              | Consistent but dense; defects 2 and 6. No semantic color tokens existed for mathematical meaning.                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Accessibility                              | Zero axe violations on sampled pages at baseline. Defect 5 for targets. Keyboard operation of the models works through the numeric inputs. Not tested with a screen reader.                                                                                                    |
| Ease of use and navigation                 | Tabs, hash links and Resume work. Defects 1–3.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Feedback and formative assessment          | Existing hints and misconception feedback in practice are per-item. Investigation had none.                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Performance, reliability, persistence      | Zero console errors and zero horizontal overflow in all 46 labs at both sizes. Saved state is versioned (`version: 1`) and additive. Field LCP / INP / CLS were not measured.                                                                                                  |
