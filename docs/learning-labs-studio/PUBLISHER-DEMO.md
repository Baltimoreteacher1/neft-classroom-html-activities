# Publisher walkthrough (about four minutes, all working features)

Open https://eduwonderlab.com/curriculum/learning-labs/recipe-remix/#investigate on a Chromebook-size window.

1. **First ten seconds (0:00).** The page opens on one question, "Compare juice to water and juice to the whole drink," with the model and its two numbers on the first screen. Nothing needs explaining. _Compare with the baseline screenshot `baseline/recipe-remix-investigate-desktop.png`, where the model was off the screen._
2. **Move one number (0:20).** Change Juice. The picture, the Table, the Equation and the sentence under "How the pieces connect" all change together; the Check stays "Holds".
3. **Predict, then test (0:50).** Under "Test an idea" press "Test this idea" next to "Multiply both amounts by 2". The page asks first: "Will the recipe stay equivalent?" Answer. Only then does it show Before → After and why: both amounts were multiplied by 2.
4. **A misconception and its response (1:30).** Test "Add the same number of cups to both". Predict "yes". The page says the relationship changed, shows 3:2 becoming 5:4, shows that 3/2 is not 5/4, and names the misconception: adding the same amount to both does not keep the ratio.
5. **Try it for real (2:15).** Press "Show this in the model" and watch the model, table and sentence move to the new, non-equivalent mixture.
6. **A new attempt (2:40).** Press "Test another idea", choose the multiplication idea again with a different factor, and predict again. Open "My experiments": both attempts are there with "Prediction matched / did not match", and "Back to before" returns the model to either state. Tick two to compare.
7. **Another model (3:15).** Open https://eduwonderlab.com/curriculum/learning-labs/missing-whole/#investigate. Test "Check a whole you think is right" with 5.4 (30% of 18): it names the classic mistake and says to work back to 100%. Then enter 60.
