# Award map, 2026-27 cycle

Researched 2026-10-16. Every fact below was read on the program's own page unless it is marked **UNVERIFIED**. Where a 2026-27 cycle is not announced, last cycle's dates appear and are labelled "prior-year reference". Fees are exact figures from the cited page; none are estimated.

Site facts used here are limited to the eight features Joel listed (see section 3). No outcome, usage, or testimonial claims are made.

## 1. Bottom line

- Two programs closed in the last week: The EdTech Awards 2027 (final deadline 2026-10-15, 10:00 pm ET) and the Learning Engineering Tools Competition 2027 Phase I (2026-10-13). Both have next cycles in 2027.
- One program with a firm deadline is still open for a plausible entry: Webby Awards, early deadline 2026-10-30.
- The best cost-to-fit programs are AASL Best Digital Tools (deadline 2027-02-01, no fee stated), the EdTech Awards (free code for public-school educators, some categories), Digital Promise certifications (rolling), and CODiE Product Awards (opens 2027-01-11).
- Several well-known items are no longer usable: Student Privacy Pledge (retired 2025-04-25), Common Sense Education product reviews (paused January 2026), PAEMST (paused).
- Nothing researched requires randomized efficacy data. Digital Promise "Evidence-Based Edtech: ESSA Tier 3" needs a correlational study; every other program found asks for demos, narrative, case studies, or research rationale.

## 4. Recommended priority order

Order combines fit, cost, effort, and timing.

1. **AASL Best Digital Tools (deadline 2027-02-01).** No fee on the page, developers may nominate. Prepare a free-access link and a short description. Low cost, so do it regardless.
2. **Email the EdTech Digest organizers now** (edtechdigest@gmail.com, +1 323 823 4192, from the [entry guide](https://www.edtechdigest.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/GUIDE-The-EdTech-Awards-2027.pdf)). The 2027 program closed one day ago; ask whether a late entry is possible, which categories take the free public-school-educator code, and when the 2028 program opens.
