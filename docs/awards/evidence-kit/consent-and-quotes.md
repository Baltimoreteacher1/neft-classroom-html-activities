# Consent and quote collection templates

Templates only. Nothing has been collected and no one has agreed to anything. Every
`[FILL IN: ...]` must be completed by the teacher. These forms are not legal advice and have not been
reviewed by the district: `[FILL IN: district office that must approve student-facing forms, approval date]`.
Do not send any form until that approval exists. Rules for this kit:

1. Permission is optional and never affects grades, placement, supports or access to any activity. Say so on the form.
2. Nothing here is a testimonial written by someone else. Quotes are the person's own words, verbatim, with
   their approval of the exact text.

   family ticks that box separately.

   material is removed where the publisher allows it, and the form says this plainly.

## D. How to collect quotes

Ask open questions. Do not ask leading questions ("Isn't the tutor great?"). Record the answer in the
person's words. Do not fix grammar inside a quote; cut with "..." only if the meaning is unchanged, and show
the person the cut version.

| Who     | Prompts (pick two)                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Student | What is one thing in the math site that helped you understand? What was confusing? What would you tell a new student? Did the site ever give you the answer when you wanted it? |
| Family  | What did you do with your child on the family page? What was easy or hard? Was the Spanish clear? Would you use it again without being asked?                                   |
| Teacher | What changed in how you plan, group or follow up? What did the site not do well? How much time did it take? Would you recommend it to a colleague and why?                      |
