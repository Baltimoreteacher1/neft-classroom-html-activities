# Sources for The Language Page front layer

Checked 2026-11-27. Each fact the new pages state is listed with where it was checked and how sure we are.
**How it was checked:** web search results that quote the WIDA pages named below. The session could not open
wida.wisc.edu PDFs directly (its network policy blocked the host), so wording marked "summary" was read from search
summaries of those pages, not from the PDF itself. Anything below "high" confidence is labelled **approximate** in the UI.

## WIDA ACCESS (the test)

| Fact used on the site | Source | Confidence | Shown as |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The test is now named **WIDA ACCESS** (formerly ACCESS for ELLs); also WIDA ACCESS for Kindergarten and WIDA Alternate ACCESS | wida.wisc.edu/assess/access; WIDA 2025–26 materials | High | Fact |
| Online grade-level clusters: 1, 2–3, 4–5, 6–8, 9–12. **Grade 3 is in the 2–3 cluster.** | WIDA Guide for Score Reports; wida.wisc.edu sample items ("Grades 2–3") | High | Fact |
| Kindergarten ACCESS is paper-based and one-on-one (redesigned for 2025–26) | wida.wisc.edu/kaccessredesign | High | Fact |
| Listening and Reading are scored by the platform and are semi-adaptive; results place a student in a tier for Speaking and Writing | wida.wisc.edu/assess/access/tests/online | High | Fact |
| Listening and Reading questions are multiple choice | inferred from WIDA sample items; not stated in one sentence we could read | Medium | **Approximate** |
| Online Speaking: a virtual test administrator gives tasks; students record answers with a headset microphone; tasks are pitched at levels 1, 3 and 5; trained raters score | wida.wisc.edu/assess/access/tests/online; WIDA state training decks | High | Fact |
| Writing: grades 1–3 handwrite; grades 4–12 type by default | WIDA test coordinator training 2026 | High | Fact |
| The test is untimed. "Up to" times: Listening 65, Reading 60, Speaking 50, Writing 90 minutes; typical times are shorter and vary by tier | wida.wisc.edu/assess/access/tests/online; WIDA MA test coordinator training 2026 | Medium | **Approximate** |
| Composite weights: Oral = 50% L + 50% S; Literacy = 50% R + 50% W; Comprehension = 70% R + 30% L; Overall = 35% R + 35% W + 15% L + 15% S | WIDA ACCESS Interpretive Guide for Student Score Reports 2025–2026 (state copy); WIDA Screener Interpretive Guide | High | Fact (used by the Score Interpreter) |
| Proficiency levels 1–6: Entering, Emerging, Developing, Expanding, Bridging, Reaching; reported to one decimal, 1.0–6.0 | wida.wisc.edu/assess/access/scores-reports | High | Fact |
| Standard setting held July 2026: 2025–26 scale scores are a new baseline and proficiency levels on 2025–26 reports should be "used with caution" | WIDA 2025–26 Interpretive Guide caution note (summary) | Medium | **Approximate**; shown as a caution on the Score Interpreter |
| The Score Interpreter computes composites from **proficiency levels**, not scale scores. WIDA computes composites from scale scores, so a composite of levels is an estimate | Same guide | High that it differs | Labelled "estimate" on screen |
| Maryland has been a WIDA member since 2011; exit criterion Overall 4.5+ on WIDA ACCESS (PL 4+ on Alternate ACCESS) | wida.wisc.edu/memberships/consortium/md; MSDE State Board presentation, 2026-01-14 | High | Fact, with "check with your district" |

## Trademark notice

No trademark page was found on wida.wisc.edu. Ownership is recorded with the USPTO (WIDA, ACCESS FOR ELLS, WIDA CONSORTIUM;
"WIDA ACCESS" application pending) and WCER licensee terms say "WIDA ... are trademarks of the Board of Regents of the
University of Wisconsin System." The site's notice: *"WIDA and WIDA ACCESS (formerly ACCESS for ELLs) are trademarks of the
Board of Regents of the University of Wisconsin System."* **Approximate:** confirm the wording with WIDA before any print use.
