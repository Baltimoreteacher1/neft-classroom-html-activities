The new student E2E starts with a clean local ledger, completes listening/evidence, reading, decision, speaking recording/self-review, and writing feedback/portfolio submission, and reaches the evidence debrief. It also exposed a progression defect: a hidden stretch question was included in the phase-completion predicate, trapping learners who did not yet have the prerequisite independent evidence. The mission now checks completion against the tasks actually visible under the deterministic stretch rule, and only records visible questions in its phase evidence. Regression coverage confirms the fresh learner can advance while the stretch question remains hidden.

Human ESOL/science editorial review is still required. This single mission begins grade-band coverage; it does not meet the specification for a complete, quality-assured collection across both bands and all language functions.
