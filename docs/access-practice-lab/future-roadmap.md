## Release gates for cloud AI and identity

Do not enable cloud speech or language models until the product has an approved provider, explicit data-flow/retention terms, student/guardian and organizational review as appropriate, an authenticated school/tenant authorization model, strict output validation, human review, model/prompt version records, teacher override history, and tested deletion/export workflows. Keep deterministic local and template fallbacks available.
