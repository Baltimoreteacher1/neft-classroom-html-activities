### Unit 1 is absent from the workbook — the Pre-Unit stands in its place

**[INFERRED]** The workbook never names "Unit 1"; its first row is _Pre Unit: Course 1 Pre Unit_
with an 11-day budget and a Unit Quiz. EduWonderLab **Unit 1 — Math Is…** (6 lessons: _Math is Mine_,
_Math is Exploring and Thinking_, _Math is In My World_, _Math is Explaining and Sharing_,
_Math is Finding Patterns_, _Math is Ours_) is the course's launch/community unit and is the only
unit that fits that slot. I mapped the Pre-Unit to EWL Unit 1. **[CONFIRM]** — if your building's
"Pre Unit" guidance document is a different set of readiness tasks, swap the six lesson rows on the
Year Pacing sheet; the dates and the 11-day budget stay as they are.

### "Components" are not the same thing as EduWonderLab lessons

**[SOURCE]** Every unit's component count exceeds its EWL lesson count — e.g. Unit 3 has 19
components against 10 lessons, Unit 9 has 13 against 4. Reveal counts launch/explore/practice
segments as separate components; EduWonderLab packages them into whole lessons.

**[INFERRED]** I therefore treated _components + additional days_ strictly as a **day budget** and
spent that budget on EWL lessons, review, assessment, project and flex. I did **not** try to make
component counts equal lesson counts.

### Conflicting fields, documented before choosing

The workbook's own week totals sum to **180.0** days, but its unit budgets sum to **179.5** —
the sheet leaves 0.5 day unallocated. **[SOURCE]**

## Flex / Reteach Days

**36 days of deliberate margin** out of 180 — **20% of the year**.

| Type     | Days | What it is                                                                                                   |
| -------- | ---- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Catch-Up | 21   | Real EduWonderLab catch-up stations (`/lessons/<id>-catchup/`), anchored to the specific lessons they repair |
| Review   | 12   | Unit retrieval, always the day before that unit's assessment                                                 |
| Flex     | 3    | Genuinely uncommitted — no plan attached                                                                     |

These are purposeful, not scattered filler:

- **At unit ends** — every unit closes Review → Assessment → Project, with catch-ups clustered before it.
- **At quarter boundaries** — Q2 opens Oct 26 mid-Unit-4; Q3 opens Jan 19 on the Unit 7 assessment; Q4 opens Apr 5 inside Unit 5.
- **After long interruptions** — Jan 4, the first day back from Winter Break, is a Catch-Up day, not new content.
- **Before MSTAR** — the MSTAR block opens with 3 preparation days before any administration day.
- **On every shortened day** — see below.

Full list with dates and purpose: **Flex Days** sheet of the pacing workbook.

**[INFERRED]** Three uncommitted Flex days across a 180-day year is thin. That is a direct
consequence of the district budget totalling 179.5 against 177.5 available days — the year has
almost no true slack. The 21 Catch-Up days are the real reserve: each is a scheduled day whose
content can be dropped without losing a lesson.

## Potential Tight Spots

Ranked. These are where the year will actually hurt.

1. **Unit 6 has zero slack.** 15 lessons + 1 review + 1 assessment + 2 project days = 19 days
   against a 19-day budget. Every other unit has at least one catch-up day to give back; Unit 6 has
   none. Nov 5 – Dec 7 also contains Nov 6 PD, Nov 25 Wellness and Nov 26–27 Thanksgiving. **Losing
   one day here pushes into Unit 7 immediately.** This is the first place to watch.
2. **Unit 2 (Statistics) is next-to-last, in May, next to the testing window.** 12 lessons in 17
   days ending May 17, three days before MSTAR prep. If anything upstream slips, Statistics absorbs
   it — and it is the unit with the most lessons per available day after Unit 6.
3. **Week 30 (Mar 22–26)** is 3.5 days: an early release plus the first day of Spring Break. It is
   also where 1.5 of the 2.5 lost days come from.
4. **Week 11 (Nov 2–6)** is 3 days — Election Day and Systemic PD in the same week, in the middle of
   the Unit 4 → Unit 6 handoff.
5. **Unit 10 runs on 8.5 days ending on a half day.** There is no room to extend it. If the year
   slips at all, Unit 10 is what gets shortened — and it is the closing reflection unit.
6. **Only 3 genuinely uncommitted Flex days exist.** Beyond those, absorbing lost time means
   spending a Catch-Up day.

## Editable Design Decisions

**DOCX** — 73 pages, US Letter **landscape**, 0.5" margins.

- Everything is **native Word**: real `Heading 1/2/3` styles, real tables, real paragraphs, native
  cell shading and borders. **No page is an image. No calendar is a picture.** Every date, lesson
  title, unit label, note, assessment and project entry is directly editable text.
- **[INFERRED]** The whole document is landscape rather than mixing orientations. Weekly pages need
  six columns and monthly grids need five wide cells; a single orientation also gives predictable
  page breaks and one paper setting for the whole print job.
- Tables use repeating header rows and `cantSplit` on every row, so no calendar row or lesson row
  can break across a page.
- Unit accents are muted and desaturated (one colour per unit). **Colour is never the only cue** —
  the unit number and name are always present as text, and day types (`ASSESSMENT`, `PROJECT`,
  `FLEX`, `Catch-Up`, `½ DAY / EARLY RELEASE`) are always spelled out.
- Links to the interactive whole-group lesson and both small-group lessons are real Word hyperlinks
  derived from the EduWonderLab manifest, not typed URLs.
- The _Assessment / Notes_ and _Small-Group / Differentiation_ columns and the _Flex / Adjustment
  Notes_ box are intentionally left empty — they are yours.

**XLSX** — 6 sheets, real cells, real dates, frozen panes, named tables for filter/sort.

| Sheet              | Rows | Purpose                                                               |
| ------------------ | ---- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Year Pacing        | 210  | One row per school date; the structured source of truth               |
| Scope and Sequence | 54   | Clean structured copy of the `Course 1` sheet; original untouched     |
| Unit Summary       | 12   | Unit ranges and totals, **all formula-driven** via COUNTIF/COUNTIFS   |
| Calendar Events    | 50   | Every non-standard school day                                         |
| Flex Days          | 36   | Purpose and location of every buffer day                              |
| Validation         | 28   | Build-time checks plus **live formulas** that recalculate as you edit |

- Dates are real Excel dates (`yyyy-mm-dd`), not text.
- Lesson **ID**, **Title**, **Standard** and **Objective** are separate fields, so the sheet can be
  filtered by standard or sorted by unit without parsing strings.
- No merged cells anywhere.
- Status shading is subtle and **always accompanied by the status word** in the `School Status`
  column — nothing is encoded by colour alone.
- Totals are formulas, not typed numbers. Edit a Day Type on the Year Pacing sheet and the Unit
  Summary and Validation counts follow.

### Daily-lesson-plan compatibility

Each instructional row on **Year Pacing** already carries Date, Day, Week, Quarter, Unit, Unit Name,
Lesson ID, Lesson Title, Standard, Objective, Day Type, Interactive Whole Group Lesson URL,
Small-Group Lesson URLs, Calendar Notes and Planning Notes — enough to generate a daily lesson-plan
document per row without touching the DOCX. No such automation was built; the data is just shaped
for it.
