## Inaccessible materials

Not seen, either because it sits behind login, is not described on public pages, or could not be read:

- The student interface itself (Teach Me animations, problem screens, avatar editor, board and team games). Only descriptions and marketing images exist publicly; images were not described by the fetch tool and were not examined.
- The list of math domains and the full skill map per grade (the Math page does not list them).
- Per-lesson mastery criteria (problems needed, accuracy needed) and the "simple success problem" step; the latter appeared only in a search snippet.
- How the placement test computes the final grade level (linked to a separate article that was not opened) and whether or how retests work (not stated on either page read).
- Fact Fluency details: what "fact levels" are, the 0-15 range semantics beyond fact selection, session structure, default time limit, whether any game layer sits on fact practice.
- Grade ranges for Fact Fluency, Numbers, and SideBySide.
- The teacher dashboard screens, Custom Reports builder, Quick Benchmarker, class-level skill-gap views, and the whole Whiteboard Activities library.
- Whether the placement test or paths expose prerequisite relationships, and whether teachers can override placement results (the blog is silent; the Teacher Guide shows lesson-sequence editing).
- Help Center articles that appeared only in search results and were not opened: 11000012362, 11000036370, 11000097599, 11000012211, 11000012346, 11000084816, 11000117654, 11000107307.
- The MobyMax Information Kit PDF (https://data.mobymax.com/mc/share/InfoKit.pdf): fetch failed on size limit.
- Parent Portal and Boost at Home screens, and the Families signup flow (a trial needs an account; not attempted).
- Third-party reviews (Common Sense Education) and independent research were not opened.

## Top 12 design lessons worth implementing as ORIGINAL equivalents

1. **Diagnose before drilling, and keep the diagnostic short.** Probe the hardest fact in each family first and expand only on a miss. End on a clear rule (MobyMax stops below 60% per grade) and tell the teacher why it ended. Cite: Help 11000012081.
2. **A visible per-fact mastery map.** MobyMax's color grid (not yet, building, mastered) with a streak reset on any miss is easy for teachers and students to read at a glance. Build your own grid with a fourth "slipping, needs refresh" state. Cite: Help 11000012366.
3. **Mastery means a streak, not one lucky answer.** Three consecutive correct answers is the public rule for a fact. Consider requiring them across separate sessions or days so recall is retained, which MobyMax does not claim to do.
4. **Interleave after mastery.** Once a set is mastered, mix operations to keep earlier facts alive. Cite: /skill-practice/fact-fluency. Add a small, steady share of already-mastered facts to every session.
5. **Teacher controls: choose the facts, daily minutes, and time per problem.** Offer presets and per-student overrides. Cite: /skill-practice/fact-fluency; Help 11000101346. Keep Joel's standing rule in view: no countdown pressure in student games (see `data/product-decisions.json`); a response-time threshold can still be measured silently for fluency.
6. **Teach before practice, in small pieces.** A very short explainer or visual strategy opens each skill, then a handful of practice items. Cite: /skill-practice/math. For the Lab, make the explainer a strategy (make-ten, doubles, skip-counting) rather than a procedure.
7. **Concrete first for number sense.** MobyMax's Numbers module puts touch manipulatives and an abacus ahead of speed work. Build original ten-frames, number bonds, and arrays in the Lab before timed recall. Cite: /skill-practice/number-sense.
8. **Remediation should be visible and explained.** MobyMax does not publicly explain prerequisites. The Lab can publish its own prerequisite graph for facts and show students "we are fixing x3 so x6 gets easier." This is an opening to do better than the public MobyMax material.
9. **Motivation that rewards effort and mastery, with teacher caps.** MobyMax pairs earned game time, board games, badges, certificates and a customizable mascot, and gives teachers on/off and scheduling control. Use an original mascot and collectible garden/map, tied to strategy learned and effort, off by default for games, with teacher caps. Avoid backward-move penalties and ranked leaderboards. Cite: /engage-connect/student-motivation; /engage-connect/game-learning.
10. **Read-aloud and bilingual support on every item.** MobyMax advertises read-aloud on each part of a problem and translated SideBySide lessons. For EduWonderLab this means EN/ES audio and text on every prompt, hint and feedback line. Cite: /skill-practice/math; /sidebyside-learning.
11. **Error-level drill-down for teachers.** MobyMax shows the exact missed problems and wrong answers. Go one step further: label the likely error pattern and suggest a short intervention group. Cite: /assessments/detailed-reporting. Pair with saved groups and an auto-suggested "shared missing facts" group the teacher confirms.
12. **Close the loop with home, using low-tech tasks.** MobyMax's Boost at Home gives parents a guided 10-minute lesson, uses pencil and paper, and sends daily updates. Mirror that with printable bilingual fact games and a short weekly family note, no account needed. Cite: /boost-at-home; /sidebyside-learning.
