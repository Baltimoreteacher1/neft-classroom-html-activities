# Math Literacy Studio: a two-minute guide for teachers

Open it from the Teachers area of The Language Page, or go to `/access-practice-lab/math-literacy`. No account is needed.

**Three ways in**
1. *Teach a skill*: choose a grade (1 to 8) and a skill. You see a teacher guide, vocabulary cards, reading passages, guided and independent practice, word problems, discussion, speaking, listening and writing tasks, exit tickets, assessments, error analysis, intervention and extension, a family page, and interactive activities for students.
2. *Create a resource*: choose a skill and a material, adjust a little, preview, then print or download.
3. *Support a learner*: say what you notice ("can calculate but cannot explain"). You get a short check, resources, scaffolds and a short plan.

**Support levels**: More support, Standard, Extension. Under "Adjust" you can set the mathematics level and the language support separately. Language support never lowers the mathematics.

**Getting it out**: Print or save as PDF (your browser's print window); Word (.docx) and PowerPoint (.pptx) with real editable text; one interactive web page that works offline. The answer key is always a separate file.

**What it does not do**: it does not label students, measure language proficiency, or send anything off your device. Standards codes are informational. Spanish text is a draft awaiting native review.
