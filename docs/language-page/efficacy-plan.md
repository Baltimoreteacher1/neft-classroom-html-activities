# The Language Page — efficacy study plan

**Status:** a plan, written 2026-11-25. **No study has been run and there are no results.** Nothing here may be cited as
evidence. It is written so that a district partner, a researcher and Joel can agree on a design before any data is
collected. ESSA Tier IV ("demonstrates a rationale") is the most this plan supports today; no tier is claimed.

## Research questions
1. **Use.** In classes that adopt the product, how often and how long do learners practise, and with what support?
2. **Independence.** Does the share of tasks done independently rise over a term for the same task type?
3. **Writing.** Do learners' kept drafts improve on the practice rubric across a term?
4. **Official growth (exploratory).** Do classes using the product show different year-over-year ACCESS growth than
   comparable classes that do not?
5. **Teacher time (exploratory).** Do teachers report less time choosing and differentiating practice?

## Design
- **Questions 1–3 and 5:** a single-group, repeated-measures pilot in 4–6 classes over one term. Descriptive only.
- **Question 4:** a quasi-experimental comparison on district data, **only with written district approval**: classes
  using the product versus matched comparison classes (grade, starting ACCESS level, school, teacher experience).
  Matching variables are fixed before outcomes are seen.

## Measures
| Measure | Source | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Use and independence | Product evidence (on device; class totals when joined) | alias only, no names |
| Writing quality | Teacher scoring of kept first and later drafts on the practice rubric | two scorers on a 20% sample; report agreement |
| ACCESS growth | Official scores **typed by the teacher or supplied by the district** | the 2025–26 scale reset means growth across the reset is not comparable; use only same-scale intervals |
| Teacher time and experience | Short survey and time log | SUS and UMUX-Lite for usability (`usability-protocol.md`) |
| Fidelity | Teacher log (`pilot-kit.md`) | minutes per week, task types, support levels |

## Analysis plan
- Report descriptives with intervals; no significance claims from the pilot.
- For Question 4: multilevel model with students nested in classes, covariates fixed in advance, and growth defined from
  same-scale scores. Report the effect with its interval and say plainly whether it is distinguishable from zero.
- **Power:** state as an assumption, not a result. With few classes the design cannot detect small effects; the plan says so.

## Ethics and data
- District and school approval, parent notice and student assent before any data collection; templates in `pilot-kit.md`
  (legal review required, H-08).
- No student names in research data; alias keys held by the teacher.
- No identity, recordings or raw student text leave the device for research unless a learner chooses to share.

## Limits
Self-selected teachers, short duration, practice-to-test transfer unproven, machine-drafted Spanish, no recorded human
audio. A positive pilot would justify a larger study, not a claim.

## Decisions needed
