# The Language Page — pilot kit

**Status:** templates, written 2026-11-25. **Every consent and notice text below is a draft for legal and district review
(H-08). Do not use any of it with families until a person with authority has approved it.** Nothing here has been used yet.

## 1. Teacher onboarding checklist (10 minutes, no extra prep)
- [ ] Open the site, choose the Teacher door, and read the one-page "What this is" (`/access-practice-lab/start`).
- [ ] Create or join a class code; choose aliases (nicknames, never full names) with students.
- [ ] Pick the grade band and one task per domain for week 1; leave the support level on "suggested".
- [ ] Tell students the practice is not a test and does not give an official score.
- [ ] Know where "Start everything fresh" and "Download my data" are (privacy page).
- [ ] Decide your weekly practice minutes (aim: the same minutes each week, so fidelity can be read).

## 2. Weekly fidelity log (one row per class per week)
| Week | Class (alias) | Minutes of practice | Task types used | Support levels used (frames / word bank / on my own) | Anything that did not work | Teacher initials |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Fidelity bands to report, not to judge: **low** under 50% of planned minutes, **partial** 50–89%, **full** 90% or more.

## 3. Draft caregiver notice and consent (English)
> **Dear family,** Our class is trying a free practice website called The Language Page. Students practise English for
> school by listening, speaking, reading and writing, and can choose how much help they get. The website does not ask for a
> name, email or account. Students use a nickname. Practice is saved on the school device. Nothing is sold, and there are
> no ads. This is classroom practice; it is not a test and gives no official score.
> **If we ask to use your child's practice records for a study,** we will ask you again in writing, say exactly what we
> would use, and keep names out of everything. You can say no at any time and your child's grades and services will not change.
> Questions: [teacher name and contact]. [School / district privacy contact]

## 4. Borrador de aviso y consentimiento (español)
> **Estimadas familias:** Nuestra clase está probando un sitio web gratuito de práctica llamado The Language Page. Los
> estudiantes practican inglés para la escuela escuchando, hablando, leyendo y escribiendo, y pueden elegir cuánta ayuda
> reciben. El sitio no pide nombre, correo ni cuenta. Los estudiantes usan un apodo. La práctica se guarda en el
> dispositivo de la escuela. No se vende nada y no hay anuncios. Es práctica de clase; no es un examen y no da una nota oficial.
> **Si pedimos usar los registros de práctica de su hijo o hija para un estudio,** se lo pediremos de nuevo por escrito,
> diremos exactamente qué usaríamos y no usaremos nombres. Pueden decir que no en cualquier momento y las notas y los
> servicios de su hijo o hija no cambiarán. Preguntas: [nombre y contacto del docente]. [Contacto de privacidad]
>
> *Traducción automática; debe revisarla una persona bilingüe antes de usarse (H-05).*

## 6. Stop rules
Stop and tell the district if a family withdraws, a learner is distressed, a technical problem loses student work, or any
privacy question comes up that this kit does not answer.
